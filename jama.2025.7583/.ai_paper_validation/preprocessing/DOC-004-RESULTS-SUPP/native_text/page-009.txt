Forest plot of 6 -month CSDH recurrence assessed by the adjudication committee for the 
primary analysis using a logistic regression model adjusted on randomization stratification 
factors, namely anticoagulant/antiplatelet use (yes versus no) and CSDH localization 
(bilateral versus unilateral). The last sensitivity analysis presented excluded  patients who did 
not receive embolization in the experimental group.
