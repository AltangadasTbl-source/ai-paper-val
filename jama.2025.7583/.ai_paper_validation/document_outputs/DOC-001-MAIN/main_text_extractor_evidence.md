## Design, analysis population, and outcome definitions

| Item | Reported evidence | Exact main-article location |
|---|---|---|
| Randomized population | 342 randomized; 171 assigned to MMA embolization and 171 to standard medical care alone. | PDF p. 1, Abstract: Design, Setting, and Participants / Intervention; PDF p. 4, Results: Patients; Figure 1 |
| Primary outcome definition | CSDH recurrence at 6 months: homolateral CSDH with midline shift >=5 mm or symptomatic homolateral CSDH (including death); homolateral CSDH >10 mm on 6-month CT; repeat surgery for homolateral recurrence; or new hospitalization related to homolateral recurrence. | PDF p. 3, Methods: End Points |
| Secondary outcomes specified | Repeat surgery for homolateral recurrence; disability/dependency (mRS >=4) at 1 and 6 months; mortality at 1 and 6 months; total cumulative CSDH-related hospital stay through 6 months; and minor/major embolization procedure-related complications. | PDF p. 3, Methods: End Points |
| Primary-analysis population / missing data | All randomized patients by allocation (full analysis set); missing data multiply imputed. The primary model was logistic regression adjusted for anticoagulant/antiplatelet use and unilateral/bilateral CSDH status. | PDF p. 3, Methods: Statistical Analysis; PDF p. 6, Table 2 footnotes b-c |
| Prespecified subgroups | CSDH localization (unilateral vs bilateral) and anticoagulant or antiplatelet medication use (yes vs no); interaction tested in the logistic regression model. | PDF p. 3, Methods: Statistical Analysis; PDF p. 6, Figure 2 caption |

## Participant flow

| Flow stage | Reported counts | Exact location |
|---|---|---|
| Assessed / excluded | 659 adults assessed; 317 excluded: 164 prespecified noninclusion criteria other than refusal, 101 patient refusal, 34 unknown reasons, and 18 other reasons. | PDF p. 4, Figure 1 |
| Randomized / assigned | 342 randomized: 171 to MMA embolization and 171 to standard care alone. | PDF p. 4, Figure 1; Results: Patients |
| Intervention delivery | MMA arm: 145 underwent intervention; 26 did not (13 dangerous anastomosis, 5 refusal, 4 catheterization failure, 2 severe atheroma, 1 postsurgical acute SDH, 1 MCA occlusion during navigation). | Follow-up discontinuation | MMA arm: 15 (9 deaths [6 neurological/undetermined; 4 after recurrence; 2 before recurrence; 3 other cause], 5 lost to follow-up, 1 withdrew consent). | Evaluable / nonevaluable cases | MMA arm: 162 evaluable; 9 nonevaluable (5 lost to follow-up, 3 death from another cause, 1 withdrawal); 24 recurrence and 138 no recurrence. Standard-care arm: 157 evaluable; 14 nonevaluable (8 death from another cause, 5 lost to follow-up, 1 withdrawal); 33 recurrence and 124 no recurrence. | PDF p. 4, Figure 1 |
| Primary analysis | 171 included in each arm; multiple imputation for 9 nonevaluable MMA-arm cases and 14 nonevaluable standard-care cases. | PDF p. 4, Figure 1 |
