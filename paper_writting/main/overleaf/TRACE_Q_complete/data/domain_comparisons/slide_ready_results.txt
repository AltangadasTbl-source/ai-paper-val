ENDPOINTS AND SCOPE
Every endpoint requires D=retain and uses the FINAL E domain.
Report/plan established inconsistency: K=substantive_inconsistency or minor_presentation; substantive subset: K=substantive_inconsistency only.
Retained candidates coded indeterminate/non_inconsistency are not established inconsistencies.
Protocol-report all retained includes every L state; substantive difference requires L=substantive_difference. These are differences, not confirmed errors.
L is optional: missing in 2/102 earlier and 3/112 later retained protocol-report candidates. A blank L does not enter the substantive-difference subset.
Descriptive extension explicitly includes original P_R/P_P relationships and domain-edited cases. It is NOT the existing report-only secondary endpoint.
Original primary/strict statistical and S_C/S_W report-only restrictions are preserved; no original files were rewritten.
Validation reproduces the original report-only secondary counts, exact P values and bootstrap confidence limits on both scales.
Report substantive extension totals 1 earlier + 4 later = 5; S_C/S_W sensitivity totals 1 earlier + 3 later = 4.
The additional case is listed in report_substantive_extension_only.csv. Domain edits are listed in domain_edits.csv.
All retained report candidates: 4 earlier, 8 later; established: 3 and 8 (one earlier indeterminate).
All retained plan candidates: 12 earlier, 20 later; established: 12 and 17 (three later indeterminate).

METHOD
Effects are later minus earlier. Exact two-sided paper-label permutation tests use all allocations of 19 of 44 papers and absolute mean differences, retaining ties.
95% percentile bootstrap intervals: 20,000 independent within-period paper resamples, seed 20261022; original analysis/paper_results.csv paper order.
Any-finding tests use binary paper outcomes. P-values are exploratory, unadjusted, and descriptive; no confirmatory family was declared.

INTERPRETATION
This is an availability-selected comparison of publication cohorts before the pandemic and in a later period; it does not estimate a pandemic effect.
Detected counts depend on AI candidate generation, document availability, final domain assignment and human adjudication. Endpoint changes must not be read as changes in research quality.
Intervals quantify paper-resampling uncertainty conditional on this candidate set and final review; they do not incorporate missed findings or adjudication uncertainty.
Sparse report/plan substantive endpoints require caution. Protocol-report retained/substantive counts are descriptive differences and cannot be pooled with confirmed reporting errors.
