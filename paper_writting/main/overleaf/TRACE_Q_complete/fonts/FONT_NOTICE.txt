TRACE Q Serif SC and TRACE Q Sans SC are renamed subsets of Noto Serif CJK SC and Noto Sans CJK SC. The original font copyright and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are supplied in Noto_CJK_copyright_and_OFL.txt. These subsets preserve the glyphs in the delivered documents, Latin U+0020–U+024F and common punctuation. Characters added later outside this coverage may require replacing the fonts with full Noto CJK fonts. Font subsetting changes storage coverage, not scientific text. DejaVu Sans Mono is redistributed under its included license. TeX Gyre and Latin Modern are distributed under the GUST Font License supplied here. All font paths are relative.
