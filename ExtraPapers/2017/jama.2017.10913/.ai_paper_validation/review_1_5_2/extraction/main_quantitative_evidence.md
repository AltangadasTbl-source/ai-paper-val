## Study definition, design, and analysis population

- p.1 abstract: randomized trial, 212 adults with sepsis (suspected infection plus at least 2 SIRS criteria) and hypotension (SBP <=90 mm Hg or MAP <=65 mm Hg); 1:1 assignment; protocol n=107 and usual care n=105. Analysis comprises 209 after 3 ineligible patients; overall mean age 36.7 (SD 12.4) years, 117 men (56.0%), HIV-positive 187/209 (89.5%).
- pp.2-3 methods: 1,500-bed hospital; enrolment Oct 22, 2012-Nov 11, 2013; data collection ended Dec 9, 2013. Enrollment within 4 h of first eligible BP and 24 h of ED registration. Random blocks 2, 4, 6. Protocol targets: initial 2 L within 1 h, further 2 L during next 4 h, maximum 4 L; stop at SpO2 decrease 3%, RR increase 5/min, or JVP >=3 cm above sternal angle. Dopamine starts 10 microgram/kg/min after initial 2 L if MAP <65 mm Hg, target >=65; transfusion if hemoglobin <7 g/dL/severe pallor.
- p.3: primary in-hospital mortality; secondary efficacy 28-day mortality/time to death; safety respiratory compromise defined as SpO2 decrease >=3% or RR increase >=5/min. Fluid windows: ED registration to 6, 24, and 72 h after enrollment; follow-up to death or 28 days. Modified ITT excludes post-randomization ineligible persons without intervention.
- p.3 statistical plan: assumed mortality 65%; n=212 gives 80% power at alpha .05 to detect 20 percentage-point absolute risk reduction (RR reduction 30.8%); one interim analysis at 50% with P<.001 Haybittle-Peto boundary; final two-sided P=.05. Tests: t, Mann-Whitney, chi-square, log-rank; primary chi-square; adjusted SAPS-3 and specified subgroup analyses; Mantel-Haenszel interaction test.
