## Scope, evidence, and checking rule

Independent pass-1 review of all 31 assigned inferential relationships: `S001` through `S031`. Evidence was restricted to the three direct package PDFs and their fresh native/layout extractions and rendered result pages. No legacy audit derivative or external source was used.

For each relationship, this pass checked the applicable point estimate, interval ordering and null containment, direction/reference group, endpoint/population identity, displayed denominator arithmetic, scale/label, repeated-result identity, and cross-document implication. A P-value/CI compatibility calculation was treated only as a labelled diagnostic unless the package identified a common test, confidence-interval construction, variance treatment, adjustment set, and sidedness. The sources identify models, adjustment variables, two-sided testing, and 95% CIs in several places, but do not identify the CI construction and exact P-value test/variance rule for the Table 2 and subgroup OR displays; this pass does not infer them.

No `P = 0`, `p = 0.000`, or equivalent display zero occurs in this assigned set. Therefore no `DISPLAY_ZERO_NOT_CANDIDATE` record is needed.

## Provisional signal for cross-lane registration

### SIG-STAT-001 — protocol-to-publication primary-analysis and sample-size divergence

- **Direct observation:** Protocol V1.1 (`joi170084supp1_prod.pdf#page=6`-`#page=7`) specifies a planned chi-square primary comparison, absolute/relative rate differences, and 161 participants per arm/322 total based on 70% and 85% rates. The published article (`jama_lapergue_2017_oi_170084.pdf#page=3`-`#page=4`) specifies a mixed logistic model with IV thrombolysis fixed and center random effects, adjusted OR/marginal risk differences, and 190 per arm/380 total based on 70%, a 15% absolute increase, and a 15% failure assumption.
- **Consistency rule:** Matched descriptions of the same trial's planned/published primary analysis and design sample size should either agree or have supplied amendment/version provenance explaining the revision.
- **Inference bounded by evidence:** The documents print different plans and sample-size premises. This pass does not infer whether the difference is a reporting defect, an approved protocol/SAP amendment, or a planned-to-final analysis revision.
- **Exact human question:** Does the supplied package contain an amendment or final statistical analysis plan that authorizes the published mixed-logistic approach and 380-person design in place of the V1.1 protocol's chi-square/322-person plan?
- **Suggested category if later registered:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency or statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Status:** Provisional quality-control signal only; no `C` ID, severity, validity, or correction assigned.
