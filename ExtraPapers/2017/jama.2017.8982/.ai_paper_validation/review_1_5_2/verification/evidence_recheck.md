- **Consistency rule applicable:** Yes. For age measured in days, `age ≥ 60` includes observations at exactly 60 days, while `age > 60` excludes them. - **Calculation or logical comparison reproduced:** Yes. No numeric recomputation is possible from the aggregate displays alone.

Missing are the operational age-filter definition, record-level ages or a count of infants exactly 60 days old, and confirmation of which filter generated the table and figure.

- **Direct observation versus inferred explanation:** Direct observations are the `≥60` and `>60` labels and the reciprocal eTable/eFigure references. The set-inclusion comparison is direct logical reasoning. A typographical shorthand, distinct data filters, and any effect of exactly-60-day records are inferred possibilities.
