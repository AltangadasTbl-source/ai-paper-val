To study the effect of receiving probiotics (rather than just being randomised to them), we will use
instrumental variables methods, (10) with randomisation as the instrument, and two measures of
exposure:
1. Initiation (Y/N) — this will provide an estimate of the effect of (at least) initiating probiotics;
2. Implementation (as a continuous variable) — using the definition in 4.2.2, with non-initiators
set to zero, this will provide an estimate of the effect on outcome per percentage point
increase in probiotics use
5. STUDY POPULATION
5.1 SCREENING DATA
A screening log of all ineligible and eligible but not consented/not approached will be kept at each
care home so that any biases from differential recruitment (with respect to age and gender) will be
detected.
5.2 ELIGIBILITY
Summary statistics on eligibility, recruitment, withdrawal and dropout will be collated for both trials
arms and will form the basis of the CONSORT flow diagram for clinical trial reporting(10).
