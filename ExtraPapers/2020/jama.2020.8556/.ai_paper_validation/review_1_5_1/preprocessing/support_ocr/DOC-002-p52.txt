analysis involving this time point.

4, STATISTICAL PRINCIPLES

4.1 LEVELS OF CONFIDENCE AND P-VALUES

All confidence intervals presented will be 95% and two-sided. All applicable statistical tests will be
two-sided and will be performed using a 5% significance level.

4.1.1 ADJUSTMENT FOR MULTIPLICITY

No adjustments will be made for multiple testing.
