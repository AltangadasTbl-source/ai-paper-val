Appendix IV: Assumptions about antibiotic administration while in hospital
The primary objective of the PRINCESS study is to evaluate the effect of a daily dose of oral
probiotics on cumulative antibiotic administration days for all-cause infections during the 12-
months post-randomisation. Any time a participant is prescribed an antibiotic for an acute
infection, the number of days that the antibiotic is administered will be recorded. CAAD will
be a summation of these days for the duration of the follow-up period. Hospitalisations are
an expected event in our study population, and antibiotic administration is likely to be high
while they are in hospital. Concern has been expressed about whether or not we will be able
to discern the number of days that participants were administered antibiotics for an acute
infection during their hospital stay. We have developed a hierarchy of assumptions that we
will use when assessing CAAD during periods of hospital stays. See Table below for list of
assumptions.
