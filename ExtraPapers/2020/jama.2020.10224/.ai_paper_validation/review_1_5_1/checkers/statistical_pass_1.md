## Scope and method

**Scope completed:** all 35 registered inferential relationships: S001-S004, S100-S113, S300-S307, and S500-S508. This pass used the canonical main/support evidence, numeric and statistical inventories, and direct supplied PDFs only. The two mapped source-label propositions were also visually confirmed against their exact PDF pages and referred to the coordinator as non-S cross-lane items; they are not candidate IDs or statistical propositions in this artifact.

For every applicable result, this pass checked point-estimate containment, endpoint order, null/sign direction, measure and scale labels, stated population/contrast/time/model, duplicate or cross-location occurrence, and linked count/denominator identities. Numerical CI/P/SE/test reconciliation was performed only when compatible definitions were supplied. Where it is reported below, a CI-derived test comparison is a **diagnostic approximation** using rounded display values; it is not a replacement for the reported analysis.

## Limitations carried forward

No source supplies the full set of unrounded coefficients, SEs, covariance matrices, test statistics, df (except the stated five-df interactions), variance estimators, or sidedness necessary to exactly reconstruct most P values. Protocol quantities are planned assumptions/definitions unless a result is independently supplied; no plan-versus-achieved difference was assumed contradictory. Several rates omit person-time denominators. These limitations restrict exact reconstruction, not the completed containment, ordering, sign/direction, label, population/contrast, duplicate/cross-location, and stated-count checks above.
