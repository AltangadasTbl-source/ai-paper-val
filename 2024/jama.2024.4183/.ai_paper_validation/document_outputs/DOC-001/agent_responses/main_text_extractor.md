# Main-article result evidence — DOC-001

## Source and scope

- **Document ID:** DOC-001
- **Source:** `jama_cinciripini_2024_oi_240036_1716416465.98349.pdf` (main article; PDF pages 1–10)
- **Derived materials read:** page-linked native text for PDF pages 1–10; rendered PDF pages 6–7 (Figures 2–3) were visually checked. No external sources used.
- **Endpoint terminology:** The prespecified **primary outcome** was self-reported 7-day point-prevalence abstinence plus expired CO <6 ppm at end of treatment (week 12): PDF p4, *Outcomes* (journal p1725). The **secondary outcome** was continuous abstinence from end of treatment, assessed at follow-up: same location. Results are Bayesian estimates (95% credible intervals [CrIs] and posterior probabilities); the main article reports no frequentist P values for these outcome contrasts.

## Strata/subgroup-style claims and internal cross-references

- The article’s effect contrasts are stratified by **initial phase-1 treatment** (CNRT vs varenicline) and **week-6 abstinence status**; it does not report conventional demographic subgroup/interaction analyses in the main article.
- **Figure 1**, PDF p3 (p1724), describes the SMART path structure: initial 2-mg varenicline vs 21-mg patch + 2-mg lozenges; week-6 abstainers continue initial medication, while nonabstainers continue, switch, or increase dose; abstinence assessed week 12.
- **Figure 2**, PDF p6 (p1727), supplies the analyzed denominators and attrition for all Figure 3 treatment paths. Its footnote states non-rerandomized nonabstainers were imputed into continuation under an intent-to-treat approach.
- **Figure 3**, PDF p7 (p1728), provides the seven endpoint numerators/denominators and corresponding 95% CrIs used in MA-P-04 through MA-P-10.
- Results narrative cross-references: phase-2 demographic detail (Supplement 2 eTable 3); NNTs (eTable 4); adverse events (eTables 5–6); adherence (eTables 7–8); continuous-abstinence contrasts (eTable 9), increase-versus-switch contrasts (eTable 10), abstainer follow-up comparison (eTable 11), and post hoc pathways (eTable 12/eAppendix 5).
