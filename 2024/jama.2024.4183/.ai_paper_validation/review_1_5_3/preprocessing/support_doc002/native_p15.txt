Subject Withdrawal
A withdrawal occurs when an enrolled subject actively withdraws consent for further participation
in the study, prior to completion of the protocol. Subjects may withdraw consent from the trial at
any time at their own request, or they may be withdrawn at any time at the discretion of the
investigator for safety reasons. All attempts will be made to collect medications, and to conduct
all evaluations required by the protocol.
