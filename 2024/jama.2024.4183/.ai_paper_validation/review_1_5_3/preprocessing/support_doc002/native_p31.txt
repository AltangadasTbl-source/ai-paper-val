Data Simulation Model

estimates and 95% credible intervals for the probability of treatment response. These
parameters correspond with the beta distributions specified for each outcome ascertainment
point. Monte Carlo simulation of the clinical trial that incorporates sampling from these
distributions permits estimates of power to detect effects taking into account uncertainty in
parameter estimates. Analyses of the data generated from the specified ~Beta distributions for
