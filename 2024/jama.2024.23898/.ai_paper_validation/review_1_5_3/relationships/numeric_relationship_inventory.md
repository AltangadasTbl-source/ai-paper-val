# Stable Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and identity

This is the package-wide, stable inventory for the complete mapped direct-source scope: DOC-001 pp. 1-10, DOC-002 pp. 1-66, DOC-003 pp. 1-40, DOC-004 pp. 1-2, and DOC-005 p. 1 (119 PDF pages). It rebuilds numeric/reporting relationships from the two current evidence maps. `N001`-`N046` retain the main-mapper relationship identities. `N047`-`N065` are the support-mapper relationships, cross-walked from its provisional `N-SUP` identifiers. An associated `S` identifier is retained in the cross-walk where the source map tracked a related inferential relationship; this inventory does not replace the dedicated statistical review.

Locations use supplied-PDF pages. Protocol and SAP PDF pages, rather than their internal printed page numbers, are the stable locations.

## Inventory count and limitations

The inventory has 65 numeric/reporting relationships (`N001`-`N065`), including 46 main/reuse-backed and 19 support/direct-source records. Support-source records preserve planned definitions and labels separately from observed results when that distinction is needed for a cross-document check. Template/dummy-table blanks, protocol recruitment projections, references, and data-sharing administrative text were retained only as explicit no-result or context records; they were not treated as observed study values. No structured data or workbook exists in the supplied scope.
