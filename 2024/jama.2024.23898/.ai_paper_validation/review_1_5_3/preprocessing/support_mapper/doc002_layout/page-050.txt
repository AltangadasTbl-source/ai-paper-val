12.4    Project timetable and milestones
The projected start date for study funding is 1 June 2017: the study duration was initially
designed to be 36 months (See figure 4) and was extended by 24 months in 2019 to 60
months and further extended in 2022 to 78 months in total.

The original milestones were: prefunding: multicentre research ethics and central R&D
approvals; month 1-5: Study set-up authorisations; months 6-25: patient recruitment;
months 7-30: patient follow up at 90 days (plus 2 months for data retrieval); months 31-36:
analysis of data, interpretation of results, report writing and dissemination. The trial will
continue to 31st October 2020.

Extension 1 revised milestones were: prefunding: multicentre research ethics and central
R&D approvals; month 1-5: Study set-up authorisations; months 6-25: patient recruitment;
months 7-54 : patient follow up at 90 days (plus 2 months for data retrieval); months 55-60:
analysis of data, interpretation of results, report writing and dissemination. The trial will continue
to 31 May 2022.

Extension 2 revised milestones are: prefunding: multicentre research ethics and central
R&D approvals; month 1-5: Study set-up authorisations; months 6-25: patient recruitment;
months 7-72: patient follow up at 90 days (plus 2 months for data retrieval); months 73-78:
analysis of data, interpretation of results, report writing and dissemination. The trial will
continue to 30 November 2023.

The Gantt chart was updated based on the approval of the recruitment extension 1 of 24
months (see figure 5) and has since been updated to show the further recruitment extension,
2, of 18 months (see figure 6),extension 3 of 1 month (see figure 7) and extension 4 of 3
months (see figure 8). The planned record linkage is not included in this time-table.
