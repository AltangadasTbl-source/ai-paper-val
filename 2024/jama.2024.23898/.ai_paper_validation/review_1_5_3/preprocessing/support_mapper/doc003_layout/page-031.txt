16.1 Derived Patient Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs)

The PROMs are p-POSSUM, OBAS, QoR-15, PONV and EQ-5D. Codes developed in-house
will be checked and validated by an independent statistician using dummy data. Table 16.1
describes how each score will be calculated.

Table 16.1 Calculation of PROMs scores


 PROMs                     Calculation
 p-POSSUM                  p-POSSUM will be calculated as follows which has been confirmed
                           by the authors of the published paper1:
                               •    A numerical score of 1, 2, 4 and 8 is assigned to each of the
                                    physiological (12 variables) and operative severity variables
                                    (6 variables).
                               •    The total score is calculated by adding up the score for each
                                    question for physiological and operative severity parts
                                    separately.
