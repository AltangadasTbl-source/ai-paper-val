# Limitations and Missing Definitions

This quantitative reporting quality-control review used only the supplied five-PDF package and current Workflow 1.5.3 artifacts. It does not include patient-level data, final endpoint-derivation records, complete amendment history, populated model output, subgroup interval-construction metadata, final ERAS categorization code, factor coding/contrast matrices, or a stated decision rule for the EQ-5D narrative summary.

The protocol and SAP are planning documents. They establish supplied definitions and planned analyses but cannot by themselves determine whether a later documented change was appropriate. These limitations prevent resolution among the source-grounded alternatives in the seven candidate cards; they do not prevent reproduction of the printed relationships and their stated comparators.

All 119 direct-source PDF pages were mapped. No scientific-coverage gap remains. DOC-004 page 2 required direct rendered-page inspection for graphical labels absent from its native text layer. No direct DOC/DOCX, workbook, or CSV source was supplied.
