# Support Quantitative Evidence Map

## Scope and method

This map covers the complete assigned direct-source scope: DOC-002, `joi240139supp1_prod_1741633738.16362.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-66; DOC-003, `joi240139supp2_prod_1741633738.17362.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-40; and DOC-005, `joi240139supp4_prod_1741633738.20861.pdf`, PDF p. 1. It covers 107 of 107 assigned PDF pages. No DOC/DOCX, XLS/XLSX, CSV, workbook formula, cached workbook value, or structured-data record was in this scope.

Fresh direct-source native and layout text were produced for every page with `pdftotext` and `pdftotext -layout` under `preprocessing/support_mapper/`. Native text was usable on every page; CPU OCR was not required. Direct rendered-page inspection was also performed for protocol pages 36-37 (assessment schedule), 49-52 (recruitment figures and Gantt charts), and 59-62 (flow diagram and score appendices), and SAP pages 17-30 (dummy-table/figure layouts). The protocol’s internal printed page numbers differ from PDF page numbers on several appendices; all locations below use the supplied PDF page number, which is the stable source location.

`Main-paper key` is a semantic match key for the main-paper mapper and cross-source checker, not an assertion that the main article has the stated value. `N` denotes a numeric/reporting relationship and `S` an inferential/statistical relationship. These provisional support IDs should be retained or cross-walked by the coordinator when the package-wide inventory is merged.

## Scope limitations and no-applicable record

These support files are a protocol, SAP, and data-sharing statement. They contain definitions, sample-size and analysis-plan quantities, templates, formulas, and planned output labels, but no populated final trial-results tables. Blank `n=`, `N=`, estimate, and P-value fields were not treated as values. No workbook/cached value/formula distinction applies because no workbook exists in the assigned scope. All 107 assigned pages were directly extracted and mapped; no scientific-coverage gap remains within this support scope.
