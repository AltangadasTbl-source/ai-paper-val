## Analysis population and participant flow

- **Analysis population definition:** all eligible patients who received treatment, analyzed in their randomized groups; per-protocol and as-treated analyses were sensitivity analyses. **Location:** PDF p.4 / print p.1062, Statistical Analysis.
- **Randomization/primary analysis:** 1409 assessed; 897 excluded; 512 randomized (256 balloon angioplasty plus aggressive medical management; 256 aggressive medical management alone). The Results narrative states that 11 were excluded due to consent withdrawal, leaving 501 for the primary analysis: 249 balloon and 252 medical-management. **Location:** PDF p.4 / print p.1062, Results—Patient Population; PDF p.5 / print p.1063, Figure 1.
- **Figure 1 exclusion detail before randomization:** no consent 326; severe stenosis outside target vessel 238; qualifying event <=2 weeks before randomization 89; angiographic stenosis <70% 73; target-artery occlusion 46; dual-antiplatelet intolerance/active bleeding diathesis 43; severe contrast allergy 35; intracranial aneurysm/other vascular malformation 24; enrolled in another study 23. **Location:** PDF p.5 / print p.1063, Figure 1.
- **Figure 1 between randomization and primary analysis:** balloon arm: 7 withdrew consent, yielding 249; medical arm: 3 withdrew consent and 1 was not successfully recruited but had been assigned a randomization number in error, yielding 252. **Location:** PDF p.5 / print p.1063, Figure 1.
- **Figure 1 per-protocol flow:** from primary analysis, balloon arm had 16 excluded after adjudication, leaving 233; medical arm had 14 excluded after adjudication, leaving 238. Balloon reasons: crossover 7, ischemic stroke onset within 2 weeks 4, tandem lesion 2, lesion length >15 mm 1, balloon-expanding stent directly without balloon angioplasty first 1, target-artery occlusion 1. Medical reasons: crossover 5, ischemic stroke onset within 2 weeks 2, tandem lesion 1, lesion length >15 mm 2, stenosis <70% 2, target-artery occlusion 1, coexisting aneurysm 1. **Location:** PDF p.5 / print p.1063, Figure 1.
- **Follow-up statement:** apart from 2 patients who died prematurely, all participants completed the 1-year follow-up. **Location:** PDF p.4 / print p.1062, Statistical Analysis.

## Outcome definitions and primary result

- **Primary endpoint definition:** composite of any stroke or death within 30 days after enrollment or after qualifying-lesion balloon angioplasty, or any ischemic stroke in the qualifying-artery territory or qualifying-artery revascularization after 30 days through 12 months. **Location:** PDF p.3 / print p.1061, Outcomes—Primary Outcome (also PDF p.1 / print p.1059, abstract).
- **Primary endpoint (primary analysis; Table 2):** balloon 11/249 (4.4%) vs medical management 34/252 (13.5%); incidence difference -9.1 percentage points (95% CI, -14.0 to -4.1); HR 0.32 (95% CI, 0.16-0.63); P<.001 (log-rank P<.001). **Location:** PDF p.5 / print p.1063, Results—Primary Outcome; PDF p.8 / print p.1066, Table 2; PDF p.9 / print p.1067, Figure 3.
- **Primary-composite component counts (reported separately; designated post hoc in Table 2):** within 30 days, any stroke or all-cause death: 8/249 (3.2%) vs 4/252 (1.6%), difference 1.6 (95% CI, -1.1 to 4.3), HR 2.05 (0.62-6.81); qualifying-artery ischemic stroke beyond 30 days to 1 year: 1/249 (0.4%) vs 19/252 (7.5%), difference -7.1 (-10.5 to -3.8), HR 0.05 (0.01-0.39); qualifying-artery revascularization beyond 30 days to 1 year: 3/249 (1.2%) vs 21/252 (8.3%), difference -7.1 (-10.8 to -3.5), HR 0.14 (0.04-0.47). **Location:** PDF p.8 / print p.1066, Table 2.
- **Composite counting note:** the individual component event numbers do not add to total composite-primary events because 1 balloon-arm patient and 10 medical-arm patients had multiple events; the time to first event was used for the composite. **Location:** PDF p.4 / print p.1062, Statistical Analysis; PDF p.8 / print p.1066, Table 2 footnote b.
- **Figure 3 landmark display:** overall HR 0.32 (95% CI, 0.16-0.63), P<.001; the figure says the curves crossed at 30 days and gives post hoc landmark HRs 2.05 (0.62-6.81) over 0-30 days and 0.10 (0.03-0.31) beyond 30 days. **Location:** PDF p.9 / print p.1067, Figure 3 and footnote a.

## Additional reported/post hoc and safety results

- **Post hoc, primary composite without revascularization:** 3.6% vs 9.1%; HR 0.39 (95% CI, 0.18-0.85); P=.01. **Location:** PDF p.6 / print p.1064, Results—Post Hoc Outcomes and Analyses.
- **Post hoc, center-adjusted primary result:** HR 0.32 (95% CI, 0.16-0.62); P=.001. The narrative reports no center-by-treatment interaction (P for interaction=.10). **Location:** PDF p.6 / print p.1064, Results—Post Hoc Outcomes and Analyses.
- **Restenosis in balloon arm:** 15.7% within 1 year; 2.0% had TIA or stroke described as clearly related to restenosis. **Location:** PDF p.5 / print p.1063, Secondary Outcomes.
- **Symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage:** 1.2% balloon vs 0.4% medical; asymptomatic intracranial hemorrhage 1.2% vs 0; disabling stroke 2.4% vs 7.1%, P=.02. **Location:** PDF p.1 / print p.1059, abstract; PDF p.6 / print p.1064, Results—Procedural Complications and Adverse Events.
- **Balloon-arm procedural complications:** abstract reports 17.4% with procedural complications and 14.5% with arterial dissection. Narrative reports vasospasm 1.2%, arterial dissection 14.5%, pseudoaneurysm 0.0%, arterial occlusion 0.4%, arterial perforation 0.4%, arterial rupture 0.0%, hemorrhage 0.4%, and thrombosis 1.7%. **Location:** PDF p.1 / print p.1059, abstract; PDF p.7 / print p.1065, Results—Procedural Complications and Adverse Events.
