2123   1. Introduction

2124   This statistical analysis plan (SAP) documents the planned statistical analyses for the BASIS

2125   study and is based on the protocol, together with any subsequent amendments. This SAP is

2126   intended for the use of project team members and should be read in conjunction with the

2127   aforementioned protocol.


2128

2129   2. Study Objective

2130   To investigate whether balloon angioplasty combined with aggressive medical management

2131  (AMM)  is superior to AMM alone for patients with high-risk intracranial atherosclerotic

2132    stenosis (sICAS) in the primary endpoint (ischemic/hemorrhage stroke or all-cause death within

2133   30 days after enrollment or after balloon angioplasty procedure of the qualifying lesion during

2134    follow-up, or any ischemic stroke or revascularization from the qualifying artery beyond 30 days

2135   through 12 months after enrollment).


2136

2137   3. Study Endpoint(s)

2138   3.1. Primary Endpoint

2139   The primary endpoint  is stroke or death within 30 days after enrollment or after balloon

2140    angioplasty procedure of the qualifying lesion during follow-up, or any ischemic stroke or

2141    revascularization of the qualifying artery beyond 30 days through 12 months after enrollment.

2142   3.2. Secondary Endpoints

2143     Any stroke (ischemic or hemorrhage stroke) inside of the territory of the target artery or

2144         all-cause deaths within 90 days after enrollment;
                                                           130        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2145     Any stroke (ischemic or hemorrhage stroke) outside of the territory of the target artery

2146         within 90 days after enrollment;


2147     mRS (modified Rankin Scale) at 90 days;


2148     Any stroke (ischemic or hemorrhage stroke) inside of the territory of the target artery or

2149         all-cause deaths within 12 months after enrollment;


2150     Revascularization of the qualifying artery within 1 year after enrollment;


2151     Any stroke (ischemic or hemorrhage stroke) outside of the territory of the target artery

2152         within 12 months after enrollment;


2153     mRS at 12 months;


2154     Restenosis rate of the target artery within 12 months (defined as rate of stenosis >70% or

2155         increased by 30% based on following-up neurovascular imaging);


2156     Combined events such as stroke, myocardial infarction, and vascular death within 12

2157       months after enrollment;


2158     Life quality assessment (EuroQol-5-Dimensions Scale [EQ-5D] questionnaire) within 12

2159       months after enrollment;

2160   3.3. Safety Assessment

2161   From enrollment to 1 year, all patients were evaluated for safety.


2162      Vital signs: blood pressure, heart rate, respiration rate, body temperature.


2163     Blood laboratory test includes blood routine (red blood cells, white blood cells, platelets,

2164        hemoglobin),   biochemistry   examinations   (alanine   aminotransferase,   aspartate

2165         aminotransferase, alkaline phosphatase, glutamylphthalase, total bilirubin, urea nitrogen,

                                                           131        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2166        serum creatinine), coagulation function, hepatic and renal function tests, fasting blood

2167        glucose and HbA1c.


2168     Adverse events and serious adverse events.


2169

2170   4. Study Hypothesis

2171   Compared to AMM alone, balloon angioplasty combined with AMM could decrease 50% risk of

2172    the primary endpoint in patients with high-risk sICAS during over 12-month follow-up period.


2173

2174   5. Design

2175   5.1 Study Design

2176    After the study is formally started, the participating study centers copied the video (including

2177    the DSA and surgical process) of 3-5 cases of balloon angioplasty in their centers and transfer

2178    the video to the core laboratory (Beijing Tiantan Hospital). After the consistency is passed, the

2179   group continued to be enrollled, so as to ensure the consistency of the process of balloon

2180    angioplasty.

2181     BASIS trial is an investigator-initiated, multicenter, prospective, randomized, open-label,

2182    blinded end-point (PROBE) trial that plans to enrolll the patient with sICAS (defined as a recent

2183   TIA (<90 days) or ischemic stroke (21-90 days) attributed to a 70-99% atherosclerotic stenosis

2184    of a major intracranial artery (anterior/posterior circulation). The patients are randomly assigned

2185    to either the experimental group or the control group at 1:1 ratio.





                                                           132        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2186   Experimental group: patients will receive balloon angioplasty (details in procedure part) plus

2187  AMM (same as the control group);

2188   Control group: patients will receive the AMM alone.


2189     Drug administration plan of AMM: aspirin 100 mg daily for the entire follow-up and

2190         clopidogrel 75mg daily for the first 90 days after enrollment. Whether to continue taking

2191         clopidogrel after 90 days per the condition of the patient，clopidogrel can be replaced with

2192          ticagrelor or cilostazol with appropriate dosage for patients with clopidogrel resistance;

2193         atorvastatin calcium tablets were used 20mg-80mg/day for one year after enrollment; the

2194        dose was adjusted to control LDL <1.8mmol/L or <70mg/dl. Whether to continue taking

2195         atorvastatin calcium tablets after one year per the patient's condition.


2196     Risk  factors management—Control blood pressure 130-140/90-100 mmHg and LDL

2197       <1.8mmol/L or <70mg/dl

2198   The first 100 participants completed the peri-operative (30 days) observation, mainly collecting

2199    the adverse events during the peri-operative period. PI needs to organize other investigators

2200   from the participating centers for interim discussion to evaluate the feasibility of this treatment

2201   plan and the continuation of later research, and follow-up research can be conducted later.

2202   5.2 Planned Analyses

2203   The analyses that are detailed in this SAP will be performed only when the database has been

2204    locked,  all  protocol  violators  identified, and  treatment  allocations have been  unblinded.

2205   Membership of the Full Analysis and Per Protocol populations will be determined using the

2206    rules set out in this SAP. At a date to be agreed within the project team, a data look will be



                                                           133        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2207   performed. This will involve production of all data displays on a subset of the data using dummy

2208    treatment codes. These are produced purely as an aide to the pre-programming of the study and

2209   no unblinding will occur.

2210   5.3 Interim Analyses

2211    Interim analysis is performed when half of patients finished 12-months visit. The mid-term

2212    analysis α1 is estimated to be 0.0015, and the final analysis α2 is 0.024.

2213   6. Sample Size Estimates


2214   The estimation of  the sample  size  is  calculated according  to  the 1-year  results of  the

2215  SAMMPRIS  trial, the incidence of target vessel stroke or death rate was 12.2% within 12

2216   months after enrollment in the experimental group. The sample size was 686 cases, with a 80%

2217   power, two-sided α = 0.05, to detect a 50 % relative reduction (6.1%) after balloon angioplasty

2218    for intracranial atherosclerosis. It is assumed that the O'Brien-Fleming spending function will

2219    lead to 6% expansion of the total type I error. The SAS software is used to calculate the sample

2220    size required for the mid-term analysis, which increases the sample size to the required 728

2221    cases. Considering the 10% lost to follow-up rate, the final sample size of this study is 802

2222    cases.

2223   7. Analysis Populations

2224   7.1 Intention-to-treat Set (ITT)


2225    All patients who have been randomized to groups and receive the study intervention will be

2226    included. And the analyses will be performed based on the intervention as per the patient

2227   randomized result, according to the basic principle of intention-to-treat (ITT).


                                                           134        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2228   This population will be the primary population for analyses of efficacy.

2229   7.2. Per Protocol Set (PPS)


2230   Per Protocol Set (PPS) is a subset of FAS (mITT). All patients with finishing the treatment or

2231   without violating the trail program seriously are included in PPS. The exact definition of a

2232    serious violation will be finalized at the time of data review and may generally include (but is not

2233    limited to) the following criteria: complied with the experimental protocol, had good compliance,

2234    did not use prohibited drugs during the experiment, and completed the contents specified in the

2235    case report form. A partial protocol violator will be included in the Per Protocol Population up to

2236    the time of their violation. Serious violations will be determined during data review. PPS is a

2237   secondary data set for the end point evaluation in this study.

2238   7.3. As-treated Set (ATS)


2239    All patients who have been randomized to groups with more than one use of study products will

2240   be included. And the analyses will be performed based on the intervention as per the patient

2241    received. ATS is a secondary data set for the end point evaluation in this study.

2242   7.4. Safety Set (SS)


2243    All patients who received at least 1-time of study products and safety assessment available will

2244   be included in the safety population. This population will be used for safety analyses.

2254   9. General Considerations for Data Analyses

2255    All programming will be performed using SAS Version 9.4. All analysis output will use the

2256   following treatment group naming conventions and treatment order:


2257   Experimental group: patients will receive balloon angioplasty (details in procedure part) plus

2258  AMM (same as the control group);

2259   Control group: patients will receive the AMM alone.


2260    All statistics were two sided with a P<0.05 considered significant.

2261   9.1 Multicenter Studies

2262   The study will include a large number of centers to participate in this trial, some centers will

2263   have limited subjects per center in this trial, in main analyses won’t consider center-effects

2264    adjustment. The analyses for consider center-effects adjustment will serve as sensitivity analyses.

2265   Centers with small sample size of subjects will be pooled with larger centers so that centers are

2266    of a reasonable size for the purpose of the statistical analyses. Cox proportional hazards model

2267    will be adopted to calculate HR for the treatment effect considering center-effects adjustment.

2268   9.2 Examination of Subgroups

2269   The rate of primary outcome will be presented for each level of the covariates listed below. The

2270    extent to which the treatment effect varies across levels of each subgroup will be assessed

2271   through interaction tests.



                                                           136        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2272   9.3 Multiple Comparisons and Multiplicity

2273  A single primary efficacy variable has been defined for this study, with all other efficacy

2274    variables identified as secondary or other. Similarly, only one treatment comparison  is of

2275    interest in the study and therefore there are no requirements to adjust for multiple comparisons

2276    or multiple endpoints within this study.

2278   10. Data Handling Conventions

2279   10.1 Premature Withdrawal and Missing Data

2280     If any subject withdraws prematurely from the study (prior to the final visit), they are required to

2281   complete the withdrawal visit in the CRF. The reasons for withdrawal will be presented in a

2282   summary table. For the purposes of summaries and analysis of clinic visit data, this visit will be

2283    assigned to the next scheduled clinic visit for that subject, regardless of whether the date falls

2284    within the next visit window.

2285    Subjects who withdraw before  the end of  the  study, but who do provide  at  least one

2286    post-baseline measure for a particular endpoint, will be included in the analysis.

2287    Subjects who do not attend any visits after randomization will be excluded from analysis of any

2288    endpoint, as no post-baseline data will be available.

2289   10.2 Event Rates

2290   The number of people and person-time of events should be recorded in detail and showing the

2291   event rate of each treatment group in summary statement.

2292   The event rate for each treatment group will be calculated as: the sum of number of event for all

2293    the patients / the sum of number of treatment periods for all the patients.

2294   10.3 Time to Event Analysis

2295    Differences between treatments in the risk of any stroke (ischemic or haemorrhagic) event or


                                                           137        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2296   combined  vascular  events  during follow-up were  assessed  using  standard Kaplan-Meier

2297    time-to-event approaches. The time to the first event was used in the model when there were

2298    multiple events of the same type. Patients were considered censored at the time of study

2299    termination or death if there were no events occurred during the study.

2301   11. Study Population

2302   11.1 Disposition of Subjects

2303   The number of subjects in each analysis population will be presented, subjects to be excluded

2304   from the Per Protocol population will be listed, and the total number of subjects attending each

2305    clinic visit will also be summarized by treatment group.

2306   The number of subjects randomized, completed and prematurely withdrawn from the study will

2307   be presented for each treatment group. The primary reasons for withdrawal both prior to and

2308    post randomization will also be presented.

2309  A data display listing and summary of deviations from the inclusion/exclusion criteria will be

2310    presented for all subjects who were either entered or randomized into the trial.

2311   11.2 Protocol Deviations

2312    Subject data will be examined for evidence of protocol violators in order to assess how well the

2313    protocol was followed. Inclusion and exclusion criteria are detailed in the study protocol.

2314    Subjects who commit protocol violations will be included in the FAS (mITT) Population but

2315   excluded from the Per Protocol Population. These protocol violations will be shown in a listing.

2316    Subjects can either be full or partial protocol violators. A full protocol violator is completely

2317   excluded from the Per Protocol Population. A partial protocol violator has only some data

2318    excluded. For subjects who violated the protocol during the treatment period due to unpermitted

2319   changes in the medication or prohibited concurrent medication, the analysis will only use data

2320   recorded prior to the violation. For all violations which reference the treatment period, the

2321    treatment start date will be used as the reference date.

                                                           138        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2322  A listing of all possible protocol violators will be produced for clinical review. The final list of

2323    subjects who are protocol violators and are therefore excluded from the Per-Protocol population

2324    will be agreed by the study team.

2326   12. Statistical Analysis

2327   12.1 Demographic and Baseline Characteristics


2328   The following demographic information will be listed and summarized for subjects in each

2329    treatment group: age, sex, height, weight, vital signs. This will also be done for medical history,

2330   medication history, stroke and TIA history.


2331      The continuous data will be presented as median and interquartile range; categorical data

2332    will be presented as n(%). Wilcoxon rank sum test will be used for comparison between two

2333   continuous data, and Chi-squared tests or Fisher exact test will be used for comparison between

2334   two categorical data.

2335   12.2 Primary Efficacy Endpoint

2336   The composite event rates and corresponding 95% CIs of the primary outcome in the two

2337    treatment groups will be estimated by Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and compared by log-rank

2338     test. Hazard ratio between the two groups and  its 95% CI will be calculated using a Cox

2339    proportional hazards regression model.

2340      The main analysis of this study will be conducted based on intention-to-treat principle, and

2341   a per protocol analysis and as-treated analysis will also be conducted as sensitivity analysis. ITT,

2342   PPS and ATS will be used to analyze for the primary efficacy outcome, respectively. If the

2343    results in the PPS and ATS population are inconsistent with the ITT population, detailed analysis
                                                           139        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2344    of the inconsistent results is required.

2345   Subgroup Analysis

2346   Summary tables will be produced for the predefined subgroups and interactions between

2347    treatment and these subgroups will be investigated, using a Cox proportional hazards model. A

2348    separate model will be used for each interaction to determine its significance. This will also be

2349    presented graphically on a forest plot. Treatment effect modification on the primary endpoint

2350    will be explored in the following subgroups:

2351      Age, <65 years old vs. ≥ 65 years old

2352      Sex, men vs. women

2353      Hypertension, yes vs. no

2354      Diabetes, yes vs. no

2355      Smoking, yes vs. no

2356      Baseline renal function, eGFR <60 ml/min/1.73m2、eGFR≥60 ml/min/1.73m2

2357      Target vessel stenosis degree, <80%、 ≥80%

2358      BMI, <25 Kg/m2、25-30 Kg/m2、 ≥30 Kg/m2

2359      Hypoperfusion, CTP, ASITN/SIR collateral circulation scale

2360      Anterior circulation vs. posterior circulation

2361      The mechanism, ischemic stroke vs. TIA


2362

2363   12.3 Secondary Efficacy Endpoint

2364     Any stroke (ischemic or hemorrhage stroke) inside of the territory of the target artery or

2365           all-cause deaths (within 90 days and 12 months after enrollment) after enrollment will be

                                                           140        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2366          analyzed using a Cox proportional hazard model. The hazard ratios with 95% CIs will be

2367           reported;

2368     Any stroke (ischemic or hemorrhage stroke) outside of the territory of the target artery

2369           (within 90 days and 12 months  after enrollment)  will be analyzed using a Cox

2370           proportional hazard model. The hazard ratios with 95% CIs will be reported;

2371     Common odds ratios of mRS (at 90 days and 12 months) will be estimated using ordinal

2372            logistic regression.

2373     Logistic regression analysis will be performed to compare the restenosis rate of the target

2374           artery within 12 months (defined as rate of stenosis >70% or increased by 30% based on

2375          following-up neurovascular imaging) between the two treatment groups and the odds

2376            ratio with 95% CI will be reported.

2377     Combined events such as stroke, myocardial infarction, and vascular death within 12

2378         months after enrollment will be analyzed using a Cox proportional hazard model. The

2379          hazard ratios with 95% CIs will be reported.

2380     Life quality assessment (EuroQol-5-Dimensions Scale [EQ-5D] questionnaire) within 12

2381         months after enrollment will be tested using student t-test or Wilcoxon rank sum test as

2382           appropriate.

2383   12.4 Safety Analyses

2384   Adverse Events


2385   Adverse events (AEs) will be coded using the MedDRA coding dictionary (Version 26 or a later

2386    release) and grouped by system organ class. Separate data display listings and summaries will be



                                                           141        BASIS                                                                        Number:2017YFC1307900


2387    presented for adverse events. Within each treatment group, the number and percentage of

2388    subjects experiencing an AE will be summarized by system organ class and preferred term and

2389    Fisher’s Exact test will be used to compare the number of each grouped AE event between

2390    treatment groups.


2391   Deaths and Serious Adverse Events


2392   Summary tables and data displays will be provided for serious adverse events (as detailed in the

2393   study protocol). In addition, all deaths and serious AE's will be documented in a case narrative

2394   format in the clinical study report.

2395   The number of deaths occurring over the treatment period will be summarized and Fisher’s

2396   Exact test will be used to compare the number of deaths between treatment groups.


2397    Vital Signs

2398   The results of vital signs at different time points will be statistically described and compared.

2399   12.5 Concomitant Medication

2400   Describe the list of concomitant medication including antiplatelet drug (sspirin, clopidogrel,

2401    ticagrelor, cilostazol), anticoagulation drug, lipid-lowering drug (statin), antihypertensive drugs

2402    (diuretic, angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor, angiotensin receptor blocker, beta-blocker,

2403   calcium channel antagonist, central α agonist, vasodilator) and so on. The Chi-squared tests or

2404    Fisher exact test will be used to compare the number between treatment groups.

2564   5.1 Study Design

2565    After the study is formally started, the participating study centers copied the video

2566    (including the DSA and surgical process) of 3-5 cases of balloon angioplasty in their

2567    centers and transfer the video to the core laboratory (Beijing Tiantan Hospital). After the

2568    consistency  is passed,  the group continued  to be  enrollled, so as  to ensure  the

2569    consistency of the process of balloon angioplasty.

2570     BASIS  trial  is  an  investigator-initiated,  multicenter,  prospective,  randomized,

2571    open-label, blinded end-point (PROBE) trial that plans to enrolll the patient with sICAS

2572    (defined as a recent TIA or ischemic stroke attributed to a 70-99% atherosclerotic

2573    stenosis of a major intracranial artery including terminated internal carotid artery [ICA]

2574   C4-C7 segment, middle cerebral artery [MCA] M1 segment, vertebral artery [VA] V4

2575   segment and  basilar artery [BA]) determined by DSA. The patients are randomly

2576    assigned to either the experimental group or the control group at 1:1 ratio.

2578    plus AMM (same as the control group);

2579   Control group: patients will receive the AMM alone.

2614   6. Sample Size Estimates

2615   According to VISSIT trial and a previous randomized trial in China, the composite event

2616    rate of the primary outcome in the medical group is anticipated to be 15%. As to the

2617    balloon angioplasty group, we assume a 7% of the primary outcome based on studies of

2618    angioplasty without stenting and investigators’ clinical practice experience in China. As a

2736   12.1 Demographic and Baseline Characteristics


2737   The following demographic information will be listed and summarized for subjects in

2738   each treatment group: age, sex, height, weight, vital signs. This will also be done for

2739   medical history, medication history, stroke and TIA history.


2740      The continuous data will be presented as median and interquartile range; categorical

2741    data will be presented as n(%).Wilcoxon rank sum test will be used for comparison

2742   between two continuous data, and Chi-squared tests or Fisher exact test will be used for

2743   comparison between two categorical data.

2745   The composite event rates and corresponding 95% CIs of the primary outcome in the two

2746    treatment groups will be estimated by Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and compared by

2747    log-rank test. Hazard ratio between the two groups and its 95% CI will be calculated

2748   using a Cox proportional hazards regression model.

2749      The main analysis of  this study will be conducted based on  intention-to-treat

2750    principle, and a per protocol analysis will also be conducted as sensitivity analysis. ITT,

2751   PPS and ATS will be used to analyze for the primary efficacy outcome, respectively. If

2752    the results in the PPS and ATS population are inconsistent with the ITT population,



                                                       159        BASIS                                                                     Number:2017YFC1307900


2753    detailed analysis of the inconsistent results is required.
