17. Assessment of endpoint and adverse events (AEs)

638

17.1 Endpoint event assessment

639

The endpoint events include target vessel haemorrhagic stroke, ischemic stroke, death, and target

640

vessel revascularization. If a patient has a potential primary endpoint event, the researcher must

641

report to a third-party monitoring company project team within 48 hours, including the patient's

642

complete medical records. The project team will submit the patient's data to the event arbitration

643

committee, and the event arbitration committee will complete the arbitration within 7 days. The

644

endpoint event arbitration committee is composed of three members. First, two members use

645

back-to-back arbitration. If the evaluation opinions are inconsistent, a third evaluation expert will

646

cast a decisive vote.

647

17.2 AEs assessment

648

An AE is the presence of any unexpected medical condition during or after being treated with

649

medical devices. It includes symptoms, signs, or abnormal laboratory parameters that can be

650

unrelated to treatment. An AE that fulfills at least one of the following criteria is classified as a

651

severe AE(SAE): AE that results in death; is life-threatening; requires/prolongs inpatient

652

hospitalization; results in serious disability; or requires medical intervention to prevent one of the

653

above-mentioned outcomes. If a potential endpoint occurs, the committee board meeting will be

654

convened to evaluate whether such an event can be categorized as the primary endpoint.
