671

AEs that may occur during the procedures include, but are not limited to:

672

Thromboembolic events

673

The possible causes include no anticoagulation or insufficient anticoagulation, insufficient

674

antiplatelet aggregation treatment before and after the procedure or aspirin resistance.

675

Anticoagulation and antiplatelet aggregation should be reasonably standardized. If embolization

676

occurs in the important vessels, it should be handled by a professional doctor and assessed

677

according to the patient's condition.

678

Cerebral vasospasm

679

Cerebral vasospasm can be caused by subarachnoid hemorrhage (SAH), or by instrumentation with

680

endovascular catheters and guidewires. If vasospasm occurs, the professional doctor will give

681

symptomatic treatment per the patient's condition.

682

Acute vessel occlusion

683

Possible causes include intimal injury, platelet aggregation, thrombosis, etc., usually occurring

684

within 30 minutes after balloon angioplasty or may be related to intimal avulsion. Preventive

685

measures: including aspirin, ticlid or clopidogrel antiplatelet drugs can effectively reduce acute

686

thrombotic

687

anticoagulation, acute vessel occlusion events can be avoided.

688

Treatment measures: Once acute vessel occlusion occurs, reasonable treatment should be performed

689

immediately. Thrombolysis can be performed in patients with acute thrombosis.

690

Thrombosis

691

Thrombosis occurs mainly due to plaque fragmentation caused by squeezing the plaque during

692

balloon dilatation. Sometimes, thrombosis is caused by the catheter and guide wire. The use of brain

693

protection devices and intraprocedure anticoagulation are the basic measures to prevent this event.

694

Vessel rupture

events.

695

A vessel rupture complication can directly lead to death. When balloon angioplasty is performed,

696

the selected balloon is preferably slightly smaller than the diameter of the target vessel. Once vessel

697

rupture occurs, symptomatic treatment should be performed immediately by a professional

698

physician.

699

Cerebral hyperperfusion syndrome

700

For severe stenotic lesions, the autoregulation ability of distal vessels decreases due to long-term

701

ischemia. The sudden expansion of the lesion site diameter may significantly increase the blood

702

flow. The distal vessels, especially the cortical arteries, cannot immediately adapt to this sudden

703

increase in blood flow and passively expand, resulting in cerebral hyperperfusion syndrome. The

704

clinical presentation includes headache, nausea, vomiting, seizure, and disturbance of consciousness.

705

Some patients can suffer intracranial hemorrhage. The possibility of this occurrence should be fully

706

707

after the procedure to prevent this event. If postoperative cerebral hyperperfusion syndrome occurs,

708

antihypertensive medications, analgesics, antiepileptic drugs), agents to reduce intracranial pressure,

surgical treatment of cerebral hemorrhage, etc.

20. Statistical Analysis

728

The composite event rates and corresponding 95% CIs of the primary outcome in the two treatment

729

groups will be estimated by Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and compared by log-rank test. A

730

hazard ratio between the two groups and its 95% CI will be calculated using a Cox proportional

731

hazards regression model. Time-to-event endpoints of the secondary outcomes will also be analyzed

732

using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and Cox regression, and common odds ratios of mRS will be

733

estimated using ordinal logistic regression. The widths of the intervals will not be adjusted for

734

multiplicity for secondary outcomes. The main analysis of this study will be conducted based on the

735

intention-to-treat principle, and per protocol analysis and as treated analysis will also be conducted

736

as sensitivity analyses. All analyses will be performed using SAS software, Version 9.4 (SAS

737

Institute). Detailed statistical methods, missing data imputation and subgroup analyses are described

738

in the statistical analysis plan.
