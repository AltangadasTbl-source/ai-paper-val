1492   13.2.3 Research drugs

1493   During the screening period of BASIS, clopidogrel will be provided free of charge for 3-5 days.

1494   After 90 days of enrollment, clopidogrel will also be provided free of charge. From enrollment

1495   to 1 year, atorvastatin required by patients will be provided free of charge, and other drugs will

1496   be purchased by the patients.

1497   At each visit point, the patient's compliance with treatment will be evaluated by counting the

1498   number of clopidogrel and atorvastatin.

1499   Patients with LDL > 1.8 mmol / L or > 70 mg / dL will be treated with atorvastatin. Whether to

1500   continue taking atorvastatin after 1 year will be assessed accordin to the patient's condition. If

1501   patients are taking other lipid-lowering drugs, patients are encouraged to switch to atorvastatin

1502   (not mandatory). For patients whose blood pressure does not reach the treatment goal,

1503   antihypertensive drugs will be given. Patients can choose to use but are not limited to the

1504   following antihypertensive drugs:

1505      Diuretic (Chlorthione 25mg)

1506      Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACEI) (Lisinopril 10mg or 40 mg)

1507      Angiotensin Receptor Blocker (ARB) (Candesartan 16mg or 32 mg),

1508      Beta-blocker (Atenolol 50mg or 100mg)
