1452   13.2 Risk factor management

1453   Neurologists and study investigators at each site will be responsible for the management of

1454   patient’s risk factors. They will receive training on risk factor management according to the

1455   Chinese Stroke Association guidelines for the clinical management of cerebrovascular

1456   disorders.15 All study investigators are regularly trained according to the BASIS protocol every

1457   6 months. If a patient fails to reach the target, a face-to-face follow-up with the local neurologist

1458   to modify their medical regime will be necessary.

1459   13.2.1 Primary risk factors

1460   Achieving target LDL cholesterol

1461   The baseline serum low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-c) level is recorded before

1462   enrollment. The target LDL-c level is < 1.8mmol/L or 70 mg/dl. If the patient’s LDL level is

1463   higher than the target level, he/she should modify the lipid-lowering drugs. Liver enzyme (AST /

1464   ALT) levels will be measured at the beginning of the study and at each visit point.
