1187   5. Objectives and Hypothesis
1188   5.1 Objectives

1189   To investigate whether balloon angioplasty combined with AMM is superior to AMM alone for

1190   patients with high-risk sICAS in the primary endpoint (ischemic/haemorrhagic stroke or

1191   all-cause death within 30 days after enrollment or after balloon angioplasty procedure of the

1192   qualifying lesion during follow-up, or any ischemic stroke or revascularization from the

1193   qualifying artery beyond 30 days through 12 months after enrollment).

1194   5.2 Hypothesis

1195   Compared to AMM alone, balloon angioplasty combined with AMM could decrease the risk of

1196   the primary endpoint by 50% in patients with high-risk sICAS over the 12-month follow-up

1197   period.

1199   6. Study Design
1200   Study introduction period

1201   After the study is formally started, the participating study centers copied the video (including the
1202   DSA and surgical process) of 3-5 cases of balloon angioplasty in their centers and transfer the
1203   video to the core laboratory (Beijing Tiantan Hospital). After the consistency is passed, the
1204   group continued to enrolll, so as to ensure the consistency of the balloon angioplasty procedure.
1205   The annual volume of BA by the neurointerventionalists in the BASIS trial was more than 50
1206   cases. The neurointerventionalists from the participating centers received standard training in
1207   technique from the leading center at regular intervals.
