1361   9. Informed Consent
1362   The clinical trial must follow the Helsinki Declaration and the relevant clinical trial norms and

1363   regulations in China. Informed consent was designed based on NMPA regulations and ICH

1364   guidelines. Each patient who agreed to participate the study will be invited to sign an informed

1365   consent form. Before signing the informed consent form, a specially trained researcher

1366   (understanding the key points of the study and the related risks of the study, the confidentiality

1367   of the management records and the source of funds, etc.) will introduce the research purpose,

1368   study plan, study cycle, potential risks and expected benefits, cost and compensation, voluntary

1369   participation principle, treatment principle of study related risks, etc.

1371   10. Randomization
1372   The BASIS trial used an interactive web response system (IWRS) for central randomization

1373   stratified by study centers. Patients who sign the informed consent and meet all inclusion criteria

1374   and do not meet any exclusion criteria will be enrollled in the study. After entering the eligible

1375   patient’s necessary information into the web-based system, the researcher in each center will

1376   obtain a random code as well as the corresponding group allocation information from the IWRS.

1377   Eligible patients will be randomized into either the experimental group or the control group at a

1378   1:1 ratio (Figure 1). Finally, researchers and research assistants need to print randomization

1379   application forms and center randomization lists.
