1473   13.2.2 Secondary risk factors

1474   Non-HDL cholesterol

1475   The target level of non-HDL cholesterol is < 100 mg/dL. Non-HDL cholesterol includes

1476   low-density      lipoproteins    (LDL),     very-low-density       lipoproteins       (VLDL),      and

1477   intermediate-density lipoproteins (IDL). When a larger statin dosage fails to lower the LDL

1478   level, or if the non-HDL cholesterol ≥ 100 mg/dL and triglycerides ≥ 200mg/dL, then other

1479   lipid-lowering drugs in addition to statin are recommended, such as ezetimibe or PCSK9

1480   inhibitors.

1481   Diabetes management

1482   The target of DM management is to achieve HbA1c < 7.0%. Fasting plasma glucose (FPG) and

1483   HbA1c levels will be measured at baseline, 30 days, 90 days, and 1 year.

1484   Lifestyle modification

1485   Smoking cessation: investigators at each site will evaluate the patient’s smoking status at each

1486   follow-up and encourage all subjects to quit smoking as soon as possible.
