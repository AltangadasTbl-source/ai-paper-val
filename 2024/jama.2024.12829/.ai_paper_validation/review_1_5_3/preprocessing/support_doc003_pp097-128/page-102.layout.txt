1742   19. Quality control
1743   To ensure that the collected data is concordant with the protocol, each sub-center of the BASIS

1744   trial will be regularly scrutinized, and by comparing data in the Electronic Data Capture System

1745   (EDC) and data in the original documents (case report form [CRF] vs. source documents) to

1746   confirm data consistency. If a patient develops an AE/severe AE or completed a 1-year

1747   observation after receiving treatment from non-study sites, duplicate copies of the medical

1748   documents will be collected for future reviews.

1750   20. Statistical Analysis
1751   The composite event rates and corresponding 95% CIs of the primary outcome in the two

1752   treatment groups will be estimated by Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and compared by log-rank

1753   test. Hazard ratio between the two groups and its 95% CI will be calculated using a Cox

1754   proportional hazards regression model. Time-to-event endpoints of the secondary outcomes will

1755   also be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and Cox regression, and common odds

1756   ratios of mRS will be estimated using ordinal logistic regression. The widths of the intervals will

1757   not be adjusted for multiplicity for secondary outcomes. The main analysis of this study will be

1758   conducted based on the intention-to-treat principle, and per protocol analysis and as treated

1759   analysis will also be conducted as sensitivity analyses. All analyses will be performed using

1760   SAS software, Version 9.4 (SAS Institute). Detailed statistical methods, missing data imputation

1761   and subgroup analyses are described in the statistical analysis plan.
