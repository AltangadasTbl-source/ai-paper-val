1681    17.3 SAE record and report

1682    The collection time of adverse events and serious adverse events in the study begins with

1683    signing the informed consent, across the study period, until the end of the study. SAEs of

1684    patients who discontinued treatment in advance should also be recorded.

1685    When any SAE occurs during the study, the researcher should immediately treat appropriately

1686    for the patient, and report in writing within 24 hours to the clinical trial institution, ethics

1687    committee, and sponsor (Beijing Tiantan Hospital, Capital Medical University). For death

1688    events, clinical trial institutions and researchers should provide the ethics committee and

1689    sponsors (Beijing Tiantan Hospital, Capital Medical University) with all the required

1690    information.

1691    17.4 Prediction of AEs and countermeasures

1692    AEs that may occur during the procedures include, but are not limited to:

1693    Thromboembolic events

1694    The possible causes include no anticoagulation or insufficient anticoagulation, insufficient

1695    antiplatelet aggregation treatment before and after the procedure or aspirin resistance.

1696    Anticoagulation and antiplatelet aggregation should be standardized. If embolization occurs in

1697    the important vessels, it should be handled by a professional doctor per the patient's condition.
