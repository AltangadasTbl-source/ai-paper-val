2657   9. General Considerations for Data Analyses

2658   All programming will be performed using SAS Version 9.4. All analysis output will use

2659   the following treatment group naming conventions and treatment order:

2660   Experimental group: patients will receive balloon angioplasty (details in procedure part)

2661   plus AMM (same as the control group);

2662   Control group: patients will receive the AMM alone.

2663   All statistics were two sided with a P<0.05 considered significant.
