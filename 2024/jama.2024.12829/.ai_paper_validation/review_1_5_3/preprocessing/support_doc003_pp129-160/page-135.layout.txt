2229   7.2. Per Protocol Set (PPS)

2230   Per Protocol Set (PPS) is a subset of FAS (mITT). All patients with finishing the treatment or

2231   without violating the trail program seriously are included in PPS. The exact definition of a

2232   serious violation will be finalized at the time of data review and may generally include (but is not

2233   limited to) the following criteria: complied with the experimental protocol, had good compliance,

2234   did not use prohibited drugs during the experiment, and completed the contents specified in the

2235   case report form. A partial protocol violator will be included in the Per Protocol Population up to

2236   the time of their violation. Serious violations will be determined during data review. PPS is a

2237   secondary data set for the end point evaluation in this study.
