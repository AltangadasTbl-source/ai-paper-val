215

Study introduction period

216

After the study is formally initiated at a site, the participating study center will copy the video

217

(including the DSA and endovascular process) of 3 to 5 cases of balloon angioplasty in their center

218

and transfer the video to the core laboratory (Beijing Tiantan Hospital). After the review is passed,

219

the group can continue enrollment, so as to ensure the consistency of the process of balloon

220

angioplasty. The annual volume of BA by the neurointerventionalists in the BASIS trial was more

221

than 50 cases. The neurointerventionalists from the participating centers received standard training

222

in technique from the leading center at regular intervals.

223

The BASIS trial is an investigator-initiated, multicenter, prospective, randomized, open-label,

224

blinded end-point (PROBE) trial that plans to enrolll patients with sICAS (defined as a recent TIA

225

(<90 days) or ischemic stroke (21-90 days) attributed to a 70-99% atherosclerotic stenosis of a

226

major intracranial artery (anterior/posterior circulation). The patients are randomly assigned to

227

either the experimental group or the control group at a 1:1 ratio.
