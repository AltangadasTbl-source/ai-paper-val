13.2.1 Primary risk factors

444

Achieving target LDL cholesterol

445

The baseline serum low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-c) level is recorded before enrollment.

446

The target LDL-c level is < 1.8mmol/L or 70 mg/dl. If the patient’s LDL level is higher than the

447

target level, he/she should modify the lipid-lowering drugs. Liver enzyme (AST / ALT) levels will

448

be measured at the beginning of the study and at each visit point.

449

Blood pressure management

450

Blood pressure will be checked at screening, randomization, discharge, 30 days, 90 days, 180 days,

451

1 year, and at the end of the trial. Patients may need to visit local sites for blood pressure

452

measurement and modify their medical regime if necessary. The target blood pressure is no more

453

than 140mmHg/90mmHg for patients without DM and no more than 130mmHg/80mmHg for

454

patients with DM. Antihypertensive drugs include Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACEI),

455

Angiotensin Receptor Blocker (ARB), beta-blocker, calcium channel antagonist (CCB), diuretic,

456

vasodilator, etc.

457

13.2.2 Secondary risk factors

458

Non-HDL cholesterol

459

The target level of non-HDL cholesterol is < 100 mg/dL. Non-HDL cholesterol includes

460

low-density lipoproteins (LDL), very-low-density lipoproteins (VLDL), and intermediate-density

461

lipoproteins (IDL). When a larger statin dosage fails to lower the LDL level, if non-HDL

462

cholesterol ≥ 100 mg/dL or triglycerides are ≥ 200mg/dL, other lipid-lowering drugs in addition to

463

statin are recommended, such as ezetimibe or PCSK9 inhibitors.
