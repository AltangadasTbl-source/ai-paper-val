# Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This stable numeric inventory covers the complete mapped direct-source scope: DOC-001, PDF pp. 1-11; DOC-002, PDF pp. 1-25; and DOC-003, PDF pp. 1-167. It was rebuilt from the canonical main and support quantitative evidence maps and the complete source-coverage ledger, then checked against the cited direct PDFs where a candidate proposal is recorded. `N` means a numeric, count, denominator, population, time, unit, label, scale, or rate/count relationship. Inferential compatibility is deliberately left to the statistical inventory and statistical passes. Repeated source occurrences are retained as provenance rather than assigned a second relationship when they report the same relationship.

## Limitations

The evidence maps do not provide row-level data, person-time totals, or every source figure's machine-readable values. A count-total was not treated as a contradiction where the source explicitly permits multiple events/person, uses a stated evaluated subset, or reports a versioned planning assumption. Statistical estimate/CI/P-value compatibility belongs to the two separate statistical passes.
