# Limitations and Missing Definitions

This quantitative reporting quality-control review used only the three supplied PDFs. Direct-source coverage is complete (203 of 203 PDF pages mapped), but the supplied material does not provide all production or analysis definitions needed to resolve every candidate.

- The PDFs do not provide participant-level data, event listings, production tables, or correction history.
- Several percentage checks use ordinary nearest-one-decimal rounding because no alternative display rule is supplied; a different disclosed rounding, truncation, weighting, or row-specific denominator could resolve some observations.
- The PDFs do not provide Cox coefficients, standard errors, covariance or variance estimators, exact interaction or Schoenfeld statistics/degrees of freedom, selected row-level tests for most Table 2 P values, Fisher sidedness, chi-square variant/statistic/degrees of freedom, final subgroup-model specifications, landmark-analysis details, or the event-versus-person basis for safety analyses.
- Some cross-location comparisons require unresolved identity conditions, including measurement time, reader, angiogram, population, outcome definition, and estimand mapping.
- Reused native text and rendered pages were useful locators; direct PDFs were the evidence authority for candidates. DOC-003 required fresh page-level mapping because it had no usable scientific extraction at inventory.
- This review identifies source-grounded candidates for human adjudication. It does not establish causes, corrections, propagation, conclusion change, or harm.
