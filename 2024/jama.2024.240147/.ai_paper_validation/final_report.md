## 3. Scientific Findings

### F1 - Participant flow inconsistency - Major

- Location: DOC-001 p. 1, Abstract; p. 2, Key Points; p. 3, Figure 1 and Trial Population.
- Compared statements: The Abstract describes 733 restrictive and 724 liberal participants as randomly assigned. Key Points describes 1508 participants as randomized. Figure 1 shows 1979 randomized participants: 983 restrictive, 994 liberal, and 2 lost envelopes.
- Basis: 750 - 17 = 733 and 758 - 34 = 724, so 733 and 724 are analyzed populations. Also, 750 + 758 = 1508, so 1508 is the intervention-stage population, not the randomized population.
- Verification: Reconcile and label the population stage used in the Abstract, Key Points, Trial Population, and Figure 1.
