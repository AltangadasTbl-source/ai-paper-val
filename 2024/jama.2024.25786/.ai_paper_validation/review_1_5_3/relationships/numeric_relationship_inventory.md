# Package-Wide Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This is the stable, package-wide numeric/reporting inventory for the complete mapped union: DOC-001 (11 PDF pages), DOC-002 (103 pages), DOC-003 (26 pages), DOC-004 (3 pages), and DOC-005 (1 page). It consolidates mapper-local IDs without using legacy candidate or report conclusions. Direct PDFs are authoritative; current mapping artifacts and page-delimited native text were used as locators. `N` identifiers are stable relationship identifiers, not candidate identifiers.

Applied checks, where the inputs existed, were arithmetic and component totals; numerator/denominator/percentage reconciliation; missingness and population identity; rounding; outcome, unit, scale, and reference-group labels; risk/proportion/rate/person-time distinctions; repeated values; and matched cross-location occurrences. A displayed percentage was considered compatible with a count ratio when it lay within half a final displayed decimal place (normally 0.05 percentage point for a one-decimal percentage). Planned or protocol-only quantities were checked only against their stated planning context, not treated as observed results. DOC-004 and DOC-005 contain no analyzed result relationship; their administrative quantities were reviewed as no-applicable-result units.
