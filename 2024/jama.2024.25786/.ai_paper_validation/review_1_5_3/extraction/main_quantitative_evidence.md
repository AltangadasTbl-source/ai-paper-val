### N001 — Trial population, setting, intervention, and observation window

- **Locations:** [p. 1](../../../jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=1), abstract; [p. 2](../../../jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=2), Methods.
- **Population/time:** Adults directly transferred after trauma with full trauma-team activation and anticipated hospital stay of at least 24 hours; enrollment December 7, 2021 to September 12, 2023; 30-day follow-up ended October 12, 2023; 15 prehospital bases and 5 major trauma centers in Denmark, the Netherlands, and Switzerland.
- **Contrast/scale:** Random assignment 1:1 for 8 hours: restrictive strategy, SpO2 target 94%, `n=733` in the abstract analysis population; liberal strategy, 12-15 L/min oxygen or FiO2 0.6-1.0, `n=724` in that population. The Methods specify variable blocks of 4, 6, or 8 and stratification by inclusion site and endotracheal intubation at randomization.

## Inferential-statistical relationships

### S001 — Primary composite adjusted comparison

- **Locations:** [p. 1](../../../jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=1), abstract; [p. 6](../../../jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=6), Figure 2 and Outcomes.
- **Population/time/contrast/model:** 733 restrictive versus 724 liberal primary-analysis patients; 30-day death and/or major respiratory complications; binary logistic regression OR adjusted for site of inclusion and intubation status at randomization.
- **Printed estimate:** OR 1.01 (95% CI 0.75 to 1.37), P=.94; risk difference 0.56 percentage points (95% CI -2.70 to 3.82).

### S002 — Analysis-model specification

- **Location:** [p. 5](../../../jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=5), Statistical Analysis.
- **Printed specification:** Primary and key secondary outcomes use binary logistic regression, ORs with 95% CIs adjusted for site and intubation status at randomization. Additional models adjust for those factors plus age, sex, ISS, and first available posttrauma GCS. Generalized estimating equations account for site clustering; inverse-probability weighting adjusts estimated differential dropout. Per-protocol analysis applies to primary/key secondary outcomes. Exploratory outcomes use analogous adjustment and logistic or linear regression by outcome type.

### S003 — Significance and multiplicity specification

- **Location:** [p. 5](../../../jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=5), Statistical Analysis.
