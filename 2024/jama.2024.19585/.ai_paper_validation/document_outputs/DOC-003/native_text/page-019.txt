     c) Missing values are high: Within each site, missing values are sampled with replacement from the

     d) Best case for intervention: Within each control site, missing values are sampled with replacement
        from the highest 25% of observed values at the site. Within each intervention site, missing values
        are sampled with replacement from the lowest 25% of observed values at the site. This is an
        unrealistic scenario, as it presumes a completely different mechanism for non-response
        according to the site’s treatment assignment.
     e) Worst case for intervention: Within each control site, missing values are sampled with
        replacement from the lowest 25% of observed values at the site. Within each intervention site,
        missing values are sampled with replacement from the highest 25% of observed values at the
        site. This is also an unrealistic scenario, as it presumes a completely different mechanism for
        non-response according to the site’s treatment assignment.
