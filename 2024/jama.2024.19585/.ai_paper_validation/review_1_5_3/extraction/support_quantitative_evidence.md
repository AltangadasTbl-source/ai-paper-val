### Design, planned estimates, and statistical definitions

* **Sample size / Table 1**, DOC-002 pp. 10 and 24 and final SAP pp. 33-34: 20 sites with 4×8, 4×32, and 12×20 patients = 400; two-sided alpha .05; within-cluster SD 8.8; 4,000 simulated trials, each analysed with a linear mixed-effects site model; percentage significant = power. ICC=.021 (95% CI 0-.123); assumed 10% missing inflated 400 to **444**. For differences 2.5/3.0/3.5, power without/with age predictor is: ICC 0: 71.0/81.4, 86.7/94.2, 95.9/98.9; ICC .021: 59.5/70.1, 75.7/85.2, 86.5/94.2; ICC .123: 26.6/32.6, 38.2/45.3, 47.7/57.0. The stated focal design result is 85.2% (described in prose as 85%) for difference 3.0, ICC .021, with age.
* **Analytic population**, final SAP p. 34: all analyses intention-to-treat; primary/secondary PROs collected at weeks 4 and 8. The planned primary measurement is the SSPedi collected on the day other PROs were obtained/planned, ensuring it is an outcome rather than intervention screening.
* **Primary model**, pp. 11 and 36: patient-level week-8 total SSPedi mixed linear regression; random site/cluster effect; fixed treatment, age, diagnosis (leukemia/lymphoma, solid tumor, CNS tumor), and binary cluster-level randomization strata (annual English/Spanish 8-18 patient count below/above average; private-insurance percentage below/above average). Report covariate-adjusted mean difference, 95% CI, two-sided no-treatment-effect P. No baseline adjustment pre-specified because prophylactic care pathways may already affect baseline. Sensitivities: outcome-normalizing transformation, ordinal regression if nonnormal residuals, and systemic-chemotherapy-only subset.
* **Secondary models**, pp. 11-12 and 37-38: each of 15 ordinal SSPedi symptoms uses mixed proportional-odds model (treatment and two strata fixed; site random); report OR and 95% CI. Fatigue and each of eight PedsQL domains: same linear mixed framework as primary. Documentation: 15 symptoms × three cohorts (all; score>0; score≥3), mixed logistic OR adjusted for strata; fixed logistic only if random effect cannot be estimated. Interventions: same three cohorts; simple difference in proportions ignoring clustering, Fisher exact P, Newcombe 95% CI. Encounters: event count per child for each of emergency, clinic, hospitalization and total; mixed Poisson or negative-binomial, zero inflation if needed, random site and strata fixed; log follow-up time offset if <8 weeks.
* **Exploratory definitions**, pp. 12-13 and 38-39: severe symptom=3/4 and mixed binary logistic model; between-site heterogeneity expressed by MOR (median odds ratio with the higher odds in numerator). High SSPedi completer = ≥15 SSPedis, denominator = those completing protocol therapy as planned. Click rate = clicks/enrolled participant; 10 site rates and random-effects Poisson/negative-binomial with log patients offset, reporting summary rate and median rate ratio. Implementation reports counts/percentages, no inferential statistics.
* **Missing data**, pp. 13 and 39: PedsQL uses its scoring guide. If >10% missing primary/secondary PROs, 20 within-treatment-group multiply imputed datasets using week 0/4 SSPedi, group, site, outcomes, age, diagnosis, and strata; pool treatment estimate/SE by Rubin’s rules. Sensitivities impute from highest/lowest quartile. Documentation/intervention has no imputation: absent record is “no”; exploratory symptom-conditioned cohorts use observed data.
* **Planned displays**, pp. 37, 40-46: stacked 0-4 SSPedi bars and 15-symptom OR forest plot; PedsQL-domain treatment-effect forest plot; planned baseline demographic table; planned table of timepoint/group SSPedi outcomes with unadjusted/adjusted mean differences or ORs; planned non-SSPedi outcomes; documentation/intervention table; encounter counts and rates per 100 patient-weeks. These shells contain no populated observed values.
