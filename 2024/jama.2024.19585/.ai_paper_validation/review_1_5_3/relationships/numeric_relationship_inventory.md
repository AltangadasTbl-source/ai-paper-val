# Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope, identity, and checking convention

This inventory is the numeric-consistency stage’s stable relationship index for the complete mapped direct-source scope: DOC-001 `jama_dupuis_2024_oi_240111_1733431204.38761.pdf` pp. 1-11, DOC-002 `joi240111supp1_prod_1733431204.57929.pdf` pp. 1-46, and DOC-003 `joi240111supp2_prod_1733431204.76024.pdf` pp. 1-23. It was rebuilt from the two canonical extraction maps and targeted direct-PDF confirmation. It does not use legacy candidate, checker, verifier, critic, or report conclusions.

Each N identifier is stable within this numeric inventory. A row can contain a coherent multi-cell displayed relationship (for example, all mutually exclusive categories of a table row); all printed inputs in that relationship were checked. `PASS` means that no candidate was generated by the applicable numeric checks, not an adjudication. Statistical-model compatibility is retained when it is needed to prevent an invalid arithmetic comparison; the dedicated statistical passes own full inferential review.

Unless a table supplies a different denominator, percentages were recalculated as 100 x numerator / printed denominator. Percentages displayed to one decimal place were allowed an inclusive rounding interval of +/-0.05 percentage points; whole percentages were allowed +/-0.5 points. Counts and mutually exclusive totals require exact reconciliation. Adjusted model estimates were not forced to equal crude differences, rates, or proportions when the source identifies adjustment, a random effect, a marginal model, or a distinct population.

## Limitations

DOC-002 is protocol/SAP material, and pp. 41-46 are intentionally unpopulated planned table shells; they cannot be tested as observed result tables. DOC-001 Figure 2 prints no exact segment values, so the check was limited to its printed axes, categories, caption denominators, and labels. DOC-003 eTable 10 has changing symptom-conditioned denominators; all printed numerator/denominator/percentage relationships were checked using the stated denominator in each cell, without treating distinct cohorts as additive. The inventory does not infer unreported adjustment, variance, sidedness, or model details.
