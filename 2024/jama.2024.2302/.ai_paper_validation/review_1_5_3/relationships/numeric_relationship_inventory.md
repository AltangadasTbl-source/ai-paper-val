# Stable Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This inventory normalizes the complete numeric/reporting evidence maps: main-article mapper IDs `N001`-`N028` and support-mapper IDs `N-SUP-001`-`N-SUP-013`.  The new IDs are package-wide, sequential, and stable within this review: `N001`-`N028` retain the main-map numbers and `N029`-`N041` correspond, in order, to `N-SUP-001`-`N-SUP-013`.  Inferential relationships (`S001`-`S013` and `S-SUP-*`) are deliberately not duplicated here; numeric inputs and labels that are necessary to check them are retained and cross-referenced.

Direct PDFs are authoritative. Reused/fresh text and renders were used as locators and transcriptions as documented in the evidence maps. DOCX-001 is structurally covered but excluded from scientific discovery because it is a legacy assertion document.
