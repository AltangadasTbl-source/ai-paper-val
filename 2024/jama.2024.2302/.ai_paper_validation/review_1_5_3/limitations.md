# Limitations and Missing Definitions

This quantitative reporting quality-control review is restricted to the supplied package. It is not a raw-data, clinical, misconduct, or broad study-design audit.

- No raw analysis data, final model code, executable endpoint or observation-window code, paired enrollment/randomization timestamps, exact gestational-age coding, or amendment/version history were supplied.
- The supplied documents do not resolve the five named human questions. These missing inputs bound interpretation; they do not establish a numerical change, a conclusion change, or downstream propagation.
- OCR, native text, rendered pages, and derived page text were used as location and transcription aids. Direct supplied PDFs were the authority for reported evidence.
- The legacy DOCX was structurally covered but excluded from scientific discovery because it contains legacy candidate assertions.
