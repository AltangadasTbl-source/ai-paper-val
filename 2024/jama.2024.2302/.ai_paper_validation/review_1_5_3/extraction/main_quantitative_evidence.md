## Page 1 — Abstract

### N001 — Trial setting, population, allocation, and follow-up

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 1, Abstract, “Design, Setting, and Participants” and “Results.”
- **Printed relationship:** multicenter randomized trial at **39 US hospitals**, conducted **September 2013-April 2021**, with follow-up completed **January 3, 2023**. The randomized population was **338** preterm infants with inguinal hernia: **172 early** and **166 late** repair strategy.
- **Population/contrast/time/unit:** preterm infants diagnosed during initial hospitalization; early repair before neonatal-intensive-care-unit discharge versus late repair after discharge and when older than **55 weeks’ postmenstrual age**; counts of infants.

### N002 — Abstract participant descriptors and operative repair count

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 1, Abstract, “Results.”
- **Printed relationship:** **320** infants underwent operative repair; **86%** male, **2%** Asian, **30%** Black, **16%** Hispanic, **59%** White, race unknown **9%**, ethnicity unknown **4%**. Mean gestational age was **26.6 weeks (SD 2.8)** and mean postnatal enrollment age **12 weeks (SD 5)**.
- **Population/unit:** the abstract’s reported operative-repair population; percentages and weeks.

### N003 / S001 — Primary outcome, abstract form

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 1, Abstract, “Results”; matched narrative and Table 2 on p. 6; Figure 3 overall row on p. 8.
- **Printed relationship:** among **308 (91%)** infants with complete data (**159 early**, **149 late**), at least one serious adverse event occurred in **44/159 (28%)** early versus **27/149 (18%)** late.
- **Effect/contrast/model/scale:** late relative to early; absolute risk difference **−7.9% (95% credible interval [CrI], −16.9% to 0%)** and **97% Bayesian posterior probability of benefit with late repair**. Table 2 identifies the model as a Bayesian logistic model including repair strategy as a covariate and study site as a random intercept; analytic models also include gestational-age group and study site covariates.
- **Matching claims:** p. 2 Key Points repeats 28% versus 18% and risk difference −7.9%; p. 6 Table 2 and narrative add relative risk; p. 8 Figure 3 displays rounded overall values.

### N004 / S002 — Secondary hospital-stay outcome, abstract form

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 1, Abstract, “Results”; matched Table 2 and narrative on p. 6.
- **Printed relationship:** during the **10-month** observation period, median hospital days were **19.0 (IQR 9.8-35.0)** early versus **16.0 (IQR 7.0-38.0)** late; posterior probability of benefit with late repair **82%**.
- **Population/contrast/unit:** early versus late analytic groups, days. Table 2 gives relative risk **0.91 (95% CrI 0.74-1.11)** and defines benefit as fewer hospital days with late repair.

### S003 — Prespecified subgroup summary in the abstract

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 1, Abstract, “Results”; matched Figure 3 and p. 7 narrative.
- **Printed relationship:** the posterior probability that late repair reduced infants with at least one serious adverse event was **99%** in each of the subgroup definitions: gestational age **younger than 28 weeks** and bronchopulmonary dysplasia.
- **Matched detailed values:** p. 7/p. 8 provide the corresponding relative risks, CrIs, numerator/denominator counts, and risk differences in S011-S013.

## Page 2 — Background and methods

### N005 — Reported background rates and trial intervention definitions

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 2, Introduction and “Study Design and Interventions.”
- **Printed relationship:** background incidence reaches **40%** in males born at **24 weeks’** gestation; cited incarceration rate range **5%-28%**. The study compares two strategies: early repair before discharge and late repair after discharge when older than **55 weeks’ postmenstrual age**; randomization is **1:1** approximately **2 weeks** before anticipated discharge.
- **Role:** contextual/definition values needed to distinguish the intervention contrast; not a trial result.

### N006 — Eligibility population and outcome-time definition

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 2, “Patients,” “Study Design and Interventions,” and page 1 “Main Outcomes and Measures.”
- **Printed relationship:** eligible infants had inguinal hernia, estimated gestational age **<37 weeks**, and treatment in a participating neonatal intensive care unit. Primary outcome: any prespecified serious adverse event during **10 months**, adjudicated by a blinded committee; secondary outcome: total hospital days during that same observation period.
- **Role:** population, outcome and time definitions for N003/N004 and later tables/figures.

## Page 3 — Participant flow and outcome methods

### N007 — Screening and randomization flow

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 3, Figure 1 and footnotes.
- **Printed relationship:** **1514** assessed; **1176** excluded. Exclusions comprise **442** not meeting eligibility (the displayed components are 184 another procedure influencing timing, 108 unavailable for repair/follow-up, 51 associated factor, 43 not in NICU, 32 congenital/chromosomal abnormality, 31 no diagnosis, 25 gestational age >37 weeks; footnote permits more than one reason), and **734** meeting eligibility but not randomized (613 refusal, 54 parent/guardian unavailable, 37 physician decision, 14 not approached, 16 other). **338** randomized, stratified by site and gestational-age category **<28** or **≥28 weeks**.
- **Allocation/population:** **172** early and **166** late.

### N008 — Flow after early-strategy allocation

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 3, Figure 1, early-repair branch.
- **Printed relationship:** of 172 early-strategy infants, **163** received treatment as randomized: **147** underwent repair before discharge; **11** did not undergo repair (7 resolution, 2 medically unstable, 1 surgeon disagreed with diagnosis, 1 transferred for cardiac surgery); **5** underwent repair after discharge (2 condition change and one each early discharge, initially clinically resolved, twin). **9** did not receive treatment as randomized (7 parent preference for late, 2 other); **4** lost to follow-up; **159** included in primary analysis.
- **Definition:** “early” is planned before NICU discharge.

### N009 — Flow after late-strategy allocation

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 3, Figure 1, late-repair branch.
- **Printed relationship:** of 166 late-strategy infants, **157** received treatment as randomized: **90** repaired after reaching 55 weeks; **28** did not undergo repair (17 resolution, 4 refusal, 2 death, 5 other); **39** repaired before 55 weeks (13 clinician preference, 11 incarceration concern, 8 concurrent gastrostomy/repair, 5 parent preference, 2 postmenstrual-age miscalculation). **9** did not receive treatment as randomized (5 parent preference for early, 4 other); **8** lost to follow-up; **149** included in primary analysis.
- **Definition:** “late” is planned after NICU discharge and older than 55 weeks’ postmenstrual age.

### N010 — Primary-outcome event definitions and observation schedule

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 3, “Outcomes.”
- **Printed relationship:** primary outcome is any adjudicated serious adverse event within the **10-month** observation period. Event domains are pulmonary, cardiac, surgical, hernia-related, and death. Family assessments occur **30 days** after repair and **10 months** after enrollment.
- **Role:** definition/time link for Tables 2-3 and Figure 3.

## Page 4 — Analysis plan and results population

### S004 — Planned primary-effect assumptions and sample size

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 4, “Statistical Analysis.”
- **Printed relationship:** a priori event rates were **30% early** and **20% late**. **586** infants with primary-outcome data would provide **80%** power, two-sided **α=.05**, to detect a **10% absolute difference**; assuming **5%** loss to follow-up, planned enrollment was **615**.
- **Role:** planned frequentist design quantities, distinct from the Bayesian primary analysis.

### S005 — Analysis populations and model specifications

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 4, “Statistical Analysis.”
- **Printed relationship:** intention-to-treat analysis includes randomized infants except those withdrawn. Primary outcome uses logistic mixed-effects model; hospital days use negative-binomial mixed model. All models include gestational-age group as covariate and site as random intercept. Frequentist primary analysis used generalized estimating equation logistic model (exchangeable site correlation) because the mixed model did not converge. Bayesian priors for categorical/count outcomes centre OR or RR at **1.0 (95% CrI 0.33-3.0)**; subgroup interaction prior is centred **1.0 (95% CrI 0.33-3.0)**. No missing outcomes were imputed.

### S006 — Interim stopping result

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 4, “Statistical Analysis.”
- **Printed relationship:** interim analysis at **309** infants in **February 2021** found **97%** probability of a decreased serious-adverse-event rate for late repair, exceeding the prespecified **95%** efficacy stopping threshold; enrollment stopped on the recommendation described.

### N011 — Eligible, randomized, and analysis populations

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 4, “Patient Characteristics”; matched Figure 1 p. 3.
- **Printed relationship:** **1072 eligible**, **338 (32%) randomized**. Of **734** eligible nonrandomized infants, refusal was **84% (n=613)**; its stated reasons were 280 early preference, 196 late preference, 71 clinician decision, and 66 other. Randomization dates September 2013-April 2021; follow-up completed January 3, 2023. After randomization, **9** were withdrawn from each group, leaving **163 early/157 late**; then **4 early/8 late** lost to follow-up, leaving primary-outcome data for **308 (159/149)**.

### N012 — Baseline summary claim

- **Location:** PDF-001 p. 4, “Patient Characteristics”; Table 1 p. 5.
- **Printed relationship:** population was **86% male**; median birth weight **820 g (IQR 640-1040)**; **50%** had bronchopulmonary dysplasia. Prior apnea requiring intervention was **137 (85%) early** versus **122 (79%) late**; narrative states no other clinically important between-group differences.
