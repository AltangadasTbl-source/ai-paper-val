191         Infants can be lost-to-follow-up between NICU discharge and the time of their scheduled
192          repair (for late IH repair) or their 10-month follow-up phone call. Infants in centers with
193          neurodevelopmental assessments can also be lost between 10-month and 22-26 month
194          follow-up assessment.
195         Infants who withdraw from the study will not have any data collected beyond the
196          timepoint of withdrawal. Data collected prior to withdrawal will be available for

198         Numbers of infants who withdraw or are lost to follow-up will be presented in the
