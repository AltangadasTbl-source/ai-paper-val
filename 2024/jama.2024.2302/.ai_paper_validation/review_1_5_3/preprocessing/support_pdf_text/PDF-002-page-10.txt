335   Study population. During the 2.5-year enrollment period, 615 patients will be randomized to either early or late
336   IH repair, after obtaining informed consent from the parent or legal guardian. Data from all eligible patients will
337   be entered into the REDCap database as well as their enrollment status. For infants that were not
338   randomized, the reasons for this will be recorded (e.g. parent or provider refusal, or other reasons).
339
340             Inclusion Criteria: 1) GA < 37 wks, 0 days; 2) In a NICU / other nursery, at participating sites; 3)
341             Diagnosed with an IH per the pediatric surgeon; and 4) Parental consent and providers willing to
342             randomize the infant.
343
344             Exclusion Criteria:
345             1. Associated factor that impacts timing of repair (e.g. clinical factors that might preclude early IH
346             repair) 2) Infant is undergoing another operative procedure and IH repair is planned as a secondary
347             procedure such that timing of IH repair is impacted (e.g. fundoplication or gastrostomy tube is planned,
348             and IH repair is considered a secondary procedure); 3) Known major congenital anomaly or
349             chromosomal abnormality; and 4) Family unable to return for follow up and late IH repair; or likely
350             unable to monitor IH as outpatient (e.g. maternal incarceration or moving away from the area).

353   Study intervention.
354   Treatment Groups.
355   a. Early IH Repair = IH repair is performed promptly after randomization and prior to NICU discharge; ~38
356   weeks PMA, based on pilot trial and experience in practice.
357
358   b. Late IH Repair = IH repair will be performed at a goal of 5 months after NICU discharge (~ 55 - 60 weeks
359   PMA). This PMA was selected as the goal time for late repair because this reflects current surgical care and
360   most Children’s Hospitals consider allowing the operation to be done as an outpatient at this time. The decision
361   regarding admission will be determined by the providers for each infant according to site guidelines.
362
363   A small percentage of infants in either treatment group will likely undergo IH repair at times other than the goal
364   times, due to clinical considerations. For example, some infants assigned to late IH repair will undergo repair
365   prior to 55-60 weeks PMA due to hernia incarceration. These occurrences are important outcome measures
