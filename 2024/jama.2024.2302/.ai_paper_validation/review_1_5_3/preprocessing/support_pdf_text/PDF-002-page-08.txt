309   Enrollment and Randomization. Every effort will be made to obtain signed informed consent by the
310   parent(s)/guardian(s) as soon as possible. When the surgeon or neonatologist determines the baby may be a
311   good candidate for the HIP trial but parent/guardian is unavailable for written consent, phone consent will be
312   obtained. The research coordinator or PI will provide the parent with the study information and answer any
313   questions the parents have. If parental consent is given over the phone, the baby will be randomized to
314   treatment group so that providers can determine the treatment plan for the participant. The consent process
315   will continue when the parent is again at the hospital where written consent will be obtained. Written consent
316   will be obtained no later than the day of surgery for the IH repair. After obtaining informed consent, research
317   coordinators will create a new patient record in the REDCap database, enter patient data, and engage the
318   REDCap randomization module. This easy to use interface will provide an immediate treatment group
319   assignment, which will be stratified according to site and GA strata. Enrollment will occur when the infant is
320   approximately 2 weeks from anticipated NICU discharge. This is the exact time at which the decision is
321   currently made (outside any study) whether to perform the IH repair or defer until later.

323   HIP Trial Setting. Participating clinical sites have been carefully selected based on a high number of
324   premature neonates with an IH, equipoise of surgeons regarding the issue being studied, and the necessary
325   infrastructure to complete the trial (surgeon and neonatology site PI and research coordinator and/or fellow).
