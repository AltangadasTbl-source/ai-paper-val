89    4. Trial Methods
90    4.1 Trial Design
91    The HIP trial is a randomized, multicenter parallel-group trial. Infants are randomized 1:1 to
92    early or late IH repair.
93    4.2 Treatment Groups
94    a. Early IH Repair: IH repair is performed after randomization, when the neonatology team
95    believes the infant is optimized from a medical perspective, and prior to NICU discharge; ~38
96    weeks PMA, based on pilot trial and experience in practice.
 97   b. Late IH Repair: IH repair will be performed when infant is > 55 weeks post menstrual age
 98   and after NICU discharge. This is estimated to typically be approximately 5 months after NICU
 99   discharge. This PMA was selected as the goal time for late repair because most children’s
100   hospitals consider allowing the operation to be done as an outpatient at this time and this
101   reflects current surgical care. The decision regarding admission will be determined by the
102   providers for each infant according to site guidelines.
103   4.3 Randomization
104   Randomization is stratified by site and GA stratum (<28 weeks and >28 weeks estimated GA)
105   using permuted variable block size. Randomization occurred 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
106   through a REDCap electronic data capture system. In cases of multiple gestations qualifying for
107   inclusion the first twin was randomized in the usual manner and the sibling was assigned to the
108   same treatment group.
