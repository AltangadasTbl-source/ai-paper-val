667   Trial Commencement: The HIP trial was started as a pilot phase at Vanderbilt only June 3, 2013. The
668   purpose of this phase is to test the REDCap data randomization module, data capture methods, establish the
669   working relationship of Dr. Blakely with the DCC, as well as gain important experience in approaching families
