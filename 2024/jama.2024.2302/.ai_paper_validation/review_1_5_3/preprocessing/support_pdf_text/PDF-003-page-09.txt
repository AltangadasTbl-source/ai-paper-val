308   observed proportions are overlayed on the plots, then the observed proportions should lie within
309   the cloud of points generated by the simulations and not appear to be outliers.
310   7.3.3 Sensitivity Analysis

311   In addition to adjusting for GA group and center as a random effect, sensitivity analyses will be
312   conducted if important baseline covariates are imbalanced across the two intervention groups.
313   This analysis will only be completed for the primary outcome and will be based on the mITT
314   sample.
315   For the primary outcome, in addition to the mITT sample-based analysis, a PP analysis will be
316   done using the PP sample.
317   7.3.4 Subgroup Analyses

318   The primary outcome will be reanalyzed for subgroup exploratory analyses. These analyses will
319   be completed using the mITT sample. To investigate treatment effect modifiers for the primary
320   and secondary outcomes (total hospital days and BSIDIII scores), we will use Bayesian
321   hierarchical models with terms of interaction between treatment group (early, late) and the
322   prespecified potential moderators: GA (<28; >28 weeks) bronchopulmonary dysplasia (yes/no),
323   maternal education <12 years (yes/no), prenatal care (yes/no), IH difficult to reduce (yes/no).
324   The conservative Bayesian approach of Dixon and Simon6 allows us to specify a priori how
325   likely (or unlikely) it is for subgroup differences to be present and to shrink the subgroup
326   estimates to the overall mean treatment effect.
327   Prior distributions for main effects will be the same as for the primary analysis. A Normal(0,
328   SD=0.6) will be used for interaction terms. Point estimates of treatment effect and 95% credible
329   intervals for each subgroup will be reported along with probability of treatment benefit/harm. We
330   will conduct similar subgroup analyses to examine differences in treatment effect according to
331   gender, race/ethnicity, although such differences are not expected (required for NIH Phase III
332   RCTs).
333   Two additional exploratory subgroup analyses will be performed (not stated in trial protocol): the
334   impact of laparoscopic versus open IH repair on primary and secondary outcomes has become
335   more important given the increasing use of laparoscopic approach to these operations. The
336   distance of the primary residence of the family to the treating facility will also be evaluated to
337   investigate whether longer distance from the facility might impact the treatment effect by
338   favoring early repair.
339   7.3.5 Analyses of association between gestational age and risk of SAE

340   In addition to the planned subgroup analyses by GA group, we will also conduct analyses to
341   investigate the effect of GA on the rate of SAE. We will treat GA as a continuous variable in the
342   model and using splines or polynomials we will evaluate whether a linear or non-linear
343   relationship provides the best fit for the data. We will use splines or polynomial to model the
344   association. All other model details will be as described for the primary analysis.
345   We will then evaluate an interaction between the final GA term (linear or non-linear) and
346   intervention effect for the primary outcome using the same methods described in the Subgroup
347   Analyses section.
