413   Neurodevelopmental Follow-up Assessment.
414   The NICHD U01 award provides limited funding for the first 200 participants with access to robust
415   neurodevelopment follow-up programs. To increase validity and efficiency and reduce costs, we will conduct
416   the follow up assessment in the 8 study centers in the NICHD Neonatal Research Network (NRN) that already
417   have certified examiners, high follow-up rates, and well demonstrated success in assessing developmental
418   effects in clinical trials (UT Houston, UT Dallas, UAB, UCLA, Children’s Mercy Hospital, Nationwide Children’s,
419   Duke, U Michigan). These selected sites have protocols for annual training of examiners to ensure reliability of
420   assessments.95 In addition to the 8 NRN sites, the neurodevelopmental assessment will also be performed at
421   other participating sites with established neurodevelopmental programs that are capable of administering a
422   neurologic exam and the Bayley Scales of Infant Development, 3rd Edition (BSID III) with current resources.
423   Participants will complete the follow-up assessment at 22-26 months correct age. The NICHD U01 award
424   provides limited funding for the first 200 participants. Additional funding is being sought to promote
425   neurodevelopmental follow-up assessment of all HIP infants. HIP sites outside of the NRN were selected partly
426   based on the presence of a neurodevelopmental PI at the site with the intention of implementing
427   neurodevelopmental follow-up once funding is secured for all participants. All participants volunteering for the
428   neurodevelopmental portion of the study will be seen by certified examiners at their site’s neurodevelopmental
429   follow-up clinic. If a HIP trial infant isn’t eligible for 2 year neurodevelopmental follow-up (e.g., very late preterm
430   infant [36 weeks gestation] with no comorbidities) and the family does not wish to return for follow-up, this
431   preference will be respected and no in-person follow-up obtained. In this case, if parents are willing and should
432   funding allow, a brief phone conversation will occur where trained personnel will ask standard questions as
433   part of routine neurodevelopmental follow-up.

435   The Bayley Scales of Infant Development, 3rd edition, (BSIDIII) will be administered96 and a neurologic exam
436   will also be performed. All assessments will be performed by NICHD NRN certified examiners or examiners
437   certified by their respective site. The BSID III Cognitive, Language and Motor scales will be administered as
438   part of the routine NRN 22-26 month corrected age visit. The BSIDIII offered at the other participating sites are
439   available to all patients within their infant follow-up clinics. The cognitive component of the BSIDIII is
440   hypothesized to differ according to treatment group assignment in the HIP trial and is the basis for the sample
441   size calculation below. The Composite scores for each of these scales are age-standardized with a mean
442   score of 100 and standard deviation of 15; a score of less than 70 (>2 SDs below the mean) indicates
443   significant delay. Children with severe neurologic and/or developmental delay as to preclude Cognitive scale
444   administration will be assigned a Composite score of 54. Cerebral palsy will be defined as a non-progressive
445   disorder characterized by abnormal tone in at least 1 extremity and abnormal control of movement and
446   posture97, and the Gross Motor Function Classification System will be utilized to classify severity. 98 Data from
447   these assessments will be entered into the REDCap database by existing follow-up program coordinators at
448   each site.
