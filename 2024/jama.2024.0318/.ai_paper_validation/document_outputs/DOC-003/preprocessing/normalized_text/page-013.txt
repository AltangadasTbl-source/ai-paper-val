eFigure 6. Subgroup analysis for participants with BMI <35 and >35 kg/m2 at randomization.
A. HbA1c over time. B. Weight loss over time. Least-squared (LS) means and error bars (i.e., one
standard error for the corresponding LS mean) are plotted over time by group.




At year 7, participants in the medical/lifestyle group who had BMI <35 kg/m2 at baseline had a mean HbA1c of 8.3%
and those in the surgical group with baseline BMI <35 kg/m2 had a mean HbA1c of 7.4%. The mean HbA1c of the
medical and surgical participants with baseline BMI >35 kg/m2 was 8.1% and 7.0%, respectively. For weight loss,
participants in the medical/lifestyle group with baseline BMI <35 kg/m2 had a weight loss of 5.6%, while the
surgical group with baseline BMI <35 kg/m2 had a weight loss of 20.4%. Weight loss of medical and surgical
participants with baseline BMI >35 kg/m2 was 10.1% and 19.3%, respectively. The group difference was 14.8% for
BMI <35 kg/m2 and 9.2% for BMI >35 kg/m2 (p=0.03).
