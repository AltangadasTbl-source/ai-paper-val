The use of the inverse probability of treatment weights essentially created a synthetic sample in which
HbA1c was not confounding with the treatment assignment, which allows for the estimation of the average
treatment effect as the target estimand 19. The average treatment effect should be interpreted as the effect
in the entire study population. The validity of the IPW approach relies on several key assumptions such as
no unmeasured confounding, positivity, and correct specification of the IPW models 18,20, and future
research is desired for an in-depth investigation of these assumptions.
