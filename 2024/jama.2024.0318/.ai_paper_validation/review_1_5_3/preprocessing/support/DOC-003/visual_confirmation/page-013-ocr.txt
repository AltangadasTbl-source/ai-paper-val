At year 7, participants in the medical/lifestyle group who had BMI <35 kg/m? at baseline had a mean HbA: of 8.3%
and those in the surgical group with baseline BMI <35 kg/m? had a mean HbA: of 7.4%. The mean HbA; of the
medical and surgical participants with baseline BMI >35 kg/m? was 8.1% and 7.0%, respectively. For weight loss,
participants in the medical/lifestyle group with baseline BMI <35 kg/m? had a weight loss of 5.6%, while the
surgical group with baseline BMI <35 kg/m? had a weight loss of 20.4%. Weight loss of medical and surgical
participants with baseline BMI >35 kg/m? was 10.1% and 19.3%, respectively. The group difference was 14.8% for
BMI <35 kg/m? and 9.2% for BMI >35 kg/m? (p=0.03).
