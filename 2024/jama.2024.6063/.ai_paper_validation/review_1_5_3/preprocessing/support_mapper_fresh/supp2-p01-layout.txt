4    Statistical analysis plan

5    Linear regression will be used to compare changes in pain, maximal effusion size
6    and function in univariate and multivariable modelling (if groups are not well matched
7    at baseline) between groups using intention to treat analysis for all outcomes.

8    Per protocol analyses will be completed for study participants consuming ≥80% of
9    capsules.

10   Significance will be p < 0.05 (two tailed).

11   Missing data will be accounted for by using propensity weighting, as appropriate.
