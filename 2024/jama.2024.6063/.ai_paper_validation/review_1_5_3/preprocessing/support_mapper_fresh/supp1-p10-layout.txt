14. Adverse events
Drug safety will be assessed by recording all important adverse events (AE’s), whether we think
they are related to the study medications or not, as per the relevant extension of the CONSORT
statement48. Blood samples will be taken for assessing change in lipids and inflammatory markers
(screening, week 12), although we expect these to improve, rather than worsen, with krill oil
treatment. We will report incidence and number of regular AE’s, by treatment group, and
categorised by type. We have no preconceived idea of what these other types might be, except for
those mentioned above. We will report incidence and number of serious adverse events (SAE’s),
including cancer, unplanned hospital admissions and joint replacements. Krill oil has a good safety
profile31, and is available over–the–counter, so a data safety monitoring board is not required.
