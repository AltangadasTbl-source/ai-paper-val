eFigure 18 Illustrative predicted probabilities of independent ambulation(mRS 0-3) and how
they change with increasing age and time from perfusion imaging acquisition to
reperfusion/end of the procedure with CTP/MRI core volume set at a) 70ml, b) 100ml and c)
150ml.
As age and time from perfusion imaging acquisition increases, the predicted probability of
independent ambulation decreases. This relationship is consistent across composite core volume
estimates, but overall predicted probability decreases as composite core volume estimate increases.
As age increases and time from imaging to reperfusion/end of the procedure lengthens, the
probability of independent ambulation decreases, such that it is very unlikely that a patient with
150mL core would achieve independent ambulation unless they are young and treated very rapidly.
