## Cross-document population and result keys

- **Trial and comparison:** SELECT2, EVT plus medical management versus medical management (MM). Protocol and SAP primary/secondary efficacy/safety analyses use the intention-to-treat assignment; DOC-006 eTable 1 and selected eTables are explicitly as-treated.
- **Main matching keys:** 90-day ordinal mRS (with SAP categories 5 and 6 merged for the rank analysis); mRS 0-2 functional independence; mRS 0-3 independent ambulation; mRS 5-6 complete dependence/death; mortality; symptomatic ICH; early neurological improvement; imaging volume/growth; imaging-stratum interaction effect.
- **Imaging definitions:** CTP core is rCBF <30%; MRI core is ADC <620 x 10^-6 mm2/s; CT hypodensity is manually delineated; composite core is the larger of CTP/MRI core and CT hypodensity. Hypoperfused tissue is Tmax >6 s. Mismatch volume = Tmax >6-s volume minus ischemic-core volume; mismatch ratio is the stated ratio of core and critically hypoperfused volume. Favorable profiles: ratio at least 1.8 and volume at least 15 mL, or ratio at least 1.2 and volume at least 10 mL.
- **Protocol eligibility key:** age 18-85 years, NIHSS at least 6, pre-stroke mRS 0-1, ICA/M1 LVO, last-known-well to treatment 0-24 h, and large core on ASPECTS 3-5, CTP rCBF <30% at least 50 cc, or MRI ADC <620 at least 50 cc. Excludes ASPECTS 6-10 with perfusion core below 50 cc and ASPECTS 0-2.

## DOC-003 protocol: numerical/statistical definitions and tables

- **Timing and assessments (pp. 8-10, 37-41):** baseline, 24 h after randomization (plus/minus 6 h), 5-7 days/discharge, 30 days (plus/minus 15), and 90 days (plus/minus 15). Primary mRS is 90 days; secondary functional independence is mRS 0-2 at day 90. Imaging outcome window is 24 h to 7 d. mRS contact window is 60-120 days when clinic/telephone assessment is unavailable.
- **Randomization (pp. 9, 33-34, 76-79):** 1:1 covariate-adaptive minimization, with age (<60/60-69/70-79/80-85), NIHSS (6-9/10-15/16-20/>20), site, M1/ICA, 0-8/9-16/17-24 h time window, core (<50/50-100/>100 cc), target mismatch (ratio at least 1.8 and volume at least 15 cc), and hemisphere. Appendix weights: age 4, NIHSS 4, location 3, time 3, core 2, mismatch 2, hemisphere 1, center 1; allocation probability p=0.9 to the lower-imbalance arm, 0.5 for a tie, and 0.1 otherwise.
- **Primary endpoint/model (pp. 45-46):** distribution of 90-day mRS under ITT; normal approximation of Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney/GLR test, with CMH adjusted analysis. Adjusted variables are the adaptive-randomization variables; age, NIHSS, treatment time, and core are continuous in that adjusted analysis.
- **Primary planning distribution (p. 46):** EVT mRS 0-6 = 4%, 8%, 12%, 16%, 19%, 13%, 28%; MM = 1%, 4%, 9%, 14%, 19%, 10%, 43%; each totals 100%. Standardized effect 0.34. Fixed design: 376 total/188 per arm, 90% power, alpha 5%; adaptive maximum 560 (376 plus 184); interim n=200 and n=380; projected adaptive power about 91% at standardized effect 0.34 with one-sided type-I error 2.5%.
- **Adaptive group/table definitions (pp. 45, 47-49):** six disjoint groups G1A/G1B/G2A/G2B/G3A/G3B by imaging and 0-12 versus 12-24 h; subgroup testing uses five ordered cumulative groups. Simulation uses 5,000 trials and group proportions 14%, 6%, 14%, 6%, 42%, 18%. Table 3 records scenarios 0-7: adaptive average n/power vs fixed n/power: #0 426/2.0% vs 560/2.5%; #1 376/86.0% vs 560/95.8%; #2 393/81.1% vs 560/93.3%; #3 500/74.1% vs 560/45.7%; #4 502/78.5% vs 560/21.2%; #5 513/67.1% vs 560/29.2%; #6 503/70.6% vs 560/21.2%; #7 503/60.5% vs 560/12.8%. Scenario 0 also reports futility stopping 59.1%.
- **Secondary analyses (pp. 50-52):** mRS 0-2 compared with logistic regression adjusted for adaptive variables; binary safety/reperfusion endpoints use chi-square/Fisher exact and adjusted logistic regression, reporting adjusted OR and 95% CI; continuous endpoints use mean plus/minus SD and median (IQR), t test or Wilcoxon rank sum; discharge uses category frequency/percentage and chi-square/Fisher. Discordant-profile and site-poolability heterogeneity use treatment-by-profile/site interaction. Sites with fewer than five subjects combine by geographic region. Missing 90-day mRS uses ordinal-logistic multiple imputation if unavailable in the 60-120 d window.
- **Safety monitoring (pp. 56-63):** sICH Step 1 is Pr(pi_sE >0.35 | data)>0.95; Step 2 is Pr(pi_sE > pi_sM +0.10 | data)>0.95. Mortality analogues are Pr(pi_mE >0.40 | data)>0.95 and Pr(pi_mE > pi_mM +0.10 | data)>0.95. Priors: Beta(0.35,0.65) for sICH and Beta(0.4,0.6) for mortality (both effective sample size 1). The threshold tables are fully source-located at pp. 58-63: for core at most 100 cc, sICH triggers at n 50/100/150/200/250 with 24/44/63/82/101 or more events; mortality at the same n with 26/49/71/92/113 or more deaths. For core >100 cc and extracranial ICA, sICH thresholds n 20-100 are 11,16,20,24,28,32,36,40,44; mortality thresholds are 12,17,22,26,32,35,40,44,49. The expected split is 80% at most 100 cc and 20% above 100 cc among maximum 280 EVT patients; expected isolated extracranial ICA is 10%/about 28 of 280.
- **Formulas/bounds (pp. 77-81):** if assigning a participant to treatment k, imbalance for variable f is I_k(Z_f=l_f)=D(N_1(...),...,N_k(...)+1,...,N_K(...)); G_k=sum(f=1 to S) w_f I_k. Under the variance D, choose k minimizing sum w_f N_k(Z_f=l_f). The standardized Wilcoxon statistic is Z_il={W_il-[n_il(n_il+n_0i^l+1)/2]}/{[n_il n_0i^l(n_il+n_0i^l+1)/12]^(1/2)}. Efficacy is Z_Jl at least b; futility uses its theta-adjusted counterpart at most b-tilde; final efficacy is Z_J,l=3 at least c. With maximum N=560, interims 200/380, alpha 0.025 and power 0.9: b=2.604, b-tilde=-1.897, c=2.772, theta=0.0791; H0 P(Y<=X)=1/2 and HA=1/2+theta, where Y is MM Rankin and X is EVT Rankin.

## DOC-004 SAP: planned result/label relationships

- **Trial/sample plan (pp. 6-12):** PROBE superiority design with two interims/final; planned maximum 560; a prespecified sample of 352 actually recruited before early stopping. First interim occurred after 200 completed 90-day follow-ups; a second planned at 380. SAP records the same 0.34 standardized difference, fixed 376/188 per arm, 90% power at one-sided alpha 2.5%, adaptive maximum 560, and projected 91% power. The DSMB reviewed unblinded data after 300 completed 90-day follow-ups without a design alteration/additional interim.
- **Outcome labels (pp. 13-14):** primary 90-day mRS distribution. Secondary: mRS 0-2, mRS 0-3, SITS-MOST sICH (local PH2/remote PH plus NIHSS increase at least 4 or death within 24 h), neurological worsening NIHSS increase >4 in 24 h, 90-d mortality, complications, mTICI at least 2b successful reperfusion, discharge, early NIHSS improvement at least 8 or NIHSS 0-1, and NeuroQOL. Tertiary includes infarct volume 24 h-7 d, lesion growth, PH2, SAH, hemicraniectomy and length of stay. One-year mRS/outcomes are explicitly not for the primary manuscript.
- **General analysis rules (pp. 14-16):** all CIs 95%; no familywise formal adjustment for secondary/tertiary/exploratory results; ITT uses assigned arm regardless of received treatment; mRS 5 and 6 merge. Missing primary mRS assumes MAR, uses 30 ordinal-logistic multiple imputations pooled by Rubin rules, plus tipping-point analysis.
- **Primary effect measures (p. 16):** WMW superiority with 95% CI/p; ARD=2(WMW-0.5); NNT=1/ARD; WMW generalized odds with 95% CI. These definitions are the key matching labels for main-paper ordinal outcomes.
- **Binary outcome measures (pp. 17-20):** mRS 0-2 and 0-3, sICH, and neurological worsening are proportions with RR/95% CI/p from modified Poisson regression; mRS derived from imputed 90-d distribution. Successful reperfusion is count/proportion only and not imputed. Discharge categories are home, other acute care, inpatient rehabilitation, skilled nursing, hospice, and in-hospital death.
- **Figures/tables/subgroups (pp. 21-23):** Table 1 baseline and Table 2 primary/key secondary outcomes; Figure 1 ITT mRS Grotta bar; Figure 2 as-treated/per-protocol mRS; Figure 3 primary-outcome 95% CI forest plot; supplemental CONSORT. Prespecified subgroups include core <70/at least70, <100/at least100, <150/at least150 mL; ASPECTS 6-10/3-5/0-2; both mismatch profiles; restricted trial populations; and US/non-US sites.

## DOC-005 secondary-analysis SAP: definitions, sensitivity, and subgroup plan

- **Population/principles (pp. 3-4):** all SELECT2 participants for treatment-effect analyses, as-treated; exploratory with no multiplicity adjustment; missing data not imputed; Stata/R. mRS 5 and 6 merge.
- **Imaging and profiles (p. 3):** primary core is larger of CTP/MRI rCBF<30% or ADC<620 and CT hypodensity; sensitivity uses CTP/MRI alone. Discordant 1: core <70 mL with ASPECTS at most 5; discordant 2: core at least70 mL with ASPECTS >5; concordant: core at least70 mL with ASPECTS at most5.

Adjust for age, presentation NIHSS, last-known-well-to-randomization time and core; omit the subgroup variable itself from adjustment. Marginal-probability plots may use logistic regression when Poisson calibration is poor. sICH is PH2/remote PH with NIHSS increase at least4 in the 4-h follow-up window; only count/proportion, no model/test because events are few.
- **Subgroup/sensitivity plan (pp. 5-7):** core thresholds 70/100/150 mL; ASPECTS 0-2/3-5/6-10 and each 3-5 point; mismatch 50/100 mL and two profiles; interaction terms test heterogeneity. Appendix lists post-hoc additions: CT hypodensity as third volume estimate, larger-of-two-modality mismatch, overestimated core, follow-up infarct volume, and within-person CT-hypodensity versus CTP/MRI core.
