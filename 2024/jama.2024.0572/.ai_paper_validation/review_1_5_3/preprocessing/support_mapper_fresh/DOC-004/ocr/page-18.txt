10.2.3.4 Secondary outcome - symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) as measured
by the SITS-MOST criteria
10.2.3.4.1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients exhibiting symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) as measured by the
SITS-MOST criteria within 24 hours.
10.2.3.4.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the proportion of patients exhibiting symptomatic
intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) are similar between patients assigned to EVT arm and patients assigned
to MM arm.
10.2.3.4.3 Treatment of missing values
Symptomatic ICH is assumed to be missing-at-random (MAR), i.e. the missingness of the data may
depend on the observed data, but not on the missing data. Important explanatory and auxiliary
variables are collected and will be examined to assess the plausibility of the MAR assumption. We will
perform multiple imputations for missing data where data will be imputed 30 times by selecting random
values from a distribution of potential values based on strata (values) of all observed explanatory and
auxiliary variables. Specifically, we will build a logistic regression model including explanatory and
auxiliary variables to impute symptomatic ICH for missing observations. Estimates will be pooled across
the imputed datasets in accordance with Rubin’s rule’.
10.2.3.4.4 Analysis method
The treatment effect will be expressed as
e The proportion of patients exhibiting symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH)
e The relative risk with its 95% confidence interval and p-value, calculated using modified
Poisson regression

10.2.3.5.1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients exhibiting neurological worsening, defined as a >4-point increase on the
NIHSS score within 24 hours due to the stroke itself.
10.2.3.5.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the proportion of patients exhibiting neurological worsening
are similar between patients assigned to EVT arm and patients assigned to MM arm.
10.2.3.5.3 Treatment of missing values
Treatment of missing values will be performed similar to secondary outcome symptomatic ICH as
described in section 10.2.3.4.3.
