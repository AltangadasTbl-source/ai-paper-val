11.2 Per-protocol population
Due to the nature of the study, some subjects in the study may have had significant protocol violation,
including eligibility criteria and treatment received. A per-protocol analysis including only individuals
who meet all eligibility criteria, including imaging evaluation as determined by the central core lab and
received treatment as indicated during randomization will also be performed.
