7.4 Operating characteristics of the adaptive design
Simulations (5000 simulated trials) were conducted to evaluate the performance of the adaptive design
(max n=560) and compare it to a traditional fixed sample-size design (fixed n=560) under various
scenarios (see the illustrative table below). For the simulations the effect size is expressed as a
standardized effect with normal approximation, where a standardized difference of 0.32 or 0.30
corresponds to a conservative projected effect of EVT (anticipated standardized difference 0.34; see
section of Power and sample size considerations). The assumptions were that the observed proportions
of patients in G1A, G1B, G2A, G2B, G3A, G3B were 14%, 6%, 14%, 6%, 42%, and 18%, respectively.
