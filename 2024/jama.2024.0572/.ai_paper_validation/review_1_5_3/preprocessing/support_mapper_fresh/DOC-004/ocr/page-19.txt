10.2.3.6 Secondary outcome — all-cause mortality within 90-day follow-up
10.2.3.6.1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients exhibiting all-cause mortality within 90-day follow-up.
10.2.3.6.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the proportion of patients exhibiting all-cause mortality are
similar between patients assigned to EVT arm and patients assigned to MM arm.
10.2.3.6.3 Treatment of missing values
Treatment of missing values will be performed similar to secondary outcome mRS 0-2 as described in
section 10.2.3.2.3.
10.2.3.6.4 Analysis method
The treatment effect will be expressed as
e The proportion of patients exhibiting mortality
e The relative risk with its 95% confidence interval and p-value, calculated using modified
Poisson regression
10.2.3.7 Secondary, outcome — Procedural complications
10.2.3.7,1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients demonstrating surgical site complications, defined as a cumulative measure
of surgical site hematoma, surgical site infection and vascular injury (peripheral or intracranial
dissection, perforation or other) caused by endovascular procedure.
10.2.3.7.2 Statistical hypothesis
No hypothesis will be evaluated.
10.2.3.7.3 Treatment of missing values
Missing data for surgical site complications will not be imputed.
10.2.3.7.4 Analysis method
The count and proportion of patients exhibiting procedural complications will be reported
