10.2.3.2 Secondary outcome - functional independence, defined as mRS score of 0-2 at 90
day follow-up
10.2.3.2.1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients exhibiting a modified Rankin Scale (mRS) score of 0-2 at day 90.
10.2.3.2.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the proportion of patients exhibiting a modified Rankin Scale
(mRS) score of 0-2 at day 90 does not differ significantly between patients assigned to EVT arm and
patients assigned to MM arm.
10.2.3.2.3 Treatment of missing values
As described in section 10.2.3.1.3, these data (modified Rankin Scale scores at 90-day follow-up) will be
imputed using multiple imputations. The values for missing mRS 0-2 will be extrapolated from the
imputed 90-day mRS distribution and used to obtain estimates of effect, which then will be pooled
across imputed datasets using Rubin’s rule.
10.2.3.2.4 Analysis method
The treatment effect will be expressed as
e The proportion of patients exhibiting a modified Rankin Scale (mRS) score of 0-2 at day 90
e The relative risk with its 95% confidence interval and p-value, calculated using modified
Poisson regression
