10.2.3.8.2 Statistical hypothesis
No hypothesis will be evaluated.
10.2.3.8.3 Treatment of missing values

10.2.3.9 Secondary outcome — Discharge location
10.2.3.9.1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients receiving discharge to home, other acute care facility, inpatient
rehabilitation facility, skilled nursing facility, hospice care and in-hospital deaths.
10.2.3.9.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the proportion of discharge locations does not differ
significantly between patients assigned to EVT arm and patients assigned to MM arm.
10.2.3.9.3 Treatment of missing values
Missing data for discharge location will not be imputed
10.2.3.9.4 Analysis method
The treatment effect will be expressed as
e The proportion (%) of patients receiving discharge to
1) home,
2) other acute care facility,
3) inpatient rehabilitation facility,
A) skilled nursing facility,
5) hospice care and
6) in-hospital deaths
10.2.3.10 Secondary outcome — Early Neurological Improvement
10.2.3.10.1 Outcome measure
The proportion of patients exhibiting early neurological improvement, defined as improvement of 28
points on NIHSS at 24 hours of presentation or an NIHSS of 0-1.
10.2.3.10.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the proportion of patients exhibiting early neurological
improvement between patients assigned to EVT arm and patients assigned to MM arm.
