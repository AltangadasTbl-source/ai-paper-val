After the first interim analysis, the trial proceeds to this stage under two scenarios: (1) continuing
enrollment in the overall population (i.e., option 1 above) and (2) limiting enrollment to a selected
subgroup (i.e., option 3a above). When scenario (1) happens, the testing at the 2nd interim analysis at
enrollment and completion of 90-day follow-up of 380 patients is identical to the first interim analysis.
When scenario (2) happens, the null hypothesis is tested in that selected subgroup:
1. If neither boundary is crossed, the trial continues to the final analysis with an enrollment of 560-
380=180 additional patients limited to the selected subgroup.
2. If the efficacy boundary is crossed, the trial stops and efficacy is declared in the selected
subgroup.
3. If the futility boundary is crossed, the trial stops for futility.
After the second interim analysis, the trial proceeds to final stage under two scenarios: (1) continuing
enrollment in the overall population and 2) limiting enrollment to a selected subgroup. After enrollment
and completion of 90-day follow-up of further 180 patients, the null hypothesis is tested in either 1)
overall population or 2) selected subgroup.
1. If the efficacy boundary is crossed, EVT is declared efficacious.
2. If the efficacy boundary is not crossed, EVT is declared as not superior to standard medical
management.
