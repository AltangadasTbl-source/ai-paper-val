10.2 Trial reporting

10.2.1 Trial profile
Flow of patients through the study will be displayed in a standard CONSORT diagram (Supplementary
Figure 1 in appendix). The report will include the number of patients included, withdrawn, lost to follow
up, the number who received the allocated treatment and were analyzed.

10.2.2 Patient characteristics and baseline comparisons
To assess balance, description of the specified baseline characteristics will be presented for the two
treatment arms (Table 1 — see Appendix). Discrete variables will be summarized as frequencies and
percentages. Unless otherwise indicated in the tables, percentages will be calculated according to the
number of patients for whom data are available. If there are more than 5% missing values, the
denominator will be added in a footnote in the corresponding summary table. Continuous variables will
be summarized by use of either mean and standard deviation (SD) or median and interquartile range
(IQR). Durations and time intervals will be summarized by medians and IQRs.

10.2.3 Outcome reporting
The description of the specified primary and secondary clinical outcomes will be presented for the two
treatment arms (Table 2 — see Appendix).

10.2.3.1 Primary. outcome - The 90-day functional outcome, as measured by the modified
Rankin Scale score
10.2.3.1.1 Outcome measure
The distribution of scores on the modified Rankin Scale (mRS) at day 90. Patients with mRS scores of 6
(death) will be merged with patients who achieved scores of 5 (bedridden with constant care required)
to avoid counting a shift from mRS 6 to 5 as a treatment success as they are assigned same utility scores”

10.2.3.1.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the distribution of scores on the modified Rankin Scale (mRS)
at day 90 in patients assigned to EVT arm does not differ from the distribution of scores on the modified
Rankin Scale (mRS) at day 90 in patients assigned to MM arm.
