9. Definitions of outcomes
9.1 Primary outcome
e The 90-day functional outcome, as measured by the modified Rankin Scale score distribution.
9.2 Secondary outcomes
e Functional independence, defined as mRS score of 0-2 at 90-day follow-up
e Independent ambulation, defined as mRS score of 0-3 at 90-day follow-up
e Symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) as measured by the SITS-MOST criteria (local
PH2/remote PH hemorrhagic transformation) associated with a 2>4-point increase on the NIHSS
score or death within 24 hours
e Neurological worsening, defined as defined as a >4-point increase on the NIHSS score due to the
stroke itself within 24 hours
e Mortality within 90-day follow-up
e Procedural complications, defined as cumulative incidence of
o. Surgical site hematoma
oO Surgical site infection
o Vascular injury (peripheral or intracranial dissection, perforation or others) caused by
the endovascular procedure
e Successful reperfusion in the EVT group, defined as modified Thrombolysis in Cerebral Ischemia
(mTICI) grade of 2b or higher
e Discharge location
e Early neurological improvement, defined as improvement of 28 points on NIHSS at 24 hours of
presentation or an NIHSS of 0-1
e Quality of life score, as measured using NeuroQOL at 90-day follow-up
e The 1-year functional outcome, as measured by the modified Rankin Scale score*
e The rate of functional independence, defined as mRS score of 0-2 at 1-year follow-up*
e The rate of independent ambulation, defined as mRS score of 0-3 at 1-year follow-up*
e Quality of life score, as measured using NeuroQOL at 1 year follow-up*
*These outcomes will not be available at the time of primary manuscript and will be reported later in
separate manuscripts.
