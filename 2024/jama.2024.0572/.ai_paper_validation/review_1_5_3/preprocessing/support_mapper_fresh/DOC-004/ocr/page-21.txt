10.2.3.10.3 Treatment of missing values
Treatment of missing values will be performed similar to secondary outcome symptomatic ICH as
described in section 10.2.3.4.3.
10.2.3.10.4 Analysis method
The treatment effect will be expressed as
e The proportion of patients exhibiting early neurological improvement
e The relative risk with its 95% confidence interval and p-value, calculated using modified
Poisson regression
10.2.3.11 Secondary outcome — Quality of life score at 90-day follow-up
10.2.3.11.1 Outcome measure
The distribution of the quality of life score, measured using NeuroQoL.
10.2.3.11.2 Statistical hypothesis
The null hypothesis (to be rejected) is that the distribution of the quality of life score does not differ
significantly between patients assigned to EVT arm and patients assigned to MM arm.
10.2.3.11.3 Treatment of missing values
Missing data for length of the quality of life score will not be imputed.
10.2.3.11.4 Analysis method
The treatment effect will be expressed as
e The distribution of quality of life scores, using median (IQR)
e The difference in median values with its 95% confidence interval and p-value, obtained using
median regression
