# Stable Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and conventions

This independent inventory covers every numeric/reporting relationship mapped in `extraction/main_quantitative_evidence.md` and `extraction/support_quantitative_evidence.md`: all six supplied PDFs, 404 mapped PDF pages. It is a numeric-consistency inventory, not a candidate ledger. `N001` onward are stable relationship identifiers, never candidate IDs. Statistical-model relationships remain in scope when they contain a rate, count, unit, population, or displayed numerical result; the statistical passes retain responsibility for inferential compatibility.

Rounding rule used throughout: a value printed to one decimal is allowed an absolute rounding interval of 0.05 in its printed unit; a value printed to two decimals is allowed 0.005; a percentage printed to one decimal is allowed 0.05 percentage points. No rounding tolerance is applied to a directly conflicting label, denominator, or three-decimal repeated value unless the source identifies one value as a differently rounded display of the same unrounded estimate.
