# Limitations and Missing Definitions

The supplied package does not include participant-level data, analysis code, posterior draws, unrounded model output, covariance matrices, model-run identifiers, data-lock dates, production history, or full multiplicity-path output. These absences prevent resolution of the candidate questions but do not prevent the completed source-unit mapping and source-location rechecks.

For C002, the intended SVC estimand and time-scale convention are not defined. For C003–C005, the posterior-run and event-definition mapping is not supplied. For C006–C007, the unrounded interval outputs, table-specific rounding rules, and fitted back-transformed contrast definitions are not supplied. For C008, the Discussion does not define whether its event total uses the Table 2 endpoint and cutoff.

Reusable derivatives were used as locators and transcription aids; direct PDF pages were the authority for candidate confirmation. No web sources, external literature, source-file changes, or GPU processing were used.
