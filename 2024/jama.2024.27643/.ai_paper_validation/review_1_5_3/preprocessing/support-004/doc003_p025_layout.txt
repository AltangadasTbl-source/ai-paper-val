6.5.8   Additional Sensitivity Analyses of Primary and Key Secondary Outcomes
To evaluate the influence of extreme values on treatment estimates from the random-slopes
model, a robust linear mixed model that down-weights extreme values (Koller 2013) will be used
as a sensitivity analysis of primary and key secondary outcomes using the FAS analysis set. The
same fixed and random effects as specified for the primary random-slopes model in Section 6.5.3
will be used. A smoothed Huber ȡ-function will be used with a tuning parameter that yields 95%
asymptotic efficiency for normally distributed data.
As an alternative to CAFS for controlling the influence of potentially information loss to follow-
up due to death or other causes, sensitivity of estimated treatment effects to potentially
informative missingness will be analyzed using a composite endpoint of time to death and 24-
week change in ALSFRS-R total score using the FAS analysis set with imputation across a range
of weights spanning relative benefit or harm from the active treatment by exponential tilting
(Wang et al. 2017).
