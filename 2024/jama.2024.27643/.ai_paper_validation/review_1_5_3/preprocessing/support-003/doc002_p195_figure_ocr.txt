significant remyelination and neuroprotection benefits were observed in preclinical
neuroprotection models at mean AUC value of 30.6 (hr*ng/mL), which suggests a minimum
human equivalent exposure to achieve a positive bioenergetic response to CNM-Au8 of 15 — 30
mg. Increased AUC exposure of 50.3 (hr*ng/mL) was observed at the CNM-Au8 60 mg dose.
