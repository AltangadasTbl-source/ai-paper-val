# Canonical Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This is the canonical, stable numeric/reporting inventory for the numeric-consistency lane. It was rebuilt from the supplied direct PDFs and the current-run main and support evidence maps, not from legacy candidates or reports. The scope is DOC-001 pp. 1-11, DOC-002 pp. 1-46, DOC-003 pp. 1-9, and DOC-004 pp. 1-48. Direct PDF pages are authoritative; current-run layout/native text and rendered pages were used only as locating and transcription aids.

`N` identifiers are new canonical numeric relationship identifiers. Inferential estimate/CI/P-value compatibility is assigned to the statistical lane, but numeric inputs, denominators, scale, population, unit, and cross-location occurrences needed for those checks are retained here. A relationship marked `CHECKED—NO CANDIDATE` reconciled under the printed rule, stated rounding tolerance, or a directly stated non-mutual-exclusivity rule. A `CANDIDATE EMITTED` entry is a pending human-adjudication question only; it has no stable candidate ID, severity, validity decision, or disposition.

## Coverage result

The inventory contains **60 canonical N relationships**: 55 checked numeric/reporting relationships, 4 table-label/denominator candidates, and 1 explicitly out-of-result contextual relationship. It covers participant flow, all tabulated count/percentage/available-N and unit relationships, outcomes/safety count relationships, subgroup count families, supplementary table totals and missingness, planned population/scale/unit definitions, and planned-versus-observed matching keys. Inferential computations are deliberately cross-referenced to the statistical lane rather than duplicated here.

## Limitations

- DOC-002 and DOC-004 are SAP/protocol sources; their blank result templates define intended terms but do not supply observed results.
- No source supplies row-level data, so group-level identities are checked only against printed totals and explicit definitions.
- The table does not explain whether more than one surgeon can be recorded for operating, fascia closure, or skin closure. That missing definition is preserved as a human question in the checker rather than assumed away.
