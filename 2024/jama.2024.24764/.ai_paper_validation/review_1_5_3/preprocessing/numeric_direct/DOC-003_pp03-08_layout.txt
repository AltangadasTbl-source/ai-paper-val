Supplementary eFigure 2. Definition of CDC criteria

 SSI within 30 days of emergency laparotomy, as defined by the
 internationally accredited Centers for Disease Control (CDC) criteria

 The infection must occur within 30-days of the index operation

 AND at least one of:
  • Purulent drainage from the wound

  • Organisms are detected from a wound swab
  • Wound opened              At the surgical wound, the patient has at least one of:
    spontaneously       AND      ○ pain or tenderness     ○ redness         ○ heat
    or by a clinician            ○ localised swelling     ○ systemic fever (>38°C)
  • Diagnosis of SSI by a clinician or on imaging
