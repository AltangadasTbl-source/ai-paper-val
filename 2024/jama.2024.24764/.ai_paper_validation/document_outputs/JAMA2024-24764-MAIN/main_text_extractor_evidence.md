## Outcome definitions and analysis context

| Evidence | Location |
|---|---|
| Primary outcome: surgical site infection (SSI) up to 30 days postprocedure (day 0 = operation), assessed using US CDC criteria. Both superficial and deep incisional SSI were included; either/both were coded as binary yes. Wound assessments were on postoperative days 7-10 (or discharge if earlier), before any reoperation, and as close as possible after day 30; the 30-day assessor was masked. Participants who died, withdrew, or were lost after an SSI were counted as an SSI; those without an SSI who left before the 30-day assessment were classified as missing 30-day outcome data. | PDF p. 4 (printed p. 856), **Outcomes** |
| Seven secondary outcomes: hospital length of stay (LOS); wound-related (excluding SSI) and other complications through 30 days, graded by 6-level Clavien-Dindo; wound-related readmission within 30 days; pain at primary laparotomy site at days 7 and 30; SF-12 at days 7 and 30; EQ-5D-5L at days 7, 14, 21, and 30; and serious adverse events (SAEs), including postoperative mortality to day 30. | PDF p. 4 (printed p. 856), **Outcomes** |
| Primary analyses used complete cases and intention-to-treat allocation. Primary SSI comparison: mixed-effects binomial regression, adjusted for minimization variables (center random effect); log link for adjusted RR and identity link for adjusted risk difference (RD). All treatment estimates were presented with 95% CIs. | PDF p. 4 (printed p. 856), **Statistical Analysis** |
| Prespecified primary-outcome subgroups: operative contamination, stoma, operative procedure, skin preparation, BMI, country, assessment method, and randomization before/on or after March 11, 2020. Effects were examined with subgroup-by-treatment interaction terms. | PDF p. 4 (printed p. 856), **Statistical Analysis**; Figure 2, PDF p. 9 (printed p. 861) |
