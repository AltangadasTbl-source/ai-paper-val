Supplementary eFigure 2. Definition of CDC criteria

SSI within 30 days of emergency laparotomy, as defined by the
internationally accredited Centers for Disease Control (CDC) criteria

The infection must occur within 30-days of the index operation

AND at least one of:
• Purulent drainage from the wound
• Organisms are detected from a wound swab
• Wound opened
spontaneously
or by a clinician
AND
At the surgical wound, the patient has at least one of:
○ pain or tenderness
○ localised swelling
○ redness ○ heat
○ systemic fever (>38°C)
• Diagnosis of SSI by a clinician or on imaging

Supplementary eFigure 3. Sensitivity analysis forest plot

Sensitivity analysis 1:
Analysis of the ITT population and excluding any 30-day forms completed before day 30 post-surgery
but includes any 30-day forms completed after day 30

Sensitivity analysis 2:
Analysis of the ITT population and only including 30-day forms completed within 30-44 days (i.e. +14
day time-window) post-surgery

Sensitivity analysis 3:
Analysis of the ITT population excluding any SSI’s identified from the patient diary only

Sensitivity analysis 4:
Analysis of the ITT population excluding any participants that return to theatre where the participant
has not already had an SSI diagnosed

Sensitivity analysis 5:
Analysis of the ITT analysis population with missing data imputed as
• SSI (worst case) for the iNPWT group and No SSI (best case) for control group

Sensitivity analysis 6:
Analysis of the ITT analysis population with missing data imputed as
No SSI (best case) for the iNPWT group and SSI (worst case) for control group

Supplementary eFigure 4. Tipping point analysis
Graph A

Graph B

Graph A demonstrates that even if all missing data are imputed as SSI in the SUNPD group
and no SSI in the control group, this will still not provide a result that statistically favours the
control group.

Similarly graph B demonstrates that even if all missing data are imputed as SSI in the control
group and no SSI in the SUNPD group, this will still not provide a result that statistically
favours the SUNPD group.
