## Outcome definitions and analysis population

| Item | Exact reported evidence and location |
|---|---|
| Primary outcome | “Treatment success at 6 months” at the patient level. Success required control of inflammation in both eyes (or the single evaluable eye), defined as: “(1) less than or equal to 0.5+ anterior chamber cells … less than or equal to 0.5+ vitreous haze … and no active retinal or choroidal lesions; and (2) no more than 7.5 mg of oral prednisone daily and less than or equal to 2 drops of prednisolone acetate 1% … per day; and (3) no declaration of treatment failure due to intolerability or safety concerns.” **PDF p. 3, Outcomes section.** |
| Randomized population | 216 patients randomized: 107 to methotrexate and 109 to mycophenolate mofetil. The abstract reports median age 38 years and 135 women (62.5%). **PDF p. 1, Results; PDF p. 3, Figure 1; PDF p. 4, Results.** |
| Eye population | 407 eyes with uveitis at randomization: 206 in the methotrexate group and 201 in the mycophenolate group. The Results state that six eyes were unevaluable due to phthisis and 19 had no uveitis history. **PDF p. 3, Figure 1; PDF p. 4, Results.** |
| Primary-analysis population | 194 patients: methotrexate 96 (185 uveitic eyes), mycophenolate 98 (181 uveitic eyes). The Results state that 22/216 (10.2%) were lost to follow-up. **PDF p. 3, Figure 1; PDF p. 4, Results; PDF p. 6, Table 2.** |
| Baseline anatomical subgroups | Intermediate/anterior-plus-intermediate uveitis: 46/216 (21.3%); posterior uveitis/panuveitis: 170/216 (78.7%). Baseline table components: methotrexate 15 anterior+intermediate and 8 intermediate only; mycophenolate 14 and 9, respectively; posterior/panuveitis methotrexate 24/60 and mycophenolate 25/61. **PDF p. 4, Results; PDF p. 5, Table 1.** |
| Prespecified secondary outcomes reported in article | Six-month treatment success by anatomical subtype and enrollment site/country; 12-month success among patients continuing randomized therapy and among switchers; six-month visual acuity and central subfield macular thickness. **PDF p. 3–4, Outcomes section.** |

The Results prose reports: “Nonserious liver function test abnormalities occurred more frequently in patients receiving methotrexate (14 [13.0%]) vs mycophenolate mofetil (8 [7.4%]).” It also reports 14 serious adverse events through the primary outcome, five deemed drug-related by the masked medical monitor: **3/107 (2.8%)** methotrexate and **2/109 (1.9%)** mycophenolate; “All drug-related serious adverse events were elevated liver function tests.” **PDF p. 7, Adverse Events section; PDF p. 8, Table 3 and footnote c.**

## Abstract-level summary result evidence

The abstract reports that **194/216 (89.8%)** completed follow-up through six months, repeats the primary and anatomical-subgroup numerical results, and states: “Elevated liver enzymes were the most common nonserious laboratory adverse event, occurring in 14 patients (13.0%) in the methotrexate group and 8 patients (7.4%) in the mycophenolate group.” **PDF p. 1, Results.**
