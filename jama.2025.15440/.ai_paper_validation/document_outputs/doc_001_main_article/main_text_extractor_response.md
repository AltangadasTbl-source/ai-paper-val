## Outcome definitions and analysis population

- **Primary outcome:** proportion with AF present in primary-care records within 2.5 years after randomization; intention-to-treat. **Location:** PDF p3, Methods - Outcomes; source text `normalized_text/page_003.txt`.
- **Secondary outcome assessments:** intention-to-treat primary-outcome comparisons by age (<80, >=80 years) and separately by sex. Sensitivity analyses used AF diagnoses in either primary or secondary care records. **Location:** PDF p3, Methods - Outcomes; `normalized_text/page_003.txt`.
- **Exploratory outcomes:** time to AF detection; time with known AF; anticoagulation exposure; numbers/proportions of stroke and all-cause/cardiovascular death. **Location:** PDF p3, Methods - Outcomes; `normalized_text/page_003.txt`.
- **Randomized population:** 5,040 total; patch 2,520 and usual care 2,520. Mean (SD) age 78 (6) years; 2,360 (47%) female; median (IQR) CHA2DS2-VASc 4 (3-5). **Location:** PDF p1, structured abstract - Results; PDF p4, Results and Table; `normalized_text/page_001.txt`, `page_004.txt`, `page_005.txt`.

- Estimated assessed for eligibility: 368,000; invited: 22,044; excluded before inclusion because GP practice participated in another trial: 1,186; included: 20,858; replies: 5,116; excluded after reply: 76 (72 negative reply; 4 positive reply with incomplete consent/no response); randomized: 5,040. **Location:** Figure 1, PDF p4; `normalized_text/page_004.txt`; image `page_images/page_004.png`.
- Patch allocation: 2,520 randomized; 2,126 patch ECG data; 394 no patch data (206 not returned; 188 returned unworn); all 2,520 included in ITT analysis. **Location:** Figure 1, PDF p4; `normalized_text/page_004.txt`; image `page_images/page_004.png`.
- Usual-care allocation: 2,520 randomized; all 2,520 included in ITT analysis. **Location:** Figure 1, PDF p4; `normalized_text/page_004.txt`; image `page_images/page_004.png`.
- Complete primary-care follow-up at 2.5 y: patch 2,408/2,520 (95.6%) and usual care 2,410/2,520 (95.6%). Incomplete: patch 112 (106 moved to nonparticipating GP; 6 withdrew consent), usual care 110 (103 moved; 7 withdrew). Figure 1 lists patch 2,310 alive/98 died and usual care 2,292 alive/118 died in the follow-up boxes. **Location:** PDF p4, Results and Figure 1; `normalized_text/page_004.txt`; `page_images/page_004.png`.
- Complete secondary-care data: patch 2,514/2,520 (99.8%); usual care 2,512/2,520 (99.7%). **Location:** PDF p4, Results; `normalized_text/page_004.txt`.

## Presented conclusions relevant to results

- The structured abstract concludes that mail-based screening produced a modest long-term increase in AF diagnosis at 2.5 years. **Location:** PDF p1, Conclusions and Relevance; `normalized_text/page_001.txt`.
- The article conclusion states that at 2.5 years the strategy increased AF diagnosis and anticoagulation exposure; more than half of patch-detected AF had burden below 10%; it suggests limited impact on stroke events at 2.5 years. **Location:** PDF p8, Conclusions; `normalized_text/page_008.txt`.
