# Canonical Numeric and Reporting Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This inventory canonically assigns `N001` through `N034` to every distinct numeric or reporting relationship mapped in the current-run main and support evidence maps. It covers DOC-001 (main article, PDF pp. 1-11), DOC-002 (trial protocol, PDF pp. 1-15), and DOC-003 (online supplement, PDF pp. 1-49). Direct PDFs are authoritative; current-run extraction is a source locator and transcription aid. Provisional mapper identifiers (`MN`, `SN`, and relevant `MS`/`SS`) are retained only as provenance. No legacy candidate, checker, report, or adjudication output was read.

`S` identifiers remain the canonical home for inferential relationships. An `N` record cross-references an `S` record only where the numeric denominator, rate/count distinction, scale, interval, or label is necessary for a numeric consistency check.
