Domain
Quality of life

Scale and scale characteristics
COPD Assessment Test1
•
Scoring: 8 Items each scaled 1 to 5. Total scores range from 0-40, high scores show
more severe impact of COPD on a patient’s life.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: Not determined. Mapping against St. George’s
Respiratory Questionnaire suggests minimal clinically important difference at a group
level is 1.6. Experts also suggest that scores <10 have a low impact, 10-20 medium,
21-30 high and >30 very high impact, requiring gradually more interventions.
Chronic Respiratory Disease Questionnaire2
•
Scoring: 20 total items across 4 components (dyspnea, fatigue, emotional function, and
mastery), all items scored from 1 to 7. Higher score represents better outcome.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: a change in score of 0.5 on a 7 point scale.
St. George’s Respiratory Questionnaire3
•
Scoring: 2 parts (symptoms and activities [activity and impacts]) 50 total items. Total
scores range from 0-100, high scores indicating more limitations.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: a mean change score of 4 units is associated
with slightly efficacious treatment, 8 units for moderately efficacious change and 12
units for very efficacious treatment.
Severe Respiratory Insufficiency Questionnaire Summary Score4
•
Scoring: 49 total items, 7 categories. Total score 0 to 100. Higher scores represent
better health related quality of life.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: The minimal clinically important difference
ranged between 5 and 7 points in patients with severe stable COPD.5

Gronigen Activity and Restriction Scale6
daily living
•
Scoring: 18 Items, total scores 18-72. Higher score shoes more disabled.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: not found.
London Chest Activity of Daily Living Scale7
•
Scoring: 15 total items with 4 components (self-care, domestic, physical, leisure). Each
item scores 0-5.Total score from 0-75. Higher scores represent minimum disability.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: Minimally important differences ranged from -2.1
to -5.9 points.
Dyspnea
Chronic Respiratory Disease Questionnaire Dyspnea2
•
Scoring: Dyspnea subscale, 5 activities identified as important by the patient are rated
from 1(most dyspnea) to 7(least dyspnea).
•
Minimal clinically important difference: 0.5 per item within a subscale.
Medical Research Council Dyspnoea scale8
•
Scoring: Grade 1-5. Higher grade is extreme breathlessness.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: not found, but may be 0.5 units9
Transitional Dyspnea Index10
•
Scoring: 24 total items with three domains(change in functional impairment, change in
magnitude of task, change in magnitude of effort) Items rate in 7 graded from -3 to
+3(major improvement) Total scores ranging -9 to +9. Lower score shows more
deterioration.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: Change of ≥1 unit.
Sleep
Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index11
•
Scoring: 19 total items scales from 0-3. Overall scale from 0-21. Lower scores shows
better sleep quality.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: not found.
Semiqualitative multipoint scale with a range 1(best) to 4(worst)12
•
Scoring: 1-4, Higher score means worse sleep quality.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: not found.
Epworth Sleepiness Scale13
•
Scoring: 8 questions scored from 0-3. Total scores 0-24. Higher score indicates severe
sleepiness.
•
Minimal clinically important difference: not found for COPD.
