### DOC-002 quantitative/statistical definitions to match later results

- **SN001 — Eligibility/time/population definition.** Adults aged >=18 years with chronic respiratory failure; home or assisted-living setting; treatment >=1 month; literature from 1995. Source: DOC-002 pp. 5-10.
- **SS001 — Meta-analysis inclusion and model rule.** More than two studies with same PICOTS plus point estimates and dispersion measures; DerSimonian-Laird random effects at k >18, Knapp-Hartung-adjusted variance otherwise. Source: DOC-002 p. 11.
- **SS002 — Heterogeneity, subgroup, sensitivity, and publication-bias rules.** I2 for heterogeneity; subgroup factors in Section II; sensitivity exclusion of high-RoB studies; funnel plots/Egger only at n >=20. Source: DOC-002 p. 11.
- **SN002 — Protocol pressure and duration thresholds.** PaO2 >=60 mm Hg and PaCO2 <=45 mm Hg definitions; duration subgroups 1/3/6 months and longer. Source: DOC-002 pp. 2 and 7.

## Candidate observations, not stable IDs and not adjudications

### CO-001 — Provisional cross-table total mismatch: Cheung 2010 RCT

**Direct observation:** DOC-003 eTable 6 gives Cheung 2010 CPAP n=24 and BPAP-ST n=23 on PDF p. 19, yielding 47 participants in the displayed two-group baseline row. DOC-003 eTable 10 reports the matched BPAP versus CPAP exacerbation comparison as “1 RCT17; 49 patients” on PDF p. 43.

**Diagnostic:** The exact row comparison, author/year, intervention pair, and citation number 17 identify the same study. Baseline total is 24+23=47, whereas the effectiveness-table total is 49. The percentage pair and effect estimates on p. 43 may reflect an analysis population not stated in the eTable 6 row; the support files do not supply an explanation.

**Human question:** Does the 49-patient effectiveness denominator include two randomized participants absent from the displayed baseline/intervention groups, or is either table total a reporting/transcription error? This is a provisional candidate observation only; it has no C ID and remains pending human adjudication.
