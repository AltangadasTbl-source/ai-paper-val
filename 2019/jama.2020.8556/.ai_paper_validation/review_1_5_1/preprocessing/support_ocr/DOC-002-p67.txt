6.4.8 RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN NUTRITIONAL STATUS, FRAILTY, CHANGE IN CAPACITY
STATUS, AND RISK OF DEATH
We will conduct exploratory analysis to investigate the association between the risk of death and
changes in nutritional status, frailty, and capacity.
6.4.9 AGREEMENT BETWEEN SELF-REPORT AND PROXY EQ-5D AND ICECAP-O MEASURES
For participants with both self-report and proxy EQ-5D (utility and VAS) and ICECAP-O measures, we
will assess the agreement between these two measures by calculating weighted kappa statistics,
intraclass correlation coefficients, and Bland-Altman plots for categorical and continuous measures
respectively.
6.5 HARMS
The number of serious adverse reaction (SAR) will be reported by their relationship as ‘definitely’ and
‘probably’ related to the study product. The number of participants experiencing SAE will be compared
descriptively by trial arms. For-each participant, only the maximum severity experienced SAE will be
displayed. The number of occurrences of each SAE will also be presented for each trial arm. No formal
statistical testing will be undertaken.
6.6 STATISTICAL SOFTWARE
Data will be cleaned and managed using IBM SPSS Statistics version 20 or later. Formal statistical
analyses will be performed in Stata version 13 or later.
