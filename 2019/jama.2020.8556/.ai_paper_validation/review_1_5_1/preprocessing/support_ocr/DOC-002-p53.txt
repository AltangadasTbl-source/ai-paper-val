4.2 ADHERENCE AND PROTOCOL DEVIATIONS
4.2.1 DEFINITION AND ASSESSSMENT OF ADHERENCE
Data regarding participants’ adherence to the study product will be collected from a number of
sources:
© MAR sheets - administration of the study product will be recorded by care home staff using
Medication Administration Record sheets (MAR sheets) and monitored by RNs during their
visits to each care home.
e Presence of probiotic organisms in bowel - the presence of the probiotic organisms will be
assessed in stool samples of participants at baseline, three months and final follow-up.
e Capsule count - counts of unused study product will be undertaken by the PRINCESS trial team
at the end of the study for each participant (i.e. final follow-up, withdrawal, or death).
4.2.2 PRESENTATION OF ADHERENCE
Descriptive statistics will be reported both overall and split by trial arm.
Our primary measure of adherence will be based on MAR sheets, as it is the only measure that is able
to provide data regarding patterns in adherence over time (i.e. daily consumption and time of
consumption). Capsule counts and presence of probiotic organisms in the bowel are measures that
will be used to verify adherence data obtained from the MAR sheets. Exploratory analyses will
consider the agreement between the different types of measures.
For each participant, the total number of days they took the study product will be expressed in a
summary form as a percentage, with the denominator being the total number of days they were
observed (i.e. still taking part in the trial, regardless of whether or not they had withdrawn from the
study product).
This will be summarised both overall and between trial arms as means and standard deviations or
medians and interquartile ranges, as appropriate.

Initiation: A participant will have been deemed as having initiated their Study Product if the data
collected from MAR sheets indicates they used it at least once during the follow-up period. This will
be summarised as frequencies and percentages.
