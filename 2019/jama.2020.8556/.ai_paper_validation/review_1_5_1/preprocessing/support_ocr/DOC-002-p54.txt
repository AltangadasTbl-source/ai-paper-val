4.2.3 DEFINITION OF PROTOCOL DEVIATION
The definition of protocol deviation is any change, divergence, or departure from the study design or
procedures defined in the protocol. important protocol deviations are a subset of protocol deviations
that may significantly impact the completeness, accuracy, and/or reliability of the study data or that
may significantly affect a subject's rights, safety, or well-being. Protocol deviations of relevance (and
that will not be identified under other sections, such as adherence or missing data issues) for the
PRINCESS study include:

e Inclusion of an individual not meeting eligibility criteria;

e Administering of incorrect study product to a participant (i.e. study product with another

individual's ID).

4.2.4 PRESENTATION OF PROTOCOL DEVIATIONS
Protocol deviations will be reported for all participants and separately for each trial arm and by site.
The frequency of any protocol deviation and frequency of different types will be reported.
4.3 ANALYSIS POPULATION
Primary and secondary comparative analyses will be conducted on an intention-to-treat (ITT) basis
without imputation of missing values {complete case set). The ITT population uses all randomised
participants in the arms they were randomised to regardless of the intervention received and the
complete case population (CC) restricts the population to include all randomised participants with
complete follow-up (specific to the outcome and time points).
