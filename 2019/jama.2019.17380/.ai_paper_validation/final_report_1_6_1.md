## C001 — Planned and reported adherence sensitivity definitions differ

**Pending Human Adjudication**

**Candidate statement:** The reported questionnaire-based adherence restriction differs from the implemented plan's continuous-at-least-80% restriction for primary-outcome robustness analyses.

**Review domain:** protocol_report

**Relationship type:** P_R

**All relationships:** P_R

**Compared document roles:** PLAN protocol p.19 and addendum p.33 vs REPORT main pp.3-4 and results supplement pp.6,10,13

**Evidence state:** reproduced

**Inclusion audit:** CONFIRMED

**Finding IDs:** F-XS-004

Duplicate checker provenance: F-ST1-003, screened out as DUPLICATE_OF_INCLUDED.

**Classification rationale:** Prospective adherence restrictions are PLAN; applied definitions/results are REPORT. Their comparison is P_R and protocol_report. The original and implemented plans agree on 80%; the report locations agree with one another, so corroboration adds neither P_P nor S_C/S_W.

**Classification ambiguity:** No later adherence amendment is supplied.

**Exact source locations:** PLAN [joi190122supp1_prod.pdf — PDF p.19, §VI.g](<../joi190122supp1_prod.pdf#page=19>) and [PDF p.33, §15g](<../joi190122supp1_prod.pdf#page=33>); REPORT [jama_de_boer_2019_oi_190122.pdf — PDF p.3, Covariates/Data Analysis](<../jama_de_boer_2019_oi_190122.pdf#page=3>), [PDF p.4, adherence/primary robustness paragraphs](<../jama_de_boer_2019_oi_190122.pdf#page=4>), [joi190122supp2_prod.pdf — PDF p.6, eTable1 definition](<../joi190122supp2_prod.pdf#page=6>), [PDF p.10, eTable5 definition/results](<../joi190122supp2_prod.pdf#page=10>) and [PDF p.13, eTable8 definition/results](<../joi190122supp2_prod.pdf#page=13>).

**Source evidence:** The original plan specifies at least 80% medication adherence; the implemented addendum specifies continuous adherence at that threshold. The report defines high adherence as at least two-thirds medication use on at least four of six questionnaires at 6 months and years 1–5. Reported adherent N is 1032 per intervention: vitamin-D active/placebo 546/486 and omega-3 active/placebo 518/514. eTable5 gives five-year eGFR differences 0.89 (95% CI −0.74, 2.52), P=.28, and 0.42 (−1.22, 2.06), P=.61. eTable8 repeats the restriction for ACR ratios 1.02 (0.85, 1.22), P=.87, and 0.99 (0.83, 1.19), P=.94.

**Reported-versus-comparator:** At least two-thirds use at four of six assessments versus continuous use of at least 80%; both serve sensitivity analysis of the primary findings.

**Reasoning procedure:** Compare threshold and continuity predicates directly, then link the applied predicate to the cited sensitivity results. Do not infer participant-level membership from aggregate N.

**Calculation:** Two-thirds is 66.666…%, 13.333… percentage points below 80%. Even a nearest-integer interval for 80%, [79.5%, 80.5%), cannot overlap the exact two-thirds threshold. Four qualifying assessments do not imply continuous qualification. A hypothetical 70%-use participant qualifying on four assessments illustrates nonequivalence without asserting actual trial membership.

**Affected result or extracted value:** Adherence eligibility metadata attached to the two eTable5 primary-outcome sensitivity differences and the claim that findings remain similar among consistently highly adherent participants; eTable8 is an additional occurrence.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Both prospective and applied restrictions are explicit, and the report does not identify an amendment connecting them. Shared adherence terminology cannot establish equivalent numerical thresholds or continuity requirements.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** A later questionnaire-based amendment may exist, or the reported restriction may define a distinct sensitivity analysis. Neither linkage is supplied. Continuous-80% membership, its N and its result estimates remain unavailable.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** All cited pages, restriction texts, assessment timings, Ns and sensitivity tuples were found and directly reread; no transcription correction was needed. The recheck reproduces membership-rule nonequivalence, confirms PLAN/REPORT roles and P_R classification, and separates the observed definitions from hypothetical membership changes.

**Potential downstream evidence impact:** If confirmed by human review, an extractor could attach the planned continuous-80% label to results actually generated using the questionnaire restriction. No change in an estimate, conclusion or evidence synthesis is established.

**Human verification steps:** Confirm the operational adherence variable and any amendment connecting it to §15g; identify which participant set and results belong to each restriction if both were analyzed.

Neither an actual population difference nor an effect change is inferred.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** The implementation cover, definition, report methods, retention, Figure1 and Table2 were found and reread. The textual comparison and N=1312 reproduce without transcription correction. The recheck expressly preserves the nonexclusive alternative and unknown union; evidence state `reproduced` refers to the definition comparison. **Potential downstream evidence impact:** An extractor could classify the published primary population as matching the prospective biosample definition without documenting its unresolved scope. No established numerical population change, 222-person any-biosample exclusion, or changed imputed effect is claimed.
