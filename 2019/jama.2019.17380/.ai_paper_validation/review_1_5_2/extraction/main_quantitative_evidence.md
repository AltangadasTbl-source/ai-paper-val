## Study design, population, intervention, and analysis quantities

| PDF page | Location | Printed quantitative evidence / definition |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Abstract | 2x2 factorial randomized trial; 1312 adults, recruited November 2011-March 2014 from all 50 US states; follow-up completed December 2017. Four cells: vitamin D3+omega-3 n=370; vitamin D3+placebo n=333; placebo+omega-3 n=289; two placebos n=320; planned intervention duration 5 years. Vitamin D3 2000 IU/d; omega-3 1 g/d. |
| 1 | Abstract/Results | Randomized N=1312; mean age 67.6 y; 46% women; 31% racial/ethnic minority; 934 (71%) completed. Baseline mean eGFR 85.8 (SD 22.1) mL/min/1.73 m2. |
| 2 | Study design/population | Parent VITAL N=25,871; men >=50 y and women >=55 y; enrollment goal 1320 participants. |
| 2 | Interventions | Randomization blocks of 8, stratified by age, sex, and race. Vitamin D3 2000 IU; omega-3 capsule 1 g containing EPA 465 mg plus DHA 375 mg; nonstudy supplemental vitamin D limited to <=800 IU/d. |
| 3 | Outcomes/measurement | Primary outcome: eGFR change baseline to year 5. Samples at baseline, year 2, year 5. Secondary threshold outcomes: >=40% eGFR decline; final urine ACR >=30 mg/g after doubling; post hoc threshold >=30% eGFR decline. |
| 3 | Sample size | N=1320; 80% power to detect 2.3 mL/min/1.73 m2 difference over 5 y; two-sided alpha=.05; assumes 80% returning follow-up samples. Clinical/surrogate comparison: >=0.75 mL/min/1.73 m2/y = >=3.75 over 5 y. |
| 3 | Data analysis | Linear mixed model; time baseline/year 2/year 5; treatment-by-time interaction; eGFR model adjusted for age, sex, baseline urine ACR; treatment-by-year-5 interaction P test; two-tailed P<.05 significant. Full randomized analytic population; M=20 imputations combined by Rubin rules. Secondary Cox models. |
