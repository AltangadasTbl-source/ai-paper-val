## DOC-002 protocol and addendum definitions

- Original protocol p.17: primary continuous outcome was percent ACR change `[(ACR4-ACR0)/ACR0]`; ACR4 was year-4 and ACR0 baseline. The original secondary eGFR change was `[eGFR4-eGFR0]`. Equation 1 was `eGFR=177.6 × SCr^-0.65 × CysC^-0.57 × age^-0.20 × (0.82 if female) × (1.11 if black)`, with SCr mg/dL and CysC mg/L.
- Original protocol p.17: composite outcome components were ACR progression (100% increase and year-4 ACR >=30 mg/g), eGFR loss >=12 mL/min in 4 years (stated as 3 mL/min/year), ESRD (maintenance dialysis or medical-record verified transplant), and/or death.
- Original protocol pp.18-19: interaction ANCOVA used log(ACR4), log(ACR0), vitamin-D, omega-3, and joint-active indicator; beta4 P<.05 represented multiplicative interaction. In no-interaction analyses, each factor contrast pooled the two corresponding factorial cells. Continuous outcomes used ANCOVA; alpha=.05 without multiplicity correction; chi-square was specified for composite proportions; complete cases were planned for continuous outcomes.
- Original protocol pp.19-20: power assumptions: n=1,500, 1,200 follow-up (20% loss), equal allocation; log(ACR) mean 3.085, SD 1.59, baseline correlation .78; eGFR mean 70, SD 25 mL/min/1.73m2, correlation .834. The displayed power table gives ACR differences 15/17/19/21/23/25% with power 81/90/96/98/>99/>99%, and eGFR differences 2.0/2.2/2.4/2.6/2.8/3.0 with power 71/79/85/90/94/97%. With 10% loss, power for 17% and 2.6 is 93%; with 30% loss, 86%. Interaction comparisons were powered at 80% for 20% and 3.2. Expected placebo incidence: ACR progression ~17%, rapid eGFR loss ~17%, ESRD <1%, death ~4.6%, composite 20-30%; power 80% for RR .69-.76 and 90% for RR .65-.73.
- Addendum p.32: eGFR baseline-to-year-5 change supersedes the originally planned two-year changes as primary: `[eGFR5-eGFR0]`. Secondary outcomes: ACR change at years 2 and 5; time to >=40% eGFR loss at year 2 or 5; and time to composite of rapid eGFR loss, verified ESRD, or death. All participants with at least one follow-up biosample were to be analyzed irrespective of adherence; missing year-5 eGFR would use chained-equation multiple imputation with baseline plus year-2 eGFR/ACR; 10 data sets combined under Rubin rules.
- Addendum pp.32-33: linear mixed interaction model has categorical j=0,2,5, participant random effect, and active-vitamin-D, active-omega-3, their product, and time interactions; beta6 P<.05 was the interaction criterion. Primary mixed model beta3 is the difference in eGFR change over time active D3 vs placebo, with year-2 description but inferential testing reserved for year 5. ACR is log-transformed continuous; secondary categorical outcomes use discrete Cox models. Power simulation: 2,000 replications, n=1,058 (80%) contributing >=1 follow-up blood sample, two-sided alpha=.05, and 80% power for a 2.3 mL/min/1.73m2 year-5 eGFR difference.
