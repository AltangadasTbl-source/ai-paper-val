## C001 — Narrative minority percentage versus explicit race-category totals

**Category:** Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency

**Relationship type:** S_W

**All relationships:** S_W

**Compared document roles:** REPORT main article abstract vs REPORT main article Table 1

**Review domain:** statistical_results

**Evidence state:** recorded_candidate

**Suggested review disposition:** retain

**Local provenance key:** NUM-M01

**Source evidence:** REPORT, DOC-001 [PDF page 1](../../jama_de_boer_2019_oi_190122.pdf#page=1), abstract Results; REPORT, DOC-001 [PDF page 4](../../jama_de_boer_2019_oi_190122.pdf#page=4), Participant Characteristics; REPORT, DOC-001 [PDF page 5](../../jama_de_boer_2019_oi_190122.pdf#page=5), Table 1 Race/ethnicity rows and nonmissing denominator footnote. Printed: Narrative: 31% racial or ethnic minority. Table 1 nonmissing race N=361+327+284+314=1286; non-Hispanic white=240+211+199+207=857; other race/ethnicity categories total 429, including Other=9+8+2+9=28.

**Calculation and rule:** If minority means every non-Hispanic-white category in the displayed race/ethnicity table, its narrative percentage must match that denominator. Derived: (1286−857)/1286×100=33.3593%, rounding to 33%; 429/1312×100=32.6982%, also 33%; excluding Other gives 401/1286×100=31.18196%, rounding 31%. Tolerance: ±0.5 percentage points for integer display.

**Alternatives and missing definition:** A source-specific minority definition may exclude the 28 Other responses. That exactly explains 31%, but the main article does not explicitly define this exclusion. This is a conditional definition/denominator question, not a claim that the percentage necessarily is wrong.

**Human question:** Does the reported 31% exclude the Other category, and what exact minority numerator and denominator were used?

**Checker provenance:** [numeric_main.md](checkers/parts/numeric_main.md), NUM-M01; N009. Pending Human Adjudication.

**Classification rationale:** Confirmed: both compared locations contain results within the same REPORT document; no PLAN discrepancy or second REPORT document is needed. S_W is the sole relationship.
