## Matching rules and statistical definitions

Compare like outcome, population, intervention, time and model; all sensitivity tables deliberately use different populations. eTables4/5 match main primary eGFR analysis only as sensitivity results, not identical estimates. eTables6-9 match urine ACR geometric change ratio and Table3 categorical ACR outcomes only with outcome-type distinction. eTable10 contains post-hoc ≥40% decline OR kidney failure and ≥30% composite variants; its ≥40% OR kidney failure row differs from ≥40% decline alone. eTable11 cells are participants with ≥1 report, not counts of episodes or incidence rates. eTables2/3 main medication matching key: medication class + baseline/year2/year5 + assignment; class use is not mutually exclusive. Percentages use nonmissing responses and follow-up denominator differs from column baseline N.

Original protocol pp17-20 uses baseline/year4 ACR primary and eGFR secondary; addendum pp31-33 explicitly changes hierarchy/time and analysis. Date and disclosed amendment must accompany every plan/report matching exercise. Original plan adherence ≥80% pp19 and amended continuous adherence ≥80% p33 differ by definition from results supplement ≥2/3 at ≥4 of6 questionnaires. Record printed definitions before comparisons. Original and amended subgroup effect directions are separate version keys, including original non-Caucasian versus amended Caucasian hypothesis.

Amended p32 intent-to-treat describes all participants returning ≥1 follow-up biosample; missing year5 eGFR chained-equation MI using baseline, year2 eGFR and ACR; 10 datasets combined by Rubin rules. Main mixed-model treatment contrasts compare active against corresponding placebo at year5. Addendum model time categorical j=0,2,5; patient random effect; interaction β6 P<.05 determines three arms versus double-placebo or factorial pooled comparison. Inference reserved year5; year2 descriptive treatment effect. ACR log-transformed continuous variable; secondary categorical outcomes discrete Cox. Main statistical checker should reconcile inferential definitions against main report rather than assume every inference has same test.

Each of eTables4-9 has 6 arm-time rows, four non-reference treatment contrasts (year2 and year5 for vitamin D and omega3), 12 marginal mean-CI cells and 8 within-arm change-CI cells; each table's year5 contrasts have P, year2 do not. eTable10 has eight HR/95% CI/P plus incidence-rate and rate-difference intervals. eFigures2/3 have 15 and11 graph rows respectively and interaction P by subgroup block, without printed exact ratio/CI endpoint values. Record graphical intervals against their drawn scale only; exact endpoints are absent. Count of support statistical relationships exceeds 120 if every marginal mean-CI and change-CI cell is separate. Downstream checker may group explicit table cells for coverage, but must cover every cell and every inference.
