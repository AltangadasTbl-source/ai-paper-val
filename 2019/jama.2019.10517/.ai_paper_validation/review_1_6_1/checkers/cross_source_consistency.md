**Printed statements:** The PLAN makes the HDRS/HAM-D relapse component a 17-item score≥18 **and** an absolute increase of5 points on at least one visit relative to RCT entry (the source prints “a mean absolute increase of 5 points”). The REPORT says declaring relapse required at least one of its four components, whose second is simply “17-item HDRS score of 18 or higher.” The same REPORT allows near-remission with HDRS11–15 at randomization. Thus the eligibility context does not establish universal redundancy. No numerical rounding convention can remove a conjunction or reconcile integer-score threshold definitions.
