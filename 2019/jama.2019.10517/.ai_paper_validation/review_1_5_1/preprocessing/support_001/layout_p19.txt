877   4.1.5 Governance: The Steering Committee, composed of the 4 principal investigators and Dr. Mulsant, and
878   co-chaired by Drs. Meyers and Flint, will be responsible for oversight of the research. The committee will have
879   weekly telephone conference calls that will review progress at the 4 sites, including recruitment and retention
880   of participants, adherence to study procedures, and the safety of participants. Protocol violations, serious
881   adverse events, and obstacles to the study’s progress will be discussed. Email will be used for as-needed
882   communication between conference calls, unless an urgent operational issue dictates the scheduling of a
883   special conference call. The Steering Committee will receive and review reports from the inter-site project
884   coordinator about results of reliability testing and the status of reliability training exercises. The Steering
885   Committee will also meet in person for a 1-day meeting, at a minimum frequency of once per year. A
886   publications sub-committee will be composed of the principal investigators and Dr. Mulsant as standing
887   members; co-investigators will be invited on an ad hoc basis, as appropriate. The publication sub-committee
888   will plan for data analyses, presentation of results at scientific meetings, and manuscript preparation, in
889   collaboration with the data manager and statistician, and will make decisions about authorship. All
890   manuscripts using project data will be reviewed and approved by the Publications Sub-committee before
891   submission. In the event of any disagreement pertaining to the study amongst the members of the Steering
892   Committee, that is not resolved through discussion, a vote will be taken and the majority will prevail.

895   4.1.6 Reliability and Quality Control:
896   Rater Training and Rater Reliability. Research associates at each site will be trained by the site’s study
897   coordinator and primary investigator in the administration of the instruments. General oversight of this training
898   will be provided by the Coordinating Center’s Project Coordinator, in collaboration with Dr. Flint and Dr.
899   Meyers. The project coordinator will have biweekly conference calls with Dr. Meyers and Dr. Flint to report on
900   study progress. Training of research associates at each site will involve the following: the observation and
901   discussion of training videotapes supplied by the Cornell Coordinating Center pertaining to the primary
902   instruments (SCID, HAM-D, DAS, and SSI); in-person observation of interviews conducted by research
903   psychiatrists and experienced research coordinators; and in-person supervision of training interviews
904   conducted by research associates. A 2-day launch meeting will take place at the Cornell Coordinating Center
905   prior to the start of the study, to solidify this training and to review rating conventions of each scale. Meryl
906   Butters Ph.D. will attend the launch meeting to train the research team on the proper administration of the
907   neuropsychological instruments. Research associates will receive ongoing supervision from research
908   coordinators and research psychiatrists at each site. In addition, the inter-site Project Coordinator will lead
909   weekly conference calls with the site study coordinators and research associates. These calls will address
910   subject recruitment and retention, training and reliability of research personnel, the completeness and accuracy
911   of data entry, and other quality assurance issues.
912
913   Inter-rater reliability of the HAM-D and DAS will be assessed on an annual basis. Reliability assessments will
914   require research associates and research coordinators at each center to independently rate 12 videotaped
915   interviews of patients with varying levels of severity of psychotic depression. Inter-rater reliability assessments
916   performed during each year of STOP-PD yielded ICCs of 0.93-0.98 for the GRID HAM-D total score and 0.69-
917   0.84 for the conviction dimension of the DAS, demonstrating good reliability for these primary measures. One-
918   day working meetings in Years 2-4 will be attended by the principal investigators, research coordinators and
919   associates, and inter-site Project Coordinator to address study operational issues, rater training, and inter-rater
920   reliability.
