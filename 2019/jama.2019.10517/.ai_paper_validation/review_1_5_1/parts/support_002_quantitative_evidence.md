## Direct-source quantitative evidence

### DOC-002 protocol and administrative quantities

- PDF p.23: historical STOP-PD statement, “no more than 7 of the 259 participants had Type 1 diabetes”; this is a count with denominator 259, not an outcome rate. The same page states 5% had received at least a three-week adequate combined antidepressant/antipsychotic trial before the index study.
- PDF p.24: side effects described as occurring in more than 10% of STOP-PD participants. A separate external-risk context states an estimated 0.9% increased absolute mortality risk over 2-3 years for olanzapine users across diagnostic groups. The protocol estimates overall RCT relapse risk as approximately 25%; an ECT statement gives up to 50% relapse in six months after discontinuation. These are design/background quantities, not reported STOP-PD II results.
- PDF pp.25-27: acute treatment lasts 12 weeks; failure to meet remission removes a participant from stabilization and the RCT. Significant worsening is CGI change score 6 or 7. RCT assessments are weekly for eight weeks and then every two weeks; anticipated relapse frequency is 3/site/year. Metabolic referral thresholds are fasting glucose >100 mg/dL, triglycerides >150 mg/dL, total cholesterol >200 mg/dL, and LDL cholesterol >130 mg/dL. SSI items 4 or 5 scored 1 or 2 define current ideation without plan/attempt for the stated management pathway.
- PDF p.28: quarterly adverse-event/recruitment/withdrawal reports; SAE notification within 48 hours; planned sample is 50% elderly and female:male approximately 2:1. These are operational/planned quantities, not analysis results.
- PDF p.29, Table 5: Race rows sum to 12 + 29 + 218 = 259. Ethnicity rows sum to 30 + 229 = 259. Percentages are consistent with whole-person completion counts after one-decimal rounding: Asian 9/12 and 6/12; Black 22/29 and 15/29; White 163/218 and 121/218; Hispanic 24/30 and 16/30; not Hispanic 169/229 and 126/229. The table does not state that race and ethnicity categories are mutually exclusive, so these are two separate partitions, not rows to add across both sections.
- PDF p.30: proposed recruitment is 38 months, about 10 blood samples/month; clinical data submitted every six months. Reported historical inter-rater ICC ranges are 0.93-0.98 (GRID HAM-D) and 0.69-0.84 (DAS conviction) across each year of a five-year STOP-PD. These are reliability ranges, not treatment-effect estimates.
- PDF pp.39-40: acute dosing is internally sequenced: 50/5 mg/day initially, 100/10 mg/day on day 4, and 150/15 mg/day on day 7, with a target within 14 days if tolerable. Inadequate psychosis after seven days at olanzapine 15 mg/day permits 20 mg/day; inadequate depressive improvement after four weeks of sertraline 150 mg/day (partial improvement defined as 17-item HAM-D <16) permits 200 mg/day. Minimum continuing doses after intolerance are sertraline 50 mg/day and olanzapine 5 mg/day. Historical end-of-week-2 means are sertraline 150 mg/day (both age groups) and olanzapine 14 mg younger/13 mg older.
- PDF pp.40-42: stabilization lasts eight weeks. Sertraline changes are 50-mg increments every seven days (50-200 mg/day); olanzapine changes are 5-mg increments every seven days (5-20 mg/day). Before randomization, sertraline exposure is stated as minimum 10 and maximum 20 weeks. During RCT, olanzapine/placebo may change by one 5-mg tablet every seven days within 5-20 mg/day. Lorazepam maximum is 3 mg/day and benztropine maximum is 2 mg/day. The historical 5/259 = 1.9305%, which rounds to printed 1.9%.
