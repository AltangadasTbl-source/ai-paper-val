708   the study, we will report the number and % of participants who meet criteria for specific adverse events, who
709   did not meet these criteria at acute phase baseline. Adverse events will be defined as: an increase of ≥2 points
710   from baseline or a score of 3 on individual items of the UKU; a global severity score of ≥2 on the AIMS; a
711   global assessment score of ≥2 on the Barnes Akathisia scale; a mean score of ≥2 on the Simpson Angus
712   Scale; a score of ≥1 on item #4 or #5 of the SSI; a reduction in systolic blood pressure of ≥20 mm Hg or in
713   diastolic blood pressure of ≥10 mm Hg within 3 minutes of standing; one or more falls. We will also report the
714   N and % of individuals who meet criteria for an adverse event from week 8 onwards of the RCT, to allow for
715   the possibility of improvement in adverse effects once open-label phase olanzapine is cleared from the body.
716   We will also report the N and % of individuals who are started on a lipid-lowering or glucose-lowering drug after
717   study enrollment. Because of the large number of variables, these data will be presented descriptively.
718   Analysis of Drug Exposure: The analyses of drug exposure are exploratory and will not directly influence the
719   findings of H1-3 and E1-2, but will instead provide supplementary information for interpretation of these
720   findings. Mixed effects linear regression analyses will compare treatment groups on magnitude and variability
721   of sertraline exposure in the randomized phase. Mixed effects linear models will compare age groups on
722   magnitude and variability of i) sertraline exposure and ii) olanzapine exposure during the open-label and
723   randomized phases. There will be separate models for each phase and for each measure of drug exposure.
724   3.10.4 Sample Size Determination and Statistical Power Analyses:
725   Assumptions for Power Calculations for H1: We propose 20% as the minimal clinically meaningful
726   difference in relapse rates between olanzapine and placebo over 36 weeks. A 20% difference would mean that
727   5 patients (NNT) would need to be treated with olanzapine to prevent 1 case of relapse. Based on the review
728   of literature (Section 3.4) and STOP-PD stabilization data (Section 3.8), we estimate that 15% of participants
729   who are maintained on sertraline+olanzapine will have a relapse in the RCT. A relapse rate of 35% in the
730   sertraline+placebo group would therefore be consistent with the hypothesized 20% difference between
731   treatment groups. A 35% relapse following olanzapine discontinuation is the same as the rate reported by
732   Aronson et al.32 in a naturalistic study of antipsychotic discontinuation in PD, but not as high as some of the
733   rates of relapse of PD following cessation of a course of ECT.4,37-39 Based on our previous experience of
734   conducting studies of the continuation and maintenance treatment of PD,38,41,139 we predict that attrition during
735   the RCT will not exceed 10%.
