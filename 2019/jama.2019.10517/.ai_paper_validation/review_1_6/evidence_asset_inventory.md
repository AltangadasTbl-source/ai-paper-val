## Fitness and source matching

- USABLE: source-linked text, images, or extraction map adequate for its stated unit or metadata function.
- PARTIAL: sparse figure-native text requiring paired images; document/package records with historical scope exclusions; metadata that locates source units but is not source evidence. Such records cannot independently count a complete reusable unit.
- DUPLICATE: identical SHA-256 to another indexed asset; the row names its first equivalent. Duplicate files add no units, and either unchanged equivalent may be used as a locator.
- STALE and UNREADABLE: none detected among eligible evidence assets. Historical restrictions in records are explicitly ignored rather than used to omit sources.
