## Findings

### F-XS-001 — Primary relapse criterion lacks the planned HDRS increase requirement

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [protocol p.12, section3.9.8, lines553–568](../../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=12); REPORT [main p.4, Primary Outcome: Relapse](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4); affected REPORT [main p.1 abstract](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1), [p.6 primary result](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=6), and [p.7 Table3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7).
- **Printed statements:** PLAN requires HAM-D≥18 **and** a mean absolute increase of5points on at least one visit relative to RCT entry. REPORT lists HDRS≥18 as a sufficient standalone relapse criterion, without the increase requirement. Both lists also separately include major depressive episode, recurrent psychosis, suicide plan/attempt, mania/hypomania and psychiatric hospitalization. Table3's hospitalization footnote supplies the psychiatric reasons, reconciling that aspect of the shorter Methods list.
- **Comparison logic:** Match the HDRS/HAM-D17-item measure, randomized baseline and first relapse. A participant entering near remission at15 and later reaching18 satisfies the printed report score rule but has a3-point rise and does not satisfy the planned score-and-increase rule through this component. This is a logical criterion comparison, not rounding of an estimate; half-unit intervals and relative numeric discrepancy are not applicable. No individual event is reassigned by this diagnostic example.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Definition of the primary relapse composite underlying13/64 versus34/62 and HR0.25(95%CI0.13–0.48).
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Route A covers changed primary composite definitions. The report does not say whether the additional planned score-increase criterion remained operational despite its omission from the report's sufficient-condition list.
- **Supported alternatives and missing definition:** The report may abbreviate an unchanged operational rule; alternatively, the score-only component may have been broadened. The plan's preferred two-visit duration has an explicit risk exception and is not treated as an unconditional duration conflict. The report's SCID psychosis description may also abbreviate the planned SADS≥3 confirmation; that semantic possibility is not asserted as an independent finding.
- **Exact human question:** Was the additional5-point HDRS increase from RCT entry required for relapse classification, and which operational primary composite produced the printed event counts and HR?

### F-XS-002 — Planned primary glycemic measure is absent from the reported metabolic result set

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [protocol p.10, Metabolic Measures, lines484–488](../../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=10); REPORT [main p.4, Secondary Outcomes](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4), [p.1 abstract metabolic results](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1), [p.7 metabolic results](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7), [p.8 Table4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=8); REPORT [supplement2 pp.1–10, outcome index and all plots](../../../joi190079supp2_prod.pdf#page=1).
- **Printed statements:** PLAN names HOMA insulin resistance the primary measure of glycemic function, to be calculated whenever fasting glucose and insulin are measured, and HbA1c the secondary glycemic measure. REPORT lists fasting glucose and HbA1c, supplies their daily treatment×time estimates−0.02mg/dL and−0.0002 with intervals and adjustedP=.99, and gives corresponding tables/plots. No HOMA or insulin-resistance result appears in the supplied REPORT union.
- **Comparison logic:** Item-by-item matching of the glycemic outcome hierarchy finds two reported glycemic measurements but no counterpart for the plan's primary glycemic measure. This is an outcome/result-set comparison, not a calculation from glucose alone; no HOMA values are inferred. Half-unit intervals and relative discrepancy are not applicable.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Primary glycemic safety assessment and interpretation of the abstract's reported glycemic findings under Aim2.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Route A covers a planned primary/key secondary analysis not reported. The report's explicit exclusion of age/genetic aims on p.2 does not explain absence of the planned primary glycemic measure in its randomized metabolic safety results.
- **Supported alternatives and missing definition:** HOMA may have been unavailable, deferred or replaced by glucose/HbA1c; no supplied amendment or reason identifies which. This finding does not claim that the reported glucose/HbA1c estimates are numerically inconsistent.
- **Exact human question:** What happened to the prespecified primary glycemic HOMA measure, and was it analyzed, deferred, or replaced for the reported randomized metabolic safety assessment?

### F-XS-003 — Planned hyperlipidemia-treatment censoring becomes a post hoc sensitivity analysis

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [protocol p.14, H2, lines673–679](../../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=14); REPORT [main p.5, Statistical Analyses](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=5), [p.7, Secondary Outcomes](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7); affected REPORT [p.1 abstract](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1).
- **Printed statements:** PLAN says new or changed hyperlipidemia treatment during the RCT causes pertinent subsequent metabolic data to be excluded from the mixed model. REPORT calls exclusion of subsequent statin/hypoglycemic observations a post hoc sensitivity analysis and says its results were qualitatively similar to analyses that included all participants. Statins started/changed for12participants,6perarm; hypoglycemics for3participants,2olanzapine and1placebo.
- **Comparison logic:** Match randomized H2 mixed-model metabolic results and the post-medication-start data window. The prespecified main-model exclusion is placed in a separate post hoc sensitivity route in the report, rather than described as the principal H2 data rule. Hypoglycemic exclusion is an additional extension; the demonstrated conflict concerns the explicitly planned hyperlipidemia rule. No exact sensitivity estimates are supplied; no numerical difference or invented precision calculation is claimed.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Which prespecified H2 analysis produced the abstract/body cholesterol and triglyceride treatment×time estimates and whether the reported main results include post-statin observations.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** The printed plan and report place the same censoring operation in different analysis roles. Qualitative similarity supplies reassurance about direction, but does not identify the planned-analysis numerical counterpart or explain the change in main-analysis specification.
- **Supported alternatives and missing definition:** A documented amendment may have changed the main data rule; alternatively, the term post hoc may refer to extension to hypoglycemic agents while the planned statin component remained in the main model. The supplied report does not separate these interpretations.
- **Exact human question:** Did the main reported metabolic estimates exclude post-hyperlipidemia-treatment observations as planned, or was this rule moved to sensitivity analysis, and where are the prespecified-analysis estimates documented?

### F-XS-004 — Planned alternative and attrition analyses of key secondary outcomes are not reported

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [protocol p.10, Anthropometric Measures, lines473–483](../../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=10), [p.16, Strategies for Attrition, lines769–772](../../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=16); REPORT [main p.4 mixed models](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4), [p.5 missingness/sensitivity methods](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=5), [p.7 secondary results](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7), [p.8 Table4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=8); REPORT [supplement2 pp.1–10](../../../joi190079supp2_prod.pdf#page=1).
- **Printed statements:** PLAN specifies depression-related weight loss taken into account in a secondary weight-change analysis, separate BMI and waist analyses as alternatives to weight for Aims2–5, and comparison of treatment-effect models with models including Intent-to-Attend at RCT entry. REPORT gives waist results and pattern-mixture investigations, but no BMI result, no weight-change analysis accounting for depression-related weight loss, and no Intent-to-Attend model comparison. Premorbid weight is reported descriptively and used to define adverse weight gain/loss; that is a different operation from adjustment in a longitudinal weight-change analysis.
- **Comparison logic:** Compare named planned alternative/sensitivity analyses for the randomized weight and metabolic results reported in the abstract. The observed omission pattern is consolidated in one finding with all three affected planned analyses; it does not assert omitted generic implementation details. Half-unit arithmetic is not applicable because no numerical counterpart is reported.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route A, category5.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** Prespecified checks supporting the weight0.13lb/day, waist0.009in/day and metabolic treatment×time results, particularly treatment effects under attrition and weight-loss adjustment.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Route A covers planned key-secondary analyses not reported. Pattern-mixture results are reported, but the plan explicitly describes Intent-to-Attend as a second analysis. Reporting premorbid weight for another purpose does not supply the planned weight-change result.
- **Supported alternatives and missing definition:** The analyses may have been performed without reporting, deferred, or omitted after an amendment. Aims3–5 are expressly outside this article's scope, but the same alternative/sensitivity specifications also apply to Aim2, whose results the report says it presents.
- **Exact human question:** Where are the Aim2 BMI, depression-related-weight-loss-adjusted weight-change and Intent-to-Attend sensitivity analyses reported, or what amendment or reason explains their absence?

### F-XS-005 — Reported adverse-event reference baseline differs from the plan

- **Compared locations and roles:** PLAN [protocol p.15, Analysis of Non-Metabolic Safety Data, lines705–717](../../../joi190079supp1_prod.pdf#page=15); REPORT [main p.4, Other Measures of Tolerability](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4), [p.7, Adverse Effects](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7).
- **Printed statements:** PLAN reports N/% newly meeting adverse-event criteria compared with acute-phase baseline and uses UKU increase≥2points or item score3. REPORT defines adverse effects, except weight gain/loss, using a2-point increase from RCT baseline or score3or4 plus an increase from baseline. The reported UKU frequencies include sleepiness/sedation7.8%vs3.2%, orthostatic dizziness7.8%vs3.2%, and nausea/vomiting3.1%vs8.1%. REPORT separately defines incident akathisia as RCT-baseline0 followed by≥2, whereas PLAN uses absence of acute-baseline adverse-event criteria.
- **Comparison logic:** Compare the exact criterion list and reference epoch, rather than equating a continuing acute-phase event with a newly emerging randomized-phase event. Acute enrollment occurs before up to12weeks acute treatment and8weeks stabilization; RCT baseline occurs after these phases. Thus the reference baselines cannot be substituted as the same time point. No raw trajectories allow event-count recomputation; half-unit intervals/relative discrepancy are not applicable to the demonstrated definition change. The score4 wording itself is not a separate finding here.
- **Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`. **Relationship:** `P_R`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route B.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** The named UKU adverse-event and incident-akathisia percentages, whose meaning changes between newly meeting criteria after acute baseline and newly meeting criteria after randomized baseline.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Both sources provide definite but different baseline rules. The report gives no amendment reconciling them; a reader comparing prospective criteria with reported safety frequencies cannot tell whether the planned or revised definition generated those frequencies.
- **Supported alternatives and missing definition:** The report may intentionally isolate newly developing randomized-phase events; the supplied plan may represent an earlier rule. No claim is made about how many participants change category, and no preferred baseline is proposed.
- **Exact human question:** Were adverse-event and akathisia incidences defined relative to acute-phase or RCT baseline, and was the reported rule an amendment to the supplied plan?
