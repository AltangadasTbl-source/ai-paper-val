3.9.8 Outcomes 552
Outcome Criteria: Relapse is the primary measure of outcome. Relapse criteria are broad, to reflect a range 553
of clinically relevant outcomes of PD. The criterion for relapse will be at least one of the following: 1) SCID 554
symptoms of major depressive episode (preferably maintained for at least 1 week over two consecutive weekly 555
visits); 2) 17-item HAM-D score of ≥18 (preferably maintained for at least 1 week over two consecutive weekly 556
visits) and a mean absolute increase of 5 points on at least one visit relative to entry into the RCT; 3) re- 557
emergence of SCID-rated psychosis (delusions or hallucinations) (preferably for at least 1 week over two 558
consecutive weekly visits) and a score of ≥3 on the SADS delusion or hallucination severity items 559
(delusion/hallucination ‘definitely present’); 4) significant clinical worsening, defined as either i) emergence of 560
‘high risk’ of suicide at any time (current suicide plan or suicide attempt), and/or ii) development of SCID-rated 561
mania or hypomania (preferably for at least 1 week over two consecutive weekly visits), and/or iii) psychiatric 562
hospitalization for depression, psychosis, suicidality, or mania/hypomania, regardless of the duration of these 563
symptoms. Although a one-week time duration is preferable, the research psychiatrist is allowed to declare that 564
a relapse has occurred if the clinical criterion is established at an in-person visit and postponing further 565
intervention for another seven days is considered to place the subject at undue risk. Time to relapse will be 566
defined as time from randomization to the first visit at which relapse is established or the start of psychiatric 567
hospitalization. These relapse criteria reflect a clinical deterioration for which most clinicians would consider a 568
change of treatment. Even though patients with a history of bipolar affective disorder will not be allowed to 569
enter the study (Section 4.2.1), mania or hypomania is included as an outcome variable because onset of PD 570
in younger adults may predict subsequent development of bipolar affective disorder. Participants who are 571
suspected of having a relapse will be seen by one of the study’s research psychiatrists who will determine 572
whether relapse criteria are met. 573
Management of Relapse: Patients who meet relapse criteria will be seen for clinical management by a 574
psychiatrist at the research site who is not affiliated with the study within 48-72 hours of referral. Alternatively, 575
patients can be referred for acute psychiatric hospitalization, if deemed appropriate by the study psychiatrist. 576
The non-study psychiatrist will be notified by the research pharmacist whether the patient was taking 577
olanzapine at the time of relapse. Other research personnel will not be aware of the treatment assignment. 578
Based on the frequency of relapse postulated for the study, we estimate that there will be an average of 3 579
cases of relapse per year per study site. 580

3.10.2 Randomization: We will randomize 88 participants to each of the two treatment arms using a 1:1 626

3.10.3 Data Analytic Procedures: Initially, baseline demographic and clinical characteristics will be 630
examined. Frequency distributions will be produced. Descriptive and graphical displays will be produced. 631
Transformations will be used when distributional assumptions are not fulfilled for inferential tests. Treatment 632
groups will be compared on baseline demographic and clinical variables using t-tests for continuous variables, 633
Mann-Whitney tests for ordinal variables, and chi-square tests for categorical variables. If significant baseline 634
group imbalance is detected on any particular variable, that variable will be included as a covariate in the 635
inferential analyses if its correlation with outcome is 0.30 or greater. Dropouts and completers will also be 636
compared on baseline variables using t-tests, Mann-Whitney tests, or chi-square tests. The primary analyses 637
will adhere to the Intent-to-Treat principle. Each statistical test for H1-3 will involve a two-tailed alpha of .05. 638
Primary Hypothesis: Efficacy. 639
H1: The combination of sertraline and olanzapine will be associated with less risk of relapse than the 640
combination of sertraline and placebo. H1 will be tested with a Cox proportional hazards model that 641
compares survival time across treatment groups. Survival time will be defined as time from randomization to 642
relapse (Section 3.9.8) and will be measured in weeks. A subject’s survival time will be classified as censored 643
at the point of study discontinuation, (e.g., due to withdrawal of consent or a severe intervening non-psychiatric 644
medical event) or at end of follow-up, if relapse has not yet occurred. Kaplan Meier survival curves will be used 64514
for descriptive analyses of time to relapse. The Cox models will include treatment group and the stratification 646
variables: site, remission group at randomization (full-remission vs. near-remission), and age group at 647
randomization (18-59 vs. ≥60 years). Treatment groups will be compared on 5 variables at the time of 648
randomization that have the potential to be associated with risk of relapse (anxiety, medical burden, executive 649
dysfunction, psychomotor change, and treatment resistance prior to study entry; Section 3.9.5); if the treatment 650
groups differ significantly on one or more of these variables (p≤.05) and the variable in question is correlated 651
with relapse (r>.30), the variable will be included as a covariate in the Cox model. Based on our prior 652
studies,38,41,139
we expect no more than 10% attrition during the RCT among these participants who have 653
already been enrolled for 12-20 weeks prior to randomization and who are in sustained remission at the time of 654
randomization. This attrition rate might appear to be low, but this is because those subjects most vulnerable to 655
attrition will likely drop out in the phases prior to randomization. Non-informative censoring will be examined by 656
comparing those who do and do not dropout on their HAM-D ratings from the prior week. The constant hazards 657
assumption will be examined by examining the incremental contribution of a treatment x vulnerable period of 658
relapse interaction (exploring the possibility of higher risk in the first 3 months following discontinuation). 659
Secondary Hypotheses: Weight and Metabolic Safety 660
H2: The combination of sertraline and olanzapine will be associated with higher weight, higher total 661
