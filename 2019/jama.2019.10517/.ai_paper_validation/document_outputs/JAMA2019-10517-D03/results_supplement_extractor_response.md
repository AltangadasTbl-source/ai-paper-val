## Main-article comparators (descriptive cross-reference only)

* **D01 `jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf`, PDF p1, Results (abstract):** identifies weight, waist circumference, lipids, glucose, and HbA1c as secondary outcomes and reports treatment-by-daily-rate comparisons: weight 0.13 lb (95% CI, 0.11–0.15); waist 0.009 inches (0.004–0.014); total cholesterol 0.29 mg/dL (0.13–0.45); LDL 0.04 mg/dL (−0.01 to 0.10); HDL −0.01 mg/dL (−0.03 to 0.01); triglyceride −0.153 mg/dL (−0.306 to 0.004); glucose −0.02 mg/dL (−0.12 to 0.08); HbA1c −0.0002 mg/dL (−0.0021 to 0.0016). These are model-result comparators, not numerical labels reproduced in eFigures 1–8.
* **D01, PDF p4, Secondary Outcomes / Statistical Analyses:** states the measurement schedule (weight/waist each visit; fasting lipid measures, glucose, and HbA1c at RCT baseline, every 8 weeks, and termination), linear mixed-model approach for anthropometric/metabolic measures, and a Poisson mixed-effects regression for Simpson-Angus Scale scores.
* **D01, PDF p7, Secondary Outcomes / Other Measures of Tolerability:** explicitly directs the reader to eFigures 1–8 for trajectories; reports the Simpson-Angus weekly-change comparison as 0.022 points (95% CI, 0.009–0.036; adjusted P=.009) and directs to eFigure 9. It also says medication-change sensitivity analyses for linear mixed-model metabolic results were qualitatively similar, but D03 itself supplies only the labeled triglyceride pattern-mixture display.
* **D01, PDF p8, Table 4:** supplies raw RCT-baseline and termination summaries, not the eFigure least-square means. For example, the displayed table gives the raw weight mean (SD) at baseline/termination as 178.6 (39.4)/183.6 (40.9) lb for sertraline + olanzapine and 182.5 (39.8)/180.2 (42.1) lb for sertraline + placebo; the table footnote says its difference may not equal termination minus baseline because of missing data.
