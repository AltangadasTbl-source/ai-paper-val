# Numeric Consistency Review

## Scope and method

Fresh review completed for all 69 assigned numeric/reporting relationships: N001-N049 and N200-N219. Only fresh mapper artifacts, freshly extracted PDF text/renderings, and supplied PDFs were used. Counts/percentages were checked as 100*n/d (one-decimal tolerance 0.05 percentage point); displayed high-minus-low differences were checked at printed precision. Subgroup totals were summed only for mutually exclusive categories. Nonexclusive drugs/exposures, conditional denominators, repeated availability, model-specific effects, and version-specific plans were not forced to match.

Every row is an explicit COMPLETE record. “No proposal” means no distinct source-grounded candidate proposal, not an adjudication.

## Candidate proposals for later registration

### P-N004 — Hypoxemia CI sign mismatch

- Category: Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- Locations: DOC-001, main PDF p.1 abstract and main PDF p.9 Table 3, hypoxemia row.
- Printed inputs/direct observation: Abstract: 5.0% vs 13.6%, difference -8.6% (95% CI -11.1 to 6.1). Table 3: same rates/difference, CI -11.1 to -6.1.
- Rule/calculation/tolerance: Same population/outcome/contrast must have the same interval. 5.0-13.6=-8.6; endpoint sign differs by 12.2 points, far exceeding 0.05-point rounding tolerance.
- Inference and alternatives: Direct observation is the two intervals. An omitted minus sign is a possible inference; alternatively the table could be wrong.
- Quality-control relevance: Abstract-only extraction could record a null-compatible interval while table extraction would not.
- Exact human question: Which CI was intended for the abstracted high-minus-low hypoxemia difference?
- Duplicate key: DOC-001|hypoxemia|high-minus-low|abstract-p1-vs-Table3-p9.

### P-N040 — DIC CI/P inconsistency

- Category: Statistical reporting inconsistency.
- Location: DOC-001, main PDF p.9, Table 3 DIC row.
- Printed inputs/direct observation: High 1 (0.1), low 0, difference 0.1 (-0.1 to 0.3), RR 2.00 (1.91 to 2.09), P>.99.
- Rule/calculation/tolerance: The reported CI excludes 1 (lower bound 1.91) while P>.99 for the same stated RR comparison. This is a direction-level conflict, not a rounding discrepancy.
- Inference and alternatives: Possible transcription, alignment, or zero-cell calculation issue; the source does not state the zero-cell method and does not establish a replacement.
- Quality-control relevance: The row could be copied as a precise elevated risk despite its printed P value.
- Exact human question: Please verify the DIC RR, CI, and P-value cell alignment/calculation for 1 versus 0 events.
- Duplicate key: DOC-001|Table3|DIC|1-vs-0|RR2.00-CI1.91-2.09-P>.99.

### P-N218 — Death figure mislabelled as extra-pulmonary complications

- Category: Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- Location: DOC-005, online supplement PDF p.41, eFigure 11 heading/caption.
- Printed inputs/direct observation: Heading Probability of death; 5-day mortality 0.5%/0.3%, HR 1.67 (0.40-6.97), P=.484; local narrative says rate of postoperative extra-pulmonary complications.
- Rule/calculation/tolerance: A figure outcome label must identify the same quantity as the printed analysis. Death is not extra-pulmonary complications; no numeric tolerance applies.
- Inference and alternatives: Carryover wording is possible; alternatively the sentence may refer to a neighbouring figure, but this is not specified.
- Quality-control relevance: An extractor could misclassify a death HR as an extra-pulmonary-complication effect.
- Exact human question: Does the phrase refer to eFigure 11 or a different figure/outcome?
- Duplicate key: DOC-005|eFigure11-p41|death-vs-extra-pulmonary-label.
