## RM1–RM8: eMethods relationships and matching keys

RM1 — [Eligibility, PDF p. 18](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=18). BMI ≥35 kg/m²; laparoscopic or non-laparoscopic operation expected >2 hours incision-to-closure under general anesthesia; ARISCAT ≥26. Seven predictors: four patient-related and three surgery-related. Excluded age <18; invasive ventilation >30 minutes within preceding 30 days; chemotherapy/radiotherapy within two months; severe cardiac disease NYHA III/IV; pulmonary artery systolic pressure >40 mmHg. Other exclusions: previous lung surgery, neurosurgery/cardiac surgery, one-lung ventilation, planned re-intubation, prone/lateral position, other interventional enrollment, refusal, pregnancy, instability/shock, severe COPD defined by home NIV/oxygen or repeated corticosteroid treatment, ARDS expected to need prolonged ventilation, intracranial injury/tumor, neuromuscular disease. Main matching key: eligibility and main participant-flow analysis population; these are thresholds, not observed counts.

RM2 — [Interventions, PDF p. 18](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=18). Tidal volume 7 mL/kg predicted body weight (PBW) both arms. High PEEP 12 cmH₂O with planned recruitment after intubation, hourly, before extubation; low PEEP 4 cmH₂O without planned recruitment. Levels maintained during mechanical ventilation. Main matching key: randomized comparison, intraoperative management and main ventilation table.

RM3 — [Recommendations, PDF p. 19](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=19). Routine treatment follows local practice; recommendations explicitly not mandatory. Postoperative VAS pain below 3; at least one peripheral venous line. Suggested agents: isoflurane/desflurane/sevoflurane; propofol/remifentanil/sufentanil; cis-atracurium/atracurium/vecuronium/rocuronium; prostigmine or neostigmine with atropine/glycopyrrolate reversal. Main matching keys: eTable 5 drug categories, eTable 7 VAS values, anesthesia-care narrative.

RM4 — [Ventilation and PBW, PDF p. 19](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=19). Volume control, lowest possible FiO₂ but ≥0.4, target SpO₂ >92%, I:E 1:2, respiratory rate to end-tidal CO₂ 35–45 mmHg. PBW male = 50 + 0.91 × (height in cm −152.4); female =45.5 +0.91 × (height in cm −152.4), PBW in kg. Inspired circuit tidal volume 7 mL/kg PBW. PEEP 4 versus 12 cmH₂O modified for rescue or physician discretion. Main matching keys: PBW denominator and ventilation measures; RF2, RF3, RF5, RF7.

RM5 — [Recruitment, PDF p. 19](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=19) and [continuation, PDF p. 20](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=20). After induction, disconnects, hourly, before extubation, if stable. Peak-pressure limit 55 cmH₂O; initial tidal volume 7 mL/kg PBW; respiratory rate ≥6/min; PEEP 12 or higher for rescue; I:E 1:1; tidal-volume steps +4 mL/kg PBW to plateau 40–50 cmH₂O. If maximum ventilator volume reached below plateau 40, increase PEEP to maximum20. Maintain 40–50 for three breaths then return rate/I:E/pause/volume to earlier settings, retain PEEP12 or rescue setting. Main key: intervention procedure and recruitment counts.

RM6 — [Rescue and deviation thresholds, PDF p. 20](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=20). Hypoxemia SpO₂ ≤92%, after excluding resistance/intrinsic PEEP/hemodynamic/ventilator problems. Low arm first increases FiO₂; high arm first increases PEEP. Physician may decrease PEEP if saturation deteriorates. Safety deviation possible at any time; specified systolic BP <90 mmHg unresponsive to fluids/drugs, intolerable vasoactive dosing, arrhythmia, massive transfusion >100% blood volume/24h or >50%/4h to maintain hematocrit >21% (printed hemoglobin >7 mg/dL), or life-threatening surgical event. Preserve printed hemoglobin unit without normalization. Main key: adverse intraoperative-event thresholds; RT2 rescue sequence.

RM7 — [Primary composite definitions, PDF p. 20](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=20) and [continuation, PDF p. 21](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=21). Twelve components: mild, moderate and severe respiratory failure; ARDS; bronchospasm; new pulmonary infiltrates; pulmonary infection; aspiration pneumonitis; pleural effusion; atelectasis; cardiopulmonary edema; pneumothorax. Mild: PaO₂ <60 mmHg or SpO₂ <90% after ≥10min air, responding to printed supplemental oxygen '=2 L/min', excluding hypoventilation. Moderate same oxygen thresholds but response only to >2 L/min. Severe: NIV or invasive ventilation, excluding sedative hypoventilation. Infection: new/progressive infiltrate and ≥2 of antibiotics, tympanic temperature >38°C, white cells <4000 or >12000/mm³, purulent secretions. Bronchospasm newly detected expiratory wheeze treated with bronchodilator; infiltrates new radiographic unilateral/bilateral without other signs; aspiration failure following regurgitated contents; effusion radiographic blunting/silhouette loss/displacement or supine haze with preserved shadows; atelectasis opacity with shift toward affected area and adjacent compensatory inflation; edema congestion and increased vascular markings/diffuse infiltrates; pneumothorax pleural air without vascular bed around visceral pleura. Main key: primary composite and each component; RT8 and RF12. Components need not be mutually exclusive.

RM8 — [Secondary definitions, PDF p. 21](../../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=21). Severe pulmonary composite excludes mild respiratory failure from the primary components. Intraoperative hypoxemia SpO₂≤92%; hypotension systolic BP<90 mmHg; bradycardia HR<50/min. Other secondary outcomes: unexpected ICU admission/readmission, hospital-free days at day90, impaired wound healing, 5-day and in-hospital mortality, extra-pulmonary composite. Extra-pulmonary components: SIRS/sepsis/severe sepsis/shock, extrapulmonary infection, coma Glasgow<8 without therapeutic sedation/coma, acute MI, RIFLE acute renal failure, DIC, gastrointestinal failure, hepatic failure. Hepatic: bilirubin day5/day1>1.7 and INR day5/day1>1.0, or new encephalopathy/coagulopathy INR>1.5 within8 weeks of liver injury without chronic disease. Main keys: secondary-outcome table/narrative; RF9–RF11. No person-time rate denominator is supplied here.
