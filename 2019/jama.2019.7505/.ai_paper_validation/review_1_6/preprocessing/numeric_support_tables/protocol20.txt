7.2.5 Blood and urine samples
Before, at the end of, and five days after surgery, blood samples (2 x 5 ml in EDTA, citrate
and heparin) and urine samples will be collected and stored at –80°C for measurement of:
   x Inflammatory mediators (cytokines, chemokines, other inflammatory proteins)
   x Markers of lung injury (angiopoietin-2, surfactant proteins A and D)
