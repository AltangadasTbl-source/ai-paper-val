Post–operative variables
The patients will be assessed daily between the first and the fifth day after surgery as well as
on the last day before discharge from hospital. Clinical data and the presence of pulmonary
and extra–pulmonary postoperative complications will be scored, the date of development of
any complication documented (for definitions, see APPENDIX iii.).
