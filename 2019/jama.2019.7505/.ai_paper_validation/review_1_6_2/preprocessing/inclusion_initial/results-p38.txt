eFigure 8. Probability of postoperative pulmonary complications in the first 5
postoperative days




Median observation time was not computed for incidence of postoperative pulmonary
complications because the maximum value observed was 0.23. With a median follow-up of 4
(2 – 5) days, the rate of postoperative pulmonary complications within the first 5 postoperative
days was 21.3% in the high PEEP group and 23.6% in the low PEEP group (hazard ratio for
postoperative pulmonary complications, 0.88; 95% confidence interval 0.73 to 1.06; P=0.190).
P value for the Schoenfeld residuals was 0.05.
