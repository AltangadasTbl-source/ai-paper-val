### N802 — Trial scale, allocation and time horizons

[PDF p.7](../../../joi190106supp1_prod_1635377898.47058.pdf#page=7), [p.14](../../../joi190106supp1_prod_1635377898.47058.pdf#page=14), [p.15](../../../joi190106supp1_prod_1635377898.47058.pdf#page=15): parallel-group multicenter primary-prevention trial, 6000 total, 3000 each arm, 20 recruiting centers approximately 300 each (150 per arm). ### N803 — Target population and eligibility

[PDF pp.14,18–19](../../../joi190106supp1_prod_1635377898.47058.pdf#page=18): men 55–75 years, women 60–75, BMI ≥27 and <40 kg/m², ≥3 metabolic-syndrome criteria; intention 50% women and diabetes ≤25%. Waist risk: Caucasian women ≥80 cm and men ≥94 cm; South American men ≥90 cm (women same). Excludes previous PREDIMED participation and prior CVD. Numeric exclusion windows: cancer within last 5 years (except nonmelanoma skin), >5 kg weight-loss program in preceding 6 months, planned bariatric surgery next 12 months, psychiatric hospitalization last 6 months, life expectancy <24 months, alcohol >50 g/day or drug abuse last 6 months, aneurysm ≥5.5 cm; infection/inflammation eligible 3 months after resolution. Other exclusions: illiteracy/unwilling consent, institutionalization, inability to diet/activity/visits, low predicted diet-change willingness, bowel resection/inflammation, endocrine obesity except treated hypothyroid, food allergy, immunodeficiency/HIV, cirrhosis, transplantation, immunosuppressive/cytotoxic/systemic corticosteroid/weight-loss therapy, concurrent RCT, other adherence-interfering condition. Main matching keys: cohort inclusion and baseline populations.

### N806 — Primary clinical-event definitions

[PDF pp.29–31](../../../joi190106supp1_prod_1635377898.47058.pdf#page=29): MI: biomarker rise/fall with ≥1 value above 99th-percentile URL AND ischemic symptoms, new ST–T/LBBB, pathological Q waves, imaging new viable-myocardium loss/wall-motion abnormality or intracoronary thrombus. Prior MI defined by Q waves, imaging or pathology. Stroke: acute neurological deficit >24 h due to blocked blood flow or hemorrhage; ischemic evidence includes pathology/imaging or symptoms ≥24 h/until death with other causes excluded. Silent CNS infarction and silent cerebral hemorrhage excluded as primary. Cardiovascular death includes sudden/non-sudden cardiac and noncardiac vascular death (thromboembolism, stroke, dissecting aneurysm, PAD). Event ascertainment by yearly records and blinded committee/medical doctors (p.29). Main matching key: composite components, thresholds/windows and ascertainment.
