## Source-grounded rationale

### F-NUM-001

Compared locations are REPORT DOC-001 PDF pp. 1, 3, 4, and 5. The source values are reproduced: 6874 randomized; 6583 in the abstract; main-arm denominators 3272 and 3311; baseline exclusions 53 and 238; unavailable nutritional information at 12 months 439 and 368; nutritional sensitivity denominators 2833 and 2943; er-MedDiet values imputed at 12 months 517. Thus 6874 minus 53 minus 238 equals 6583, 3272 plus 3311 equals 6583, 2833 plus 2943 equals 5776, and 6583 minus 517 equals 6066. The rounded 96% is compatible with 6583/6874, approximately 95.77%; the latter counts are distinct outcome-availability quantities, not alternative printed visit-completion totals.

The comparison potentially concerns focus A and the main-analysis size, so it was considered under the qualifying population tier before the four criteria. However, the source figure labels unavailable **nutritional information**, not withdrawals, absent visits, or failure to complete follow-up. The [methods and results](../../../jama_saynorea_2019_oi_190106_1635377898.43062.pdf#page=4) distinguish principal analyses with imputed follow-up from completeness-based sensitivities, and [Table 2](../../../jama_saynorea_2019_oi_190106_1635377898.43062.pdf#page=5) supplies instrument-specific imputation counts. Follow-up attendance and availability of a particular questionnaire are not logically identical. The abstract does not assert that every analyzed score is observed, and the immediate analysis label identifies 6583 as the analyzed population. Reproduction of the proposed inconsistency would require an additional completion-event definition or a matched attendance count. Consequently the finding does not establish a change to the primary score denominator or estimate; the named observed-completion extraction remains dependent on an unsupported equivalence. The screening decision concerns that conditional comparison, not a replacement completion count inferred from imputation.
