## Scope and method

This durable inventory covers `MAIN-S001` through `MAIN-S055` in `DOC-001` and every inferential result or definition embedded in `SUP-N001` through `SUP-N013` and `SUP-S001` through `SUP-S013` across `DOC-002` through `DOC-005`. It was reconstructed from the current mapping artifacts and their current direct-source/layout evidence only. No legacy candidate, checker, verifier, critic, or report record was consulted.

Each canonical `S` ID below denotes one printed inferential result, test/P-value comparison, or required inferential definition. The supplement result tables had been compacted by the mapper; they are expanded here. Within a table-result block, IDs advance in this fixed order: listed row order; then 6-month before 12-month; then intervention change, control change, and intervention-minus-control difference/P value. Thus every printed effect/CI/P comparison in the block has one unique canonical ID. The table's population, model, scale, direction, and column-label definitions are represented by the preceding structural ID. This permits exact recovery of each individual record without conflating the row effects.

## Pass-1 check boundaries and limitations

- P-value/test/statistic/SE reconciliation was limited to results for which the source supplies a two-sided mixed-effects comparison and a 95% CI. No test statistic, SE, degrees of freedom, covariance, variance estimator, multiplicity rule for an individual contrast, or estimate-to-P computational definition was supplied for the table effects; none was inferred.
- The SAP screening table does not state its test/model/variance/sidedness. Its P values were not reverse engineered.
- Figure 1/2/3 graphical coordinates and some P labels are not reliably encoded as text; they remain explicit graphical limitations rather than inferred missing values.
- Historical protocol/SAP planned analyses and assumptions are not treated as directly comparable observed-study estimates without an exact shared estimand, population, time, and model.
- All CIs/means/P values were checked as printed, allowing ordinary finite display rounding. No tiny positive tail probability was derived and no display-zero P-value candidate was created.
