# Stable numeric relationship inventory

## Scope and method

This inventory covers the exact numeric mapper scope: MAIN-N001 through MAIN-N100 and SUP-N001 through SUP-N013, across DOC-001 through DOC-005. Canonical IDs are deterministic source/PDF-page/display order. A table row is one relationship even where it contains baseline, two group changes, an adjusted contrast, interval, and P value. Exact row inputs are retained in the listed direct-source page-preserving transcription, which is part of this inventory.

**Check notation:** ARITH = arithmetic; DENOM = count/population/missingness identity; ROUND = printed-precision tolerance; LABEL = measure/unit/time/direction/scale/reference; RATE = rate/risk/proportion/count distinction; XREF = matched occurrence. Tolerance is exact for counts/category sums, ±0.5 percentage points for an integer percent, ±0.05 for one decimal, and ±0.005 for two decimals. An adjusted mixed-effects contrast is not required to equal the difference of rounded group summaries.
