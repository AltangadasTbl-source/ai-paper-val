## DOC-003 — Supplementary Online Content 2: protocol and amendment

### Protocol-only quantitative definitions (pp. 1-6)

- **p. 1:** contents; original protocol p. 2 and amendment p. 7; no result.
- **pp. 2-3, cohort derivation:** surgical eligibility includes T2DM (HbA1c >6.4% or diabetes medication), age 18-80, BMI at least 30 kg/m2, procedure through 2017-12-31; exclusion includes ED admission within five days, cancer/GI cancer code within one year, and ejection fraction <20%. Nonsurgical comparators get one random surgical index date; exclusions include death within 30 days, T2DM definition HbA1c <6.5% and not on medication, age outside 18-80, BMI <30, and the named exclusions. The protocol says the T2DM cohort is approximately N=287438, dates 1998-01-01 through 2017-12-31. Matching is described as each nonsurgical patient matched by propensity score to five surgical patients using index date, age, BMI 30-35/35-40/40+, sex, insulin, location, and complications. These are planned-method definitions, not final results.
- **p. 4, outcomes:** six outcomes; primary composite is first occurrence of outcomes 1-6; secondary composite is mortality, MI, ischemic stroke. Baseline-history conditions are omitted from follow-up composite definition. Nephropathy: two eGFR <60 values at least 90 days apart without intervening value at least 60; MDRD eGFR. **Main-paper matching key:** endpoint definitions and composite membership; must be matched by endpoint population and risk-set exclusions.
- **p. 5:** individual-outcome risk-set rule: patients with prior outcome excluded; nephropathy also excludes baseline eGFR <60 or dialysis history. The page lists collected variables/units relevant to longitudinal results (HbA1c, creatinine, eGFR, blood pressure, LDL/HDL/triglycerides, UACR and medicines). This is protocol-only, not an estimate.
- **p. 6, planned analysis:** cause-specific rates per 100 patient-years, cumulative incidence, fully adjusted Cox-PH HRs, all baseline variables for confounding adjustment, PH test using weighted residuals; five multiply imputed datasets; predictive mean matching/logistic/polytomous logistic by numeric/binary/categorical variable and Rubin’s formula for SEs/contrasts; significance .05, 95% CIs, no multiplicity adjustment for secondary exploratory endpoints, R 3.5.0. Planned sensitivity: five index-date samples × 1:1/1:5/1:10 = 15 datasets, reported analyses from one 1:5 dataset, and time-varying HRs at 2/5/8 years. These definitions are the method comparators for DOC-002 pp. 6-20, not reported results.

### Amendment (p. 7)

- Adds planned early <90-day and late-through-last-follow-up adverse-event collection, nutritional variables hemoglobin, protein, albumin, vitamin D; definition/result status: administrative protocol content, not a count/rate.
- Adds E-value sensitivity analysis for all eight composite/individual endpoints, calculated for HR estimates and CI limits closest to null. Defines E-value as minimum confounder association on risk-ratio scale conditional on measured covariates. This is the method comparator for DOC-002 pp. 19-20.
