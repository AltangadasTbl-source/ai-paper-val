Source: `jama_aminian_2019_oi_190103.pdf` (PDF pp. 1-12; printed pp. 1271-1282). This is an extraction artifact, not an error assessment. The machine-readable evidence is in `main_text_extraction.json`.

## Core sample and endpoints

- Analysis set: 2287 metabolic-surgery patients and 11 435 matched nonsurgical controls (total 13 722; 1:5 matching). PDF p4, Results; PDF p3, Figure 1.
- Median follow-up: 3.9 y overall (IQR 1.9-6.1); 4.0 y (2.1-6.1) nonsurgical and 3.3 y (1.2-6.3) surgery. PDF p4, Results.
- Primary endpoint: extended MACE—first occurrence of all-cause mortality, coronary artery events, cerebrovascular events, heart failure, nephropathy, or atrial fibrillation. PDF p1, Main Outcomes and Measures; PDF p7, Figure 2 caption.
- Key secondary endpoint: 3-component MACE—all-cause mortality, myocardial infarction, and ischemic stroke. PDF p1; PDF p3, Primary and Secondary End Points; PDF p7, Figure 2 caption.

## Subgroups and other claims

- No heterogeneity for primary MACE was reported by sex, age, BMI, HbA1c, eGFR, insulin, sulfonylurea, or lipid-lowering-medication use; values are cited to Supplement 1 eFigure 1. PDF p4, Primary End Point.
- Sensitivity analysis: 15 datasets from five nonsurgical index-date assignments and 1:1/1:5/1:10 matching; manuscript says differences in HRs across all eight outcomes were negligible. PDF p4, p8, and p9; detail cited to Supplement 1 eFigure 4.
- At 8 years, weight reduction was 29.1 kg (95% CI 28.8-29.3) vs 8.7 kg (8.6-8.9), difference 20.3 kg (20.1-20.6); HbA1c change difference 1.1% (1.0-1.2), P < .001. PDF p7; Figure 4, PDF p9.
- Figure 5 reports lower 8-year medication use after surgery: P < .001 for noninsulin diabetes drugs, renin-angiotensin system inhibitors, other antihypertensives, lipid-lowering drugs, and aspirin; P=.008 for insulin. Numerical proportions are cited to Supplement 1 eTables 8 and 10. PDF p10 (printed p1280), Figure 5A-F.
- 90-day postoperative counts and percentages are in PDF p7, Adverse Events After Metabolic Surgery; all values are retained in the JSON artifact.
