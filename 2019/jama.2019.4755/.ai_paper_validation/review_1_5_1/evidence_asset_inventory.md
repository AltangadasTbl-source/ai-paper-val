# Reused Evidence Asset Inventory

## Method and classification

Inventory command: `find .ai_paper_validation -type f` filtered to OCR text/JSON and rendered pages, native/normalized/layout text, page manifests, document records, and source-location/evidence maps. Hashes are in `reused_artifact_hashes_before.sha256`; all paths there are package-relative. No old candidate, checker, verifier, critic, endetail, queue, or final-report content was used to determine scientific scope.

`USABLE` means page-linked and adequate as a locator/transcription aid. `PARTIAL` means it covers only the named pages. `STALE` means a superseded or quality-failed derivative exists for the same source page. `DUPLICATE` means it adds no independent page coverage. `UNREADABLE` was not observed.
