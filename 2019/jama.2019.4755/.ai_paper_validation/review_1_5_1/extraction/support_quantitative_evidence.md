### Protocol/SAP timing reconciliation key

The protocol calls the medication day “day 1” and its post-dose collection days 3, 4, and 5. The SAP explicitly redefines medication intake as day 0, therefore protocol days 3, 4, and 5 are SAP days 2, 3, and 4 (DOC-003 PDF p. 4 footnote 1). This re-labelling is essential when matching main-paper day-2 results to the protocol.

## Formula and terminology register

The SAP supplies the following operational definitions: sensitivity = positive / diseased; specificity = negative / healthy; PPV = diseased / positive; NPV = healthy / negative; likelihood ratio for a result = its diseased proportion divided by its healthy proportion. The ROC construction is healthy positive proportion versus sensitivity over increasing positivity thresholds. The derived utilization metrics are total FITs divided by diseased positives, and positive FITs divided by diseased positives. BMI is weight in kg divided by squared height in m. The eTables label quantitative cutoffs in µg Hb/g, whereas the SAP raw quantitative measure is ng Hb/mL buffer.
