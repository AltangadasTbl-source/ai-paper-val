## Scope, authority, and method

- **Reviewer execution:** fresh `gpt-5.6-terra` high-effort statistical-pass-1 agent, runtime ID `/root/statistical_pass_1`.
- **Assigned stable scope:** every inferential relationship in `statistics/relationship_inventory.md`: `S001` through `S023` (23 relationships; 12 main-paper lanes and 11 support lanes).
- **Evidence used:** the current relationship and quantitative-evidence maps as locators, then the cited pages of the supplied direct PDFs: DOC-001 `jama_brenner_2019_oi_190039.pdf`, DOC-002 `joi190039supp1_prod.pdf`, DOC-003 `joi190039supp2_prod.pdf`, and DOC-004 `joi190039supp3_prod.pdf`. No legacy candidate/checker/recheck/final-report material was consulted.
- **Checks applied when applicable:** point-estimate containment and interval ordering; contrast sign and stated direction; matched repetitions; effect-measure/unit/scale labels; population, day, and analysis-set matching; count/denominator implications supplied by the mapped tables; and P-value/CI/test/statistic/SE compatibility only where the source itself supplied compatible inferential definitions.
- **Compatibility limit:** the PDFs do not give the exact test statistic, degrees of freedom, variance estimator, covariance, sidedness-to-CI mapping beyond the stated two-sided threshold where applicable, or a universal P-value calculation rule for the Table 2 contrasts. Consequently, no numerical P value was reconstructed from an interval, standard error, or convention. The direct logical checks below are not substitutions for the reported analyses.
- **Display-zero rule:** no relationship reports `P = 0`, `p = 0.000`, or an equivalent P-value display zero. `0.00E+00` in S022/S023 is the displayed random-intercept standard deviation, not a P value; therefore `DISPLAY_ZERO_NOT_CANDIDATE` is not applicable to this scope.

### SP1-001 — Lower quantitative-FIT cutoff is printed without its microgram unit in one SAP occurrence

- **Proposed primary category:** `Measure, label, or scale inconsistency`.
- **Affected relationship:** `S017` (also relevant to the cutoff-label evidence in `S014` and the main-paper results S004/S006).
- **Exact source locations:** DOC-003 `joi190039supp2_prod.pdf` PDF p. 6, §3.2, Primary variables; DOC-003 PDF p. 8, §5.4, Primary analysis; DOC-001 `jama_brenner_2019_oi_190039.pdf` PDF pp. 1 and 5 (abstract and Table 2).
- **Direct observation:** DOC-003 p. 6 prints the two quantitative-test thresholds as “17 μg Hb/g feces and 10.2 Hb/g feces.” DOC-003 p. 8 prints the lower threshold as “10.2 μg Hb/g feces.” DOC-001 prints the matched 10.2 cutoff as `10.2-μg Hb/g stool` / `10.2 μg Hb/g`.
- **Comparator and rule:** These locations identify the same lower FOBGold quantitative-test cutoff. A matched quantitative cutoff label should retain the unit prefix that defines its scale. The p. 6 `10.2 Hb/g feces` label omits `μg`, whereas the matched SAP and main-paper occurrences specify `μg Hb/g`.
- **Diagnostic limits:** This is a printed-label comparison, not a derived unit conversion or a claim that the analysed data were necessarily on the wrong scale. The source does not state whether the p. 6 omission was intentional shorthand inheriting `μg` from the preceding 17-unit threshold or a production omission. No external conversion, model assumption, or correction was imposed.
- **Exact human question:** Does DOC-003 p. 6 intentionally inherit the `μg` unit from the preceding threshold, or should the lower cutoff be explicitly printed as `10.2 μg Hb/g feces` to match the same SAP's §5.4 and the article's result tables?
