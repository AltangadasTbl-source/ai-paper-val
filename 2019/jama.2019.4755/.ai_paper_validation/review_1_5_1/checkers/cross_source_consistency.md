# Cross-Source Quantitative Consistency Check

## Scope, evidence rule, and result

This check covers the matched quantitative occurrences reconstructed in the completed maps and canonical inventories for DOC-001 through DOC-005: 62 numeric/reporting relationships (`N001`-`N062`) and 23 inferential/statistical relationships (`S001`-`S023`). Direct PDFs were the authority. Native/layout text was used only to locate material; the potentially qualifying eTable 5 cells were visually checked in the direct DOC-004 PDF.

Each comparison required identity of population, analysis set, time definition, treatment contrast, test and cutoff, outcome/measure, scale/unit, reference direction, and displayed precision. A planned value was not compared as an observed result; a per-protocol result was not compared as an intention-to-screen result. Protocol day 3 was reconciled to SAP/main-paper day 2 using the SAP's explicit medication-day-0 footnote.

- **Matched comparison groups checked:** 16.
- **Qualifying proposals:** 4 (`XC001`-`XC004`).
- **No-candidate matched groups:** 15.
- **Not-applicable source units:** DOC-002 administrative/appendix material, DOC-003 reference page, and DOC-005 availability statement; none contains a matched quantitative result.

## Qualifying proposals

## XC001 — eTable 5 omits the women’s quantitative-10.2 PPV difference point estimate

- **Category:** Numeric or arithmetic inconsistency.
- **Exact source location:** [joi190039supp3_prod.pdf — PDF p. 7](<../../../joi190039supp3_prod.pdf#page=7>), DOC-004, eTable 5, Women, Quantitative, cutoff 10.2 µg.
- **Printed values:** Aspirin PPV 15.9%; placebo PPV 34.1%; the `Difference in PPV [95% CI]` point-estimate cell is `-`, while its displayed 95% CI is [-34.7, -1.3].
- **Comparator and rule:** Every other eTable 5 row prints a signed PPV-difference point estimate before its CI. A difference column with two arm PPVs and a printed CI lacks its named point estimate in this row. The rounded arm values diagnostically imply a negative difference of approximately -18.2 percentage points (15.9 - 34.1), but exact unrounded inputs are required to establish the exact intended estimate.
- **Supported alternatives:** The dash may be an intentional display convention or a production omission; the supplied table does not explain it. It must not be replaced from rounded arm values without confirmation.
- **Human verification steps:** Open DOC-004 p. 7; confirm the dash and CI in this exact cell; inspect the production source or analysis output for the unrounded aspirin-minus-placebo PPV difference and whether the dash was intentional.
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

## XC004 — eTable 5 gives the cutoff unit as µg rather than µg Hb/g

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [joi190039supp3_prod.pdf — PDF p. 7](<../../../joi190039supp3_prod.pdf#page=7>), DOC-004, eTable 5, `Cutoff [µg]` header; and [joi190039supp3_prod.pdf — PDF p. 6](<../../../joi190039supp3_prod.pdf#page=6>), DOC-004, eTable 4, `Cutoff [µg Hb/g]` header for the same day-2 per-protocol tests and cutoff values.
- **Printed values:** eTable 5 lists cutoffs 10.2 and 17.0 under `Cutoff [µg]`. The immediately related eTable 4 reports the same 10.2 and 17.0 cutoffs under `Cutoff [µg Hb/g]`; the main article also describes them as micrograms Hb/g feces.
- **Comparator and rule:** After matching day-2 per-protocol population, treatment contrast, quantitative/qualitative test identity, and cutoff values, a cutoff labelled only in µg is dimensionally different from a concentration label in µg Hb/g. The eTable 5 PPV/NPV outcome differs from eTable 4 sensitivity/specificity, but that does not change the test-cutoff unit.
- **Supported alternatives:** `µg` may be a shortened header intended to inherit the established µg Hb/g unit rather than a different analytic scale. The supplied page does not state that inheritance, so the table alone does not establish whether the label was intentionally abbreviated.
- **Human verification steps:** Compare DOC-004 pp. 6-7 and the main article's cutoff definition; inspect the supplement table source or author-approved legend to determine whether eTable 5 should explicitly state µg Hb/g feces or whether a documented shared-header convention applies.
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

## Limitations

This checker evaluated supplied PDFs only. It did not use the web, legacy candidates/checkers/reports, external protocols, raw data, or unprovided production files. Point estimates inferred from rounded arm PPVs are explicitly diagnostic, not replacements for missing source values. No stable `C` identifier, AI validity decision, severity, or correction is assigned here.
