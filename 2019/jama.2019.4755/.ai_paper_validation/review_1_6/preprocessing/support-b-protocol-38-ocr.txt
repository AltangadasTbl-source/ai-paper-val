The eCRF does not contain the participant’s identifying information, but only his/her

participant number. The investigator will ensure that the participant’s pseudonymity will be

maintained. Entries in the eCRF may only be made by the investigator or persons authorized

by him. If corrections in the eCRF are necessary, the initial entry will be kept in an electronic

log (audit-trail). Potential mistakes found during monitoring visits will be corrected by

authorized site staff. Reason for change must be recorded.

A ist will be kept of the individuals who are authorized to make data changes.

Informed consent

For each participant in the clinical study written informed consent will be obtained and kept

by the investigator in the investigator site file.

Participant diary

All participants will receive a patient diary. In this diary, they shall document if and when

they took the study medication, if and when they collected the stool samples. This diary

should besend to the coordinating center in Heidelberg using a prepaid envelop.

List of participants (participants’ log, participant identification list

‘The responsible investigator of a study center keeps a confidential list with full names, date of

birth, and addresses of all participants in the study, giving reference to the participant’s

records. This list is kept in the investigator site file. For enrolled participants the date of

enrolment and the allocation/random number will be recorded. With the help of this list it will

be possible to identify the participants and their medical records, e.g. in case of questions

arising after completion of the study. The investigator is responsible for ensuring that this list

is kept confidential and is stored for 10 years after the end of the study. The identity of the

participants will not be revealed to unauthorized persons.

Screening failures will also be documented on this list (without a participation number, which

is available only after randomization) together with the reason for failure. Participant log is

optional.

9.12 Patient medical records/source data

Patient medical records will be kept for all patients taking part in the study. Participation in

the study, administration of study medication, and key data recorded during the study will be

documented in the patient medical record as source data unless specified otherwise in this

study protocol. Data in the patients’ medical records (including original histology reports,

colonoscopy reports, etc.) are the ‘source data” and entries into the eCRF will be checked

against these data (source data verification).

9.13 Drug accountability sheet

‘The investigator will document on the drug accountability sheet the receipt of study

medication, supply of study medication to patients, and return of study medication. All data

collected in the study will be reported on paper and kept for ten years.
