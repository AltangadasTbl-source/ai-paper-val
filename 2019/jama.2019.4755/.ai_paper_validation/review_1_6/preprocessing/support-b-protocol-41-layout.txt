10.4 Steering Committee
There will be a Steering Committee for this study, consisting of the director of the
coordinating center, the clinical pharmacology consultant, and the principal investigator of the
study (LKP). In addition, the principal investigator from each study center may participate.
This committee will meet once before the start of the study and regularly after this, either in
person or via a telephone conference, to discuss study progress, monitoring reports, and any
study-related problems. The conclusions of these meetings will be documented.


10.5 Publication policy
The results of this study will be written up in one or more scientific paper(s). The aim is to
publish the main results in a high-ranking international peer-reviewed medical journal.
Writing of all papers will be primarily led by the director of the coordinating center. The
study coordinator, the clinical pharmacology consultant, the head of the Department of
Clinical Pharmacology and Pharmacoepidemiology, the principal investigator of the study
(LKP), and, at least, one investigator of those study centers which contributed substantially to
the study will have the possibility to participate. Coauthorship will be based on: 1.
Contributions to study concept and design, acquisition of data, analysis and interpretation of
the data, 2. Drafting or revising the article for important intellectual content, and 3. Final
approval of the version to be published, according to the Uniform Requirements for
Manuscripts Submitted to Biomedical Journals (http://www.icmje.org/urm_main.html).
