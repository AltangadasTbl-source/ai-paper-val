10. QUALITY ASSURANCE AND MONITORING

10.1 Documentation and data handling
All participant data, except data from the participants diary, the questionnaire, colonoscopy
and histology reports, will be collected in an electronic Case Report Form (eCRF) by the
study physicians and physician assistants on site. All data will be transferred digitally into a
database at the DKFZ. Data entries will be checked for inconsistencies. In case of
inconsistencies, original reports will be checked for validation.
Participants will be asked to fill out in a participant diary if and when they took the study
medication, if and when they collected the stool samples and which other medication (if any)
they took during the study. In addition, they are asked to bring the empty package of the study
medication or any leftover medication when they come to the study center for the
colonoscopy.
The information collected in the diary and from histology and colonoscopy reports will be
entered into an additional standardized study database using double entry by two independent
and blinded reviewers. Discrepant coding will be resolved according to standard operating
procedures to achieve the maximum accuracy possible. Data entries will be checked for
inconsistencies through comparison of data sets. In case of differences in data sets, original
reports will be checked for validation. Missing data or reports will be requested from the
study centers by (e)mail or collected during monitoring visits.


10.2 Monitoring
Monitoring procedures will be adapted to the study-specific risk for the patients, interpretation
of ICH-GCP (E6) and GCP-V, and standard operating procedures (SOP) of the KKS to ensure
patient safety and the integrity of the clinical data.
Source documents (including pseudonymized colonoscopy reports, histology reports,
questionnaires and participant diaries) from the participating study centers will be checked for
completeness and plausibility by the monitorat the coordinating center in Heidelberg. The
recruitment rate and compliance with eligibility criteria will be supervised and reported back
to the study centers, the study coordinator, and the clinical pharmacology consultant.
The participating study centers will be visited regularly, including an initiation visit, a number
of interim visits during the recruitment phase and a close-out visit. The number of visits per
center (excluding initiation and close-out visits) is estimated at 4 per year, but may vary
depending on data quality. Two of these visits will be done by the KKS, the other two will be
done by the Division of Clinical Epidemiology and Aging Research of the German Cancer
