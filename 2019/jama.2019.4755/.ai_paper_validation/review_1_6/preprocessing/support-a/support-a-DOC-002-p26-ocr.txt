6.9 Feasibility of recruitment
‘To compensate for the time and effort invested and to ensure a high participation rate, the
study centers will receive a financial incentive (75€) for each patient recruited. This incentive
will be given to the study centers after receipt of the colonoscopy/histology results or after
notification by the study center that a participant who had been included and randomized did
not undergo colonoscopy within 3 months and the study center has completed the study for
this participant according to the study protocol (e.g. documentation of adverse events). In
spite of the fact that this financial incentive will be relatively low, we expect that inclusion of
2,400 participants will be feasible, based on our previous experiences in a large ongoing
‘multi-center observational study of early detection of colorectal carcinoma (BliTz Study), in
which a smaller financial incentive is given and 4,150 participants from a number of smaller
gastroenterology private practices have been included since January 2006. The cooperation
structure with the gastroenterology practices that has been built up for this observational study
is highly successful, which supports the feasibility of recruiting participants in this manner.
Given the size of the study centers involved in this study and our experience so far, we expect
that recruitment of 2,400 participants for this study should be possible within a 48-month
period. If recruitment rates are too low, additional study centers may be included.
6.10 Stopping rules
For the individual participant
Participants can discontinue participation in the study at any time without giving a reason. In
addition, participants may ask to have their samples destroyed. The investigator will continue
to observe patients withdrawn from the study because of intolerable AEs until the findings
have been clarified. In all cases where the patient withdraws within 3 weeks, the reason for
withdrawal must be recorded in the eCRF (electronic Case Report Form). It is not allowed to
destroy data generated in the study. Therefore, in the case of withdrawal of consent the stored
data will be checked for further use. Upon request of the participant data not necessary any
longer is deleted immediately. Collected personal data will be stored in a pseudonymous
‘manner after completion and publication of the main trial, for at least 10 years, if there are no
other regulatory or contractually archiving periods.
‘The investigator may decide to withdraw a participant, if the participant suffers from a serious
