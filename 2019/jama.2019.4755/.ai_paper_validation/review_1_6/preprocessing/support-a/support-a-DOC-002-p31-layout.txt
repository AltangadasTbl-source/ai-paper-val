For other (higher risk) endoscopic procedures the guideline recommends stopping
acetylsalicylic acid 5 days before the procedure. To minimize any risk in the present study
(where acetylsalicylic acid is given without a clinical indication) a washout phase of at least 4
acetylsalicylic acid-free days before the planned colonoscopy was chosen. If a participant has
taken the study drug too late, so that there are less than 4 full days between the day on which
the study drug was taken and the day on which the colonoscopy is scheduled, the participant
is withdrawn from the study or the colonoscopy postponed. In case of withdrawal, the
decision whether the colonoscopy can take place or not then lies with the responsible
physician, based on clinical grounds.
