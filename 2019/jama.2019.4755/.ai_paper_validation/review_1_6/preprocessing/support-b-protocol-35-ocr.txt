9.2 Submission to the Ethics Committees and Competent Authority (BfATM)

‘The required documents will be submitted to the Ethics Committee of the Medical Faculty of

the University of Heidelberg (‘*federfiihrende Ethikkommission”) and the opinion of the

Committee will be obtained in writing. The required documents will also be submitted to the

relevant other ethics committees (“*beteiligte Ethikkommission”).

‘The required documents will be submitted to the BfArM and their approval obtained in

writing. Participants will not be included until unconditional approval of BfArM and the

ethics committees is received in writing.

If the study is terminated as planned, the competent authorities, including the Ethics

Committees and the BArM, will be informed about this within 90 days. In case of unplanned,

premature termination, this should be done within 15 days.

Within one year after the end of the study, a clinical study report will be written and sent to

the competent authorities, as described in the GCP-V.

9.3 Notification of Authorities

According to § 67 of the German “Arzneimittelgesetz” the local competent authorities will be

notified about the study.

9.4 Participant information and informed consent

Before an individual is included in the study, the objectives, risks, and methods of the study

will be explained to him or her orally and in writing. The individual gives his/her consent by

signing an informed consent form. With their consent, participants agree that their data are

given to the competent authorities in pseudonymised form, if necessary. In line with GCP, the

participants will receive a copy of the signed and dated informed consent form and the written

participant information. If there are any updates to the consent forms or amendments to the

written information during the subject’s participation in the study, the subject will be

informed about this.
