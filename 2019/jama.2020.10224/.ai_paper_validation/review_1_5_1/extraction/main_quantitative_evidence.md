## Scope and method

- **Direct source:** `jama_okereke_2020_oi_200066.pdf` (10 PDF pages; printed article pp. 471-480).
- **Scope completed:** every PDF page 1-10. Native and layout text were used as locators; each page and each table/figure reported below was directly inspected in the PDF render. Page references below are PDF pages.
- **Population/intervention frame:** VITAL-DEP ancillary randomized trial, vitamin D3 2000 IU/d versus matching placebo, within a 2 × 2 factorial design also involving fish oil. The PHQ-8 scale is 0 (least symptoms) to 24 (most symptoms); its stated minimal clinically important change is 0.5 points.

## Page-by-page evidence

### PDF p. 1 (abstract; printed p. 471)

The abstract defines 18 353 participants aged at least 50 years, 16 657 at risk for incident depression and 1696 at risk for recurrent depression. Randomization occurred November 2011-March 2014; treatment and follow-up ended December 31, 2017. It reports 9181 randomized to vitamin D3 and 9172 to placebo; mean age 67.5 (SD 7.1) years; 49.2% women; median treatment duration 5.3 years; completion 90.5%, or 93.5% among those alive at trial end.

For the primary total-depression outcome, vitamin D3 had 609 events and 12.9 per 1000 person-years and placebo had 625 events and 13.3 per 1000 person-years; HR 0.97 (95% CI 0.87-1.09), P = .62. For longitudinal mood, the between-group mean difference in change is 0.01 points (95% CI −0.04 to 0.05). The conclusion repeats no statistically significant difference over median 5.3 years.

### PDF p. 2 (introduction/methods; printed p. 472)

Current-study quantitative definitions: 18 353 adults without depression at baseline; six annual mood assessments; parent VITAL n = 25 871 (men aged at least 50 years and women aged at least 55 years), randomized November 2011-March 2014. Vitamin D3 was 2000 IU/d; fish oil was 1 g/d containing 840 mg omega-3 fatty acids (465 mg EPA and 375 mg DHA); factorial randomization blocks had size 8. Baseline serum samples were collected in 16 956 participants. Follow-up ended December 31, 2017 with median 5.3 years (IQR 5.0-5.7). Ancillary eligibility after exclusions was 18 353.

### PDF p. 3 (Figure 1 and outcomes; printed p. 473)

**Figure 1, participant flow (all printed counts):** Parent VITAL randomized 25 871: vitamin D3 12 927 and placebo 12 944. Excluded: vitamin D3 3746 (13 VITAL diagnosis dates inconsistently reported; 3733 VITAL-DEP exclusions) and placebo 3772 (4 and 3768, respectively). The nonexclusive VITAL-DEP exclusion categories are vitamin D3/placebo: core depression features 1572/1580; current medication or counseling 1501/1493; missing/unknown depression status 1288/1326; alcohol/substance disorder, delirium, neurocognitive, psychotic, bipolar, PTSD/OCD, or clinical hypothyroidism category 826/804; current symptoms PHQ-8 at least 10, 371/384. The figure explicitly says individuals could meet more than one exclusion criterion.

Included VITAL-DEP and primary-analysis counts are 9181 vitamin D3 and 9172 placebo. At intervention end, known/presumed alive was 8859/8872 and deaths 322/300 (each pair vitamin D3/placebo).

Outcome definition: two primary outcomes are total depression/clinically relevant depressive symptoms and longitudinal mood scores. A depression event includes new reported clinician diagnosis, new treatment, or PHQ-8 at least 10 on annual questionnaires. A CTSC validation subset has n = 1054. Incident risk is participants without current symptoms or history; recurrent risk is history of depression but no current clinically relevant symptoms and no treatment in the previous two years; the article states that incident cases recurring later were not double counted.
