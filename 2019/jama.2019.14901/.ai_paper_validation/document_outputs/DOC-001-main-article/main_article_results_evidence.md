| Flow stage | Reported count and detail |
|---|---|
| Screened population | 3121 patients extubated in 30 participating ICUs, April 2017-January 2018. |

| High-risk extubated after ≥24 h mechanical ventilation | 1661. Footnote: high risk was age >65 y or underlying chronic cardiac or lung disease. |
| Second exclusions | 692: 274 do-not-reintubate order at extubation; 182 long-term NIV or continuous positive airway pressure at home; 119 unplanned extubation; 41 contraindication to NIV; 39 underlying chronic neuromuscular disease; 37 traumatic brain injury. |
