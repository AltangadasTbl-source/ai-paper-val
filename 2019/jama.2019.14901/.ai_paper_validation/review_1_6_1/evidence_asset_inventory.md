# Reusable evidence-asset inventory

128 eligible existing assets were inventoried and hashed. They comprise native/layout text, OCR text, rendered pages, extraction maps, page manifests, document records, and OCR metadata. Legacy policy records were preserved but are not extraction assets. No old candidate, checker, screening, classification, or final report was used. Every file below is read-only; hashes cover all assets designated as source locators, transcription aids, visual evidence, or extraction provenance, including overlapping provenance assets.

Fitness applies to extraction and location coverage only. `PARTIAL` map/metadata records do not independently establish full-page transcription. A usable companion asset can close their derivative scope. Overlapping images/text are retained as complementary evidence rather than independently counted source units.

Every page-native text asset was compared with direct `pdftotext -layout -f N -l N SOURCE -` after removing whitespace; all 69 match. Engine: Poppler 26.01.0. Existing OCR engine: RapidOCR ONNX Runtime CPU, as recorded in page metadata and ocr_backend.json; native/layout engine is direct PDF text, consistent with current Poppler layout output. Rendered PNGs were checked with `file`; all are readable PNGs. Existing image renderer/version is not fully recorded and is not fabricated. No new derivatives were produced in curation.
