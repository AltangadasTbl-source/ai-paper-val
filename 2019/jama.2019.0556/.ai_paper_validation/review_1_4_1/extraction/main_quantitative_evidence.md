## Cross-location checks and candidate handoff

No direct-PDF/OCR transcription disagreement was found in the visual assets used.

One candidate-level relationship is retained for downstream checking: **N032**, the duplicated “supplements without therapy” label in Table 2 footnote d. It is a printed measure/label inconsistency, not an alteration of the reported event counts, percentages, OR, CI, or P value. It requires human adjudication.
