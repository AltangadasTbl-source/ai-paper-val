# Protocol Quantitative Evidence Map — DOC-002

## Scope and method

This mapper covers the complete 60-page protocol, `joi190007supp1_prod.pdf` (DOC-002), version 7 dated July 11, 2017. Every PDF page was directly extracted and read with layout-preserving text extraction. The protocol is a prespecification source: the records below map numerical, population, endpoint, timing, denominator, contrast, and analysis rules that can be compared to reported results; they do not diagnose any inconsistency. Direct-PDF links are the authority.

Mapper-local labels use `P-N` for quantitative/reporting relationships and `P-S` for statistical relationships; they do not alter package-wide `N`/`S` identifiers.

## Design, population, allocation, and intervention rules

| Local ID | Exact source location | Mapped prespecified relationship |
|---|---|---|
| P-N001 | [DOC-002 — PDF p. 8](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=8>); [p. 15](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=15>) | One-year 2 × 2 factorial, randomized, placebo-controlled prevention trial with multinutrient supplement versus placebo and food-related behavioural change (FBC) versus no behavioural-change therapy. Both participants and researchers are blinded to pill/placebo assignment. |
| P-N002 | [p. 15](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=15>); [p. 16](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=16>) | Four planned cells each contain 250 participants (about 63 in the Netherlands): supplement+FBC, supplement+no therapy, placebo+FBC, placebo+no therapy; the four-country pooled target is 1000. Recruitment is planned July 2015-June 2016. |
| P-N003 | [p. 16](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=16>) | Eligibility is age 18-75 years, BMI 25-40, and PHQ-9 ≥5. Exclusions include current/past-6-month DSM-IV MDD by MINI, recent antidepressant or psychological treatment, specified psychiatric/substance conditions, competing supervised behaviour intervention or nutritional supplements, prescribed supplements, pregnancy/breastfeeding, bariatric surgery, severe illness, or severe cognitive impairment. These rules define the intended analysis population. |
| P-N004 | [p. 8](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=8>); [p. 28](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=28>) | The main endpoint is 12-month cumulative incident MDD assessed with MINI at baseline, 3, 6, and 12 months. The summary calls the diagnostic standard DSM-IV; the methods page calls it DSM-5 with MINI V5.01. Both printed definitions are retained as prespecified source facts. |
| P-N005 | [p. 8](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=8>); [p. 28](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=28>); [pp. 34-36](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=34>) | Depressive symptomatology is measured by IDS/IDS-SR30 at baseline, 3, 6, and 12 months. PHQ-9, GAD-7, EQ-5D-5L, TFEQ-18, food/diet, activity, sedentary behaviour, body-image, habit, and behavioural-activation measures have the displayed baseline/follow-up schedule; depression and secondary outcomes (except evaluation questions) are also intended at 24 months. |
| P-N006 | [p. 16](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=16>)-[p. 17](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=17>) | Sample-size assumptions: the protocol states a `difference of 25%`, followed by 30% 12-month DSM-IV depressive-disorder incidence in placebo/no-FBC control versus 15% in the intervention group; additive-interaction planning uses 180 per cell, two-sided α=.05, power .90, and 20% dropout, yielding 250 per cell/1000 total. If synergistic, pure treatment groups are compared with stated main-outcome power .80. |
| P-N007 | [p. 8](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=8>); [pp. 18-19](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=18>); [pp. 24-26](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=24>) | The intended 12-month supplement regimen is daily omega-3 capsule (>700 mg EPA and >100 mg DHA; described elsewhere as 1000 mg, EPA:DHA 3:1) plus multinutrient pill (calcium 100-200 mg, selenium 25-55 μg, folate/B11 400-600 μg, vitamin D 20 μg); placebo contains corresponding inert/sunflower-oil products. FBC comprises 21 sessions: 15 individual ×30 minutes and 6 group ×60 minutes. |
| P-N008 | [p. 18](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=18>); [p. 26](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=26>) | Pill/capsule supplies are distributed at baseline and months 3, 6, and 9; returned bottles and unused pills/capsules are weighed at follow-up to calculate nonconsumption, and an adherence questionnaire is completed every 3 months. |
| P-N009 | [p. 29](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=29>)-[p. 30](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=30>); [pp. 33-37](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=33>) | Follow-up visits occur at months 3, 6, and 12; an online 24-month follow-up is intended. MINI-present-MDD is scheduled at 3/6/12 months; lifetime MDD is baseline only. Optional blood collections are at baseline/6/12 months, with the displayed total 37 mL at each of those times. |
| P-N010 | [p. 39](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=39>) | A participant may leave at any time; intervention withdrawal prompts recording reasons and encouragement to continue measurements, expressly to minimize follow-up loss and permit both intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses. The estimated dropout is already included in sample-size planning. |
| P-N011 | [p. 45](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=45>); [p. 47](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=47>) | A participant developing MDD reaches the study endpoint but may continue. MINI-diagnosed MDD with IDS ≥39 and/or suicidal thoughts/plans triggers GP contact; the later safety protocol also states participants with IDS >13 may continue unless hospitalization is required. These are follow-up/safety rules rather than reported outcome values. |

## Prespecified statistical relationships

| Local ID | Exact source location | Mapped prespecified relationship |
|---|---|---|
| P-S001 | [DOC-002 — PDF p. 43](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=43>) | Data from all four study centres are pooled. Main analyses follow intention-to-treat principles; per-protocol analyses are supplementary. Baseline four-group comparisons use chi-square tests, t tests, and analyses of (co)variance. |
| P-S002 | [p. 43](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=43>) | For incident MDD time-to-event data, planned analyses are Kaplan-Meier, log-rank, and Cox proportional-hazards models. The factorial design has two-way and four-way analyses for separate and combined intervention effects, adjusted for randomization strata country and history of depression. |
| P-S003 | [p. 43](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=43>) | Mixed-model regression with baseline/3/6/12 measurements tests main effects and interaction on MDD onset and IDS severity, accounting for repeated within-participant observations and country clustering. |
| P-S004 | [p. 43](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=43>) | Secondary-outcome mixed models test the two nutritional strategies and their interaction for food intake, BMI/weight, activity, sedentary behaviour, PHQ-9, anxiety, body image, eating-behaviour factors, and quality of life, allowing country clustering. |
| P-S005 | [pp. 43-44](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=43>) | Planned mediation uses four-step regression with bootstrapping for indirect effects through food behaviour/intake, serum nutritional status, body image, eating factors, weight, physical activity, and sedentary behaviour on MDD onset and depressive-symptom change. A blood-sample subgroup uses linear regression for nutritional-status change, adjusted for intervention type. |
| P-S006 | [p. 44](<../../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=44>) | Planned effect-modification terms are country, sex, education, income, baseline BMI, and age group; they enter Cox models for incident MDD and mixed models for depressive symptoms. |
