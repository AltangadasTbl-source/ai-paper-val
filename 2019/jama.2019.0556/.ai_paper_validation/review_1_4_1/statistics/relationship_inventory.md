## Shared labels and analysis constraints

- OR and HR have a null value of 1; B and Cohen d have a null value of 0.  PHQ, IDS, and GAD higher scores indicate worse symptoms; higher utility indicates better health utility.  These directions are defined in DOC-001 p. 4.
- DOC-001 Table 3 estimates are effect-coded coefficients.  Its footnote expressly requires multiplying estimate and CI by 2 to obtain adjusted differences between intervention conditions.  That displayed conversion rule supports the matching narrative translations in S015; it is not silently generalized to other model displays that do not print an equivalent conversion statement.
- DOC-004 p. 21 reports B/95% CI for overall no-MI GEE analyses and B/SE for T12 robust linear regressions.  DOC-004 p. 22 CACE models use multiply imputed data, adjust for site/history of MDD, use logistic regression for MDD, and ANCOVA for symptom scores; `*` means `P<.05` and `**` means `P<.01`.
- The exact family of confidence interval, sidedness for each individual test, degrees of freedom, covariance estimators, and unrounded values are not printed for many relationships.  PASS 1 uses only stated labels and approximate compatibility checks; it does not infer unstated model details.
- DOC-002 and DOC-003 are prospective protocol/analysis-plan evidence.  Their planned-model statements are retained for cross-source checking, but an apparent difference from the published analysis is not itself a candidate without a source-established requirement that the plan was final and unchanged.
- DOC-003 p. 3 is an exception requiring a candidate observation: its printed two-sided significance-threshold sentence omits the operator that makes `p 0.05` a usable comparison rule.
