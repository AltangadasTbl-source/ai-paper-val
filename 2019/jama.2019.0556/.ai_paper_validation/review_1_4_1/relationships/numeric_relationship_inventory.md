# Unified Numeric and Reporting Relationship Inventory

## Scope and identifier rule

This is the canonical numeric/reporting inventory for the two completed mapper scopes. Mapper-local identifiers were replaced with the unique stable IDs below. It includes all mapped counts, totals, proportions, scales, units, population/time definitions, labels, and result-linked narrative/table/figure relationships. Inferential relationships (`S` records in the mapping artifacts) are assigned to the statistical lanes and are not duplicated here except where a numeric label or definition is necessary for a numeric check.
