## C001 — Planned total and four factorial cell counts

**Pending Human Adjudication.** Discovery provenance: NC-01; XC09; SC01; N010; S001.

**Candidate statement:** The main article describes 392 participants as 196 in each of four intervention combinations; those literal total and per-cell counts do not reconcile.

**Category:** Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency

**Relationship type:** S_W

**All relationships:** S_W

**Compared document roles:** REPORT main p4 total versus four cell counts

**Review domain:** statistical_results

**Evidence state:** recorded_candidate

**Suggested review disposition:** retain

**Classification rationale:** Both conflicting expressions occur in one REPORT paragraph, giving S_W only. PLAN descriptions merely corroborate the factorial structure; the denominator/total category derives statistical_results.

**Classification ambiguity:** What 392 and 196 each count is unresolved; document role and within-document relationship are clear.

**Exact source locations:** [jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf — physical PDF p. 4, Sample Size paragraph](<../jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf#page=4>)

**Source evidence:** The sentence prints “392 participants (196 in each of the 4 possible intervention combinations)”; it subsequently gives 250 per combination allowing 22% attrition. The paragraph specifies power for the main effect of each intervention.

**Reported-versus-comparator:** Printed total 392 versus four printed cell counts of 196. This concerns planning wording, separate from the observed randomized N 1025.

**Reasoning procedure:** Match the count unit within the sentence and sum four equal cells. Integer counts permit no rounding tolerance. A marginal main-effect comparison must be distinguished from a whole-trial total.

**Calculation:** 4 × 196 = 784; 784 − 392 = 392. This is exact count arithmetic, not a reconstructed power calculation.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** The stated main-effect target supports the possibility that 392 is a marginal two-group quantity. The source does not define that count unit, so a marginal explanation is possible but unestablished.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** Visual source inspection matched 392, 196, four combinations and 250. The literal count identity was reproduced; the intended count unit remains missing. This determines recorded_candidate.

**Quality-control relevance:** Incompatible planned total and cell counts impede reproducible extraction of the sample-size description.

**Potential downstream evidence impact:** If confirmed, a reviewer could copy an incompatible planned total or per-cell N into a trial characteristics table. No achieved-power or conclusion effect is established.

**Human verification steps:** Read the cited sentence and obtain the governing power worksheet. What population or comparison does each of 392 and 196 count, and how should the four-combination parenthetical be understood?
