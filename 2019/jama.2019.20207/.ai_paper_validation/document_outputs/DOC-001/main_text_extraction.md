- **Document ID / source:** DOC-001 — `jama_parsons_2020_oi_190140.pdf` (main article; 9 PDF pages).
- **Scope/source basis:** Native and normalized derived text for PDF pages 1–9; page image checked for PDF page 5 (printed page 144) to confirm Table 1 and the adjacent outcome text. No external source used. PDF page numbers below are followed by the printed journal page in parentheses.
- **Outcome terminology:** The protocol-specified primary outcome was time to progression (TTP), from randomization to PSA >=10 ng/mL, PSA doubling time <3 years, or pathological progression on follow-up biopsy (PDF p3/printed p142, **Primary Outcome**). The article labels analyses other than the primary outcome as exploratory because of multiple comparisons (PDF p4/p143, **Statistical Methods**).
