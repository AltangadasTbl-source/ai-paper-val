Additional Analysis
1. We will repeat the primary efficacy analysis based on the Kaplan-Meier method where
clinical progression only considers Gleason score in its definition (i.e. for men < 70 years
old, Gleason sum on repeat biopsy ≥ 7; for men ≥ 70 years old, Gleason sum on repeat
biopsy ≥ (4 + 3) = 7); in other words, the definition of clinical progression in this analysis
will ignore serial PSA values and changes in tumor volume.
