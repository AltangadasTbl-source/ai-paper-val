## C001 — Vitamin D middle-baseline count and percentage

Category: Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency. Confirmed relationship S_W; all relationships S_W. Provenance: numeric-main NM-01; N022/N051-N053. Both discrepancy sides are REPORT main article. No PLAN comparator is needed.

Exact locations: [main PDF p. 5, Table 1 baseline 25(OH)D rows](../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=5); [p. 4, baseline strata](../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=4); [p. 6, missingness](../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=6). Printed vitamin D cells are low 102 (41), middle 142 (58), high 4 (1.6); randomized vitamin D n=251. The package gives seven baseline values missing overall and pooled strata 173/232/5.

Rule and calculation: common measured category denominator 102+142+4=248; 100×142/248=57.2581%, 0.7419 percentage point from printed 58%, beyond nearest-integer tolerance 0.5 point. Randomized denominator 251 gives 56.5737%, also not 58%. The observation concerns the individual cell, not a rounded column sum. Direct facts are printed counts and percentages; the measured denominator is a derived identity.

Alternatives and missing definition: an undisclosed cell-specific denominator, count transcription or rounding convention may explain the pair. Removing the high group yields 58.1967% for middle but conflicts with a common low-cell percentage. Human question: which denominator and rounding rule produced 142 (58), and do all three baseline cells use one measured population? Potential reuse: extraction of baseline subgroup composition. No conclusion change is inferred.
