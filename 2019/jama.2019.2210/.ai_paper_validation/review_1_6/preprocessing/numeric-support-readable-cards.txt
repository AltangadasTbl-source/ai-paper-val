### NS-A — Initial PLAN daily dose and capsule strength do not reconcile

Rule and calculation: daily IU = capsules/day × IU/capsule; 2 × 600 = 1200 versus 2 × 1000 = 2000, a difference of 800 IU/day. Tolerance: zero for these stated integer strengths. Direct observation: different capsule strengths inside the same supplied initial protocol. Derived inference: the capsule-preparation statement implies a daily dose that differs from the objective and intervention. Alternative: later 1000-IU language may have been copied into the initial version. The amendment on p.45 documents the later 1200 → 2000 change but does not resolve this internal version discrepancy. Quality-control relevance: the supplied initial dose can be extracted two ways, affecting treatment labels in evidence tables. No conclusion impact is asserted. Exact human question: Which capsule strength and daily dose belong to the initial protocol, and was p.12 retrospectively updated? All compared discrepancy locations are PLAN.

### NS-C — RFS censor rule uses OR where the endpoint definition uses AND

Relationship N149. Compared locations and roles: [initial endpoint p.9](../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=9) PLAN and [final endpoint p.25](../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=25) PLAN define the earliest relapse or death and censor patients who do not relapse AND remain alive. [Initial censor p.13](../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=13) PLAN and [final censor p.29](../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=29) PLAN say relapse-free OR alive. Printed inputs are the event and censor labels; no event count is printed at these locations. Reproducible rule: for R = relapse and F = death, event = R OR F and no event = NOT R AND NOT F. The printed OR censor rule also accepts R = 1, F = 0 and R = 0, F = 1. Tolerance: zero logical-state disagreements. Direct observation: conflicting censor labels for the same quantitative time-to-event measure. Derived inference: the literal OR rule labels an event as censored. No assertion is made that this rule was implemented. Alternative: informal or mistranslated OR wording while the analysis used the intended AND. Quality-control relevance: the RFS statistic and estimand definition can be misextracted. Exact human question: Is OR a wording error, and which event and censor definition generated the reported RFS? All compared discrepancy locations are PLAN.
