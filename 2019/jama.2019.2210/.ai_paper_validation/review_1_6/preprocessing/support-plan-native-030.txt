2.

Sample size calculation
We postulated that the 5-year RFS would be 75% and 62% in the vitamin D and
placebo groups, respectively, with a type I error (two-sided) of 5% and a power of
80%, assuming a 1% loss to follow-up. Therefore, we calculated that 400 patients
with digestive tract cancers divided in a 3:2 ratio (Vitamin D group, n = 240;
placebo group, n = 160) would be sufficient to detect a significant difference.
