## Numeric rules and precision

Count equality and subgroup sums use zero-count tolerance after matching population, time and outcome. Percent cells use half of the displayed last percentage unit (0.05 percentage point for one decimal; 0.5 for integer percentage), permitting a tie to round either way. Mixed integer and decimal display precision is applied per cell. Medians use printed precision, ordering and units; medians of within-person change need not equal percentage changes of marginal medians. Hazard ratio positivity, CI endpoint order, estimate containment, reference and effect direction are checked, but numeric checking does not reconstruct P values without compatible test/variance details. At-risk arrays must be nonnegative integers and nonincreasing over 0,2,4,6 years, with zero tolerance. Mutually exclusive and exhaustive subgroup arrays are compared at each common time only when the definitions establish the same population. Missing genotype/BMI/site data are permitted and explicitly separated from arithmetic discrepancies. Cumulative hazard heights are not event percentages or HRs.
