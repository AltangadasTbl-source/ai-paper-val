# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- Some legacy native-text, OCR, and page-image derivatives point to historical absolute source paths and lack fingerprints for the current package files. They are retained unchanged but classified `STALE`; current source-matched native text or fresh direct-PDF inspection covers every affected source unit.
- Reusable current native text is a locator and transcription aid. Candidate evidence will be mechanically rechecked against the direct PDFs.
- The package contains no workbook, CSV, DOC, or DOCX direct source and therefore provides no structured raw-result source for independent recalculation beyond the printed documents.
- Missing statistical definitions, if any, will be named at the relationship or candidate level rather than inferred.
- Candidate-level unresolved inputs include SNP-specific available-case denominators, BMI missingness, Figure 3 weighting semantics, the operative accrual-stop rule, the final de novo-cancer endpoint hierarchy, the intended high-25(OH)D boundary operator, the intended Cdx2/CDK2 locus label, participant-level identity of randomization and first-dose dates, and Table 2 HR contrast orientation.
- Several supplement subgroup figures omit model, adjustment, test, confidence-interval construction, sidedness, and HR reference-orientation details; checks therefore use containment, endpoint order, printed threshold coherence, and exact repeated-result comparisons without inventing those definitions.
- The review is limited to quantitative and statistical reporting consistency within the supplied package. It is not a general study-design, clinical, novelty, misconduct, or raw-data audit.
