- Boundary: this artifact maps evidence and relationships. It does not diagnose candidates, adjudicate observations, or use legacy candidate/report conclusions.

Provisional keys below are stable within this extraction artifact. `MP-N###` denotes a numeric, denominator, label, scale, timing, or population relationship; `MP-S###` denotes an inferential-statistical relationship. The coordinator may translate these into canonical `N###` and `S###` IDs without losing the source locations or component values.

## Numeric and reporting relationships

### MP-N001 — Trial design, centers, dates, and follow-up

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): 36-week randomized clinical trial at 4 academic medical centers; acute treatment lasted up to 12 weeks; qualifying remission/near-remission was maintained for 8 weeks before randomization; study period November 2011 to June 2017; final follow-up June 13, 2017.
- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): the same 4 centers, November 2011-June 2017 period, and June 13, 2017 final follow-up are repeated.

### MP-N002 — Study phases and randomized population

- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): 3 phases were named: acute, stabilization, and randomization. Acute-phase enrollment age was 18-85 years.
- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): stabilization lasted 8 weeks; persons still meeting full- or near-remission criteria and with MMSE at least 24 were eligible for the 36-week randomized phase.

### MP-N003 — Acute treatment doses and pill strengths

- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): sertraline target 150-200 mg/d in 50-mg pills; olanzapine target 15-20 mg/d in 5-mg pills.
- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): at randomization, median sertraline dose 150 mg/d (IQR, 150-200) and median olanzapine dose 15 mg/d (IQR, 10-20).

### MP-N004 — Randomized allocations and intervention timing

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): 64 allocated to continue olanzapine and 62 allocated to switch to placebo; all continued sertraline.
- [DOC-001 p. 4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): computer-generated 1:1 allocation; block-size range 4-8; stratified by age 18-59 versus 60-85 years, remission versus near remission, and site. Placebo assignment used a 4-week olanzapine taper.

### MP-N005 — Abstract randomized cohort demographics

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): 126 randomized participants; mean age 55.3 years (SD, 14.9); 78 women (61.9%).

### MP-N006 — Abstract completion total

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): 114 of 126 randomized participants (90.5%) are described as having completed the trial.
- Matching flow components are separately preserved in MP-N019 and MP-N020 rather than interpreted here.

### MP-N007 — Relapse counts and percentages repeated across summary locations

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): relapse in 13 olanzapine participants (20.3%) and 34 placebo participants (54.8%).
- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): Key Points repeats 13 (20.3%) and 34 (54.8%) among 126 persons.
- [DOC-001 p. 6](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=6): results text specifies 13/64 (20.3%) and 34/62 (54.8%).

### MP-N009 — Acute-phase diagnostic and exclusion thresholds

- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): current major depressive episode with at least 1 associated delusion; 17-item HDRS total at least 21; delusion-severity item at least 3; at least 2 on any of 3 conviction items.
- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): exclusion included IQCODE mean at least 4, substance abuse/dependence within the preceding 3 months, and type 1 diabetes, among other nonnumeric clinical exclusions.

### MP-N010 — Remission and near-remission definitions

- [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=2): full remission required absent delusions/hallucinations plus 17-item HDRS at most 10 for 2 consecutive weeks.
- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): near remission after 12 weeks required absent delusions/hallucinations, HDRS 11-15 with at least 50% reduction from baseline, and Clinical Global Impression rating of “very much improved” or “much improved.”

### MP-N011 — Outcome identities and metabolic measurement frequency

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1): primary outcome was risk of relapse; main secondary outcomes were change in weight, waist circumference, lipids, serum glucose, and HbA1c.
- [DOC-001 p. 4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): weight and waist were measured at each visit; fasting cholesterol, triglycerides, glucose, and HbA1c at randomized baseline, every 8 weeks, and termination.

### MP-N012 — Visit schedule and relapse criteria

- [DOC-001 p. 4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): visits weekly during the first 8 weeks, then once every 4 weeks until week 36, relapse, or early termination.
- Relapse required at least 1 of: DSM-IV major depressive episode; 17-item HDRS at least 18; SCID-rated psychosis; or other significant worsening (suicide plan/attempt, mania/hypomania, or psychiatric hospitalization).

### MP-N013 — Movement-scale ranges and incident definitions

- [DOC-001 p. 4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): Simpson-Angus total range 0-40; Barnes global assessment range 0-5; AIMS described as 0-5 on each of 10 items.
- Incident akathisia: Barnes global score 0 at randomized baseline and at least 2 subsequently. Incident tardive dyskinesia: Schooler-Kane criteria.
- [DOC-001 p. 6](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=6): Table 2 footnotes describe Barnes global range 0-5, AIMS global severity range 0-4, and Simpson-Angus total excluding head-dropping item range 0-36.

### MP-N014 — Other adverse-effect operational definitions

- [DOC-001 p. 4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): UKU scale range 0-3 on each of 48 items. Except weight gain/loss, an adverse effect was present for a 2-point increase, or score 3 or 4 plus an increase from baseline.
- Adverse weight gain: measured weight more than 7% above premorbid weight; adverse weight loss: more than 7% below premorbid weight.

### MP-N015 — Planned and revised randomized sample size

- [DOC-001 p. 4](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): planned n=176 for 80% power to detect a 20% risk difference with up to 15% attrition; a 20% difference was equated to treating 5 patients to prevent 1 relapse.
- After 3 years of recruitment, revised target n=128 because overall relapse risk was higher than anticipated.

### MP-N016 — Figure 1 consent, eligibility, and acute-phase totals

- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): 350 consented/assessed; 81 excluded (60 not eligible, 16 withdrew consent, 5 other); 269 started open-label sertraline plus olanzapine; 195 completed acute treatment; 33 then excluded because symptoms did not remit; 162 entered stabilization.

### MP-N017 — Figure 1 acute discontinuation components

- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): 74 discontinued acute treatment: 31 withdrew consent, 21 insufficient response/clinical worsening, 7 not appropriate for research participation, 5 adverse events, 1 medical event, and 9 other reasons.

### MP-N018 — Figure 1 stabilization and nonrandomization components

- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): 15 discontinued stabilization (4 clinical worsening, 2 withdrew consent, 1 died not by suicide, 1 not appropriate for research participation, 7 other); 147 completed stabilization; 21 were not randomized (12 not eligible, 9 did not consent); 126 randomized.

### MP-N019 — Figure 1 olanzapine branch

- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): 64 allocated; 59 received treatment as randomized; 5 did not (3 discontinued, 2 switched to open-label olanzapine); all 64 included in the primary analysis; 43 completed in remission/near remission; 13 relapsed; 8 discontinued (1 died not by suicide, 5 withdrew consent, 1 moved, 1 transportation problem).

### MP-N020 — Figure 1 placebo branch

- [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=3): 62 allocated; 60 received treatment as randomized; 2 did not (1 discontinued, 1 switched to open-label olanzapine); all 62 included in the primary analysis; 24 completed in remission/near remission; 34 relapsed; 4 discontinued (1 medical event, 3 withdrew consent).

## Inferential-statistical relationships

### MP-S001 — Prespecified analysis framework

- [DOC-001 pp. 4-6](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=4): analyses included all randomized participants. Primary analysis used a Cox model with treatment plus age group, remission status, and site; proportional-hazards assessment used complementary log-log plots and Schoenfeld-residual/time tests.
- Secondary continuous outcomes used linear mixed models with participant random intercept and slope and fixed site, time, treatment, and treatment × time effects. Simpson-Angus used overdispersed Poisson mixed effects.
- All tests were 2-sided at overall 5%. Secondary-outcome P values used post hoc Holm stepdown adjustment and were described as exploratory.

### MP-S002 — Primary treatment effect

- [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=1) and [pp. 6-7](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=6): treatment HR 0.25; 95% CI 0.13-0.48; P<.001. Population: all 126 randomized participants; contrast: sertraline + olanzapine versus sertraline + placebo; outcome: time/risk of relapse over 36 weeks; adjusted for the 3 stratification-variable families.

### MP-S003 — Primary-model covariate effects

- [DOC-001 pp. 6-7](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=6): age young versus old HR 0.78 (0.42-1.46), P=.44; remission versus near remission HR 2.45 (0.98-6.13), P=.06; Massachusetts, Pittsburgh, and Toronto versus Cornell HRs 1.53, 1.09, and 1.80 with CIs/P values fully preserved in MP-N037.

### MP-S004 — Figure 2 log-rank comparison

- [DOC-001 p. 7](../../../jama_flint_2019_oi_190079.pdf#page=7): comparison of relapse-free proportions gives log-rank P<.001; plotted shaded regions are 95% CIs; at-risk data and follow-up summaries are in MP-N039.

### MP-S005 — Post hoc primary-outcome sensitivity analysis
