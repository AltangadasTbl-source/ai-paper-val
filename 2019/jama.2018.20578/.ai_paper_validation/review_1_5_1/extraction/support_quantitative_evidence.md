## DOC-002 — Supplement 1 protocol

| PDF page(s) | Result-relevant content and exact quantitative/statistical labels |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cover page only; no result-relevant quantitative content. |
| 2 | Population: participants without cardiovascular disease; intervention aspirin at any dose; comparator placebo or no aspirin. Primary composite cardiovascular outcome is cardiovascular mortality, non-fatal myocardial infarction, and non-fatal stroke. Secondary cardiovascular outcomes: all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality, all myocardial infarction, all stroke, and all ischaemic stroke. Primary safety outcome: major bleeding; secondary safety outcomes: intracranial haemorrhage and major gastrointestinal bleeding; exploratory outcomes: incident cancer and cancer mortality. |
| 3 | Search protocol: prior USPSTF search date January 6, 2015; planned update January 1, 2015 through the search date; MEDLINE, Embase, and CENTRAL from inception through August 31, 2018. Screening split evenly between two reviewers; review in parallel independently. |
| 4 | Inclusion criteria: randomized clinical trial; no known pre-existing cardiovascular disease; aspirin any dose versus placebo/no treatment; follow-up at least 12 months; more than 1,000 participants; prespecified cardiovascular/bleeding outcome information; English language. |
| 5 | Two reviewers extract independently. Extracted result fields: follow-up duration; demographics and cardiovascular-risk characteristics; treatment/control raw event counts; reported RR or HR; upper/lower 95% CI; P value. BMI obesity threshold is BMI >=30. |
| 6 | Planned Bayesian hierarchical pairwise meta-analysis in GeMTC/R 3.4.1. Fixed/random selected by smallest DIC; MCMC used; HR with 95% CrI. When HR unavailable, event counts, total number, and follow-up duration enter a Poisson likelihood with log link to generate HR. ARD method: RR and its 95% CI multiplied by placebo event rate, then subtracted from placebo risk; negative favors aspirin and positive favors no aspirin. Sensitivity exclusions: open-label studies; daily aspirin dose >100 mg; publications before 2000. Frequentist supplement analysis has two-sided P cutoff 0.05. |
| 7 | Protocol changes: sensitivity exclusions for asymptomatic peripheral vascular disease identified by ABPI and for ASCEND in the primary cardiovascular outcome because ASCEND defines stroke exclusively as ischaemic whereas other primary-composite studies include ischaemic, haemorrhagic, and unknown etiologies. Search amended to inception through November 1, 2018, done independently in duplicate by both authors. |

## Mapper observations requiring later source-to-source checking (not candidate determinations)

1. **DOC-003 p. 16 versus p. 24:** eTable 4 describes total stroke as 12 studies and totals 73,883/72,317, while the frequentist total-stroke forest plot displays 13 study rows and totals 81,623/80,057. The difference in both arms is 7,740, the displayed ASCEND per-arm total on the forest plot. eTable 1 says ASCEND has no all-stroke outcome because it only reports ischaemic stroke. This is recorded solely as a matched-definition/count comparison for a later checker.
2. **DOC-002 p. 7 and DOC-003 pp. 9, 16, 24:** the protocol explicitly anticipates excluding ASCEND from the primary composite due to stroke definition; p. 9 labels ASCEND all stroke as not included, whereas p. 24’s total-stroke forest plot displays ASCEND. The direct source does not state in these units whether the forest’s ASCEND row is intentionally treated as total stroke for the frequentist analysis.
