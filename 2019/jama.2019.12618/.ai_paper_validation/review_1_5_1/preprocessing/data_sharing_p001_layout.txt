Data Sharing Statement

Data available:
Yes

Data types:
Deidentified participant data
Data dictionary

How to access data:
Data requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by Dr. Nisha Acharya and the FAST
Executive Committee. Interested researchers should send an email request to
nisha.acharya@ucsf.edu along with the research proposal for consideration.

When available:
beginning date: 06-01-2020

Document types:
None

Who can access the data:
Data will be made available to researchers whose proposed use of the data has been
approved by Dr. Nisha Acharya and the FAST Executive Committee.

Types of analyses:
Data will be made available to researchers with a clear and defined research plan, and
whose proposal has been approved by Dr. Nisha Acharya and the FAST Executive
Committee.

Mechanisms of data availability:
Data will be made available after approval of a proposal.

Any additional restrictions:
Data provided will be de-identified.
