## Source-grounded consistency notes for downstream review

- The protocol distinguishes two OCT quality thresholds: Heidelberg score ≥20 (`#page=131`) and Zeiss signal strength ≥5 (`#page=134`). They are instrument-specific and must not be directly compared as the same scale.
- The revision history changes eligibility and collection definitions across protocol versions. A cross-document comparison must match the main paper's enrolled period and the applicable protocol version before treating a difference as a contradiction.
- The page-136 non-serious upper boundary is `<5` times the upper limit of normal and the serious threshold is `≥5`; those boundaries are internally contiguous. The earlier page-135 example says “twice” the upper limit for a non-serious event without giving its upper boundary; it is not an incompatible competing rule when read with the page-136 classification.
- No `P = 0` or equivalent display-zero result occurs in this scope.
- No candidate is registered in this extraction map. Any numerical match or discrepancy with the main paper requires population, time point, instrument, outcome definition, and applicable protocol-version matching by the downstream cross-source reviewer.
