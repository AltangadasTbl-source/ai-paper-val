## Scope, evidence rule, and result

This artifact covers exactly 89 canonical relationships: `N189` through `N248` (60 numeric/reporting relationships) and `S052` through `S080` (29 statistical relationships). The canonical inventories and SAP mapping shards were used as locators; the direct supplied SAP PDF and the direct main-article PDF were then checked at the cited pages. In particular, the initially planned four-site description and later nine-site revision were not treated as competing statements about one same-version design; the article reports the realized nine-centre trial. No web source, legacy candidate output, stable candidate ID, severity, or adjudication was used.

## Matched groups and comparison logic

1. **Primary six-month success:** revised SAP PDF pp. 45, 52–56 and article PDF pp. 3–4, 6–7 match the patient-level endpoint, MTX/MMF contrast, six-month time point, complete-case primary set, site-random-effect logistic model, and reporting scale. Planned `216`, `108/group`, 80%, `0.40/0.60`, and 10% loss are not assertions that realized arms must be `108/108`.
2. **Twelve-month continuation and rescue:** revised SAP PDF pp. 45–48 and 57, 61–62 and article PDF pp. 6–7 match only after separating initially randomized treatment from switched treatment, success-at-month-12 from success-six-months-after-rescue, and continued-treatment from switching populations.
3. **Randomization/site history:** January-2013 SAP PDF pp. 8–10 (four sites) and revised-2017 SAP PDF pp. 49–50 (nine sites) are different plan versions. Article PDF p. 2/pp. 3–4 gives the realized trial. This version history cannot establish a same-version numerical conflict without an amendment/status record.
4. **Safety:** SAP PDF pp. 35–36 and 60 defines planned person-level proportions separately from recurrent-event rates. Article PDF p. 8/Table 3 reports `No. (%)` patients with at least one event. No rate/count transposition was found.
5. **Noninferiority:** revised SAP PDF p. 60 and article PDF p. 4 both state a 10% margin, but the SAP does not name the signed risk-difference orientation in its CI decision sentence. Article's `−5.3%` lower bound cannot be used to pronounce a cross-document conflict from that incomplete comparator.

## Limitations

- SAP pages in scope are planning/definition material, not an observed-result dataset; the supplied package has no amendment history, randomization list, interim report, participant-level classifications, or fitted-model output to resolve version history or implementation beyond the article's reporting.
- The January 2013 and March 2017 SAP sections differ in design details such as site number. Their dates and source locations are direct evidence of version change, but the package does not identify every approval/effective date.
- No workbook, CSV, or other structured result source was supplied. Direct PDF text was usable; the cited direct pages were checked without OCR.
