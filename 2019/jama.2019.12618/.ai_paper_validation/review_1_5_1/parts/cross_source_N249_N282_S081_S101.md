# Cross-source consistency review: N249--N282 and S081--S101

## Scope and method

This review covers exactly 55 canonical relationships: N249, N250, N251, N252, N253, N254, N255, N256, N257, N258, N259, N260, N261, N262, N263, N264, N265, N266, N267, N268, N269, N270, N271, N272, N273, N274, N275, N276, N277, N278, N279, N280, N281, N282, S081, S082, S083, S084, S085, S086, S087, S088, S089, S090, S091, S092, S093, S094, S095, S096, S097, S098, S099, S100, and S101. Canonical inventories and the mapper shards were used as relationship locators. I then checked the relevant direct PDFs with native layout extraction and visually inspected Supplementary Results PDF pp. 5, 14, and 15. All PDF page numbers below are physical PDF pages.

The matching rule required the same population, phase/time point, treatment contrast, analysis set, measure, model where applicable, unit, and displayed precision. A prospective SAP quantity was not treated as a conflict with a later observed result merely because the observed result differs from its planning assumption. Count percentages were checked as `100 x numerator / displayed denominator`, rounded to one decimal (0.05-percentage-point rounding tolerance).

Direct sources: [SAP PDF](../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf) and [Supplementary Results PDF](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf). Main-article comparators, when available, were checked in [main article PDF](../../../jama_rathinam_2019_oi_190092.pdf).

## Candidate Draft 1 — Main Table 3 MMF header denominator versus corresponding supplement tables

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [main PDF p. 8, Table 3](../../../jama_rathinam_2019_oi_190092.pdf#page=8); [Supplementary Results PDF p. 10, eTable 4](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf#page=10); [Supplementary Results PDF p. 11, eTable 5](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf#page=11); [Supplementary Results PDF p. 12, eTable 6](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf#page=12).
- **Printed values:** Main Table 3 labels the mycophenolate column `n = 109` and prints decreased/defective vision `19 (17.6)` and fatigue `59 (54.6)`. Its footnote says one mycophenolate-assigned patient never received study drug. eTables 4-6 label the treated mycophenolate population `N=108`; only eTable 4 repeats the matched decreased/defective-vision cell `19 (17.6)`. The fatigue value is an internal main-table denominator comparison, not an eTable 6 repeated cell.
- **Comparison logic:** `19/108 x 100 = 17.592...`, displayed as 17.6%, and `59/108 x 100 = 54.630...`, displayed as 54.6%. The printed percentages therefore use N=108, as expressly labelled in the supplement, rather than the `n=109` main-Table-3 column header. If main Table 3's header is the percentage denominator, `19/109` is 17.4% and `59/109` is 54.1%, neither printed value. Same event definitions, six-month period, and treatment group are matched.
- **Supported alternatives:** Table 3 may intentionally label the randomized assignment total (109) while calculating percentages among 108 drug recipients, with its footnote intended to communicate that denominator change. The header `n=109` nevertheless does not state the percentage denominator and differs from the explicitly labelled N=108 supplement tables.
- **Human verification steps:** Confirm the intended denominator convention in the statistical output/table shell; determine whether the main header should identify 108 treated patients or whether the footnote should explicitly state that all MMF percentages use 108; then verify every Table-3 MMF percentage against that approved convention.
- **Status:** Candidate draft for human verification only. No stable C ID, severity, or adjudication is assigned here.

## Candidate Draft 2 — eTable 9 MMF serious-diarrhea percentage

- **Category:** Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency.
- **Exact source location:** [Supplementary Results PDF p. 15, eTable 9](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf#page=15).
- **Printed values:** The mycophenolate mofetil column header is `N=20`, and its Serious Systemic diarrhea row prints `1 (3.4)`.
- **Comparison logic:** The table defines the entries as number of patients reporting at least one event (%). `1/20 x 100 = 5.0%` at one decimal place, not 3.4%. The same table displays `1 (5.0)` in other N=20 cells and `1 (3.4)` in the N=29 methotrexate column, supporting the stated denominator calculation.
- **Supported alternatives:** An unprinted alternative denominator of approximately 29 would yield 3.4%, but no alternative denominator is provided for this row and the displayed column denominator is 20.
- **Human verification steps:** Check the underlying adverse-event tabulation and denominator used for this exact MMF serious-diarrhea row; confirm whether the numerator, percentage, or column denominator was transcribed incorrectly; check whether any omitted eligibility/exposure subset was intended.
- **Status:** Candidate draft for human verification only. This is the assigned N281 supplement draft relationship; no stable C ID, severity, or adjudication is assigned here.

## Candidate Draft 3 — eTable 8 serious-ocular hypertension label versus eTable 1 definition

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [Supplementary Results PDF p. 5, eTable 1](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf#page=5); [Supplementary Results PDF p. 14, eTable 8](../../../joi190092supp1_prod.pdf#page=14).
- **Printed values/statements:** eTable 1 defines non-serious ocular hypertension as `≥ 24 mm Hg` and serious ocular hypertension as `Surgery required (laser or incisional)`. eTable 8 places `Ocular hypertension >24mm Hg` under the `Serious Ocular` heading and reports MTX `1 (1.6)` and MMF `0 (0.0)`; its footnote points to “eFigure 2,” not eTable 1. eTable 1 is a separate supplied comparator, and eTable 9 labels the serious event as surgery required.
- **Comparison logic:** The eTable-8 serious-row threshold label describes the p. 5 non-serious criterion, whereas the cited serious criterion is surgery requirement. The same population, event type, severity framework, and months-6-to-12 table context are matched; the contradiction is label/definition, not a difference in the displayed 1.6% or 0.0% values.
- **Supported alternatives:** The p. 14 row may have intended to describe a surgery-required event but retained the non-serious threshold text, or the continuing-after-success cohort may have used a different seriousness definition. The supplied footnote points to “eFigure 2,” whose content is not present in the supplied supplement text.
- **Human verification steps:** Verify the event-level case and the intended seriousness criterion for the MTX event; determine whether the label should say surgery required, whether the event belongs in a non-serious row, or whether a cohort-specific definition exists outside the supplied table; check the matching eTable 9 serious row, which explicitly says `Ocular hypertension, surgery required`.
- **Status:** Candidate draft for human verification only. This is the assigned N282 supplement draft relationship; no stable C ID, severity, or adjudication is assigned here.

## Counts and limitations

- **Coverage:** 55/55 assigned IDs reviewed; 48 records had no qualifying candidate draft, and seven relationship records map to the three distinct candidate drafts recorded above.
- **Matched groups:** SAP sample-size/power design matched the main-article 216/80%/20-point design; Supplement eTable 7 India and country interaction P values matched the main article; eTables 4-6 shared six-month AE values with main Table 3; eTable 1/eTable 8/eTable 9 supplied the two assigned supplement-draft comparisons.
- **Limitations:** Most SAP records in this scope are prospective plans without an exact observed counterpart, so they cannot establish a cross-document numeric conflict by plan-versus-result difference alone. Supplement eTable 7 supplies no compatible estimate, CI, statistic, degrees of freedom, or variance for reconstruction, and the main article does not print the site-interaction P value. No external sources, legacy candidate conclusions, or unsupplied data were used.
