# Cross-source consistency review — N095–N188 and S026–S051

## Scope, evidence, and comparison rule

This shard checked all 94 numeric/reporting records `N095`–`N188` and all 26 statistical records `S026`–`S051` (120/120). Direct authorities were the supplied protocol, SAP, and main article PDFs, with the canonical mapper shards used only as locators/transcription aids. The check compared a value only after matching population, phase/time, outcome or measure, contrast, analysis set, model, scale/unit, reference group, and displayed precision. A planned definition, historical value, procedural threshold, or different instrument/version was not treated as an observed-result comparator.

The assigned protocol records contain no observed trial-result table, effect estimate, confidence interval, or P value. The assigned SAP records are prospective 29 January 2013 plan statements; the main article reports the later completed trial (2013–2018). Consequently, a plan-versus-observed difference in a model or site structure is recorded below as a non-comparable version/status difference unless it also creates a concrete conflicting reported number, statistic, denominator, label, or interpretation.

2. Establish the applicable protocol/SAP version and whether the item is a prospective plan, revision history, or an observed completed result.
