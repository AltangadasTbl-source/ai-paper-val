## Scope and method

- **Direct source:** `joi190092supp3_prod.pdf`, PDF pages 1-32 only (printed SAP pages 1-31, with the supplementary-content cover as PDF page 1).
- **Locator/transcription aid:** `.ai_paper_validation/review_1_5_1/preprocessing/sap_pp001_032_layout.txt`. Direct page-level `pdftotext -layout` extraction from the source PDF agreed with the run-local layout text. Direct visual review of PDF pages 23, 24, and 26 confirmed the displayed equations and tables where layout text did not preserve symbols cleanly.
- **Source type:** administrative/protocol/SAP material. It supplies planned definitions, analysis rules, sample-size and power calculations, rather than observed trial results. It is nonetheless mapped because its definitions and planned estimands are keys for matching reported results in the main paper and other supplements.
- **Main-paper match key convention:** the key is a precise outcome/contrast/population/time/model phrase for later matching; it is not a claim that this SAP result was necessarily reported in the main paper.

## Source-local observations for later cross-source checking

- **No observed-result candidate is registered in this mapping artifact.** This SAP is a plan and its numeric values are planning assumptions, not trial-result assertions. Any comparison with the main paper must first match outcome definition, population, phase, analysis set, time point, treatment contrast, model, and planned-versus-observed status.
- **N021 / S001-S004 are the strongest main-paper match keys:** Aim 1 patient-level 6-month ITT treatment success, methotrexate coded 0 and mycophenolate 1, logistic regression with site within country, two-sided alpha .05, permutation P value.
- **N027 / S016-S017 are the strongest rescue match keys:** Phase II 6-month rescue success after initial-treatment failure, adjusted logistic comparison, success proportions and 95% CIs.
- **N025 / S009 and N026 / S012-S014 preserve special outcome conventions** (logMAR 2.0 for no assessable eye at month 6; baseline-adjusted macular thickness; clustered eye-level vitreous-haze analysis) that must be checked before treating a result as comparable.
- **Formula transcription was source-visual-confirmed:** N029/S020 (PDF p23), N032/S022 (PDF p24), and N035-N036/S024-S025 (PDF p26). These are planning equations and contain no cached workbook values.

## Limitations

- PDF pages 1-32 contain no observed final-result tables. The extraction maps all result-relevant planned quantitative/statistical content in the assigned pages, but cannot itself establish a cross-document inconsistency.
- The SAP refers to a Manual of Operations, a full proposal, and later final reports/manuscripts for some endpoint details. Those external-to-this-shard definitions were not inferred.
- Pages 1-4 were directly reviewed and explicitly recorded as not applicable; no OCR was needed because native PDF text was usable and equation pages received visual confirmation.
