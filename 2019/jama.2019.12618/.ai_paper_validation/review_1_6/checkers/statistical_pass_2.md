## Second-pass rules and limits

Every realized effect record was revisited for point containment, interval endpoint order, direction/reference group, measure/scale, repetitions, population/denominator, count/rate and attached test identity. Descriptive medians are not model means, adjusted ORs are not forced to equal crude ORs, and eye populations are not patient populations. Conditional rescue columns identify received drug. Same displayed P values for different contrasts are not duplicate-value contradictions.

Exact interval/P/statistic/SE compatibility was assessed only to the extent definitions are supplied. Random-effect variance, exact permutation/CI inversion, pooled MI covariance/df and actual adjusted design matrices are missing in the named records; no convention supplies these gaps. Prospective relationships without realized statistics were checked for arithmetic, coefficient/null direction, model and label identity; numerical CI/P checks are inapplicable rather than omitted.

Numeric tolerance follows displayed precision: a nearest one-decimal percentage uses ±0.05 percentage point; a whole-percent nearest display uses ±0.5 point; exact integer counts, reference labels and inequality membership have zero tolerance. These are explicit conditional rounding rules, not a claim the sources universally promise nearest rounding. Approximate planning values are not treated as precise equality guarantees.
