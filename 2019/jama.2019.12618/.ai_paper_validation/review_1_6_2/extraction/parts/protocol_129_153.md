### N-P005-001 — Vitreous haze and active inflammation definitions

Source [DOC-003 p129](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=129), Figure2 and Grading Retinal/Choroidal Inflammation. Figure title: “Miami 9-point system for grading vitreous haze.” Visual panels are numbered 0,1,2 / 3,4,5 / 6,7,8, with increasing opacity; retain 0–8 photographic grade coding, not nine grades starting at1. Caption identifies Miami photographic grading. Active inflammation: clinical exam with ancillary angiography/photography/B-scan when needed; posterior-to-equator vasculitis qualifies and should be confirmed by fluorescein angiogram under SUN. Macular edema in isolation does not qualify; bullous serous retinal detachment with any accompanying vitreous, retinal or choroidal inflammation does qualify. Text also allows ≥1+ anterior chamber inflammation, vitreous haze and/or other retinal/choroidal lesions aside from macular edema. Matching keys: inflammatory control; vitreous haze scale; retinal/choroidal lesions; macular edema separate from inflammatory activity. This 9-point photographic Miami scale is separate from clinical NEI haze scale and SUN thresholds.
