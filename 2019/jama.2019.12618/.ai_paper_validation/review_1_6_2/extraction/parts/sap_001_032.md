## Design, endpoints, populations and measurement relationships

### S-SAP001-001: Trial design and original aim hierarchy

[PDF pp. 5–8](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=5). Phase III international multicenter observer-masked block-randomized comparative-effectiveness trial, methotrexate (MTX) versus mycophenolate mofetil (MMF). Eligible noninfectious intermediate/posterior/panuveitis requiring steroid-sparing therapy. Randomization and assignment unit is patient, not eye. Aim 1 primary objective is which treatment gives higher corticosteroid-sparing treatment success on an intent-to-treat basis; primary outcome is the difference in proportions assigned MMF versus MTX achieving success at six months, definition delegated to MOP §2.5.1 (p. 6) and §2.6 (p. 14). Successful six-month patients continue original treatment for another six months, with visits at 9 and 12 months; failure before month 12 on continued treatment removes patient from study. Aim 2 concerns rescue treatment after first-drug failure due to intolerance, safety or lack of efficacy; eligible failures switch masked to second drug, visits every four weeks until six months or failure. Aim 2 primary outcome is proportion successful six months after starting rescue, same success definition as Aim 1. Aim 2 secondary six-month objectives repeat Aim 1. Matching report keys: primary six-month steroid-sparing success, 12-month continued-treatment success, rescue six-month success, randomized treatment unit.
