## Statistical and design relationships

### S-P004-01 — Eligibility, baseline, and randomization timing

[Source p.97](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=97), section 4.3 and 4.3.1. Baseline exam/assessments may occur up to **14 days** before enrollment/randomization. Laboratory exceptions **4 weeks or 90 days**, depending on test. Active inflammation eligibility: **≥1+ anterior chamber cells, ≥1+ NEI vitreous haze, or active retinal/choroidal inflammation**, satisfied within the **14 days** before randomization; steroid-related improvement by enrollment does not preclude using qualifying screening values for the baseline exam. Baseline clinical eye form must contain qualifying inflammation. Consent and assessments can precede final laboratory eligibility, but randomization occurs only after all eligibility criteria verified. Matching main-paper keys: baseline inflammation population, randomization time zero, eligibility thresholds.

### S-P004-02 — Randomization unit and assignment procedure

[Source p.98](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=98), 4.3.2. Patient is assigned next patient-ID number; coordinator performs patient-specific randomization in **REDCap**, records assigned treatment, dispenses it, reviews dosing. Randomization at Baseline-I after eligibility and signed consent; can share screening date or occur on return for consent. If enrollment on later date, relevant labs within **4 weeks** prior. No allocation ratio, block size, stratification, or dose is defined on this page. Matching keys: participant-level randomization, allocated drug assignment. Do not infer randomization method details from REDCap use alone.

### S-P004-03 — Follow-up data availability and continuing Phase I treatment

[Source p.99](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=99), 4.4. Collect data inside visit window if possible, but collect regardless of timing. Phase II procedures/protocol same as Phase I. Clinical eye exam, acuity, OCT, and labs required; secondary examiner, photos, and quality of life have starred timing (Phase I failure and six-month visit, whichever first, and 12-month visit; Phase II failure and six-month visit). ### S-P004-04 — Continued assessment and Phase II eligibility

### N-P004-06 — Refraction procedure values and repeated locations

Letter-chart high/moderate/low procedures [pp.104-110](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=104); tumbling-E corresponding procedures [pp.115-121](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=115). Begin plus sphere, continue while same/better; minus changes only when reading improves even **one letter**. High-acuity initial test **+0.50/-0.37 D**, replacement **+0.50/-0.25 D**; cross-cylinder **±0.50 D**, insert **+0.50 D**, power refinement **±0.25 D**, increments **±0.25 D**; final spherical test **±0.37 D**, increments **±0.25 D**. Moderate: sphere **±1.00 D**, cross-cylinder **±1.00 D**, increments **±1.00 D**; final spherical refinement **±0.50 D**. Low: sphere **±2.00 D**, cross-cylinder **±1.00 D**, cylinder increments **±1.00 D**, final sphere **±1.00 D**. At 1 meter add **+0.75 D** to initial approximation (pp.108,119). Initial cylinder axis **90°/180°**, then **45°/135°**; compare cross-cylinder positive axis **45° either side**; if no preference initially, move cylinder axis **15°** and repeat. If adjustment crosses into negative cylinder, remove power and test positive cylinder at axis **90°** away. Starting at cylinder power **≥1.00 D**, each **0.5 D** cylinder change accompanied by **0.25 D** sphere change of opposite sign to preserve spherical equivalent (pp.105,107,109,116,118,121). Printed p.117 moderate-acuity retest sentence uses **+0.50 D**, alongside neighboring **+1.00 D** sphere instructions; record literal value without diagnosing. Always finish by checking plus power. Outcome matching key: standardized refraction before BCVA; these lens choices are procedural, not randomized intervention doses.
