3, Treatment plan
3.1. Study drugs
Patients will be randomized to receive either methotrexate or mycophenolate mofetil. In
addition, folic acid supplements will be given to all patients who have been randomized to
methotrexate. All study ophthalmologists, refractionists, OCT operators, fundus
photographers and fundus graders will be masked to treatment assignment. For dosing
schedules, see Tables 4 and 5.
