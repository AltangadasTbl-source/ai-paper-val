## End points and analysis population

- **Primary composite end point:** intrapartum stillbirth; neonatal death; Apgar score <4 at 5 minutes; umbilical cord artery pH <7.0; hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy; neonatal seizures; neonatal respiratory support >4 hours; neonatal-unit admission >48 hours; persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn; or meconium aspiration syndrome. **Secondary end points:** individual composite components and emergency cesarean delivery or instrumental vaginal birth for intrapartum fetal distress. *Source PDF p. 1, Abstract, Main Outcome and Measures.*
- **Randomized:** 3257 women: sildenafil citrate 1626 women/1634 infants; placebo 1631 women/1641 infants. *Source PDF p. 1, Abstract, Results; p. 4, Figure 1 / Results - Participants and Adherence.*
- **Final intention-to-treat outcome data:** after 9 women withdrew consent, 3248 women (3266 infants); primary end point available for 3250 infants. *Source PDF p. 4, Results - Participants and Adherence.*

## Additional reported outcome claims

- **Missing cord-pH testing/sensitivity analyses:** testing was performed for 1223/1629 (75.1%) sildenafil participants and 1159/1637 (70.8%) placebo participants. The primary outcome was reported as RR 1.01 (95% CI, 0.75-1.36) with imputation and RR 0.98 (95% CI, 0.73-1.31) when adjusted for missing values. *Source PDF p. 6, Primary and Secondary Outcomes.*
- **Tertiary outcome highlighted in Results:** postpartum hemorrhage >1 L: 164/1621 (10.1%) vs 128/1627 (7.9%); absolute risk difference 2.2% (95% CI, 0.2% to 4.3%); RR 1.29 (95% CI, 1.03-1.60). The text calls it 1 of 11 hypothesis-generating tertiary outcomes. *Source PDF pp. 7-8, Other Maternal and Neonatal Outcomes / Table 4.*
- **Other Table 4 effect with CI excluding the null:** spontaneous vaginal birth: 864/1629 (53.0%) vs 931/1637 (56.9%); absolute difference -3.8% (95% CI, -7.3% to -0.4%); RR 0.93 (95% CI, 0.88-0.99). *Source PDF p. 8, Table 4.*
- **Conclusion claim:** sildenafil citrate did not result in a lower incidence of adverse perinatal outcomes potentially related to intrapartum hypoxia or a reduction in emergency operative birth for intrapartum fetal compromise. *Source PDF p. 9, Conclusions; see also p. 1, Abstract, Conclusions and Relevance.*

## Site-specific/post hoc result claim
