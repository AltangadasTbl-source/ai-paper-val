DOC-004 page 1 is administrative; DOC-002 pp. 1-4, 7-8, 18-20, 22-26 and DOC-003 pp. 1-4 contain no result-relevant relationship beyond identifiers/indexes. No OCR was required: direct native/layout text was legible, and DOC-004 visual/table confirmation agreed with source text.
