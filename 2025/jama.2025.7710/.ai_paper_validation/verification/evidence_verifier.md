# Evidence Verification — Round 1

## Candidate 1 — Verified

- **Category:** Presentation inconsistency
- **Finding:** Primary composite analysis-unit labels conflict.
- **Evidence:** DOC-001 p. 1 reports the primary outcome as 83/1625 women vs 84/1625. DOC-001 p. 4 states that the endpoint was available for 3250 infants, and Figure 1 shows 1625 infants with primary-outcome data per arm. DOC-001 p. 6 repeats 83/1625 women vs 84/1625 women. DOC-001 p. 7 reports the same counts in Table 3 and calls site-specific counts women. DOC-001 p. 8 Figure 2 places the same overall counts under “total No. of patients.” DOC-004 p. 3 eTable 2 reproduces the same analysis set: 883+742=1625 and 887+738=1625; 16+67=83 and 24+60=84.
- **Basis:** Identical infant-level outcome records are labeled infants, women, and patients. Randomized women and infants are separately enumerated.
- **Human verification:** Confirm the intended primary-outcome analysis unit and harmonize the Abstract, Results, Figure 2, and site-specific reporting.

## Candidate 2 — Verified

- **Category:** Presentation inconsistency
- **Finding:** Adjacent placebo ethnicity reporting uses different denominator conventions.
- **Evidence:** DOC-001 p. 5 Table 1 uses placebo ethnicity n=1629 and reports Australia/New Zealand as 874 (53.7%) and Pacific Islander as 53 (3.3%). DOC-001 p. 4 prose uses the full placebo cohort n=1631, including 2 missing observations, and reports the same counts as 874 (53.6%) and 53 (3.2%).
- **Calculation:** 874/1629=53.65%→53.7%; 874/1631=53.59%→53.6%; 53/1629=3.25%→3.3%; 53/1631=3.25%→3.2% at displayed precision.
- **Basis:** Both calculations are arithmetically supportable, but the adjacent presentations use different, unexplained denominator conventions.
- **Human verification:** Confirm whether the intended denominator is nonmissing observations or all randomized participants and state the convention consistently.

## Candidate 3 — Rejected

- **Category considered:** Statistical reporting inconsistency
- **Evidence:** DOC-004 p. 5 eTable 2 reports other-site spontaneous vaginal birth as 370/746 (49.6%) vs 410/750 (54.7%), RR 0.91 (95% CI, 0.82-1.00), P=.050. DOC-001 p. 4 defines significance as two-tailed P<.05.
- **Basis:** At printed precision, .050 is not less than .05 and the confidence interval includes the null endpoint 1.00. No contradictory significance claim is made; unavailable unrounded values do not support a correction.
- **Human verification:** Confirm the printed row and significance rule; no correction is supported from the supplied documents.

No second verification round was required.
