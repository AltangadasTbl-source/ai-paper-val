# Critic Disposition

## Finding 1 — Accepted (Major)

- **Category:** Presentation inconsistency
- **Refined finding:** The primary neonatal composite outcome is reported with identical arm-level counts but conflicting analysis-unit labels: women, infants, and patients. This could obscure whether the primary analysis concerns randomized women or infants.
- **Evidence:** DOC-001 p. 1 Abstract Results and p. 6 Results report 83/1625 vs 84/1625 as women; p. 4 Results/Figure 1 identifies 1625 infants per arm with primary-outcome data; p. 7 Table 3/site prose again labels these counts as women; p. 8 Figure 2 calls the same totals patients. DOC-004 p. 3 eTable 2 reproduces the denominators and events: 883+742=1625 and 16+67=83; 887+738=1625 and 24+60=84.
- **Verification:** Confirm the intended primary-outcome analysis unit and harmonize the Abstract, Results, Table 3/site reporting, and Figure 2.

## Finding 2 — Accepted (Minor)

- **Category:** Presentation inconsistency
- **Refined finding:** Adjacent placebo-group ethnicity reporting applies different, unexplained denominator conventions to the same category counts, producing different displayed percentages.
- **Evidence:** DOC-001 p. 5 Table 1 uses 1629 nonmissing ethnicity observations: 874/1629=53.65%, displayed as 53.7%, and 53/1629=3.25%, displayed as 3.3%. DOC-001 p. 4 prose uses all 1631 randomized placebo participants, including 2 with missing ethnicity: 874/1631=53.59%, displayed as 53.6%, and 53/1631=3.25%, displayed as 3.2%.
- **Verification:** Confirm whether percentages should use the nonmissing or randomized denominator, then apply or explicitly explain that convention consistently.
