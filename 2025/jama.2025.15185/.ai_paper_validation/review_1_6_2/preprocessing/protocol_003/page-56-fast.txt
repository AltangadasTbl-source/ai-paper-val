Number of Enrolment of 610 participants is planned over the duration of 37 months.

Participants with A drop-out rate of 10% is assumed leaving 549 evaluable patients.

Rationale: Assuming, that the FMMA is normally distributed with a standard deviation
of 25 points, 548 patients will allow to detect a mean difference between
the Levodopa- and the control-group in the FMMA score of 6 points (which
is assumed to be patient-relevant) at 3 months with a power of 80% (two-
sided significance level of 5%).

Study Centre(s): Multicenter-study. Study sites: stroke centers and units (11 acute centers)
as well as rehabilitation centers (10 centers) with experience in stroke
patient rehabilitation.

Statistical The FMMA at 3 months will be analyzed by a mixed effects model, with

Considerations: FMMA-score at baseline as covariate to adjust for differences at baseline

. and treatment as two-level factor (“levodopa’” vs “placebo”) as well as type

of event (acute ischemic vs hemorrhagic stroke) as fixed effects. Center
will be included as random factor.
The secondary outcomes will be analyzed by regression models
appropriate to the data type. Missing data will be addressed using multiple
imputation techniques by chained equations.
