Multiple Imputation Details


We used 100 multiple imputations using chained equations. The imputed values, that have

been generated by the imputation model, were displayed in plots. Thereafter the model was

fit on each imputed data set. Estimates and variances were combined using Rubins rules to

give a single estimate and variance for each parameter of interest across all imputations. For

the Fugl-Meyer Motor Assessment the missing or incomplete data was imputed on the item

level, whereas for the secondary outcomes the summary scores were imputed. Outcomes for

potential harm were not imputed.



Deviations From the Statistical Analysis Plan


We had two deviations from the Statistical Analysis Plan:


    1.) The modified Rankin Scale was not imputed for participants who passed away before

        the three-month assessment but was set at 6 as defined by the scale.

    2.) Missing data for the subjective Patient-reported Assessment of Relevance of Motor

        Improvement (PRAI) were not imputed.

    3.) A spelling error was identified in the Statistical Analysis Plan regarding the estimated

        adjusted mean difference for the secondary outcome ‘affected side’. Initially stated

        as the affected side, the calculation was performed for the unaffected side, which is

        the correct approach.
