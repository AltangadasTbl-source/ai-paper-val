Outcomes:
Theprimary outcomeis thebetween-group difference of scoresin the
Fugl-Meyer-Motor Assessment (FMMA)measured 3 months after
randomization.
Secondaryoutcomes
a)Between-groupdifferencesin:
i) theFMMA total score at the end of study treatment(at5weeks),
at 6 and 12 months
i)theFMMAupperextremityandlower extremityscoreat 5
weeks,3,6,and 12months
ii)NIH-Stroke Scale score,modified Rankin Scale,Patient-
reported outcomes (PROMIS 10 and 29), Patient-reported
assessmentofrelevanceofmotorimprovementandthe
RivermeadMobility Index at5weeks,3,6and 12months
b)Relative risks of mortality,recurrent stroke,re-hospitalization,serious
adverse events and selected other adverse events possiblyrelated to the
Investigational MedicinalProduct(IMP).
c) Between-group differences(assessed in a subset of patients inselected
centers only) in:
dynamometer test, Motricity index,Trunk control,Functional
ambulation categories,10-meter walk test,Montreal Cognitive
Assessment,and daily life activities measured with movement
sensors,at5weeks,3,6and12months
Study design:
Multicenter,randomized (ratio 1:1), parallel-group.placebo-controlled
superiority trial with blinded patients,care-providers,investigators,and
outcomeassessors.
