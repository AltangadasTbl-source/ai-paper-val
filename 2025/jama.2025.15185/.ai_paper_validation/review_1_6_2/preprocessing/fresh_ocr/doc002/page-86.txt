11.4.1Datasets tobe analysed,analysis populations
The full analysis set (FAS) consists of all patients who were randomized. According to the intention-to-
treat principle,each patient will be analyzed according to the treatment they were randomly allocated
to.
The complete cases analysis set willinclude all patients in the full analysis set without any missing
values in the FMMA at baseline or at 3months.
The per protocol (PP) set consists of patients in theFAS who had at least 80% of the prescribed study
7.4 of the study protocol (reduction to 1-1-0 per day).Furthermore,PP patients will have to attend at
least5sessions of rehabilitation therapyper week.
Allpatientswillbe analyzedaccording to theirrandomlyallocated treatmentReasonsforwithdrawal
