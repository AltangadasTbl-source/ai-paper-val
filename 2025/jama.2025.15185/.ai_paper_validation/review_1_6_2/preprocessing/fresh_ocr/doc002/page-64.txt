The investigator affirms and upholds the principle of the participant's right to privacy and shall comply
withapplicableprivacylaws.Especially，anonymityof theparticipants shallbe guaranteed when
presenting the data at scientificmeetings or publishing them in scientific journals.
Individual subject medical information obtained as a result of this study is considered confidential and
For data verification purposes,authorised representatives of the Sponsor-Investigator,the CEC or CA
may require direct access to parts of the medical records relevant to the study,including participants'
medical history.
