12.QUALITYASSURANCEANDCONTROL
12.1Datahandlingandrecordkeeping/archiving
12.1.1 Electronic Case Report Forms
For each enrolled study participant, a CRF is maintained.A unique patient identifier willbe used to
identify patients.Site Principal Investigators and, if applicable, delegates ateach site willbe authorized
to do eCRFentries.
12.1.2 Specification of source documents
AE/SAE forms,etc. Source data will be available at all sites and may be found in paper or electronic
form.
12.1.3 Record keeping /archiving
All study data will be archived fora minimum of 10 years afterstudytermination orpremature termination
of the clinical trial.Any studyrelevant source data and documents will be archived at each study site for
a minimum of 10 years.
