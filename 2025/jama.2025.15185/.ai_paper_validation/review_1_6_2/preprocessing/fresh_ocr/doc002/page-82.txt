Suspected Unexpected SeriousAdverseReactions(SUSARs)
The Sponsor-lnvestigator evaluates any SAE that has been reported regarding seriousness, causality
andexpectedness.If the event is related to the investigationalproduct and is both serious and
unexpected, it is classified as a SUSAR.
