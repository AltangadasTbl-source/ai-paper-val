Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reactions (SUSARs)
The Sponsor-Investigator evaluates any SAE that has been reported regarding seriousness, causality
and expectedness. If the event is related to the investigational product and is both serious and
unexpected, itis classified as a SUSAR.

10.1.3. Reporting of serious adverse events (SAE) and other safety related events
Reporting of SAEs
All SAEs must be reported immediately and within a maximum of 24 hours to the Sponsor-Investigator
of the study. The Sponsor-Investigator will re-evaluate the SAE and return the form to the site. SAEs
resulting in death are reported to the lead CEC via BASEC within 7 days. The other in the trial involved
Ethics Committees receive SAEs resulting in death in Switzerland via Sponsor-Investigator via BASEC
within 7 days.
