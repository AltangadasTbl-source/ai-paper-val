## Findings

### F-XS-121 — Two component lists for the same motor-eligibility threshold in the latest protocol

- **Compared locations and document roles:** PLAN protocol version1.4 [physical p55, synopsis inclusion criterion2](../../../joi250066supp1_prod_1761597796.4601.pdf#page=55) and PLAN [physical p79, section9.2.1 prescreening](../../../joi250066supp1_prod_1761597796.4601.pdf#page=79). PLAN [physical p68, section3.8 population justification](../../../joi250066supp1_prod_1761597796.4601.pdf#page=68) and PLAN SAP [physical p4, section3.3](../../../joi250066supp2_prod_1761597796.4701.pdf#page=4) corroborate the expanded synopsis component list; they do not introduce a REPORT relationship.
- **Printed statements:** The current protocol synopsis requires total≥3 points from NIHSS motor arm, motor leg and limb ataxia, adding that distal arm paresis is equivalent to one of those items. Section3.8 repeats that expanded definition. In the same protocol version, section9.2.1 says meaningful hemiparesis, NIHSS≥3 for arm plus leg motor functions, has to be present to fulfill the inclusion criteria. The SAP repeats the synopsis arm/leg/ataxia definition and distal-paresis equivalence.
- **Comparison logic and reproduction:** Both statements are mandatory screening/enrollment rules for the same trial population and numerical threshold. Let A,L,T denote the listed arm, leg and ataxia scores. The explicit synopsis requires A+L+T≥3; the prescreen statement requires A+L≥3. These predicates are not equivalent when ataxia contributes to reaching3. An illustrative logical counterexample, not an observed participant, is A=1,L=1,T=1: the expanded total is3 but the arm+leg total is2. The distal-paresis extension is also absent from the prescreen list. The original protocol [p9](../../../joi250066supp1_prod_1761597796.4601.pdf#page=9) and [p32](../../../joi250066supp1_prod_1761597796.4601.pdf#page=32) both used the narrower arm+leg formulation; version evolution explains the source of the two lists but does not make the two current mandatory predicates equivalent.
- **Display precision:** These are categorical component lists and an exact eligibility cutoff, not rounded copies of a measured quantity. No arithmetic rounding interval can add or remove the ataxia component; the display-precision rule is not applicable to this logical comparison. No realized enrollment-count discrepancy is asserted.
- **Focus area:** D, planned target/analysis-population definition.
- **Proposed review domain:** `plan_material`.
- **Proposed relationship type / all relationships:** `P_P` / `P_P`.
- **Qualifying route:** Route B.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** The target population underlying the primary three-month FMMA treatment effect, specifically whether participants whose motor-arm+motor-leg sum is below3 but whose arm+leg+ataxia total reaches3, or whose distal paresis meets the extension, were eligible. This affects interpretation of the population to which that primary result applies; no unsupported claim about how many actual participants met either rule is made.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** The later synopsis and SAP identify the broader intended criterion, but the contemporaneous operational paragraph explicitly states the narrower threshold is necessary to fulfill inclusion. It does not label its component list a shorthand or state that the expanded criteria supersede that numerical prerequisite. Choosing which numerical screening predicate governed recruitment requires information beyond that paragraph. This is a difference in contributing scored components at an eligibility cutoff, rather than two names for the same population.
- **Supported alternatives:** The synopsis/SAP expansion may have governed actual screening and section9.2.1 may preserve the earlier description; alternatively arm+leg≥3 may have remained the operational prerequisite despite the expanded synopsis. The supplied documents do not establish the actual screening practice for participants near the cutoff.
- **Classification ambiguity / amendment context:** PLAN content throughout. Expansion from the original version is explicit in the later synopsis; the candidate concerns two simultaneously printed definitions within version1.4, not the disclosed change between versions alone. Corroborating SAP remains PLAN and no protocol-versus-report comparison is asserted.
- **Exact human question:** Which NIHSS components and distal-paresis equivalence were used to determine eligibility after the version1.4 expansion, and was an arm+leg total below3 admissible when the expanded criterion was satisfied?
