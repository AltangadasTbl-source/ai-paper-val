## Primary outcome

- **Outcome definition:** between-group difference in Fugl-Meyer Assessment (FMA) total score at 3 months (+/-14 days) after randomization; FMA range 0-100, higher scores better motor function. Primary model adjusted for baseline FMA total score; survivors with missing/incomplete scores were multiply imputed.
  - **Location:** PDF p. 3 (journal p. 1525), Outcomes—Primary Outcome; PDF p. 4 (journal p. 1526), Statistical Analyses.
- **Reported result:** 3-month median (IQR) FMA total score: 68 (42-85) points with levodopa vs 64 (44-83) with placebo. Adjusted mean difference (levodopa minus placebo): **-0.90 points** (95% CI, **-3.78 to 1.98**; **P = .54**).
  - **Location:** PDF p. 6 (journal p. 1528), Primary Outcome; Figure 2; also PDF p. 1 (journal p. 1523), Abstract—Results.
- **Reported interpretation:** levodopa added to standardized rehabilitation did not significantly improve motor function at 3 months compared with placebo plus standardized rehabilitation.
  - **Location:** PDF p. 1 (journal p. 1523), Abstract—Conclusions and Relevance; PDF p. 8 (journal p. 1530), Conclusions.

## Main-article result claims

- The Discussion states that levodopa/carbidopa compared with placebo showed “neither benefit regarding motor recovery nor harm after acute stroke,” that the adjusted primary difference was less than 1 point on the 100-point FMA scale and not statistically significant, and that no secondary outcome showed a between-group difference.
  - **Location:** PDF p. 7 (journal p. 1529), Discussion, paragraphs 1-2.
- The Abstract reports the conclusion that the results do not support levodopa as an adjunct to rehabilitation to enhance motor recovery after acute stroke.
  - **Location:** PDF p. 1 (journal p. 1523), Abstract—Conclusions and Relevance.
