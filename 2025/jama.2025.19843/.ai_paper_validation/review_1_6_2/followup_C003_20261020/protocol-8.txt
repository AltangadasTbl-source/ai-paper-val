                                                 -    ECMO weaning will be considered successful only if the patient
                                                      survives without ECMO, other mechanical circulatory support
                                                      device or heart transplantation 30 days after ECMO removal.
                                                 - Thus all ECMO weaning from randomization to 30 days after
                                                      randomization will be considered, and the qualification for
                                                      successful ECMO weaning will need 30 days of follow-up after
                                                      ECMO removal (thus until day 60 after randomization for an
                                                      ECMO weaning performed on day 30 after randomization).
                                                 - Patients still under ECMO 30 days after randomization will be
                                                      considered as censored.
                                                 - The number of days alive without ECMO was not an appropriate
                                                      primary outcome due to the high proportion of patients dying
                                                      with ECMO and thus ex-aequo (0 days).
                                                 The analysis will therefore model the risk of successful ECMO
                                                 ablation in the presence of two competing risks (death and weaning
                                                 failure).
                                                 Secondary objectives/endpoints:
                                                 To assess the efficacy of early administration of Levosimendan on
                                                 mortality, durations of ECMO support, mechanical ventilation, ICU
                                                 and hospital stay, the occurrence of major adverse cardiac events,
                                                 the evolution of hemodynamic parameters and organ function.
                                                 - mortality on D30 and D60

                                                      between inclusion and D30/D60
                                                 - duration of ICU stay and duration of hospitalization;
                                                 - major adverse cardiovascular events defined as death, cardiac
                                                      transplant, escalation to permanent left ventricular assist
                                                      device, stroke, dialysis, rehospitalization for heart failure on
                                                      days 30 and 60
                                                 - time to improvement intu hemodynamics (systolic, diastolic,
                                                      mean blood pressure, heart rate…) and time to hemodynamic
                                                      stabilization
 Secondary objectives and endpoints              - number of days with organ failure, defined by SOFA score, and
                                                      number of days alive without organ failure between inclusion
                                                      and D30;
                                                 - duration of hemodynamic support with catecholamines and
                                                      number of days alive without hemodynamic support between
                                                      inclusion and D30;
                                                 - duration of mechanical ventilation and number of days alive
                                                      without mechanical ventilation between inclusion and D30 and
