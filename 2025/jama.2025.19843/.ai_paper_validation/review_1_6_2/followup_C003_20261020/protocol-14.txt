2.7     Summary of the known and foreseeable benefits and risks for the study participants
Reducing the duration of ECMO support with levosimendan may be associated with less ECMO-related
complications such as hemorrhages, stroke or infections. It may also be accompanied by a reduction
in the duration of ICU and hospital stays, therefore reducing the costs associated with the care of these
critically ill patients. Better long-term outcomes might also be expected with earlier ECMO weaning in
these severe cardiogenic shock patients.
Known risk associated with levosimendan infusion include hypotension and supraventricular and
ventricular arrhythmias. However, this side effects may be significantly mitigated in patient on VA-
ECMO.


3     OBJECTIVES
3.1     Primary objective
The primary objective will be to assess the efficacy of the early administration of levosimendan on the
time to successful ECMO weaning in patients on VA-ECMO support for acute cardiogenic shock
refractory to conventional therapy. Weaning will be considered successful only if the patient survives
without ECMO, other mechanical circulatory device or heart transplantation 30 days after ECMO
removal [8, 36]. Patients still under ECMO 30 days after randomization will be considered as censored
at 30 days.
Patients with unsuccessful ECMO weaning will be considered as weaning failure (competing risk) at
the date of the weaning attempt.


3.2     Secondary objectives
Secondary objectives of the trial are to assess the efficacy of early administration of Levosimendan
on mortality, durations of ECMO support, mechanical ventilation, ICU and hospital stay, the occurrence
of major adverse cardiac events, the evolution of hemodynamic parameters and organ function.
 − Mortality on D30 and D60
 − Duration of ECMO support between inclusion and D30, and between inclusion and D60; and
     number of ECMO-free days between inclusion and D30/D60
 − Duration of ICU stay; of hospitalization;
 − Major adverse cardiovascular events defined as death, cardiac transplant, escalation to
     permanent left ventricular assist device, stroke, dialysis, re-hospitalization for heart failure on
     days 30 and 60.
 − Time to improvement in hemodynamics (systolic, diastolic, mean blood pressure, heart rate…)
     and time to hemodynamic stabilization
 − Number of days with organ failure, defined by SOFA score, and number of days without organ
     failure between inclusion and D30;
 − Duration of hemodynamic support with catecholamines and number of days alive without
     hemodynamic support between inclusion and D30;
 − Duration of mechanical ventilation and number of days alive without mechanical ventilation
     between inclusion and D30 and D60;
 − LV function assessed with echocardiography at Day 30 following randomization
 − Incidence of drug adverse effects (atrial fibrillation, ventricular arrhythmias, ventricular
     fibrillation, “torsades de pointes”, hypokalemia)
 − Primary and secondary endpoints in predefined subgroups: acute myocardial infarction,
     myocarditis, post-cardiac surgery and post-cardiac arrest patients.
