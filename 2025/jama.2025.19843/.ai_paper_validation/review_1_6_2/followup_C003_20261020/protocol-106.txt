4         DESCRIPTION OF THE TRIAL
4.1        Concise description of the primary and secondary endpoints
4.1.1       Primary endpoint
-     Time to successful ECMO weaning within the 30 days following randomization
         o The event of interest is the successful weaning of ECMO (within 30 days after
              randomization) defined as: the patient survives without ECMO, other mechanical
              circulatory support device or heart transplantation 30 days after ECMO removal [8, 36].
              Thus all ECMO weaning from randomization to 30 days after randomization will be
              considered, and the qualification for successful ECMO weaning will need 30 days of follow-
              up after ECMO removal (thus until day 60 after randomization for an ECMO weaning
              performed on day 30 after randomization).
         o ECMO weaning failure (i.e. unsuccessful weaning), within 30 days after randomization, is
              a competing event.
         o Death within 30 days after randomization, without an attempt to wean, is a competing
              event.
         o No event after 30 days after randomization will be considered and all patients without any
              of the three events defined above are censored at 30 days post randomization.
         o A protocolized management regarding weaning of VA-ECMO will be applied to both groups
              (see paragraph 5.2.1 for details) [8, 36].
         o The number of days alive without ECMO was not an appropriate primary outcome criterion
              due to the high proportion of patients dying on ECMO and thus ex-aequo (0 days). The
              analysis will therefore model the risk of successful ECMO weaning in the presence of
              competing risks (death and weaning failure).


4.1.2       Secondary endpoints
      -  Mortality on Day 30 and Day 60
      -  Total duration of ECMO support between inclusion and D30 and between inclusion andD60;
      -  Number of ECMO-free days between inclusion and D30/D60.
      -  Duration of ICU stay and of hospitalization;
      -  Major adverse cardiovascular events defined as death, cardiac transplant, escalation to
         permanent left ventricular assist device, stroke, dialysis, re-hospitalization for heart failure on
         days 30 and 60
    - Time to improvement in hemodynamic parameters (systolic, diastolic, mean blood pressure,
         heart rate …) and time to hemodynamic stabilization
    - Days with organ failure, defined by the SOFA score, and days alive without organ failure
         between inclusion and D30;
    - Duration of hemodynamic support with catecholamines and days alive without hemodynamic
         support between inclusion and D30;
    - Duration of mechanical ventilation and days alive without mechanical ventilation between
         inclusion and D30 and D60;
    - LV function assessed with echocardiography at Day 30 following randomization
    - Incidence of adverse drug reactions (including atrial fibrillation and other supraventricular
         arrhythmias, ventricular arrhythmias including ventricular tachycardia, ventricular fibrillation
         and torsades de pointes, hypokalemia).
The Primary and secondary endpoints will be also assessed in predefined subgroups: acute myocardial
infarction, myocarditis, post-cardiac surgery and post-cardiac arrest patients.
