Source comparison
All nine repeated protocol endpoint locations name post-cardiac arrest as the fourth predefined etiological subgroup. The allocation/minimization paragraph separately names other causes. These are different planning functions; the allocation paragraph documents an Other category but does not by itself prescribe an Other outcome subgroup analysis.
All nine SAP pages were visually checked. SAP p.6 includes other causes as a primary-etiology adjustment category in the Fine and Gray model; it does not prescribe an Other subgroup analysis. No SAP page specifies replacing the post-arrest subgroup with Other. No corresponding analytical subgroup amendment was identified in the fresh protocol text search or supplied materials inspected.
Main Figure 3 and supplementary eFigure 9 explicitly concern predefined subgroups. Their fourth subgroup is Other/Others, not a separately identified post-cardiac-arrest population.

Bounded downstream implications
The reported sHR 1.61 and CI belong to the heterogeneous 42-person Other subgroup. Extracting them as a post-cardiac-arrest-specific effect would change the attributed analysis population. A meta-analysis targeting post-arrest patients cannot identify the needed effect from these aggregate data.
The reported P value for interaction .74 and the statement of no statistically significant heterogeneity apply to the displayed subgroup scheme; they do not supply a separate interaction test or effect for a planned cardiac-arrest subgroup, and do not establish equality of all underlying diagnosis-specific effects.
The 15/21 and 12/21 totals do not identify the successes, event times, censoring, or competing events of the cardiac-arrest-labeled component. The post-arrest sHR cannot be reconstructed by scaling the Other sHR or using the count ratio 42/6. No numerical reconstruction, conclusion reversal, selective-reporting motive, or analysis preference is inferred.
Prespecified status remains unreconciled against the supplied analytic subgroup prescriptions. Other was prospectively present for minimization and adjustment; this observation must be retained. An unsupplied detailed plan/amendment may establish the reported analytical subgroup status, but its existence and timing cannot be inferred from the supplied evidence.
