Difference between groups will be tested with the logrank test. To adjust for prognostic factors, a cox
model will be used.


12.2  Sample size calculation


We estimated the cumulative incidence of successful ECMO weaning in the control arm at 50% in the
presence of competing causes (death or weaning failure) from a 30-day cohort study of similar
patients. According to Latouche et al. [38], with HR=1.75 (subdistribution hazard ratio), an alpha risk
of 5% and a power of 80%, we need 101 events (= successful ECMO weaning) and 206 subjects in total
(103 per group).


12.3  State whether subjects who exit the study prematurely will be replaced and in what
      proportion.

No replacement is foreseen.


12.4  Anticipated level of statistical significance

All comparisons will be done with a two-sided type I error of 5%.


12.5 Management of modifications made to the analysis plan for the initial strategy.

A detailed analysis plan will be defined and validated by the study scientific committee. Later
modifications must be made before unblinding of the database and systematically be validated by the
scientific committee.


12.6  Selection of populations

An intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis will be performed as the principal analysis, i.e. all randomized
patients will be included in the analysis in the group in which they first were randomized and all their
data will be used, regardless of any changes of treatment over the study duration.


13 QUALITY CONTROL AND ASSURANCE

Every research project involving human participants managed by AP-HP is ranked according to the
projected risk incurred by study participants using the classification system specific to AP-HP
sponsored interventional research involving human participants.Every clinical study managed by AP-
HP is ranked according to the projected risk incurred by the study participants using a classification
system specific to AP-HP-sponsored clinical trials.


13.1  General organisation

The sponsor must ensure the safety and respect of individuals who have agreed to participate in the
trial. The sponsor must have a quality assurance system for monitoring the implementation of the
study at the research centers.

For this purpose, the sponsor shall appoint Clinical Research Associates (CRA) whose primary role is to
carry out regular follow-up visits at the study sites, after completing their initial visits.
The purpose of monitoring the study, as defined in the Good Clinical Practices (GCP section 5.18.1), is
to verify that:
          -   the rights, safety and protection of the research participants are met
          -   the data reported are exact, complete and consistent with the source documents
          -   the study is carried out in accordance with the protocol in force, the GCPs and the statutory
         and regulatory provisions in force
