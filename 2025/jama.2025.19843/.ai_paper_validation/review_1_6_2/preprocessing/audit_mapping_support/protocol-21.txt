5.6  Termination and exit rules

5.6.1   Criteria and procedures for prematurely terminating the study treatment


- Temporary suspension of treatment: the investigator must document the reason for suspending
   and resuming the treatment in the subject's source file and the case report form (CRF). The subject
   remains enrolled in the study
- Premature termination of treatment, but the subject remains enrolled in the study until the end of
   the subject's participation: the investigator must document the reason
- Premature termination of treatment and exit from the study. The only reasons for premature exit
   from the study are: death,  lost to follow-up, or patient’s consent withdrawal. Premature
   termination of treatment or adverse reaction should not imply premature exit from the study.

The investigator must:
       o  Document the reason(s)
       o   Collect all endpoints at the moment the subject exits from the study, if the subject agrees
       o  Schedule further follow-up visits, especially in case of a serious adverse event.

Study treatment must be discontinued  if they experience any of the following:
       o  Anaphylactic shock
       o   Ventricular fibrillation
       o  “Torsades de pointes”
       o   Ventricular tachycardia
       o  Refractory hypotension defined as a mean arterial blood pressure <45 mmHg for more
            than 60 minutes despite:
             −   vascular filling with two 500 mL successive crystalloid boluses AND
             −  norepinephrine ≥4 g/kg/min OR epinephrine ≥ 2 g/kg/min.


Criteria and procedure for premature withdrawals and exits from the study

-  Subject may exit the study at any time and for any reason.
- The investigator can temporarily or permanently withdraw a subject from the study for any safety
   reason or if it is in the subject’s best interests
-  Subject lost to follow-up: the subject cannot be located. The investigator must make every effort
   to reconnect with the subject and record his attempts in the source file (at least to determine
   whether the subject is alive or dead).

If a participant leaves the research prematurely, data relating to the participant can be used unless
an objection was recorded when the participant signed the consent form.
