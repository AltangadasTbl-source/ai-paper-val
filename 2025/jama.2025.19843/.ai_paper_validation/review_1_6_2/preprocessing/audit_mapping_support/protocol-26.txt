A continuous infusion of Levosimendan will be administered over 24 h, with no initial bolus. The

7.3  Common management regarding other inotropic drugs, heart failure medications and
     antiagregants and anticoagulants

Additional inotropic agents may be used at any time in either treatment group as clinically indicated
and as recommended by the latest European Society of Cardiology [26]. Dobutamine will be the
inotropic agent of choice.
Others heart failure medications (such as diuretics, ACE inhibitors, steroidal antimineralocorticoids),
antiaggregants and anticoagulants will be prescribed according to the latest European Society of
Cardiology [26].
Open-label Levosimendan (ZIMINO) will not be possible in either group during the entire course of the
study.


7.4  Common management regarding the ECMO circuit

- Parameter settings for ECMO
The pump flow will be set to 3.5-5 l/min, to provide adequate systemic perfusion. Pump flow might be
reduced in case of cessation of LV ejection or major pulmonary edema. Percentage of oxygen
contained in the ventilating gaseous air–oxygen mixture will be adjusted to obtain PaO2 between 65
and 90 mmHg and/or arterial oxygen saturation >90%. The membrane ventilation will be adjusted to
maintain PaCO2 between 40 and 45 mmHg. Extracorporeal circulation will be run at normal body
temperature.
