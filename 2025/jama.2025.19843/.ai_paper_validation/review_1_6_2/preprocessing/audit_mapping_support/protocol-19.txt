5.2  Follow-up visits

5.2.1 The following Information will be collected on days 1, 2, 3… 30 or date of death or
date of ICU discharge
   −  Hemodynamic parameters (arterial pressure, heart rate).
   −  Catecholamine doses
   −  Mechanical ventilation: yes/no
   −  Renal replacement therapy: yes/no
   −  Urine output
   −  Ionogram (including potassium), serum lactate, Blood Gazes, WBC, Platelets, renal and hepatic
       markers
   −  SOFA score
   −  ECG
   −  Echocardiographic parameters (LVEF, LV outflow VTI, Tissue Doppler systolic velocity the
         lateral mitral annulus).
   −  Weanability test [8, 36]: yes/no
        o  A protocolized management regarding weaning of VA-ECMO will be applied to both
               groups.
        o  An ECMO-weaning trial will be undertaken every day as soon as the patient will be
               considered hemodynamically stable, i.e. baseline MBP >60 mm Hg while receiving no
               or low-dose vasopressors and a pulsatile arterial waveform maintained for at least 24
                 h.
        o  The ECMO flow will be decreased gradually decreased to 66%, and to 33% of its
               baseline value and then to a minimum of 1–1.5 L/min.
        o    If MBP drops significantly and constantly <60 mm Hg during the trial, ECMO flow will
             be returned to 100% of the initial flow and the trial will be stopped.
        o  Patients achieving aortic VTI ≥12 cm, LVEF >20-25% and TDSa ≥6 cm/s at minimal
          ECMO flow support, will be considered weanable from ECMO [8, 36].
