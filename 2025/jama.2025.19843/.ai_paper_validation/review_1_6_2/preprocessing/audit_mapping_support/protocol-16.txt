     -   Randomized
     -   Double blind
     -   Comparative, Placebo controlled
     -   Superiority design
     -  Two treatment arms
           -   Early administration of Levosimendan vs. placebo
           -   1:1 ratio

All consecutive eligible patients will be proposed to participate.
Allocation concealment will be guaranteed by use of a centralized randomization process with a web
server (see below) and use of the minimization method of randomization. Investigator masking to
group assignment after randomization will be guaranteed by use of a tested and validated e-CRF and
by other blinding methods like patient identification numbers, boxes numbered which contain
levosimendan or placebo.

Because acute cardiogenic shock is a complex and multifactorial process, one key issue in every trial
evaluating a new therapeutic strategy is how patients in both groups are managed and how standard
care for these patients recommended by current guidelines are implemented in the participating ICUs.
In the LEVOECMO  trial, common management regarding other inotropic drugs, heart  failure
medications and antiagregants and anticoagulants will be conducted according to the latest European
Society of Cardiology [26]. The ECMO circuit will be managed according to recommendations of the
ELSO organization [37]. And see paragraph 7.4 for details.


4.2.2  Number of participating sites

The study will involve 13 sites in France. Subjects will be recruited in ICU departments.

4.2.4   Randomization

After baseline screening, patients will be randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive levosimendan or
placebo. The computer-generated randomization process will be implemented using the minimization
method with a probability of assignment of 0.8 after the 20 first included patients (being assigned by
balanced random) by a statistician of the Clinical Research Unit of Pitié Salpêtrière Hospital, under the
responsibility of Pr Florence TUBACH. Stochastic minimization will be according to the primary etiology
of cardiogenic shock (acute myocardial infarction, myocarditis, post-heart surgery and other causes)
and by center. The assignments of participants will be concealed by use of a centralized 24-hour
Internet service (CleanWeb, Télémedecine Technologies, Boulogne, France) and thus without
knowledge of the next assignment in the sequence.
