7.1.2.3 Boxes of levosimendan or placebo

Each box will be assigned a unique treatment number according to the treatment numbers list.

Supply :
The numbered boxes of Levosimendan (ZIMINO®) 2,5 mg/ml and placebo  will be sent on
investigational sites by CTD of AGEPS.

Storage
Boxes of Levosimendan (ZIMINO®) 2,5 mg/ml and placebo must be kept at +2 to +8 °C in the outside
packaging, shielded from the light.

Administration
The products (levosimendan or placebo) will be prepared by non-protocol manipulators (nurses) A
pool of manipulators will have been previously identified and trained in services. Thus, participants,
physicians and nurses in charge for the patients will remain blinded of the treatment administered.

A continuous infusion of Levosimendan or placebo will be administered over 24 h, with no initial bolus.
The starting infusion rate will be 0.15 µg/kg/min and will be increased to 0.20 µg/kg/min after 2 hours
in the absence of rate-limiting side effects.
