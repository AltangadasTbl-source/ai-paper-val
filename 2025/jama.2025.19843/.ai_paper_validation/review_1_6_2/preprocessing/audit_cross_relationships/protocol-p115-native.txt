6.2 
Consent and randomization procedure 
Patients satisfying all of the Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria are classified as ‘Eligible’.  
To randomize an eligible patient, consent must be obtained from a close relative or surrogate.  
Should such a person be absent, the patient will be randomized according to the specifications of 
emergency enrollment and the patient will be asked to give his/her consent for the continuation of 
the trial when his/her condition will allow. 
Reasons for not enrolling an eligible patient will be recorded (lack of consent from substitute decision 
maker; refusal from attending physician; study team not available; study team missed the patient, 
patient enrolled in another trial).
