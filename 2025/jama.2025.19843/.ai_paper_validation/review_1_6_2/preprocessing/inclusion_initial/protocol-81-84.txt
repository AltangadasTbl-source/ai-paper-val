-   Death before D30 after randomization, without ECMO weaning: competitive event

12.2  Sample size calculation


We estimated the cumulative incidence of successful ECMO weaning in the control arm at 50% in the
presence of competing causes (death or weaning failure) from a 30-day cohort study of similar
patients. According to Latouche et al. [38], with HR=1.75 (subdistribution hazard ratio), an alpha risk
of 5% and a power of 80%, we need 101 events (= successful ECMO weaning) and 206 subjects in total
(103 per group).

An intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis will be performed as the principal analysis, i.e. all randomized

data will be used, regardless of any changes of treatment over the study duration.
