2.2 Secondary endpoints
The secondary endpoints of the trial include:

e Mortality: Mortality rates at day 30 (D30) and day 60 (D60).

e ECMO Support Duration: Total duration of ECMO support between inclusion and
D30, and between inclusion and D60; and the number of ECMO-free days from
inclusion to D30/D60.

e Duration of ICU and Hospital Stay: Length of stay in the ICU and total hospitalization
duration.

e Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE): Defined as the occurrence, on days 30
and 60, of any of the following: death, cardiac transplant, escalation to a permanent
left ventricular assist device, stroke, or re-hospitalization for heart failure.

e Hemodynamic Improvement: Time to improvement in hemodynamic parameters
defined as achieving a mean blood pressure > 60 mmHg.

e Organ Failure: Number of days with organ failure, defined as a Sequential Organ
Failure Assessment (SOFA) score > 2 in at least one of the six organ systems, and the
number of organ failure-free days between inclusion and D30.

e Hemodynamic Support with Catecholamines: Duration of catecholamine support
and the number of catecholamine-free days between inclusion and D30.

e Mechanical Ventilation: Duration of mechanical ventilation and the number of
ventilatory-free days between inclusion and D30, as well as between inclusion and
D60.

e Renal replacement therapy (RRT): Duration of RRT and the number of RRT-free days
between inclusion and D30.

e Left Ventricular Function: Left ventricular function assessed via echocardiography on
day 30 post-randomization by Fraction Ejection of Left Ventricular (FELV) in %.

e Safety:

1. Drug Adverse Effects: Incidence of drug-related adverse effects, including
atrial fibrillation, ventricular arrhythmias, ventricular fibrillation, torsades de
pointes, and hypokalemia.
