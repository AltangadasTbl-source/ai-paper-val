- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.
- **Cited locations found:** Yes. The abstract is at [DOC-001 PDF p. 1](../../../jama_combes_2025_oi_250087_1766516490.94011.pdf#page=1); the general statistical methods and mortality-related test families are at [DOC-001 PDF p. 3](../../../jama_combes_2025_oi_250087_1766516490.94011.pdf#page=3); the day-60 mortality Results sentence is at [DOC-001 PDF p. 4](../../../jama_combes_2025_oi_250087_1766516490.94011.pdf#page=4); the prespecified day-30/day-60 mortality analysis is at [DOC-003 PDF p. 7](../../../joi250087supp2_prod_1766516490.96511.pdf#page=7); and eFigure 2 is at [DOC-004 PDF p. 11](../../../joi250087supp3_prod_1766516490.97011.pdf#page=11).
- **Source printed value/text matched:** DOC-001 p. 1 prints day-60 mortality as `27.7% vs 25.0%`, risk difference `2.7% [95% CI, -9.0% to 15.3%]`, and `P = .78`. DOC-001 p. 4 prints 28 of 101 deaths (27.7%) for levosimendan and 26 of 104 (25.0%) for placebo, the same risk difference and confidence interval, relative risk `1.11 [95% CI, 0.70-1.75]`, and `P = .78`; that sentence expressly cites eFigure 2 in Supplement 3.
- **Comparator printed value/text matched:** Direct visual inspection of DOC-004 p. 11 found eFigure 2 titled “Cumulative Incidence of D30 and D60 Mortality.” Its day-60 panel prints `p = 0.56` and `Log-rank`. The plotted cumulative event table ends at 28 for levosimendan and 26 for placebo at day 60, matching the Results counts. The figure legend identifies the same two randomized groups. The rendered inspection aid is [DOC-004 p. 11 image](../preprocessing/evidence_recheck/doc004_p11.png).
- **Applicable consistency rule:** When two locations indicate the same endpoint, time point, randomized groups, and treatment contrast, different P values require distinct analysis definitions to coexist coherently. Here the prose points directly to eFigure 2, the figure expressly labels its value as log-rank, and the supplied plan states that between-group 30-day/60-day mortality differences will be tested with the log-rank test. If `.78` and `.56` are intended to represent the same inferential result, they should agree apart from display rounding.
- **Calculation and logical comparison reproduced:** `28 / 101 = 0.2772`, which rounds to 27.7%; `26 / 104 = 0.2500`, or 25.0%; and the displayed crude difference is approximately `27.72% - 25.00% = 2.72` percentage points, which rounds to 2.7 percentage points. Thus the counts, denominators, percentages, and displayed risk difference are mutually coherent. The printed P values are numerically unequal: `.78 - .56 = .22`. That gap cannot result from ordinary rounding of one underlying P value. No P value was reconstructed from the relative-risk confidence interval because a binary relative-risk interval and a time-to-event log-rank test need not encode the same analysis.
- **Necessary inputs available:** The endpoint (all-cause mortality), day-60 time point, randomized-group labels, total group denominators, cumulative death counts, displayed proportions, risk difference, relative risk, prose P value, figure P value, figure test label, and the planned survival-analysis/log-rank method are available. DOC-001 p. 3 also states generally that categorical outcomes were compared with chi-square or Fisher exact tests and censored outcomes over time with log-rank tests. DOC-003 p. 7 states that mortality time runs from randomization to death, last news, or end of follow-up and names log-rank for the group difference and Cox modeling for adjustment by primary shock etiology.
- **Exact missing inputs or definitions:** The prose does not identify the test or model that produced `.78`; whether it is a fixed-time categorical comparison or a time-to-event comparison; whether it is adjusted or unadjusted; whether a continuity correction or another implementation choice was used; its exact estimand and analysis population; or its censoring and missing-follow-up handling. The supplied summary pages do not provide individual death times, censoring times, or complete risk-set/event data needed to independently reproduce the figure's log-rank `.56`. The figure gives selected numbers at risk and cumulative event totals, which are insufficient for an exact log-rank recalculation.
- **Source-grounded alternative interpretation:** The two values may intentionally come from different analyses. DOC-001 p. 3 permits chi-square or Fisher exact tests for categorical outcomes and log-rank tests for censored outcomes, while DOC-004 p. 11 explicitly labels `.56` as log-rank. Therefore `.78` could represent an unlabelled fixed-time categorical comparison and `.56` the survival-curve comparison. The source does not link `.78` to a named procedure, so this alternative cannot be distinguished from a reporting mismatch using the supplied summary evidence.
- **Direct observation versus inferred explanation:** Directly observed are the matched day-60 endpoint and groups, 28/101 versus 26/104 counts, 27.7% versus 25.0% proportions, prose `P = .78`, the Results cross-reference to eFigure 2, figure `p = 0.56`, figure `Log-rank` label, and the SAP's planned log-rank mortality comparison. The possibility that `.78` arose from a fixed-time categorical test or another distinct prespecified analysis is an inferred explanation, not text printed alongside `.78`.
- **Exact remaining human question:** Does `.78` represent a different prespecified analysis, and if so, what exact test/model, estimand, analysis population, adjustment, and time-to-event/censoring rule produced it; or was one of the two displayed P values reported in error?
