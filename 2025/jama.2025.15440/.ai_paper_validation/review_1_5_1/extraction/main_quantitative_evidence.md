## Page 1 — abstract

* **MN001, trial population and allocation:** 22,044 invited; 5,040 (22.9%) randomized; intervention/patch `n=2,520`, control/usual care `n=2,520`. Population: eligible people aged at least 65 years, CHA2DS2VASc at least 3 (men) or 4 (women), no previous AF/flutter. PDF p. 1.
* **MN002, baseline/adherence summary:** mean (SD) age 78 (6) years; 47% female; median (IQR) CHA2DS2VASc 4 (3-5); 2,126 (84.4%) returned/wore patch. PDF p. 1.
* **MN003, patch AF:** 89 (4.2%) patch-detected AF; 55% had burden below 10%. Denominator as printed in this abstract is not restated; p. 5 supplies 89/2,126 = 4.19% among returners and 89/2,520 = 3.53% randomized. PDF p. 1.
* **MN004, primary AF occurrence:** at 2.5 years, intervention 172 (6.8%) and control 136 (5.4%) with postrandomization AF record. PDF p. 1.
* **MS001, primary proportion comparison:** ratio of proportions 1.26 (95% CI 1.02-1.57), `P=.03`; intention-to-treat, primary-care AF record within 2.5 years, intervention versus control. PDF p. 1.
* **MN005, anticoagulation exposure:** mean exposure through 2.5 years was 1.63 months (95% CI 1.50-1.76) intervention and 1.14 (1.01-1.26) control; printed difference 0.50 months (0.24-0.75). PDF p. 1.
* **MS002, anticoagulation-exposure comparison:** permutation comparison for the MN005 contrast, `P<.001`. PDF p. 1 and method definition PDF p. 3.
* **MN006, stroke:** intervention 69 (2.7%) and control 64 (2.5%). PDF p. 1.
* **MS003, stroke time-to-event comparison:** rate ratio 1.08 (95% CI 0.76-1.53) in abstract (the detailed figure/narrative prints 1.08 [0.77-1.51]); method is log-rank with RR from O−E/V. PDF p. 1; detailed matching display pp. 5, 7.

## Pages 2-3 — design, outcomes, and analysis definitions

* **MN007, design units:** 27 UK primary-care practices; recruitment May 2, 2019-February 28, 2022; last follow-up August 29, 2024; analysis May-July 2025. PDF pp. 1-2.
* **MN008, screening duration and randomization:** single-lead patch intended for 14-day continuous monitoring; 1:1 randomization, minimization factors age, sex, and residual CHA2DS2VASc. PDF pp. 1-2.
* **MN009, primary definition:** proportion with AF in primary-care record within 2.5 years postrandomization, ITT. Secondary: age (<80/≥80) and sex subgroups; sensitivity: AF in primary or secondary care. Exploratory: time to AF, days with known AF, anticoagulation exposure, stroke and all-cause/cardiovascular death. PDF p. 3.
* **MN010, planned sample-size inputs:** initial sample 2,500, increased before unblinding to 5,000; estimated 2.5-year AF detection 4.4% patch and 1.75% usual care (ratio 2.5); stated >90% power at two-sided `P<.01`, retained for twofold difference; approximately 90% power at two-sided `P<.05` for age and sex subgroup analyses. PDF p. 3.
* **MS004, primary/subgroup test rule:** χ² ITT comparisons for primary outcome and age/sex subgroups, with χ² heterogeneity tests. PDF p. 3.
* **MS005, time-to-event rule:** log-rank comparisons; RR=`exp((O−E)/V)`; censoring for primary-care AF at death, withdrawal, loss to primary-care follow-up, or 2.5 years. Oral anticoagulation, stroke, and death analyses not censored at primary-care loss; anticoagulation time printed in months because dispensing data have month/year but no dates. PDF p. 3.
* **MS006, permutation/test interpretation rule:** permutation tests compare days with AF and total anticoagulation exposure; primary-outcome threshold two-sided `P<.05`; no formal multiplicity adjustment for secondary/sensitivity/exploratory results and their 95% CIs are not definitive treatment-effect tests. PDF p. 3.
