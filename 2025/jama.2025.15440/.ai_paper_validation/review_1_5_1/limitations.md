# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- DOC-002's native text layer was unusable and a targeted local CPU Tesseract attempt produced empty text. All 26 protocol pages were nevertheless mapped through direct rendered-page inspection, including visual confirmation of the planning tables on pp. 20-22; this is a reusable-derivative limitation, not a scientific-coverage gap.
- The supplied aggregate sources do not include event times, arm-specific censoring records, O-E, V, unrounded endpoints, or the exact confidence-interval output needed to select the intended interval for C001.
- The supplied aggregate sources do not include participant-level stroke classifications, overlap counts, recurrence rules, or the event-versus-participant counting convention needed to determine whether the subtype rows in C002 should partition `Any stroke`.
- Several permutation, heterogeneity, and time-to-event relationships cannot be exactly reconstructed because the package does not supply raw observations, resampling output, test statistics, degrees of freedom, covariance, or complete model output. Diagnostic checks were limited to compatible printed quantities and did not replace reported analyses.
- No structured participant-level dataset was supplied. These evidence limitations are preserved as human questions and did not leave any direct-source page unmapped.
