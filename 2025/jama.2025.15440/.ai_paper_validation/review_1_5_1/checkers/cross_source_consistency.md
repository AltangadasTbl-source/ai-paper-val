# Cross-Source Consistency Check

## Scope and method

Completed cross-source consistency review of all mapped numeric/reporting relationships `N001-N062` and inferential/statistical relationships `S001-S024` across the supplied direct sources: the main article (DOC-001, 9 PDF pages), protocol (DOC-002, 26 pages), statistical analysis plan (SAP; DOC-003, 24 pages), and results supplement (DOC-004, 11 pages). The check used the direct PDF as authority and the current mapping artifacts only as locators and transcription aids. No legacy candidate, checker, verifier, adjudication, or report artifact was used.

Before calling a difference, each comparison was matched on population, observation interval, contrast, analysis set, model/test, effect-measure scale, reference group, and displayed precision. Protocol/SAP quantities labelled as planning or planned definitions were not treated as observed-result comparators unless the final article represented them as identical values or definitions.

## Provisional cross-source candidate

### XC001 — Stroke rate-ratio confidence interval differs between the abstract and the detailed outcome figure

- **Primary category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Exact supplied locations:** DOC-001 [abstract, PDF p. 1](../../../jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf#page=1); DOC-001 [Figure 4B, PDF p. 7](../../../jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf#page=7).
- **Matched result identity:** Intention-to-treat randomized patch versus usual-care comparison, stroke through 2.5 years, 69/2520 (2.7%) versus 64/2520 (2.5%), rate ratio 1.08. The figure caption identifies the stroke ascertainment source and calls the intervals 95% CIs; the abstract identifies the same two arm counts and rate ratio. Population, time window, contrast, measure, scale, and displayed two-decimal precision therefore match.
- **Printed values:** The abstract prints `rate ratio, 1.08 [95% CI, 0.76-1.53]`. Figure 4B’s `Any stroke` row prints `1.08 (0.77-1.51)`.
- **Comparison logic and consistency rule:** A single matched rate-ratio result displayed to two decimal places should retain the same rounded 95% CI endpoints in the abstract and detailed figure, unless the sources identify different estimators, follow-up sets, or confidence-interval methods. Neither location supplies such a distinction. The lower endpoints differ by 0.01 and upper endpoints by 0.02 while the counts and point estimate are identical.
- **Supported alternatives:** (1) one location may contain a transcription/production rounding error; (2) separate interval computations may have been used but not labelled; (3) the figure could have been updated without the abstract being synchronized. The supplied package does not establish which is authoritative.
- **Human verification question:** What exact 95% CI calculation and underlying analysis output correspond to the 69 versus 64 stroke log-rank rate ratio of 1.08, and should both abstract and Figure 4B display those same rounded endpoints?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.
