## Verified Scientific Findings

### C-01 — Inconsistent confidence interval for the repeated “Any stroke” result

- **Evidence status:** Verified.
- **Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Minor.
- **Exact source locations:**
  - [jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf](../jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf), PDF p. 1, journal p. 1349, structured abstract, Results, final sentence.
  - Same file, PDF p. 5, journal p. 1353, Results, final paragraph.
  - Same file, PDF p. 7, journal p. 1355, Figure 4B, “Death and stroke,” row “Any stroke,” with the panel-B caption.
- **Reported values/statements:**
  - Structured abstract: patch/intervention group **69 participants (2.7%)**; usual-care/control group **64 (2.5%)**; rate ratio **1.08 (95% CI, 0.76-1.53)**.
  - Results text: the same **69 (2.7%)** versus **64 (2.5%)**; event rate ratio **1.08 (95% CI, 0.77-1.51)**.
  - Figure 4B: patch group **69 (2.7%)** of 2520; usual-care group **64 (2.5%)** of 2520; rate ratio **1.08 (95% CI, 0.77-1.51)**. The caption defines panel B as time-to-event rate ratios for events between randomization and 2.5 years.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Identify the compared result.** The direct source observations agree on the outcome label (“Any stroke”/stroke), randomized groups, event counts, displayed percentages, denominators, point estimate, and 2.5-year horizon. The Methods on PDF p. 3 identify stroke as an exploratory long-term randomized assessment and state that stroke time-to-event analyses used log-rank methods. No cited location labels the abstract value as a different population, outcome definition, horizon, or analysis.

2. **Reproduce the displayed percentages.** Using the printed randomized denominator of 2520 per group:

   - Patch group: `69 / 2520 × 100 = 2.7381%`, which displays as **2.7%** to 1 decimal place.
   - Usual-care group: `64 / 2520 × 100 = 2.5397%`, which displays as **2.5%** to 1 decimal place.

   These calculations confirm that all three locations refer to the same printed counts and group denominators. They do not reconstruct the time-to-event rate ratio or its CI.

3. **Compare the point estimate and CI endpoints.** All three locations print the point estimate **1.08**. The abstract and the two body displays differ at both CI limits:

   - Lower endpoint difference: `0.77 - 0.76 = 0.01`.
   - Upper endpoint difference: `1.53 - 1.51 = 0.02`.
   - Abstract interval width: `1.53 - 0.76 = 0.77`.
   - Results/Figure 4B interval width: `1.51 - 0.77 = 0.74`.

   The interval-width calculations are descriptive diagnostics; they do not identify which interval is correct.

4. **Check internal interpretation.** Both printed CIs contain the point estimate **1.08** and the null rate ratio **1.00**. Thus, the disagreement does not change the displayed effect direction, point estimate, or null-value interpretation. It remains a repeated-value inconsistency because the same result is printed with two different endpoint pairs.

5. **Distinguish observation from derivation.** The event counts, denominators, percentages, point estimate, CI endpoints, outcome label, and 2.5-year horizon are direct source observations. Percentage calculations and endpoint/width differences are derived from those printed values. No event-time dataset, log-rank observed-minus-expected statistic, variance, or model output is supplied for an independent CI reconstruction.

6. **Consider the recorded alternatives without changing the decision.** The completed workflow considered whether different analyses or rounding could explain the values, but no cited source labels an alternative analysis for the abstract. The package also does not establish a transcription, rounding, typesetting, or analysis-production mechanism. Consequently, the existing Verified decision concerns only the coexistence of inconsistent repeated CI values; it does not determine how the discrepancy arose or which endpoint pair should replace the other.

- **Existing supported conclusion:** The structured abstract reports **0.76-1.53**, whereas the Results text and Figure 4B report **0.77-1.51** for the same printed any-stroke comparison. This is the critic-retained Minor statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Limit on interpretation:** The package does not establish the intended CI. The finding does not validate either interval, reconstruct the log-rank analysis, imply a different statistical conclusion, or assess clinical importance.
- **Verification instruction:** Open the linked main article and compare the final structured-abstract Results sentence on PDF p. 1 with the final Results paragraph on p. 5 and the “Any stroke” row of Figure 4B on p. 7. Confirm **69 versus 64**, denominators **2520 versus 2520**, percentages **2.7% versus 2.5%**, and rate ratio **1.08** at all locations. Then consult the authoritative analysis output or production record to determine whether **0.76-1.53** or **0.77-1.51** is intended and correct the discordant repetition.

## Rejected and Excluded Interpretations

### C-02 — Recruitment-stage omissions do not establish a participant-flow inconsistency

- **Evidence status:** Rejected.
- **Category considered:** Participant flow inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Not assigned because the candidate was rejected.
- **Exact source location:** [jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf](../jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf), PDF p. 4, journal p. 1352, Figure 1, “Flow of Participants in a Trial of Remote Patch-Based Electrocardiogram (ECG) Monitoring for Atrial Fibrillation Diagnosis.”
- **Reported values/statements:** Figure 1 shows **368,000** assessed for eligibility (explicitly footnoted as estimated), **22,044** invited, **1,186** excluded because the GP practice participated in another trial, **20,858** included, **5,116** replies received, **76** excluded after reply, and **5,040** randomized. The 76 comprise **72** negative replies and **4** positive replies with incomplete consent and no response to further contact.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Trace the recruitment sequence.** Directly displayed values reconcile at the two places where the figure supplies an exclusion node:

   - Invitation/practice exclusion: `22,044 - 1,186 = 20,858` included.
   - Reply/post-reply exclusion: `5,116 - 76 = 5,040` randomized.
   - Post-reply subcategories: `72 + 4 = 76` excluded.

2. **Reproduce the differences that motivated the candidate.** The figure does not print separate boxes for these derived stage differences:

   - Assessed but not invited: `368,000 - 22,044 = 345,956`.
   - Included but without a reply received: `20,858 - 5,116 = 15,742`.

   These values are arithmetically inferable, but they are not direct source labels or author-designated exclusion categories. The first also depends on a denominator expressly marked as estimated.

3. **Check randomization and arm allocation.** The displayed overall and arm-specific totals reconcile:

   - `2,520 patch + 2,520 usual care = 5,040 randomized`.
   - Each arm includes all `2,520` randomized participants in the intention-to-treat analysis.

4. **Check the patch-data partition.** Within the patch arm:

   - No patch ECG data: `206 not returned + 188 returned unworn = 394`.
   - Patch-data partition: `2,126 with patch ECG data + 394 without = 2,520` randomized to patch.

5. **Check the 2.5-year primary-care follow-up partitions.** The displayed mutually exclusive arm-specific categories reconcile:

   - Patch incomplete follow-up: `106 moved + 6 withdrew = 112`.
   - Patch complete plus incomplete: `2,408 + 112 = 2,520`.
   - Patch complete follow-up status: `2,310 alive + 98 died = 2,408`.
   - Usual-care incomplete follow-up: `103 moved + 7 withdrew = 110`.
   - Usual-care complete plus incomplete: `2,410 + 110 = 2,520`.
   - Usual-care complete follow-up status: `2,292 alive + 118 died = 2,410`.

6. **Apply the verifier’s locked logical basis.** The labels “assessed,” “invited,” “included,” and “replies received” identify different recruitment stages. Figure 1 does not claim that every person who fails to advance between those stages is an exclusion requiring a separate node. The explicitly displayed exclusion transitions, randomization split, patch-data partition, and follow-up partitions are internally consistent. Therefore, the unshown stage differences may limit process detail but do not establish an internal arithmetic or logical contradiction in the supplied figure.

7. **Address the recorded alternative.** Treating the 345,956 and 15,742 derived differences as mandatory displayed exclusion categories would require an external participant-flow reporting rule. External standards were outside the evidence-verification scope and were not used. This supports the existing Rejected disposition; it is not a new assessment of reporting completeness under an external guideline.

- **Existing supported conclusion:** The source supports the locked rejection. The two inferred stage losses are not, on the supplied page alone, evidence of a participant-flow inconsistency, while all formally displayed exclusions and downstream partitions reconcile.
- **Limit on interpretation:** Rejection does not assert that Figure 1 provides the maximum possible recruitment detail or that it satisfies every external reporting standard. It means only that this package did not establish the proposed internal inconsistency.
- **Verification instruction:** Open Figure 1 on PDF p. 4. Trace every arrow; recalculate `22,044 - 1,186 = 20,858`, `5,116 - 76 = 5,040`, and `72 + 4 = 76`; then confirm the arm, patch-data, and follow-up sums above. Verify that **368,000** is marked estimated and that the figure does not state that all noninvited individuals or nonresponders are separately enumerated exclusion categories.
