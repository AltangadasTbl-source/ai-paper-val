# Coordinator Candidate Selection

## Scope

Candidates were deduplicated from the table-arithmetic, figure/flow, and statistical-consistency checks. The protocol and SAP were not used. Two candidates are forwarded to evidence verification, below the package maximum of 10.

## Candidate C-01

- **Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency
- **Source:** `jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf`
- **Locations:** PDF p. 1, structured abstract Results; p. 5, Results; p. 7, Figure 4B.
- **Reported evidence:** The same any-stroke comparison reports 69/2520 versus 64/2520 and rate ratio 1.08 throughout. The abstract gives 95% CI 0.76-1.53, whereas the Results text and Figure 4B give 0.77-1.51.
- **Basis:** Both CI limits differ for a repeated outcome with the same counts, denominators, point estimate, and follow-up horizon; no different analysis is identified.
- **Verification:** Visually compare all three locations, confirm that they report the same any-stroke analysis, and determine whether either CI is a transcription or analysis-specific value.

## Candidate C-02

- **Category:** Participant flow inconsistency
- **Source:** `jama_wijesurendra_2025_oi_250068_1760999665.28362.pdf`
- **Location:** PDF p. 4, Figure 1.
- **Reported evidence:** The figure moves from an estimated 368,000 assessed to 22,044 invited without a node for 345,956 not invited. It then shows 22,044 invited minus 1,186 practice-level exclusions = 20,858 included, followed by 5,116 replies, without a node for 15,742 individuals with no reply. The later transition 5,116 replies minus 76 exclusions = 5,040 randomized is complete.
- **Basis:** Two large losses between consecutive displayed flow nodes are not explicitly categorized while smaller exclusions are shown.
- **Verification:** Trace the arrows and labels in Figure 1 and determine whether the two omitted groups are required participant-flow categories or whether the displayed nodes represent different populations/process stages that make the omission nonerroneous.
