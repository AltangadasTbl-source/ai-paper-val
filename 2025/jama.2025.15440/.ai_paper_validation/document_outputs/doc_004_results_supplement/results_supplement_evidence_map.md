## Retained evidence

### eTable 1 - patch usage metrics (PDF p. 4)

- Randomized to patch: **N = 2520**. Wore and returned a patch: **2126 (84.4%)**. Of those returned, median days from randomization to activation was **10 (IQR 6-18)**, days worn **13.9 (13.2-14.0)**, analyzable-data days **13.5 (12.6-13.9)**, and analyzable-data percentage **98.8% (95.8%-99.6%)**.
- Patch worn at least 10.5 days: **1960 (77.8%)**; worn but under 10.5 days: **166 (6.6%)**. The latter group is the denominator for removal reasons: attachment problems **43 (25.9%)**, skin reaction **18 (10.8%)**, other **8 (4.8%)**, unknown **97 (58.4%)**.
- Returned unworn: **188 (7.5%)**; not returned: **206 (8.2%)**. The table therefore supplies the patch-group flow and the distinct denominators for adherence and removal-reason data.

### eTable 2 - baseline characteristics by patch ECG availability (PDF pp. 5-6)

- Comparison groups: all randomized to patch **N = 2520**; patch ECG data **n = 2126 (84.4%)**; no patch ECG data **n = 394 (15.6%)**. Row percentages are stated as the compliance rate within the baseline-characteristic category; missing data are excluded from P-value calculations.
- Reported statistically compared continuous/ordinal values: age mean **77.6 (SD 5.9)** vs **77.8 (6.1)**, P = **.50**; BMI mean **28.0 (5.0)** vs **29.3 (6.1)**, P **<.001**; CHA2DS2-VASc median **4 (IQR 3-5)** vs **4 (4-5)**, P **<.001**. The age-category and BMI trend P values are **.62** and **.03**, respectively; CHA2DS2-VASc categorical/trend P values are both **<.001**.
- Other reported P values at or below .05: diabetes **.02**; stroke/TIA **.05**; thromboembolism **.03**; heart failure **.006**; peripheral arterial disease **.04**; oral anticoagulation **.03**. Reported values for those respective rows (data vs no data) are **582/713 (81.6%) vs 131/713 (18.4%)**; **395/485 (81.4%) vs 90/485 (18.6%)**; **216/271 (79.7%) vs 55/271 (20.3%)**; **191/244 (78.3%) vs 53/244 (21.7%)**; **170/214 (79.4%) vs 44/214 (20.6%)**; and **137/174 (78.7%) vs 37/174 (21.3%)**.
- Other reported P values: age category **.75** (trend **.62**); sex **.47**; race/ethnicity **.17**; hypertension **.32**; chronic kidney disease **.61**; myocardial infarction **.87**; statin **.54**; RAAS inhibition **.36**; proton-pump inhibitor or H2-antagonist use **.81**; calcium-channel blocker **.07**; diuretic **.30**; aspirin/dipyridamole **.23**; beta-blocker **.39**; P2Y12 inhibition **.33**; direct oral anticoagulant **.08**; vitamin K antagonist **.31**; insulin **.22**.

### eTable 3 - patch-detected conditions (PDF p. 7)

- Denominators: randomized to patch **N = 2520**; wore and returned patch **N = 2126**.
- AF: **89** participants, **3.53%** of those randomized to patch and **4.19%** of those who wore/returned a patch; AF without flutter **74 (2.94%; 3.48%)**; AF with flutter **15 (0.60%; 0.71%)**.
- Other urgent-report findings: flutter without AF **13 (0.52%; 0.61%)**; complete AV block **5 (0.20%; 0.24%)**; high-grade AV block **10 (0.40%; 0.47%)**; second-degree Mobitz II **8 (0.32%; 0.38%)**; second-degree 2:1 block **1 (0.04%; 0.05%)**; pauses over 6 seconds **6 (0.24%; 0.28%)**; sustained ventricular tachycardia over 30 seconds **2 (0.08%; 0.09%)**.
- Nonurgent findings: nonsustained ventricular tachycardia at most 30 seconds **676 (26.83%; 31.80%)**; sustained supraventricular tachycardia over 30 seconds **134 (5.32%; 6.30%)**; nonsustained supraventricular tachycardia at most 30 seconds **1772 (70.32%; 83.35%)**.

### eFigure 1 - reported outcome by allocation and subgroups (PDF p. 8)

- Outcome definition: primary- or secondary-care record of AF from randomization to 2.5 years. Overall: patch **251/2520 (9.96%)**, usual care **207/2520 (8.21%)**; absolute difference **1.75% (95% CI, 0.16% to 3.33%)**; ratio of proportions **1.21 (95% CI, 1.02 to 1.45)**, **P = .03**.
- Age: under 80 years - patch **142/1715 (8.28%)** vs usual care **110/1744 (6.31%)**; absolute difference **1.97% (0.24% to 3.71%)**; ratio **1.31 (1.03 to 1.67)**. At least 80 years - **109/805 (13.54%)** vs **97/776 (12.50%)**; difference **1.04% (-2.28% to 4.36%)**; ratio **1.08 (0.84 to 1.40)**. Heterogeneity P = **.28**.
- Sex: females - **105/1180 (8.90%)** vs **70/1180 (5.93%)**; difference **2.97% (0.86% to 5.08%)**; ratio **1.50 (1.12 to 2.01)**. Males - **146/1340 (10.90%)** vs **137/1340 (10.22%)**; difference **0.67% (-1.66% to 3.00%)**; ratio **1.07 (0.85 to 1.33)**. Heterogeneity P = **.07**.
- Caption specifies 95% CIs are not adjusted for multiplicity.
