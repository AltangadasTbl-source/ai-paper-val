### C-03 — Table 1 and Figure 2 differ by one control participant for baseline FIO2

- **Evidence status:** Verified; retained by the critic.
- **Category:** Presentation inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Minor.
- **Exact source locations:** [jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf](../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf), PDF p. 6 / journal p. 1457, Table 1 continuation, “FIO2 at baseline” and “High FIO2 (≥0.5) at baseline,” plus footnote b; and PDF p. 9 / journal p. 1460, Figure 2, subgroup “Baseline FIO2,” plus footnote c.

   `228 − 86 = 142`.

   Figure 2 displays 142 below 0.50 and 86 at or above 0.50:

   The arm therefore reconciles exactly between Table 1 and Figure 2.

**Limit on interpretation:** The aggregate article does not establish whether Table 1 should contain 231, Figure 2 should contain 230, the below-0.50 subgroup should be 149 or 150, or a distinct analysis flag explains the difference. The discrepancy does not by itself show that the subgroup relative risks, the primary result, or any clinical conclusion changed.

**Human verification instruction:** Compare Table 1 and footnote b on PDF p. 6 with both baseline-FIO2 rows and footnote c in Figure 2 on PDF p. 9. - **Exact source locations:** [joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf](../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf), PDF p. 7, eTable 4 estimate header and RR-labelled binary rows; PDF p. 8, abbreviation line and analysis note. #### Reported statements

5. Main-article Table 3 separately defines `RD` as risk difference and `RR` as relative risk; its methods footnote likewise states that binary outcomes report RDs and RRs and that RRs were estimated by robust Poisson regression.

#### Reasoning procedure

3. **Check internal journal convention.** Main Table 3 distinguishes `RD, risk difference` from `RR, relative risk`. That direct internal definition confirms the same convention used in eTable 4's header and methods.

4. **Locate the isolated conflict.** Only the supplement's p. 8 abbreviation line expands `RR` as “risk difference.” That expansion conflicts with all other cited source statements.

5. **Separate observation from conclusion.** The quoted header, row, methods note, abbreviation line, and main-table definitions are direct observations. The conclusion that one expansion is internally inconsistent follows by direct term matching; no numerical or model assumption is required.

**Existing supported conclusion:** The eTable 4 abbreviation line incorrectly expands `RR` as “risk difference”; within the same table and the main article, `RR` is defined and used as “relative risk,” while `RD` denotes “risk difference.”

**Limit on interpretation:** This finding establishes a labeling conflict. It does not independently validate the fitted models or every displayed estimate, and it does not show that the numeric RR values were calculated as risk differences. The supplied text instead describes robust Poisson estimation of relative risks.

**Human verification instruction:** On supplement PDF p. 7, compare the estimate header and an RR-labelled binary row with the abbreviation and binary-outcome methods lines on p. 8; then compare the main-article Table 3 abbreviation line on main PDF p. 8. If the source production file matches the supplied display, change the eTable 4 abbreviation to `RR = relative risk` while leaving `RD = risk difference` where applicable.
