CHAPTER5.
RESPIRATORYSTATUS/LABORATORYMEASURES
5.1
Overview
This chapter records the type and magnitude of respiratory support needed by the infant in the first
weekafterbirthalongwithassociatedbloodgasvaluesandbloodglucosevaluesduringthesametime
period.
5.1.1Instructions for Completing Respiratory Status/Laboratory Measures Form (BB03)
This form will be completed for all enrolled infants.
SectionA.RespiratorySupport/VitalSign/BloodGas/GlucoseMeasures
Record respiratory support and vital sign measures q12hr x 7 days (168 hours of life). Record all
respiratory support from birth (baseline) regardless of when study drug is initiated. Baseline measures
arefrom theinitial NicU admissionforinfantswhoreceivestudydruginthedeliveryroom.Foroutborn
infants,datacollectionmaybeginonNRNNicU admissionunlesstransfercharts containthese data.

Record the date therespiratory measureswere obtained.Dailymeasures tostartwith unit
policy whether routine monitoring begins at 0700 or 0800(or other timeframe) daily.
2. Time
Record at 12-hour interval time points (+/- 1hr) through DOL 7 following unit practice e.g.
07:00, 19:00.
