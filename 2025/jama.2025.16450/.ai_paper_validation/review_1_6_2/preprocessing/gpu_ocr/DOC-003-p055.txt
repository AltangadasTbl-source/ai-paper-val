CHAPTER10.FOLLOWUPRESPIRATORYOUTCOMES
10.1 Overview
The purpose of this questionnaire is to obtain an interval respiratory history. Questions are designed to
ascertain the frequency and severity of wheezing and coughing episodes in enrolled infants. The person
being interviewed does not need to be the primary caretaker (see relationship codes below). This
interview should be administered by a trained studyinterviewer at the 22-26 month corrected age
follow-up visit.
