CHAPTER6.CONCOMITANTMEDICATION
6.1
Overview
Basedonamedicationorder(firstandlastdosedeliveredwithinthatorder),recordallmedications
given intravenously, intra-arterially, intramuscularly, subcutaneously, intratracheally, inhaled (example:
inhaled steroids, bronchodilators or iNo), as eye drops, or enterally. Non-systemic medications (topical
ointments)will beexcluded.
Medication Course: For each course of medication (example: An antibiotic is given on DOL 3 x 72 hours),
recordthedateofthefirstandlastdoseforthisepisode.Ifthesamemedicationisstartedagainlater
(e.g., antibiotic is started again in 5 days), record the medication again and record the date of the first
and last dose for this episode.
Bolus/MaintenanceMedications:Treatbolus/maintenancemedicationsasacourseifgiven
consecutively(atleastdaily).Ifthereareseparateordersand/orabreakindeliveryofthesame
medication,recordseparateentries.
PRN:Treat prn medications as a course if given consecutively (at least daily). Once there is a break in
delivery,record that as the stop date.If the prn order still stands and more medication is given 2 days
later, treat that as a new course.
TPN: Medications added to parenteral nutrition (examples would be iron, erythropoietin, etc.) will be
considered medications (refer to Appendix F : Nutrition vs Medications).
Single Dose: Single dose medications will be documented by recording the same date in the'Start Date'
and'Stop Date' column.
