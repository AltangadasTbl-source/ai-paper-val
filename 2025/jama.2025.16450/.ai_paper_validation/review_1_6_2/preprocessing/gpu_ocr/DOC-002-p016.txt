4.2. DETAILED STUDY PROCEDURES
4.2.1. Screening
Clinical research coordinators will screen pregnant women admitted at 22-28 weeks of gestation
who are considered at risk of preterm delivery, and will screen extremely preterm infants
admitted to the neonatal intensive care unit for meeting eligibility criteria.
