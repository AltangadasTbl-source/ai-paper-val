7.4.3
Futility
Futility will be assessed when 50% and 75% of the total planned enrolled infants have been
evaluated for the primary outcome (death or BPD at 36 weeks PMA) or been discharged or
transferred to another facility. To aid in the assessment of efficacy, the DCC will calculate the
conditional power and associated two-sided 80% confidence interval for the primary test of
treatment effect based on accrued data for the interim report. The confidence interval for
conditional power can be calculated from the following equation,
