The Network Coordinator, or her/his designee, enters daily onto the log the following information:
1.Infant's Name (Last, First)
Recordtheinfant'snameusingtheformatoflastnamefirstfollowedbytheinfant'sfirst
name.Thiswill notbe entered into theEDC.
2.Infant's Hospital Number
Record the infant's medical record number or otheridentifying hospital number. This will
notbeentered into theEDC.
3.Last Date Eligible
Record the final date of eligibility for the infant. Record as DD/MMM/YYYY e.g.
must be enrolled/randomized within 48 hours of birth but may be treated up to 50 hours.
This will notbe entered into theEDC.
4.
LastTime Eligible
Record the final time of eligibility for the infant. Record using 24-hour clock time HH:MM
e.g. 18:00. This will not be entered into the EDC.
5.Consent (Y/N)
Record Y if consent is signed otherwise record N. Antenatal consent is preferable so first
study drug dose can be given at delivery if needed. If antenatal consent is not available,
earlypostnatal consentcan be obtained sothatenrollment and randomization can occur
≤ 48h postnatal age and intervention can be started in the window of ≤ 50h postnatal age.
ThiswillnotbeenteredintotheEDC.
6.Enrolled/Randomized (Y/N)
Enrolled=Randomized=Treatmentkitopened.Infantswillbeconsideredenrolled and
assigned to the randomization number when the masked drug kit is opened,which will
occurafterassessmentofeligibilityanddirectlybeforetreatmentadministration.RecordYif
asignedconsentwasobtainedANDtheinfantwasenrolled.If theinfantwasnotenrolled
record N.
7.Study Number
Study Number is generated by the web-based EDC system (RAVErm) when study staff select
the‘add a new patient' button. RAVErm will then generate a five-digit (alphanumeric) Study
Number(B+4-digit familynumber+birthorder).Multiplegestationinfantswillbe
independentlyrandomized and enrolledwhere eligible.
