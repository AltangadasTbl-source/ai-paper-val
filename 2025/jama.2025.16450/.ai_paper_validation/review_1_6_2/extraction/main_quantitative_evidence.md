## Definitions and context needed for extraction

Primary outcome: physiologic BPD or death by 36 weeks PMA. Physiologic BPD: mechanical ventilation or CPAP, oxygen hood FIO2 >0.3, nasal cannula effective FIO2 >0.3, or failed oxygen reduction from hood/effective cannula FIO2 <0.3. Discharge/transfer before 36 weeks uses respiratory support at discharge/transfer. Table 2 footnote c: oxygen reduction challenge eligibility includes effective oxygen <27% with majority saturation ≥90% in preceding 24 hours; effective oxygen 27%–30% with majority saturation ≥96%; or room air by nasal cannula. Definitions occur pp3,7.

Five secondary outcomes at 36 weeks PMA: death, physiologic BPD, ordinal pragmatic BPD severity, grade 3 pragmatic BPD, and additional postnatal steroid use from 7 days after last dose through 36 weeks PMA. Table 3 footnote b: no BPD=no support; grade 1=nasal cannula <2 L/min; grade 2=nasal cannula >2 L/min or noninvasive positive airway pressure; grade 3=invasive mechanical ventilation. These are retained as printed, without boundary interpolation. NIH exploratory severity uses gestational age, respiratory-support duration through day 28 and respiratory support at 36 weeks PMA.

Safety window: within 7 days after last study dose for hyperglycemia, hypertension, prolonged hypoxemia with bradycardia, tube blockage and other moderate-or-worse adverse events. Moderate hyperglycemia: 120–180 mg/dL (6.66–9.99 mmol/L), on two consecutive determinations ≥6 h apart or >2 determinations in 24 h without intervening normal value. Severe: >180 mg/dL. Intestinal perforation and periventricular leukomalacia monitored through 30 days after final dose. Hypotension, pulmonary air leak, culture-positive sepsis and intracranial hemorrhage monitored within 7 days. Clinical outcomes through day 120; standardized growth at 36 weeks PMA. Definitions occur p3.

Randomization: infant is unit, multiples separately randomized, 1:1; center and gestational strata <26/≥26 weeks, step-forward block urn kit randomization. At least 3 kit sets per gestational stratum and 2 kits per randomization number support rapid use and possible second dose. Eligibility: 22 weeks 0 days–28 weeks 6 days or 401–1000 g; clinical surfactant decision ≤48 h; prior surfactant or systemic steroid excluded. Maternal indomethacin within 24 h or infant prior use excluded; planned indomethacin within 7 days after last dose excluded. Treatment via endotracheal tube, first dose within 50 h, maximum 2 doses before 50 h. First budesonide dose 0.25 mg/kg, 1 mL/kg with poractant alfa 2.5 mL/kg; second budesonide 1 mL/kg with surfactant 1.25 mL/kg. >2 surfactant doses within 50 h permitted as open-label at discretion. Systemic steroids <7 days discouraged. Occurrences pp1–3,7.
