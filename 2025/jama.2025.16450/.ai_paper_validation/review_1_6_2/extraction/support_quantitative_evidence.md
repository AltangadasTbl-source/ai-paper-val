## Protocol extraction, source-confirmed

### N201: primary endpoint and hypothesis

[Protocol page 4](../../../joi250072supp1_prod_1761000786.68881.pdf#page=4), repeated pages 7 and 20: early intratracheal budesonide plus surfactant versus surfactant alone reduces incidence of physiologic BPD or death by 36 weeks PMA. Primary endpoint applies to all randomized extremely preterm infants, recorded by coordinators using existing GDB criteria. Main match key: primary composite, 36w PMA, physiologic BPD definition, randomized population.

### N202–N203: eligibility and enrollment window

Pages 4, 16–17: liveborn 22 0/7–28 6/7 weeks gestation OR 401–1000 g birth weight inclusive, clinical decision for surfactant, enrollment/randomization ≤48 h PNA; study initiation ≤50 h with +2 h grace period. Antenatal or postnatal consent permitted. Exclusions: terminal illness (heart rate <100/min and unresponsive to resuscitation) or clinician judgment unlikely survival; decision to limit support; prior surfactant; infant systemic steroids before enrollment; mother indomethacin within 24 h before delivery, infant prior indomethacin or intended indomethacin from enrollment to 7 days following last study dose; major congenital/chromosomal conditions, known congenital infection, permanent respiratory neuromuscular disease, conflicting trial. Main keys: randomized eligibility, treatment window and consent.

### N204: allocation, randomization unit and strata

Pages 4, 17–18: 1:1 randomization, individually randomized multiple gestations, study site NICU and gestational age <26 versus ≥26 weeks strata; block urn with stratum-specific maximum tolerable imbalance known to DCC. Central randomization schedule, sequential use-next kits. Randomized when masked kit opened after eligibility assessment directly before administration; unopened kit returned and number unused. At least three kit sets per GA stratum, two kits per randomization number, two respules per kit/four per number. Main keys: stratified allocation, multiple-birth handling and randomized-versus-treated definition.

### N205–N206: secondary status and definitions

Pages 5, 7, 20–21: eight secondary outcomes: death by 36w PMA; physiologic BPD at 36w; Jensen 2019 severity at 36w; grade 3 BPD at 36w; postnatal steroids for evolving chronic lung disease from 7 days after last dose through 36w; severe NDI at 22–26 months corrected age; death by 22–26 months assessment; severe NDI or death at this assessment. Jensen no BPD = room air; grade 1 = nasal cannula ≤2 L/min; grade 2 = cannula >2 L/min or noninvasive positive airway pressure; grade 3 = invasive mechanical ventilation. NDI definition to be agreed at first follow-up owing to BSID-IV transition; examples: cognitive composite <70, GMFCS levels 3–5, severe functional hearing or bilateral visual impairment despite amplification/correction. ITT unless specified otherwise. Main keys: secondary outcome hierarchy, BPD grade, steroid window, deferred long-term outcomes.

### N207: exploratory status

Pages 5 and 21–23: NIH/NICHD 2000/Jobe-Bancalari 2001 BPD severity none/mild/moderate/severe at 36w; invasive ventilation days to PNA day 28 and to 36w; intubation after 50h to PNA day 28 and to 36w; repeat surfactant after 50h and within 7 days following last study dose; recurrent wheezing/chronic cough by 22–26 months CA. Possible ancillary biorepository without required trial specimens. Main keys: exploratory ventilation counts/time points, intubation and repeated surfactant outcome status.

### N208: safety outcomes and observation

Pages 5, 22–23: AEs from treatment initiation through 7 days/168 h after final dose, SIP/PVL through 30 days/720 h. Within 7 days: hyperglycemia, hypertension, prolonged hypoxemia/bradycardia, tube blockage, other moderate-or-worse events; mild events recorded only if unexpected. Additional prematurity-associated events: hypotension, air leak, culture-positive early/late sepsis, intracranial hemorrhage with 3-day ultrasound grace period. PVL within 30 days. Growth weight/length/head circumference at 36w PMA from GDB. Serious events followed to satisfactory resolution, chronic/stable status or 36w study status. Main keys: safety participant/event experience, GDB versus AE windows, growth endpoints.

### N209–N211: study dose and restrictions

Pages 18–20: poractant alfa/Curosurf 2.5 mL/kg first dose, 1.25 mL/kg second dose; intervention includes 0.25 mg/kg budesonide in 1 mL/kg each dose. Maximum two doses, optional second within 48+2=50 h PNA. First mixture total 3.5 mL/kg and second mixture total 2.25 mL/kg (derived addition, not a separately printed endpoint). After 50h surfactant per clinician preference. Suggested first-dose clinical criteria intubation, FiO2 >.25 to maintain SpO2 >90% or PaCO2 >65 torr at <48h. Second-dose suggestions: intubation <48h, mean airway pressure >6 cm H2O and FiO2 >.30 to maintain SpO2 >90%, or PaCO2 >65 torr. Early systemic steroids <7 days after last dose discouraged except fluid/vasopressor resistant hypotension or low cortisol/hyponatremia; suggested thresholds ≥20 mL/kg fluid in <2h and dopamine ≥10 mcg/kg/min. Indomethacin prohibited within 7 days last dose. Main keys: randomized dose, exposure and violation windows.

### N212: long-term and ancillary status

Page 23: usual NRN 2-year follow-up at 22–26 months CA includes NDI and additional recurrent wheeze/chronic cough. No post-hospital procedures otherwise. Funding may be sought for 5–6-year assessment. No specifically required specimens; possible ancillary aspirate/blood/urine biomarker/genomic studies.

### N213–N216: models, analysis population and missingness

Page 25 section 5.1: primary death/physiologic BPD model robust Poisson with sandwich variance, fixed NICU and dichotomous GA strata. ITT primary and secondary efficacy unless specified. Small NICU enrollment may pool with another NICU in same NRN center; similar pooling with convergence issues. Secondary/safety continuous = linear regression, dichotomous = robust Poisson, counts = Poisson, ordinal = proportional odds, nominal >2 levels = generalized logits; fixed stratification factors for all. Safety as treatment received. Prespecified secondary analyses as treated because randomized infants may stabilize or die before treatment. Missing data/loss-to-follow-up plans to be detailed in SAP before unmasked review. No imputation formula is specified on this protocol page. Secondary comparisons descriptive. Main keys: model/adjustment, pooled center, ITT versus SAF, missingness and count/ordinal model.

### N217–N219: power and recruitment

Pages 25–26: target background 24% death and 45% physiologic BPD among survivors giving about 58% composite; all-GDB comparison 19% death and 42% BPD among survivors/about53%. Assumed 10 percentage-point reduction 58% to48%, RR null=1; large-sample comparison of two proportions, three interim efficacy analyses. Table 5 two-sided overall/final alpha .05/.044 and .01/.009: power80% n406/n593; 90% n539/n753; 95% n664/n900 per arm. At90%, nominal large-sample n539/arm=1078, robust-Poisson simulation n550/arm=1100; about5% attrition, target1160 equally allocated with independently randomized multiples. Main keys: sample-size assumptions and target; administrative recruitment arithmetic is retained separately.

### N220–N224: monitoring and stopping

Page27: AE clinically significant untoward occurrence regardless relation; unexpected inconsistent with investigator brochure/risk plan. Mild transient expected events not recorded unless unexpected; baseline event becomes AE if worsened or resolves and recurs. Death/immediate life-threat reported within24h site awareness; other SAEs within72h (page28); monitor FDA unexpected fatal/life-threat suspected reactions no later than7 calendar days after site submission.

Pages28–29: formal safety after first40 infants/about3% reached primary status/discharged/transferred and25%,50%,75%; Lan-DeMets Pocock-type alpha spending, illustrative cutoffs .0033/.0196/.0212/.0214. Formal efficacy at25%,50%,75% planned enrollment reaching36w; two-sided overall alpha .05 with Lan-DeMets O'Brien-Fleming-type spending, thresholds .000015/.0030/.0183 and final .0440; exact alpha follows information.
