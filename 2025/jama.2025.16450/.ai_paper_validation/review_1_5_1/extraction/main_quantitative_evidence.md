# Main-paper quantitative evidence map — DOC-001

## Scope and evidence method

Assigned scope: `jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf`, PDF pages 1-11 (DOC-001). All pages were directly re-extracted from that PDF on 2026-09-02 with `pdftotext` and `pdftotext -layout`; pages 4-9 tables/figures were additionally rendered directly from the PDF for visual transcription confirmation. Reusable page text was a locator only. Fresh owned derivative paths are `preprocessing/main/direct-native-page-{1..11}.txt`, `preprocessing/main/direct-layout-page-{1..11}.txt`, and (pp. 4-9) `preprocessing/main/direct-render-page-{4..9}.png`.

Conventions: `MAIN-N` records numeric/reporting relationships; `MAIN-S` records inferential relationships. A record’s match key identifies the same population, time, contrast, measure, and analysis set for downstream cross-source matching. These are extraction records, not candidate assessments or judgments. `B+S` means budesonide plus surfactant and `SA` surfactant alone. CIs are 95% unless stated otherwise.

Exact hyperglycemia counts/denominators and the detailed adverse-event/PDA table are not in DOC-001; their supplied-source match keys are retained for support-document mapping.
