## Provisional pass-1 observations for coordinator-led duplicate merge

### STAT1-CAND-001 — Table 3 open-label-surfactant measure label versus stated estimator

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** DOC-001, [main article — PDF p. 8](<../../../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=8>), Table 3 row “Additional open-label surfactant”; Table 3 footnote g.
- **Direct observation:** The row labels the estimate `RR: 0.69 (0.33 to 1.46)`. Footnote g instead says “Relative risk was estimated by Mantel-Haenszel methods, approximated by the common OR,” after robust-Poisson convergence issues.
- **Consistency rule:** RR and common OR are different effect measures/scales. A source may use an OR as a rare-event approximation to an RR only if the intended reported measure and approximation are made unambiguous.
- **Diagnostic, not replacement analysis:** Crude risks are 13/312 (4.2%) and 18/299 (6.0%), whose crude risk ratio is approximately 0.69. This diagnostic does not identify the stratified estimator or replace it.
- **Human question:** Does the published row intend to report a Mantel-Haenszel relative-risk estimator or a common odds-ratio approximation, and should the estimate/column/footnote carry the corresponding unambiguous label?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

### STAT1-CAND-002 — eTable 3 low-prevalence estimates: relative-risk header versus odds-ratio footnote

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** DOC-005, [results supplement — PDF pp. 5-6](<../../../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=5>), eTable 3 header and footnote b (continues on PDF p. 6).
- **Direct observation:** eTable 3 labels its effect-estimate column `Relative Risk (95% CI) or P-value`. Footnote b says marked low-prevalence rows used a crude, unadjusted odds ratio with exact 95% CI as the approximation. - **Consistency rule:** Relative risk and odds ratio are distinct measures; calling an odds-ratio approximation a relative risk without an unambiguous row-level effect-measure label can change a reader’s interpretation.
- **Diagnostic, not replacement analysis:** The displayed event counts are consistent with rare events, but no diagnostic approximation establishes which estimator the authors intended to label as the reported measure.
- **Human question:** Were these marked estimates intended to be reported as crude odds ratios, or as explicitly labeled approximations to relative risks, and can the affected rows/header/footnote be made internally unambiguous?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

### STAT1-CAND-003 — eTable 4 RR abbreviation expanded as risk difference

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** DOC-005, [results supplement — PDF p. 7](<../../../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=7>), eTable 4 header; [PDF p. 8](<../../../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=8>), abbreviations and binary-outcome model text.
- **Direct observation:** The eTable 4 header reads `Relative Risk (RR) or Mean Difference (MD)` and its model text says binary outcomes report relative risks. The abbreviation line instead says `RR = risk difference`. For example, the PDA row prints `RR: 0.86 (0.75, 0.99)`, a ratio-scale estimate.

Therefore no P-value, SE, or test-statistic calculation was inferred from convention. Protocol/SAP plans and final results were only compared after matching population, timing, endpoint, contrast, and model; prospective changes alone were not treated as candidates.
