5. Measurement Definitions
5.1

ALSFRS-R

The definitions of ALSFRS-R scores are the same as described in the M-SAP. Pre-baseline slope
in ALSFRS-R (delta-FRS) is defined as 48 minus the baseline ALSFRS-R total score then
divided by the number of months from onset of symptomatic weakness to the Baseline Visit.
ALSFRS-R domain scores are exploratory measures of the primary efficacy endpoint ALSFRSR.
