## Relationship inventory for later matching

### N-SUP002-01 — Primary binary outcome definition and time points

- **Source:** DOC-002 PDF p. 56 (printed p. 44), section 15.5.
- **Definition:** Binary primary outcome is achievement of MCID in pain severity, defined as a **30% reduction from baseline** in overall BPI-SF pain severity.
- **Time points:** **3 months** is primary; **6 and 12 months** are secondary follow-ups.
- **Comparison/labels:** Both active interventions compared with usual care; intervention-by-time effects are specified.

### S-SUP002-01 — Primary binary-outcome model and statistical convention

- **Source:** DOC-002 PDF p. 56 (printed p. 44), sections 15.2-15.5.
- **Population:** Intent-to-treat, all randomized individuals irrespective of engagement/exposure.
- **Model:** Modified Poisson regression using GEE for the binary MCID outcome; time indicators at **3, 6, 12 months** and intervention×time interactions.
- **Adjustment:** Baseline pain severity; site; rural/medically underserved residence; prespecified outcome predictors (multiple pain and co-occurring mental-health condition).
- **Inference:** CIs and P values two-sided; significance threshold **0.05**.

### N-SUP002-02 — Secondary outcome and model definitions

- **Source:** DOC-002 PDF p. 57 (printed p. 45), section 15.6.
- **Outcome set:** Overall pain severity/intensity, pain-related interference (MCID and overall), social-role functioning, physical functioning, patient global impression of change.
- **Model rule:** Linear regression for continuous outcomes; Poisson regression for binary and count outcomes; primary secondary-analysis time point **3 months**, with **6- and 12-month** secondary time points.

### S-SUP002-02 — Moderation and mediation definitions

- **Sources:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 57-58 (printed pp. 45-46), section 15.7.
- **Moderators:** Sex, age, race/ethnicity, rural/medically underserved residency, multiple pain conditions, mental-health mood disorders, and negative social determinants of health. Moderator model contains main moderator effect plus moderator×intervention×follow-up-time interaction; primary comparison is the intervention interaction at **3 months**.
- **Mediators:** Pain catastrophizing, pain self-efficacy, perceived support; assessed baseline to **3 months**; primary mediator analysis targets primary MCID outcome at **6 months**.

### N-SUP002-03 — Economic endpoint, scale, and net-benefit formula

- **Source:** DOC-002 PDF p. 58 (printed p. 46), section 15.8; continuation PDF p. 59 (printed p. 47).
- **Endpoint:** Incremental cost per additional patient attaining pain-severity MCID (**30% reduction from baseline**) at **12 months**; QALY estimated with **EQ-5D-5L**.
- **Cost-effectiveness framing:** Net-benefit regression / CEAC, with example WTP range **$30,000 to $100,000 per QALY**.
- **Formula as printed:** person-level net benefit `nb_i = λ·effect_i − cost_i`.

### N-SUP002-04 — Qualitative-evaluation sampling quantities

- **Sources:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 35-36 (printed pp. 23-24).
- **Values:** Patient qualitative sample approximately **100-120**; approximately **20** early-launch interviews and approximately **100** remaining interviews; recruitment/initial interviews approximately **3-5 months** and repeat approximately **12-14 months** after randomization; up to **10** health-system stakeholders and up to **10** referring clinicians.
- **Interpretation:** These are planned sampling/operational quantities, not outcome denominators.

### N-SUP002-05 — Intervention-dose and operational timing definitions

- **Sources:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 33, 37, 43, 45-46 (printed pp. 21, 25, 31, 33-34).
- **Values:** exclusion windows **6 months**, next **month**, and next **12 months**; eligibility survey approximately **10-15 minutes**; intervention **8 sessions** over **12 weeks**, **30 minutes** per session; training completion within **12 weeks**.
- **Interpretation:** These are eligibility/intervention-delivery definitions, not reported trial results.
