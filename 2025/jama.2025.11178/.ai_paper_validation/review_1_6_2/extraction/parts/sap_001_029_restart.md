### Multiplicity, missing data, primary/secondary analysis

- **SAP-K64 — Masking scope** ([p.19](../../../../joi250046supp2_prod_1755300121.15087.pdf#page=19)): 2 levels, assignment and outcomes. Participants cannot be assignment-masked. Administrative/intervention PI/co-investigators/staff know assignment but are outcomes-masked. Primary/secondary outcome assessors assignment-masked but know individual outcomes.
- **SAP-K65 — Multiplicity rule** (p.19 section 10): one primary outcome, 3-month severity MCID. Fisher LSD: omnibus Wald for any difference among 3 groups at **.05**; pairwise differences each at **two-sided alpha .05** only if omnibus significant. Secondary analyses use similar approach for three-group comparisons. Safety multiplicity not considered because study not powered for safety. No alpha allocation across different secondary outcomes is numerically stated here.
- **SAP-K66 — Missingness, trigger and imputation/IPW** (p.19 section 11): primary analyses analyze patients with follow-up; missingness initially MAR given baseline covariates; dropout reasons tracked. Sensitivity required e.g. **>15%** missing 3-month outcomes or differential group missingness. First approach pattern-mixture imputation relaxing MAR conditional on longitudinal missing-data pattern among patients with ≥1 follow-up measure, with IPW for those missing **all** follow-up time points. SAP-K09 retains amendment rationale. No numeric number of imputations, IPW model coefficients or weight truncation limit is printed here.
- **SAP-K67 — Worst/best-case sensitivity** (p.19): missing active-arm outcomes assumed **not** achieving MCID at every missing time; missing UC outcomes assumed **all achieving** MCID at every missing time. Described as extreme smallest intervention effect relative to UC.
- **SAP-K68 — Outlier handling** (p.19 section 12): questionnaire measures, example **11-point Likert**; no outlier correction for survey data. Economic sensitivity Winsorizes outliers at **99th percentile**.
- **SAP-K69 — Descriptive follow-up and covariate scope** ([p.20](../../../../joi250046supp2_prod_1755300121.15087.pdf#page=20)): by arm, mean/median/SD/minimum/maximum and categorical frequencies/percentages/tabulations. Baseline prereandomization function/symptom scores, comorbidities and factors predicting outcomes, heterogeneity or dropout. Candidates listed age, sex, race/ethnicity, residency, multiple pain, mood disorders and SDOH.
- **SAP-K70 — Explicit seven subgroup cut points** (p.20): 1 sex male; 2 age **≥65**; 3 race White/non-Hispanic, Black/non-Hispanic, Hispanic, Other; 4 rural/underserved; 5 **>1** ICD-based pain condition; 6 anxiety/depression ICD mood disorder; 7 negative/poor SDOH. Additional factor groupings dictated by size/balance.
- **SAP-K71 — Primary and later-time hypotheses** (p.20 section 14): both active arms expected higher responder proportion than UC at **3 months** primary; same hypothesis at **6 and 12 months** secondary. Responder = **30% overall BPI-SF reduction**.
- **SAP-K72 — Primary model/estimand/correlation** (p.20): modified Poisson regression fit using **GEE** for binary MCID over 3/6/12 months. Poisson family with **log link**, robust standard errors correcting binary-outcome variance; estimate of interest **relative risk**, rather than logistic because outcome not rare. **Working independence correlation matrix**; robust sandwich accounts for within-person and within-health-coach correlation and misspecified mean–variance structure.

Primary contrast each intervention/UC at 3 months, testing intervention coefficient there. Adjust baseline pain severity; strata sex, site, rural/underserved; a priori outcome predictors **multisite pain and co-occurring mental-health condition**. UC and **3 months** reference groups.
