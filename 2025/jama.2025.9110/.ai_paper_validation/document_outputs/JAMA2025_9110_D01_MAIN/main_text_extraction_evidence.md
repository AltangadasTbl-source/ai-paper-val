## Primary outcome

| Location | Outcome, groups, and effect estimate |
|---|---|
| p. 3, Outcome Measures | Primary outcome: days free of the index hospital and alive at day 90. Calculated as 90 minus index-hospital admission days after formula commencement and minus any index-hospital readmission days within 90 days; deaths during this period assigned 0 days. |
| p. 5, Results—Primary Outcome; Table 2 (p. 7) | Quantile mixed model: median (IQR) `62.0 (0-77)` days augmented vs `64.0 (0-77)` usual; adjusted median difference `−1.97 days` (95% CI `−7.24 to 3.30`), `P=.46`; intracluster correlation coefficient (ICC) `0.004`. The source labels reduction in days free/alive as worse. |
| p. 7, Table 2—secondary analyses | Linear mixed model: mean (SD) `47.5 (34.0)` vs `48.9 (33.5)`; mean difference `−1.26` (95% CI `−3.59 to 1.06`), `P=.29`, ICC `<0.001`. Bayesian quantile mixed model: median (IQR) `62.0 (0-77)` vs `64.0 (0-77)`; median difference `−1.50` (95% credible interval `−3.86 to 0.90`), posterior probability of any benefit `0.109`, ICC `0.023`. |
| p. 7, Table 2—sensitivity analyses | Excluding known nontrial formula: `64 (0-78)` vs `65 (0-78)`; median difference `−0.97` (95% CI `−6.04 to 4.10`), `P=.71`, ICC `0.009`; exclusions `234` (144 augmented, 90 usual). Excluding palliative-care/organ-donation admissions: `63 (0-77)` vs `64 (0-77)`; difference `−1.12` (95% CI `−7.17 to 4.93`), `P=.72`, ICC `0.007`; exclusions `27` (17 augmented, 10 usual). |
