- **Audit disposition:** Audited for result-relevant material on source PDF pp. 1-32. Source pp. 33-34 (references) were **Not Audited by Design**. No protocol or SAP material was opened.

## Analysis-supporting supplementary methods

| Source location | Evidence retained | Main-article cross-reference hook |
|---|---|---|
| eMethods, pp. 3-4 | Nutrition intake was recorded on days 1-5, 10, 20, 30, and 90. Trial-formula duration was calculated in hours from first commencement to cessation of the final formula episode, including any index-admission ICU readmission episode. Eight delivery audits were undertaken; formula at bedside was compared with assigned formula. | Intervention delivery / adherence and duration results. |
| eMethods, pp. 4-5 | Invasive-ventilation duration was analysed as a lower-bounded continuous response using a tobit mixed-effects model, adjusted for period and delayed start, with ICU random effect; reported effect is a difference in means (95% CI). AEs were restricted to treatment-related events; AEs collected through 48 h after formula cessation and SAEs through day 90. Biochemistry was collected only on study days 1, 5, and 10 while in ICU. | Secondary-outcome model; safety and biochemistry reporting. |
| eMethods, p. 5 | Primary-outcome secondary analyses: linear mixed model; Bayesian quantile mixed model yielding posterior median difference and 95% credible interval. Planned sensitivity analyses excluded known non-trial-formula recipients and those admitted for palliative care/organ donation. Missing-primary-outcome imputation was not required because the supplement states there were no missing primary-outcome data. | Primary-outcome secondary/sensitivity analyses. |
| eMethods, pp. 5-6 | Primary quantile mixed model used `lqmm`, Nelder-Mead estimation and 1,000 bootstrap samples for 95% CIs. Bayesian model used four chains and 1,000 retained post-burn-in samples each (4,000/parameter); convergence assessed with trace plots, R statistic, and ESS. Subgroup interaction P values came from likelihood-ratio tests comparing quantile mixed models with/without interaction; subgroup effects and 95% CIs used block bootstrap. | Primary effect estimate, Bayesian analysis, subgroup forest plot. |
| eTable 2, p. 8 | Primary outcome: days free of index hospital and alive at day 90 = 90 minus index-hospital and qualifying readmission days; deaths within 90 days assigned 0. Secondary definitions cover survivor-only days free, day-90 survival, invasive ventilation, competing-risk ICU/hospital discharge durations, tracheostomy, new KRT, and hospital destination. | Outcome definition and analysis population. |
