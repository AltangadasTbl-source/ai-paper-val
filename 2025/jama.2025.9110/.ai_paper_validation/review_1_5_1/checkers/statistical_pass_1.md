## Limitations and precise missing definitions

- For the GEE/corrected binary and cause-specific Cox results (especially S002 and S005–S007), the package does not fully state the exact shared test statistic, CI construction, variance estimator/application, and sidedness necessary to turn a manually reconstructed P/CI relation into a decisive rule. Such approximations were not candidates.
- Bootstrap quantile intervals and Bayesian credible intervals were not treated as normal-theory confidence intervals.
- D02/D03 prospective analyses and PRO-SCAN statements remain useful context, but population, time point, estimand, and final-analysis identity are not sufficiently supplied to treat a planned-versus-final methodological difference as a numeric contradiction.
- Linked numeric relationships N001–N068 were reviewed only for implications of the assigned inferential relationships; their standalone arithmetic/denominator checks remain owned by the numeric checking lane.
