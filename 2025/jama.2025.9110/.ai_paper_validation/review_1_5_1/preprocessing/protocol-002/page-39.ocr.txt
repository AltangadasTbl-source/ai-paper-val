1317 Data Category

1318 Re-identifiable: A master enrolment log of participants will be completed for the sub-study at each participating

1319 site. This will record identifying details of each participant and will be used to assign each participant a sub-

1320 study number. The enrolment logs will be retained securely and confidentially at each participating site. The

1321 investigators propose that the data obtained remain re-identifiable to enable the data to be interrogated/corrected

1322 in the case of unexpected aberrant data e.g. database and data entry errors. Re-identifiable data may be used for

1325 Data Storage and Transfer

1326 The local site principal investigators will be responsible for data storage at the participating sites. CT digital

1327 files will be extracted locally and all patient identifying labels removed. The anonymised files will be

1328 transferred to the coordinating centre via an encrypted file. Sites will store enrolment logs and non-identifiable

1329 (anonymised) participant data according to local site data management policies.

1331 Data Access

1332 The principal investigator is responsible for the data overall. Each participating centre retains ownership of their

1333 local site data managed by the site principal investigator. Only those persons directly involved in data collection

1334 and analysis will have access to site study data. The pooled anonymised data will be held at the coordinating

1335 centre and remain the property of the TARGET Protein Management Committee and only study investigators

1336 will have access to stored non-identifiable pooled electronic data.

1338 Data Retention and Destruction
