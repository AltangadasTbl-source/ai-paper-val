1267 Study Procedures

1268 All CT scans of the chest, abdomen or pelvis performed for clinical care will be identified from electronic

1269 medical records in patients enrolled into TARGET Protein at participating sites. Subsequent CT scans are

1270 required for the analysis of only one of the dual primary outcomes (i.e., aim 1). For a subsequent CT scan to be

1271 included, the scan must be taken a minimum of 5 days after the initial scan to ensure clinically meaningful effect

1272 of nutrition intervention. The appropriate CT slice at L3 will be identified and images extracted.

1273

1274 The digital imaging and communications in medicine (DICOM) data files will be deidentified at the site level,

1275 labelled with date of scan and study ID, and transferred to the coordinating centre via an encrypted file. At the

1276 coordinating centre files will be uploaded into Voronoi Health Analytics Data Analysis Facilitation Suite

1277 software for analysis. The skeletal muscle will be identified using boundaries in Hounsfield units (HU) *.

1278 Skeletal muscle CSA in cm? will be computed by summing the skeletal muscle tissue pixels and multiplying by

1279 the surface area of each pixel “*. This methodology has been previously reported in critically ill patient”.

1280 Skeletal muscle density will be assessed by calculating the mean radiologic muscle attenuation of all muscles

1281 visible at the L3 level measured in HU ™.

1283. DATA MANAGEMENT

1284 Data Collection

1285 CT images will be extracted from electronic medical records and de-identified at participating sites. The

1286 following baseline and clinical data will be extracted from the TARGET Protein database: patient demographics

1287 (age, sex, weight), admission diagnosis, duration of ICU and hospital stay, hospital outcome, clinical frailty

1288 score on admission, ventilation status and duration of mechanical ventilation. Daily nutrition data collected on

1289 days 1-5, 10, 20, 30 and 90 while receiving TARGET Protein study EN will also be extracted including the

1290 volume of TARGET Protein study EN delivered, delivery of parenteral nutrition and protein supplements, and

1291 date and time of commencement and cessation of TARGET Protein study EN.

1292

1293 Data Analysis

1294 Baseline skeletal muscle area, and the association with clinical outcomes will be assessed both as a continuous

1295 covariate and, depending on the observed relationships, as a binary grouped variable (low/normal). For the

1296 binary clinical outcome of hospital death, interaction effects for study group with age, gender and the presence

1297 of sepsis will be explored. For clinical outcomes where death is a competing event, (e.g. duration of invasive

1298 mechanical ventilation, ICU and hospital LOS), competing risks regression will be employed using a cumulative

1299 incidence framework as per Fine and Grey *. Hospital/ICU site will be included as a random effect if study

1300 numbers allow and confidence intervals will be calculated using robust standard errors for all models. As all

1301 analyses will be regarded as exploratory, no adjustment will be made for multiple comparisons and p-values

1302 provided for perspective only, rather than dichotomous statistical significance ™. All analyses will be

1303 —_— undertaken using Stata/MP 17.0 (StataCorp LP, Texas, USA).
