602 Adverse events
603 It is recognised that the patient population in the ICU will experience a number of aberrations in
604 laboratory values, signs and symptoms due to the severity of the underlying disease and the impact of
605 _ standard treatments in the ICU. These will not necessarily constitute adverse events or serious adverse
606 events unless they are considered to be related to study treatment or in the principal investigator's
607 clinical judgement are not recognised events consistent with the patient’s underlying critical illness
608 and/or chronic diseases and expected clinical course.
609
610 Accordingly, the reporting of adverse events will be restricted to events that are considered to be
611 __ related to study treatment (possibly, probably or definitely).
612
613 Adverse events (AEs) are defined as any untoward medical occurrence in a patient or clinical
614 investigation subject administered an investigational intervention and does not necessarily have to
615 have a causal relationship with this intervention (adapted from the Note for Guidance on Clinical
616 Safety Data Management: Definitions and Standards for Expedited Reporting (CPMP/ICH/377/95
617 July 2000). In all cases, the condition or disease underlying the symptom, sign or laboratory value
618 should be reported e.g. renal failure rather than hyperkalaemia, and agitation rather than self-
619 extubation.
620
621 AEs will be collected from commencement of study EN up until 48 hours post cessation of study EN
622 only. The patient should be followed until the event is resolved or explained. Frequency of follow-up
623 _ is left to the discretion of the investigator.

625 Serious adverse events
626 The baseline mortality of intensive care patients is high due to the illness that has necessitated their
627. ICU admission. Intensive care patients will frequently develop life-threatening organ failure(s)
628 unrelated to study interventions and despite optimal management. Therefore, consistent with ICU
629 _ international best-practice and, as outlined above, deaths that are part of the natural history of the
630 primary disease process or expected complications of critical illness will not be reported as serious
631 — adverse events in this study*’. In particular, events already defined and reported as study outcomes
632 (e.g. mortality) will not be reported separately as adverse or serious adverse events unless they are
633 considered to be causally related to the study intervention or are otherwise of concern in the
