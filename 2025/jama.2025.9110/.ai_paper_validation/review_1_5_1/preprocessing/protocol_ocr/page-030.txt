991 including the patient’s name, date of birth, hospital identification number and unique study number
992 and date and time of randomisation. Subsequent data will be identified by the unique study number
993 only. The enrolment log and study data will be kept separately. The data will be kept confidentially at
994 the site according to each sites’ identifiable data storage policy such as, a locked cabinet in the
995 research office. For further information see section 8 “Data Management”.
