307 _A large high-quality trial is now needed
308 Based on the observational data, meta-analysis, our single-centre mechanistic study, and our multi-
309 centre feasibility trial, we submit that a large trial is warranted to determine whether augmenting
310 dietary protein to levels recommended in international guidelines improves outcomes that are
311 important to patients. Enteral nutrition is an extremely inexpensive therapy, so any reduction in
312 mortality and/or morbidity will represent a highly cost-effective intervention.

314. 5. RESEARCH HYPOTHESES
315 Our primary hypothesis of the TARGET Protein trial is that augmenting dietary protein to critically ill
316 adult patients when compared to usual care will increase the number of days free of the index hospital
317 andalive at day 90.
318 Our secondary hypotheses are that augmented dietary protein to critically ill adult patients when
319 — compared to usual care:
320 1. Reduces all-cause 90-day mortality
321 2. Reduces duration of mechanical ventilation
322 3. Reduces duration of admission to ICU and hospital
323 4. Reduces the incidence of tracheostomy
324 5. Does not increase the incidence of new renal replacement therapy
325 6. Increases days alive and out of the index hospital in those who survive at least 90 days
