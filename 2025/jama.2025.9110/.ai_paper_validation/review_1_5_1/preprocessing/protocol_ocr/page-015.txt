4668. DATA MANAGEMENT

467 The project data management conforms to the NHMRC guidelines published in 2019 ‘Management of

468 Data and Information in Research: A guide supporting the Australian Code for the Responsible

469 — Conduct of Research’.

470

471 The local site principal investigators will be responsible for data management at participating sites.

472 The TARGET Protein chief investigator and the coordinating centre (Royal Adelaide Hospital) will

473 be responsible for the management of pooled non-identifiable data.

474

475 8.1 Data category:

476 Re-identifiable: A master enrolment log of participants will be completed for the study at each

477 participating site. This will record identifying details of each participant and will be used to assign

478 — each participant a study number. The enrolment logs will be retained securely and confidentially at

479 each participating site. The investigators propose that the data obtained remain re-identifiable to

480 enable the data to be interrogated/corrected in the case of unexpected aberrant data e.g. database and

481 data entry errors. Re-identifiable data may be used for data verification and query resolution and only

482 referred to, if required, by local site investigators.

483

484 8.2 Data storage:

485 The local site principal investigators will be responsible for data storage at the participating sites. Data

486 will be collated, anonymised and entered into a secure password protected web database electronic

487 Case Report Form at participating sites. Sites will store enrolment logs and non-identifiable

488 — (anonymised) participant data according to local site data management policies.

489

490 Pooled (all sites) anonymised data will be stored at the coordinating centre as a secure password

491 protected electronic file.
