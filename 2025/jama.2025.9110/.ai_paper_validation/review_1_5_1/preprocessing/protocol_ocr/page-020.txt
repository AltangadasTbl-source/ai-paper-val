650 9.1 Statistical considerations and sample size

651 Sample size has been calculated using The Shiny Cluster Randomised Trial Calculator

652 (https://clusterrets.shinyapps.io/rshinyapp/) and data from TARGET Calories’. In TARGET Calories

653 _ the standard deviation for the primary outcome we will use in TARGET Protein (days alive and out of

654 hospital censored at day 90) was 11.3 days. Assuming the coefficient of variation for cluster size is

655 0.5 — based on 6 ICUs (ie. 6 clusters) each enrolling a mean of 160 patients/3 month period (95%

656 confidence interval, 80 to 240 patients per cluster per period) - and the default settings for cluster

657 trials of a intracluster correlation coefficients of 0.02 (with a lower extreme of 0.001 and upper

658 extreme of 0.1) 60 to 65 patients per period per cluster would provide 80% power, and ~80 patients

659 _ per cluster per period would provide 90% power to find a difference of I day in days alive and out of

660 hospital to day 90. Assuming a parametric distribution a sample size of 2560 would provide 90%

661 power. However, the primary outcome (days alive and out of hospital) is unlikely to be normally

662 distributed and parametric calculations are optimistic. For this reason, we have inflated the sample by

663 15% to allow for the likely skewed distribution of data, such that ~3,000 patients should provide 90%

664 power. We are assured of enrolling at least 3,000 participants in a 12-month period as we will have 8

665 _ high volume ICUs as clusters.
