373. Randomisation
374 All patients in a given ICU (cluster) who meet eligibility criteria will receive the same formula across
375 a 3-month period. The formula assigned to the ICU (cluster) for that period is the variable randomised.
376 This allows the intervention to be delivered as if it were usual care, which increases the efficiency of
377 data collection and reduces the burden on participating sites. After a 3-month period, the ICU will
378 then administer the alternative formula for all patient admissions over the next 3 months. Participants
379 will continue to receive the treatment that they were originally assigned if they remain in the ICU
380 during a crossover period. The process is then repeated so each ICU (cluster) crosses over twice
381 (Figure 2).
