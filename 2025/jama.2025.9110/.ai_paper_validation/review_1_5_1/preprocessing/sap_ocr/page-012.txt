269 4. Duration of admission to ICU (days)

270 Defined as the time after commencement of trial enteral formula until ICU discharge for
271 the index ICU admission, plus any days that the patient is readmitted prior to hospital
272 discharge, but does not include subsequent hospital admissions.

273 5. Duration of admission to hospital (days)

274 Defined as the time after commencement of trial enteral formula until hospital discharge
275 for index hospital admission (i.e., it excludes any time spent in hospital if readmitted,
276 which will be counted for the primary outcome).

277 6. Incidence of tracheostomy insertion
