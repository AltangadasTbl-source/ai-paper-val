64 ABSTRACT

65 Background

66 The TARGET Protein trial will evaluate the effect of greater enteral protein delivery
67 (augmented protein) on clinical outcomes of critically ill adult patients when compared to
68 —_ usual care.

69

70 Objective

71 ~~ ‘To describe the statistical analysis plan for the TARGET Protein trial.

72

73 Methods

74 TARGET Protein is a cluster randomized, cross-sectional, double cross-over, open-label,
75 registry-embedded, pragmatic clinical trial conducted across Australia and New Zealand. The
76 ~~ trial randomized eight intensive care units (ICU) to receive enteral formula containing either
77 _ higher dose enteral protein (augmented protein) or usual dose protein in a 1:1 ratio, Each ICU
78 received one trial formula for a 3-month period and then switched to the alternate formulae.
79 This sequence was repeated, for a total trial length of 12 months. The primary outcome is the
80 number of days free of the index hospital and alive at day 90. Secondary outcomes include
81 proportion of patients alive at day 90, survivor-only analysis of days free of the index hospital
82 at day 90, duration of invasive ventilation, ICU and hospital length of stay, incidence of
83 tracheostomy insertion, renal replacement therapy, and discharge destination. The statistical
84 methods and models which will be used to estimate the effects for the primary and secondary
85 outcomes are described. All statistical models will account for the cluster-randomized cross-
86 over design to ensure correct estimation of the 95% confidence intervals. Trial enrolment is
87 complete with 3412 patients enrolled. Data linkage is ongoing.
