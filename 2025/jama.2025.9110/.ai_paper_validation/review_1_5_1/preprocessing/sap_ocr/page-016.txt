360 Main analysis

361 Comparisons of treatment groups will use individual patient-level data and a quantile mixed
362 effects model will be fitted to compare the median (0.5 quantile) response of the treatment
363 groups. The quantile mixed effects model will include treatment group (augmented protein
364 versus usual care), period (1, 2, 3 and 4) and the stratification variable used in randomization,
365 delayed start (“Group 1 - Commencing May 2022” and “Group 2- Commencing August
366 2022”), as fixed effects. Cluster (ICU) will be included as a random effect and assumed to be
367 normally distributed with mean zero and variance components oZ (31). The effect of
368 treatment comparisons will be presented as a difference in medians (95% confidence interval
369 (CI); the 95% CI will be calculated using the block-bootstrap method(32). If model
370 convergence issues occur, then the analysis will be performed on the scaled primary outcome
371 (zero mean and unit variance). The treatment effect will then compare the median of the
372 scaled primary outcome between treatment groups.

374 Secondary analyses

375 A Bayesian quantile mixed model for continuous outcomes will be used to model the median
376 (0.5 quantile) response(33, 34). The same fixed and random effects described in the main
377 analysis will be included in the Bayesian quantile mixed model. The effect of treatment
378 comparisons will be presented as the posterior mean of the regression coefficient for
379 treatment (difference in medians) and 95% credible interval. Bayesian quantile mixed
380 modelling will be performed using brms package (35, 36) in R. The default hyperprior
381 distributions in the brms packaged will be specified for the fixed effects (improper flat
382 hyperprior) and standard deviation of the cluster random effect (weakly informative half
383 student-t hyperprior).
