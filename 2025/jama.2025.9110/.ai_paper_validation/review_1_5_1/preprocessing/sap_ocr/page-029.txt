SENSITIVITY ANALYSES
The following sensitivity analyses of the primary result will be performed:
1. Exclusion of patients if they are known to have received non-trial enteral formula.
2. Exclusion of patients admitted for palliative care or organ donation.
3. Imputation of missing data using worst-best and best-worst scenarios (further
details provided in section “Handling missing data”)
