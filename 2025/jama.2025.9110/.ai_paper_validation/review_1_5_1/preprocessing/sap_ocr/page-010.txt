222 ‘The primary outcome is the number of days free of the index hospital and alive at day 90.
223 This is a patient-centered outcome measure that captures both death and morbidity(23-26).
224 = This will be calculated as 90 days minus all days admitted to the index hospital after
225 commencement of trial enteral formula, minus any days readmitted to the index hospital
226 within 90 days. As patients may be readmitted for part of a day (e.g. for dialysis) a
227 readmission will only be counted when the patient remains in hospital at midnight. Patients
228 will be considered alive if they survive the index-hospital episode, and there is no evidence of
