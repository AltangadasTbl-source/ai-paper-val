170 METHODS

171 TARGET Protein is a cluster randomized, cross-sectional double cross-over open-label trial
172 conducted within eight ICUs across Australia and New Zealand. The study was
173 prospectively registered at the Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry on 1
174 November 2021 (ACTRN12621001484831).

175

176 = Clusters

177 Four of eight ICUs were randomly allocated to the sequence augmented protein enteral
178 formula alternating after each 3-month period with usual care enteral protein formula; with
179 the other four ICUs following the opposite sequence. Further details regarding treatment
180 allocation generation and randomization are provided in the published study protocol (15). In
181 the overall 12-month trial duration, this resulted in four periods with alternate enteral
182 nutrition formulae within each of the eight ICU clusters. Patients admitted to each ICU
183 during each 3-month cluster-period received the same trial enteral formula until either the
184 patient reached day 90, was discharged from ICU, or died.
