457 Handling missing data

458 Given that this trial will be using routinely collected data from the ANZICS APD for the
459 primary and secondary outcomes, we anticipate minimal missing data. However, a
460 sensitivity analysis assessing the impact of missing primary outcome data for the main
461 analysis will be performed and involve imputing outcomes under “worst-best” and “best-
462 worst” case scenarios (42) (see below).

463

464 — Primary outcome

465 In the “worst-best” scenario, a “worst” outcome event (e.g., 0 days free of the index hospital
466 and alive) will be assigned to all patients missing the outcome in one treatment group, and a
467 “best” outcome event (e.g., 90 days free of the index hospital and alive) will be assigned to
468 all patients missing the outcome in the other treatment group. The “best-worst” scenario is
469 the exact opposite assignment of outcomes.

470

471 _ If substantively different conclusions do not arise from these two analyses, then no further
472 missing data assessments will be performed for that outcome. If a substantively different
473 conclusion does arise, then a more refined analysis will employ a complete case analysis
474 adjusting for baseline covariates predictive of missingness of the specific outcome. These
475 analyses use data from all patients who have complete outcome data, and are valid under the
476 “covariate missing at random assumption” that missingness depends on the baseline
477 covariates only and not on the value of the missing outcome itself or of other outcomes(43).
478 If more than 5% of the data for a primary outcome is missing(42), and when one outcome is
479 missing for a patient but other outcomes are present, further analyses will use multiple
480 imputation methods that take into account the outcomes that are available and the clustered
481 — data structure(44).
