309 patients per period per cluster would provide 80% power, and ~80 patients per cluster per
310 period would provide 90% power to find a mean difference of one day for the primary
311 outcome. Assuming a normal distribution, a sample size of 2560 from the 8 clusters would
312 provide 90% power to detect a mean difference of 1 day free of the index hospital and alive at
313 day 90. However, the primary outcome (days free of the index hospital and alive at day 90) is
314 unlikely to be normally distributed and parametric calculations are optimistic. For this reason,
315 the goal sample size was inflated by 15% to allow for the likely skewed distribution of data,
316 such that ~3,000 patients from the 8 clusters should provide 90% power (30). After the trial
317 was completed, it was noted that the sample size estimates specified eight clusters based on
318 — eight ICUs being included in the trial. However, the design utilizes four clusters in each of
319 the two treatment sequences. With only four clusters and cluster period size of 260 patients
320 there was still >80% power to detect a two-day difference in the number of days free of the
321 index hospital and alive at day 90 under a range of plausible assumptions.
