337 Analysis sets

338 Because of the cluster-randomized cross-over design of this trial, the intention-to-treat
339 principle applies at both the cluster level and the participant level. At the cluster level, the
340 _intention-to-treat population includes all randomized ICUs that recruited subjects, regardless
341 of the degree of adherence to the study interventions. At the participant level, the intention-
342 — to-treat population means that all patients will be analyzed according to the randomized
343 treatment of their ICU at the time of their index ICU admission, regardless of treatment
344 actually received.

346 Statistical methods

347 Descriptive statistics at cluster level will be presented by allocated treatment sequence, and
348 patient characteristics will be summarized by allocated treatment sequence and observation
349 period. Descriptive statistics of variables concerning trial enteral formula delivery (e.g.,
350 duration of trial nutrition (hours), mean volume of trial enteral formula delivery (ml/day),
351 calories delivered (kcal/day), and protein delivered (g/day)) will be summarized by allocated
352 treatment sequence. The median (25th and 75th percentiles) of protein (g/day) and calories
353 (keal/day) from trial nutrition on days 1-5, 10, 20 and 30 of the study will be presented
354 visually by treatment group. The statistical methods for the primary and secondary outcomes
355 are described below. Details regarding the main analysis plan, sensitivity analyses, and
356 subgroup analyses are located in Table 1.
