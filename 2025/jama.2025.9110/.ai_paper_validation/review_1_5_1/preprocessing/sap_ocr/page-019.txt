434 A descriptive analysis will be performed, where the frequency of each discharge destination
435 will be presented by allocated treatment sequence and observation period.

437 Subgroup analyses

438 Planned subgroup analyses will assess heterogeneity of the treatment effect for the primary
439 — outcome across the following baseline variables: mechanical ventilation, age groups (70 years
440 and older vs younger than 70), body mass index (categorized as 35 kg/m? and greater vs less
441 than 35 kg/m’), and those with acute kidney injury (AKI; yes vs no), defined as receiving
442 new renal replacement therapy between hospital admission and commencement of trial
443 enteral formula on ICU admission. Subgroup analyses will include treatment x subgroup
444 interaction terms in respective regression models. We will present subgroup specific
445 estimates of the treatment effect (95% CI), which will be derived from the treatment main
446 effect estimate and interaction term estimate and the p-value from a likelihood ratio test of an
447 _ interaction between treatment and the subgroup variable.

449 Sensitivity analyses

450 The following sensitivity analyses of the primary outcome and main analysis will be
451 performed:

452 1. Exclusion of patients if they are known to have received non-trial enteral formula.

453 2. Exclusion of patients admitted for palliative care or organ donation.

454 3. Imputation of missing data using worst-best and best-worst scenarios (further details
455 provided in section “Handling missing data”)
