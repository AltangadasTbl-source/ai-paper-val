P-002 (DOC-002 pp. 7–9, 24–25): Background-only quantities: up to 80% muscle wasting; guideline protein 1.2–2.0 g/kg/day or higher; around 130000 ICU admissions/year, >40000 receiving >24-hour organ support/artificial nutrition, ~25% mortality.
