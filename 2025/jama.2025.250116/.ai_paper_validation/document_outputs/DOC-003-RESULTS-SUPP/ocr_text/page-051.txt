eFigure 7. Attainment of the Primary Endpoint According to Baseline Severity
A) According to the pre-defined statistical analysis plan, cut-offs of baseline APACHE,
CCI and SOFA score were calculated which indicate increased risk for 28-day
mortality. The odds for attainment of the primary endpoint (at least 1.4-point decrease
of means SOFA of days 2 to 9 from baseline day 1) for each cut-off are provided.
B) Interaction tests of subgroups with the studied intervention for the primary
endpoint.
Abbreviations: APACHE, acute physiology and chronic health evaluation; CCl,
Charlson’s comorbidity index; Cl, confidence interval; OR, odds ratio; SC, standard
care, SOFA, sequential organ failure assessment.
