## C01 — Day-15 SOFA responder numerator conflict

- **Evidence status:** Verified; critic accepted.
- **Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Minor.
- **Exact source locations:**
  - [jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf](../jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf#page=6), PDF p. 6, journal p. 780, Table 2, “Main secondary outcomes,” row “≥1.4-Point decrease of mean SOFA score d 2 to 15,” Precision immunotherapy column.
  - [jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf](../jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf#page=7), PDF p. 7, journal p. 781, Results, “Secondary End Points,” fourth endpoint.
  - [joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf](../joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf#page=22), PDF/printed p. 22, eTable 10, days-2-to-15 rows for macrophage activation-like syndrome (MALS) and sepsis-induced immunoparalysis (SII).
- **Reported values:** Main Table 2 reports **52/131 (39.7%)** for precision immunotherapy and **34/145 (23.4%)** for placebo, with P=.004. The main narrative reports **39.7%; 51 of 131** for precision immunotherapy. eTable 10 reports precision-immunotherapy responders of **12/25 (48.0%)** in MALS and **40/106 (37.7%)** in SII.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Direct comparison.** Table 2 and the narrative identify the same outcome (at least a 1.4-point decrease in mean SOFA score from baseline day 1 over days 2-15), the same treatment arm, the same denominator of 131, the same displayed percentage of 39.7%, and the same P value of .004. They differ only in the responder numerator: 52 in Table 2 versus 51 in the narrative.
2. **Why the values should agree.** These are not different time points, populations, or precision levels. Both locations describe the fourth prespecified secondary endpoint in the precision-immunotherapy group. The supplement partitions the same arm by immune-dysregulation state; its footnotes define MALS using ferritin greater than 4420 ng/mL and SII using ferritin at or below that threshold plus the HLA-DR criterion. The main article also states that the precision arm comprises 25 MALS and 106 SII participants.
3. **Stratum reconciliation.** The published supplement values yield:

   - responder numerator: `12 + 40 = 52`;
   - arm denominator: `25 + 106 = 131`.

   The two displayed immune-state denominators therefore exhaust the reported precision-immunotherapy denominator, and their displayed responder counts reproduce Table 2's numerator.
4. **Percentage checks.** From the printed values:

   - `52 / 131 × 100 = 39.6947%`, which rounds to **39.7%**;
   - `51 / 131 × 100 = 38.9313%`, which rounds to **38.9%**, not 39.7%.

5. **Direct versus derived evidence.** The three numerator/denominator statements are direct source observations. The sums and percentage conversions are derived checks. No participant-level data were used.
6. **Alternative interpretations.** The package does not establish which published element the authors would amend or how the mismatch arose. The agreement between the main table, the displayed percentage, and the supplement strata explains the verifier's locked decision, but it does not authorize this report to replace the narrative value.

- **Existing supported conclusion:** The main narrative's `51 of 131 (39.7%)` conflicts with Table 2's `52/131 (39.7%)`; eTable 10's mutually classified strata sum to 52/131.
- **Limit on interpretation:** This report does not infer a corrected source value, a transcription mechanism, or participant-level truth.
- **Verification instruction:** Compare the author-approved Table 2 listing, the journal-p. 781 narrative sentence, and the two eTable 10 strata. Confirm which numerator belongs in the narrative and ensure that the final count, denominator, percentage, and underlying analysis output reconcile.

## C02 — Day-15 SII odds-ratio point estimate

- **Evidence status:** Verified; critic accepted.
- **Category:** Arithmetic inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Minor.
- **Exact source location:** [joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf](../joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf#page=22), PDF/printed p. 22, eTable 10, section “≥1.4-point decrease of mean SOFA of days 2 to 15 from baseline day 1,” SII row, the two treatment columns and `OR unadjusted (95% CI)` column.
- **Reported values:** Precision immunotherapy **40/106 (37.7%)**; placebo **29/122 (23.8%)**; difference **13.9% (95% CI, 1.9 to 25.6)**; unadjusted OR **1.194 (95% CI, 1.09 to 3.45)**; P=.030.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Construct the published 2×2 table.** The eTable supplies responders and totals. The complementary nonresponder counts are derived by subtraction:

   | Arm | Responder | Nonresponder | Total |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | Precision immunotherapy | 40 | `106 − 40 = 66` | 106 |
   | Placebo | 29 | `122 − 29 = 93` | 122 |

2. **Why the point estimate should reconcile.** The table explicitly labels the estimate `OR unadjusted`; the displayed counts define the corresponding crude 2×2 comparison. The main article, [jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf](../jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf#page=6), PDF p. 6, journal p. 780, says ORs and 95% CIs for primary and secondary endpoints were calculated using the Mantel-Haenszel test. This report therefore treats the simple cross-product calculation as a diagnostic reproduction of the published cells, not as a replacement analysis.
3. **Point-estimate calculation.** The odds ratio for response in precision immunotherapy versus placebo is:

   `OR = (40/66) / (29/93) = (40 × 93) / (66 × 29) = 3720 / 1914 = 1.9436`.

   That does not reproduce the displayed point estimate `1.194`.
4. **Diagnostic CI check.** Using the displayed cells and a conventional log-OR Wald calculation:

   `SE[ln(OR)] = √(1/40 + 1/66 + 1/29 + 1/93) = 0.2922`.

   `exp{ln(1.9436) ± 1.96 × 0.2922} ≈ 1.10 to 3.45`.

   The diagnostic interval is approximately compatible with the printed interval of 1.09 to 3.45 and with a point estimate near 1.94, not with 1.194.
5. **Direct versus derived evidence.** All four event/total values, the printed OR, CI, and P value are direct observations. Nonresponder counts, the cross-product OR, and the diagnostic CI are derived.
6. **Alternative interpretations.** The package lacks the statistical-program output and exact interval construction. A stratified Mantel-Haenszel estimate or another method can differ from a crude cross-product OR; however, this row is explicitly presented within a single SII stratum as unadjusted, and the printed CI closely tracks the diagnostic calculation. The locked finding identifies a point-estimate inconsistency; it does not assert that `1.9436` is an authorized correction or that the displayed P value must come from the Wald calculation.

- **Existing supported conclusion:** The displayed counts yield a diagnostic crude OR of approximately 1.94, whereas the table prints 1.194; the printed CI is approximately compatible with the former.
- **Limit on interpretation:** The exact estimator, CI method, rounding pipeline, and source-output value cannot be confirmed without the original analysis output. No correction mechanism is inferred.
- **Verification instruction:** Recalculate the designated unadjusted OR using the prespecified analysis and compare the program output with the published event cells, point estimate, CI, and P value. Determine the intended published point estimate from the source output rather than from this diagnostic calculation alone.

## C03 — eFigure 9 APACHE point estimate outside its confidence interval

- **Evidence status:** Verified; critic accepted.
- **Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Minor.
- **Exact source location:** [joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf](../joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf#page=53), PDF/printed p. 53, eFigure 9, “90-Day Mortality According to Baseline Severity,” panel A APACHE II ≥25 row and panel B `APACHE II ≥25 × Precision Immunotherapy` row.
- **Reported values:** Panel A reports deaths of **32/40** with standard care plus placebo and **31/38** with standard care plus precision immunotherapy. Panel B reports **OR 0.11 (95% CI, 0.36-3.42), P=.86** for `APACHE II ≥25 × Precision Immunotherapy`.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Endpoint-order and containment check.** A reported point estimate should lie between the two endpoints of its displayed confidence interval. Here, `0.11 < 0.36 < 3.42`; therefore, 0.11 is outside the interval 0.36-3.42. The interval endpoints themselves are in ascending order and preserve positive OR scale.
2. **Why the figure components should reconcile.** The row displays one OR followed immediately by one 95% CI and one P value. Panel A provides the linked APACHE II ≥25 mortality cells for the two randomized treatment groups.
3. **Cell reconstruction.** From panel A, nondeath counts are `40 − 32 = 8` for placebo and `38 − 31 = 7` for precision immunotherapy. The within-high-stratum treatment-versus-placebo mortality OR is:

   `OR = (31/7) / (32/8) = (31 × 8) / (7 × 32) = 248 / 224 = 1.1071`.

4. **Diagnostic CI.** The conventional log-OR standard error is:

   `√(1/31 + 1/7 + 1/32 + 1/8) = 0.5757`.

   This gives `exp{ln(1.1071) ± 1.96 × 0.5757} ≈ 0.358 to 3.421`, which rounds approximately to the printed 0.36-3.42.
5. **Direct versus derived evidence.** The figure's point estimate, CI, P value, and event/total cells are direct observations. The nondeath counts, cross-product OR, and diagnostic CI are derived.
6. **Alternative interpretations.** An interaction estimate need not equal a within-stratum treatment OR, and a P value need not test a zero coefficient using the same method as the CI. Those caveats do not resolve the direct containment failure. The cell reconstruction explains why the completed workflow associated the printed CI with a value near 1.11; it does not authorize substitution of 1.11 for the published 0.11.

- **Existing supported conclusion:** The published OR of 0.11 lies outside its own displayed 95% CI, while the displayed APACHE II ≥25 cells yield a diagnostic OR near 1.11 and an interval near 0.36-3.42.
- **Limit on interpretation:** The supplied package does not establish whether the OR has a missing digit, whether the CI is attached to the wrong estimand, or whether another production error occurred.
- **Verification instruction:** Inspect the source model output and figure-generation table for the APACHE II ≥25 row. Confirm the intended estimand and reconcile its point estimate, CI, P value, panel-A cells, row label, and caption.

## C05 — Within-high-stratum treatment effects labeled and interpreted as interaction tests

- **Evidence status:** Verified with scoped wording; critic accepted. The finding is supported by eFigures 7 and 9 and does not rely on the duplicated eFigure 8 panel.
- **Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Major.
- **Exact source locations:**
  - [joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf](../joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf#page=51), PDF/printed p. 51, eFigure 7, panel A event cells, panel B labels/estimates, and caption B.
  - [joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf](../joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf#page=53), PDF/printed p. 53, eFigure 9, panel A event cells, panel B labels/estimates, and caption B.
  - [jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf, PDF p. 7](../jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf#page=7), journal p. 781, and [jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf, PDF p. 8](../jama_giamarellosbourboulis_2025_oi_250116_1771885794.23757.pdf#page=8), journal p. 782, “Post Hoc and Subgroup Analyses.”
- **Reported statements:** Both figure captions call panel B “Interaction tests of subgroups with the studied intervention.” Rows are labeled with forms such as `CCI ≥5 × Precision Immunotherapy`. The main text states that interaction tests were statistically significant for CCI ≥5 and SOFA ≥10 for the primary endpoint and for 28-day mortality, and that no significant interactions were found for 90-day mortality.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Identify the compared quantities.** A within-high-stratum treatment OR compares precision immunotherapy with placebo among participants at or above a severity cutoff. An interaction OR, by contrast, compares treatment effects across the high and low strata—for a simple unadjusted diagnostic, a ratio of the two stratum-specific treatment ORs. The caption and `severity × treatment` labels present the panel-B entries as the latter.
2. **Reconstruct eFigure 7's high-stratum entries.** Using the primary-endpoint event cells:

   - APACHE II ≥25: `(12 × 32) / (26 × 8) = 384 / 208 = 1.846`, matching panel B **1.85**;
   - CCI ≥5: `(20 × 69) / (34 × 7) = 1380 / 238 = 5.798`, matching **5.79**;
   - SOFA ≥10: `(25 × 57) / (42 × 11) = 1425 / 462 = 3.084`, matching **3.08**.

   Thus, all three entries labeled `cutoff × Precision Immunotherapy` numerically reconstruct as treatment ORs *within the high-severity strata*.
3. **Contrast with an interaction diagnostic.** For SOFA <10 in eFigure 7, the treatment OR is:

   `(21 × 62) / (43 × 15) = 1302 / 645 = 2.019`.

   The crude ratio of high- to low-stratum treatment ORs is therefore `3.084 / 2.019 = 1.53`, not the displayed `3.08`. This ratio is illustrative only; a fitted interaction model can use different methods, covariates, variance estimators, or coding.
4. **Independent eFigure 9 check.** For 90-day mortality, the same numerical pattern appears:

   - CCI ≥5: `(39 × 12) / (15 × 64) = 468 / 960 = 0.4875`, matching the labeled interaction entry **0.49**;
   - SOFA ≥10: `(49 × 11) / (18 × 57) = 539 / 1026 = 0.5253`, matching **0.53**.

   The eFigure 9 APACHE entry has the additional digit/CI inconsistency covered separately by C03. The CCI and SOFA reconstructions are sufficient to support C05 without relying on that malformed row or on eFigure 8.
5. **Cross-text check.** The main narrative calls the cited values “Interaction tests” and interprets significance by severity cutoff. Yet the eFigure 7 and eFigure 9 values labeled as interaction terms reconstruct from high-stratum treatment cells alone. This aligns the presentation concern across figure labels, captions, and prose.
6. **Direct versus derived evidence.** Labels, captions, narrative statements, event cells, and displayed triplets are direct observations. Within-stratum ORs and the crude ratio-of-ORs are derived diagnostics.
7. **Alternative interpretations and inferential limits.** The package does not supply a fully specified interaction model, design matrix, coefficient coding, variance estimator, degrees-of-freedom correction, or source output. A P value cannot be assumed to test `H0: interaction coefficient = 0`, nor can it be assumed to share the same inferential method as a displayed CI, unless the source output establishes that. These missing definitions limit explanation of how the values arose; they do not change the locked finding that the published entries numerically equal within-high-stratum effects while being labeled and interpreted as interactions.

- **Existing supported conclusion:** In eFigures 7 and 9, multiple entries labeled as severity-by-treatment interaction results reproduce treatment ORs within the high-severity strata; the main text interprets selected entries as interaction tests. The finding remains independently supported after excluding eFigure 8.
- **Limit on interpretation:** The correct interaction estimates and P values cannot be derived authoritatively without the prespecified model and its source output. This report does not claim that the crude ratios shown above are the intended corrections.
- **Verification instruction:** Obtain the figure-generation/model output and record, for every panel-B row, the dependent outcome, treatment and severity coding, interaction term, reference strata, covariates, estimator, CI method, and tested null. Match each displayed OR/CI/P triplet to the appropriate model coefficient. Then align labels, captions, and main-text claims. Review eFigure 8 under C04 separately.

## C06 — Malformed severe-adverse-event cell

- **Evidence status:** Verified; critic accepted.
- **Category:** Presentation inconsistency.
- **Severity:** Minor.
- **Exact source location:** [joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf](../joi250116supp2_prod_1771885794.27755.pdf#page=30), PDF/printed p. 30, eTable 14, “Any AE; Classification by Maximum Severity, n (%)” section, `Severe` row, `Standard care + rhIFNγ (N=106)` column.
- **Reported value:** The cell visibly reads **`45 42.5)`**. The row's other cells are **12 (48.0)**, **14 (60.9)**, **45 (36.9)**, and total **116 (42.0)**.

**Reasoning procedure**

1. **Notation check.** The eTable section header explicitly specifies `n (%)`. The reported cell contains a count, a percentage, and a closing parenthesis but lacks the opening parenthesis used throughout neighboring cells.
