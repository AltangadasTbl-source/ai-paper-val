Recruitment method Morning (N=1680) Bedtime (N=1677)

* PCP recruitment included mailing a letter of introduction / study information to all attached patients with
hypertension, clinic posters, and tear away notepads describing the trial that could be handed to patients during
appointments. 436 PCPs mailed recruitment information (429 family physicians and 7 nurse practitioners).
41,128 letters were mailed in total. A median of 77.5 letters were mailed per PCP (IQR 77.25). Overall, 6.2% of
all letters mailed resulted in a randomized participant.
† Other includes: 1) word of mouth, 2) a website describing the trial (i.e. the BedMed study website, the
pragmatic trials collaborative website, or the University of Alberta website), 3) public events, or 4) the same
social media ad instead running on closed circuit television within Alberta government run outpatient clinics.
