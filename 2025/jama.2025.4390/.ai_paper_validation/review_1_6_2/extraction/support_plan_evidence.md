## Protocol numeric and statistical specifications

[DOC-002 p. 2](../../../joi250019supp1_prod_1749674951.29554.pdf#page=2): Event-driven PROBE design. Eligibility: hypertension, >=1 BP medication, community dwelling. Exclusions: palliative, unable to consent, personal glaucoma history or glaucoma medication. Background MAPEC reports 187/1084 vs 68/1072, RR 0.39 (95% CI 0.29-0.51), P<.001, median 5.6 years, 61% reduction; this is historical context rather than BedMed evidence. Other contextual population/cost/risk-reduction figures on this page do not define BedMed analyses.

[DOC-002 p. 3](../../../joi250019supp1_prod_1749674951.29554.pdf#page=3): Bedtime intervention switches one or more morning BP drugs one at a time as tolerated, or maintains bedtime use; which/how many at provider discretion. Morning comparator maintains or switches to morning. Primary is all-cause death or hospital admission/ED visit for ACS/MI, HF, stroke. Study ends at 406 participants with a primary event, quarterly monitoring. Secondary: each primary component; all-cause admission; resource intensity weight and length of stay; nursing-home admission; EQ-5D five functional measures and overall health score. Safety: new glaucoma, glaucoma surgery, hip fracture, vision since study start (unchanged/slightly/much worse), syncope and falls in prior month (yes/no). Tolerability: overnight urination count, prior-month nocturia burden (no/minor/major problem), lightheadedness/faintness prior month. Process: baseline BP meds switched at 6 months by class; 24-hour BP at 6 months.

N100/S103: survival analysis, allocation 1:1, 25% relative risk reduction, power .8, alpha .05, 379 events without useful covariates. N101: increase by 7% to 406 events for withdrawal/noncompliance/loss, projected together 5%. N102: control event rate 2.9%, trial-wide 2.0%, rates from 2005/06 claims excluding death to avoid overlap; trial-wide lower rate assumes MAPEC-like reduction. N103/N104: 35 enrollees/day, 50 weeks/year, 8750 over 12 months and another 22 months follow-up, expected 406 outcomes. The method is not printed as an equation; the event inputs are retained for reproduction by checkers.

[DOC-002 p. 4](../../../joi250019supp1_prod_1749674951.29554.pdf#page=4): Individual simple randomization, no stratification/blocking, central REDCap concealed/irreversible assignment. Follow-up 1 week, 6 weeks, 6 months, then every 6 months, optional online after 6 months (31% pilot chose online). Interim at 200 primary events, expected 20 months; DSMB clinical judgement if P<=.001 benefit or P<=.05 harm. BP substudy 100 bedtime +100 morning at 6 months, 24-hour BP. Diuretic substudy after 200 people whose only BP drug is diuretic (including combination): analyze six-week adherence in all bedtime-allocated BP drug users; substantially worse adherence can exclude further diuretic-only enrollment (no numerical adherence cutoff printed).

Original primary Cox baseline covariates: age >=80, sex, binary Tilburg frailty, binary Short Blessed cognitive impairment, smoking, hospitalization prior 6 months, >=3 BP meds, diabetes, CHF, stroke/TIA, CAD, significant renal impairment (claims renal insufficiency diagnosis, eGFR<40 or dialysis).

[DOC-002 p. 5](../../../joi250019supp1_prod_1749674951.29554.pdf#page=5): Feasibility inputs 365 physicians x250 invitations =91250, 85% BP medication use, 12% interested/eligible, expected 8750 enrolled; historical response 20.4%, typical panel 1500-1800 and prevalence 19.6%. Thirty-five/workday with 50% time of 3 assistants. Stated duration under lowest expected event rate 2 years 43 weeks; at powered 25% reduction, 2 years 4 months, six months sooner; true 37% reduction gives 50% probability interim P<=.001 at 20 months. Overall completion/implementation 23-36 months including 2-3 months analysis. Historical physician-network counts are administrative context, not current trial sample counts.

[DOC-002 p. 6](../../../joi250019supp1_prod_1749674951.29554.pdf#page=6): Outcomes from DAD hospital records, ambulatory database for ED, dispenses for costs, labs for renal status; quarterly primary outcome counts and full interim/final extractions. References occupy remainder and p. 7; no supplied underlying datasets.

## SAP endpoint specifications

[DOC-003 p. 2](../../../joi250019supp2_prod_1749674951.30054.pdf#page=2): Unless stated, all endpoints across study duration. Primary MACE first occurrence of all-cause death or admission/ED for ACS/MI, stroke, CHF. Six secondary headings: each primary component separately; all-cause hospitalization/ED; LTC nursing home or assisted living; nonvertebral fracture; new glaucoma; cognitive decline at 18 months defined >=2-point worsening in Short Blessed Test versus baseline.

Supplementary safety: vision much worse versus previous follow-up once, or slightly worse on >=2 occasions (every 6 months); cognition newly Short Blessed >=10 at 18 months or new dementia diagnosis at any follow-up; symptomatic hypotension lightheaded/faint without LOC, fainting with LOC, falling in preceding month, plus hip fracture. Symptom questions at 6 weeks, 6 months and every 6 months. Nocturia: change baseline overnight urinations/week at 6 weeks/6 months; burden preceding month no nocturia/no problem/minor problem/major problem at those same times.

[DOC-003 p. 3](../../../joi250019supp2_prod_1749674951.30054.pdf#page=3): Cost acute (admission resource intensity weight and stay length); total acute +medication +physician costs. Entirely claims; no claims means exclude cost analysis. Exploratory overall EQ-5D-5L health score at 12 months. Process proportion BP doses at allocated time at 6 months, BID counts half AM/half PM; sleep-time systolic BP after 6 months, consecutive 302 Alberta residents. Investigators unblinded only aggregate adherence and aggregate 24-hour BP, allowing alterations if poorly applied; otherwise blinded outcomes.

ITT loss: ongoing claims imply alive, censor on last medical service or indication of death, whichever later; no claims censor last successful phone/email. Withdrawal retaining claims access uses same rule; no consent censor withdrawal. Missing each analysis: preceding/subsequent follow-up imputation or exclude, favor minimizing bias, or conservative against benefit/toward harm if unavoidable (sentence finishes p. 4). Nonadherence not exclusion except harm meaningful only on treatment (example nocturia diuretic vs nondiuretic among bedtime switchers).
