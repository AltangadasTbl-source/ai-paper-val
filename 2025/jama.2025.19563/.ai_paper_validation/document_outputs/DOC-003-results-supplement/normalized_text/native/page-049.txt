1Rationale: Participants were instructed to wear an ActiGraph device for one week each month to
measure moderate-to-vigorous physical activity. Each month, study staff sent reminders and follow-up
messages to prompt device use. If no valid wear data were recorded despite this outreach, MVPA was
imputed as zero for that month to avoid overestimating physical activity. This approach assumes that
failure to wear the monitor after repeated prompting may reflect disengagement and low likelihood of
physical activity. Given that activity was assessed monthly over a year, this method minimizes the
impact of any single missing week and provides a reasonable approximation of long-term behavior.
Actigraphy non-compliance did not alter a participant’s ability to achieve the primary endpoint through
A1C or body weight change.
