## Merge decisions and matching precautions

- Matching occurrences within a mapper key are preserved as one record with all locations.
- Main primary, age-adjusted, subgroup, per-protocol, and sensitivity results are not merged when model or analysis condition differs.
- Planned protocol definitions are not merged with observed results.
- No Office workbook, DOC/DOCX, or CSV is supplied; no formula-versus-cached-value record exists.
- Explicit no-applicable pages are recorded in canonical extraction maps and component parts; no page was omitted.
