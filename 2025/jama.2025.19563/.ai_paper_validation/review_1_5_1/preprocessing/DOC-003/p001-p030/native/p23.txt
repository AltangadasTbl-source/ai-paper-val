JITAI algorithm 1.0
Data is collected automatically from the user's smartphone and connected devices
(Apple Health, Google Health, activity data, location, calendar data--when
permissions are enabled). Users can also manually input data, including meal logs,
activities not detected by sensors (e.g., swimming), and weight measurements.
Only collected data is used; missing data is not imputed.
Data is processed exclusively by the AI system. No human intervention is
involved in data processing. The AI autonomously selects goal recommendations,
educational content, and the timing of notifications.
The AI system determines which notifications to send, when to send them, and the
content of messages, optimizing for behavior change based on user data.
The AI does not engage in clinical decision-making. It provides behavioral
nudges, goal-setting recommendations, and habit-forming prompts but does not
make clinical determinations.
