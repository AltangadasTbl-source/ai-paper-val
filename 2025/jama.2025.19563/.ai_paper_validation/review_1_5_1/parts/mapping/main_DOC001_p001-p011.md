# DOC-001 Main Quantitative Evidence Map: PDF pp. 1-11

## Scope, method, and coverage

**Direct source:** `jama_mathioudakis_2025_oi_250084_1765403089.60451.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-11.  Every page was mapped. Reused page-linked native text was used as a locator; direct PDF layout extraction was used across pp. 1-11 and the direct rendered PDF was visually inspected for Figure 3 (p. 8). Existing rendered companions were available for pp. 5-9. No candidate assessment or adjudication is made here.

Pages 10-11 contain article information, disclosures, and references only; no new study result, table, figure, numerical analysis, or statistical relationship was found there. Page 1 contains the abstract; p. 2 contains the Key Points and study design text; pp. 3-4 contain outcome definitions and analysis methods; pp. 5-9 contain the flow diagram, baseline table, results, Figures 2-4, and matching discussion claims.

`M-N` keys are provisional numeric/reporting-relationship keys; `M-S` keys are provisional inferential-statistical keys. Matching occurrences are deliberately cross-referenced rather than separately counted as distinct relationships.

## Study frame, outcome definition, and analysis rules

| Key | PDF location | Printed evidence and relationship definition |
|---|---|---|
| M-N001 | p. 1 Abstract; p. 2 Key Points; p. 4 Outcomes/Results | Population: adults aged >=18 years with prediabetes and overweight/obesity; phase 3, parallel-group, pragmatic RCT at 2 US sites, Oct 11 2021-Dec 16 2024; 12-month interventions. Total randomized/primary ITT population = 368. |
| M-N002 | p. 1 Abstract; p. 2 Key Points; p. 3-4 Outcomes | Composite primary outcome at 12 months: HbA1c <6.5% throughout study **and** >=5% weight loss, or >=4% weight loss plus >=150 min/wk actigraphy-measured MVPA, or absolute HbA1c reduction >=0.2 percentage points. HbA1c-change component applies only to baseline HbA1c 5.7%-6.4%. |
| M-S001 | p. 1 Abstract; p. 4 Sample Size/Primary Outcomes | AI referral versus human referral, risk difference (AI minus human) estimated by binomial regression in all randomized participants; noninferiority if lower boundary of the 1-sided 95% CI is >= -15 percentage points. Primary missing 12-month visits counted as nonachievement. |
| M-S002 | p. 4 Sample Size Calculation | Design assumptions: 15-point noninferiority margin; reference study difference 46.7% (95% CI 43.1%-50.2%) from 47% versus 0.3%; preserving 50% would give 23 points. Calculated n=276 (138/group), 80% power, one-sided alpha .05; allowing 25% attrition gives target n=368 (184/group). |
| M-S003 | p. 4 Statistical Analysis | Five post hoc sensitivity families: multiple imputation (MAR); baseline-imbalance adjustment; alternative actigraphy assumptions; pattern-mixture MNAR; cluster-robust SEs for shared program exposure. Restricted descriptive analysis: 12-month data and no prohibited medicines. Individual components use binomial regression; initiation/completion and incident HbA1c >=6.5% use chi-square tests; secondary CIs/P values descriptive without multiplicity adjustment. |
