- **Source / scope:** `joi250033supp4_prod_1750956987.77981.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-15. Native text was used for all pages; the rendered/OCR source-linked pages were reviewed for pp. 8-15. This map extracts evidence only; it makes no error finding.

### Supporting analysis methods

- **eMethods, pp. 2-5 (trial/patient/procedure support):** independent blinded committee adjudicated possible recurrence; the intervention was CT angiography followed by MMA embolization within 7 days of surgery, with procedure details on pp. 3-5. Patients treated with craniotomy or twist-drill rather than burr-hole were not eligible (p. 3).
- **eMethods, p. 6 (primary-analysis support):** patients not evaluable for the main endpoint were multiply imputed, except neurological- or unknown-cause deaths, which were counted as CSDH recurrence. MICE used recurrence at 6 months, demographics, CSDH features, clinical/surgical variables, and complications; 10 datasets were generated and pooled by Rubin rules.

### Figures and participant flow

- **eFigure 1, p. 8 - exclusion flow:** 659 adults who had surgery for CSDH and were at risk of recurrence; 317 excluded and 342 randomized. Exclusion groups: refusal 101; other pre-specified non-inclusion criteria 164; other reasons 18; unknown reasons 34. The detailed subcounts are supplied on this page: pre-specified criteria 38, 35, 29, 19, 19, 11, 8, and 5; other reasons 8, 3, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1, and 1.
- **eFigure 2, p. 9 - primary endpoint and sensitivity analyses:** adjusted logistic regression for 6-month adjudicated CSDH recurrence, adjusted for anticoagulant/antiplatelet use and unilateral/bilateral CSDH. Reported rows: primary, full analysis set with multiple imputation, 24/162 vs 33/157, OR 0.64 (95% CI, 0.36-1.14), P=.13; complete adjudicated cases, 24/162 vs 33/157, OR 0.64 (0.35-1.14), P=.13; on-site assessment with multiple imputation, 27/162 vs 38/156, OR 0.61 (0.35-1.06), P=.08; adjudicated cases without multiple imputation, 24/171 vs 33/171, OR 0.66 (0.37-1.19), P=.17; excluding non-embolized MMA patients with multiple imputation, 22/138 vs 33/157, OR 0.71 (0.39-1.30), P=.27. The footnote states that losses to follow-up and non-neurological deaths were considered no event in the no-imputation analysis.
