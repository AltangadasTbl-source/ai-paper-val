1 Brief summary  
 
Standard of care for the management of symptomatic chronic subdural hematomas (CSDH) is 
neurosurgical burr-hole evacuation followed by drainage. Post-operative recurrence rates 
may be as high as 10 to 20 %. In particular, recurrence rate increases with antiplatelet and 
anticoagulant therapy. Middle meningeal artery (MMA) embolization has been proposed as a 
potential treatment to prevent CSDH recurrence. The aim of the EMPROTECT (Embolization 
of the Middle Meningeal Artery for the Prevention of Chronic Subdural Hematoma Recurrence 
in High Risk Patients) trial is to assess the efficacy of MMA embolization, performed within 7 
days of CSDH burr-hole surgery, in reducing the risk of CSDH recurrence at 6 months as 
compared with standard medical treatment in patients at high risk of postoperative 
recurrence. 
EMPROTECT is a multicenter open randomized controlled trial with blinded endpoint 
assessment by an adjudication committee. Two follow-up visits are planned: One month 
intermediate visit and 6 month final visit.  
2 Objectives and endpoints 
2.1 Main objective and primary endpoint 
The primary objective of the trial is to assess the efficacy of MMA embolization in reducing 
the risk of postoperative recurrence of SDH at 6 months after burr-hole surgery as compared 
to standard medical treatment in patients at high risk of post-operative recurrence. 
The primary outcome is the rate of chronic subdural hematoma recurrence 6 months after 
index burr-hole surgery, defined as: 
• Reappearance of a homolateral CSDH with a midline shift > 5mm or a symptomatic 
homolateral SDH, including leading to death  
• Or, the presence of a homolateral CSDH > 10mm in maximal thickness on the 6 months 
control head CT scan  
• Or, the need for repeated surgery for a homolateral CSDH recurrence  
• Or, the need for a new hospital admission in relation to a homolateral CSDH recurrence 
A blinded assessment of all potential recurrences according to the definition of the primary 
endpoint will be ensured by an independent adjudication committee. 
 
The committee will adjudicate the following cases: 
• A recurrence following the definition reported in this protocol according to the local 
investigator  
• A hospital admission potentially related to the CSDH  
• A medical visit other than the M1 and M6 planned visits, potentially related to the

2.2 Secondary endpoints 
The secondary endpoints are the following: 
• Rate of repeated surgery for a homolateral CSDH recurrence during the 6-month 
follow-up period 
• Rate of disability and dependency at 1 and 6 months, defined by a mRS score ≥4. The 
mRS is an ordered scale coded from 0 (no symptoms at all) through 5 (severe disability) 
to 6 (death) 
• Mortality rate at 1 and 6 months 
• Total cumulative duration of hospital stay, during the 6-month follow-up period, 
directly or indirectly related to the CSDH 
• Minor and major embolization procedure-related complication rates. 
o Major complications are defined as : 
 Major complications at the puncture site (retroperitoneal hematoma or 
arterial pseudo-aneurysm requiring surgical treatment) 
 Deterioration of renal function with a persistent drop in creatinine 
clearance of more than 10 points 
 Neurological accident with permanent sequalae 
 Other major complications 
o Minor complications are defined as: 
 Superficial hematoma at the puncture site 
 Transient deterioration of renal function 
 Transient neurological accident or asymptomatic arterial dissection 
 Other minor complications  
3 Methods 
 
3.1 Flow chart 
A CONSORT flow chart will be provided to describe the number of eligible patients and the number 
of patients effectively included and randomized in total and in each of the two arms as well as 
patient follow-up (number lost to follow-up per arm). It will also present the number of patients 
analyzed in each group. 
3.2 Sample size calculation

(control group) to 5% (intervention group) with an 80% power, an overall bilateral alpha (α) risk

of 5%, 142 patients per group are necessary. The sample size has been calculated considering  two

3.3 Analysis population 
The primary analysis will be carried out in the intention-to-treat (ITT) population; each patient 
randomized in the study will be analyzed in the arm assigned by randomization regardless of the 
treatment actually received. 
 
A sensitivity analysis will be carried out by excluding patients from the experimental group who did 
not receive embolization following the results of the CT angiography. 
3.4 Descriptive analysis 
For each group and at each evaluation time, the categorical variables will be described as number, 
percentage and missing data by response modality and the quantitative variables by mean, 
standard deviation or median and interquartile range (IQR) according to the normality distribution 
of the variable. 
 
3.5 Primary endpoint analysis  
The primary endpoint will be analyzed in 2 stages sequential design: 
1. An interim analysis planned for 129 randomized patients with known recurrence status 
at 6 months.  
2. A final analysis at the end of the study 
 
This sequential analysis is based on a 2-step Lan and Demets approach with an O’Brien & Fleming 
type alpha risk spending function. 
 
Interim analysis 
For the interim analysis, 6-month CSDH recurrences by group will be analyzed using a mixed 
logistic model regression adjusted on randomization stratification factors. The randomization 
group (intervention versus control), the use of anticoagulant/antiplatelet and the unilateral or 
bilateral nature of the CSDH will be included as fixed effects and the center will be introduced as 
a random effect. In case of non-convergence of the model, a Mantel-Haensel test (adjusted on the 
center and other randomization stratification factors) will be used.  
Patients deceased without prior recurrence will be considered as recurrences. 
 
The primary analysis will be carried out according to the results of the adjudication and after 
imputation, which we will be completed with the analysis without adjudication of the recurrence 
of CSDH (assessment of the primary outcome by on-site investigators), in sensitivity analysis.

The conditional power will also be estimated by simulations with the assumptions leading to the 
calculation of the necessary number of subjects knowing the recurrence rates observed during the 
interim analysis. It will be given for information to the independent Data Safety Monitoring Board 
(DSMB). Stopping the trial for futility could possibly be decided if the conditional power is 
considered by the DSMB to be excessively low. However, no futility limit is provided. 
 
In the event of a decision to stop the study, following the interim analysis, for rejection of the 
hypothesis of equality of CSDH recurrences rates between the two groups at the nominal 
threshold of alpha at 0.001, the final analysis will include the patients analyzed who led to the 
decision to stop, as well as all the patients for whom the primary endpoint could be collected 
subsequently (over-running patients). Likewise, we will continue to monitor included patients who 
have already benefited from embolization until 6 months and they will be included in the final 
analysis. 
 
The interim analysis has been carried out and the results did not meet the predefined stopping 
rule. Therefore, the DSMB recommended to continue the trial. 
 
 
Final analysis of the primary endpoint 
• 
Primary analysis 
The primary analysis will be the analysis of the primary outcome as assessed by the adjudication 
committee, in the intention-to-treat population, using a mixed logistic regression model adjusted 
on randomization stratification factors. The randomization group (intervention versus control), 
the use of anticoagulant/antiplatelet, the unilateral or bilateral nature of the SDH will be included 
as fixed effects and the center will be introduced as a random effect (to take into account the 
multicenter nature of the study). An adjusted absolute risk difference, in the recurrence rate at 6 
months, will also be estimated as well as its 95% confidence interval using bootstrap.  
Missing data will be imputed using multiple imputation and patients deceased without recurrence, 
with a cause of death considered to be neurological or undetermined, will be considered as 
treatment failure.  
• 
Subgroup analyses 
Two subgroup analyses for the primary outcome will be performed according to the unilateral 
versus bilateral CSDH status and anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy medication status 
(yes vs no).  For the two analyses, we will test interaction between randomization group and the 
two variables, respectively, in the logistic model describe above. 
 
• 
Sensitivity analyses 
The primary analysis will be completed with sensitivity analyses: 
o Analysis without adjudication of the recurrence of CSDH (assessment of the 
primary outcome by on-site investigators) 
o Analysis in the intent to treat population but without imputation (ie, lost to follow-
up will be considered as no event) 
o Analysis without patients who did not receive embolization in the experimental 
arm (following CT angiography or for other reasons) if any (ie, modified intent to 
treat analysis) 
o Analysis without patients wrongly included (ie, patients presenting at least one 
non-inclusion criteria or not all inclusion criteria) if any

3.6 Secondary endpoints analyses  
• 
Rate of repeated surgery for a homolateral CSDH recurrence during the 6-month follow-
up period will be compared between the two treatment groups using Chi-square test or 
Fisher exact test as appropriate. 
• 
Rate of disability and dependency, defined by an mRS  score ≥ 4, at 1 month and 6 months 
will be compared between the two treatment groups using a GEE logistic model. This 
model will include as parameters the randomization arm (intervention versus control), the 
time as categorical variable (1 and 6 months) and the time*randomization arm interaction. 
The interaction term will determine whether a significant difference is observed between 
the two arms of the study at one month and at 6 months. At each time (1 month and 6 
month) and in each group, rate of disability and dependency will be estimated with its 95%  
confidence interval  
• 
Mortality rate at 1 and 6 months will be compared between  the two treatment groups 
using Chi-square test or Fisher exact test as appropriate 
• 
Total cumulative duration of hospital stay, during the 6-month follow-up period, directly 
or indirectly related to the CSDH, will be compared between the two treatment groups 
using Wilcoxon rank-sum test.   
• 
Minor and major complications will be compared between the two treatment groups using 
Chi-square test or Fisher exact test as appropriate. 
 
 
Adverse events observed at 1 month and 6 months will be described overall and according to their 
nature by treatment group, in terms of number, and percentage of patients affected. Serious 
adverse events will be described separately. Fisher exact test will be used to compare the 
proportions of patients who died, with at least one adverse event/serious adverse event, at least 
one serious adverse event between the two treatment arms. The number of adverse events and 
the number of serious adverse events will be compared between the two groups using Poisson 
regression model. 
 
3.7 Statistical significance level planned  
All analyses will be carried out at the two-sided alpha risk of 5% (except for the interim analysis 
carried out at the nominal threshold of 0.001). 
 
3.8 Statistical criteria for stopping research  
Inclusions could be stopped after execution of the interim analysis, in the event of a difference 
between the two groups proven at the threshold of 0.001 or due to futility in the event of 
conditional power deemed excessively low. 
 
The research will end at the end of the follow-up of the last patient included.

3.9 Missing data management  
In the primary endpoint analysis, a multiple imputation method will be implemented for patients 
who cannot be evaluated for the main endpoint. 
  The Multiple Imputation by Chained Equations (MICE) technique will be used to replace missing 
values. The variables used in the imputation phase will be recurrence at 6 months, age, sex, 
unilateral or bilateral nature of CHDS and use of anticoagulant/antiplatelet as well as the 
characteristics of CSDH at inclusion, namely: Type of CSDH (initial episode or recurrence), proven 
recent head trauma, GCS score, ASA score, focal neurological deficit, hemiparesis, phasic disorder, 
gait disorder, headache , maximum thickness of the SDH, Park classification, type of surgery (burr 
hole, Trephine craniotomy), complications (infection, subdural empyema, epileptic seizure 
occurring during surgery or immediately afterward ). Analyses will be carried out using R software 
and the mice package. 10 datasets will be generated. The results from each imputed dataset will 
be pooled using Rubin's rules to obtain overall estimates and their standard errors.
