Statistical Analysis 
Baseline characteristics are described in each group with median (interquartile range) for 
quantitative variables and number (percentage) for qualitative variables. 
In the primary endpoint analysis, a multiple imputation method was implemented for patients 
who could not be evaluated for the main endpoint except those who died from a neurological 
or unknown cause who were considered as having a CSDH recurrence.  The Multiple 
Imputation by Chained Equations (MICE) technique was used to replace missing values. 8 The 
variables used in the imputation phase were recurrence at 6 months, age, sex, unilateral or 
bilateral nature of the CSDH and use of anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy as well as the 
characteristics of CSDH at inclusion, namely type of CSDH (initial episode or recurrence), 
proven recent head trauma, Glasgow Coma Scale ( GCS) score, American Society of 
Anesthesiologists (ASA) score, focal neurological deficit, hemiparesis, phasic disorder, gait 
disorder, headache, maximum thickness of the CSDH, Park classification 9, type of surgery 
(burr hole, trephine cranio stomy), complications (infection, subdural empyema, epileptic
