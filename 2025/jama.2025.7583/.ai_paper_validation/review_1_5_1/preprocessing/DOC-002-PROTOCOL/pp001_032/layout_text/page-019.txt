4.3.2    Randomization


After signing an informed consent form, eligible patients will be randomized 1:1 between
embolization (experimental group) and usual management (control group) within 7 days of
evacuation of the cSDH. Randomization will be carried out with small permuted blocks of
random size and will be stratified according to center, whether the patient is being treated with
an anticoagulant or an antiplatelet medication (yes or no), and whether the cSDH is unilateral
or bilateral. It will be centralized via the e-crf.



5     PRACTICAL RESEARCH UNFOALDING



Before any examination or act related to the research, the investigator obtains the free,
informed and written consent of the person undergoing the research, or of his or her
legal representative where applicable.


Persons likely to undergo research mentioned in 1° of article L. 1121-1 of the French
Public Health Code are entitled to a preliminary medical examination appropriate to the
research.




5.1     Selection visit

The screening visit takes place during the initial post-operative hospitalization, immediately
after surgical evacuation of the cSDH. It will be carried out by a neurosurgeon or interventional
neuroradiologist. Inclusion and non-inclusion criteria will be verified.
In particular, the following will be checked:
- Patient's age
- Past medical history
- chronic alcoholism or liver disease
- allergy to iodinated contrast media (not to be confused with allergy to Betadine or seafood)
- Usual treatments, in particular long-term antiplatelet and anticoagulation medication. The
indication for resumption of these treatments after surgery will be evaluated.
