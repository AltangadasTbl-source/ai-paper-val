8     EVALUATION OF PERFORMANCE OR EFFECTIVENESS

8.1   Description of s performance or efficiency evaluation parameters

The primary endpoint is the rate of recurrence of cSDH at 6 months, defined as recurrence of
cSDH with midline shift greater than or equal to 5mm or symptomatic, including leading to
death, or the presence of a cSDH of more than 10mm maximum thickness on the 6-month
follow-up CT scan, or the need for neurosurgical re-intervention for recurrence of homolateral
cSDH during the study period, or the need for re-hospitalization for recurrence of homolateral
cSDH.


Secondary performance or efficacy endpoints are the proportion of dependent patients
(modified Rankin score greater than or equal to 4) at 1 and 6 months, mortality at 1 and 6
months, cumulative length of hospital stay at 6 months directly or indirectly related to cSDH,
and the rate of re-intervention for a recurrence of cSDH during the 6-month period.


8.2   Methods and timetable for measuring, collecting and analyzing a performance or
      efficiency evaluation parameters

Primary and secondary endpoints will be assessed at a neurosurgery consultation at 1 month,
and at an interventional neuroradiology consultation at 6 months, following a follow-up brain
CT scan. If an event occurred during the follow-up period, all hospitalization, consultation and
imaging reports will be collected.
