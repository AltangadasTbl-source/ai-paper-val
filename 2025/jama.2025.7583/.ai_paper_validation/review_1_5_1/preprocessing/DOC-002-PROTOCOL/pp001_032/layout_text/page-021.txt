A follow-up visit is scheduled at one month (T1). It can take place no earlier than 7 days before
the theoretical T1 date and no later than 15 days after this theoretical date. This visit is part of
the usual follow-up of patients operated for cSDH.
The following data will be collected in the e-crf :
- The occurrence of an adverse event related or unrelated to embolization
- The occurrence of a recurrence of CSDH defined by :
       - recurrence of a cSDH with midline shift greater than or equal to 5mm or symptomatic,
       - or the need for neurosurgical re-intervention due to recurrence of homolateral cSDH,
       - or the need for re-hospitalization for a recurrence of homolateral cSDH
- Modified Rankin Score (mRS)
- Cumulative length of hospitalization since randomization


5.4   End of research visit at 6 months (T2)

An end-of-research consultation will be scheduled 6 months after inclusion, with a follow-up
brain CT scan. This consultation can be carried out no earlier than 28 days before the calculated
theoretical date of T2 and no later than 28 days after the theoretical date of T2.


The following data will be collected in the e-crf :
- The occurrence of an adverse event related or unrelated to embolization
- The occurrence of a recurrence of the cSDH defined by :
       - recurrence of cSDH with midline shift greater than or equal to 5mm or symptomatic
       (during the 6-month follow-up period after randomization or on the 6-month follow-up
       CT scan),
       - or the presence of a cSDH of more than 10mm maximum thickness on the CT scan
       performed at 6 months,
       - or the need for neurosurgical re-intervention for recurrence of homolateral cSDH
       within 6 months of randomization,
       - or the need for re-hospitalization for recurrence of homolateral CSDH within 6 months
       of randomization,
- Modified Rankin Score (mRS)
- Cumulative duration of hospitalization since randomization (start and end dates of
hospitalization will be noted).
