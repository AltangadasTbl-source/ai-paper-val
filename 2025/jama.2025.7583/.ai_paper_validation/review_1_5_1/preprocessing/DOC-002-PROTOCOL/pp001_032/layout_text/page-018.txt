- Others
Minor complications :
- Superficial hematoma at puncture site
- Transient deterioration of renal function
- Transient neurological attack or asymptomatic arterial dissection
- Others


Secondary endpoints will be assessed at a neurosurgical consultation at 1 month (part of the
usual management of operated cSDH patients, with a particular focus on procedural
complications), and at 6 months with an interventional neuroradiologist following a follow-up
brain CT scan.




4.2     Description of research methodology

4.2.1    Experimental design


Multicenter, open-label, randomized controlled trial with two parallel groups in a 1:1 ratio to
test the superiority of middle meningeal artery embolization over standard management for the
prevention of postoperative recurrence of cSDH.


4.3     Description of measures taken to reduce and avoid bias

4.3.1    Subject identification


For the purposes of this research, subjects will be identified as follows:
center no. (3 numeric positions) - person selection order no. in center (4 numeric positions) -
initial surname - initial first name
This reference is unique and will be kept for the duration of the research.


A randomization number will be assigned at the time of randomization. It will be in the
following format: RXXX in accordance with the sponsor's procedures.
