- Patient's state of autonomy before cSDH by modified Rankin score (mRS)
- Renal function by measuring creatinine levels, usually available in the medical record
- The possibility of pregnancy for women of childbearing age, if necessary by a beta-HCG assay

For patients fulfilling all inclusion criteria and none of the non-inclusion criteria, the patient
and/or a family member or the trusted support person designated by the patient will be informed
of:
- The risk of recurrence of the operated cSDH.
- Potential consequences of cSDH recurrence.
- The possibility of performing MMA embolization, with calibrated particles, under local
anesthesia or conscious sedation, to prevent recurrence.
- Modalities and risks of embolization.
- The possibility of participating in the EMPROTECT randomized trial.

5.2

Inclusion visit (T0)

After a reflection period of no more than 24 hours following the screening visit, an inclusion
visit will be held to obtain the patient's signed informed consent. If consent is signed,
randomization will be carried out during the inclusion visit.
The inclusion visit can take place at the same time as the selection visit for patients who do not
wish a reflection period.
