ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

6.1

Inclusion criteria

Patients
- aged 18 or over
- Operated for a cSDH recurrence or operated for a first episode of cSDH if at least one of the
following recurrence risk factors is present:
- Chronic alcoholism : daily alcohol consumption > 30g/day
- Liver cirrhosis
- Antiplatelet therapy or effective anticoagulant therapy with a formal indication to resume
treatment after surgery
- Thrombocytopenia (platelet count < 100 G/L)
- cSDH surgery without use of external drain

- affiliated to a social security scheme (beneficiary or beneficiary's beneficiary) (excluding Aide
Médicale d'État)
- signed informed consent or written informed consent obtained from family/trusted support
person

6.2

Non-inclusion criteria

Patients :
- operated by craniotomy or twist-drill craniostomy
