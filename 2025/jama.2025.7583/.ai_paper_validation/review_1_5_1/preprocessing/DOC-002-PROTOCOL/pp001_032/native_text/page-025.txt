- beyond 7 days after initial cSDH surgery or cSDH recurrence surgery,
- dependent before the occurence of cSDH with a modified Rankin score ≥ 4,
- with an estimated life expectancy of less than 6 months,
- with chronic renal failure (clearance less than 30 ml/min),
- during pregnancy,
- with a history of allergy to iodinated contrast media,
- Contraindication to conscious sedation AND local anesthesia (e.g. agitation making it
impossible to perform the procedure under local anesthesia or conscious sedation),
- refusing to participate
- whose follow-up is deemed difficult (patients living abroad, socially isolated or in very
precarious situations),
- under guardianship or curatorship.

6.3

Recruitment procedures

Justification of recruitment capacity with regard to the number of subjects to be included ;
Patients will be recruited during hospitalization for cSDH surgery following hematoma
evacuation.

All participating centers have neurosurgery teams that routinely manage cSDHs. Participating
INR teams have expertise in the endovascular treatment of complex cerebrovascular
pathologies. Almost all the centers participating in the PDSES (Permanence des soins en
établissements de santé) for neurosurgery/INR/neuro-resuscitation in the Ile-de-France region,
and thus managing neurosurgical emergencies, have agreed to participate. This means that the
protocol covers almost the entire population of the Ile de France region. In addition, the
participation of several provincial teams will further increase the speed of inclusion and hence
the feasibility of the study. The participating centers totaled more than 1,000 cSDH surgeries
in 2017. Assuming an eligibility rate of 1/3 and an inclusion rate of 50% of eligible patients,
the expected speed of inclusion is around 150 patients/year, giving an approximate recruitment
time of 24 months.
