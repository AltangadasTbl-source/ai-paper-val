Cases will be forwarded to the adjudication committee in the event of :
- Recurrence as defined in the primary endpoint, according to the local investigator
- Hospitalization, whatever the reason
- Consultation outside M1 and M6 scheduled consultations
- Follow-up cerebral imaging in addition to the 6-month follow-up CT scan
- New surgery of any kind.

SPECIFIC RESEARCH COMMITTEES

9.1

Steering Committee

9.1.1

Committee members :

Dr Eimad Shotar (coordinating investigator), Pr Frédéric Clarençon (scientific lead), Dr Agnes
Dechartres (methodologist) as well as Mrs Anne Bissery (URC project manager) and a DRCI
project manager (representatives of the sponsor and the URC).
9.1.2

Missions :

Define the general organization of the research, coordinate information, initially determine the
methodology and monitor the research process.
Suggest actions to be taken during the course of the research, taking into account the
recommendations of the DSMB, where applicable. The DRCI sponsor remains the decisionmaker.
9.1.3

Practical organization :

Meetings scheduled before the start of the study and every 6 months for the duration of the
study

9.2

Adjudication committee

9.2.1

Members:

The adjudication committee will consist of 3 members, neurosurgeon(s) and interventional
neuroradiologist(s), all practicing in centers unrelated to the study.
9.2.2

Mission :

Blinded assessment of whether patients recurred as defined in the primary endpoint.
