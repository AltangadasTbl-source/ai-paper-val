3

OBJECTIVES

3.1

Main objective

To evaluate the efficacy of MMA embolization in reducing the risk of 6-month post-operative
recurrence of cSDH surgically evacuated by burr-hole or trephine compared with usual
management in patients at high risk of recurrence.
3.2

Secondary objectives

1/ Determine the impact of MMA embolization on the rate of disability and dependence at 1
month and 6 months compared with usual management.
2/ Determine the impact of MMA embolization on 1-month and 6-month mortality rates
compared with usual management.
3/ Determine the impact of MMA embolization on cumulative length of hospital stay, in relation
to cSDH, compared with usual management.
4/ Determine the impact of MMA embolization on the rate of re-intervention for homolateral
recurrence of cSDH
5/ Determine the safety and tolerance of the procedure.

3.3

Objectives of potential ancillary studies

1/ Identify the clinical and radiological risk factors most significantly associated with
postoperative recurrence of cSDH (in the control group).
2/ Describe the angiographic findings in patients with cSDH.
3/ Describe the angiographic anatomical variants of the middle meningeal artery.
4

RESEARCH DESIGN

4.1

Research evaluation criteria

4.1.1

Primary endpoint

CSDH recurrence rate, at 6 months, defined as :
- Reappearance of a homolateral cSDH with a midline shift≥5mm or a symptomatic homolateral
SDH, including leading to death
- Or, the presence of a homolateral cSDH > 10mm in maximal thickness on the 6 months control
head CT scan
