Stopping rules

6.4.1. Criteria and procedures for premature discontinuation of procedures performed in
connection with research or administration of the product

Different situations exist.

- In the event of randomization to the intervention arm, CT angiography of the supra-aortic
trunks and circle of Willis will be performed systematically. On the basis of this examination,
a small number of patients will be excluded from the embolization procedure in cases of floating
thrombus in the aortic arch or birth of the MMA from the ophthalmic artery with agenesis of
the foramen spinosum. These patients will remain in the study, will be followed up for up to 6
months and will also be included in the analysis according to the intention-to-treat principle.

- Temporary stop: the investigator must document the reason for the stop and its resumption in
the subject's source file and CRF.
- Premature termination, but subject remains in research until end of participation, investigator
must document reason for termination
- Premature termination and cessation of participation in research: the investigator must
document the reason(s).

6.4.2. Criteria and procedures for premature termination of a subject's participation in research:
- Any subject may stop participating in research at any time, for any reason.
- The investigator may temporarily or permanently discontinue a subject's participation in the
research for any reason that has an impact on the subject's safety or that would be in the subject's
best interest.
