          - Or, the need for neurosurgical re-intervention during the study period for recurrence
          of cSDH
          - Or, the need for re-hospitalization related to the recurrence of homolateral cSDH
          during the study period.


          •      Special circumstances


* Complications related to subdural hematoma and hematoma evacuation surgery:
- Surgical site infection, subdural empyema
- Epileptic seizures occurring prior to the MMA embolization procedure
- Complications of general anesthesia
- Cerebral hemorrhage, pericerebral hemorrhage, acute subdural hematoma
- Inhalation pneumonitis


* Other events :
- Hospitalization for medical or surgical treatment scheduled prior to research.
- Admission for social or administrative reasons.


          •   Adverse events likely to be related to treatments prescribed as part of care
              (e.g. corticosteroid therapy) during research follow-up.
These adverse reactions must be reported by the investigator to the regional pharmacovigilance
center.




          •   Adverse events occurring after the investigational medical device has been
              put into service
Adverse events occurring after the experimental medical device has been used will be reported
without delay during the study period.
