- Any errors, omissions or inadequacies in the instruction manual, operating instructions or
maintenance manual.


According to article R.1123-46 of the French Public Health Code and the notice to sponsors of
clinical trials involving medical devices and in vitro diagnostic medical devices (ANSM):
 • New fact
Any new data that could lead to a reassessment of the risk-benefit ratio of the research or the
product under investigation, to changes in the use of the product, in the conduct of the research,
or in the documents relating to the research, or to the suspension, interruption or modification
of the research protocol or similar research. For trials involving the first administration or use
of a health product in people with no medical condition: any serious adverse event.


Examples:
- Any clinically significant increase in the incidence of an expected serious adverse event;
- Suspected unexpected serious adverse events in participants who have completed the trial,
which are reported by the investigator to the sponsor, as well as any follow-up reports;
- Any new fact concerning the conduct of the clinical trial or the use of the medical device,
when this new fact is likely to affect the safety of the participants.
For example:
       - a serious adverse event likely to be linked to the trial's investigations and diagnostic
       procedures and which could alter the course of the trial,
       - a significant risk for the trial population, such as a lack of efficacy of the medical
       device used in the treatment of a life-threatening disease,
       - significant results from a completed preclinical study that may call into question the
       assessment of risk versus expected benefit (such as a biomechanics study),
       - early termination or temporary interruption for safety reasons of a trial conducted with
       the same medical device in another country,
       - an unexpected serious adverse event linked to a non-investigational health product
       required for the trial (e.g. challenge agents, rescue treatment),
- The recommendations of the DSMB, if any, if they are relevant to personal safety,
- Any unexpected serious adverse event transmitted to the sponsor by another sponsor of a
clinical trial involving the same medical device in a third country.
