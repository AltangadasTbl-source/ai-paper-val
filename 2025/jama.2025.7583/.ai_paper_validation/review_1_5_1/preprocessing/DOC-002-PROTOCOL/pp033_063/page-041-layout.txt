*Complications associated with the middle meningeal artery embolization procedure :
- Puncture-site complications: superficial hematoma with deglobulation (loss of 2 Hb points)
and/or requiring transfusion, retroperitoneal hematoma with or without deglobulation, arterial
pseudoaneurysm at puncture site requiring surgical treatment, femoral artery occlusion and/or
acute limb ischemia, puncture-site abscess.
- Degradation of renal function with a persistent drop in creatinine clearance of more than 10
points
- Severe allergic reaction to iodinated contrast media: anaphylactic shock, angioedema,
bronchospasm with desaturation, widespread skin rash.
- Severe allergic reaction to embolization product
- Asymptomatic occlusive or stenosing dissection of a cervico-encephalic artery
- Symptomatic dissection of a cervico-encephalic artery or one requiring salvage stenting
- Symptomatic intracranial cerebral emboli or those requiring mechanical thrombectomy
- Migration of embolic agent into the ophthalmic artery with visual acuity loss or visual field
amputation
- Post-radiation complications, in particular transient post-radiation alopecia or permanent
