In accordance with article L. 1122-1-1 of the French Public Health Code, no interventional
research involving the human body may be carried out on a person without his or her free and
informed consent, obtained in writing after the person has been provided with the information
specified in article L. 1122-1 of the same code.

A 24-hour reflection period is allowed between the time the person is informed and the time he
or she signs the consent form.

The person's free, informed and written consent is obtained by the principal investigator, or by
a physician representing him/her, before the person is included in the research. Consent is
obtained during the same hospitalization.

The information note and a copy of the consent form, dated and signed by the person undergoing
the research and by the investigator or the physician representing him or her, are given to the
person prior to participation in the research.

In addition, the investigator will specify in the patient's medical record the person's participation
in the research, the procedures for obtaining his/her consent or the consent of any other person
in the cases provided for in articles L. 1122-1-1 to L. 1122-2 of the CSP, and the procedures
for providing the information needed to obtain it. He/she keeps a copy of the consent form,
dated and signed.

Special case: If it is physically impossible for the person to give written consent, consent is
attested by a third party. This third party must be totally independent of the investigator and the
sponsor.

Special case: Mention of the possibility for the investigator to reserve certain information
related to the diagnosis, where applicable in accordance with paragraph 4 of article L1122-1.
