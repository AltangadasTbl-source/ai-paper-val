10 SAFETY ASSESSMENT - RISKS AND CONSTRAINTS ADDED BY RESEARCH
10.1 Definitions
According to article R.1123-46 of the French Public Health Code :
• Adverse event (AE)

Any harmful event occurring in a person undergoing research involving the human body,
whether or not the event is related to the research or the product to which the research relates.
• Adverse reaction to a medical device (MD) or in vitro diagnostic medical device
(IVDD)

Any harmful and undesired reaction to a medical device, or any incident that could have led to
such a reaction if appropriate action had not been taken, in a person undergoing research or in
the user of the medical device, or any effect related to a failure or alteration of an in vitro
diagnostic medical device and harmful to the health of a person undergoing research.
• Serious adverse event or reaction

Any adverse event or effect that results in death, endangers the life of the person undergoing
the research, requires hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization, causes significant or
lasting disability or incapacity, or results in a congenital anomaly or malformation, regardless
of the dose administered for a drug.
• Unexpected adverse reaction

Any adverse reaction whose nature, severity or course is not consistent with the information on
the products, procedures, practices and methods used in the research.
