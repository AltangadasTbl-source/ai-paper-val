10.2.4 Procedures and deadlines for notifying the sponsor

Initial notification of a SAE is recorded in a written report signed by the investigator, using a
research-specific SAE notification form (in the eCRF).

Each item in this document must be completed by the investigator to enable the sponsor to carry
out a relevant analysis.

Initial notification of a serious adverse event to the sponsor must be followed up promptly by
detailed written follow-up report(s) to monitor the progress of the case under vigilance or to
supplement the information.

Whenever possible, the investigator will forward any documents that may be useful to the
sponsor (medical reports, biological results, results of additional examinations, etc.). These
documents must be made anonymous. In addition, they must be completed with the following
information: research acronym, participant number and initials on all pages.

Any adverse event will be followed up until its complete resolution (stabilization at a level
deemed acceptable by the investigator, or return to the previous state), even if the participant
has withdrawn from the research.

Initial notification, SAE follow-up reports and any other documents will be sent to the sponsor,
represented by the Vigilance Sector, exclusively by e-mail (eig-vigilance.drc@aphp.fr). Please
note that the Vigilance Sector can be contacted by fax (01 44 84 17 99) only in the event of an
unsuccessful attempt to send SAE reports by e-mail (to avoid duplication).
