between the two groups attested at the 0.001 threshold, or for reasons of futility if the
conditional power is too low.
The research will end at the end of the follow-up of the last included patient.


12.5 Method for taking into account missing, unused or invalid data

The main analysis will be conducted on an intention-to-treat basis. All randomized subjects will
be analyzed (in the randomization group initially allocated to them). In the main analysis, a
multiple imputation method will be used for patients who cannot be evaluated for the primary
endpoint.



12.6 Manage changes to the initial strategy analysis plan.

Any modification of the initial analysis plan will be justified in the study report.


12.7 Population selection

Intention to treat (ITT)
The ITT population is defined as all patients randomized to the study, with each patient
remaining in the arm to which he or she was randomly assigned. The analysis of this population
constitutes the main analysis of the study.



13 CONTROL AND quality assurance

Each interventional research project involving humans undertaken by the AP-HP is classified
according to the anticipated risk incurred by the people taking part in the research, using the
classification of interventional research involving humans promoted by the AP-HP.


13.1 General organization

The sponsor must ensure the safety and respect of those who have agreed to take part in the
research. They must set up a quality assurance system to monitor the progress of the research
in the investigating centers.
