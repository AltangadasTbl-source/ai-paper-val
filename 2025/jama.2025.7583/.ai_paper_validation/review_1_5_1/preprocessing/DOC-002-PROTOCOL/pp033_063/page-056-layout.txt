During this inclusion period, the subject may not participate in any other interventional research
protocol involving the human body.
Subjects may, however, participate in other non-interventional research projects


14.3 Subject compensation

No compensation is provided.


14.4 Registration in the national file of persons undergoing research involving the person

No registration is planned as none of the situations described below apply.


In accordance with article L. 1121-16 of the French Public Health Code, registration is required:
       - for persons with no medical condition who voluntarily agree to undergo research;
       - for patients when the purpose of the research is unrelated to their medical condition;
       - in certain special cases at the request of the ethical review board ;

14.5 Site authorization



The research is carried out in health-care departments on people with a clinical condition for
which the departments are competent, and requires procedures normally performed as part of
their activities. It is therefore not necessary to obtain authorization for a specific research site.
