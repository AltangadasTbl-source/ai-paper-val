The investigator should specify whether the serious adverse event is the result of an incident

Any investigator who becomes aware of an incident must report it to the sponsor without delay.
He/she fills in the incident or risk of incident reporting form (see appendices 19.3 and 19.4) and

10.2.1 Serious adverse events requiring immediate notification by the investigator to
the sponsor

The investigator must notify the sponsor without delay, from the day he/she becomes aware of
them, of all serious adverse events occurring in the course of research mentioned in 1o of article
L.1121-1, with the exception of those listed in the protocol (see corresponding section) and,
where applicable, in the investigator's brochure as not requiring immediate notification (extract
from article R.1123-49 of the French Public Health Code).

A serious adverse event meets one of the following criteria:
1- Event leading to death,
2- A life-threatening event for the person undergoing research,
3- Event requiring hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization,
4- An event that causes significant or lasting disability or handicap,
5- An event that results in a congenital anomaly or malformation.
