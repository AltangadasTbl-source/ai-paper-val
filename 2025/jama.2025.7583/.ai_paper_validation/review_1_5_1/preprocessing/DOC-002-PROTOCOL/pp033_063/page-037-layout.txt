- neurological events,
- renal events,
- major complications at the puncture site,
- ophthalmological events,
- others.


10.2.2 Protocol specifics




10.2.2.1 Other events requiring immediate notification by the investigator to the sponsor

    •   Adverse events deemed “medically significant”



These adverse events must be notified to the sponsor by the investigator without delay from the
day he/she becomes aware of them, according to the same procedures and deadlines as for
serious adverse events (see above).


        •    In utero exposure
Any pregnancy occurring during the course of research, even if not associated with an adverse
event, must be notified to the sponsor by the investigator without delay from the day on which
he/she becomes aware of it.



10.2.2.1 Serious adverse events not requiring immediate notification by the investigator to the
            sponsor

These serious adverse events are only recorded in the eCRF. An extraction of these serious
adverse events from the observation book will be made once a year, when the annual safety
report is drawn up, and forwarded by the Clinical Research Unit to DRCI's Vigilance sector.


    •   Natural and usual course of the disease:

- Worsening of the pathology studied: recurrences of cSDH, with the exception of those with a
fatal outcome, defined by :
        - Recurrence of a cSDH greater than 10 mm or symptomatic during the study period
