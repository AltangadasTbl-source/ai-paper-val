15.7 Archiving:

Documents specific to research involving the human body that does not concern a health
product will be archived by the investigator and the sponsor for to be completed depending on
the type of data (the choice of duration depends on the type of research) after the end of the
research.

This indexed archive includes :
➢ A sealed envelope for the investigator containing a copy of all the information notes and
consent forms signed by all the people at the center who took part in the research;
➢ A sealed envelope for the sponsor containing a copy of all the information notes and
consent forms signed by all the people at the center who took part in the research;
➢ “Research” binders for the Investigator and Sponsor including (non-exhaustive list):
•

successive versions of the protocol (identified by version number and date), its
appendices

•

ANSM authorizations and ethical review board approvals

•

correspondence,

•

inclusion list or register,

•

research-specific appendices

•

the final research report.

➢ Data collection documents.
