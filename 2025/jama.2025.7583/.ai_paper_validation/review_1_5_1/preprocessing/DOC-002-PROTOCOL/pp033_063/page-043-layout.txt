- all serious adverse events likely to be linked to the implementation of the investigational
medical device being the object of the research,
- any incident leading to a serious adverse event.
NB: the sponsor declares any incident leading to the occurrence of a serious adverse event to
the ANSM and the ANSM's materiovigilance unit.


The sponsor reports any suspected serious unexpected adverse reaction (SUSAR) or serious
adverse event that may be related to the procedure for implementing the medical device
occurring in France and outside the national territory concerned, within the regulatory
deadlines, to the Agence nationale de sécurité des médicaments et des produits de santé
(ANSM):
- The initial report must be made without delay from the day on which the sponsor becomes
aware of it in the case of serious unexpected adverse reactions and serious adverse events which
may be related to the procedure for implementing the medical device and which have resulted
in death or are life-threatening, and within 15 days from the day on which the sponsor becomes
aware of it in the case of other serious unexpected adverse reactions and serious adverse events
which may be related to the procedure for implementing the medical device;
- In the event of a serious unexpected adverse reaction or serious adverse event that may be
related to the procedure for implementing the medical device, resulting in death or is life-
threatening, the relevant additional information is notified to the French National Agency for
the Safety of Medicines and Health Products (ANSM) within eight days.


- In other cases of serious unexpected adverse reactions and serious adverse events that may be
linked to the procedure used to implement the medical device, the relevant additional
information is sent to the Agence nationale de sécurité du médicament et des produits de santé
(ANSM) within a further fifteen days.


The sponsor informs all investigators concerned of any data that could have an adverse impact
on the safety of research subjects.
