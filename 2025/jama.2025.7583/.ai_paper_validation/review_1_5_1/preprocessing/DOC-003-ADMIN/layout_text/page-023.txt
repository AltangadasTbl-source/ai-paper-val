19.7    Addendum V: Radiological classification of cSDH




           19.7.1 Radiological classification of cSDH according to Nakaguchi et at.




According to Nakaguchi et al. Factors in the natural history of chronic subdural hematomas that influence
their postoperative recurrence. J Neurosurg. 2001;95:256-62. Upper Left: Homogeneous type. Upper
Right: Laminar type. Lower Left: Separated. Lower Right: Trabecular type.



           19.7.2 Radiological classification of HSDc according to Park et al.




According to Park H-R et al. Multiple Densities of the Chronic Subdural Hematoma in CT Scans. J.
Korean Neurosurg. Soc. 2013;54:38-41. Chronic SDH classification according to CT density. A:
Hypodensity (<25 HU). B: Homogeneous isodensity (25-35 HU). C: Layered type. D: Mixed type.
