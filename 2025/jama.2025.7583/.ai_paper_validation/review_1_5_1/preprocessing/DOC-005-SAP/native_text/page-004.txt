o Minor complications are defined as:
 Superficial hematoma at the puncture site
 Transient deterioration of renal function
 Transient neurological accident or asymptomatic arterial dissection
 Other minor complications

3.1 Flow chart
A CONSORT flow chart will be provided to describe the number of eligible patients and the number
of patients effectively included and randomized in total and in each of the two arms as well as
patient follow-up (number lost to follow-up per arm). It will also present the number of patients
analyzed in each group.
