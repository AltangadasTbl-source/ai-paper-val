## Provisional numeric/reporting relationships

### P1-N001 — Primary composite recurrence endpoint and threshold components

- **Source locations:** PDF p. 7 (synoptic summary); pp. 16–17 (section 4.1.1); p. 21 (T2 collection); p. 31 (section 8.1).
- **Printed evidence:** The endpoint is cSDH recurrence at 6 months. Components are a homolateral cSDH with midline shift `≥5 mm` or symptomatic cSDH (including death); cSDH `>10 mm` maximal thickness on the 6-month CT; repeat neurosurgery for homolateral recurrence; or new/re-hospital admission for homolateral recurrence. For bilateral cSDH, pp. 17 states that any unilateral or bilateral recurrence meets the primary endpoint.
- **Relationship/definition:** Composite binary recurrence rate at 6 months; four component routes and explicit imaging thresholds. Page 21 expresses the follow-up period as 6 months after randomization; p. 7 describes it as after index burr-hole surgery.
- **Main-paper merge key:** primary outcome / 6-month cSDH recurrence / MMA embolization versus usual care; retain the exact anchor (index surgery versus randomization) and component wording when matching.

### P1-N002 — Secondary endpoint set, time points, and operational definitions

- **Source locations:** pp. 7–8, 16–18, 21, 31.
- **Printed evidence:** Secondary outcomes are dependence, `mRS ≥4`, at 1 and 6 months; mortality at 1 and 6 months; cumulative cSDH-related hospital stay at 6 months; and neurosurgical re-intervention for homolateral recurrence during 6 months. For bilateral cSDH, any recurrence leading to surgery qualifies for the re-intervention endpoint (p. 17). p. 17 limits the hospital-stay measure to admissions related to initial cSDH, recurrence, or direct/indirect consequences and includes day hospitalization. Major renal complication is a persistent creatinine-clearance decrease of `>10 points`.
- **Relationship/definition:** Outcome measure labels, scales, denominators/populations, and time anchors required to distinguish a proportion/rate from duration or count.
- **Main-paper merge key:** secondary outcomes / mRS dependency / mortality / hospital length of stay / reoperation.

### P1-N003 — Study design, allocation, and analysis-population definition

- **Source locations:** pp. 8–10, 18–19, 26.
- **Printed evidence:** Multicenter open-label two-parallel-arm randomized trial; eligible participants randomized `1:1` to embolization or usual care in small permuted blocks of random size, centralized via eCRF. Stratification variables are center, anticoagulant/antiplatelet treatment (yes/no), and unilateral/bilateral cSDH. p. 26 states that intervention-arm patients excluded from embolization after CT angiography remain followed to 6 months and are included by the intention-to-treat principle.
- **Relationship/definition:** Randomization level is the patient; treatment contrast is MMA embolization versus usual care; ITT includes procedural non-treatment after post-randomization feasibility findings.
- **Main-paper merge key:** randomization / 1:1 allocation / stratification / intention-to-treat population.

### P1-N004 — Planned sample size, centers, and study duration

- **Source locations:** pp. 9–10, 22, 25–26.
- **Printed evidence:** `342` subjects, `12` planned centers, `24 months` inclusion, `6 months` participation/follow-up, and `30 months` total research duration. The recruitment table reports `14` subjects per center per year and `1.2 on average` per center per month.
- **Relationship/definition:** Planned—not observed—sample/recruitment quantities. The chronology specifies selection up to `7 days` after surgery and an intervention-arm embolization window D0–D7 after surgery.
- **Main-paper merge key:** planned versus randomized/analysed sample; number of centers; recruitment and follow-up period.

### P1-N005 — Recruitment-capacity assumptions

- **Source locations:** p. 25 and p. 26.
- **Printed evidence:** Participating centers totaled `more than 1,000` cSDH surgeries in 2017. The protocol assumes eligibility `1/3`, inclusion `50%` of eligible patients, an expected `around 150 patients/year`, and approximate `24 months` recruitment time; p. 26 displays the planned recruitment quantities in P1-N004.
- **Relationship/definition:** Forecast assumptions supporting planned recruitment, not a reported trial result. The approximate figures and the `more than` qualifier must be retained if used in any arithmetic comparison.
- **Main-paper merge key:** recruitment feasibility / planned enrollment.

### P1-N006 — Eligibility population and numerical thresholds

- **Source locations:** pp. 8, 13, 19–20, 24–25.
- **Printed evidence:** Inclusion requires age `≥18 years`, surgery for recurrence or first cSDH with at least one listed risk factor; explicit factors include daily alcohol `>30 g/day` and platelet count `<100 G/L`. Non-inclusion includes more than `7 days` after index surgery, baseline `mRS ≥4`, life expectancy `<6 months`, and creatinine clearance `<30 mL/min`.
- **Relationship/definition:** Eligibility thresholds determine the protocol population and therefore comparability of any main-paper denominator or analysis set. P. 13 says approximately one-third of surgically managed patients have at least one specified characteristic.
- **Main-paper merge key:** eligibility / high recurrence risk / baseline dependency / timing / renal function.

### P1-N007 — Follow-up visit windows and endpoint collection anchors

- **Source locations:** pp. 20–23 and 31–32.
- **Printed evidence:** T1 is 1 month, no earlier than `7 days before` and no later than `15 days after` theoretical T1. T2 is 6 months, with a window `28 days before` to `28 days after` theoretical T2. At T1, recurrence, mRS, and cumulative hospitalization since randomization are collected; T2 adds 6-month CT and the `>10 mm` component. P. 22 says each patient participates for `6 months`.
- **Relationship/definition:** Assessment-time rules and collection windows; anchors distinguish the planned nominal outcome time point from allowable visit dates.
- **Main-paper merge key:** 1-month outcome / 6-month outcome / CT follow-up / visit window.

### P1-N008 — Attrition assumption and nonreplacement rule

- **Source locations:** pp. 9–10 and 27.
- **Printed evidence:** The sample-size narrative includes a `20%` lost-to-follow-up rate. P. 27 says patients lost to follow-up or withdrawing consent will not be replaced and that a `20%` loss rate is included in the number-required calculation.
- **Relationship/definition:** Planned loss-to-follow-up allowance; not an observed loss rate. This is a denominator/planning definition relevant when comparing recruited, follow-up, and analysis counts.
- **Main-paper merge key:** attrition / lost to follow-up / sample-size allowance.

### P1-N009 — Intervention/device parameters and bilateral-treatment rule

- **Source locations:** pp. 8–9, 14–15, 28–30.
- **Printed evidence:** Intervention is MMA embolization with calibrated `300–500 µm` EmboSphere particles after CTA, within `7 days` of surgical evacuation. Femoral procedures have recommended bed rest of `12 to 24 hours`. For eligible bilateral cSDH patients randomized to embolization, both right and left sides must be embolized.
- **Relationship/definition:** Intervention identity, unit/scale (particle diameter), timing, and the bilateral handling rule needed to interpret treatment exposure and patient-level versus side-level results.
- **Main-paper merge key:** intervention definition / particle embolization / CTA / bilateral cSDH.

### P1-N010 — Comparator and concomitant-treatment definition

- **Source locations:** pp. 8–10 and 30–31.
- **Printed evidence:** Comparator is standard/usual care, including nursing care, symptomatic treatment, corticosteroids at neurosurgical discretion, monitoring/imaging if needed, and rehabilitation if needed. Heparin during embolization is at operator discretion; the study imposes no time limit on resumption of anticoagulant or antiplatelet treatment.
- **Relationship/definition:** Comparator/treatment-exposure label and nonfixed cointerventions. It is not an effect estimate.
- **Main-paper merge key:** usual care / control group / concomitant treatment.

### P1-N011 — Background disease-frequency and outcome estimates

- **Source locations:** pp. 7–8, 11–12.
- **Printed evidence:** The synoptic summary estimates postoperative recurrence at `10%–20%`. P. 11 reports annual cSDH incidence `5–14 per 100,000`, `58 per 100,000 per year` over age 65, mortality `4%`, morbidity `10%`, recurrence around `10%–20%`, mostly within `3–6 months`. P. 12 describes a `72`-case nonrandomized series.
- **Relationship/definition:** Background literature/contextual estimates, not EMPROTECT result data; preserve the source’s qualifiers and literature framing.
- **Main-paper merge key:** none required for outcome-result matching; potential contextual comparator only.

### P1-N012 — Safety/stopping quantities and reporting time

- **Source locations:** pp. 17–18 and 27–28.
- **Printed evidence:** Major complications include a persistent creatinine-clearance fall `>10 points`; p. 27 refers to serious adverse-event imbalance between `2` study arms. Upon safety-related premature termination, the sponsor sends the decision/justification within `15 days` (p. 28).
- **Relationship/definition:** Safety-outcome definition and administrative time rule; no observed safety count is reported in these pages.
- **Main-paper merge key:** adverse events / serious complications / safety analysis.

### P1-N013 — Adjudication process quantities

- **Source locations:** pp. 17, 31–32.
- **Printed evidence:** Potential recurrences are blindly assessed from anonymized source materials. Cases are sent for locally identified primary-endpoint recurrence, any hospitalization, unscheduled consultation, additional cerebral imaging, or new surgery. The adjudication committee consists of `3` independent neurosurgeon(s)/interventional neuroradiologist(s); steering meetings are before study start and every `6 months`.
- **Relationship/definition:** Endpoint adjudication/ascertainment method, triggers, and committee size; no adjudicated result count is reported.
- **Main-paper merge key:** blinded adjudication / primary outcome ascertainment.

## Provisional inferential-statistical relationships

### P1-S001 — Planned primary effect target and sample-size operating assumptions

- **Source locations:** pp. 9–10.
- **Printed evidence:** The design aims to reduce recurrence from `15%` (control) to `5%` (embolized), with `80%` power, overall two-sided alpha `5%`, and `20%` lost to follow-up; required sample is `342`.
- **Statistical definition:** Planned superiority design parameters, not an observed treatment effect. The absolute planned risk difference is 10 percentage points by subtraction of printed targets, but this is a diagnostic restatement rather than a reported analysis result.
- **Main-paper merge key:** primary-outcome effect / sample-size calculation / planned control and treatment risks.

### P1-S002 — Group-sequential interim/final alpha specification

- **Source locations:** pp. 9–10 and 27.
- **Printed evidence:** Two sequential tests follow Lan-DeMets with an O’Brien-Fleming-type alpha spending function. Interim analysis occurs after inclusion and end of follow-up of `129` patients, information fraction `37.5%`, at nominal `alpha=0.001`; final analysis threshold is `alpha=0.05`. P. 27 states a possible stopping condition as significant difference `p<0.001` at planned interim analysis after `129` subjects.
- **Statistical definition:** Planned group-sequential testing/stopping framework. The different displayed notation (`alpha=0.001` / `p<0.001`) is preserved as wording from the protocol; this map does not infer test equivalence or assess consistency.
- **Main-paper merge key:** interim analysis / alpha spending / 129 participants / stopping rule.

### P1-S003 — Planned primary analysis model

- **Source locations:** p. 10.
- **Printed evidence:** The primary endpoint will be analyzed in the intention-to-treat population using a mixed logistic regression model adjusted for unilateral/bilateral cSDH and anticoagulant/anti-aggregant treatment as fixed stratification-factor effects, with random center effect.
- **Statistical definition:** Patient-level binary primary endpoint; mixed logistic regression; fixed treatment-stratification variables and random center effect. Effect-measure presentation is not specified in this page.
- **Main-paper merge key:** primary analysis / mixed logistic regression / adjusted model / ITT.

### P1-S004 — Allocation method and stratification definition

- **Source locations:** pp. 9 and 19.
- **Printed evidence:** Centralized randomization uses small permuted blocks of random size and stratifies by center, anticoagulant/antiplatelet status (yes/no), and unilateral/bilateral cSDH.
- **Statistical definition:** Randomization—not analysis—specification. It provides the precise strata relevant to matching any adjusted or subgroup result.
- **Main-paper merge key:** randomization strata / adjusted covariates.

### P1-S005 — Endpoint scale/type distinctions relevant to analysis

- **Source locations:** pp. 16–18 and 31.
- **Printed evidence:** Primary outcome is a recurrence *rate* under a composite event definition. Secondary outcomes include a *proportion* of dependent participants (`mRS ≥4`), mortality *rates*, cumulative hospital-stay *duration*, and re-intervention *rate*.
- **Statistical definition:** This record preserves the source’s distinct labels and scales; it does not assume that all are analyzed by the same model or denominator.
- **Main-paper merge key:** outcome measure label / rate-versus-proportion-versus-duration.
