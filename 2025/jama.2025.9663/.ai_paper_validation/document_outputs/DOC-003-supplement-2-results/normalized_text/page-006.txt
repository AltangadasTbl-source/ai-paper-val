Outcomes

Primary clinical outcome

90-day mortality

The primary clinical outcome was                                  90-day mortality, defined as death due to any cause by 90
days following randomization.

Date of death was collected from linked death registration records, the CMP or directly from
the sites. Where data were discrepant between sources, quality assurance checks were
conducted with sites and the site data considered paramount. Where sites were unable to
validate discrepancies in linked records, deaths identified by either linked data source were
considered true, with death registrations considered paramount for discrepant dates of
death.

Secondary clinical outcomes

Mortality at discharge from the ICU and acute                                                         hospital

Patient outcomes                       (alive/dead) at discharge from ICU and acute hospital  were recorded as
part of routine CMP data collection and were                                                                               collected from linked CMP records,
supplemented by data collected directly from sites where necessary. Mortality at discharge
from the                                                                   ICU was defined as death due to any cause before discharge to any location
providing a level of care less than Level 2 (high dependency care). Mortality at discharge
from acute hospital was            defined as death due to any cause before discharge from acute
hospital.            Patients transferred from the original acute hospital to another acute hospital were
followed up until they had left                                   acute hospital. ICU and hospital mortality were censored at 90
days following randomization.

Mortality at 60 days and one year was            defined as death due to any cause by 60 days and

one                                                                  year follow-up and were                                                                                                                                                  excluded from analysis of this outcome.
