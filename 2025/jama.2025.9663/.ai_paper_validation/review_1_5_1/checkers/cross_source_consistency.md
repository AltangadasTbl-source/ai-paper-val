# Cross-Source Quantitative Consistency Check

## Scope and method

This fresh cross-source check covers the canonical numeric/reporting inventory `N001`--`N033` and inferential-statistical inventory `S001`--`S034`. It compared repeated material only after matching population, time horizon, treatment contrast, analysis set, model, effect measure, units, reference group, and displayed precision. Direct PDFs are the authority; mapping/extraction artifacts were used as locators. DOC-002 is a versioned prospective protocol/SAP source, so its historical amendments and planned quantities were not treated as contradictions of observed results without a same-version, same-definition comparator.

Sources checked: [DOC-001 main article](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf), [DOC-002 protocol/SAP supplement](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf), and [DOC-003 results supplement](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf). ## Qualifying observations for later candidate registration

### Observation 1 — Main-paper arterial-oxygenation sentence carries `mm Hg` into the SpO2 value

- **Category if registered:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact linked locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p. 6](../../../jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf#page=6), Oxygen Exposure paragraph; [DOC-003 PDF p. 21](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=21), eTable 5, SpO2 and PaO2 rows.
- **Printed values:** DOC-001 prints conservative values “median SpO2 of 93.3% (2.8%) and the median PaO2 of 71.5 (13.9) mm Hg” and usual values “95.1% (2.4%) mm Hg and 79.5 (17.9) mm Hg, respectively.” DOC-003 eTable 5 labels the matched values separately: SpO2 `93.3 (2.8)` versus `95.1 (2.4)` with percent scale, and PaO2 `71.5 (13.9)` versus `79.5 (17.9)` with mm Hg scale.
- **Comparison logic:** “Respectively” pairs the first usual value with SpO2 and the second with PaO2. The unit string `mm Hg` follows the usual-group SpO2 parenthesis even though the same sentence gives SpO2 in percent and the matched eTable 5 has `mm Hg` only for PaO2. This is a direct printed unit-placement mismatch, not an inference from clinical expectation.
- **Supported alternative:** the values and ordering agree exactly with eTable 5, so the source supports a unit carryover/typesetting explanation rather than a changed oxygenation value.
- **Human verification steps:** visually inspect the typeset DOC-001 p.6 sentence and the published source file if available; confirm whether `mm Hg` was intended to modify only `79.5 (17.9)` or is attached to both usual-group values; verify whether a corrected article version exists locally before editing downstream extraction.

### Observation 2 — DOC-003 contents entries for eTables 1-4 identify different tables than the numbered tables in the same PDF

- **Category if registered:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency (document/table identity).
- **Exact linked locations:** [DOC-003 PDF p. 1](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=1), contents; [DOC-003 PDF pp. 15-19](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=15) (actual eTable 1), [p.17](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=17) (eTable 2), [p.18](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=18) (eTable 3), [p.19](../../../joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf#page=19) (eTable 4).
- **Printed values/labels:** contents p.1 labels eTable 1 “Results of quality assessment per study,” eTable 2 “Diagnostic performance of serological tests – test combinations,” eTable 3 “Patients randomized by site,” and eTable 4 “Additional patient characteristics.” The printed tables are eTable 1 “Patients randomized by site,” eTable 2 “Additional patient characteristics,” eTable 3 “Representativeness of patients recruited to the UK-ROX trial,” and eTable 4 “Patient baseline characteristics by data collection group.”
- **Comparison logic:** a contents entry’s table number and title should identify the same numbered table in that PDF. eTables 5-10 do match their contents entries, isolating the mismatch to eTables 1-4. This is one common document-identity issue, not four separate observations.
- **Supported alternative:** the first two contents titles appear unrelated to UK-ROX and the p.1 eTable 3/4 topics align with actual eTable 1/2 after a two-number shift. The supplied PDF therefore supports an uncorrected/template contents-page explanation, but does not establish the production mechanism.
- **Human verification steps:** compare the author-approved supplement contents list and its publication-production version, identify the intended eTable 1-4 entries, and confirm whether the p.1 contents page should be replaced while retaining the actual tables unchanged.

### Observation 3 — SAP separation/adherence definition contains an unresolved internal cross-reference

- **Category if registered:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency (internal quantitative-definition reference).
- **Exact linked location:** [DOC-002 PDF p. 118](../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=118), SAP p.8, immediately following Table 1/separation-adherence text.
- **Printed text:** “See Section 3.2 and Error! Reference source not found. for further details about assessment of separation and treatment adherence.”
- **Comparison logic:** the text expressly points to a second reference but replaces its target with an error string. It follows numerical separation thresholds and the FiO2/SpO2 adherence rule, so the reader cannot identify the promised fuller definition from the printed document.
- **Supported alternative:** `Section 3.2` may supply some relevant exposure description, but the printed sentence unambiguously asserts another reference whose identity is absent. The supplied package does not establish which section was intended.
- **Human verification steps:** inspect the SAP source/Word field or final approved SAP version, identify the missing cross-reference target, and determine whether it should be restored or the sentence revised. Do not infer a target merely from nearby section numbering.

## Exclusions and limitations

- No observation was created solely from a display-zero P value; none was used as a candidate basis.
- Graphical eFigure values without printed tabular precision were not digitized or compared as exact numbers.
- Prospective protocol/SAP amendments, sample-size scenarios, and analysis-plan evolution were retained as version constraints, not treated as observed-result conflicts.
- This checker records observations only. It assigns no candidate IDs, severity, validity, disposition, or correction.
