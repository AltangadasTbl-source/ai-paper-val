## Quantitative and reporting relationships

### D2A-N001 — Registry protocol identity, intended enrolment, and primary outcome

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 7 (printed protocol p. 6), registry summary.
- **Printed values/definitions:** ISRCTN13384956; registration date 8 December 2020; anticipated first enrolment 1 February 2021; target sample size **16,500**; intervention is conservative oxygen therapy versus usual oxygen therapy; primary outcome is **90-day all-cause mortality**.
- **Matching key:** original-protocol target sample size / intervention / primary endpoint / 90-day time point. Do not compare the planned 16,500 directly with a final analysed denominator without version/status qualification.

### D2A-N002 — Registry eligibility definitions

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 7 (printed protocol p. 6).
- **Printed values/definitions:** adults aged **>=18 years**; receiving invasive mechanical ventilation after an unplanned ICU admission or ventilation started in ICU; receiving supplemental oxygen with FiO2 **>0.21** at enrolment. Exclusions include previous UK-ROX randomisation in the last **90 days** and current extracorporeal membrane oxygenation.
- **Matching key:** population and eligibility denominator; age, FiO2 threshold, ICU/ventilation setting, and prior-randomisation washout.

### D2A-N003 — Registry secondary and economic outcome time points

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 8 (printed protocol p. 7).
- **Printed values/definitions:** hospital mortality censored at **90 days**; mortality at ICU discharge, **60 days**, and **1 year**; ICU/acute hospital stay censored at **90 days**; EQ-5D-5L HrQoL at **90 days**; incremental costs, QALYs, and net monetary benefit at **90 days**; resource use/costs at **90 days** and estimated lifetime incremental cost-effectiveness.
- **Matching key:** outcome type plus exact time point/censoring rule. Hospital mortality and 90-day all-cause mortality are distinct outcomes.

### D2A-N004 — Trial design and registry status

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 8 (printed protocol p. 7).
- **Printed values/definitions:** interventional, large-scale multicentre data-enabled registry-embedded RCT with internal pilot and integrated economic evaluation; allocation randomised; blinding cannot be done; primary purpose prevention.
- **Matching key:** original protocol’s design and allocation descriptor, not a result.

### D2A-N005 — Original background evidence quantities

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 12-13 (printed pp. 11-12).
- **Printed values/definitions:** approximately **184,000** UK adult ICU admissions/year; over **30% (55,000)** receive advanced respiratory support/MV. Prior ICU-ROX: **1,000** MV patients, conservative SpO2 **91%-96%** versus usual oxygen, ventilator-free days through day **28**. Four ICU RCTs totalled **1,983** patients and gave mortality RR **0.91 (95% CI, 0.75-1.09)**. ARDS study n=**205**, no difference in 28-day survival. CMP: **337,312** ICU admissions between 1 April 2017 and 31 March 2019; **96,028 (29%)** received MV; **34%** died before hospital discharge and anticipated **37%** by 90 days; COVID-19 ICU patients receiving MV **72%**. Meta-analysis values cited as RR **1.21 (95% CI, 1.03-1.43)** and OR **1.22 (95% CI, 1.12-1.33)**.
- **Matching key:** cited historical/context evidence only. These quantities are not UK-ROX final results.

### D2A-N006 — Original Mega-ROX harmonisation and planned effect size

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 14 (printed p. 13).
- **Printed values/definitions:** planned UK-ROX contribution **16,500** patients, described as **41%** of intended Mega-ROX patients; UK-ROX powered at **90%** as standalone NHS trial for absolute risk reduction **2.5%** in 90-day mortality. Pilot planned **60** participants across two sites; **34** recruited before stopping for time constraints; conservative target SpO2 **88%-92%**, usual-care control SpO2 **>=96%**. Only **15%** anticipated to require additional primary data collection.
- **Matching key:** original protocol power/effect-size assumptions and pilot history; distinguish from final protocol/SAP assumptions and observed event rates.

### D2A-N007 — Aim, outcome hierarchy, and economic definitions

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 14-15 (printed pp. 13-14).
- **Printed values/definitions:** population is non-elective ventilated adults receiving supplemental oxygen in ICU; conservative versus usual oxygen comparison; primary clinical outcome 90-day all-cause mortality. Primary economic outcomes are incremental costs, QALYs and net monetary benefit at 90 days. Secondary clinical outcomes include ICU/hospital mortality censored at 90 days, mortality at 60 days and one year, ICU/acute hospital length of stay censored at 90 days, and HrQoL at 90 days. Primary economic endpoint and secondary economic endpoints are explicitly distinguished.
- **Matching key:** endpoint hierarchy, endpoint label, and time point.

### D2A-N008 — Sites and randomisation population

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 15 (printed p. 14), pp. 16-17 (printed pp. 15-16).
- **Printed values/definitions:** **100** adult NHS ICUs across England, Wales and Northern Ireland planned. Inclusion: aged **>=18**, invasive MV in ICU after unplanned ICU admission or initiated in ICU, and supplemental oxygen at enrolment. Exclusion: prior randomisation within **90 days**, current ECMO, or treating clinician considers one arm indicated/contraindicated. Randomisation is **1:1** to conservative versus usual oxygen; recruitment route may use a 24-hour/week remote randomisation service.
- **Matching key:** trial population, site denominator, allocation ratio, and prior-randomisation exclusion.

### D2A-N009 — Consent and timing rules

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 17-20 (printed pp. 16-19).
- **Printed values/definitions:** eligible patients can be randomised without prospective consent if capacity is absent; consent to receive assigned treatment sought as soon as possible and no later than **12 hours** after confirming eligibility. If deferred-consent approach is used, first approach anticipated within **24-48 hours** of regaining capacity. When a patient is discharged, telephone contact is attempted within **14 working days**; the protocol specifies up to **three** telephone-contact attempts before postal contact. If re-admitted to ICU before consent procedures complete, research team contacts receiving hospital to hand over consent procedures.
- **Matching key:** consent timing is an operational definition, not an analysis endpoint; it can qualify the status of an enrolled/randomised population.

### D2A-N010 — Intervention exposure and oxygen target definitions

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 20-22 (printed pp. 19-21).
- **Printed values/definitions:** all oxygen must be administered to maintain SpO2 **>=88%**; FiO2 may not be raised above **0.30** solely to maintain SpO2 **>=88%**; maximum SpO2 alarm **96%**. For usual care, upper SpO2 alarm must not be used. The intervention continues until ICU discharge or **90 days** after randomisation, whichever is sooner; recommence if readmitted to ICU within **90 days**. A need for ECMO or hyperbaric oxygen ends the intervention.
- **Matching key:** arm-specific SpO2/FIO2/alarm rules and 90-day exposure window; do not confuse a saturation target with a delivered oxygen concentration or an outcome rate.

### D2A-N011 — Adverse-event and serious-adverse-event definitions

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 22-25 (printed pp. 21-24).
- **Printed values/definitions:** AE is any untoward medical occurrence; SAE includes death, life-threatening event, inpatient/prolonged hospitalisation, persistent/significant disability/incapacity, or congenital anomaly/birth defect. An event is assessed as related when plausibly/probably/definitely related to trial treatment and unexpected. SUSAR definition requires suspected relatedness plus unexpectedness. SUSAR reporting is within **24 hours** of awareness; certain expedited reports to REC are within **15 calendar days**. Events between randomisation and 90 days after randomisation (or ICU discharge, if sooner) follow the protocol’s reporting scope.
- **Matching key:** safety event type, relationship/expectedness, and reporting window. This is an original protocol definition, not a final AE count.

### D2A-N012 — Original sample-size calculation inputs

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 26 (printed p. 25), section 6.1.
- **Printed values/definitions:** maximum target **16,500** patients; assumed baseline primary-outcome event rate **34%**; detectable absolute event-rate difference **2.5%** (**34%** to **31.5%**); two-sided alpha **0.05**; **90%** power; allowance for up to **5%** whose trial-wide data may be unavailable from routine sources.
- **Matching key:** original calculation assumptions only. Required reconciliation rule for a later comparison: same endpoint (90-day all-cause mortality), same assumed control event rate, same effect metric (absolute risk difference), alpha, power, and missing-data allowance.

### D2A-N013 — Original pilot and recruitment-fidelity definitions

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 26 (printed p. 25), sections 6.2-6.4.
- **Printed values/definitions:** internal pilot prospectively assesses first **10** patients per site for data quality/early safety/informing internal pilot; retrospective chart abstraction in first **10** per site for consent/eligibility/CRF measures; survivors followed for **90 days**. Recruitment window table: screening months **0-12**; recruitment months **20-60**; maximum recruitment rate **40-50 patients/site/month**; minimum **>=40/site/month**; recruitment-time rules include **<4** months, **4-12** months, and **>=12** months as printed in the table.
- **Matching key:** original recruitment plan and pilot process, not observed final recruitment.

## Statistical relationship records

### D2A-S001 — Original primary sample-size inferential design

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 26 (printed p. 25).
- **Definition:** two-arm individually randomised design; primary endpoint 90-day all-cause mortality; baseline risk 34%; 2.5-percentage-point absolute risk reduction (34% to 31.5%); two-sided alpha 0.05; 90% power; n=16,500; up to 5% routine-data unavailability allowance.
- **Status:** protocol assumption, **not** a final-trial estimate, confidence interval, or P value.

### D2A-S002 — Cited background inferential estimates

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF pp. 12-13 (printed pp. 11-12).
- **Definition:** cited meta-analytic mortality RR 0.91 (95% CI, 0.75-1.09) from four RCTs, and cited hyperoxia associations RR 1.21 (95% CI, 1.03-1.43) and OR 1.22 (95% CI, 1.12-1.33). The protocol presents these as external/background evidence.
- **Status:** not UK-ROX trial results; retain measure labels (RR versus OR), CI level, and source status if compared elsewhere.

### D2A-S003 — Pilot allocation and saturation-target contrast

- **Location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 14 (printed p. 13).
- **Definition:** feasibility RCT planned n=60/two sites, actual n=34 before stop; randomised conservative SpO2 88%-92% versus usual control SpO2 >=96%. The text reports no statistically significant difference in ventilator-free days in the cited ICU-ROX comparison but provides no numerical test statistic/P value on these pages.
- **Status:** historical pilot/background comparison, not final UK-ROX analysis.
