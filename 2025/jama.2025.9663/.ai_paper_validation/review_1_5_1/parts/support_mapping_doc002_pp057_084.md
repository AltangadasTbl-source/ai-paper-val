## Numeric/reporting relationships

### D2C-N001 — allocation, intervention and time window

- **Source:** PDF p. 57 (printed p. 16), section 3.4.1; PDF pp. 60-62 (printed pp. 19-21), sections 3.6.1-3.6.3.
- **Printed definition:** eligible patients are randomised **1:1** to conservative oxygen therapy versus usual oxygen therapy. Conservative oxygen target is SpO2 **90 (±2)%**; alarms prevent SpO2 below **88%** and above **92%**. Intervention continues until ICU discharge or **90 days** after randomisation, whichever is sooner; it recommences on ICU readmission within 90 days.
- **Matching key:** intervention arm / target / randomisation / 90-day treatment window.

### D2C-N002 — capacity and consent timing

- **Source:** PDF pp. 57-60 (printed pp. 16-19), sections 3.4.2-3.4.4.
- **Printed values:** randomisation occurs as soon as possible and no later than **12 hours** after eligibility; deferred-consent first approach anticipated within **24-48 hours** of regained capacity; Personal Consultee approach ideally within **24-48 hours** of randomisation; at least **three** telephone attempts before patient notification by post; minimum pseudonymised data after refusal/withdrawal includes survival status at **90 days** after randomisation only.

### D2C-N003 — SAE surveillance windows and deadlines

- **Source:** PDF pp. 62-65 (printed pp. 21-24), sections 3.7.1-3.7.4 and Figure 1.
- **Printed values:** specified expected SAEs recorded from randomisation through ICU discharge or **90 days**, whichever first; SAE notification to ICNARC CTU within **24 hours**; related unexpected SAE report to REC within **15 calendar days**; all other adverse events recorded from randomisation through **90 days** or ICU discharge, whichever first.

### D2C-N004 — primary versus enhanced collection denominator

- **Source:** PDF p. 66 (printed p. 25), section 3.8; PDF p. 67 (printed p. 26), Table 1.
- **Printed values:** basic primary collection: **n=14,000**; enhanced collection: **2,500 (15%)**; total stated data fields from routine sources: **85%**. Table 1 explicitly labels basic **14,000 / 16,500** and enhanced **2,500 / 16,500**.
- **Matching key:** data-collection sampling frame / planned total sample size.
- **Arithmetic note:** 14,000 + 2,500 = 16,500. 2,500/16,500 = 15.15%, which is compatible with the displayed whole-percent “15%” after rounding; this is not a candidate.

### D2C-N005 — enhanced data elements and collection schedule

- **Source:** PDF pp. 66-67 (printed pp. 25-26), sections 3.8.3-3.8.5 and Table 1.
- **Printed definition:** first **10** patients per site are prospectively collected; remaining enhanced cases are retrospectively sampled across sites, arms, and trial course. Enhanced data include SpO2, FiO2, PaO2, SaO2, adherence, contact details and SAE; enhanced follow-up includes EQ-5D-5L/HrQoL and health service/resource use at **90 days**. Both collection levels include mortality and linked health-services/resource-use data.

### D2C-N006 — follow-up questionnaire / SWAT

- **Source:** PDF p. 68 (printed p. 27), sections 3.9-3.9.1.
- **Printed values:** follow-up at **90 days**; questionnaire designed for no more than **15 minutes**; nonresponders telephoned **three weeks** after posting. Thank-you-card SWAT allocation is **1:1**; outcomes are questionnaire completion proportion, response time, and returned-questionnaire completeness.

### D2C-N007 — internal-pilot progression thresholds

- **Source:** PDF p. 69 (printed p. 28), Table 2 and section 3.10.1.
- **Printed values:** pilot runs months **10-15**, analysis month **17**. Sites open: green **>50**, amber **20-50**, red **<20**. Recruitment per site/month: green **≥75%** anticipated, amber **40-75%**, red **<40%**. Separation: green SpO2 **≥3% OR FiO2 ≥0.1**; amber (SpO2 **1.5-3% AND FiO2 <0.1**) OR (SpO2 **<3% AND FiO2 0.05-0.09**); red SpO2 **<1.5% AND FiO2 <0.05**. Adherence: green **≥75%** cases, amber **50-75%**, red **<50%**.

### D2C-N008 — outcome definitions

- **Source:** PDF p. 69 (printed p. 28), section 3.10.2; PDF p. 82 (printed p. 41), NSA18 version history.
- **Printed definitions:** clinical primary outcome is **90-day all-cause mortality**. Economic primary outcomes are incremental costs, QALYs and net monetary benefit at 90 days. Secondary outcomes include ICU/hospital mortality censored at 90 days; mortality at **60 days** and **one year**; ICU/acute-hospital duration censored at 90 days; days alive and free from organ support at **30 days** (ordinal, death worst outcome); and EQ-5D-5L HrQoL at 90 days. NSA18 v1.8 states the 30-day DAWOS outcome was added and does not change existing outcomes.

### D2C-N009 — sample-size planning inputs

- **Source:** PDF p. 70 (printed p. 29), section 4.1.
- **Printed values:** CMP N=**96,028** (April 2017-March 2019); Risk II N=**82,075** (April 2014-March 2016); assumed 90-day mortality **37%**; target absolute risk reduction **2.5%**, corresponding stated relative risk reduction **6.8%**, mortality **37% to 34.5%**; **90%** power requires **15,444** patients; allowance **6%** for refusal/withdrawal/loss leads planned recruitment **16,500**. Assumed rate **8** patients/unit/month, **100** ICUs, recruitment within **two years**, staggered opening over **6 months**.
- **Calculation check:** (37.0−34.5)/37.0 = 6.76%, compatible with displayed 6.8% rounding. 15,444 / (1−0.06) = 16,429.8, compatible with a rounded recruitment target of 16,500. No candidate.

### D2C-N010 — interim analysis boundaries

- **Source:** PDF p. 71 (printed p. 30) and PDF p. 73 (printed p. 32), sections 4.2.2 and 6.1.1.
- **Printed definition:** two interim analyses after recruitment and 90-day follow-up of **4,500** and **10,000** patients; Peto-Haybittle stopping rule **P<0.001**, for either effectiveness or harm; further analyses possible at DMEC request.

### D2C-N011 — health-economic value/time specifications

- **Source:** PDF pp. 71-72 (printed pp. 30-31), section 4.2.3.
- **Printed values/definitions:** detailed in-patient intervention/adherence data on **15%** of trial patients; total cost and QALYs reported at **90 days**; decedents before 90 days assumed **zero QALYs**; survivors use EQ-5D score at 90 days with zero at randomisation and linear interpolation; net monetary benefits value QALY gains at **£20,000 per QALY**.

### D2C-N012 — monitoring, closure, and retention

- **Source:** PDF p. 73 (printed p. 32), sections 5.2, 6.1, 6.2.
- **Printed values:** anticipated site visit for **25%** of sites at least once during recruitment; end of trial is last patient’s last **90-day** follow-up; central and local retained documents for **5 years** after trial end.

### D2C-N013 — protocol-version history and changed treatment range

- **Source:** PDF pp. 81-82 (printed pp. 40-41), Appendix 1.
- **Printed history:** v1.1 dated **14 December 2020**; v1.2 dated **19 March 2021** changes prior SpO2 target range **90-93%** to **90 (±2)%**; v1.8/NSA18 dated **14 October 2024** adds DAWOS at 30 days. Version history is essential to avoid matching earlier protocol targets to final-v1.8 targets.

### D2C-N014 — visual intervention labels

- **Source:** PDF p. 83 (printed p. 42) and PDF p. 84 (printed p. 43), Appendix 2.
- **Printed figure values:** when receiving oxygen, upper alarm prevents SpO2 **>92%**, reduce FiO2 until SpO2 **90%**, lower alarm prevents SpO2 **<88%**. When on **21%** oxygen/room air, lower alarm prevents SpO2 **<88%**; no contrary upper-alarm instruction is printed in that room-air figure. These labels agree with N001.

## Inferential-statistical relationships

### D2C-S001 — planned primary-effect estimand/model

- **Source:** PDF pp. 70-71 (printed pp. 29-30), section 4.2.2.
- **Definition:** intention-to-treat primary analysis of 90-day all-cause mortality; adjusted for site, hypoxic-ischaemic encephalopathy, acute brain pathology (excluding HIE), and sepsis as stratification variables plus prespecified baseline risk factors. Regression includes site random effects; reports absolute and relative risk reductions.

### D2C-S002 — primary sample-size relationship

- **Source:** PDF p. 70 (printed p. 29), section 4.1.
- **Definition:** 90% power for a 2.5-percentage-point absolute mortality reduction (37% to 34.5%), 15,444 evaluable patients, 6% attrition allowance, recruitment target 16,500. Confidence level, test sidedness, and model variance are not stated in this page slice; do not infer them.

### D2C-S003 — secondary outcome models

- **Source:** PDF p. 70 (printed p. 29), section 4.2.2 continuing to PDF p. 71 (printed p. 30).
- **Definition:** binary secondary outcomes use binomial/Poisson-family regression; continuous outcomes use normal-family regression; ICU/acute-hospital duration uses Wilcoxon rank-sum stratified by survival; survival uses Kaplan-Meier display and Cox proportional-hazards models with shared site-level frailty.

### D2C-S004 — subgroup and interim-analysis definition

- **Source:** PDF p. 71 (printed p. 30), section 4.2.2.
- **Definition:** limited prespecified subgroup interaction tests in adjusted primary-outcome models; named subgroups HIE, acute brain injury excluding HIE, and sepsis. Interim analyses use Peto-Haybittle P<0.001 at n=4,500 and n=10,000.

### D2C-S005 — pilot traffic-light decision rule

- **Source:** PDF pp. 68-70 (printed pp. 27-29), Table 2 and sections 3.10.1/4.2.1.
- **Definition:** green criteria permit unamended progression; any amber criterion triggers amendment; any red criterion stops the trial. This is a prespecified operational decision rule, not an effect-estimate result.

### D2C-S006 — health-economic analysis definitions

- **Source:** PDF pp. 71-72 (printed pp. 30-31), section 4.2.3.
- **Definition:** CEA follows intention-to-treat; reports mean (95% confidence interval) incremental costs, QALYs, and net monetary benefit at 90 days; multilevel linear regression accounts for patient clustering at site and adjusts baseline covariates. Sensitivity analyses assess robustness; lifetime projection extrapolates survival/HrQoL and costs.

### D2C-S007 — no displayed observed inferential result

- **Scope:** all PDF pp. 57-84.
- **Result:** this original protocol contains planned analyses and administrative thresholds only. It contains no observed arm estimate, confidence interval, test statistic, P value, or realised subgroup/sensitivity result to reconcile against a main-paper result in this shard.
