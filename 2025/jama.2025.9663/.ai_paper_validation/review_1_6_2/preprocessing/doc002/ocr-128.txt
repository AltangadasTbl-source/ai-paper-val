A data-adaptive approach will be used to identify additional subgroups using an honest estimation
approach whereby a random forest will be estimated on half of the data and the most representative
tree will be used to identify potential subgroups of interest. Effects and standard errors for these
groups will then be estimated using the remaining data (not used to identify the subgroups, thereby
avoiding overfitting).
Thefinal forestswillbeused toestimatethe Conditional AverageTreatmentEffect(CATE)for each
individual along with their standard errors, by predicting from the forest using their covariate values.
These individual CATEs will then be aggregated to obtain conditional ATEs for each subgroup using
in the grf package for R.
Individual treatment effectswillbe reported as absolute risk differences，andcompared against
primary clinical outcome.
