3. Statistical Principles
3.1Confidence intervals and Pvalues
All statistical tests will be two-sided with significance set at P<0.o5 unless otherwise specified. Effect
estimates will be reported with 95% confidence intervals. There will be no adjustment for multiple
testing.Theresultsof subgroup analyseswillbeinterpreted takinginto account accepted criteriafor
credible subgroup effects.11,12
