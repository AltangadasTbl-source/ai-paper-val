2.2Randomisation
Patients are randomised using a dedicated telephone/web-based randomisation service available 24
hours/seven days per week. Allocation is by randomised permuted blocks (with variable block
lengths), stratified by recruiting site and Mega-ROX subgroup (for harmonisation with Mega-ROx). The
foursubgroupsforrandomisationare:
·hypoxic ischaemic encephalopathy (HIE)
·
sepsis
·
acute brain injury (excluding HIE)
·
noneof the above
and are treated as mutually exclusive categories. In the event that a patient fits into more than one of
these categories, the randomisation will be hierarchical: HlE, then sepsis, and finally acute brain
pathologies(except HiE)-consistentwith the hierarchy used for randomisation at the start of
Mega-ROX.Althoughinformationis collected onCOViD-19 status，this is not usedfor
stratification.
