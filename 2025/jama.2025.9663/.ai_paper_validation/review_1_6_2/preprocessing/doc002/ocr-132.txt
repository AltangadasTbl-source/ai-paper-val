Multivariate Imputation using Chained Equations (MiCE) algorithm, with each imputation model
analysis being multiply imputed but complete case results will be reported alongside to facilitate
interpretation. Twenty multiply imputed datasets will be generated. Models will be fitted in each
imputed dataset and results combined using Rubin's rules. If computational considerations prevent
the combination of bootstrapping (to derive absolute risk differences) and multiple imputation (to
address missing data), two alternative approaches will be considered: reducing the number of
imputed datasets to five and bootstrap replications to 1oo0, or presenting complete case results as
both risk differences and odds ratios but multiply imputed results as odds ratios only.
8.Safety
Expected Serious Adverse Events (SAEs) (sinus tachycardia, supraventricular tachycardia, atrial
fibrillation, myocardial ischaemia/infarction, mesenteric ischaemia) and any other possibly related
SAEs, will be recorded for all randomised patients while in Icu, from the time of randomisation up to
90days(includingreadmissionstoICu).
The total number of patients experiencing one or more serious adverse event from randomisation
until critical care discharge/90 dayswillbe comparedbetweengroupsusingFisher's exacttest.The
adverse events, overall and by type, will be presented by allocated treatment group.
