7. Handling of missing data
The amount of missing clinical primary outcome data is anticipated to be minimal and will be
accounted for in a sensitivity analysis. The primary analysis will be repeated once assuming that all
group with missing outcomes did not survive. The analysis will then be repeated again with the
opposite assumptions. This will provide the absolute range of how much the results could change if
the data were complete. If necessary, missing data in baseline variables included in the adjusted
models will be multiply imputed. Multiple imputation will be undertaken using the Multivariate
Imputation using Chained Equations (MlCE) algorithm, with the model including all baseline variables
included in the adjusted models and alloutcomevariables.Subgroupanalyseswill berestricted to
patients with complete data. Missing data in costs and HrQoL for participants with enhanced data
collection will be imputed using MiCE approach. Twenty multiply imputed datasets will be generated.
Modelswill befitted ineach imputed dataset and results combined using Rubin's rules.
8.Safety
Expected Serious Adverse Events (SAEs) (sinus tachycardia, supraventricular tachycardia, atrial
fibrillation, myocardial ischaemia/infarction, mesenteric ischaemia) and any other possibly related
SAEs, will be recorded for all randomised patients while in Icu, from the time of randomisation up to
90 days(includingreadmissions toICU).
The total number of patients experiencing one or more serious adverse event from randomisation
until critical care discharge/90 days will be compared between groups using Fisher's exact test. The
numbers ofserious adverse events,and the counts andpercentages ofpatients experiencingserious
adverse events, overall and by type, will be presented by allocated treatment group.
9.Statistical software
The analyses will be conducted in Stata/MP version 16.1 (or update). Other packages, such as R, may
beusedforspecific analyses.
