The primary analysis of the primary outcome (90-day all-cause mortality) will be adjusted for site,
diagnostic subgroup(the stratification variables) and the list of additional control variables specified

statisticalmodelsisnecessarytoavoidbiasedp-valuesandconfidenceintervals,maintainthetypeI
error rate at its nominal level andavoid areductioninpower.15Adjustmentfor additional baseline
covariates that are strong predictors of outcome can increase the precision of the estimate of
treatment effect, and therefore the power of the trial, and adjustfor any chance imbalance between
thetreatmentgroups.The additionalbaselinevariablesadjusted forwillbe:

Phrased in terms of the components of an estimand,16 the analysis population is all patients enrolled
in thetrialwhodonotwithdrawcompletely;thevariableof interestis90-dayall-cause mortality(as
described in Section 5.1); the summary measure is the adjusted risk difference, and a “treatment
policy" strategy will be used to address the loss to follow-up (the only anticipated intercurrent event)
(as described inSection7).
Adjusted risk differences will be estimated as the difference in marginal probabilities predicted using
conditional logistic regression models. Models will include normally distributed random effects of site
and fixed effectsofbaselinecovariates.15Toestimateriskdifferences,model outputswillbefitted to

torisk differences)will be provided tofacilitateinterpretation.
