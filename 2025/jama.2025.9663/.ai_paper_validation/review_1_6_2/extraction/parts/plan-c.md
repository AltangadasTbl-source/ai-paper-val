## Allocation, population, intervention and ascertainment

[Source p57](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=57): randomisation 1:1 conservative versus usual oxygen using central telephone/web service, randomised permuted blocks of variable size, stratified by site, hypoxic ischaemic encephalopathy (HIE), sepsis and acute brain pathologies excluding HIE. Deferred-consent randomisation as soon as possible, no later than 12 hours after fulfilling eligibility. Co-enrolment observational permitted; interventional only when no conflict with UK-ROX objectives. Screening logs record enrolled/excluded/eligible-not-enrolled.

[Source p60](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=60): refusal/withdrawal leaves accrued data retained unless removal requested. For refusal without all-data-removal request, collect only minimal pseudonymised primary survival status alive/dead at 90 days after randomisation. [Source p76](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=76): collect minimal identifiable data for deaths prior to consent/no consultee and specified discharges without capacity to permit inclusion; national data opt-out without explicit consent means no identifiable upload or NHS Digital linkage, but minimum pseudonymised consent status and 90-day primary survival status collected. Explicit consent/consultee opinion overrules NDOO. These are prospective analysis-population and missingness pathways, not observed exclusions.

[Source p61](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=61): lowest possible oxygen concentration to keep SpO2 90±2%; receiving oxygen should not rise above92%; alarms prevent below88% and above92%. If SpO2/SaO2 significantly discrepant, use arterial SaO2. Continue same target after extubation regardless delivery modality. At 21% oxygen/room air keep this exposure even if target not attainable and deactivate upper alarm. Continue to ICU discharge or90 days, whichever sooner; restart after ICU readmission within90 days. Suspend for acute life-threatening events/high-oxygen need and document reason; newly developed contraindication leaves continuation to treating team. Control defined by clinician/local practice; target documented daily, lower alarm discretionary, no upper alarm. [Source p62](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=62): SpO2 continuously monitored in both arms; arterial gases local practice; intervention terminated if ECMO/HBOT required.

[Source pp62–65](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=62): AE any untoward occurrence; serious if death/life-threatening/inpatient admission or prolongation/disability/congenital anomaly, including events requiring intervention to prevent these. Severe/life-threatening classifications qualify as SAE. Relatedness categories none/unlikely/possibly/probably/definitely; expected if listed Appendix3. All randomised monitored to ICU discharge or90 days, restart at readmission; unexpected nonlisted SAEs reported only reasonably consequent on oxygen therapy. Death itself is collected as trial outcome, suspected cause assessed. Figure1 footnote: uncertainty of treatment association means report. CTU notification24hours; related unexpected SAE REC15calendar days. [Source p86](../../../../joi250042supp1_prod_1753377747.92525.pdf#page=86) lists sinus tachycardia, supraventricular tachycardia, atrial fibrillation, myocardial ischaemia/infarction, mesenteric ischaemia.
