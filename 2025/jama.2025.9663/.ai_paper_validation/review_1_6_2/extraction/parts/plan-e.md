## Analysis population, monitoring and reporting definitions

Page 121 treats FiO2 readings 22% or 23% as 21% only when identifying protocol deviations, explicitly not for other comparisons. Potential deviations trigger site queries; trial management may decide transient observations do not constitute deviations and the total of such events will be reported. All analyses follow ITT, original oxygen assignment irrespective of target-range maintenance or adherence. All known-primary-outcome patients included. Re-randomization allowed after at least 90 days if all inclusion criteria again met, so patients may contribute more than once. Screening logs include invasive MV after unplanned ICU admission or MV begun in ICU; flow counts assessed, eligible, ineligible with reasons, eligible randomized and eligible not randomized. Additional screening percentages distinguish all screened, inclusion-eligible and fully eligible denominators.

Pages 121–122: adult age ≥18, enrolled within 12 hours after meeting eligibility; invasive MV after unplanned admission or initiated in ICU; FiO2 >0.21 at enrollment. Exclusions: UK-ROX randomization in last 90 days, current ECMO, or clinician judges one arm indicated/contraindicated. Flow includes loss, primary-analysis included and excluded with reasons. Deferred consent/nonresponse may permit data retention; complete withdrawal excludes analysis. Mortality/length-of-stay analyses include available outcomes despite absent explicit consent or refusal of continued participation without complete withdrawal. Page 123 extends patient-reported analyses to those patients in enhanced collection with multiple imputation as required. Questionnaire loss denominator is enhanced-subset survivors at day 90; total loss includes true unavailable data, withdrawals, and questionnaire consent never given. Baseline comparisons for questionnaire responders use enhanced-subset living nonresponders and all known day-90 survivors.

Pages 123–124 prescribe baseline summaries by group without statistical tests. Variables: age mean(SD)/median(IQR)/seven age categories; sex and ethnicity counts(%); BMI five categories; severe respiratory disease; previous hospital stay and invasive-MV duration; four mutually exclusive diagnostic strata; COVID-19; randomization SpO2, FiO2, PaO2 and PaO2/FiO2 categories; ICNARC predicted mortality and APACHE II. Severity indices use first 24 ICU hours, replacing PaO2 and FiO2 with randomization values to mitigate postrandomization exposure; indirect effects on other physiology may remain.

## Clinical outcomes and methods

Primary endpoint (p124): all-cause death by 90 days after randomization. Interim-only possible substitute is acute-hospital mortality censored at 90 days if death registration linkage has substantial delay. Death-date precedence (p125): sites validate discrepancies and validated site data paramount; if sites cannot validate, death in either linked source counts and death registration determines discrepant dates.

Secondary endpoint list (pp125–126): ICU mortality before discharge to care below Level 2; acute-hospital death includes transfer follow-up until leaving acute hospital; both censored at 90 days. All-cause mortality at 60 days and one year; participants not reaching one-year follow-up before trial closes at final participant's day 90 excluded from one-year analysis. ICU stay is linear time from randomization to discharge/death plus later ICU admissions during same acute-hospital stay. Hospital stay uses calendar days from randomization to discharge/death. Organ-support-free endpoint is ordinal composite: death by day 30 score −1; survivors ranked by calendar days without respiratory, cardiovascular or renal support during days 1–30, randomization day counts as day 1, outside ICU assumed free. For CMP version 3.1 aggregate-only data assume all support at start, take maximum of the three organ-support durations, capped at 30. HRQoL at 90 days EQ-5D-5L collected only among enhanced-subset survivors, target 2500 (15%).

Primary adjusted analysis (pp126–127): site and diagnostic strata plus age, baseline SpO2 and PaO2/FiO2 each restricted cubic spline with 3 knots, COVID-19 indicator, randomization date aggregated in 2-week periods with 7-knot restricted cubic spline. Date×COVID interaction explored. Estimand: all enrolled except complete withdrawals, 90-day mortality variable, adjusted RD summary, treatment-policy strategy for loss to follow-up. Conditional logistic regression with normally distributed random site effects and fixed baseline effects; marginal predictions to whole cohort under all-conservative and all-usual assignments give RD. Percentile 95% CI from 2000 bootstrap samples at 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. Conditional OR and marginal RR provided additionally.

Confirmatory subgroups (p127): four diagnostic strata (HIE expected greater conservative benefit; sepsis and acute brain injury excluding HIE less benefit; none greater benefit); COVID positive versus not (greater conservative benefit in COVID); ethnicity (possible interaction, direction unclear). Repeat primary model with treatment×subgroup interaction; dummy interactions and joint interaction test if >2 categories; report subgroup ORs, RDs, CIs and interaction P. Exploratory HTE (pp127–128): causal forest in R `grf`, first phase these confirmatory covariates, second phase all baseline/eCRF/CMP covariates prioritized by clinical input. Start 50,000 trees, retain variables importance above 0.2×mean importance, retrain; missing incorporated in attributes. Honest split half data to identify subgroups with representative tree, remaining data estimate effects/SE. Individual CATE/SE aggregated to subgroup conditional ATE using augmented inverse-propensity weighting in `average_treatment_effect`; absolute RDs compared with primary-style parametric logistic estimates.

Secondary analysis (p128): mortality counts/percentages and analogous adjusted regression with same baseline covariates; continuous outcomes mean(SD), median(IQR), overall and survival-status strata plus histograms. ICU/hospital duration among survivors uses proportional-hazards regression with Fine–Gray competing death method. Organ-support-free composite uses ordered logistic adjusted proportional OR. Survival Kaplan–Meier; Cox adjusted for same primary covariates, shared site frailty.

## Economic outcomes and methods

Pages 128–131: primary economic endpoint NMB from incremental cost and QALY at 90 days. Secondary economic outcomes 90-day HRQoL, resource use/cost, lifetime cost/QALY/NMB. CEA conservative versus usual at 90 days, health/personal-health-services perspective. CRF/CMP/HES costs plus questionnaires in random 15% enhanced subset; missing non-subset cost and HRQoL multiply imputed with MAR justification from random selection. EQ-5D-5L mapped to 3L valuation using DSU function. Survivors QALYs from zero utility at randomization with linear interpolation to day-90 utility; deaths through day90 zero QALY. Incremental NMB = £20,000×incremental QALY − incremental cost. ITT means/95% CIs, bivariate SUR; multilevel when ICC >10%, single-level if ≤10%; same baseline covariates as clinical primary. Report all clinical subgroups, cost-effectiveness plane and acceptability curves.

CEA sensitivity (p130): random enhanced subset only; complete cost/QALY cases; alternative Hout mapping and QALY gamma distribution; alternative cost distributions including gamma; CMP-only ICU readmissions instead of combined CMP/questionnaire; HES hospital readmission costs instead of questionnaire. Lifetime (p131): available survival extrapolated using Weibull, exponential, lognormal, log logistic or Gompertz with fit/plausibility against age/sex-matched population. During predicted excess mortality use fitted survival, age-related decrement of day90 HRQoL and day90 morbidity cost; thereafter population survival/HRQoL. Combine to lifetime QALYs, incremental costs/QALYs/NMB. No external cited paper opened.

## Missingness, safety and amendments

Exposure hourly imputation (p131): carry mode forward until final hourly observation; FiO2 0.21 if no respiratory support (query higher recorded values); remaining interval-missing FiO2/SpO2/SaO2/PaO2 carry forward. Does not affect outcome analysis, using only baseline covariates. Pages131–132 state primary missingness minimal via linkage/sites; secondary missing expected with refusal/withdrawal. MICE includes every adjusted baseline covariate and all outcomes; primary analysis multiply imputed with complete cases alongside; twenty datasets with Rubin combination. Computational alternatives: five datasets and 1000 bootstraps or complete-case RD/OR with imputed OR alone.

Safety p132: expected SAEs sinus/supraventricular tachycardia, atrial fibrillation, myocardial ischemia/infarction, mesenteric ischemia and other possibly related SAE. Record all randomized while in ICU through 90 days including readmissions. Compare patients with ≥1 SAE until critical-care discharge/90 days by Fisher exact test; report event counts and patient counts/% by group overall/type. Stata/MP 17.0 or later, other packages including R for specific analyses.
