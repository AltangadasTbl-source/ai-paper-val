Independent reconstruction
All 27 site rows were checked. The rule 100*death_count/40 reproduces 26 rows; only site 2008 differs. Nonzero comparator rows identify N=40; zero-death rows are compatible with that rule but do not independently identify a denominator.
The site 2008 count gives 100*5/40=12.5%, compared with printed 7.5%, a difference of 5 percentage points.
Using the project's deliberately generous half-unit rule: 100*[4.5/40.5,5.5/39.5]=[11.111111,13.924051]%; printed 7.5 permits [7.45,7.55]%. Gap=3.561111 percentage points; relative discrepancy=3.561111/7.5=0.474815, exceeding 0.05. These rounding enclosures are arithmetic tolerance ranges, not statistical confidence intervals.
At N=40, 7.5% corresponds to three deaths. This observation does not establish which source field should change.
N=35 from the observed abstinence columns cannot reconcile five deaths with 7.5%: 100*5/35=14.285714%. The 35 belongs to observed abstinence contributors, not an explicitly defined mortality denominator.
Exact 5/0.075 implies N=66.666667. With exact integer count five and percentage rounded to one decimal, N=67 would display 7.5%. Such a denominator exceeds the site's printed recruited population of 40. Any subset of those 40 has N<=40 and therefore a five-event percentage >=12.5%; a different population would need an explicit source definition. No such definition was found in the cited display or population corroborators.
