2624 The study requires a total of 2700 smokers newly diagnosed with pulmonary TB
2625 (~43 recruits from 63 TB centres, the clusters) assuming that 20% might not
2626 provide primary outcome data. This sample also includes the first 16 participants
2627 taking part in the pilot study, in case the intervention needed major refinement
2628 then we would be unable to use their data.
2629 The required sample size for the superiority trial (phase 3) was determined by
2630 using a power of 90%, a significance level of 5% and expected abstinence rates
2631 of 18% for mTB- Tobacco and 8% for normal treatment after 6 months.13 The
2632 study will comprise a total of 27 TB centres, considering the ratio of intervention
2633 to control as 2:1. After considering a 20% attrition rate, it was determined that
2634 the sample size would be 704 participants. This would result in an average of
2635 around 26 individuals in each cluster. Given that the study will be carried out as
2636 acRCT with an intracluster correlation coefficient of 0.02, the design effect was
2637 computed to be 1.50. This results in an effective sample size of around 1080
2638 people (or 40 subjects per site).
