2449 progress and assessing effectiveness outcomes in the Quit4TB trial. This

2456 2 Study design
2457 Using an adaptive design, this study will be conducted as a multi-center, cluster
2458 randomized, controlled trial consisting of four phases.
2459 Phase 1 — PPI
2460 Involves consultation with Patients and Public Involvement (PPI) group about the
2461 study processes. PPI members will also review and provide feedback on the
2462 participant facing materials.
2463 Phase 2 - Pilot study
2464 Testing feasibility of the study intervention and database
2465 Phase 3 (superiority trial)
2466 A multicenter Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial (CRCT) spanning 12 months,
2467 with 6 months allocated for recruitment and 6 months for follow-up. The trial
2468 comprises two arms: mTB-Tobacco (Intervention A) and usual care (Control),
2469 Phase 4 (non-inferiority trial),
2470 which will last for another 12 months (6 months recruitment and 6 months follow
2471 ups), we will compare mTB-Tobacco (intervention A) with face-to-face
2472 behavioural support (intervention B).
