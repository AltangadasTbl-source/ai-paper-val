2586 5 Randomization

2587 In this Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial (cRCT), interventions will be

2588 administered at the group level, specifically in health facilities.

2592 The randomization process involves the allocation of health facilities (clusters) in

2593 a 2:2:1 ratio to the mTB-Tobacco intervention (Intervention A), face-to-face

2594 intervention (Intervention B), or standard care (Control). To ensure an unbiased

2595 allocation sequence, an independent statistician, situated at the University of

2596 Edinburgh, will employ computer-generated random-number lists.

2597 6 Blinding

2598 In this study, we used a blinding methodology to guarantee that specific key

2599 individuals remained uninformed about the allocation of treatments. The

2600 allocation sequence will be determined by an independent statistician, who has

2601 no knowledge of the participating facilities. Nevertheless, as the SMS receipt is a

2602 noticeable component, participants will be able to determine whether they are

2603 receiving the SMS or not. Therefore, it is not possible to achieve total blindness

2604 at the participant level in this circumstance. In addition, the principal investigator

2605 (Pl) and the research teams are aware of the specific cluster assigned to each

2606 treatment group. However, steps will be taken to potentially prevent the data

2607 analyst from knowing the treatment assignments in order to reduce bias during

2608 the analysis of the data. Thus, the blinding in this trial will occur during

2609 randomising the clusters to intervention or control by an independent statistician

2610 and also at the level of data analyst while analysing the data.
