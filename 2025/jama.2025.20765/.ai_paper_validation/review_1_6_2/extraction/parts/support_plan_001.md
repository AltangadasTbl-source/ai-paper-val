## Outcome hierarchy, thresholds and comparison keys

### Primary objective and primary endpoint, p8 lines 146–164

Primary objective: in people with TB who smoke daily, assess effectiveness and cost effectiveness of mTB-Tobacco in achieving continuous abstinence for at least six months. Secondary objective: enhance TB treatment adherence and improve clinical outcomes.

Primary endpoint: biochemically verified continuous abstinence at six months post randomization. Self-report must indicate no more than five cigarettes, bidis or water-pipe sessions since the quit date, verified by breath CO <10 ppm at month 6. For concomitant smokeless tobacco use, Accutest NicAlert urine cotinine strips provide levels 0–6; a level <3 (printed explanation: equivalent to 100–200 ng/mL cotinine) counts as tobacco abstinence. If a self-reported abstainer has CO >10 ppm (and cotinine indicating active use in concomitant users), biochemical verification supersedes self-report and the patient is defined as a tobacco user. Preserve strict <10 and >10 readings as printed.

Matching main-paper key: six-month biochemically verified sustained/continuous abstinence, five-use allowance, CO and cotinine criteria, reference date and population.

### Secondary outcomes, pp8–9 lines 165–187

- Point abstinence: self-reported nonuse of tobacco over the previous seven days, measured at week 9 and month 6.
- TB medication adherence: all registered patients' anti-TB medication logs on Treatment Support Cards; copy obtained from TB paramedic and attached to patient CRF.
- TB programme outcomes: proportions of treatment success (cured plus treatment completed), treatment failure, default and death, drawn from TB03 register at month 6.
- Cured: initially smear-positive and smear-negative in the last treatment month (month 6) and on at least one previous occasion.
- Treatment completed: completes treatment at month 6 without meeting cure or failure criteria.
- Treatment failure: initially smear-positive and remains smear-positive at month 5 or later.
- Default: treatment interruption for two consecutive months or longer.
- Death: death from any cause during treatment.
- Relapse: previously treated and declared cured/completed, now recurrent TB, either true relapse or a reinfection episode.

Matching report keys: outcome status, success composite, point-abstinence assessment windows, adherence derivation, all-cause mortality and relapse; no separate “key secondary” designation occurs in this shard.

## Design, sample size, adaptation and allocation

### Design, p9 lines 188–200

Adaptive multicentre cluster randomized controlled trial with four phases. Phase 1 is patient/public consultation; Phase 2 pilot. Phase 3 is superiority of A=mTB-Tobacco over C=usual care, lasts 12 months (six-month recruitment plus six-month follow-up). Phase 4 is noninferiority of A versus B=face-to-face behavioral support and lasts a further 12 months with the same six/six-month breakdown. Progression to Phase 4 is conditional on A superiority to C in sample-size descriptions.
