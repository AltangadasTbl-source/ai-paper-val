- [PDF p. 47](../../../../joi250093supp1_prod_1768590553.08963.pdf#page=47), 19.1.2–19.2.2 lines 1289–1325: secondary objective effectiveness and cost effectiveness in enhancing TB treatment adherence and improving clinical outcomes. Primary endpoint biochemically verified continuous abstinence at six months postrandomization. Abstinence means self-report of no more than five cigarettes, bidis or water-pipe sessions since quit date; breath CO less than 10 ppm at month 6. Concomitant smokeless use: Accutest NicAlert urine cotinine strips, reading scale 0–6, level less than 3 (printed equivalent 100–200 ng/mL) considered abstinence. For self-reported abstinence with CO greater than 10 ppm (and cotinine in concomitant users indicating active use), biochemical verification supersedes self-report and defines participant as tobacco user. Preserve the strict signs as printed; this page does not expressly state a rule for a CO reading exactly 10 ppm.
- Secondary endpoints on p. 47: point abstinence is no tobacco in prior seven days at week 9 and month 6; adherence from anti-TB medication logs on Treatment Support Card, copied into CRF; TB programme outcomes proportions of treatment success (cured plus completed treatment), treatment failure, defaulted and died from TB03 register at month 6. Cured = initially smear-positive, smear-negative in last treatment month (month 6) and at least one earlier occasion. Completed treatment = completion at month 6 without cure or failure criterion. Failure = initially smear-positive and remains smear-positive at month 6 or later during treatment. Defaulted = interruption for at least two consecutive months. Died = any cause during treatment. Relapse begins here and continues on [PDF p. 48](../../../../joi250093supp1_prod_1768590553.08963.pdf#page=48): previously treated, cured/completed most recent course, now recurrent TB (true relapse or reinfection). These are planned outcome statuses/definitions, not observed frequencies. Matching main-paper keys: continuous smoking abstinence, point abstinence, adherence, treatment success/cure/completion/failure/default/death/relapse.
