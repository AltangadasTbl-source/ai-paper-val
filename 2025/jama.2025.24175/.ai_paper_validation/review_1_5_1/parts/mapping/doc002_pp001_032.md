# DOC-002 support quantitative mapping — pages 1-32

## Scope and method

Direct source: `joi250116supp1_prod_1771885794.26255.pdf#page=1` through
`#page=32` (the PDF's printed pagination is not the PDF-page numbering). This
is protocol/administrative support material for the ImmunoSep trial, version
7.0, 9 December 2020. Fresh `pdftotext` native and layout extraction was made
for all 32 assigned pages. The embedded font encoding makes that extraction
visibly garbled; direct rendered-page review was used for the key endpoint,
sample-size, allocation, dosing, and statistical-method pages. CPU Tesseract
was attempted on rendered pages but returned empty output in this environment;
it was not used as evidence. Direct PDF page locations below remain the source
authority.

There are no completed trial results, result tables, figures containing
outcomes, or inferential estimates in this scope: this is a planned protocol.
The relationships below are nevertheless result-relevant definitions and
planned numerical/statistical relationships for comparison with the main
paper and other support sources.

The protocol bases the 15-day period on survival curves and describes a marked survival drop through day 15 with stable survival to day 28. |

## Evidence-quality repair (direct-source recheck)

`verification/evidence_recheck.md` was used only to correct the prior stale
transcriptions in this owned mapping, against the direct-source locations. The
correct protocol values are: IFN-gamma **100 micrograms** on PDF page 6;
primary SOFA target **1.4 points**; immunoparalysis entry HLA-DR/quantibrite
**below 5,000 ABC**; **24** ImmunoSep sites; and **117 participants per arm**
as the analyzable sample-size target. The **8,000 ABC** value remains the
separate immune-reversal definition. These repaired values are definitions and
planning inputs, not candidate findings; this mapping makes no inconsistency
or correction claim.

## Explicit no-applicable units and mapping limitations

- PDF pages 1-5 and 30-32 contain no additional result-relevant quantitative relationship beyond the administrative definitions noted above.
- Page 15 is a flow figure with no extracted outcome numbers; it was mapped as a screening-process figure rather than treated as an absent unit.
- Native/layout text preserves enough wording and many values to locate the direct PDF pages, but embedded-font encoding prevents trustworthy character-level extraction. CPU OCR produced empty files; it is not a source of mapped values. Exact values above were recorded only where direct source review or unambiguous extracted print permitted transcription. Ambiguous display characters (notably units in several dosing/sample lines) should be checked visually against the direct PDF during any later candidate recheck.
- This mapping registers no candidate or adjudication. It is a complete relationship/definition inventory for the assigned source pages.
