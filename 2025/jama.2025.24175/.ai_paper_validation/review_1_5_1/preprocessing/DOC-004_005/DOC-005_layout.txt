Additional Information
Who can access the data: Who can access the data: Any qualified researchers who engage
in rigorous, independent scientific research
Types of analyses: Types of analyses: For rigorous, independent scientific research, access
to anonymized, individual and trial-level data (analysis data sets), as well as other information
(eg, protocols and Clinical Study Reports), as long as the trials are not part of an ongoing or
planned regulatory submission is possible. This includes requests for clinical trial data for
unlicensed products and indications.
Mechanisms of data availability: Mechanisms of data availability: Can be requested and will
be provided following execution of a Data Sharing Agreement (DSA).
Any additional restrictions: Any additional restrictions: Access possible as long as the trials
are not part of an ongoing or planned regulatory submission. Data will be accessible for 12
months, with possible extensions considered.
