# Statistical Relationship Inventory — Passes 1 and 2

## Scope and rules applied

This is the canonical inventory for every inferential relationship mapped from the four supplied PDFs: DOC-001 (main article, 11 pages), DOC-002 (SAP, 46 pages), DOC-003 (results supplement, 9 pages), and DOC-004 (protocol, 48 pages). It was rebuilt from the current-run main and support evidence maps and confirmed against the cited supplied PDF pages. `S` IDs are stable, begin at `S001`, and are not candidate IDs. Pass 2 revisited all 35 S records against the then-complete cross-lane ledger (C001-C007) and the mechanical recheck; its additional plan-versus-final LOS population observation was subsequently registered and rechecked as C008.

Checks applied to every record were point-estimate containment, endpoint order, sign/direction, effect-measure and scale label, matching repetition, and (only where compatible supplied definitions exist) interval/P-value/test/statistic/SE compatibility.  The SAP/protocol establish two-sided 95% CIs and two-sided 5% tests, but do not supply per-result degrees of freedom, fitted covariance parameters, model convergence/fallback status, or result-specific SEs.  Therefore exact CI-to-P reconstruction is not asserted where those inputs are absent.  Any calculation described below is a labelled diagnostic approximation only.

No supplied observed result is printed as `P = 0`, `p = 0.000`, or an equivalent display zero. `DISPLAY_ZERO_NOT_CANDIDATE` is therefore not applicable to an observed result in this package. The planned interim `P < .001` boundary is a decision definition, not a display-zero result.

## Pass-1 candidate records (no C IDs assigned)

### SP1-CAND-001 — Longitudinal QoL covariance specification differs between the SAP and final article

- **Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency.
- **Direct observation:** The SAP states that SF-12/EQ-5D repeated-measures mixed-effects models assume a general unstructured covariance structure and incorporate robust sandwich SEs (DOC-002 p.26). The final article says that longitudinal SF-12/EQ-5D analyses assumed an independent covariance structure (DOC-001 p.4).
- **Consistency rule:** These are differently named covariance specifications for the same stated longitudinal outcome-model family. The final article points readers to the SAP.
- **Inference boundary:** The supplied sources do not state whether a documented analysis amendment, model diagnostic, convergence issue, or reporting condensation explains the difference. The results supplement gives adjustment terms but not the covariance structure or SE.
- **Exact human question:** Which covariance and variance-estimation specification generated the reported SF-12/EQ-5D estimates and P values, and was any departure from the SAP prespecified or documented?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

### SP1-CAND-002 — Length-of-stay effect measure/model differs between SAP/protocol plan and final article

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Direct observation:** The SAP and protocol state a mixed-effects linear model yielding an adjusted mean difference for length of stay and, if skewed, an unadjusted difference between medians (DOC-002 p.25; DOC-004 p.39). The final article states that LOS was log transformed and presents an adjusted ratio of geometric means (DOC-001 p.4 and Table 3 on p.8).
- **Consistency rule:** The displayed planned and final analysis descriptions use different effect measures and model/reporting rules for the same endpoint.
- **Inference boundary:** The sources do not provide a final model-selection rationale, a documented amendment, or the fitted-model diagnostics. This record does not infer that an unreported change was improper.
- **Exact human question:** Was the log-transformed ratio-of-geometric-means analysis a documented change from the stated SAP/protocol plan, and which final effect measure was intended for extraction and interpretation?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

## Pass-1 limitations

- The SAP/protocol specify broad inferential conventions but not final fitted covariance parameters, degrees of freedom, result-specific SEs, model fallback/convergence status, or all estimand mappings needed for exact P/CI/statistic algebra.
- The primary and binary model source specifies possible convergence fallbacks, but the selected fallback (if any) is not printed for the final analyses.
- Tipping-point graphs have no numerical coordinates or uncertainty table. They were checked for printed null/direction/narrative consistency only.
- Blank SAP/protocol presentation templates establish planned labels and definitions, not observed results.
