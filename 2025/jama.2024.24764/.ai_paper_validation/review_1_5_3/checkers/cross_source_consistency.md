# Cross-Source Consistency Review

## Scope and method

This review compared every matchable quantitative or statistical occurrence mapped in DOC-001 (main article, including abstract, narrative, tables, figures, captions, and footnotes), DOC-003 (observed-results supplement), DOC-002 (SAP), and DOC-004 (protocol). DOC-002 and DOC-004 were used as planned-definition comparators only: their result tables are unpopulated templates and a planned value was not treated as a competing observed result.

Before comparing printed values, each relationship was matched on trial population, analysis set, outcome definition, time window, contrast, reference group, model/effect measure, scale or unit, and displayed precision. `iNPWT` in the main article/results supplement and `SUNPD` in the SAP/protocol were treated as the same intervention only where the source explicitly equated them. Percentages with different printed precision (for example, 28% in a table and 28.4% in narrative) were treated as compatible when their shared numerator and denominator established ordinary rounding.

Direct-source locations cited below are the supplied PDFs. This artifact assigns no stable candidate IDs and makes no adjudicative judgment.

## Qualifying candidate consistency issue

### Proposed candidate — control-arm mortality count differs between the participant-flow footnote and Table 3

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Pending human adjudication:** Yes.
- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 Figure 1, PDF p. 3](<../../jama_atherton_2025_oi_240145_1741627844.85412.pdf#page=3>); [DOC-001 Table 3, PDF p. 8](<../../jama_atherton_2025_oi_240145_1741627844.85412.pdf#page=8>); supporting narrative [DOC-001 PDF p. 6](<../../jama_atherton_2025_oi_240145_1741627844.85412.pdf#page=6>).
- **Printed values:** Figure 1 footnote b says, “A total of 25 deaths were reported (10 in the iNPWT group and 15 in the surgeon’s preference group).” Table 3 prints “Mortality within 30 d” as `10/411 (2)` and `14/410 (3)`. The main narrative reports `2.4%` (10/411) and `3.4%` (14/410).
- **Matched comparison:** Both locations describe mortality in the retained randomized groups (411 iNPWT, 410 surgeon's preference). The intervention/control labels, population, unit (people dying), and displayed group counts match. Time is explicit in Table 3 (within 30 days) but not in the Figure 1 footnote, which says total deaths reported.
- **Comparison logic:** The iNPWT value agrees at 10. The surgeon's-preference group is printed as 15 in Figure 1 but 14 in Table 3/narrative. Thus the group totals are `10+15=25` versus `10+14=24`; the one-death difference exceeds a display-precision difference and is not a rate-versus-count conversion.
- **Supported alternatives:** The Figure 1 footnote may include one death outside the 30-day Table 3 window, while Table 3/narrative may deliberately restrict mortality to 30 days. Alternatively, one location may contain a reporting/transcription discrepancy. The current supplied text does not state the timing of the additional Figure 1 control-arm death, so it cannot resolve these alternatives.
- **Human verification steps:** (1) Inspect the participant-level follow-up/SAE source or trial database, if available, for all 15 control-arm deaths and their procedure-to-death dates. (2) Confirm whether Figure 1's “total deaths reported” was intended to include post-day-30 deaths. (3) If it was, label Figure 1's time window explicitly; if it was not, reconcile the control count and total across Figure 1, Table 3, and the Results narrative. (4) Reconfirm whether the 30-day safety denominator remains 410 after the resolution.
