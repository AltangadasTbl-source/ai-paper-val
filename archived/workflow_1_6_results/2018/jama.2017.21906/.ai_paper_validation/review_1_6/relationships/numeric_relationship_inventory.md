Inferential interval/test checks are additionally owned by the statistical inventory; this inventory checks their numeric labels, units, population and repeated occurrences.

Rules: exact integer equality for counts; nearest-display rounding intervals ±0.05 for one decimal, ±0.005 for two decimals, ±0.5 for integer percentages/means. A difference of two rounded quantities can deviate by the sum of their half-units plus the difference's half-unit. Weighted means require actual variable-specific n; absent n is an explicit computation limit. Medians, IQRs, SDs and adjusted estimates are never added or treated as crude differences. Sums of k rounded integer percentages tolerate k/2 percentage points. A displayed rate in percent can consistently mean participant risk/proportion; person-time is not supplied. No external clinical unit assumptions are used.
