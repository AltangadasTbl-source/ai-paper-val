554   8.0   STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

555         8.1   Study Design/Study Endpoints
556               Study design and endpoints are covered in detail within the ACS QUIK
557               Statistical Analysis Plan (Version 22 September 2014).
558
559               As described in the ACS QUIK SAP, the primary endpoint is “Composite
560               of 30-day major adverse event rate, defined as all-cause death,
561               myocardial infarction, stroke, and major bleeding defined by GUSTO
562               criteria (intracerebral hemorrhage or bleeding resulting in substantial
563               hemodynamic compromise requiring treatment (GUSTO Investigators. N
564               Engl J Med 1993; 329:673-682))”.
565
566               As described in the ACS QUIK SAP, secondary endpoints include “30-day
567               all-cause mortality rate, 30-day cardiovascular disease mortality rate, in-
568               hospital all-cause mortality rate, 30-day myocardial infarction (re-
569               infarction) rate, 30-day stroke rate, 30-day major GUSTO bleeding rate,
570               optimal in-hospital medication use, optimal discharge medication use,
571               tobacco cessation advice”.
572

573         8.2   Sample Size and Accrual
574               Power and sample size are covered in detail within the ACS QUIK
575               Statistical Analysis Plan (Version 22 September 2014). In brief “At an
576               alpha of 0.05, the anticipated sample size of 15,000 patients will result in
577               80% power to detect a 2.4% difference from a baseline 10.4% 30-day
578               MACE rate. This sample size is increased to 15,750 to account for up to
579               5% drop out between discharge and 30-day follow-up.”
580
