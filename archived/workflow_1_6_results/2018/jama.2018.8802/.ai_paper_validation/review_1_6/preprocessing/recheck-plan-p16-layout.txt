216     Adherence is expressed as a composite measure or all-or-none measure. The

217   composite measure is defined as the total number of interventions performed among

218   eligible patients divided by the total number of possible interventions among eligible

219   patients. 28 The all-or-none measure is defined as the proportion of eligible patients

220   who receive all of the performance measure interventions.28

222   Secondary outcomes

223     The secondary outcomes include in-hospital death, a new clinical vascular event

224   (ischemic stroke, hemorrhagic stroke, myocardial infarction, or vascular death),

225   disability measured by Modified Rankin Score (mRS = 3-5), and all-cause mortality

226   at 3, 6, and 12-months after initial symptom onset.
