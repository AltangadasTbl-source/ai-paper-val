### DOC-002 PDF pp. 9–11 (printed protocol pp. 8–10), PLAN

[Summary endpoint](../../../joi180004supp1_prod.pdf#page=9): primary objective compares two airway techniques for day-28 survival with favorable neurological function; endpoint is survival at day 28 with Glasgow-Pittsburgh Cerebral Performance Categories (CPC) ≤2. Secondary objectives: survival at admission/day 28, neurological outcome, immediate adverse/serious adverse events related to TI, intubation difficulty, mask-ventilation difficulty, time to complete TI, duration of interrupted chest compression. Secondary endpoints in summary include survival at hospital admission, day 28 and discharge, mRS at day 28, ROSC rate, IDS, and TI complications (failure, esophageal or mainstem intubation, vomiting, pulmonary aspiration, dental trauma).

[Summary continuation](../../../joi180004supp1_prod.pdf#page=10): mask complications = regurgitation of gastric content. Technique failure = mortality at day 28 OR regurgitation during procedure OR procedure failure. Mask difficulty = visual analog scale, Han classification, difficult-mask signs. Time to complete TI from blade touching patient to cuff inflation. Interruption duration during TI; interruption during advanced CPR from medical-team arrival to decision to stop CPR; advanced-CPR duration uses same origin/end. Trial is prospective multicentre open randomized noninferiority in physician-staffed EMS. Expected population =2000 adult OHCA patients at medical-team arrival with resuscitation attempted. Inclusion age ≥18, medical insurance; exclusions suspected massive aspiration, do-not-resuscitate order, pregnancy, prisoners. Phase III. Twenty centres in two countries (France/Belgium). Subjects =2000. Inclusion =24 months; participation =28 days; total duration =24 months and 28 days. Recruitment printed as 100 patients/centre and 5 patients/month/centre. Analyses labelled ITT and per protocol for mask ventilation noninferiority relative to TI.

[Statistical summary](../../../joi180004supp1_prod.pdf#page=11): primary ITT calculates two-sided 95% CI for π_bag − π_tracheal. Noninferiority criterion: lower CI limit higher than −0.01. Exact rather than asymptotic CI if necessary. Secondary ITT proportions described as “rates” use chi-square; corresponding 95% CIs on odds ratio and differences presented. Continuous secondary criteria use t-test or Mann–Whitney according to Gaussian/non-Gaussian distribution. Interim analyses after 50% and 75% inclusion, only futility and/or sample-size recalculation, ADDPLAN. Sample calculation: Hasegawa historical mask favorable-neurological survival 3%; Gueugniaud historical TI survival 2%; noninferiority margin 1%; 956/group required for power 0.8, type-I error 0.025; 2000 recruited; 5000 simulations, Newcombe-Wilson score method (citation spelling in source is “Newcomb-Wilson”; no correction inferred).
