Dose of vitamin D supplement

   Dose of vitamin D per day was increased from 1,200IU/day to 2,000IU/day
   before starting the trial at 2009.10.8.


Sample size
   Since this is the first trial, it was difficult to predict survival rate in vitamin D
   group and placebo group. Thus, sample size was blank at 2008.12.25, but
   fixed as 400 before starting the trial at 2009.10.8.


Subgroup analysis
   We decided to perform subgroup analyses stratified by serum levels of
   25(OH)D at 20ng/ml and 40ng/ml, after starting this trial and before first
   midterm analysis, at 2012.12.18.


Polymorphisms of vitamin D binding protein
   In addition to SNPs analyses of the vitamin D receptor (VDR), vitamin D
   binding protein (DBP) was added at 2012.12.8.


Double (triple) cancer incidence
   De novo cancer appearing in organs other than site of primary cancer after
   starting supplementation was first included in severe adverse events.
   However, separated from secondary and safety outcomes and inserted as
   tertiary outcome at 2012.12.8.
