D means randomized vitamin D, P means randomized placebo. Main randomized efficacy population is D251/P166; complete-case baseline subgroups are low D102/P71, middle D142/P90; high D4/P1 has no reported inferential analysis. Main timing starts at randomization; main p.4 says randomization and medication initiation occurred together. Relapse-free survival concerns earliest relapse or any-cause death, whereas overall survival concerns any-cause death. Mean-over-trial vitamin D groups pool treatment arms and must not be aligned as randomized treatment effects.

Rule E: Check positive ratio estimate and CI endpoints, lower≤estimate≤upper, lower≤upper, contrast identity, direction against adverse-event outcomes and narrative, null inclusion as a qualitative screen, and matched repetitions. Cox confidence level95%, two-sided significance rule P<.05, nominal uncorrected subgroup inference, and randomized analysis are supplied (main p.3; PLAN pp.14/31). The package does **not** supply the coefficient P-test choice (Wald/score/likelihood ratio), CI construction, coefficient covariance/variance estimator, or full model fitting options. Therefore no exact CI/P identity is imposed. CI-null/P screens are descriptive; rounding-boundary cases do not establish contradiction. No standard error, coefficient test statistic or degrees of freedom is printed. Ratios describe hazards/subdistribution hazards, not direct event probability ratios.

Rule I: A P-only interaction lacks an interaction coefficient/CI, statistic, covariance and precise coding/test details; check printed range, repeated occurrence, endpoint/subgroup identity and significance wording. Baseline main interaction explicitly uses treatment, baseline low/middle group and product term. Do not reconstruct interaction P from separate HR CIs without covariance and joint-model specification.

Rule W: Wilcoxon signed-rank within-arm and Mann–Whitney between-arm change-ratio tests are explicitly supplied (main p.4). Medians/IQRs do not determine paired ranks, ties, change-ratio distribution or P. Equality of displayed medians does not imply zero change in every patient. Missing rank counts, test statistics, sample sizes and exact/asymptotic conventions prevent independent P reproduction.

Rule H: Historical/background supplied claims are checked only for repetitions, direction, printed measure/unit and model labels; original observations/test details are unavailable. External references are not consulted.
