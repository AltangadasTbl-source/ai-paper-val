4.3 SCREENING AND RECRUITMENT OF THE PATIENTS
All intubated patients will be screened and recruitment procedure for eligible patients will
be done as following:


                   Screening of all intubated patients in the ICU
                                                       Patients died or transferred before meeting
                                                      criteria for extubation
                                                       Patients tracheotomized without prior extubation
                                                       Terminal extubations (end of life)
                                                       Unplanned extubations

                                   Screening of all planned extubation
                                                       Patients ventilated less than 24h
                                                       Patients extubated with a do-not-reintubate order

     Planned extubation without do-not-intubate order after at least 24h of intubation




  All patients > 65 years or with underlying chronic cardiac or respiratory disease
                                    will be eligible




WEANING PROCEDURE
An international consensus panel on weaning insisted on the need to perform the first
weaning test as soon as the patient meets the following criteria [42]:
   x Resolution of the initial reason for intubation,
   x Cardiovascular stability with minimal or no need for vasopressors,
   x Patient awake with a RASS between +1 and -2 according the Richmond Assessment
       Sedation Scale,
   x No continuous sedation,
   x Respiratory rate ≤ 35 breaths per minute and adequate oxygenation defined as SpO 2
       > 90 % on FiO2 ≤ 40% or PaO2/FiO2 ≥ 150 mm Hg with PEEP up to 8 cmH2O.

Early identification of patients who are able to breathe spontaneously results in better
outcomes and it has clearly demonstrated that the use of a weaning protocol, including daily
screening followed by weaning trial and systematic extubation if successful, shortened
intubation time without increased risk of reintubation [54, 55]. In absence of a weaning
protocol in the ICU, the weaning protocol used in our ICU will be proposed to manage
weaning (Appendix n°2). The first weaning trial will be performed as soon as the patient
meets weaning criteria above-mentioned and patients will be categorized into three groups
according to the difficulty of their weaning process.
