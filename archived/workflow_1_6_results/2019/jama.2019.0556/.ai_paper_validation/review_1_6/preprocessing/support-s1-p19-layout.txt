5.2 Behavioural therapy
The intervention will consist of 21 sessions (15 individual sessions, 30 minutes/each; 6
group-based sessions, 1 hour/each). The intervention will be supervised by a gz-psychologist
and will include detailed analysis of each individual’s behavior to determine idiosyncratic
triggers and functions of unhelpful (e.g., mood‐related snacking) and helpful food‐related
behavior, thereby, to reinforce helpful behaviors and to implement effective alternatives to
unhelpful behaviors, building on behavioral approaches proven effective in depression. A
Behavioural Activation therapist who will receive a specific training in Exeter on food and
food behavior will deliver the intervention. A dietician will be available for consultation.
