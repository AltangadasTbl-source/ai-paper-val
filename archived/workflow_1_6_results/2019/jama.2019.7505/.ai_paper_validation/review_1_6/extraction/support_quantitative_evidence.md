## P001–P004: Identity, population, hypothesis and background

P001. [DOC-002 p. 4](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=4): BMI = body mass index; PEEP = positive end-expiratory pressure; PPC = postoperative pulmonary complication; CPAP/NPPV and ARDS labels are defined. [p. 5](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=5) and [p. 9](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=9): multicenter international randomized controlled trial; obese population BMI ≥35 kg/m² at intermediate-to-high PPC risk, surgery under general anesthesia. Primary endpoint is proportion of patients with PPCs. Matching main keys: eligibility, BMI unit/threshold, primary endpoint denominator, intervention contrast.

P002. [p. 5](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=5) and [p. 8](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=8): higher PEEP with recruitment versus lower PEEP without recruitment hypothesized to prevent PPCs; lower outcome incidence is beneficial. Summary secondary endpoints: intraoperative complications; postoperative invasive/noninvasive ventilatory support; unexpected ICU admission/readmission; hospital-free days; 90-day survival/mortality. Objective also covers pulmonary/extra-pulmonary outcomes and length of hospital stay. No superiority margin beyond sample-size effect assumptions, noninferiority margin, or equivalence hypothesis is printed. Matching keys: contrast, endpoint direction and time horizon.

P003. [p. 6](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=6): ARISCAT ≥26 denotes intermediate/high risk independently of BMI. Historical lower-tidal-volume/PEEP trial population BMI <35 kg/m²; recently concluded higher-PEEP trial BMI <40 kg/m², mostly nonobese BMI <30 kg/m²; no historical effect estimate printed here. [p. 7](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=7): observational survey in 28 French centers, most general-surgery patients including obese ventilated at low PEEP ≤4 cmH₂O or zero, average PEEP higher for obese; protective tidal volume range 6–8 ml/kg predicted body weight irrespective of BMI. Matching keys: PEEP exposure/unit, PBW scale; historical populations must not be equated to trial results.

P004. [p. 10](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=10): intended recruitment over 2 years, about 90 centers, 2013 patients randomized, each center at least 24; daily screening registry includes screened patients regardless of eligibility. Inclusions: general anesthesia, ARISCAT ≥26, BMI ≥35 kg/m², expected surgery ≥2 h. Exclusions: age <18 years; prior lung surgery; persistent hemodynamic instability/shock; severe COPD specified by home NIV/oxygen or repeated systemic corticosteroid exacerbation treatment; chemotherapy/radiation up to 2 months prior; NYHA III/IV, acute coronary syndrome or persistent ventricular tachyarrhythmias; invasive ventilation >30 minutes in previous 30 days; pregnancy; prevalent ARDS expected to require prolonged postoperative ventilation; systolic pulmonary arterial pressure >40 mmHg; intracranial injury/tumor; neuromuscular disease. [p. 11](../../../joi190055supp1_prod_16092.pdf#page=11) continues exclusions: prone/lateral position, one-lung ventilation, cardiac surgery, neurosurgery, planned reintubation, other interventional enrollment/refusal of consent. Matching keys: randomized target versus actual enrollment, center count, flow exclusions, denominator population.
