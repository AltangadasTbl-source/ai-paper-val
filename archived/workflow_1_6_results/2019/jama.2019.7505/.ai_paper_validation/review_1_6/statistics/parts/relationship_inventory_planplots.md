## Definition-limited diagnostics, deliberately separate from candidate discovery

All estimates with supplied numeric intervals are inside ordered positive intervals. Historical adjusted ORs equal exp(beta) at printed OR precision; coefficients and scores match results eTable1. References carry OR1 and no printed CI/P. No standard errors or inferential test statistics are printed for historical estimates. Display-zero P values: none in this assigned inferential scope; no candidate uses the precision of zero.

The sequential efficacy P thresholds approximately match two-sided standard-normal tails for the tabulated positive boundary magnitudes, and futility thresholds approximately match two-sided tails for the inner magnitudes. This is a diagnostic approximation: a source Z distribution, exact signed contrast and variance formula are not supplied. It does not settle the directly printed sign/region mismatch below. Cumulative alpha spending is not the same quantity as per-look P.

The original dropout total748 can arise from rounded additive 5% allowance. The revised total2013 matches rounding up1912/.95. The package does not prescribe one inflation formula for both versions; no candidate is emitted from the difference alone. Protocol p12's retained748 procedure total versus revised2013 belongs to numeric/cross-source review, not a duplicate S record.
