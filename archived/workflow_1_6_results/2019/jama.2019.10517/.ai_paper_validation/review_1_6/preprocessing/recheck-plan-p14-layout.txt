654   already been enrolled for 12-20 weeks prior to randomization and who are in sustained remission at the time of

656   attrition will likely drop out in the phases prior to randomization. Non-informative censoring will be examined by

659   relapse interaction (exploring the possibility of higher risk in the first 3 months following discontinuation).
660   Secondary Hypotheses: Weight and Metabolic Safety
661   H2: The combination of sertraline and olanzapine will be associated with higher weight, higher total
662   cholesterol, and higher triglycerides compared with the combination of sertraline and placebo in the
663   randomized phase. This will be tested using separate mixed-effects linear regression analyses for weight,
664   cholesterol, and triglycerides during the randomized phase. Models will include up to 15 repeated
665   assessments of weight and up to 5 repeated measures of total cholesterol and triglycerides over the 36-week
666   trial, as the respective dependent variables. Using a regulatory approach to safety analyses (H2, H3, E1 and
667   E2), we will classify subjects based on medications taken during protocolized treatment. Likelihood ratio (LR)
668   tests will successively examine the incremental contributions of a quadratic term for time and two interactions
669   (treatment by site and treatment by time).140 The decision rule calls for rejection of H0 if the latter interaction is
670   statistically significant. Interaction and quadratic terms will only be included in subsequent models if statistically
671   significant. LR tests will determine if the time better approximated with weekly time indicators that allow for
672   other forms of non-linear change over time. In addition a spline-based approach will examine metabolic
673   changes. If a participant receives new treatment or a change of treatment for hyperlipidemia during the RCT,
674   his/her pertinent metabolic data from that point onwards will be excluded from the mixed model, although the
675   metabolic measures in question will continue to be collected for safety analyses and reporting to the DSMB.
676   Based on data from STOP-PD, we expect that fewer than 5% of participants (i.e. <10 participants in the RCT)
677   will have metabolic data censored for this reason. We chose not to include these post-randomization metabolic
678   data as covariates in the mixed model, because doing so could confound ‘cause and effect’ and result in a
679   biased estimate of the treatment effect, thereby compromising the primary goal of the analysis.141
680   H3: Older age will be associated with less weight gain during the open-label phase. Mixed-effects linear
681   regression analysis will examine weight change from Acute Phase baseline. The model will include up to 6
682   weight assessments over the 12-20 week acute and stabilization phases. We will use the strategy above (H2)
683   except that age will be the primary fixed effect because treatment is a constant in the open-label phase. LR
684   tests will examine incremental contributions of a quadratic term for time and the age by time interaction. In
685   addition a spline-based approach will be used to fit metabolic measures. H0 will be rejected if the interaction is
686   statistically significant.
