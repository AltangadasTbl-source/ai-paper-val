807   Use of multiple sites (through the Collaborative Clinical Studies of Mental Disorders mechanism [PAR-09-153])
808   is justified by the fact that the majority of patients with PD need to be recruited from inpatient settings due to
809   the severity of their illness. Recruiting and retaining patients in a treatment study of PD is labor intensive and
810   challenging; a single site would not be able to recruit and retain the number of patients with PD that are
811   needed for the proposed study.
