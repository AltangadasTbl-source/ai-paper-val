9.9 Protocol modifications and amendments
If the study protocol has to be changed after approval, a written amendment is required that
must be signed by the principal investigator of the study (LKP), the director of the
coordinating center, and the clinical pharmacology consultant. Any protocol amendment will
only be implemented after approval has been granted by the ethics committees and the
competent authorities, where applicable.
Substantial amendments affecting the benefit to risk ratio must be approved by the Ethics
Committees and the BfArM. These procedures must be completed before any modifications
can come into operation, except when they are necessary to eliminate immediate hazards for
the study participants.
9.10 Access to source data, quality control, data handling
For the purpose of ensuring compliance with the Clinical Study Protocol, Good Clinical
Practice, and applicable regulatory requirements, the investigator must _ permit
auditing/inspection by applicable regulatory authorities and by representatives of the sponsor
(clinical monitors).
‘The investigator agrees to allow the auditors/inspectors/ monitors to have direct access to the
study records for review, being understood that personnel is bound by professional secrecy,
and as such will not disclose any personal identity or personal medical information. The
investigator will make every effort to help with the performance of the audits, inspections and
monitoring, giving access to all necessary facilities, data, and documents.
‘The participant consents in writing to release the investigator from his/her professional
discretion in so far as to allow inspection of original data for monitoring purposes by health
authorities and authorized persons. Authorized persons (clinical monitors and inspectors) may
inspect the participant-related data collected during the study observing the federal data
protection law.
‘The confidentiality of the data is verified and the protection of the participants should be
respected during these inspections.
