APPENDIX 2: Blood withdrawal and handling of samples

Blood withdrawal
During the first appointment (day 0), if all inclusion and exclusion criteria are met (and the
patient consented to the optional blood sampling) four extra tubes of blood 9-10ml each (2
EDTA tubes and 2 serum tubes) will be taken for analysis of additional biomarkers (optional).
Nevertheless, if in any of the participating centers blood withdrawal is not part of the routine
pre-colonoscopy examination, patients will be asked if they agree to an additional venous
puncture.

Handling and storage of blood samples
After blood withdrawal, serum samples will be kept at room temperature for 30 minutes and
for a maximum of 60 minutes, to allow complete coagulation of the serum sample. Followed
by 10 minutes centrifugation at 2500-3500 RPM. Blood samples (EDTA tubes) will be gently
shaken (turned upside down) 8 to 10 times to prevent coagulation. Both serum and plasma
samples will be stored at the centers at 4°C to 8°C until being picked up by the Limbach
Laboratory within 2 to 3 days of blood withdrawal. Blood samples will be transported (on
ice). At the laboratory of the Division of Clinical Epidemiology and Aging Research
(C070)blood samples will be centrifuged (2500-3500 RPM) and aliquoted on the same day.
After centrifugation the buffy coat (white blood cells) will be collected from the plasma
samples. Aliqouts from both serum and plasma will be stored at -20°C.

Blood samples may also be transported to laboratories of cooperating partners (including
international partners) for future studies on biomarkers for risk and early detection of
advanced adenomas / colorectal cancer including genetic factors. All laboratory analyses will
be done in a blinded fashion with respect to both treatment given and clinical/colonoscopy
data.
The handling and storage of the blood samples will be conducted in a similar fashion as is
currently done in the ongoing observational studies. The blood samples will only be used for
research with the specific aims outlined above. Participants will be informed about this and
will have the option of participating in the main study without providing a blood sample. In
addition, participants will be informed that they can request destroying their blood samples at
any time. In that case participants have to contact their study center, which in turn will give
the specific participant number to the coordinating center. Participants will not be informed
