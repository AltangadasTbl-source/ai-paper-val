3.5 Study type
Multicenter, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.


3.6 Study phase
This study is an academic, investigator-initiated trial (IIT) using a well known drug. Formally,
it could be categorized as a phase III trial, since a potential new indication is analyzed, using a
confirmatory approach.


3.7 Study population
Men and women of 40 to 80 years of age with planned screening colonoscopy or planned
diagnostic colonoscopy.


3.8 Inclusion and exclusion criteria
Inclusion criteria
x Age 40 to 80 years (both males and females; premenopausal women must have a negative
    pregnancy test before inclusion into the study, postmenopausal women are women who
    have not had menstrual bleeding for at least 12 months, or have been surgically sterilized)
x Planned screening or diagnostic colonoscopy
x Able to speak and understand German sufficiently to be able to give written informed
    consent and comply with the study requirements

Exclusion criteria
x Factors potentially influencing the primary endpoint
