6. DESIGN ASPECTS


6.1 Outcome measures
Primary endpoint
Sensitivity at predefined cutpoints of test positivity of the FOBGold®Tube Screen test to
detect advanced neoplasms.

Secondary endpoints

   x   positive and negative predictive values
   x   specificity and the area under the curve in ROC analyses
   x   likelihood ratios
   x   gender-specific performance of the tests
   x   gain in diagnostic performance by test application on multiple days
   x   serious adverse events



6.2 Inclusion / exclusion criteria
Inclusion criteria
x Age 40 to 80 years (both males and females; premenopausal women must have a negative
    pregnancy test before inclusion into the study, postmenopausal women are women who
    have not had menstrual bleeding for at least 12 months, or have been surgically sterilized)
x Planned screening or diagnostic colonoscopy
x Able to speak and understand German sufficiently to be able to give written informed
    consent and comply with the study requirements

Exclusion criteria
x Factors potentially influencing the primary endpoint
