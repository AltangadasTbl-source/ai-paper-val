6.3 Controls / Comparators
Diagnostic performance (sensitivity, specificity, positive and negative predictive values,
likelihood ratios, area under the curve) of the two immunochemical FOBTs in participants
who received placebo.
6.4 Dose and route of administration
‘The study medication consists of a single dose of 300 mg acetylsalicylic acid or a placebo to
be taken orally.
6.5 Randomization
‘The randomization list will be prepared after preparation of study drugs. All study drugs will
be individually packed and randomly assigned a unique number between 1 and 4,000. A list
will be made linking the numbers to the type of study medication given. This list will be kept
in a sealed envelope at division of Clinical Epidemiology and Aging Research at the DKFZ.
All centres will receive a sufficient number of “blocks”. For each patient to be randomized,
the study physician will take the study medication with the next participant number (in
ascending order) within the block. This participant number will be documented

For each participant number, there will be a sealed envelope with the information on whether
it was verum or placebo. One set of these envelopes will be kept at the respective study
centers (will be provided together with a “block” of study drugs), one set will be at the KKS
and one set will be at the DKFZ. These envelopes are not to be opened at all, except in case of
the need for emergency unblinding. In this case, only the envelope corresponding to the study
participant for whom unblinding is necessary may be opened. As such, blinding will remain
intact for all other participants. Any unblinding must be documented (including the reason for
unblinding). During the monitoring visits, the presence and state of the envelopes will be
checked. After completion of the study, the sealed envelopes will be returned to the
coordinating center, where they will be checked again for completeness and intactness.
If unblinding has taken place for any reason, the study physician will report this to the
coordinating center as soon as possible.
6.6 Methods against bias
‘The study will be randomized to account for potential confounding factors. All laboratory
tests as well as extraction of clinical data from colonoscopy and pathology reports will be
done in a blinded manner to avoid information bias. Data extraction and data entry (where
applicable) will be done by two independent and blinded reviewers. Discrepant coding will be
resolved according to standard operating procedures to achieve the maximum accuracy
possible.
