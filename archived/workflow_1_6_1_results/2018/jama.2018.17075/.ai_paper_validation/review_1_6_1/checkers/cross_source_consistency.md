### F-XS-001

**Title:** The two intention-to-treat bleeding P values exchange outcome assignments between the article and results supplement.

**Compared locations and document roles:** REPORT [main Table 2, PDF p7](../../../jama_cooper_2018_oi_180132.pdf#page=7), REPORT [eTable 6, results supplement PDF p10](../../../joi180132supp2_prod.pdf#page=10). The abstract's intracranial bleeding rates also appear at REPORT [PDF p1](../../../jama_cooper_2018_oi_180132.pdf#page=1). REPORT [statistical methods p3](../../../jama_cooper_2018_oi_180132.pdf#page=3) states unadjusted chi-square equal-proportions comparisons for adverse events within10 days.

**Printed values:** Intracranial bleeding is 47/260 (18.1%) versus37/240 (15.4%) in both tables. Main Table2 gives P=.70; eTable6 gives P=.43. Extracranial bleeding is8/260 (3.1%) versus6/240 (2.5%) in both tables. Main Table2 gives P=.43; eTable6 gives P=.70. In main Table2 the intracranial row additionally prints RR1.23 (0.43–3.5) and extracranial RR1.17 (0.79–1.74); those effect blocks are retained as source facts, not used to redefine the populations.

**Comparison logic:** Identical outcomes, arms, counts, denominators, time window and stated unadjusted test produce different P assignments. The [.695,.705] and [.425,.435] display-rounding intervals are disjoint. Thus display precision cannot reconcile them. The two-value reversal is one repeated pattern covering both rows, not two findings.

**Focus area:** B. **Proposed review domain:** statistical_results. **Proposed relationship:** S_C; the suspect effect-block association within Table2 also concerns S_W, to be assessed by the numeric/statistical lanes.

**Affected result or extracted value:** The unadjusted P value for increased intracranial bleeding, explicitly reported as an abstract safety result, and its paired extracranial bleeding comparison. The contradiction also affects which inferential block accompanies each bleeding endpoint in a data extraction.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Both tables attribute their P values to the same printed events; neither describes a different analysis. A reader must choose between incompatible outcome-specific P values or independently recompute the stated test. Both comparisons are nonsignificant, so no change in the paper's overall neurologic conclusion is asserted.

**Supported alternatives:** The supplement may preserve the intended P associations, or Table2 may reflect a different unreported computation; the paired reversal supports checking row assignment but does not establish a final correction. No raw database is supplied.

**Human verification steps:** Open both linked table pages, confirm row positions and identical counts, then identify the original adverse-event analysis output and which P/CI/RR belongs to each endpoint. Review together with any same-pattern count/estimate finding from another lane.

**Supported alternatives:** The7day report may describe a later operational extension, or may summarize optional normothermic management beyond the96h assigned period; alternatively AppendixF may retain an earlier duration. ### F-XS-003

**Title:** The final interim-adjusted significance threshold differs from the report's stated final testing rule.

**Compared locations and document roles:** PLAN [Version9 §15.1.3, PDF p84](../../../joi180132supp1_prod.pdf#page=84); REPORT [Statistical Analysis, PDF p4](../../../jama_cooper_2018_oi_180132.pdf#page=4). PLAN [2015 DSMC charter p188](../../../joi180132supp1_prod.pdf#page=188) provides earlier two-look context; PLAN [Version9 amendment pp178–179](../../../joi180132supp1_prod.pdf#page=178) confirms subsequent expanded monitoring.

**Printed values:** Final Version9 specifies that, absent early stopping, the final analysis would be conducted at±1.996 SD (P=.0459) rather than±1.96 SD (P=.05), after11 planned interim analyses. The report says2-sided P<.05 was used to indicate statistical significance for all analyses. It separately says no multiple-comparison adjustment and that secondary outcomes are exploratory. The earlier2015 charter refers to two looks, final1.975/P=.048, and nominal alpha=.05; that historical rule is not substituted for Version9.

**Comparison logic:** The final-plan paragraph explicitly contrasts .0459 and .05 as different decision thresholds. This is not ordinary rounding of a reported P value: the statement specifies an adjusted critical value and says it replaces the nominal1.96/.05 rule. The report states the nominal rule with no corresponding final-look exception. Lack of endpoint multiplicity adjustment does not explain absence of the separately specified interim-monitoring adjustment.

**Focus area:** D. **Proposed review domain:** protocol_report. **Proposed relationship/all relationships:** P_R.

**Affected result or extracted value:** The prespecified significance rule for the primary six-month favorable GOS-E comparison, reported P=.94, and the statistical-design extraction of its interim adjustment. The primary nonsignificant conclusion remains the same under either threshold; no reversal is claimed for any printed primary/key-secondary result.

**Why context does not resolve it:** The supplied latest full protocol expressly selects a different final boundary; the report does not disclose a replacement or distinguish nominal descriptive P values from the final decision boundary. A reader cannot determine from the supplied text whether the final rule was changed or its description shortened. The threshold is itself a planned decision rule within focus areaD.

**Supported alternatives:** The final analysis may have used the stricter boundary while reporting nominal P values and summarized its rule as.05, or it may have used the stated nominal threshold. A later published SAP may clarify this, but only its citation and limited update pages are supplied; no external document was opened.

**Human verification steps:** Establish the operative final boundary from the actual prespecified analysis specification and final analysis output, then compare it with the p4 statement. Keep the distinction between a decision-rule discrepancy and the unchanged observed primary conclusion explicit.
