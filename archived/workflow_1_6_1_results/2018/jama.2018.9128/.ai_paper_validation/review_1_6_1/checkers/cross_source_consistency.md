### F-XS-001

**Title:** Intensive-phase telephone-session duration differs between report and revised protocol.

**Compared exact locations and roles:** REPORT [DOC-001 p2, Intervention Description, final paragraph](../../../jama_barkin_2018_oi_180075.pdf#page=2); PLAN [DOC-002 p64, lines2393–2398](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=64). PLAN [DOC-002 p63, lines2381–2384](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=63) establishes the matched 90-minute in-person delivery. PLAN [DOC-002 p106, amendment dated12/18/12](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=106) discloses telephone makeup availability but no longer telephone duration. PLAN [DOC-002 p113, primary-analysis change summary](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=113) addresses statistical revisions, not telephone-session length.

**Printed statements:** The report describes a 12-week intensive phase with weekly “90-minute skills-building sessions via either in-person groups or telephone calls.” The revised protocol states that families can choose face-to-face or coaching telephone sessions in the intensive phase and that the telephone sessions “will be 20 minutes in length” because small-group discussion, hands-on child activity and cooking are omitted. The revised protocol separately specifies 90-minute in-person sessions.

**Comparison logic:** Match intensive phase, weekly session, same intervention arm and telephone alternative; do not compare maintenance calls. The per-session telephone duration is 90 minutes under the report sentence versus 20 minutes under the revised protocol, a difference of70 minutes per telephone-delivered session. With durations treated even as rounded whole minutes, intervals [89.5,90.5] and [19.5,20.5] do not overlap. This is not an estimate-versus-crude comparison. It does not establish how many sessions any participant actually received by telephone.

**Focus area:** D, planned-versus-reported randomized intervention dose/duration.

**Proposed review domain:** protocol_report.

**Proposed relationship type / all relationships:** P_R / P_R. The plan p63 only corroborates the distinction between delivery modes; it does not create a separate plan-internal conflict.

**Affected result or extracted value:** Intensive-phase telephone-session contact duration used to describe or extract the behavioral intervention accompanying the primary 36-month child BMI trajectory result (joint P=.39), including treatment-dose extraction by delivery mode.

**Why context does not resolve it:** The report sentence explicitly applies 90-minute sessions to either delivery mode; the supplied revised protocol explicitly assigns telephone delivery a shorter20-minute duration and provides its content rationale. Neither immediate paragraph supplies an amended telephone length or states that the report's90 minutes refers only to in-person delivery. The amendment list acknowledges phone makeup but does not reconcile the durations. This leaves the relationship between reported delivery and the supplied planned telephone dose unclear, although the primary effect estimate itself is not shown to change.

**Supported alternatives:** The report may compress a 90-minute in-person session and20-minute telephone substitute into one overall program description; alternatively, telephone delivery may have changed to90 minutes after the supplied protocol. Both are plausible descriptions, but neither is established by the supplied package. The relevant missing definition is the applicable telephone-session duration in the reported trial.

**Human verification steps:** Reread the three linked intervention paragraphs and amendment entry; determine whether90 minutes in the report applies to telephone sessions, whether20-minute calls remained the intended substitute, or whether an additional dose amendment is needed to reconcile the descriptions. Do not infer actual received telephone minutes from attendance percentages.

### F-XS-002

**Title:** School-readiness session duration differs between report and revised protocol.

**Compared exact locations and roles:** REPORT [DOC-001 p3, control-condition paragraph](../../../jama_barkin_2018_oi_180075.pdf#page=3); PLAN [DOC-002 p63, control-condition box and shared-content footnote](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=63); PLAN [DOC-002 p64, lines2407–2422](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=64). PLAN [DOC-002 p106](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=106), [p107](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=107) and [p113](../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=113) supply amendment/version context.

**Printed statements:** The report says the school-readiness curriculum consisted of “six 30-minute group-based activities” delivered concurrently with data collection and received by both groups. The revised protocol diagram says “Six45-minute sessions,” administered during data-collection time points, and its footnote says intervention participants also receive control content. Revised-protocol narrative states a45-minute school-readiness program for both arms at each data-collection point. Its later optional collection language is not used to change the matched six-session36-month comparison.

**Comparison logic:** Match school-readiness program, both trial arms, six core sessions through36 months and per-session contact time. The diagram and report both establish six sessions, so their planned core contact totals are6×45=270 minutes versus reported6×30=180 minutes, a90-minute difference; the per-session difference is15 minutes. Whole-minute rounding bounds for each per-session duration do not overlap. The original protocol's earlier12-session curriculum is not used as the applicable comparator.

**Focus area:** D, planned-versus-reported randomized comparator/shared intervention dose/duration.

**Proposed review domain:** protocol_report.

**Proposed relationship type / all relationships:** P_R / P_R. Plan narrative corroborates45-minute duration; optional later follow-up does not create a separate included issue.

**Affected result or extracted value:** School-readiness comparator and shared-session dose for intervention-description extraction accompanying the primary BMI trajectory contrast (joint P=.39), specifically30 versus45 minutes per core session and180 versus270 scheduled core minutes.

**Why context does not resolve it:** Both locations describe the same library school-readiness component shared by the randomized groups. The report and revised diagram agree on six core sessions but explicitly give different session lengths. No matching duration amendment appears in the supplied critical amendment register or statistical-change summary. Knowing that the report describes delivery and the plan describes intention does not explain whether this is an intended revision or a duration-description discrepancy. The dose can change intervention extraction even though no change in the primary statistical effect is demonstrated.

**Supported alternatives:** The delivered sessions may have been shortened to30 minutes after the supplied protocol, or the report may summarize only30 minutes of a45-minute scheduled activity. The package does not identify either explanation or distinguish scheduled contact from curriculum time.

**Human verification steps:** Compare the report paragraph, revised diagram and revised narrative directly; establish whether the core curriculum's applicable scheduled and delivered duration is30 or45 minutes and whether a disclosed change or different definition reconciles it. Keep optional48-month follow-up separate from the core36-month dose.
