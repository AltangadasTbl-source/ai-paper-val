Source evidence: Main Table 1 prints placebo DBP1 TT 82 (55%), TG 57 (39%), GG 9 (6%); vitamin D TT 134 (58%), TG 87 (38%), GG 10 (4%). Reported-versus-comparator: Main baseline placebo TG 57 versus supplementary initial placebo TG 59. The main placebo DBP1 category sum is 148, whereas the corresponding plotted initial category sum is 150. Vitamin D category sums are 231 in both locations. Placebo TT and GG counts agree. Category totals are exact derived sums, not separately printed whole-trial analytic denominators.

Reasoning procedure: Match allocation, DBP1 genotype, endpoint and zero-year randomization origin. Compare exact participant counts while distinguishing starting subgroup populations from later contributors and actual Cox model membership. Apply the explicit analysis-population exception to peripheral comparisons. Examine adjacent categories, stated imputation and variable-specific availability; then consolidate the two-person difference and its category-total consequence as one issue.

Calculation: 59 − 57 = 2 participants; 82 + 57 + 9 = 148; 82 + 59 + 9 = 150; 150 − 148 = 2; 134 + 87 + 10 = 231 in both documents. Participant counts are exact integers. Even permissive half-unit display intervals [56.5,57.5) and [58.5,59.5) do not overlap. Percentage-rounding context does not reconcile the matched integer count pair. No crude HR or inferred event-count calculation is imposed.

Affected result or extracted value: The placebo DBP1 TG starting subgroup population accompanying eFigure 3Q's relapse-or-death HR 1.00 (95% CI 0.44–2.24), and the placebo DBP1 category total implied by the displayed genotypes. An extractor could associate the display with 57/148 or 59/150 depending on the unresolved population relationship. No discrepancy in the printed HR, CI, P values or 417-person whole-trial efficacy population is asserted.

Why context does not resolve it: Neither Table 1 nor the supplementary figure/caption states a distinct genotype set, reclassification or additional participants. Identical allocation/genotype labels, agreeing adjacent placebo categories and agreeing vitamin D categories do not select a reconciliation. Baseline 25(OH)D imputation concerns another variable. Variable-specific genotype availability makes distinct subsets possible but does not define this particular two-person difference or identify the intended extracted count at once.

Neither explanation is established. The package supplies no participant-level genotype membership or changed-selection records accounting for 57 versus 59. The printed zero-year risk count does not independently prove Cox model membership or a changed estimate.
