## Design, endpoint and analysis-unit relationships

### SAP-IDENTITY / SAP-DESIGN

Original [PDF pp.2–5](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=2) and final [PDF pp.42–45](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=42) specify a block-randomized, observer-masked phase III comparative-effectiveness trial of mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) versus methotrexate (MTX), with prospective analyses and separately identified unplanned/exploratory analyses. Final version number and date must be matched to any plan/report comparison; original and final versions are not interchangeable comparators.

### SAP-PRIMARY / SAP-FOLLOWUP

[Original p.6](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=6); [final p.46](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=46). Aim 1 primary estimand is difference in proportions achieving corticosteroid-sparing success at 6 months, MMF versus MTX, ITT objective, definition delegated to MOP §2.5.1. Participants succeeding at 6 months continue their assigned agent another 6 months: Phase I months 6–12, visits at months 9 and 12; first-agent failure before month 12 removes the participant from study treatment. Matching main-paper keys: primary six-month success proportion, difference/OR, treatment assignment, continuation-success denominator and 12-month outcomes.

### SAP-OUTCOMES / SAP-OUTCOMES-F

[Original pp.7,14–15](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=7) and [final pp.47–48,55–56](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=47) specify all of: time to first inflammation control; time to first corticosteroid-sparing control; success at month 5 sustained ≥28 days through month 6; anatomy-specific posterior/pan and anterior/intermediate results; BSCVA baseline to month 6 or failure; health-related QoL (SF-36 PCS/MCS), vision-related NEI-VFQ-25 and IND-VFQ; discontinuation for safety, tolerability or lack of efficacy; macular edema presence and thickness; clinical and photographic vitreous haze on NEI and Davis scales; AE rates and serious-AE/tolerability discontinuation proportions; immunosuppressive dose reduction; VKH success. These are examined again at month 12 for first-line continuation and at rescue month 6. Twelve-month steroid-free both-eye inflammation control is additional. Final adds vasculitis at enrollment, treatment success controlling for baseline vasculitis, ≥2+ anterior cells or haze with ≥2-step reduction (2+→0.5+, 3+→1+, 4+→2+), baseline ≥1+ anterior cells/haze decreasing to grade 0, and dynamic-process/hidden Markov exploration. No numerical results are reported on these pages: these are prespecified variables and secondary/exploratory objectives.

Selected covariates [original p.15](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=15), [final p.56](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=56): treatment assignment; anatomical location intermediate=0, posterior/panuveitis=1; country; site; gender; age; baseline QoL, BSCVA, haze and thickness; final additionally vasculitis. Anatomy is classified by most serious involvement across the patient's eyes [original p.14](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=14), [final p.55](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=55).

### SAP-WINDOW

[Original pp.15–16](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=15), [final p.56](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=56): all outcomes included within −2 weeks/+4 weeks of Phase I month 6, Phase I month 12 and Phase II rescue month 6. These are outcome windows, not separate efficacy time points.

### SAP-EYE

[Original pp.11–14](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=11), [final pp.52–55](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=52). A=fully assessable without qualifying inflammation; B=partly assessable without qualifying inflammation in assessable area; C=fully assessable with qualifying inflammation; D=partly assessable with qualifying inflammation; E=absent or permanently too diseased to assess. Patient randomization gives 25 possible paired-eye combinations. Baseline eligibility matrix: eligible iff right or left eye is C; OD C row all eligible, every other OD row eligible only in OS C column. Baseline E contributes no eye outcome.

Six-month matrix: baseline A/B/C/D to follow-up A=success; follow-up B=conditional according to field rule; C or D=failure; E=Fail* (failure unless loss unrelated to disease). Baseline E row is NA in every follow-up column. Both eyes must satisfy success for patient success. Missing portions use worst previously observed inflammation for that field: anterior cells, haze or retinal/choroidal lesions. If unassessable at baseline and again final but other criteria met, that portion can be success; formerly assessable portions becoming unassessable use last worst observation; uveitis-related loss of an eye at visit 6/12 makes patient a failure. Inflammation assessments are baseline, week 2, month 1 and each subsequent 4 weeks through month 6, plus months 9/12 for continuation. Secondary eye-level vision, edema and inflammation-control analyses include eyes meeting baseline inflammation eligibility and use mixed models. The patient-versus-eye distinction must be retained in all matched denominators and clustering.

### SAP-RANDOM / SAP-RANDOM-F

[Original pp.8–10](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=8): four sites, two arms, blocks within site: size 4 selected with probability 2/3, size 6 with 1/3, random permutation within each block. Patient, not eye, assignment; sequential enrollment IDs. Original site codes 1=Proctor, 2=Madurai, 3=Coimbatore, 4=Casey; five-character ID is site digit + checksum letter + three sequential digits from 001.

[Final pp.49–51](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=49): nine sites AEHM, AEHC, AEHP, OHSU, KKESH, CERA, APEC, NWU, UCSF. Same block-size probabilities; final ID codes 1=CERA, 2=UCSF, 3=NWU, 4=OHSU, 5=APEC, 6=KKESH, 7=AEHM, 8=AEHC, 9=AEHP. Assignment moves to REDCap 18 January 2017; prior lists destroyed and all earlier patients' assignments retained electronically. [Original p.39](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=39) specifies confidential ≥8-digit seed, R generation and an algorithm description. Site counts, IDs, personnel and seed administration are recorded for completeness, not treated as clinical results.

## Statistical model, contrast and sensitivity relationships

### SAP-MODEL-O

[Original pp.16–17](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=16): six-month patient success logistic regression with treatment arm, country and site within country as fixed effects. X1=0 MTX and 1 MMF; Y=1 success, 0 failure. Lost to follow-up/dropout for other reasons is missing; discontinuation for intolerance or AE, including abnormal laboratories, is Y=0. Printed Equation (1) specifies logit(Yi)=b0+b1 Xi1+b2i Z2i+b3i Z3i+b4i Z4i, site 1 reference, illustrative two US/two Indian sites. Null b1=0, likelihood-ratio test 1 df. Two-sided α=.05 and proposed P value by permutation based on blocked assignment. Supplementary models: drug+site+drug×site; drug+site; drug+country; drug+country+drug×country+site within country. No site/country interaction evidence leads to pooled effects and CIs; evidence leads to site/country-specific estimates, site exclusion repetition. Page 17 names Wald T-test b12=0 for country interaction. These labels are retained as printed rather than harmonized.

### SAP-MODEL-F

[Final p.57](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=57): six-month patient success logistic regression uses treatment predictor plus study-site random intercept for primary analysis. Outcome/treatment coding, AE/intolerance failure and other-dropout missingness remain as above. Null treatment coefficient=0, LRT 1 df; two-sided .05; proposed P value by block-randomization permutation. Same four supplementary drug/site/country interaction models; sites or countries with <3 observations pooled together. Pooled versus site/country-specific effects depend on interaction evidence. This final random-intercept specification is distinct from the original fixed-site specification; version changes are map facts, not findings. Main-paper match keys are six-month success, primary adjusted OR/model, adjustment for site/country and P-value generation.

### SAP-SUBGROUP

[Original p.17](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=17), [final pp.57–58](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=57): prespecified anatomy by history: anterior/intermediate or intermediate alone versus posterior/panuveitis; supplementary enrollment anatomy: anterior, anterior/intermediate or intermediate alone, posterior/panuveitis (three categories). X3 intermediate=0, posterior/pan=1. Add anatomy and treatment×anatomy terms to base logistic model; null interaction coefficient=0 controlling country. Alternative models omit country, site and treatment×country terms. Within-stratum effects/RRs via RR regression; additionally age/gender covariates, repeated by gender, country and anatomy. Prespecified VKH, study-site and country effects [original p.20](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=20), [final p.61](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=61). Main-paper match keys: intermediate/posterior-panuveitis success, interaction P, subgroup effect scale and denominator.

### SAP-12MO / SAP-STEROIDFREE

[Original pp.18–19](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=18), [final pp.58,60](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=58): compare proportion successful in all study eyes at both 6 and 12 months, retaining successful first-line participants on same drug. Same model with described Wald procedure; test treatment and treatment×anatomy coefficients jointly equal zero. Steroid-free success proportion at 12 months is both-eye control off corticosteroids, denominator number starting therapy, logistic regression otherwise identical to primary. These denominator definitions are separate from a conditional proportion among six-month successes; mapper does not equate them.

### SAP-TIME

[Original p.18](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=18), [final pp.58–59](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=58): patient-level time to first steroid-sparing control is principal time endpoint, separately first control of inflammation. Cox PH, treatment and interaction predictors, Wald treatment test. Weibull, gamma and interval-censored sensitivity models; fixed site repetition, random-effects survival exploration; supplementary age/anatomy. One time per patient despite eye-level secondary general statement. Country coding 0 US, 1 India; additional second-site indicators within those countries are specified in these sections.

### SAP-VISION

[Original pp.18–19](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=18), [final p.59](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=59): BSCVA change baseline→final (6 months for successes, at failure for failures), two-eye scores but only eligible eyes with baseline inflammation. Linear mixed model with treatment and patient random effect, supplementary anatomy, treatment×anatomy, site. Maximum likelihood lmer; treatment LRT. Unassessable vision carries last observation forward; no eye at month 6 receives logMAR 2.0. Sensitivity all-time mixed effects controlling time; power/log/monotone transformations; additional age, gender, ethnicity and monthly steroid dose. Alternative final BSCVA outcome adjusts baseline BSCVA with otherwise same methods. Match main vision scale/eye count/change versus final outcome before comparison.

### SAP-QOL

### SAP-EDEMA / SAP-THICK

### SAP-BAYES / SAP-ALT / SAP-HAZE / SAP-EXPLORATORY

[Original pp.15,20](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=20), [final pp.56,60–61](../../../../joi190092supp3_prod.pdf#page=61): prior elicited from uveitis experts before collection for between-arm difference; likelihood corresponding to Equation (1), posterior quantiles, sensitivity to model/prior/influential observations. Exploratory clustered multinomial longitudinal anterior cells, haze and retinal/choroidal lesions at all time points. Final additionally dynamic process/hidden Markov models.

### SAP-NI

This mapping makes no adjudication or candidate diagnosis. Match primary risk-difference/CI orientation and reported secondary noninferiority statement.
