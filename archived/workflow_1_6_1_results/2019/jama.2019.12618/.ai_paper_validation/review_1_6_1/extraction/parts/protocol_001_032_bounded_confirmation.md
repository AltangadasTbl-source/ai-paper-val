### Failure, dropout, missing visits and rescue

Pages 15–17: discontinuation for efficacy, tolerability or safety may produce failure at any time. Efficacy includes failure to reach ≤7.5 mg/day prednisone and ≤2 topical drops/day by six months or clinician-defined futility of persistent uncontrolled inflammation. Intolerability can trigger dose reductions and ultimately failure. SAE requires immediate exit without rescue. Nonserious laboratory abnormalities trigger an immediate medication hold, permit one month to regain eligibility, and permit resumption if normal by then; otherwise failure and exit. Other nonserious events may lead to temporary interruption. If interruption leaves inflammation uncontrolled or prednisone >7.5 mg/day at six months, efficacy failure remains possible.

Dropout is explicit unwillingness to participate or death, not a missed visit or temporary drug discontinuation. Follow-up is attempted after missed visits and temporary interruptions. Drug-related death counts as failure; unrelated death counts as dropout, under medical-monitor judgment. These statements define event handling; no missing-outcome imputation model or statistical estimator is specified on pp.1–32.
