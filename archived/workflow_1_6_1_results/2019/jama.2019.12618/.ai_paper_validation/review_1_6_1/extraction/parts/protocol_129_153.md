## Extracted relationships and matching main-paper keys

### PC-01: ocular-inflammation measurement, definition and observer

[DOC-003 p129](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=129), MOP65: Figure 2 Miami nine-point vitreous haze has panels labeled 0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8. The grade images show increasing haze, without numerical patient data. Retinal/choroidal active inflammation is assessed clinically with ancillary imaging if needed; active vasculitis posterior to equator qualifies, requiring fluorescein confirmation under SUN. Macular edema alone does not qualify; bullous serous retinal detachment with any accompanying vitreous/retinal/choroidal inflammation qualifies. Text also specifies ≥1+ anterior chamber inflammation, vitreous haze and/or other retinal/choroidal lesions aside from macular edema as active.

Inter-observer agreement is assessed at baseline (Phases I/II), month6 (Phases I/II), month12 (Phase I), or treatment failure, whichever earlier. Two certified physicians measure anterior chamber cells/flare, vitreous cells/haze and retinal/choroidal lesions. Inter-observer variation will be reported; primary study ophthalmologist measurements are used for primary analysis. [p130](../../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=130) requires ≥80% open-book exam score for physicians/coordinators. Physicians grade five patients before trial; differences >1 grade trigger more grading until five consecutive patients are within one level for each measurement. Patients with zero inflammation in all areas by both graders do not count. Repeated annually, with duplicate measurements at month6 and/or12. Matching main keys: primary success inflammation components; inflammation grading scale and masking; eye-level measurement versus participant-level success.

### PC-04: laboratory collection and treatment failure
