### F-XS-001 — Five component-outcome absolute risk differences do not reconcile with their matched marginal risks

**Exact compared locations and roles:** REPORT [main article Table 2, physical PDF p. 7](../../../jama_aminian_2019_oi_190103.pdf#page=7), cumulative-incidence columns versus absolute 8-year risk difference column and footnote a. REPORT [methods, physical PDF p. 4](../../../jama_aminian_2019_oi_190103.pdf#page=4) says Kaplan–Meier cumulative-incidence estimates and absolute risk differences are calculated for 8 years. REPORT [supplement eTable 5, physical PDF p. 7](../../../joi190103supp1_prod.pdf#page=7) independently repeats all ten marginal risks and their CIs; it corroborates one side rather than introducing a separate conflicting relationship. REPORT [abstract, physical PDF p. 1](../../../jama_aminian_2019_oi_190103.pdf#page=1) describes all seven secondary outcomes as prespecified and reports their significant associations.

**Printed values and reproduced comparison:** All values are percentage points at 8 years; contrast is nonsurgical minus surgery. Each Table2 row shares its own outcome-specific baseline excluded population between the marginal-risk and ARD columns.

Primary, secondary composite and mortality ARDs reconcile under the same subtraction rule, supporting consolidation of the five component rows as one reporting pattern.

**Focus area:** B, estimate/measure logic. **Proposed review domain:** statistical_results. **Proposed relationship type:** S_W. **All relationships:** S_W. The report supplement corroborates the marginal risks and does not create S_C; the plan is not a conflicting side.

**Affected result or extracted value:** The absolute 8-year risk differences and their uncertainty for five prespecified secondary component endpoints. A data extractor could take12.9/4.2/1.8/11.1/6.5 from the labeled ARD column or12.1/3.7/1.5/10.2/5.7 from the displayed risks. The difference matters to numerical extraction of absolute associations. This observation does not dispute the displayed adjusted HRs or their significance.

**Why context does not resolve it:** The same row, same time, same contrast and same risk-set labels provide two nonreconciling representations of absolute risk reduction. No adjusted, standardized or alternative point estimate definition is given for the ARD column; the Cox model footnote applies specifically to HRs and P values. The supplement repeats the marginal estimates without identifying an alternative risk difference procedure. None of the five discrepancies fits displayed precision.

**Supported alternatives:** The ARDs may represent a separately calculated quantity whose point estimate definition is absent, or one of the risk/ARD displays may require reconciliation against the analysis output. The package does not identify which. CIs alone do not establish an alternative quantity or the applicable point estimate.

**Human verification steps:** Confirm the five rows and footnote against the PDF; identify the original8-year KM estimates for each outcome/population and the procedure used to produce the ARD point estimate; determine whether the risk and ARD columns use the same estimand and sample; reconcile the corresponding bootstrap intervals after the estimand is identified. No preferred analysis or final correction is proposed.
