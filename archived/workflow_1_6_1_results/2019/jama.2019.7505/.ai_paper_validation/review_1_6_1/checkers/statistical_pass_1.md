REPORT DOC-005 [per-protocol eTable8 p.29](../../../joi190055supp4_prod_16092.pdf#page=29) repeats the generic effect-estimate presentation for the separately named PP population. Exact complete vectors appear in S005, S008, S038–S065, S067–S072, S074–S079 and S097; their direct PDF labels and main specifications were re-read.

**Printed values:** The report says the intervention effect on primary incidence is estimated with “risk ratios” and95%CIs using a “Wald likelihood ratio approximation test”, with chi-square hypothesis tests. The only described site-random-effect model is a separate primary sensitivity analysis. Table3 prints H989/L987 as reference totals and primary211/233, RR.93(.83–1.04),P.23; hypoxemia49/134, RR.51(.40–.65),P<.001; hypotension313/170, RR1.43(1.31–1.56),P<.001; bradycardia98/59, RR1.27(1.11–1.45),P.001; pleural effusion43/21, RR1.35(1.14–1.62),P.005. **Rule and calculation:** An incidence risk ratio for H relative to L compares the outcome probabilities in those two randomized groups. With the displayed binary counts and reference totals, the corresponding incidence ratios are .903748, .364932,1.837453,1.657658 and2.043478 for primary PPC, hypoxemia, hypotension, bradycardia and pleural effusion, respectively; the PP primary incidence ratio is .885100. Those quantities fall outside the printed ratio half-unit display intervals [.925,.935), [.505,.515), [1.425,1.435), [1.265,1.275), [1.345,1.355) and [.915,.925). This is an effect-measure/analysis-identity check of the reported incidence contrast, not a comparison with an explicitly adjusted or model-based estimate.

A **diagnostic approximation of the apparent orientation**, not an asserted reconstruction of the authors' code, compares the probability of H allocation among participants with an event with the probability of H allocation among participants without an event: [H_event/(H_event+L_event)]/[H_nonevent/(H_nonevent+L_nonevent)]. It gives .935791 for primary PPC, .510737 for hypoxemia,1.431233 for hypotension,1.274329 for bradycardia,1.357955 for pleural effusion and .923735 for PP primary PPC. These values closely track the printed vectors across unlike event frequencies. Some differ from ordinary rounding of the final point estimate, so they do not establish an exact computational reconstruction. The primary incidence contrast and this allocation contrast are different quantities. The supplied statistical description does not identify an adjusted primary estimator, transformation, alternative reference population or denominator that maps the printed ratio to the declared incidence contrast.
