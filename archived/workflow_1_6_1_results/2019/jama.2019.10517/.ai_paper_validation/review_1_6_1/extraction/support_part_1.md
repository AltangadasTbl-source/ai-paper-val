491   A
         The duration of the Acute Phase is variable and can last between 4 and 12 weeks, depending on time to response.
492   B
        In-person clinical evaluation will be performed by a research psychiatrist at weeks 1,2,3,6, and 10 of the Acute Phase (more frequently
493   for inpatients) in order to provide intensive clinical monitoring during the early part of the study.
494   C
         Participants will have over-the-phone clinical evaluation by research associates (RAs) at weeks 2 and 6 of the Stabilization Phase and
495   weeks 10, 14, 18, 22, 26, 30 and 34 of the RCT, so as to allow for close clinical monitoring throughout these phases of the study, yet
496   lessening the burden on participants that would be associated with more frequent in-person assessments. During the phone
497   assessments, RAs will administer the SCID major depressive episode questions, the SSI, and the DAS conviction items. These clinical
498   assessments will be reviewed by a research psychiatrist. Participants with evidence of clinical deterioration will be promptly seen in
