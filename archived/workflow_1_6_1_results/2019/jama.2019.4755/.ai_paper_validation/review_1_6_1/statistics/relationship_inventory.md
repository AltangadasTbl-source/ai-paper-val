Raw difference need not equal adjusted interval midpoint. Agresti-Coull group intervals are specified but not displayed; they are not inferred from between-arm intervals.

Site SD tests use a stated0-vs->0 likelihood-ratio hypothesis; reference distribution, likelihood statistic, fixed-effects coefficients and covariance are not inferred.
