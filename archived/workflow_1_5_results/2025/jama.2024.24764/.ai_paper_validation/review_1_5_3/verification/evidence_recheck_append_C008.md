DOC-002 p. 18 defines length of hospital stay as `Date of discharge – date of surgery` and says, `This is calculated and reported for UK patients only.` DOC-002 p. 25 says, `This outcome will be analysed for patients randomised from the UK only, and so all patients randomised from Australia will be excluded from the analysis for this outcome.` DOC-004 p. 24 defines length of hospital stay and says, `this will be reported for UK patients only`.
- **Comparator printed value/text matched:** Yes. DOC-001 p. 8 reports one row for `Length of hospital stay after procedure (UK patients only)` with ratio of geometric means 0.91 (95% CI, 0.82 to 1.02), `P = .12`, and a second row for `Length of hospital stay after procedure (UK and Australian patients)` with ratio of geometric means 0.96 (95% CI, 0.88 to 1.06), `P = .21`. DOC-001 p. 1 reports the latter median values and ratio in the abstract; the same page states that 536 participants were randomized in the UK and 304 in Australia. The article narrative at [DOC-001 PDF p. 6](<../../../jama_atherton_2025_oi_240145_1741627844.85412.pdf#page=6>) independently labels the first result `In UK participants` and introduces the second with `When the Australian sites were included`.
- **Consistency rule applicable:** Yes. The analysis population is part of a result's identity. A UK-only length-of-stay result and a result explicitly labelled for UK and Australian patients are different population-specific results. This rule establishes the population difference; it does not by itself establish whether the second result was a prospectively planned additional analysis, a documented amendment, a sensitivity analysis, or another type of analysis.
- **Calculation or logical comparison reproduced:** No arithmetic reconstruction is needed. The planned population is UK randomized participants only, with Australian-randomized participants explicitly excluded. The published Table 3 presents both a UK-only result and a second result explicitly including UK and Australian patients. At the stated site-population level, `UK only` is not identical to `UK and Australian patients`. The abstract's 536 UK and 304 Australian randomization counts show that Australia was a material trial stratum, but the exact numbers contributing data to either length-of-stay model are not printed on the cited result pages.
- **Necessary inputs available:** The endpoint definition, the planned UK-only population restriction, the protocol's UK-only reporting statement, both published population labels, both reported ratios, intervals and P values, and the country-level randomized counts are available. Missing inputs or definitions are: any later or final SAP or amendment authorizing the Australia-inclusive analysis; the date and prospective status of such a change; the intended classification of the all-country result as designated secondary, additional, sensitivity, exploratory, or post hoc; the result-specific analysis counts by treatment group and country; the handling of missing length-of-stay observations; and a rationale for presenting the all-country result in the abstract.

The supplied cited pages do not label the all-country result as sensitivity, exploratory, post hoc, amended, or otherwise explain its status.
