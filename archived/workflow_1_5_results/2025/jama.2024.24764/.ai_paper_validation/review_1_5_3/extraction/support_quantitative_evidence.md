### SAP/protocol definitions, planned models, and matching keys (DOC-002/DOC-004)

12. **Trial population, contrast, randomisation and matching key** — SAP pp.6,8 and protocol pp.8,13,21-22. International multicentre phase III pragmatic RCT; emergency laparotomy; iNPWT/SUNPD versus surgeon's-preference dressing; individual 1:1 allocation. Minimise on contamination (clean/clean-contaminated/contaminated/dirty), stoma yes/no, and recruiting site. `iNPWT` in DOC-003 is the intervention corresponding to `SUNPD` in SAP/protocol; control labels correspond to surgeon's preference.
13. **Primary endpoint and source hierarchy** — SAP pp.7,18,25; protocol p.24; DOC-003 p.7. SSI within 30 days, CDC definition: infection within 30 days plus purulent drainage, organism from swab, wound opened plus specified sign(s), or clinician/imaging diagnosis. Binary yes if detected on day-7/discharge, day-30, return-to-theatre, or diary antibiotic item within 30 days; no only with completed 30-day assessment and no qualifying evidence. Diaries on/before discharge and return-to-theatre forms after day-30 assessment are excluded. Measurement is day 7/discharge, diary interim, day 30; day-30 assessor blinded.
14. **Primary-model estimands/direction** — SAP pp.15,25,40; protocol p.38. Counts/percentages by group; mixed-effects binomial regression adjusted for minimisation variables, centre random effect, other factors fixed, control reference. Log link estimates adjusted RR; identity link adjusted risk difference; both 95% CI; P value is for adjusted RR. RR<1 and RD<0 indicate better SUNPD outcome. If log-binomial does not converge: Poisson robust SE, then no centre random effect, then unadjusted log-binomial; final report should say why.
15. **Sample-size relationship** — SAP p.9; protocol p.38. Control SSI `25%`; target relative reduction `40%`, `25%` to `15%` = `10%` absolute difference; two-sided difference-between-proportions test, power `90%`, alpha `.05`; `336` per group=`672` randomised. With `20%` attrition/loss (approximately 10% 30-day death plus 10% further dropout): recruit `840`, `420` per group. Stata 15/two-sample proportions test.
16. **Inferential conventions and interim plan** — SAP pp.10,12; protocol pp.38,41. Two-sided 95% CI and two-sided 5% tests; no multiplicity correction. Proposed DMC Haybittle-Peto-like `P<.001` for interim major-endpoint stopping consideration; final threshold remains `P=.05`. These are definitions, not reported final results.
17. **Analysis populations/adherence** — SAP p.12 and p.38. Primary/secondary/safety analyses ITT: analyse all in allocated arm irrespective of received intervention. Per-protocol primary sensitivity: SUNPD received and retained >=3 days or until earlier discharge; control receives no SUNPD and surgeon's-preference dressing. More stringent SUNPD sensitivity uses >=5 days or earlier discharge. Observed adherence result is relationship 1.
18. **Time-window and sensitivity rules** — SAP pp.13,29; protocol pp.13,25-29. Day 7/discharge window days 5-10; day-30 window days 30-44 (+14). ITT uses all completed 30-day forms; planned sensitivities exclude early forms or use 30-44 only, excluding diary-only SSI, excluding certain return-to-theatre records, per-protocol, and two opposite missingness imputations. These match DOC-003 p.8 results.
19. **Secondary endpoint definitions and units** — SAP pp.7,18-20,25-27; protocol pp.24-27. Length of stay=discharge date minus surgery date in days; same-day=0; UK only. Wound complication and wound-related readmission within 30 days binary yes/no. SF-12 PCS/MCS at baseline/day 7/day 30; EQ-5D-5L index and health score baseline/days 7,14,21,30; pain and acceptability 10-point VAS, pain day 7/day 30 and acceptability day 7. SAE through 30 days binary plus count. Wound complications follow Clavien-Dindo I-V (DOC-004 p.48).

SF-12 and EQ-5D use repeated-measures mixed-effects linear regression with baseline/post-treatment times; fixed participant, treatment, baseline score, time and minimisation variables; centre random; categorical time; treatment×time interaction; unstructured covariance; robust sandwich SE. Mean differences at relevant times are derived from interaction model. This matches the DOC-003 p.3-4 adjustment footnotes.
