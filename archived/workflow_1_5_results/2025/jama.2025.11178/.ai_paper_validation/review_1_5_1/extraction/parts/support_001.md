## Mapped numeric and statistical relationships

### Protocol history and target denominators

- **N001 — BPI-SF item-count definition.** DOC-002 PDF p. 3 (change log dated 08/06/20) says the modified BPI-SF is **11 items**, correcting “10 items”; the pain-intensity subscale is **4 items**, correcting “3 items.” This is a definition/key for matching main-paper outcome descriptions.
- **N002 — Follow-up windows and nominal labels.** DOC-002 PDF p. 3 says the final windows are **80-140 days** (“3-month,” formerly 75-110), **170-230 days** (“6-month,” formerly 165-200), and **350-410 days** (“12-month,” formerly 345-480). Use these as timing comparators; do not equate a nominal month label with an exact day without considering its window.
- **N003 — Active intervention duration.** DOC-002 PDF p. 3 states the maximum active-intervention duration is **12 weeks**.
- **N004 — Health-coach qualitative sample definition.** DOC-002 PDF p. 4 says “up to 10” was revised to all coaches, then projected as **approximately 12**.
- **N005 — KP chronic-pain encounter rule.** DOC-002 PDF p. 5: at the **3 KP sites**, the criterion was changed to **2** pain-related encounters at least **30 days** apart.
- **N006 — Essentia encounter rule.** DOC-002 PDF p. 5: Essentia remains at least **1** encounter; this must not be conflated with the KP rule in N005.
- **N007 — Surgery exclusions.** DOC-002 PDF p. 5: an EHR exclusion concerns surgery in the previous **60 days**; planned/scheduled surgery in the subsequent year is also an exclusion criterion.
- **N008 — Enrollment versus randomization target.** DOC-002 PDF p. 6 (change log dated 02/24/22) explicitly distinguishes a variable consented/enrolled target of **2,380** from an invariant target of **2,331 randomized participants**; enrollment may need to exceed 2,380 because not all consenting/enrolling persons proceed to randomization. This is the principal denominator key for matching reported recruited, consented, enrolled, and randomized counts.

### Synopsis, design, and follow-up

- **N009 — Three-arm design.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13 (printed p. 1, Synopsis) defines a **3-arm parallel** comparative-effectiveness trial: online program, live coach-led telephonic/video sessions, and usual care; both active arms are compared with usual care.
- **N010 — Intervention dose.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13: participants randomized to either active intervention complete **8 CBT-based sessions**, approximately **1 per week**; delivery differs between self-directed online and one-to-one live coach formats.
- **N011 — Participant assessment schedule.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13: all participants are enrolled for **12 months** and complete self-reported assessments at baseline, randomization notification, and **3-, 6-, and 12-months**.
- **N012 — Qualitative patient interviews.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13: a subset participates in a **1-hour** interview at approximately **3-5 months** after baseline and **12-14 months** after follow-up. These are qualitative process times, not quantitative efficacy endpoints.
- **N013 — Stakeholder/staff interview samples.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13: approximately **20** health-care-system stakeholders across **4** study sites, and staff supporting painTRAINER or delivering virtual CBT-CP (approximately **12**), are identified for qualitative interviews.
- **N014 — Primary efficacy construct and time.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13: Aim 1 compares active interventions with usual care on clinically meaningful improvement in **pain severity (pain intensity + pain-related interference)** at **3 months**.
- **N015 — Preplanned secondary/exploratory domains.** DOC-002 PDF p. 13: pain outcomes, related quality-of-life outcomes, social-role/physical functioning, patient global impression of change, long-term opioid use, comorbid symptoms, high-impact chronic pain, chronic pain grade, subgroups, and mediators are planned domains; labels require population/time/construct matching before cross-source comparison.
- **N016 — Economic comparison.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 13-15 (Synopsis continuation) states cost and incremental cost-effectiveness are assessed for online and virtual coach-led CBT-CP versus each other and usual care; no numerical result is reported in this protocol section.
- **N017 — Qualitative aim.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 13-15 maps qualitative evaluation to participant experience and implementation/stakeholder perspectives. No efficacy statistic is reported.
- **N018 — Target-count relationship.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 13-16 (Synopsis and schedule) retains the distinction between enrollment target **2,380** and randomization target **2,331**; source wording indicates the first is variable and the latter is the planned randomized analysis population.
- **N019 — Nominal assessment-time relationship.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 14-16, read with N002, uses baseline, 3-, 6-, and 12-month labels for assessments; matching must retain nominal visit and allowable day window.
- **N020 — Planned analysis populations.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 14-15 identifies randomized participants and intervention arms; this is a protocol population definition, not proof that a reported paper table used a particular denominator.
- **N021 — Schedule of Activities/Table 1: baseline and randomization.** DOC-002 PDF p. 16 (printed p. 4) lists the study activity schedule. Baseline and randomization are distinct events; use the table to avoid treating baseline-assessed as randomized.
- **N022 — Schedule of Activities/Table 1: follow-up.** DOC-002 PDF p. 16 maps the planned 3-, 6-, and 12-month follow-up occasions; exact acceptable day windows are N002.
- **N023 — Schedule of Activities/Table 1: dose constraint.** DOC-002 PDF p. 16 lists active intervention activities with a maximum **12-week** duration, consistent with N003; this is a cross-source key for intervention-duration labels.

### Outcome, analysis, and population definitions

- **N024 — Planned primary comparison.** DOC-002 PDF p. 19 (printed p. 7) restates a **3-month** primary comparison of each active CBT-based intervention against usual care on pain severity. It is a planned contrast, not an observed result.
- **N025 — Pain-severity composite definition.** DOC-002 PDF p. 21 (printed p. 10) defines pain severity as the composite of **pain intensity + pain-related interference**. Any main-paper label “pain severity” must be matched to this composite rather than to either component alone.
- **N026 — BPI-SF scale definition.** DOC-002 PDF p. 21, read with N001, maps modified BPI-SF as **11 items** with a **4-item pain-intensity** subscale. This supports cross-source checking of outcome/item-count labels.
- **N027 — MCID status.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 3 and 21 identifies assessment of MCID for the two pain-severity subscales (pain intensity and pain-related interference). This is a planned interpretation rule; no MCID numerical threshold is established in the assigned excerpt.
- **N028 — Secondary outcome timing/domain key.** DOC-002 PDF p. 22 (printed p. 11) maps secondary objectives/outcomes. This is a definition/comparator record; no directly reported result, effect estimate, CI, or P value occurs in the assigned protocol page.
- **N029 — Economic endpoint labels.** DOC-002 PDF p. 23 (printed p. 12) defines economic objectives including incremental cost-effectiveness and QALY terminology. No effect amount is stated.
- **N030 — Subgroup/moderator labels.** DOC-002 PDF p. 24 (printed p. 13) identifies planned subgroup assessment by **sex, age, race/ethnicity, rural/medically underserved residence**, multiple pain conditions, mental-health disorders, and negative social determinants. These are planned strata/moderators and must not be mistaken for observed subgroup counts.
- **N031 — Mediator labels.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 24-25 lists theory-based mediators, pain catastrophizing, pain-related self-efficacy, and perceived support. No mediation estimate is reported.
- **N032 — Qualitative sample/process comparator.** DOC-002 PDF pp. 26-27 (printed pp. 15-16) describes qualitative objectives; it supplies process definitions only and no result-relevant inferential statistic.
- **N033 — Randomized trial structure.** DOC-002 PDF p. 28 (printed p. 17) defines the study as a three-arm parallel intervention design. This confirms N009 at the design section and is a matching key for whether an outcome contrast is active-vs-usual-care or active-vs-active.
- **N034 — Arm labels.** DOC-002 PDF p. 29 (printed p. 18) defines intervention arms and usual care. Preserve the intervention modality (online vs virtual coach-led) when matching labels; do not collapse both active arms.
- **N035 — Inclusion population definition.** DOC-002 PDF p. 31 (printed p. 20) provides participant inclusion criteria, including adult and chronic-pain/EHR eligibility definitions. It establishes screening/eligibility population terminology, not a reported analysis denominator.
- **N036 — Exclusion population definition.** DOC-002 PDF p. 32 (printed p. 21) provides participant exclusion criteria, including surgery-time and site-specific rules. It is a population-definition comparator only.
