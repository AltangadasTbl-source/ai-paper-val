## Pending Human Adjudication

Every observation in this report is **Pending Human Adjudication**. It is a reproducible quantitative reporting quality-control review, not a finding of study invalidity, author intent, or conclusion change. No severity, validity determination, disposition, or correction is assigned here.

## Executive Quality-Control Summary

Complete source coverage and two independent statistical passes identified 37 distinct candidate consistency issues (C001-C037). They include conditional participant-flow arithmetic, follow-up and workbook denominator/label observations, printed confidence-interval containment or endpoint-order observations, and matched cross-document differences. Small preventable defects can matter when downstream users extract numeric evidence, but this review does not claim that propagation, harm, or conclusion change occurred.
