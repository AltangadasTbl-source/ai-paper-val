## Scope and method

Direct-source scope: `joi250084supp1_prod_1765403089.61351.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-30, inclusive (30/30 assigned fresh-required pages). Fresh `pdftotext` native and layout outputs were saved in `preprocessing/DOC-002/p001-p030/`; both are glyph-encoded and unsuitable for transcription. CPU-rendered source pages in the same directory were inspected for the quantitative/definition-bearing pages. This is an extraction map, not candidate diagnosis or adjudication.

## Extraction limitations

Native and layout text are glyph-encoded. Rendered-page inspection supplied the transcription above. These pages are a protocol rather than outcomes results; values labeled projected, planned, or defined are retained as matching keys for later main-paper/supplement and statistical mapping. Statistical details needed to calculate a non-inferiority result (margin, alpha, confidence interval, model, and analysis population) are not present in this assigned page range.
