## Qualifying cross-source proposals

### XSC-001 — Incompatible stated economic price year within the protocol

**Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.

**Exact linked locations:** [DOC-002 p. 16, section 7.2.3](<../../../joi250034supp1_prod_1750956984.09018.pdf#page=16>) and [DOC-002 p. 17, section 8.2](<../../../joi250034supp1_prod_1750956984.09018.pdf#page=17>).

**Printed values and comparison logic:** Section 7.2.3 says the payer-perspective one-year cost-utility analysis and five-year budget-impact analysis will present “all costs” in **2024/25 dollars**, with no discounting. Section 8.2 describes the same payer-perspective, one-year modelled cost-utility analysis comparing SC with placebo and the same five-year budget-impact analysis, but says all costs will be in **2023/24 dollars**, also with no discounting. The analysis perspective, time horizons, and no-discounting statement align, leaving the financial-year label as the nonmatching field.

**Supported alternatives:** The two sections could refer to different protocol revisions or to separate price-year conventions that were not explained in the supplied text. No completed economic result is supplied, so this does not establish the price year actually used.

**Human verification steps:** Confirm the approved protocol/amendment history and economic-analysis plan; identify the prespecified base year; verify the year used in any trial economic output; correct or annotate the nonauthoritative statement if appropriate.

### XSC-002 — Economic comparison label “CS vs standard care” conflicts with the detailed economic comparator

**Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.

**Exact linked locations:** [DOC-002 p. 16, section 7.2.3](<../../../joi250034supp1_prod_1750956984.09018.pdf#page=16>) and [DOC-002 p. 17, section 8.2](<../../../joi250034supp1_prod_1750956984.09018.pdf#page=17>); trial intervention/control in [DOC-002 p. 5](<../../../joi250034supp1_prod_1750956984.09018.pdf#page=5>).

**Printed values and comparison logic:** Section 7.2.3 describes “Cost effectiveness and budget impact of **CS vs standard care**.” Section 8.2 says the Markov microsimulation will compare treatment with **SC** with placebo (standard of care), and the trial synopsis identifies SC as sildenafil citrate. `CS` is not defined there as sildenafil citrate and ordinarily appears elsewhere in the protocol as cesarean section, whereas the detailed economic section specifies the randomised pharmacologic comparison.

**Supported alternatives:** “CS” could be a transposition of “SC,” a locally defined abbreviation not visible in the supplied protocol, or an intentional but insufficiently explained economic comparison. The supplied package has no economic outcome, so it cannot establish intent from a completed analysis.

**Human verification steps:** Check the approved economic-analysis specification and abbreviation list; determine whether “CS” should read “SC”; confirm the treatment and reference group that any economic-analysis dataset/model used; amend the label or explain the alternative comparison if it was intentional.

### XSC-003 — SAP specifies adjusted component P values; main article reports no multiple-testing adjustment

**Category:** Statistical reporting inconsistency.

**Exact linked locations:** [DOC-003 p. 12, section 3.2](<../../../joi250034supp2_prod_1750956984.11521.pdf#page=12>), [DOC-003 p. 25, proposed Table 11](<../../../joi250034supp2_prod_1750956984.11521.pdf#page=25>), and [DOC-001 p. 4, Statistical Analysis](<../../../jama_kumar_2025_oi_250034_1750956984.08518.pdf#page=4>) with component P values in [DOC-001 p. 7, Table 3](<../../../jama_kumar_2025_oi_250034_1750956984.08518.pdf#page=7>).

**Printed values and comparison logic:** The SAP says that P values adjusted for the ten component comparisons “will be derived using the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure,” and its proposed Table 11 includes a Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted P-value column. The main article states: “No adjustment for multiple comparisons was made,” and Table 3 displays unadjusted component P values. Both statements concern the ten individual components of the same primary composite; they differ in the planned versus reported multiplicity treatment, not in a numerical P-value comparison.

**Supported alternatives:** DOC-003 is labelled draft v1.2 (25 October 2024); an approved later SAP, protocol amendment, or documented analysis decision may have superseded the planned adjustment. The article’s raw P values are not thereby arithmetically inconsistent, and no claim about trial conclusions follows from this observation.

**Human verification steps:** Obtain the final approved SAP/amendment log and analysis-program specification; determine whether Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment was abandoned, performed but omitted, or replaced; if it was performed, verify the adjusted values and reporting rationale; if it was changed prospectively, ensure the final report documents that change.
