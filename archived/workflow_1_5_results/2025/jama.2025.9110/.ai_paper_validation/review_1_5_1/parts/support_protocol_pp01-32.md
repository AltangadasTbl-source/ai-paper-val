## Numeric/reporting relationships

### P1-N001 — formula contrast and units (PDF p. 9)

Intervention (augmented dietary protein): Nutrison Protein Intense, 1.26 kcal/mL and 100 g protein per 1000 mL. Control (usual dietary protein): Nutrison Protein Plus, 1.25 kcal/mL and 63 g protein per 1000 mL. Both are stated to have 49 g fat/1000 mL and osmolality 340 mOsmol/kgH2O; both are fibre-free. The protocol describes formula choice as the treatment contrast, while other nutrition management follows usual unit practice. Matching main-paper key: augmented versus usual dietary-protein formula contrast; retain units (g/1000 mL, kcal/mL, mOsmol/kgH2O).

### P1-N002 — study time, participant handling, and outcome horizon (PDF p. 8-10)

Design is cluster randomised and cross-over. The diagram alternates ICU A and ICU B between augmented and usual care. Active recruitment per site is planned for 12 months; no washout is included because the trial is open label. Study enteral nutrition continues until day 90, ICU discharge, or death. Readmitted ICU patients within 90 days of enrolment who still require EN are recommenced on their previously allocated study EN. These are definition/denominator keys, not observed counts.

### P1-N003 — registry source and data availability (PDF p. 12)

The ANZICS Adult Patient Database is stated to have been established in 1992 and to hold >2 million patient episodes. This is source-database context, not a trial denominator. The protocol proposes data availability 3 years after publication and ending 7 years after publication. No candidate seed: these are policy/planning statements rather than matched outcome results.

### P1-N004 — outcome-variable and collection schedule definitions (PDF p. 13)

Baseline variables include APACHE II, ICU diagnosis category, ethnicity, clinical frailty within 2 months before first ICU admission, and highest/lowest biochemical values within 24 h of ICU admission. Post-intervention variables include ventilation hours, tracheostomy, renal replacement therapy, ICU/hospital durations and mortality, readmissions, and discharge destination. Additional EN volume is collected calendar days 1-5, 10, 20, 30, and 90; study day 1 begins on formula commencement and ends at 2400 h (a partial day). Biochemistry is closest to 0800 h on days 1-5 and 10. These are exact time/definition keys for matching result tables.

### P1-N005 — primary-outcome follow-up and safety horizons (PDF p. 14)

Primary outcome is explicitly named “days free of the index hospital and alive at day-90”; registry data and National Death Index linkage are planned. Follow-up is until death or 90 days after enrolment, with hospital discharge censored in the data-entry description. Adverse events are collected during study EN through 48 h after cessation; serious adverse events considered related to enteral nutrition are reported through day 90 post-inclusion. A random adherence audit checks the feed hung closest to 8 am on randomly allocated days around cluster changeover.

### P1-N006 — adverse-event definitions and reporting time (PDF p. 15)

An AE is an untoward medical occurrence and need not be causally related. Events reported as adverse events are restricted to those considered possibly, probably, or definitely related to study treatment. Mortality/outcome events are not separately reported as adverse events unless related/otherwise concerning. Serious adverse events must be reported to the coordinating centre within 72 hours of investigator awareness. These are reporting definitions, not results.

### P1-N007 — feasibility-trial comparator values (PDF p. 21)

The waiver rationale cites a separate multi-centre feasibility trial: intervention mean (SD) 1.52 (0.52) versus usual care 0.99 (0.27) g protein/kg ideal body weight/day; group difference 0.53, 95% CI 0.38 to 0.69. Mortality difference is -1.3%, 95% CI -17.7% to 15.0%, calculated with a generalised estimating equation. These values describe the feasibility trial, not the current TARGET Protein outcome dataset. Matching requires source/trial identity; do not compare them directly with the main paper.

## Statistical relationships

### P1-S001 — planned cluster-cross-over sample size (PDF p. 16)

Primary outcome used for planning: days alive and out of hospital censored at day 90; assumed SD 11.3 days from TARGET Calories. Assumptions: coefficient of variation for cluster size 0.5; six ICUs/clusters; mean 160 patients per 3-month period (95% CI 80 to 240); intracluster correlation 0.02, lower extreme 0.001 and upper extreme 0.1. The protocol states 60-65 patients per period per cluster gives 80% power and approximately 80 gives 90% power to detect a 1-day difference. Parametric total n=2560 gives 90% power, inflated 15% for expected skewness to approximately 3,000 participants and 90% power, with at least 3,000 expected in 12 months across eight high-volume ICUs. This is a planned calculation, not an achieved analysis; no candidate seed absent a same-estimand comparator.

### P1-S002 — planned primary and first-secondary analysis (PDF pp. 16-17)

For days free of index hospital and alive at day 90 and the first secondary outcome, the protocol plans individual-level quantile mixed-effects modelling of median response by treatment. Fixed effects: treatment group, period, and randomisation delayed-start stratum (Group 1 commencing May 2022; Group 2 commencing August 2022). ICU is a normally distributed random effect with mean zero and variance component sigma-squared. Treatment effect is difference in medians with 95% CI calculated by block bootstrap. This establishes planned estimand, adjustment labels, scale, and CI method.

### P1-S003 — planned binary-secondary-outcome model (PDF p. 17)

Binary outcomes are alive at day 90, tracheostomy insertion, and renal replacement therapy. The protocol plans GEE with logarithmic link, exchangeable working correlation, robust standard errors, and ICU as clustering unit. Any published estimate must be matched to this planned model only if population, outcome definition, and final analysis version agree.

### P1-S004 — planned time-to-event model (PDF p. 17)

Time-to-event secondary outcomes are duration of invasive ventilation, admission to ICU, and admission to hospital. Planned analysis is Cox regression adjusted for period and delayed start, stratified by ICU, with robust SE clustered at ICU level; estimand is cause-specific hazard ratio with confidence interval. Death before discharge/extubation is censored at death. This is a critical direction/censoring/measure-label key.

### P1-S005 — planned sensitivity and subgroup definitions (PDF p. 17)

Sensitivity analyses are planned for missing data and competing risk of death. Prespecified subgroups: invasive mechanical ventilation; age >=70 years; BMI >=35 kg/m2; and acute kidney injury before trial EN. AKI is defined as receiving new renal replacement therapy between hospital admission and commencement of trial formula. The protocol notes AKI on ICU admission was added after EFFORT publication. These are planned definitions; no P value display zeros occur in this scope.

### P1-S006 — displayed feasibility mortality estimate (PDF p. 21)

The displayed feasibility-trial mortality difference is -1.3% (95% CI -17.7% to 15.0%) under GEE. The point estimate is contained by the interval and interval endpoints are ordered. It is coherent on its own. `DISPLAY_ZERO_NOT_CANDIDATE`: not applicable; no display-zero P value is present.
