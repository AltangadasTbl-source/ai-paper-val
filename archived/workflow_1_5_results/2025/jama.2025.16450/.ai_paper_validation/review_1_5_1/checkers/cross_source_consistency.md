## Scope and method

I used the canonical maps as locators and confirmed the reportable observations against the direct PDF locations named below. Protocol, manual, and SAP values are prospective definitions or plans unless a final result uses the same estimand; they are not treated as final-result values merely because their labels overlap.

## Qualifying observations for human adjudication

### CROSS-CAND-001 — eTable 4 expands RR as risk difference instead of relative risk

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-005 [p.7](../../../../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=7) labels the effect-estimate column “Relative Risk (RR) or Mean Difference (MD) (95% CI)” and displays, for example, PDA `RR: 0.86 (0.75, 0.99)`. DOC-005 [p.8](../../../../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=8) says both that binary outcomes report “relative risks” and that `RR = risk difference`.
- **Direct observation:** The abbreviation expansion conflicts with both the same eTable’s column header/model description and the displayed multiplicative estimates.
- **Comparison logic:** A risk difference is an additive percentage-point measure; the table’s RR values and robust-Poisson description identify a relative-risk measure. The conflict is a measure-label inconsistency, not a claim that the numeric estimates themselves are wrong.
- **Supported alternative:** The abbreviation line may be a localized copyediting error; the header/model text could be the intended definition.
- **Human verification:** Inspect the production source or correction log for eTable 4 and confirm whether `RR` should be expanded as “relative risk”; verify that all footnotes and downstream table exports use the corrected label.

### CROSS-CAND-002 — First interim alpha differs between protocol and SAP

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-002 [p.29](../../../../joi250072supp1_prod_1761000786.68881.pdf#page=29) gives nominal alpha values `.00015, .0030, .0183, .0440` for the 25%, 50%, 75%, and final looks. DOC-004 [pp.26-28](../../../../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=26) gives `.000015, .0030, .0183, .0440` for the same look sequence.
- **Direct observation:** The first nominal alpha is printed as 0.00015 in the protocol and 0.000015 in the SAP; the latter values and information times otherwise align.
- **Comparison logic:** These are both stated components of the same primary efficacy interim framework. The first threshold differs tenfold, beyond a rounding display difference.
- **Supported alternative:** A version-specific amendment or a typographical zero in either prospective document could account for the difference.
- **Human verification:** Retrieve the signed version history/amendment governing the first efficacy look and the analysis code/boundary output; identify the approved nominal alpha and whether it was used.

### CROSS-CAND-003 — Final primary-analysis alpha differs between final article and prospective documents

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-001 [p.3](../../../../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=3) and Table 2 [p.7](../../../../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=7) state primary alpha `.049`. DOC-002 [p.29](../../../../joi250072supp1_prod_1761000786.68881.pdf#page=29) and DOC-004 [p.26](../../../../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=26) state final alpha `.0440`.
- **Direct observation:** The sources print different final nominal alpha values for the primary sequential-analysis framework.
- **Comparison logic:** Both refer to the primary BPD/death analysis and interim multiplicity control, yet 0.049 and 0.0440 are not the same displayed threshold.
- **Supported alternative:** A Lan-DeMets boundary recalculated at realized information/stopping time, or a post-version amendment, may justify `.049`; neither is documented in the supplied mapped text.
- **Human verification:** Check the final SAP amendment, DSMB statistical output, and primary-analysis program for the information fraction and alpha-spending calculation that produced the article’s `.049`.

### CROSS-CAND-004 — Trial center count differs between final article and prospective documents

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-001 [p.1](../../../../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=1) states `17` US centers. DOC-002 [p.29](../../../../joi250072supp1_prod_1761000786.68881.pdf#page=29) and DOC-004 [p.8](../../../../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=8) state `15` centers.
- **Direct observation:** The supplied documents provide 17 versus 15 centers for the BiB trial.
- **Comparison logic:** Center count affects the documented trial setting and may affect interpretation of the pooled-center model. It is not a population, time, or precision mismatch.
- **Supported alternative:** The network may have added two sites after the protocol/SAP text was issued.
- **Human verification:** Compare version dates and site-activation records; confirm the final number of enrolling centers and whether the SAP’s pooled-center structure reflects the final 17-site configuration.

### CROSS-CAND-005 — Prospective eligibility upper gestational-age limit differs

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-002 [p.4](../../../../joi250072supp1_prod_1761000786.68881.pdf#page=4) prints eligibility through `27 6/7` weeks. DOC-001 [p.2](../../../../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=2), DOC-003 [p.7](../../../../joi250072supp2_prod_1761000786.6938.pdf#page=7), and DOC-004 [p.15](../../../../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=15) print through `28 6/7` weeks.
- **Direct observation:** The upper eligibility threshold differs by one completed gestational week across supplied governing documents.
- **Comparison logic:** All sources describe the same trial eligibility definition in weeks/days and retain the same lower bound; this is a concrete population-definition difference.
- **Supported alternative:** DOC-002 may be a superseded protocol version and the later manual/SAP may embody an approved amendment.
- **Human verification:** Verify the protocol amendment/version in force during enrollment and check enrolled-infant eligibility records near 28 weeks’ gestation against that version.

### CROSS-CAND-006 — Prospective first-dose surfactant volume differs

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-002 [p.4](../../../../joi250072supp1_prod_1761000786.68881.pdf#page=4) prints first-dose poractant alfa `1.25 mL/kg` in both arms. DOC-001 [p.2](../../../../jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf#page=2), DOC-003 [p.7](../../../../joi250072supp2_prod_1761000786.6938.pdf#page=7), DOC-004 [p.15](../../../../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=15), and DOC-005 [p.4](../../../../joi250072supp4_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=4) print `2.5 mL/kg` for dose 1 and `1.25 mL/kg` for dose 2.
- **Direct observation:** The protocol’s first-dose volume differs by a factor of two from the final-document specification, while the later documents agree with each other.
- **Comparison logic:** This is the same first-dose treatment quantity and unit, not a difference caused by analysis population or timing.
- **Supported alternative:** A documented dose amendment or an early synopsis transcription error could explain the discrepancy.
- **Human verification:** Inspect protocol amendment history, pharmacy manual, and randomization-kit specifications to establish the authorized first-dose volume throughout enrollment.

### CROSS-CAND-007 — Severe NDI definition differs between Manual and SAP

- **Locations and printed values:** DOC-003 [pp.14-17](../../../../joi250072supp2_prod_1761000786.6938.pdf#page=14) uses BSID-IV cognitive `<70` or GMFCS `3-5` among severe-NDI components. DOC-004 [pp.33-34](../../../../joi250072supp3_prod_1761000786.6988.pdf#page=33) uses Bayley-III cognitive `<70` and GMFCS `4-5`.
- **Direct observation:** The documents differ in both the named assessment edition and motor-severity threshold for the same 22-26-month severe-NDI outcome label.
- **Comparison logic:** This is a definition/scale inconsistency. No current-paper NDI estimate is compared because that outcome has not yet been reported.
- **Supported alternative:** One document may be a later operational update or an uncorrected carry-forward definition.
- **Human verification:** Establish the version controlling long-term follow-up, confirm the instrument administered, and ensure any later NDI/death report uses a definition consistent with the approved SAP/manual.

## Non-candidate checks and limitations

- No candidate was created from the coherent `P = 0.004` or `P = 0.42` displays in eTable 3; neither is a display-zero value.
- Low-prevalence eTable 3/4 values called crude odds-ratio approximations are retained as explicitly qualified exceptions to the usual RR model. The supplied footnotes make the measure change visible, so this review did not treat the generic column heading alone as a separate conflict.
- The main article contains no raw participant-level structured dataset. Reconciliation is limited to supplied printed documents and their direct source pages; absent amendments, DSMB output, pharmacy records, and follow-up data cannot be inferred.
- The prospective-document observations above remain observations for human adjudication. This artifact assigns no final candidate IDs, correction, severity, validity judgment, or AI disposition.
