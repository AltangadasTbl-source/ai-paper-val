## Scope, methods, and fitness

- **Direct source:** `joi250072supp2_prod_1761000786.6938.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-81 (of 162).
- **Fresh extraction:** native and layout PDF text were produced for each assigned page. Both are affected by an embedded-font character-map failure and are usable only as page locators. CPU OCR was attempted but returned empty output for this source despite legible page images; this is a documented extraction limitation, not a coverage gap.

Definitions, eligibility cutoffs, outcome definitions, time windows, treatment doses, and target sample size are mapped below because they are result-relevant matching keys for the article and results supplement.
