# Bounded Review Limitations

- Review evidence is limited to the five supplied local PDFs. No web source, external literature, raw participant-level dataset, pharmacy record, analysis program, or external amendment history was used.
- DOC-002 through DOC-004 contain nonstandard embedded-font encoding. Native/layout text and OCR were unreliable for exact transcription, so mapped pages were inspected through direct-PDF renders. Exact-source recheck corrected four earlier transcriptions: C002 (`28 6/7`, not `27 6/7`), C003 (first dose `2.5 mL/kg`, not `1.25 mL/kg`), C005 (`4th edition (BSID-IV)`, not Bayley-III), and C006 (`0.000015` in both schedules, not `0.00015` versus `0.000015`). The earlier artifacts are preserved as provenance but are not authoritative for those four values.
- Individual/stratum-level observations, fitted-model coefficients and covariance output, exact interaction statistics, and exact CI algorithms are not supplied. Adjusted estimates and sparse-result intervals therefore cannot be independently reconstructed beyond the printed-source checks.
- Realized information fractions, alpha-spending output, unspent-alpha recovery details, and amendment history are absent. These missing records limit C007 to a conditional comparison of printed `.049` and planned `.0440` thresholds.
- Center activation/enrollment records and a final center-to-pooled-center map are absent. C008 cannot establish whether 15 planned centers and 17 completed-study centers use the same time basis or counting definition.
- No long-term NDI result is reported in the current article. C004 concerns a prospective endpoint-definition difference only; its numerical effect cannot be estimated without the governing definition and participant classifications. C005's originally claimed instrument-edition difference was not reproduced.
- C009 and C010 lack stratified estimator output or exact interval-construction details. Their scope is limited to effect-measure labelling and target-versus-approximation wording, not a claim that the printed estimates are numerically incorrect.
- No candidate is based on a display-zero P value. No paper-level conclusion change, actual downstream propagation, or severity assessment is established by this review.
- At the time of this audit, final report assembly, final manifest updates, token accounting, post-run hashes, standalone HTML rendering, and mechanical validation were still pending coordinator stages.
