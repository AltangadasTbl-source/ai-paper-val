# Main-article results evidence

- **Document ID:** `JAMA2025-16450-MAIN`
- **Source PDF:** `jama_ambalavanan_2025_oi_250072_1761000786.6838.pdf` (11 PDF pages; JAMA printed pages 1452-1462)
- **Scope:** Main article only; extracted from the retained normalized page text and retained Figure 1/Table page images. This is descriptive evidence, not an error assessment.

## Outcomes and analysis frame

- **Primary outcome:** physiologic bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD) or death before 36 weeks' postmenstrual age (PMA). BPD definition and five prespecified secondary outcomes: death, physiologic BPD, pragmatic BPD severity (ordinal), grade 3 BPD, and postnatal steroid use from 7 days after last study-drug dose through 36 weeks' PMA. **Location:** PDF p3 / printed p1454, *Outcomes*.
- **Planned/enrolled sample:** 1,160 planned (550/group plus 5% attrition), 90% power for an absolute primary-outcome reduction of 10% (58% to 48%; target RR 0.83). Enrollment stopped at 641 infants (55.3% of plan) after the 50%-enrollment interim analysis met the prespecified futility criterion. **Locations:** PDF p3 / p1454, *Sample Size Estimation and Statistical Analysis*; PDF p1 / p1452, *Results*; PDF p4 / p1455, *Trial Population*.
- **Analysis specification:** primary ITT robust Poisson model adjusted for center and gestational-age stratum; primary tested at alpha=.049 because of interim efficacy analyses. Secondary/exploratory comparisons were stated to be exploratory/descriptive (alpha=.05); the article does not give outcome-specific P values in Tables 2-3. **Location:** PDF p3 / p1454, *Sample Size Estimation and Statistical Analysis*; PDF p7 / p1458, Table 2 footnote b; PDF p8 / p1459, Table 3 footnote a.

## Participant flow and analysis denominators

- Figure 1: 5,353 assessed; 4,711 excluded (3,000 ineligible, 1,268 consent declined, 443 other); 642 randomized, with 1 participant withdrawn/redacted after randomized and treated in error following declined consent. **Location:** PDF p4 / p1455, Figure 1 and footnotes a-e.
- Of the 642 shown randomized, 323 were assigned budesonide + surfactant and 318 surfactant alone after the withdrawn/redacted participant. Budesonide arm: 319 received assigned treatment, 1 received surfactant alone, 3 untreated, and 2 withdrew before the primary end point. Surfactant arm: 312 received assigned treatment, 3 received budesonide + surfactant, and 3 untreated; none withdrew before primary end point. **Location:** PDF p4 / p1455, Figure 1.
- ITT primary-analysis population: 321 budesonide + surfactant and 318 surfactant alone (639 total; two missing primary end points excluded by model). ITT sensitivity excluding untreated: 319 and 315 (634); per protocol: 309 and 308 (617). Text reports sensitivity-population sizes of 635 and 617, respectively. **Locations:** PDF p4 / p1455, Figure 1 footnote j; PDF p7 / p1458, *Sensitivity and Subgroup Analyses* and Table 2.
- Textual recruitment accounting: 4,042 antenatally screened mothers plus 1,311 postnatally screened infants; among 1,311 consented, 670 eligible; 641 randomized (637 eligible/consented, 1 nonconsented, 3 ineligible). **Location:** PDF p4 / p1455, *Trial Population*.
