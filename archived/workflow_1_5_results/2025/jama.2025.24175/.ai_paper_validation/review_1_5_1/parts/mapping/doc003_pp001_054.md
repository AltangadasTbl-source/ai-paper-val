## Cross-location keys and mapper observations (not adjudications)

1. The recurring randomization denominators are precision immunotherapy 131 and placebo 145 (total 276), with immune-state strata MALS 25+23=48 and SII 106+122=228. These provide matching keys for eTables 5-15 and eFigures 1-9.
2. eTable 8 supplies the primary-endpoint SOFA availability/imputation mechanism; eTable 10 and eFigure 2 use the >=1.4-point mean-SOFA-decrease endpoint but have different follow-up/time and sensitivity populations. They must not be numerically equated without population matching.
3. eFigure 7B and eFigure 8B have identical printed six-row OR/CI/P content despite captions naming different outcomes. The separate records D3-S011 and D3-S013 preserve both exact locations and outcome labels for an independent cross-source/numeric checker. No candidate disposition is made in this extraction map.
4. eFigure 9B's APACHE interaction displays OR 0.11 with CI 0.36-3.42. The exact printed relationship is preserved in D3-S015 for independent statistical recheck; no explanation is inferred here.
5. OCR is a locator only. Direct PDF native text/render inspection corrected, for example, eTable 7 platelet SD on p. 18 to 135,123 (not the truncated OCR `135,12`) and visually confirmed the graphical counts in eFigures 7-9.
