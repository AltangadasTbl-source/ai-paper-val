## Numeric/reporting relationships

### Protocol population, classification, and endpoint definitions

- **D2B-N001 — enrolled classification population.** PDF pp. 38-40 describes 240 screened/enrolled patients split by ferritin threshold into macrophage-activation-like syndrome (MALS; ferritin >4,420 ng/mL) and non-MALS; non-MALS patients are further classified by HLA-DR/CD14/CD16 monocyte measures as immunoparalysis, intermediate immune state, or other. The protocol reports classification groups of MALS `n=48`, immunoparalysis `n=103`, and intermediate state `n=89` (total 240). Direct table confirmation: p. 46.
- **D2B-N002 — first-stage immune classification reproducibility.** PDF p. 40 reports reproducibility of classification on days 1 and 2 as 88.3%; the comparison is based on the first and second sampling days.
- **D2B-N003 — screening threshold and group sizes.** PDF pp. 39-40: ferritin >4,420 ng/mL identifies MALS; ferritin <4,420 ng/mL is used for the non-MALS pathway. The protocol narrative gives 48 MALS, 103 immunoparalysis, and 89 intermediate-state participants.
- **D2B-N004 — mortality relationships by classification.** PDF pp. 40-41 reports 28-day mortality of 51.1% (95% CI 39.7%-62.4%) for MALS and 21.4% (95% CI 13.5%-30.7%) for intermediate state. The source labels immune classification as an independent predictor of 28-day mortality in multivariable Cox analysis; model estimates are a separate statistical relationship (D2B-S004).
- **D2B-N005 — randomized trial allocation.** PDF pp. 41-42 describes the double-blind/double-dummy stage: 36 patients were randomized, 21 to placebo and 15 to personalized immunotherapy. Table 2 on p. 48 uses the same denominators.
- **D2B-N006 — trial mortality counts.** PDF p. 42 reports 28-day mortality did not differ: 11 deaths in the placebo arm (28-day mortality 47.6%, 95% CI 27.0%-68.2%) and 15 deaths in the personalized-immunotherapy arm (28-day mortality 80.0%, 95% CI 51.9%-95.7%). The narrative/table context requires human review of the printed arm/death wording because the stated death count and percentage appear to be presented in opposite-arm order; this is recorded as mapping evidence only, not a candidate diagnosis.
- **D2B-N007 — eligibility score mechanics.** PDF pp. 58-61 gives infection-site criteria and score components, including a clinical pulmonary infection score (CPIS) in which component values are scored 0-2 and summed. It also gives time-window rules such as infection-onset / hospital-admission thresholds (including 48-hour criteria) and organ-dysfunction criteria. These are protocol definitions, not outcomes.
- **D2B-N008 — randomization algorithm combinations.** PDF pp. 62-64 describes nine immune-guiding randomization combinations based on serial time points and ferritin threshold branches; all combinations are intended to determine eligibility for personalized therapy. This is an administrative/algorithmic definition, not a result table.

## Statistical relationships

- **D2B-S001 — classification-table tests.** Table 1 p values use ANOVA for continuous rows (`*`) and Pearson chi-square for categorical rows (`**`) (PDF p. 47).
- **D2B-S002 — randomized-table tests.** Table 2 p values use Student t test (`*`) and Fisher exact test (`**`) (PDF p. 48).
- **D2B-S003 — protocol analysis plan.** PDF pp. 39-40: qualitative variables are percentages and 95% CIs; quantitative variables are mean±SD or SE; comparisons use Student t test or Mann-Whitney as appropriate; prognostic performance uses ROC curves/Youden index; multivariable stepwise Cox regression is used for 28-day mortality, with 95% CIs and p<0.05 as the stated significance threshold.
- **D2B-S004 — classification/mortality Cox relationship.** PDF pp. 40-41 describes multivariable Cox analysis for 28-day mortality and reports immune classification as an independent predictor, with confidence intervals. The exact model covariate list and coefficient values should be transcribed from the associated figure/table during statistical pass.
- **D2B-S005 — display-zero rule.** Table 1 uses `p<0.0001` for APACHE II and SOFA comparisons. These are finite-precision threshold displays, not `P=0`; no display-zero candidate implication arises.
- **D2B-S006 — Figure 2 ROC/diagnostic definitions.** PDF p. 51 defines its ROC estimates as mortality prediction results, reports AUC with 95% CIs and p values, and presents sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, and RR for the specified <5000 HLA-DR/cell threshold.
- **D2B-S007 — Figure 3 time-to-event analysis.** PDF p. 52 explicitly identifies log-rank tests for the survival curves and univariate/multivariable Cox regression for 28-day mortality; HRs, 95% CIs, and p values are reported in D2B-N021.
