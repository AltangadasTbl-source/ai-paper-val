# Numeric Consistency Check

## Scope and method

Complete numeric-consistency review of global relationships N001--N088 in `.ai_paper_validation/review_1_5_1/relationships/numeric_relationship_inventory.md`. Direct PDFs remain authoritative; current-run maps and page-linked extractions were used as locators. Checks applied where relevant: count/denominator/percentage arithmetic, totals, subgroup sums, rounding, direction, population identity, repeated values, rate-versus-count distinctions, and measure/label/scale identity. Protocol design values were not treated as observed trial results. No legacy checker, candidate, or report output was consulted.

`COMPLETE` means every assigned relationship received a review record, not that it was adjudicated. All candidates below are provisional quality-control observations and remain Pending Human Adjudication. `NC` identifiers are lane-local only and are not stable candidate IDs.

## Provisional numeric-consistency candidates

### NC001 — Adjusted self-reported abstinence interval endpoint printed as 42

- **Category:** Numeric or arithmetic inconsistency.
- **Exact source location:** DOC-001, `jama_zahid_2025_oi_250093_1768590553.08463.pdf#page=6`, Table 2, “Continuous abstinence, self-reported only (ITT),” adjusted RR column.
- **Printed inputs:** adjusted RR `2.8 (1.9 to 42)`; same row crude RR `2.7 (1.8 to 4.1)`; Table 2 verified-outcome adjusted intervals are also of single-digit magnitude.
- **Direct observation:** Direct rendering confirms the endpoint is printed `42`, not `4.2`.
- **Rule/calculation and tolerance:** A 95% interval for a point estimate 2.8 may be wide, but the printed upper endpoint is 15.0 times the estimate while the lower endpoint is 0.68 times it; the adjacent crude interval and comparable adjusted RR intervals make a decimal-place transcription/label issue reproducibly plausible. No rounding tolerance can turn 42 into 4.2.
- **Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** A genuinely very wide adjusted mixed-model interval is possible; the supplied source does not provide SE/model output to exclude it.
- **Quality-control relevance:** The interval is a directly reusable effect-estimate field and may be transcribed into evidence tables.
- **Human question:** Does the source PDF intentionally report `2.8 (1.9 to 42)`, or should the endpoint be `4.2`/another value under the fitted adjusted model?

### NC002 — Discussion calls all deaths TB deaths

- **Category:** Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** DOC-001 PDF p. 8 Discussion (`#page=8`): “`4.8%` of trial participants died of TB”; DOC-003 `joi250093supp2_prod_1768590553.09463.pdf#page=6`, eTable 4: `32/52 (61.5%)` deaths attributed to TB; DOC-001 Table 2 PDF p. 6: 25+27=52 deaths among 1080 randomized.
- **Printed inputs:** 52/1080=4.81% total deaths; 32/1080=2.96% TB-attributed deaths.
- **Direct observation:** The discussion assigns the 4.8% total-death percentage to TB deaths, whereas the cause table attributes TB to only 32 of 52 deaths.
- **Rule/calculation and tolerance:** With denominator 1080, `52/1080×100=4.81%` rounds to 4.8%; `32/1080×100=2.96%` rounds to 3.0%. The 1.8-point gap is far beyond one-decimal rounding tolerance (0.05 point).
- **Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** “Died of TB” may be loose study-context wording rather than cause attribution; eTable 4 is the explicit cause classification.
- **Quality-control relevance:** Cause-specific mortality and all-cause mortality are different outcomes.
- **Human question:** Should p. 8 say participants “died” (all causes) rather than “died of TB,” or is a different cause-of-death denominator intended?

### NC003 — 178-message planned dose conflicts with daily/weekly frequency schedule

- **Category:** Numeric or arithmetic inconsistency.
- **Exact source location:** DOC-002 PDF p. 16, `joi250093supp1_prod_1768590553.08963.pdf#page=16`.
- **Printed inputs:** `178` SMS over six months; first two months `4–5/day`, next two `2–3/day`, final two `1–2/week`.
- **Direct observation:** The source prints both the total and schedule.
- **Rule/calculation and tolerance:** Using conservative 30-day months, schedule minimum = `2×30×4 + 2×30×2 + 2×4×1 = 368` messages, exceeding 178 by 190. Even using 28-day months gives at least 352; no ordinary calendar/rounding tolerance resolves it.
- **Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** Frequency units or phase durations may be misstated, or 178 may refer to a different version/schedule.
- **Quality-control relevance:** Intervention dose is a reproducible exposure definition.
- **Human question:** Which total/frequency schedule was intended for this protocol version?

## Explicit noncandidate decisions and limitations

- `N016`: the positive death difference is consistent with an unsigned absolute difference, and Table 2 does not define a signed direction.
- `N061`: 704.4 to 704 and 26.07 to 26 are within ordinary integer display rounding/truncation; no stated rounding rule supports calling this an error by itself.
- `N088`: `<.001` is a threshold display, not a literal/display-zero P-value candidate.
- Blank templates, graphical approximations, planned administrative timings, and contextual country values cannot supply a result-level numeric contradiction without a same-population comparator.
- Some protocol sections are different versions. Version changes were not candidates unless the same page/source made incompatible simultaneous claims or a matched repeated plan had no supplied distinction.
