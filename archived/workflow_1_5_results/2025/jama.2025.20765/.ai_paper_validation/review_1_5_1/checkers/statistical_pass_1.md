## Provisional candidate emitted by this pass

### SP1-001 — Phase-3 design-effect multiplication and “effective sample size” label require clarification

- **Candidate type:** Statistical reporting inconsistency (arithmetic/effect-scale label).
- **Exact source location:** DOC-002, `joi250093supp1_prod_1768590553.08963.pdf`, PDF p. 63 (printed p. 22), lines 1894-1907; direct page image `preprocessing/DOC-002/page_images/page-063.jpg` was visually checked.
- **Printed source evidence:** The page gives base `n=587`, then `N = 587 + (587*0.2) = 704`, `704/27 = 26`, `DE = 1 + 0.02(26-1) = 1.50`, followed by `ESS = effective sample size = 704 * 1.50 ≈ 1080 (40 subjects for each site)`. Its diagram also prints 27 sites and 40 subjects/site, which equals 1,080.
- **Rule and calculation:** Under the source’s own displayed multiplication, `704 × 1.50 = 1,056`, not 1,080. Conversely, `1,080 / 704 = 1.5341`, not the printed design effect 1.50. Further, multiplication by a design effect is an inflation of a recruitment requirement, whereas the displayed label calls the product “effective sample size”; no definition on the page resolves that label.
- **Direct observation versus diagnostic reasoning:** Direct observations are the printed operands, formula, product/approximation, label, and 27×40 diagram total. The two calculations above are transparent diagnostics; they do not reconstruct the source’s power analysis.
- **Source-grounded alternative interpretation:** The protocol may have first applied the 1.50 inflation to 704, then rounded upward at cluster level to 40 participants per each of 27 sites (1,080). This would explain the numerical gap as a cluster-rounding policy, but that policy is not stated next to the displayed equation. The intended word may also have been *inflated recruitment sample* rather than information-equivalent “effective sample size.”
- **Exact human question:** Was 1,080 intentionally obtained by rounding the 1,056 design-effect-inflated target upward to 40 participants/site across 27 sites, and if so, should the equation and/or “effective sample size” label explicitly state that convention? 
- **Relation provenance:** S026 is the direct relationship. S025 and S028 provide planning context but are not separate candidate records.
