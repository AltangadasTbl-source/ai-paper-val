## Scope, sources, and matching rule

This review reconciled all result-relevant occurrences represented in the current canonical maps across the complete supplied package: DOC-001 main article (9 PDF pages), DOC-002 protocol (63), DOC-003 administrative addenda (23), DOC-004 results supplement (15), and DOC-005 SAP (9): **119 of 119 direct-source PDF pages**. The review used the current main and support evidence maps plus the numeric (`N001`-`N089`) and statistical (`S001`-`S052`) relationship inventories as locators. Exact candidate evidence below was then confirmed in the direct PDFs. No legacy candidate, checker, verifier, audit, or report output was used.

An occurrence was treated as the same result only where its population/analysis set, time anchor, contrast, outcome component, model or estimand where applicable, measure/scale, unit, reference group, and displayed precision aligned. Protocol and SAP planning statements were not treated as competing observed results merely because observed estimates differ from assumptions or because a final analysis legitimately differs from an interim plan or amended definition.

## Provisional candidate requiring human verification

### Endpoint midline-shift boundary is printed inconsistently across otherwise matched final endpoint definitions

**Candidate type:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency; cross-document numeric inconsistency.

**Exact direct-source locations:**

- [Main article — PDF p. 3](<../../../jama_shotar_2025_oi_250033_1750956987.75881.pdf#page=3>), `End Points`: a homolateral CSDH with a midline shift of “**5 mm or greater**.”
- [Protocol — PDF p. 7](<../../../joi250033supp1_prod_1750956987.76581.pdf#page=7>) and [PDF p. 16](<../../../joi250033supp1_prod_1750956987.76581.pdf#page=16>), primary endpoint: midline shift “**≥5 mm**.”
- [Results supplement — PDF p. 15](<../../../joi250033supp4_prod_1750956987.77981.pdf#page=15>), EMPROTECT endpoint in eTable 4: midline shift “**≥5 mm**.”
- [SAP — PDF p. 3](<../../../joi250033supp5_prod_1750956987.78281.pdf#page=3>), `2.1 Main objective and primary endpoint`: midline shift “**> 5mm**.”

**Identity/matching rule:** Each occurrence defines the six-month post-index-surgery primary composite recurrence endpoint for EMPROTECT, in the same embolization-versus-standard-care trial population. Each names the same homolateral CSDH imaging component and retains the same symptomatic-CSDH, thickness, reoperation, and readmission alternatives. This is an endpoint-definition comparison, not a comparison of a protocol planning rate with a final effect estimate.

**Printed values and comparison logic:** `≥5 mm` includes a measured shift exactly equal to 5 mm; `>5 mm` excludes it. Therefore, the sources do not specify the same inclusion boundary for the imaging component. The difference is not attributable to rounding: the displayed threshold is an integer cut point and the inequality itself changes inclusion at exactly 5 mm.

**Calculation/diagnostic:** Let `x` be the recorded midline shift in millimetres. Main/protocol/supplement include the component when `x ≥ 5`; SAP includes it when `x > 5`. At `x = 5`, the first condition is true and the latter false. No supplied table reports how many adjudicated cases, if any, had exactly 5 mm shift, so the impact on the 24 versus 33 primary-event counts cannot be calculated from the package.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** The SAP may contain a typographic inequality difference, or it may reflect a threshold version that was superseded before the final analysis. The supplied SAP version-change table records a death-definition change but does not explain a midline-shift-boundary change. Conversely, the repeated `≥5 mm` wording in the main article, protocol, and results supplement may reflect the final operational rule. The package does not establish which wording governed adjudication of an exactly-5-mm case.

**Provenance:** Mapped as MAIN-N009/S001-S002, P1-N001, AS-S001/S026, and RS-S013/S051. The comparison was confirmed directly in the four cited PDFs.

**Human verification steps:**

1. Confirm the approved SAP version and protocol amendment in force for final endpoint adjudication.
2. Inspect the adjudication charter/eCRF or source imaging review rule, if available, for whether exactly 5 mm qualifies.
3. Determine whether any potential or adjudicated recurrence had exactly 5 mm midline shift and whether the boundary could affect the primary outcome count.
4. If the SAP is the discrepant document, correct its inequality or document the intended version relationship while preserving the final analysis definition.

**Status:** Pending Human Adjudication. This is a quality-control candidate, not a validity, severity, or correction determination.

## Limitations

The package supplies no participant-level imaging measurements, adjudication charter, or analysis dataset. Thus, this review can establish the printed threshold discrepancy but cannot determine its operational impact. Planned protocol/SAP material was matched only where it supplied the same endpoint, population, time, or model definition as a reported result; it was not used to infer unreported numerical results.
