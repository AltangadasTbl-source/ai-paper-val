C001 — Extraneous pressure unit after the usual-group SpO2 summary

Pending Human Adjudication

Candidate statement: The main article places mm Hg immediately after the usual-group SpO2 summary, producing 95.1% (2.4%) mm Hg, although the sentence’s respective pairing and the matched supplement table identify SpO2 as percent and PaO2 as mm Hg.

Category: Measure, label, or scale inconsistency

Exact source locations: DOC-001, jama_martin_2025_oi_250042_1753377747.91025.pdf — PDF p. 6 (printed article p. 403), Oxygen Exposure paragraph; comparator DOC-003, joi250042supp2_prod_1753377747.93025.pdf — PDF p. 21, eTable 5.

Source evidence: DOC-001 prints conservative-group median SpO2 as 93.3% (2.8%) and PaO2 as 71.5 (13.9) mm Hg, then usual-group values as 95.1% (2.4%) mm Hg and 79.5 (17.9) mm Hg, respectively. DOC-003 eTable 5 separately labels the corresponding rows Patient median SpO2, % and Patient median PaO2, mmHg, with values 93.3/95.1 and 71.5/79.5.

Reported-versus-comparator: The reported DOC-001 usual-group first value has both a percent sign and a trailing pressure unit. The comparator labels the 95.1 (2.4) value as SpO2 on a percent scale and the 79.5 (17.9) value as PaO2 in mm Hg.

Reasoning procedure: Apply the explicit “respectively” construction to the two measures and values in each group: first measure/value is SpO2/percent; second is PaO2/mm Hg. Compare the placement of each unit in DOC-001 with the unambiguous separate row labels in DOC-003.

Calculation: No arithmetic; logical unit pairing reproduced.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: Because the next phrase reports PaO2, a reader might visually associate the trailing mm Hg with that following phrase despite its grammatical placement. eTable 5 supports that possible intended pairing. A unit carryover, displacement, or production mechanism is not established by the supplied package.

Mechanical evidence recheck: The direct PDF location and comparator were found; the printed strings, respective pairing, separate row units, logical comparison, available inputs, and remaining human question were reproduced. The direct observation is the unit string and pairing; any typesetting explanation remains inferred. See review_1_5_1/verification/evidence_recheck.md.

Quality-control relevance: The printed unit placement is a candidate label consistency issue. It can make a narrative measurement summary ambiguous even though the matched supplement table supplies a clear measure/unit pairing.

Potential downstream evidence impact: If confirmed, a data extractor could copy the usual-group SpO2 summary with a pressure unit or preserve an ambiguity in a downstream evidence table. This report does not assert that such extraction or propagation occurred.

Human verification steps: Compare the approved article source and production proof against the direct PDF sentence and eTable 5; determine whether mm Hg after 95.1% (2.4%) should be removed or repositioned so it applies only to PaO2.

Source evidence: The contents page calls eTable 1 “Results of quality assessment per study,” eTable 2 “Diagnostic performance of serological tests – test combinations,” eTable 3 “Patients randomized by site,” and eTable 4 “Additional patient characteristics.” The actual headings are eTable 1 “Patients randomized by site,” eTable 2 “Additional patient characteristics,” eTable 3 “Representativeness of patients recruited to the UK-ROX trial,” and eTable 4 “Patient baseline characteristics by data collection group.”

Reported-versus-comparator: For same-numbered eTables 1, 2, 3, and 4, the contents-page title differs from the actual heading. The contents eTable 3 topic matches actual eTable 1, and the contents eTable 4 topic approximates actual eTable 2, while the first two contents topics do not identify their actual tables.

Reasoning procedure: Match each contents number-title pair to the table bearing the same number in the same supplied PDF, then compare titles. The four mismatches share one contents list, comparator set, and identity rule, so they constitute one candidate rather than four ranked findings.

Calculation: No arithmetic; four same-number identity comparisons reproduced.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: The contents titles may be retained from another document, shifted, or incompletely updated. These are possible explanations only; the supplied package does not establish the production history or intended replacement wording.

Mechanical evidence recheck: The contents page and all four table headings were found directly; all eight number-title pairs and the four identity comparisons were reproduced. The direct observation is the mismatch. A stale-template or shift explanation remains inferred. See review_1_5_1/verification/evidence_recheck.md.

Quality-control relevance: The contents entries do not provide reliable navigation to the same-numbered quantitative tables in the printed supplement.

Potential downstream evidence impact: If confirmed, a reviewer or extractor relying on the contents page could open or cite the wrong table when locating quantitative material. This report does not assert that mis-citation or evidence propagation occurred.

Reasoning procedure: Identify the two destinations joined by “and” in the sentence offering further details. Confirm whether each destination can be located in the direct PDF. Section 3.2 resolves, whereas the second destination does not.

Calculation: No arithmetic; one of two stated reference destinations is identifiable and the other is not.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: Sections 3.2 and 3.2.2 may already supply sufficient detail to apply the rules. The wording nevertheless promises a second source of further details. The intended target, its content, and an unrefreshed cross-reference mechanism are not established by the supplied package.
