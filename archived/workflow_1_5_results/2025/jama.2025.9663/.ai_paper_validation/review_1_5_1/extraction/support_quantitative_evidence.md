## DOC-002: protocol/SAP relationship consolidation

All DOC-002 quantities are prospective unless explicitly described as external historical/background data. They are not observed UK-ROX results. The applicable original protocol versions are v1.1 (14 December 2020), v1.8 (14 October 2024), and SHEAP/SAP v1.0 linked to protocol v1.5 (12 April 2022), with SAP v1.1 dated 25 November 2024. Cross-document comparison must retain this version distinction.

### Planning population, intervention, endpoints, and collection

- **D2A-N001--N014; D2B-N001; D2C-N001--N008; D2D-N001--N004; D2E-N001--N004.** The protocol/SAP identifies ISRCTN13384956; a planned 16,500-person, 1:1 conservative-versus-usual oxygen trial; adults aged at least 18 receiving invasive ventilation and supplemental oxygen/FIO2 >0.21; 90-day all-cause mortality as primary clinical endpoint; 90-day incremental cost/QALY/NMB as primary economic endpoint; ICU/hospital mortality (censored at 90 days), 60-day and one-year mortality, ICU/hospital duration, 90-day HrQoL, and (added in v1.8/SAP v1.1) ordinal 30-day days alive and free from organ support. Conservative target is SpO2 90 plus/minus 2% (88%-92%), with lower alarm 88%, upper alarm 92% while oxygen is used, and treatment until ICU discharge or day 90. The earlier v1.1 target 90%-93% was explicitly amended. Usual care has no upper SpO2 alarm. Basic/enhanced data collection is planned as 14,000/2,500 (about 15%), including first 10 per site and a subsequent random sample.
- **D2A-N002, N008--N011; D2C-N002--N006; D2E-N003.** Eligibility, 90-day prior-randomisation exclusion, ECMO and clinician exclusions, consent windows (randomisation within 12 hours; deferred approach 24-48 hours), safety windows (through ICU discharge or 90 days), 24-hour SAE notification/15-calendar-day REC deadline, 90-day questionnaire and 1:1 thank-you-card SWAT, and enhanced-data selection/blocking are operational definitions rather than results.
- **D2A-N003--N007, N013--N015; D2B-N004--N006; D2C-N007, N011--N014; D2E-N001.** Economic 90-day/lifetime definitions, 20,000 GBP per QALY threshold, pilot traffic-light rules, monitoring/retention, historical background, protocol version history, administrative pages, and original data-collection schedules are retained. Administrative/references/no-result units: DOC-002 pp. 1-6, 9-11, 32-41 where not otherwise noted, 74-80, 85-86, 114-115, and 134-136.

### Planning sample size, interim, model, and statistical definitions

- **D2A-N012--N013; D2B-N002--N003; D2C-N009--N010; D2E-N002.** Version-specific planned assumptions include either 34% to 31.5% or the later 37% to 34.5% 90-day mortality scenario, a 2.5-percentage-point absolute reduction, 90% power, alpha 0.05, target 16,500, allowance 5% or 6%, and interim information sizes 4,500/10,000 with Peto-Haybittle P<0.001. The different baseline scenarios and allowances are prospective version distinctions, not observed-result contradictions. Mapper arithmetic checks found 15,444/(1-.06)=16,429.8 compatible with rounded 16,500 and 2,500/16,500 compatible with rounded 15%.
- **D2A-S001--S003; D2B-S001--S004; D2C-S001--S007; D2D-S001--S002; D2E-S001--S004.** Planned primary ITT mortality analysis, site/random-effect or multilevel adjustment, absolute/relative risks, binary/continuous/duration/survival models, prespecified interaction analysis, economic ITT/95% CI approach, pilot stopping/traffic-light rules, global two-sided/P<.05/95% CI/no-multiplicity convention, re-randomisation after at least 90 days, missing-data plans, and version/outcome constraints are retained as prospective statistical relationships. No DOC-002 shard reports a completed UK-ROX effect estimate, CI, P value, event count, or realised sensitivity/subgroup result.
