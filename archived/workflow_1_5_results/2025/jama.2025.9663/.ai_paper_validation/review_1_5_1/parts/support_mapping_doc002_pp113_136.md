## Numeric/reporting relationships

### D2E-N001 — pilot separation/adherence threshold definitions (PDF p. 118)

Table 1 defines traffic-light boundaries and the associated FiO2/SpO2/adherence calculations exactly as mapped above. The 22%/23%-as-21% convention applies to measurement-error handling for protocol-deviation identification. This relationship is a definition/threshold source for matching results, not an observed result.

### D2E-N002 — sample-size and recruitment relation (PDF p. 117)

The printed design relation is 37% anticipated mortality, 2.5 percentage-point absolute reduction to 34.5% (6.8% relative reduction), 90% power, 5% alpha, N=15,444, and N=16,500 after 6% allowance. This is internally directionally coherent as a planned calculation; no candidate is registered from this mapping.

### D2E-N003 — enhanced-data subset sampling relation (PDF p. 119)

The SAP states N=2,500 (15%) enhanced collection: first 10 patients per site (~1,000) plus random subsequent patients (~1,500), selected with 10% probability using site/treatment block lists of size 10. Approximation marks are printed for component counts. This is planning metadata, not an observed denominator.

### D2E-N004 — oxygen-exposure scale conversion (PDF p. 120)

The displayed conversion is one hour at 100% O2 = two hours at 60.5% O2 = one 100%-equivalent hour. This is the stated cumulative-exposure scale.

## Statistical relationships

### D2E-S001 — global inference convention (PDF p. 120)

Two-sided statistical tests, P<0.05 unless otherwise specified, 95% CIs, and no adjustment for multiple testing. Interim primary-endpoint stopping threshold is separately P<0.001 (PDF p. 119).

### D2E-S002 — primary-outcome estimand and interim rule (PDF pp. 119, 124, 126)

Primary clinical outcome is death from any cause by 90 days after randomisation. Interim analyses at N=4,500 and N=10,000 use Peto-Haybittle P<0.001 for early benefit/harm, with no conditional-power analysis or futility stopping. Final primary comparison is planned for ITT, adjusted for site, Mega-ROX subgroup, and calendar time, reported with 95% CI and P value.

### D2E-S003 — analysis-population/re-randomisation definition (PDF p. 121)

ITT analyses use initial assignment and include all participants with known primary outcome regardless of adherence; re-randomisation can occur after >=90 days, so repeat representations are explicitly planned.

### D2E-S004 — prespecified subgroup and economic relations (PDF pp. 127-131)

Subgroup interaction/heterogeneity and cost-effectiveness/lifetime/sensitivity analyses are planned only; these pages contain no reported effect estimates, intervals, P values, or results to reconcile.
