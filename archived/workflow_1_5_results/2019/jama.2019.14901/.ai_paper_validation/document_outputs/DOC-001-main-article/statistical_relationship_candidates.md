# Document-grounded statistical-relationship candidates

**Audit scope.** DOC-001-main-article (`jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf`), PDF pp. 1-9, and DOC-003-results-supplement (`joi190108supp2_prod.pdf`), PDF pp. 1-9.  This review used the main-article and result-relevant-supplement evidence maps and their linked extracted pages only.  No protocol, SAP, administrative document, or external source was used.  The main article says that day-7 postextubation respiratory-failure rates and reintubation rates at predefined times were compared by a chi-square test (DOC-001 PDF p. 4 / printed p. 1468, **Statistical Analysis**, para. 2); it does not specify details such as a continuity correction.

## Candidate 1 — Different reported P values for the identical day-7 respiratory-failure comparison

- **Category / status:** Statistical reporting inconsistency — **Uncertain** (a reporting discrepancy is verified; the available materials do not identify which displayed P value, if either, results from the intended chi-square implementation).
- **Issue statement:** The same day-7 postextubation respiratory-failure comparison is reported with P = .01 in the main article but P = .02 in the results supplement.
- **Main-article evidence:** DOC-001, `jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf`, PDF p. 8 / printed p. 1472, **Table 2**, “Postextubation respiratory failure at day 7”: HFNO alone **88 (29)** of 302; HFNO+NIV **70 (21)** of 339; absolute difference **−8.5% (95% CI, −15.2% to −1.8%)**; **P = .01**.  The narrative repeats the endpoint and P value at PDF p. 6 / printed p. 1470, **Results—Secondary Outcomes**, para. 1 (21% vs 29%; **P = .01**).
- **Supplement evidence:** DOC-003, `joi190108supp2_prod.pdf`, PDF p. 2, **eTable 1**, “Post-extubation respiratory failure at day 7, No. (%)”: HFNO alone **88 (29)** of 302; HFNO+NIV **70 (21)** of 339; **P = .02**.
- **Direct comparison and logical basis:** The endpoint label, time point, randomized-group labels, numerators, denominators, and displayed group percentages are the same: `(88/302, 70/339)`.  Therefore the two reported P values purport to describe the same group comparison but differ by .01.  The reported confidence interval excludes the null and both P values are below .05, so the source materials alone cannot determine whether the difference reflects rounding or an unstated variation in chi-square implementation; the stated method does not give enough detail to resolve that model-dependent question.
- **Bounded impact:** Neither displayed P value changes the stated nominal-significance conclusion or CI direction, but the article package supplies no single reproducible P value for this endpoint.
- **Human verification:** 1. In the cited Table 2 and eTable 1 rows, confirm the identical endpoint and counts. 2. Check the analysis output or prespecified test settings for continuity correction/other implementation details. 3. The candidate is resolved if one P value is confirmed as a deliberately distinct, labelled analysis; otherwise standardize the reported P value.

## Candidate 2 — Main-text point estimate conflicts with its repeated table estimate and the stated counts

- **Category / status:** Statistical reporting inconsistency — **Candidate supported by direct arithmetic**.
- **Issue statement:** For day-7 postextubation respiratory failure, the main-text narrative gives an absolute difference of −8.7 percentage points, whereas Table 2 gives −8.5 percentage points for the same counts, CI, and P value; the stated counts reproduce −8.5 percentage points.
- **Narrative evidence:** DOC-001, `jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf`, PDF p. 6 / printed p. 1470, **Results—Secondary Outcomes**, para. 1: “21% vs 29%; difference, **−8.7% [95% CI, −15.2% to −1.8%]; P = .01**.”
- **Table evidence:** DOC-001, `jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf`, PDF p. 8 / printed p. 1472, **Table 2**, “Postextubation respiratory failure at day 7”: HFNO alone **88/302 (29%)**, HFNO+NIV **70/339 (21%)**; absolute difference **−8.5% (95% CI, −15.2% to −1.8%)**; **P = .01**.  DOC-003, `joi190108supp2_prod.pdf`, PDF p. 2, **eTable 1**, independently repeats the same counts and percentages.
- **Calculation / logical basis:** The table defines the contrast as HFNO+NIV minus HFNO alone.  Using the reported counts and denominators: `(70 / 339 − 88 / 302) × 100 = −8.490...` percentage points, which rounds to **−8.5** to one decimal place.  The table's −8.5 therefore agrees with its data.  The narrative instead supplies −8.7 while repeating the same CI and P value; no alternate estimator or adjustment is labelled in the narrative.  This check is arithmetic and does not rely on a model-dependent CI-symmetry assumption.
- **Bounded impact:** The direction (lower respiratory failure with HFNO+NIV), CI exclusion of zero, and reported P-value interpretation are unchanged.  The point estimate for this secondary outcome is internally inconsistent by 0.2 percentage points.
- **Human verification:** 1. Confirm the counts, denominators, and contrast order in Table 2 and eTable 1. 2. Recompute the unadjusted risk difference using the formula above. 3. Check whether the narrative’s −8.7 came from a separately adjusted or otherwise distinct analysis; if none is documented, correct or qualify the narrative point estimate.

## Candidate 3 — Nonhypercapnic-subgroup narrative P value does not match the supplement’s reported exact value under ordinary rounding

- **Category / status:** Statistical reporting inconsistency — **Candidate supported by repeated reported values; limited impact**.
- **Issue statement:** The main-text nonhypercapnic subgroup narrative reports P = .10 for the day-7 reintubation comparison, while the result-relevant supplement reports P = .1057 for the same counts and contrast; .1057 rounds to .11, not .10, to two decimal places.
- **Main-article evidence:** DOC-001, `jama_thille_2019_oi_190108.pdf`, PDF p. 7 / printed p. 1471, **Results—Subgroup Analysis and Additional Analyses**, para. 1: among the **530** patients with PaCO2 **≤45 mm Hg**, reintubation was 13% (HFNO+NIV) vs 18% (HFNO alone), difference **−5.0% (95% CI, −11.2% to 1.1%)**; **P = .10**.
- **Supplement evidence:** DOC-003, `joi190108supp2_prod.pdf`, PDF p. 7, **eTable 4**, “Reintubation at day 7, No. (%)”: HFNO alone **45/254 (18%)**; HFNO+NIV **35/276 (13%)**; difference **−5.0% (95% CI, −11.2% to 1.1%)**; **P = .1057**.  The subgroup label and group totals match the main-text statement (`254 + 276 = 530`).
- **Direct comparison and logical basis:** The outcome, PaCO2 subgroup label, treatment counts, contrast, and CI are repeated exactly.  Applying ordinary nearest-hundredth rounding to the supplement’s displayed P value gives `.1057 → .11`; it does not give `.10`.  The eFigure at DOC-001 PDF p. 8 / printed p. 1472, **Figure 3B**, labels a *log-rank* P value of .11.  Because the figure explicitly names a different test, it is corroborative of the rounding concern only and is not treated as proof that the narrative and eTable must use identical test outputs.
- **Bounded impact:** All reported values remain above .05 and the CI crosses zero; the subgroup’s stated nonsignificant interpretation is unchanged.  The discrepancy affects precision/reproducibility of the reported P value, not the sign or threshold conclusion.
- **Human verification:** 1. Confirm the eTable 4 row and the main-text subgroup sentence. 2. Check the statistical output and journal rounding rule for the narrative’s P value. 3. The candidate is resolved if .10 is tied to a separately specified analysis; otherwise revise it to the value/rounding supported by the identified analysis.

## No additional candidates retained

The remaining auditable point-estimate/CI/null/P relationships in the evidence maps were directionally and threshold-consistent at their reported precision.  In particular, no candidate was made from CI symmetry or from apparent differences between labelled chi-square and log-rank P values, because those would require model/test details not supplied in the audited result materials.
