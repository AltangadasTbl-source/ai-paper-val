## Intervention, outcome definition, and analysis quantities

| Location | Relationship/value and scale |
|---|---|
| PDF pp. 2-3, randomization/intervention | Central computer randomization, permuted blocks of 4, 1:1 allocation, stratified by center and PaCO2 <=45 or >45 mm Hg. HFNO alone: >=48 h, 50 L/min, FiO2 adjusted for SpO2 >=92%, humidifier 37 degrees C. NIV: first session >=4 h and >=12 h/day during first 48 h; pressure support >=5 cm H2O, target tidal volume 6-8 mL/kg predicted body weight, PEEP 5-10 cm H2O, SpO2 >=92%; both groups treated minimum 48 h. |
| PDF p. 4, outcome thresholds | Primary: reintubation within 7 days. Reintubation respiratory-failure criteria include >=2 of respiratory rate >35/min, distress signs, pH <7.25 with PaCO2 >45 mm Hg, FiO2 >=80% for SpO2 >=92%, or PaO2:FiO2 <=100 mm Hg. Secondary respiratory-failure definition requires >=2 of respiratory rate >25/min, distress signs, pH <7.35 with PaCO2 >45, FiO2 >=50% for SpO2 >=92%, or PaO2:FiO2 <=150. Secondary time points: 48 h, 72 h, ICU discharge, ICU/hospital/day-28/day-90 mortality. |
| PDF p. 4, power and inferential plan | Planned enrollment 590 for 80% power to detect 8% absolute reintubation difference: control 18% versus intervention 10%, two-sided alpha .05; inflated 10% to 650. Primary and stated secondary proportions compared by chi-square; day-7 reintubation Kaplan-Meier/log-rank; 90-day mortality Kaplan-Meier/log-rank. Multiple logistic regression adjusted for PaCO2 stratum and baseline imbalance; chronic lung disease only imbalance (`P < .05` rule); reports ORs with 95% CIs. Post hoc random-effects multilevel logistic model: treatment fixed, hospital random. Interaction test for PaCO2 subgroup. Two-tailed `P < .05`; SAS 9.4. |
| PDF p. 6, treatment delivery | HFNO-alone gas flow 50 (5) L/min, FiO2 0.41 (0.13). NIV pressure support 7.8 (2.5) cm H2O, PEEP 5.3 (1.1) cm H2O, FiO2 0.34 (0.10), tidal volume 8.6 (2.9) mL/kg predicted body weight. NIV delivered 22 (9) h during first 48 h (13 h first 24 h); 20/339 (6%) received <=4 h owing to intolerance. HFNO alone delivered 42 (11) h in first 48 h. |
