# Numeric Consistency Check — Stable N Relationships

## Scope and method

This checker covers all 60 stable numeric relationships, `N001` through `N060`, in `relationships/numeric_relationship_inventory.md`. It used the assigned mapper parts as locators and checked the printed inputs against the supplied PDFs where a concrete discrepancy was identified or needed confirmation. It applies arithmetic, totals, subgroup sums, denominators, percentages, missingness, labels, units, reference populations, rate/count distinctions, duplicated values, and population checks when applicable. Rounding tolerance is one half of the last displayed decimal place unless the source declares an exact method or the calculation uses only integer counts. A planned protocol quantity was not required to equal a later observed trial result unless the same population, phase, and time frame were printed.

`Candidate lead` below means a source-grounded observation for downstream registration and exact-source recheck only. No stable candidate ID, severity, validity, disposition, or correction is assigned here. Every such observation remains pending human adjudication.
