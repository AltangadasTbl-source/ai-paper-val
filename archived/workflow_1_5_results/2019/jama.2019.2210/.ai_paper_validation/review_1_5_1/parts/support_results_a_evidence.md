# DOC-003 Results Supplement, Shard A: Quantitative Evidence Map

## Scope and method

Direct source: `joi190023supp2_prod.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-21 only. The current source-matched native text for pp. 1-21 was used as the primary locator and was checked against direct PDF extraction. Current rendered PDF pages were visually inspected where the charts contain values not represented in native text (pp. 2, 3, 5, and 7); the direct PDF remains authoritative. No OCR was needed. Pages 1-21 are completely mapped. This shard does not inspect pp. 22-41.
