# Cross-Source Consistency Check

## Scope, method, and completion record

This check compared the complete supplied canonical relationship inventories `N001`-`N089` and `S001`-`S083` against the direct-source evidence maps for DOC-001 through DOC-004. The comparison was performed only after matching the applicable population, time origin/follow-up, contrast, outcome, analysis set, model or adjustment, measure and scale, unit, reference group, and displayed precision. Direct PDFs were the authority; the mapped native/layout text was used only to locate repeated material. The following targeted direct-PDF visual confirmations were additionally made: DOC-001 p. 7 (Figure 3), DOC-003 pp. 2-3 (eFigure 1), pp. 13-15 (eFigures 3G-3I), and pp. 32-33 (eFigures 6A-6B).

|---|---|

The randomized total (417), allocation (251/166), flow (439/22/417), event counts, five-year RFS (77%/69%), five-year OS (82%/81%), primary and subgroup HRs/CIs/P values, biochemical values, and abstract safety counts were matched only when the analysis set was the same. ### No-match and non-comparator scope

- Planned sample-size values, design assumptions, external background citations, blank initial-SAP fields, administrative appendices, PCR conditions, allocation examples, and DOC-004 availability text have no same-result reported comparator. They were not transformed into candidates.
- DOC-003 average-25(OH)D analyses are post hoc analyses using average values over follow-up; they are not the same exposure/time definition as the main paper's baseline-25(OH)D analyses. Its 413-person eTable 1 total and graphical eFigure 2 summaries therefore have no valid main-result numeric comparator.
- Cumulative-hazard risk tables are participant-at-risk counts, not event counts, percentages, risks, or incidence rates. They were not compared to event numerators. No display-zero P-value-only candidate was identified.
- Where the supplement omits model orientation, adjustment, sidedness, or an interaction-test definition, this omission was retained as a matching limitation and was not inferred as a conflict.

## Qualifying cross-source leads

### CROSS-LEAD-001 — Figure 3 says panel-C risk numbers are absent although panel C prints them

- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p. 7](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=7>), Figure 3 panel C and its caption/footnote.
- **Printed comparator values:** Panel C (`Death, baseline 25(OH)D level 20-40 ng/mL`) prints `No. at risk` rows: placebo `90, 88, 70, 51, 34, 22, 11` and vitamin D `142, 139, 115, 88, 61, 41, 20` at 0 through 6 years. The caption states: `Numbers at risk for panel C are not given because of weighting.`
- **Comparison logic:** The same panel and time scale visibly contain the two risk-number rows that the caption says are not given. This is a direct display-versus-caption contradiction, not a comparison of a risk-set count with an event count or survival estimate.
- **Supported alternative interpretations:** The caption may have been intended to refer to a different weighted display or an earlier figure version; alternatively, the printed panel-C row may be retained inadvertently. The supplied sources do not resolve which statement should govern.
- **Human verification steps:** Open the cited PDF page; confirm the two panel-C rows and the caption sentence; inspect the publisher production file or figure source to establish whether panel C used weighting and whether the rows should be retained or the caption revised.

### CROSS-LEAD-002 — The results supplement identifies the genotype as CDK2 while the main article and protocol identify Cdx2/CDX2

- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p. 5](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=5>), Table 1 (`Cdx2` genotype rows); [DOC-002 PDF p. 27](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=27>), VDR SNP list (`CDX2, rs11568820`); [DOC-003 PDF pp. 13-15](<../../../joi190023supp2_prod.pdf#page=13>) [p. 14](<../../../joi190023supp2_prod.pdf#page=14>) [p. 15](<../../../joi190023supp2_prod.pdf#page=15>), eFigures 3G-3I (`CDK2 GG`, `CDK2 GA`, `CDK2 AA`).
- **Printed comparator values:** The main Table 1 Cdx2 rows are vitamin D/placebo `GG 89/49`, `GA 103/77`, and `AA 38/24`. The three supplement panels labelled CDK2 have those same baseline at-risk counts: `GG` placebo/vitamin D `49/89`; `GA 77/103`; `AA 24/38`. The protocol names the planned VDR polymorphism `CDX2, rs11568820`.
- **Comparison logic:** Identical three-genotype, two-arm baseline counts establish that the supplement panels correspond to the main article's Cdx2 rows, while the supplement's printed gene label is `CDK2`, a different character sequence. Population, time, arms, outcome, and displayed counts match; only the genetic-measure label conflicts.
- **Supported alternative interpretations:** `CDK2` may be a repeated typesetting/transcription error for the Cdx2/CDX2 polymorphism, or the supplement could require a source-data check to establish whether a different locus was analysed. The supplied package does not provide the underlying genotype dataset.
- **Human verification steps:** Verify the labels and baseline counts on the four cited pages; compare the intended SNP identifier with the protocol's `rs11568820`; check analysis code, laboratory report, or figure source to confirm the locus represented by eFigures 3G-3I.

### CROSS-LEAD-003 — Table 1 and complementary BMI subgroup risk tables use unlabelled 249/165 analysis totals below the 251/166 arm headers

- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p. 5](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=5>), Table 1 BMI quartiles; [DOC-003 PDF p. 32](<../../../joi190023supp2_prod.pdf#page=32>), eFigure 6A; [DOC-003 PDF p. 33](<../../../joi190023supp2_prod.pdf#page=33>), eFigure 6B.
- **Printed comparator values:** Main Table 1 BMI quartile counts sum to vitamin D `63+62+59+65=249` and placebo `36+43+45+41=165`, despite arm headings `n=251` and `n=166`. The complementary supplement BMI figures at year 0 show BMI `<25 kg/m2`: placebo `144`, vitamin D `208`; BMI `>=25 kg/m2`: placebo `21`, vitamin D `41`; hence `144+21=165` and `208+41=249`.
- **Comparison logic:** Both the main table and supplement consistently use 249 vitamin-D and 165 placebo participants for BMI, two and one below the randomized-arm headers. This is a concrete unlabelled denominator/analysis-set ambiguity; no missing-BMI statement is printed at either location.
- **Supported alternative interpretations:** The BMI subgroup curves may deliberately use complete BMI data, with two vitamin-D and one placebo values unavailable or excluded; Table 1's placebo BMI categories may likewise be complete cases. If so, a missing-data/analysis-set note is needed to distinguish the figures from the 251/166 randomized columns. A transcription error in one displayed count is another source-grounded possibility.
- **Human verification steps:** Confirm all eight Table 1 BMI counts and both eFigure baseline risk tables; consult the analysis dataset/code for BMI availability and the subgroup-analysis population; determine whether the table headings, a BMI cell, or a missing-data footnote requires correction.

### CROSS-LEAD-004 — Final protocol page 23 gives an incomplete high-25(OH)D stratum label while page 31 and the main article define it as greater than 40 ng/mL

- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-002 PDF p. 23](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=23>), Study design; [DOC-002 PDF p. 31](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=31>), subgroup analyses; [DOC-001 PDF p. 3](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=3>), Statistical Analysis; [DOC-001 PDF p. 7](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=7>), Figure 3 caption.
- **Printed comparator values:** DOC-002 p. 23 describes `low (< 20 ng/mL), middle, (≥ 20 to ≤ 40 ng/mL) or high (40 ng/mL)`; DOC-002 p. 31 defines `high, >40 ng/mL`. DOC-001 defines the high baseline group as `>40 ng/mL` and Figure 3 says `high (>40 ng/mL)`.
- **Comparison logic:** A printed `high (40 ng/mL)` label does not specify whether it means exactly 40, at least 40, or greater than 40. It conflicts with the same final protocol's explicit `>40` definition and the reported study label. This is a subgroup-boundary/measure-label difference, not a claim that any participant's value at the boundary has been identified.
- **Supported alternative interpretations:** The p. 23 parenthetical may be a shortened or typographical rendering of the p. 31 definition; the later explicit definition could be the intended operative rule. The supplied package does not state whether a baseline value exactly 40 ng/mL occurred.
- **Human verification steps:** Confirm both final-protocol passages and the main study definition; check the signed SAP/version-controlled subgroup specification and baseline data for handling of an exactly-40 ng/mL value.

### CROSS-LEAD-005 — Reported survival time origin is randomization, whereas the final protocol defines it as starting supplementation

- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p. 2](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=2>), Outcomes; [DOC-001 PDF p. 6](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=6>), Figure 2 x-axis; [DOC-003 PDF pp. 2-3](<../../../joi190023supp2_prod.pdf#page=2>), eFigure 1 x-axis; [DOC-002 PDF p. 25](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=25>), primary/secondary outcome definitions; [DOC-002 PDF p. 29](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=29>), censoring definitions.
- **Printed comparator values:** DOC-001 defines relapse-free survival as time `from randomization` to relapse or all-cause death, and Figure 2 uses `Years After Randomization`. DOC-003 curves use `Years since Randomization`. DOC-002 defines RFS and OS as elapsed time `from starting supplementation` to the event/censoring date.
- **Comparison logic:** The documents specify different displayed time origins for the same time-to-event measures. Because hazard/survival time and risk-set membership depend on the origin, this is a concrete outcome-definition discrepancy even though no competing numerical HR is printed.
- **Supported alternative interpretations:** Randomization and starting supplementation may have occurred on the same date for every analysed participant, making the operational time scale identical. The supplied documents do not explicitly establish that identity or identify any delay.
- **Human verification steps:** Verify the randomization and first-dose timestamps in the trial database/protocol implementation record; establish whether the analysis origin was randomization or first supplementation; confirm that the article, supplement axes, SAP, and statistical code use the same origin.

### CROSS-LEAD-006 — De novo cancer is labelled as an adverse event in the main report and protocol synopsis, but as a tertiary outcome in the final protocol

- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p. 8](<../../../jama_urashima_2019_oi_190023.pdf#page=8>), Table 3 `Adverse Events` and footnote; [DOC-002 PDF p. 19](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=19>), final synopsis; [DOC-002 PDF p. 25](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=25>), Tertiary outcome; [DOC-002 PDF p. 29](<../../../joi190023supp1_prod.pdf#page=29>), safety reports.
- **Printed comparator values:** DOC-001 Table 3 includes `De novo cancer` under adverse events: per-protocol-until-censoring vitamin D/placebo `15 (6.6%)/8 (5.4%)`; one-year adherent `16 (6.6%)/9 (5.6%)`; randomization group `16 (6.4%)/9 (5.4%)`. The table footnote defines it as cancer in an organ other than the primary site. DOC-002 p. 19 says safety/adverse events include de novo cancer; p. 25 labels it `Tertiary outcome`; p. 29 includes `double cancer` in safety-outcome reports.
- **Comparison logic:** The same named, numerically reported event is assigned incompatible reporting categories across the final protocol and report. This matters to extraction of the Table 3 counts as safety-event versus tertiary-outcome results; it does not allege that the counts themselves differ.
- **Supported alternative interpretations:** The protocol may intentionally classify de novo cancer as a tertiary efficacy/safety-related outcome while still requiring it in safety reporting and presenting it within an adverse-event table. The supplied materials do not declare a hierarchy resolving those labels.
- **Human verification steps:** Confirm the endpoint hierarchy in the approved SAP and trial registry/source documentation; determine whether de novo cancer should be designated an adverse event, tertiary outcome, or both, and verify the corresponding Table 3 label and data-extraction guidance.

## Counts and limitations

- **Relationship inventory coverage:** 172 assigned canonical relationships (`N001`-`N089` and `S001`-`S083`) reviewed across all four direct sources; all mapped repeated or definition-level comparators were checked.
- **Qualifying distinct cross-source leads:** 6 (`CROSS-LEAD-001` through `CROSS-LEAD-006`).
- **No-match/non-comparator scopes:** protocol design/background/administrative material; data-sharing statement; average-follow-up-25(OH)D post hoc analyses without a same-definition main comparator; graphical values without printed numeric coordinates; and relationships lacking enough supplied model details for identity testing.
- **Limitations:** No raw data, analysis code, production figure files, first-dose timestamps, or version-controlled endpoint hierarchy were supplied. These leads identify printed inconsistencies or unresolved definition differences only and require human verification. No C IDs, severity labels, dispositions, AI judgments, or final corrections are assigned here.
