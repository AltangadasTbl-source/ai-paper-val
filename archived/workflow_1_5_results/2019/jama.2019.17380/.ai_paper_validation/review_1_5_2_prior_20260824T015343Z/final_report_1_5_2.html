C001 — eFigure 2 and eFigure 3 comparator wording conflicts with the active-versus-placebo contrast

Candidate statement: The repeated eFigure 2 and eFigure 3 caption wording appears to use a follow-up timepoint where the displayed active-versus-placebo contrast requires a comparison group.

Category: Measure, label, or scale inconsistency

Exact source locations: DOC-003 eFigure 2 — PDF p. 18 and eFigure 3 — PDF p. 19; comparator context at DOC-003 eTable 6 — PDF p. 11 and DOC-001 Results — PDF p. 4.

Source evidence: eFigure 2 is titled “Effects of Vitamin D Versus Placebo” and eFigure 3 “Effects of Omega-3 Fatty Acids Versus Placebo.” Each caption states that estimates compare “the active treatment assignment to year 5,” while also describing change from baseline to year 5. eTable 6 labels the contrast “Ratio of change from baseline, active to placebo,” and the main article describes both active interventions versus placebo.

Reported-versus-comparator: The titles identify placebo as comparator, but the repeated caption phrase names “year 5,” a timepoint, as the object of comparing the active assignment.

Reasoning procedure: Compare the figure titles and caption wording with the matched eTable 6 contrast and the main-text treatment contrast. A timepoint can describe an outcome window, but it cannot by itself identify the comparison group in an active-versus-placebo estimate.

Calculation: No arithmetic is required. The captions already specify baseline to year 5 as the time window; their “to year 5” comparator phrase does not reconcile with the titles’ “Versus Placebo” wording.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: Acrobat native-text segment order may omit, reorder, or spatially separate a visual caption element. Alternatively, both captions may reproduce the same wording defect. The report does not infer an intended correction.

Mechanical evidence recheck: The cited locations and printed title/caption text were found again through direct page-specific Acrobat queries. The eTable 6 and main-text comparator wording also matched. No rendered page was available to verify visual glyph placement.

Quality-control relevance: This is a reproducible label-consistency observation about a displayed treatment comparison, with both direct observations and the inference about intended wording kept separate.

Potential downstream evidence impact: If confirmed, a data extractor could copy an ambiguous comparator label for these subgroup effects. The package does not establish propagation or any change in conclusions.

Human verification steps: Visually inspect the two supplied PDF pages to confirm the exact caption phrase and any layout context; then determine whether the comparison-group wording should identify placebo while retaining baseline-to-year-5 as the outcome window.

C002 — Omega-3 eGFR panel repeats vitamin-D contributor counts instead of the omega-3 allocation counts

Candidate statement: The omega-3 eGFR panel reports the vitamin-D active/placebo contributor-count sequence rather than the distinct omega-3 sequence shown in the matched table.

Category: Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency

Exact source locations: DOC-001 Figure 2 panels A and B — PDF p. 7 and Table 2 omega-3 rows — PDF p. 8.

Source evidence: Figure 2 panel A (vitamin D versus placebo) gives 701/607 at baseline, 531/459 at year 2, and 496/438 at year 5. Panel B (omega-3 versus placebo) repeats exactly those counts. Table 2 gives matched omega-3 active/placebo counts of 657/651, 499/491, and 472/462.

Reported-versus-comparator: Panel B reports 701/607, 531/459, and 496/438; Table 2 reports 657/651, 499/491, and 472/462 for omega-3. The panel-B values instead equal the vitamin-D panel-A grouping.

Reasoning procedure: Match endpoint, omega-3-versus-placebo contrast, and baseline/year-2/year-5 timepoints. Compare the arm-specific participant contributor counts; equal combined totals do not make factorial arm allocations interchangeable.

Calculation: Baseline: 701−657=44 and 607−651=−44, with both totals 1308. Year 2: 531−499=32 and 459−491=−32, with both totals 990. Year 5: 496−472=24 and 438−462=−24, with both totals 934.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: Native text might have reordered panel labels or sequences, although it places the repeated sequence within the omega-3 panel. Reuse of panel-A values is a possible explanation, not an established cause or correction.

Mechanical evidence recheck: Direct page-specific source queries found the panel titles, the repeated three-timepoint sequence, the Figure 2 contributor note, and the Table 2 omega-3 sequence. The arithmetic reproduced exactly; layout confirmation remains unavailable.

Quality-control relevance: The printed values preserve each timepoint total while allocating different numbers to the active and placebo groups, producing a concrete denominator/allocation inconsistency.

Potential downstream evidence impact: If confirmed, an evidence extractor could copy incorrect arm-specific contributor denominators for omega-3 eGFR timepoints. The package does not show that this occurred or that effect estimates or conclusions changed.

Human verification steps: Visually inspect panel B and its arm labels on the supplied Figure 2 page, then reconcile its count sequence with Table 2’s matched omega-3 contributor counts.

Reasoning procedure: Match the endpoint, omega-3-versus-placebo contrast, and timepoints between Figure 2 and eTable 6. Compare arm allocations rather than only their combined totals.

Quality-control relevance: This is a distinct endpoint and printed count set from C002. It is a concrete denominator/allocation consistency issue, not a severity assessment.

C004 — eTable 7 reports 944 analyzed participants but its footnote states that 991 were included

Candidate statement: eTable 7’s title and displayed factorial denominators identify 944 participants, whereas its attached footnote says that 991 participants were included in the analysis without defining a further 47-person reduction.

Category: Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency

Exact source locations: DOC-003 eTable 7 title, rows, and footnote — PDF p. 12.

Source evidence: The title states N = 944* for participants with available baseline and year-5 measurements. The two factorial dimensions total 504+440=944 and 477+467=944. The attached footnote says 991 participants donated both baseline and year-5 urine samples and “were included in this analysis”; it also lists 320 baseline-only participants and 1 without baseline as excluded from 1312.

Reported-versus-comparator: The title and displayed denominators are 944; the inclusion footnote is 991. The difference is 47 participants.

Reasoning procedure: Compare the table’s analysis N and arm denominators with the footnote explicitly attached to that N. An inclusion statement should reconcile with the displayed analysis population or provide a source-defined additional exclusion/population rule.

Calculation: 504+440=944; 477+467=944; 991−944=47; and 991+320+1=1312. The stated flow does not identify the additional 47-person reduction represented by the title and rows.

Alternative source-grounded interpretations: Some of the 991 donors may have lacked usable ACR measurements or another required input, but the supplied footnote says they were included and does not state that further rule.

Mechanical evidence recheck: The direct page-specific source query found the title, row totals, asterisked footnote, and all stated flow counts. The arithmetic reproduced. The necessary source-defined explanation of the 47-person difference was not present in the table text.

Quality-control relevance: This is a reproducible analysis-denominator consistency observation. It separates the direct printed values from any inferred explanation and leaves the population definition for human adjudication.

Potential downstream evidence impact: If confirmed, an evidence extractor could record the wrong complete-case analysis denominator. The package does not establish propagation or impact on effect estimates or conclusions.

Human verification steps: Confirm the title, arm rows, and attached footnote in eTable 7, then locate or request the source-defined analysis-population rule accounting for the 47 participants.
