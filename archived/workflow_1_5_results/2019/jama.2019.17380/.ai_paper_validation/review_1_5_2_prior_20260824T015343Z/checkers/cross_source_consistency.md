## Explicit checks of apparent differences

### C002 — Figure 2 omega-3 eGFR contributor counts versus Table 2 omega-3 contributor counts

- **Direct observation:** Figure 2 panel B, DOC-001 p.7, is labeled omega-3 fatty acids versus placebo and gives contributor counts of 701/607 at baseline, 531/459 at year 2, and 496/438 at year 5. The matched omega-3 Table 2 rows on DOC-001 p.8 give 657/651, 499/491, and 472/462. Panel B's sequence exactly repeats the vitamin-D panel-A/Table-2 grouping.
- **Comparison logic and calculation:** Both displays concern the omega-3-versus-placebo eGFR timepoints. Totals agree (1,308, 990, and 934), but the omega-3 active/placebo allocation differs by +44/-44, +32/-32, and +24/-24, respectively. Equal totals do not make factorial arm splits interchangeable.
- **Direct versus inferred:** The named panel, both printed sequences, matching totals, and distinct Table 2 omega-3 sequence are direct observations. A duplicated-panel production mechanism is inferred only.
- **Alternative and exact human question:** Acrobat native text is not layout-preserving and could have reordered visual elements. Does a rendered DOC-001 p.7 associate 701/607, 531/459, and 496/438 with panel B's omega-3-versus-placebo labels; if so, why do those arm counts differ from Table 2? This is registered `C002`, Pending Human Adjudication.

### C004 — eTable 7 internal title/footnote denominator discrepancy

- **Direct observation:** DOC-003 eTable 7 p.12 (N059) title and rows give `N = 944*`; the displayed factorial rows reconcile as 504+440=944 and 477+467=944. - **Direct versus inferred:** The title, rows, footnote wording, and arithmetic are direct observations. Possible unusable paired ACR values or another analysis criterion are inferred explanations not stated on the page.
- **Exact human question:** Which eTable 7 count is the intended analysis denominator, 944 or 991, and what source-defined rule accounts for the 47 participants? This is registered `C004`, Pending Human Adjudication; it is not counted as a cross-source proposal in this lane.

- **Comparison logic:** the documents contain different counts, but the protocol wording is prospective (`will be`) whereas the main article reports the analysis performed. The protocol is not an observed duplicate result. The assigned rule excludes planned-versus-observed differences from candidate registration.
- **Disposition for this lane:** not a qualifying cross-source candidate; retained as a complete-coverage boundary for N051/S032 and S003.

### Full-analytic versus complete-case eGFR contrast values

- **Direct observation:** Table 2 (DOC-001 p.8) reports full-analytic, multiple-imputation year-5 contrasts of D3 0.9 (-0.7 to 2.5), P=.25, and omega-3 0.9 (-0.7 to 2.6), P=.27. eTable 4 (DOC-003 p.9) reports the explicitly paired baseline/year-5 subgroup (`N=932`) contrasts of D3 0.87 (-0.83 to 2.58), P=.32, and omega-3 0.09 (-1.61 to 1.80), P=.92.
- **Comparison logic:** population, missing-data treatment, and displayed precision do not match. DOC-001 labels Table 2 change values as all participants with multiple imputation; DOC-003 labels eTable 4 available baseline/year-5 measurements. The differing estimates are therefore expected sensitivity-versus-primary outputs, not an identity conflict.
- **Disposition for this lane:** not a qualifying cross-source candidate.

### Main Table 3 versus post-hoc eTable 10 event rates and hazard ratios

- **Direct observation:** the D3 Table 3 composite in DOC-001 p.9 is 85 versus 79 events, rate 2.5 versus 2.7/100 person-years, HR 0.92 (0.68-1.25). DOC-003 eTable 10 p.15 lists a post-hoc >=40% eGFR-decline-or-kidney-failure result of 46 versus 39, rate 1.7 versus 1.7, HR 1.03 (0.68-1.58).

- **Disposition for this lane:** not a qualifying cross-source candidate.
