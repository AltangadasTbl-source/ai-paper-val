## Study, population, endpoint, and model context

- The abstract (PDF p. 1) states 287,438 adults with diabetes in the Cleveland Clinic Health System from 1998-2017; 2,287 underwent metabolic surgery and were matched 1:5 to 11,435 nonsurgical patients with diabetes and BMI at least 30, with follow-up through December 2018. The matched analysis population is 13,722.
- The primary endpoint is extended MACE: first occurrence after index date of all-cause mortality, coronary artery events, cerebrovascular events, heart failure, nephropathy, or atrial fibrillation (PDF p. 1; Figure 2 caption, PDF p. 7). Secondary 3-component MACE is all-cause mortality, myocardial infarction, and ischemic stroke (PDF pp. 1, 3, 7).
- Baseline values are median (IQR) or number (%). Outcomes use cumulative incidence (Kaplan-Meier) at 8 years, absolute 8-year risk difference (nonsurgical minus surgery) with 95% bootstrap CI from 1,000 samples, and HR/95% CI/P from fully adjusted Cox models for surgery versus nonsurgical patients (PDF pp. 3-4, Table 2 footnotes on p. 7). Fully adjusted models include all Table 1 baseline variables. Baseline missing values were multiply imputed in five data sets; Rubin-formula SEs were used (PDF p. 4).
- Tests are two-sided at .05. Mean longitudinal changes used a 4-knot spline interacted with treatment; medication proportions at 1, 2, 5, and 8 years used a two-sample proportions test in Methods, while Figure 5 caption names Fisher exact test for displayed 8-year comparisons (PDF pp. 4, 10). The main article directs detailed time-point medication proportions/sample sizes to Supplement 1.

## Participant-flow and population evidence

Figure 1 (PDF p. 3) gives the following flow. Surgical branch: 2,818 surgery-coded; 61 excluded after chart review; 2,757 potentially eligible; 470 excluded for enrollment criteria; 2,287 included. The displayed mutually nonexclusive reason counts are diabetes evidence 240, BMI 133, cancer 99, ED admission within 5 days 29, age 24, organ transplant 19, and ejection fraction below 20% 13. Nonsurgical branch: 284,620 initial controls; 195,677 excluded (116,031 diabetes diagnosis after assigned date and 79,646 no follow-up); 88,943 potentially eligible; 49,676 enrollment exclusions; 39,267 eligible before matching; 27,832 not matched in the 1:5 process; 11,435 matched controls. The nonexclusive reason counts are BMI/missing BMI 39,821, age 12,004, diabetes evidence 9,888, cancer 3,291, ejection fraction below 20% 1,006, transplant 855, ED admission 537, and death within 30 days 87. The caption explicitly says some patients met multiple exclusion criteria.

Results on PDF p. 4 report surgery type: Roux-en-Y gastric bypass 1,443 (63%), sleeve gastrectomy 730 (32%), adjustable gastric banding 109 (5%), and duodenal switch 5; BMI categories in surgery patients are at least 40: 1,713 (75%), 35-39.9: 465 (20%), and 30-34.9: 109 (5%). Median follow-up is 3.9 years (IQR 1.9-6.1) overall, 4.0 (2.1-6.1) nonsurgical, and 3.3 (1.2-6.3) surgery.

## Outcome, figure, and adverse-event evidence

Table 2 (PDF p. 7) is the authoritative outcome table. The eight outcomes, exact 8-year values, risk-set denominators, risk differences, adjusted HRs, CIs, and P values are retained in `parts/main_numeric_relationships.md` and `parts/main_statistical_relationships.md` as MN006-MN013 and MS001-MS008. Figure 2 (PDF p. 7) reproduces primary and secondary HR/CIs/P values and gives risk sets at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 years. Figure 3 (PDF p. 8) does so for six individual endpoints. The narrative on PDF p. 4 gives event counts for primary MACE (385 vs 3,243), secondary MACE (194 vs 1,765), and mortality (112 vs 1,111), all matching the named outcome rows/definitions.

Other directly reported results: at 8 years mean body-weight reduction was 29.1 kg (95% CI 28.8-29.3) surgery and 8.7 kg (8.6-8.9) controls, mean difference 20.3 kg (20.1-20.6); HbA1c change difference was 1.1 percentage points (1.0-1.2), P<.001 (PDF p. 7). Figure 4 (PDF p. 9) additionally displays total-weight-loss difference 14.7% (14.5-14.9), P<.001, and HbA1c difference 1.1% (1.0-1.2), P<.001. Figure 5 (PDF p. 10) reports P<.001 for noninsulin diabetes medications, renin-angiotensin inhibitors, other antihypertensives, lipid-lowering drugs, and aspirin, and P=.008 for insulin; percentages/sample sizes are not printed in the main article and are explicitly directed to Supplement 1.

Ninety-day postoperative outcomes (PDF p. 7): bleeding requiring transfusion 68 (3.0%); pulmonary adverse events 58 (2.5%); venous thromboembolism 22 (1.0%); cardiac events 17 (0.7%); renal failure requiring dialysis 4 (0.2%); no cerebrovascular events; abdominal surgical intervention 109 (4.8%); death 15 (0.7%); endoscopy 16.7%; interventional radiologic procedures 1.6%; parenteral nutrition 1.4%. Median postoperative stay was 2 days (IQR 2-3).
