# Main Quantitative Evidence Map — DOC-001, PDF pages 1-7

## Scope and method

- **Direct source:** `jama_brenner_2019_oi_190039.pdf` (DOC-001), 7 PDF pages; all pages 1-7 mapped.
- **Authority and locator:** the direct PDF is the authority. Current normalized native/layout assets and the page-linked rendered locator for PDF page 5 were used only to locate and transcribe material; Table 2 and Table 3 were visually confirmed against a fresh direct-PDF render of PDF page 5.
- **Scope boundary:** this map records the article's result-relevant populations, counts, denominators, percentages, test performance measures, differences, confidence intervals, P values, model labels, time points, units, table/figure footnotes, and matching narrative claims. It does not make a consistency finding or adjudication.
- **Identifier convention:** `MN` is a numeric/reporting relationship and `MS` is an inferential/statistical relationship. `Match key` is the exact population/time/test/cutoff/contrast identity for later cross-source comparison.
