# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- The supplied package does not contain participant-level outcome classifications, event-level adverse-event records, or analysis datasets. Consequently, it cannot identify how the six switch-ineligible failures were allocated by original arm, how the eight post-day-90 injection cases were classified, or whether the eTable 8 serious ocular-hypertension case required surgery.
- The supplied package does not contain row-specific missed-dose analytic sample sizes, unrounded participant summaries, transformations or weights, analysis code, or Welch-test output. The C005 calculation is therefore a conditional diagnostic based on the displayed summaries and column n values, not a replacement analysis.
- The supplied documents do not include a complete approved amendment chain, the final randomization-list specification, or generated randomization lists. The 2012 protocol's block sizes and timeline can be compared with the article, later manual, and SAP, but the version governing every implementation detail cannot be established from the package.
- Main Table 3 supplies a randomized MMF header of n=109 and a footnote that one assigned participant did not receive treatment, but it does not state a row-level denominator convention for the percentages. Supplement eTables use treated N=108; the approved table shell or output is not supplied.
- Supplement eTable 8 points to “eFigure 2” for seriousness criteria, but no eFigure 2 content is present in the supplied supplement text. eTable 1 and eTable 9 provide direct comparator definitions, but the intended target of the footnote cannot be resolved from the package.
- The direct sources are PDFs only. No workbook, CSV, structured result file, cached formula value, or external analysis output was supplied for independent cell-level or model-level reconstruction.
- Protocol PDF p. 80 has no usable native text layer. It was rendered and visually inspected, so this is a reusable-derivative limitation rather than an open scientific-coverage gap.
- All 263 direct-source pages were mapped using 26 reusable and 237 fresh-required units. No scientific source-unit gap remains; missing materials above limit explanation of the printed relationships, not their documented direct-source comparison.
