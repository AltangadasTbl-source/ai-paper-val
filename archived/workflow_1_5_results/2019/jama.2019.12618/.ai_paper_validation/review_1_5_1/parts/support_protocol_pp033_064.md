# DOC-003 support extraction: protocol PDF pp. 33–64

## Scope and method

Assigned and completed scope: `joi190092supp2_prod.pdf` (DOC-003), PDF pp. 33–64 inclusive (32 PDF pages; printed MOP pp. 32–62, followed by the Version 4.5 cover). Direct authority was the supplied PDF. Fresh `pdftotext -layout -f 33 -l 64` extraction was compared byte-for-byte with `.ai_paper_validation/review_1_5_1/preprocessing/protocol_pp033_064_layout.txt` and matched. PDF p. 47 was additionally rendered and CPU-OCR checked because Figure 2 is image based; direct visual inspection confirmed its nine labelled panels, 0 through 8.

This MOP section contains prospective measurement definitions, outcome-linked assessment procedures, quality-control thresholds, and administrative plans. It contains no accrued trial results, arm-level counts, effect estimates, confidence intervals, P values, or reported statistical test results. The records below are mapping records only; they do not diagnose candidates.
