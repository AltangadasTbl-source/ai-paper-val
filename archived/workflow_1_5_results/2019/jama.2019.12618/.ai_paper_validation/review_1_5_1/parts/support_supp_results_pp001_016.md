# DOC-002 quantitative and statistical evidence map: Supplementary Results, pp. 1-16

## Scope and method

Direct source: `joi190092supp1_prod.pdf`, PDF pp. 1-16 (16/16 units mapped). I read the reusable page-specific native layout text for pp. 1-16 and confirmed it against fresh direct `pdftotext -layout` extraction. I visually confirmed direct PDF p. 15 because it contains the apparent arithmetic issue recorded below. The reusable result map was used only as a locator.

Result form: unless otherwise stated, each table cell is **number of patients reporting at least one event (percent)**. Individual event rows need not sum to group N because a patient can report multiple events. Percentages are printed to one decimal place.

## pp. 1-4: contents and eligibility definitions (context/no outcome result)

- pp. 1-2: Contents names eBox and eTables 1-9. It identifies eTable 7 as tests of homogeneity for the 6-month primary outcome; eTables 8-9 are six- to 12-month AE tables for continuing-after-success and switching-to-the-other-antimetabolite populations.
- p. 3: Entry thresholds: age >=16 years; active disease within 180 days is >=2+ anterior-chamber cells and/or >=2+ vitreous haze and/or active retinal/choroidal inflammation; active disease at enrollment is >=1+ cells and/or >=1+ haze and/or active retinal/choroidal inflammation. Entry alternatives include failure after 4 weeks of high-dose 1 mg/kg prednisone equivalent, flare on taper in 180 days, >=10 mg/day prednisone equivalent for >=90 days, or injection 4-180 days before enrollment. Prednisone start is 1 mg/kg or 60 mg/day, whichever is less; alcohol recommendation is <=2 drinks/month.
- p. 4: Further numeric exclusions include noncorticosteroid immunosuppression within 12 months; age <16; IOP <5 mm Hg for >3 months in both eyes; injection in past 4 weeks; fluocinolone implant <3 years; surgery <30 days or planned within 180 days; pregnancy test within 4 weeks; CBC <=2,500 leukocytes, <=75,000 platelets, and/or <=9 hemoglobin within 4 weeks; ALT/AST >=2 x laboratory ULN and/or creatinine >=1.5 within 4 weeks; infectious testing within 90 days. These are eligibility definitions, not outcome results.

## pp. 5-7: eTable 1 seriousness definitions (context/no event counts)

Table label: `eTable 1. Definitions of Serious and Nonserious Adverse Events`. These definitions determine the labels in eTables 8-9.

- Ocular: non-serious ocular hypertension is >=24 mm Hg; serious ocular hypertension requires laser/incisional surgery. Non-serious glaucoma is suspect/confirmed diagnosis; serious glaucoma is confirmed diagnosis with >3 lines of vision loss or mean-deviation decrease >=10 dB. Non-serious hypotony is IOP <5 mm Hg without specified structural changes; serious hypotony is IOP <5 mm Hg with them. Visually significant cataract/surgery indicated is non-serious; any endophthalmitis or non-serous retinal detachment is serious.
- Laboratory: leukocytes: >1,000 and <2,500/uL <28 days versus <2,500/uL >=28 days or <1,000/uL; platelets: 20,000-75,000/uL <28 days versus <75,000/uL >=28 days or <20,000/uL; hemoglobin: >6.5 to <9 g/dL <28 days versus <9 g/dL >=28 days or <6.5 g/dL; SGOT(AST)/SGPT(ALT): 2-5 x ULN <28 days versus 2-5 x ULN >=28 days or >5 x ULN; creatinine: >=1.5 to <2 mg/dL <28 days versus >=1.5 to <2 mg/dL >=28 days or >=2 mg/dL.
- Systemic: serious fever is >=103 F/39.5 C for 12 hours (non-serious >100.6 to 103 F/39.5 C); serious fatigue reduces normal activity >50% or prevents work/self-care; serious muscle weakness limits function; serious systemic infection requires hospitalization or lasts >28 days. Seizure, cancer, disability, hospitalization, life-threatening event, death and other listed events are serious on any occurrence. No counts, effect measures, or tests are printed.

## Draft local consistency records (not adjudications)

### N-DOC002-DRAFT-001 — eTable 9 apparent denominator/percentage inconsistency

- **Category:** Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency.
- **Direct observation:** DOC-002 p. 15 headers specify mycophenolate mofetil `(N=20)` and `Number of Patients Reporting at Least One Event (%)`; Serious Systemic `Diarrhea` prints `1 (3.4)`.
- **Reproducible relationship:** 1/20 x 100 = 5.0% to one decimal, not 3.4%. The table uses 3.4% for 1/29 in the methotrexate column and 5.0% for other 1/20 cells.
- **Human question:** Does serious diarrhea use an unprinted alternative denominator, or is 3.4 a transcription/copy error? No alternate denominator is supplied.
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication. Draft local record for coordinator merging and independent recheck; not a final candidate ID.

### N-DOC002-DRAFT-002 — eTable 8 serious-ocular label versus seriousness definition

- **Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Direct observation:** DOC-002 p. 5 defines serious ocular hypertension as `Surgery required (laser or incisional)` and non-serious ocular hypertension as `>=24 mm Hg`. DOC-002 p. 14 places `Ocular hypertension >24mm Hg` in its `Serious Ocular` section (methotrexate 1 [1.6], mycophenolate 0 [0.0]).
- **Reproducible comparison:** The p. 14 numeric threshold label matches the p. 5 non-serious criterion, while p. 5 says the serious criterion is surgery requirement.
- **Human question:** Is p. 14 intended to denote a surgery-required event but labeled with the non-serious threshold, or does this continuing cohort use another seriousness definition? Footnote a points to “eFigure 2,” whose content is not present in the supplied supplement text; eTable 1 remains a separate supplied comparator.
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication. Draft only; no causal or clinical inference.
