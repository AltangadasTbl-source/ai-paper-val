## Candidate draft 1 — allocation block-size description differs across the supplied protocol and article

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Exact linked locations:** [Main article — PDF p. 2](../../../jama_rathinam_2019_oi_190092.pdf#page=2), Randomization: “permutated blocks of size 4 and 6”; [Protocol — PDF p. 13](../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=13), §2.4: “block size will vary (4, 6, or 8 with equal probability).” Related canonical records are `N002` and `S014`; `S001` is unrelated primary-outcome inference.
- **Printed values and comparison logic:** Both passages describe site-block randomization for the same two-arm FAST trial. The protocol explicitly includes three possible block sizes (`4, 6, 8`) and says they have equal probability. The article lists only two (`4, 6`). After matching the trial, allocation process, and site-stratified contrast, the printed set of possible block sizes differs: `{4,6,8}` versus `{4,6}`.
- **Supported alternatives:** The September 2012 protocol is a planned document, while the article can be reporting the blocks actually used. The supplied SAP at physical PDF p. 9 and revised SAP pp. 49–50 specify blocks 4 (probability 2/3) and 6 (probability 1/3), recorded as `N196` and `N237`, matching the article and supplying version-change context.
- **Human verification steps:** Locate the final approved protocol/amendment and randomization-list specification; determine whether size 8 was removed before enrollment or simply never drawn; then confirm whether the article is intended to state the planned scheme or the realized scheme.

## Candidate draft 2 — six-month treatment-success definition includes an injection criterion in the version-4.5 manual but not in the article's stated definition

**Workflow 1.5.1 audit correction:** The precise provenance is `N004`, `N026`, `N112`, `N263`, and `S024`; `S001` is unrelated. `N263` is on physical SAP PDF p. 70 and provides sensitivity-analysis context for an injection at 90 days, not proof of the primary-analysis rule. This correction supersedes the older provenance wording below.

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Exact linked locations:** [Main article — PDF p. 3](../../../jama_rathinam_2019_oi_190092.pdf#page=3), Treatment/Outcomes: injections within the first 90 days may be given for macular edema and “other injections were considered protocol deviations”; the explicitly enumerated success definition requires controlled inflammation, `<=7.5 mg/day` prednisone, `<=2` topical drops/day, and no safety/intolerability failure. [Protocol manual, version 4.5 — PDF p. 80](../../../joi190092supp2_prod.pdf#page=80), §2.5.1: success requires controlled inflammation, `7.5 mg/day` prednisone, “no periocular or intravitreal corticosteroid injections after the first 90 days of follow up,” `<=2 drops/day`, and no safety/tolerability/futility failure. Related canonical records are `N004`, `N026`, `S001`, and `S024`.
- **Printed values and comparison logic:** These are otherwise matched patient-level treatment-success definitions at six months for the same two randomized treatments. The manual expressly adds a timing rule—no periocular/intravitreal corticosteroid injection after day 90—whereas the article's complete three-part definition does not include injection status; it instead labels nonpermitted injections as protocol deviations. This is a definition/analysis-classification difference with a numeric timing threshold (`90 days`) that could alter whether an affected participant is counted as a success.
- **Supported alternatives:** The article may intentionally report a simplified endpoint while treating a post-day-90 injection as efficacy failure under a separate operational rule. The endpoint may also have changed across protocol versions; the supplied original protocol's earlier endpoint text does not establish the version in force for every enrolled participant. The package does not identify the status of the eight article-reported patients with injections after 90 days, so the observed `64/96` and `56/98` success counts cannot be recalculated from supplied evidence.
- **Human verification steps:** Check the approved protocol/amendment history and treatment-assessment forms in force at enrollment; identify each post-day-90 injection and its primary-outcome classification; verify whether the article's “protocol deviation” wording was intended to be equivalent to treatment failure; then confirm whether the published numerator/denominator requires clarification.

## Limitations

The supplied package contains protocol versions dated September 2012 and January 2017 but no amendment history, randomization list, participant-level endpoint classifications, or outcome-assessment forms. Therefore, planned-versus-realized differences were not called discrepancies unless the documents made directly conflicting statements about the same matched definition or allocation feature. No supplied workbook, CSV, or structured outcome data exists for an independent recalculation.
