## Scope and evidence method

- **Direct source:** `jama_rathinam_2019_oi_190092.pdf`, PDF pp. 1–10 (printed article pp. 936–945).
- **Reusable locator/transcription assets:** the complete native-text set `.ai_paper_validation/preprocessing/JAMA2019-12618-MAIN/native_text/page-001.txt` through `page-010.txt`, listed `USABLE` in `evidence_asset_inventory.md`. Direct PDF text was additionally inspected for pp. 3–10; no OCR was needed.
- **Mapped units:** PDF pp. 1–10. Pages 1–8 contain result-relevant data/definitions; p. 9 restates conclusions and limitations without new quantitative result values; p. 10 is acknowledgments/references without result-relevant numeric relationships.
- **Conventions:** `MTX` = methotrexate; `MMF` = mycophenolate mofetil. Counts are patients unless explicitly labelled eyes. `N` records numeric/reporting relationships; `S` records inferential/model relationships. These are evidence-map records, not candidates or adjudications.

- **Relationship records:** 39 numeric/reporting (`N001`–`N039`) and 13 inferential/statistical (`S001`–`S013`), 52 total.
- **Tables/figures fully mapped:** Figure 1 (p. 3), Table 1 (p. 5), Table 2 (p. 6), Figure 2 (p. 7), Table 3 (p. 8), plus matching abstract/narrative statements.
- **No-applicable units:** PDF p. 10 contains no main-study result-relevant relationships. PDF p. 9 contains no new numeric result beyond the qualitative limitations/conclusion linkage captured in N039.
- **Limits for later checking:** This file records printed evidence and model labels. It does not diagnose discrepancies, assess candidate status, infer omitted model details, or adjudicate any issue. Apparent method-label differences (for example, the eye-level model wording) are preserved as direct-source context for later source-grounded review.
