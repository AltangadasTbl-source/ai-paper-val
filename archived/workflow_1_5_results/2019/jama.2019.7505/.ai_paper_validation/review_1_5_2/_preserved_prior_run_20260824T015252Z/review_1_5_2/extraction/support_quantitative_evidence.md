## DOC-002 protocol: population, intervention, outcomes, and quantitative definitions

* **Population and sample-size history — PDF pp. 10-11.** Planned BMI >=35 kg/m2, ARISCAT >=26, surgery expected >=2 h; about 90 centers, at least 24 randomized patients/center, planned randomized total 2013. Original calculation: 356/group at two-sided alpha 0.05 and 80% power for PPC 30% (high PEEP) versus 40% (low PEEP), RR 0.75; with 5% dropout, 748 total/374 per group. Re-estimation after first 618 patients: control incidence 0.20 rather than 0.40; 80% power, overall alpha 0.05, RR 0.75; maximum 1912 before 5% dropout and 2013 after. Main-result match keys: `population_mITT`, `primary_PPC_5d`, `sample_size_randomized`.
* **Outcome definitions — PDF pp. 12, 19-20, 22, 31-33.** Primary PPC is a collapsed composite in first five postoperative days: aspiration pneumonitis, bronchospasm, mild/moderate/severe respiratory failure, ARDS, pulmonary infection, atelectasis, cardiopulmonary edema, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, and new pulmonary infiltrates; any >=1 component meets endpoint. Secondary: intraoperative complications, postoperative ventilatory support, unexpected ICU admission/readmission, hospital readmission within 30 d, hospital-free days day 90, 90-d mortality, postoperative extra-pulmonary complications (PEPC), wound healing. Daily follow-up days 1-5 plus discharge; 90-d status. Key thresholds: mild failure PaO2 <60 mmHg or SpO2 <90% in room air >=10 min and responds to oxygen; moderate same oxygenation threshold but requires >2 L/min; severe requires noninvasive/invasive ventilation. Intraoperative protocol thresholds include desaturation SpO2 <=92% >1 min, recruitment-maneuver hypotension systolic BP <90 mmHg >2 min, and rescue hypotension/bradycardia/hypoxemia as BPsys <90 mmHg (or >10-mmHg fall when already <90), HR <50 bpm (or >20% fall when already <50), and SpO2 <=92% (or >5% fall when already <92).
* **Randomization and ventilation definition — PDF pp. 12-15.** Permuted blocks length 6, 1:1, balanced by center and ARISCAT risk; low PEEP 4 cmH2O without planned recruitment versus high PEEP 12 cmH2O with recruitment. Volume control, FiO2 >=0.4, target saturation >=93%, I:E 1:2, end-tidal CO2 35-45 mmHg, tidal volume 7 mL/kg IBW. IBW/PBW formula: male 50 + 0.91*(height cm - 152.4); female 45.5 + 0.91*(height cm - 152.4). Recruitment: pressure limit 55 cmH2O, initial PEEP 12, rate >=6/min, I:E 1:1; raise tidal volume in 4 mL/kg steps to plateau 40-50 cmH2O; PEEP maximum 20; allow 3 breaths. Main-result match keys: `assigned_peep`, `tidal_volume`, `ventilation_timecourse`.
* **Rescue strategy — PDF p. 15.** Low-PEEP steps: FiO2/PEEP = 0.5/4, 0.6/4, 0.7/4, 0.8/4, 0.9/4, 1.0/4, 1.0/5, 1.0/6, 1.0/7(+optional RM). High-PEEP steps: 0.4/14(+RM), 0.4/16(+RM), 0.4/18(+RM), 0.5/18, 0.6/18, 0.7/18, 0.8/18, 0.9/18, 1.0/18, 1.0/20(+RM); if saturation worsens while hemodynamically stable, consider PEEP 10 then 8.
* **Collected variables and units — PDF pp. 16-21.** Intraoperative values were intended after induction, hourly, and immediately before/after recruitment: peak/plateau pressure and PEEP (cmH2O), tidal volume (mL), respiratory rate, I:E, FiO2 (%), SpO2 (%), end-tidal CO2 (mmHg), BP (mmHg), blood loss/urine output (mL), fluids/transfusion amounts. Blood/urine collections before/end/day 5: 2 x 5 mL in EDTA, citrate, and heparin, stored -80 C. Clinical planned pain target VAS <3; massive transfusion is >100% blood volume/24 h or >50%/4 h to maintain Hct >21% (Hb >7 mg/dL); adult blood volume approximately 70 mL/kg.
* **Analysis plan — PDF pp. 22-23.** Interim looks: N=956, 1434, 1912 (50%, 75%, 100%); gamma spending gamma=-4 for alpha/beta, power .80, alpha .05, p1=.20, p2=.15. Efficacy/futility P boundaries: <=.006 (>.82), <=.015 (>.35), <=.044 (>.044). Primary comparison was planned with logistic regression, described as OR/RR with 95% CI; time-dependent data planned in proportional hazards model adjusted for possible baseline imbalances. Primary confirmatory; all other analyses exploratory; ITT, two-sided p=.05, 95% confidence levels. These protocol descriptions are superseded for final analysis by DOC-004 where definitions differ.

## DOC-004 SAP: final analysis definitions

* **Outcomes and analysis population — PDF pp. 1-2.** Primary: collapsed PPC within first 5 days; 12 components as above; any component qualifies. Severe PPC excludes mild respiratory failure. Secondary: PEPC, intraoperative adverse events, unexpected ICU admission, impaired wound healing, in-hospital mortality, hospital-free days at day 90. Modified ITT excludes withdrawal of consent or cancelled surgery; complete-case outcome analysis; if primary-outcome missingness >1%, planned multiple-imputation/estimating-equation sensitivity analysis.
* **Primary and secondary effect definitions — PDF p. 2.** Primary and binary secondary outcomes: counts/percentages, RR and 95% CI using Wald likelihood-ratio approximation and chi-square hypothesis tests; two-sided alpha .044 for primary owing to interim analysis. Kaplan-Meier/log-rank for time to PPC. Hospital-free days at day 90: Student t test, mean difference; generalized additive model with zero-inflated beta distribution for mean ratio confirmation. No multiple-comparison P adjustment; continuous distributions assessed by histogram and D'Agostino-Pearson test.
* **Subgroups and sensitivity methods — PDF pp. 2-3.** Subgroups: non-laparoscopic/laparoscopic; BMI <40/>=40 kg/m2; SpO2 <96/>=96%; peripheral/upper-abdominal incision; waist-hip ratio <1.0/>=1.0. Interaction assessed in generalized linear models/forest plot. Sensitivity: GLMM with center random effect. Composite: count compared Mann-Whitney, proportional-odds logistic OR/95%CI; individual component GLM and 99.58% Bonferroni CI (1-.05/12=.9958); common-effect GEE OR; distinct-effect GEE average relative effect and treatment-by-component interaction; severity-weighted GEE common OR.
