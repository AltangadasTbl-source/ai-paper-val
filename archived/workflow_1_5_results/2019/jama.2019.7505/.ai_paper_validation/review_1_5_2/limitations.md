# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- The package supplies published PDFs but no raw data, analysis code, unrounded estimates, or publisher production source; candidate records therefore identify human questions rather than final corrections.
- Table 2 and several supplement tables do not fully specify their test, variance, repeated-measures, or interval construction rules. The review checked ordering, containment, direction, labels, and cross-source matches but did not import external conventions.
- The DIC row does not name a zero-cell correction or alternative estimator, so no replacement RR, CI, or P value is proposed.
- eTable 8 labels its numeric column only as “Effect Estimate 95% CI.” Because no conflicting measure label is printed in that table, the omission is documented here rather than assigned a candidate ID; human users may still wish to confirm that the intended measure is risk ratio.
- The DOC-001 page-2 ARISCAT phrase was resolved visually: “18” is a superscript citation marker after “score,” not a printed eligibility threshold. The visible eligibility text is “score¹⁸ of 26 or greater,” consistent with the supplied ≥26 protocol/eMethods definition.
- The statistical pass did not infer unreported group-sequential sidedness/implementation details or equate distinct HR, RR, OR, ITT, per-protocol, and sensitivity estimands.
- No coherent display-zero P value was treated as a candidate; no such standalone case occurred in the mapped relationships.
- DOC-006 is a data-sharing statement and contains no result-bearing numeric or statistical relationship.
