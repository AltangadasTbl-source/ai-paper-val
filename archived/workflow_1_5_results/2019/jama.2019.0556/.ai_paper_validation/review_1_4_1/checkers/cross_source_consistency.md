# Cross-Source Quantitative Consistency Review

## Scope and comparison method

Complete cross-source comparison of the four current mapping artifacts: DOC-001 main article; DOC-002 complete protocol; DOC-003 analysis plan (SAP); DOC-004 complete supplement (including pp. 1-25); and DOC-005 data-sharing statement. Every matched definition, prespecified relationship, result display, and administrative source unit represented in those maps was compared only after matching population, time, intervention contrast, analysis set, model, outcome scale, unit, reference group, and displayed precision. Direct-PDF locations below are authoritative. This is a candidate-observation register only: it contains no stable `C` IDs, adjudication, severity, validity decision, or correction.

## Candidate observations requiring human adjudication

### Candidate — Table 2 footnote duplicates the wrong treatment-cell label

**Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.  
**Exact source locations:** DOC-001 Table 2 footnotes d/f and Results, [PDF pp. 6-7](<../../../jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf#page=6>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** Footnote d prints `32 of 256 (12.5%) in the supplements without therapy group` and `22 of 256 (8.6%) in the supplements without therapy group`. Footnote f and the Results group listing identify 22/256 (8.6%) as supplements **with** therapy. The four mutually exclusive cells require distinct labels; 32+22=54 and 256+256=512 still reproduces the supplements main-effect total.  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** This may be a repeated-word production error confined to footnote d; inspect the production proof and four-cell analysis data to confirm that its second `without therapy` should be `with therapy`.

### Candidate — Pill-adherence cutoff changes from inclusive to strict wording

**Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.  
**Exact source locations:** DOC-001 Methods/Results, [PDF pp. 4, 6](<../../../jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf#page=4>); DOC-004 eAppendix 8/12, [PDF pp. 16, 22](<../../../joi190007supp3_prod.pdf#page=16>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** Methods and supplement tables define good pill adherence as `>=70%`, but Results says 77% had adherence `of more than 70%`. The eAppendix 8 multinutrient pill-weight count is 515/666=77.3%, matching the rounded 77%. `>=70%` includes exactly 70%; `more than 70%` excludes it.  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** `More than` may be informal prose for the prespecified inclusive criterion, or no participant had exactly 70%. Verify operational code, exact-cutoff count, and which pill component created the 515/666 summary.

### Candidate — eAppendix 8 adherence-row asterisks point to an unrelated Morisky note

**Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.  
**Exact source location:** DOC-004 eAppendix 8, [PDF p. 16](<../../../joi190007supp3_prod.pdf#page=16>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** Four pill-adherence percentage rows bear `*`, while the single-asterisk note defines the average Morisky score across four follow-ups; the Morisky median row lacks a visible marker. The kappa note `**` (0.73 multinutrient, 0.70 omega) has no visible double-marker attached to a row. Markers should identify the qualified row/statistic.  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** Markers may have shifted during production. Inspect the table proof/source to identify intended rows and restore distinct markers.

### Candidate — Main-article sample-size statement does not reconcile its total with four stated cell counts

**Category:** Numeric or arithmetic inconsistency.  
**Exact source location:** DOC-001 Sample Size, [PDF p. 4](<../../../jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf#page=4>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** The article states `392 participants (196 in each of the 4 possible intervention combinations)` were needed. Four combinations with 196 participants each total 784, not 392. The immediately following attrition statement instead gives 250 per combination (1000 total).  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** The 392 may have been intended as 196 participants in each side of a factorial main-effect comparison, rather than 196 in every four-way cell. Verify the original power calculation and intended unit of allocation for 392.

### Candidate — Protocol sample-size text calls 30% versus 15% a 25% difference

**Category:** Numeric or arithmetic inconsistency.  
**Exact source location:** DOC-002 Sample Size, [PDF p. 16](<../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=16>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** The protocol says it is powered to detect `a difference of 25%` and then specifies 12-month incidence 30% in control versus 15% in intervention. Those displayed incidences differ by 15 percentage points and correspond to a 50% relative reduction; neither calculation is 25% under the stated quantities.  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** `25%` may refer to an unstated effect metric or be a typographical carryover. Confirm the protocol power-calculation input and whether the intended phrase was 15 percentage points, 50% relative reduction, or another defined measure.

### Candidate — Protocol and publication state different primary-outcome sample-size assumptions

**Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.  
**Exact source locations:** DOC-002 [pp. 16-17](<../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=16>); DOC-001 [p. 4](<../../../jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf#page=4>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** Protocol plans 30% control versus 15% intervention incidence, 20% dropout, and 250 per arm/cell. Publication says 30% control versus 20% active incidence, 22% attrition, and 250 per intervention combination. These cannot be the same stated effect-size and attrition assumptions, though both end at a 1000-participant target.  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** The documents may reflect a legitimate amendment or a revised calculation between protocol version 7 and publication. Verify dated protocol amendments/SAP provenance and state which power assumptions governed final enrollment.

### Candidate — Protocol gives incompatible DSM editions for the endpoint definition

**Category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.  
**Exact source locations:** DOC-002 summary [PDF p. 8](<../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=8>) and outcome methods [PDF p. 28](<../../../joi190007supp1_prod.pdf#page=28>); DOC-001 primary outcome [PDF p. 4](<../../../jama_bot_2019_oi_190007.pdf#page=4>).  
**Printed evidence and rule:** Protocol p. 8 labels the endpoint DSM-IV depressive disorder; p. 28 describes the same MINI V5.01 assessment under DSM-5. The publication labels its corresponding MINI 5.0 endpoint DSM-IV. A single endpoint definition should name a consistent diagnostic edition or explicitly distinguish revised versions.  
**Alternative source-grounded interpretation / human question:** The difference may be a protocol drafting carryover or an instrument/version convention rather than a changed endpoint. Confirm the MINI module and diagnostic criteria used for enrollment and outcome adjudication.
