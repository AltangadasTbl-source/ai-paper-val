# Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This is the complete numeric/reporting relationship inventory for the mapped direct-source scope: DOC-001-MAIN pp. 1-14 and DOC-002 through DOC-007 pp. 1-30, 1-82, 1-40, 1-7, 1-53, and 1, respectively. It contains 113 stable `N` relationships: 35 from the main-paper map and 78 from the support-source map. Inferential estimates that are tracked as `S` relationships remain in the statistical inventory; their numeric labels, denominators, reference groups, and scales were nevertheless checked here where they affect reporting consistency.

For displayed percentages, the default rounding tolerance is the interval created by the printed precision: a whole-number percentage must be compatible with the count divided by its stated denominator after rounding to the nearest 1 percentage point, and a one-decimal percentage after rounding to the nearest 0.1 percentage point. A subgroup total is required only where the categories are stated or displayed as mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive. A plan/source difference is not treated as a candidate unless it gives a concrete conflict in a reported quantitative definition, label, denominator, or interpretation.
