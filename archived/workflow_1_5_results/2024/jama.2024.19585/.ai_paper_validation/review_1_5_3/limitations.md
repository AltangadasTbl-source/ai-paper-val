# Limitations and Missing Definitions

This review used only the supplied package files and local reusable derivatives. It is a quantitative-reporting quality-control review, not a clinical, methodological, raw-data, authorship, or misconduct audit. Direct PDFs were the evidentiary authority; reusable text and rendered pages were used as locators and transcription aids.

- The package does not provide the numerator, denominator, decision population, or aggregation definition that produced the main-text 6.4% rejected value in C002.
- The package does not define whether the eTable 10 header `Difference (95% CI)` is intended as a generic comparison label or identify any block that uses an additive estimand; eMethods identifies the modeled estimand as an odds ratio.
- C001's direct visual evidence supports the reconciled parent-child hierarchy (7 + 4 + 2 = 13; top-level reasons = 58). A human should nevertheless confirm that the production layout intentionally encodes that hierarchy. The stable ID is retained without repeating a 71-versus-58 discrepancy.
- Several inferential relationships lack degrees of freedom, standard errors, variance estimators, confidence-interval construction, multiplicity details, or eTable 10 per-cell model-fallback selection. No such detail was inferred from convention.
- Token usage, costs, final timing, post-run hashes, HTML rendering, and final mechanical validation are coordinator completion artifacts and are not represented as completed here.
