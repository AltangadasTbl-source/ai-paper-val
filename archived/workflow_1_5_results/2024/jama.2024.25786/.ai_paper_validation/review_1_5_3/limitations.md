# Limitations and Missing Definitions

The supplied package contains PDFs only. It does not include raw data, table-production files, statistical code, fitted-model objects, unrounded estimates, covariance matrices, inverse-probability weights, or publication-production files. Direct PDF pages are authoritative; reused native text and rendered images were used only as locators or transcription aids.

Accordingly, the package cannot resolve which printed field is authoritative for C001-C002 and C004-C005; whether the adjusted models in C003 are identical; the intended exclusion populations and denominators in C006-C007; or the source-stage cause of the duplicated separator in C008. The review did not conduct a clinical, methodological, raw-data, or external-literature audit. These limitations do not leave a source-unit, relationship, stable-ID, or mechanical-recheck coverage gap.
