### S018-A — TBI <3 liberal percentage conflicts with its count and main Figure 4

- **Exact source locations:** `joi240147supp2_prod_1738701765.29201.pdf#page=20`, eTable 7; `jama_arleth_2024_oi_240147_1738701765.27201.pdf#page=8`, Figure 4.
- **Direct observation:** eTable 7 prints liberal `48/473 (9.2)` for AIS <3; the same source prints 48/473. The supplied main Figure 4 prints the matched field as `48/473 (10.1)`.
- **Reproducible comparison:** 48 divided by 473 times 100 is 10.15%, which rounds to 10.1% at one decimal; it does not round to 9.2%. The effect estimate/CI is separately compatible and is not the basis of this observation.
- **Candidate rule:** denominator/proportion arithmetic plus matched cross-location numeric consistency.
- **Human question:** Confirm whether eTable 7’s liberal percentage is a typesetting/transcription error and determine the authoritative displayed value.

### S019-A — Known-lung-disease liberal percentage conflicts with its count and main Figure 4

### S026-A — Missing-as-event primary numerator/denominator display has a doubled separator

- **Exact source location:** `joi240147supp2_prod_1738701765.29201.pdf#page=25`, eTable 11, missing counted as event primary outcome.
- **Direct observation:** the direct PDF visibly prints `135//750 (18.0)` while the column/table convention is `no./total no. (%)` and the paired cell prints `155/758 (20.4)`.
- **Reproducible comparison:** the displayed count and percentage are numerically compatible because 135/750 equals 18.0%; the independent issue is the doubled slash in the printed numerator/denominator notation.
- **Candidate rule:** measure/count-label presentation consistency, not P-value formatting and not a derived tail-probability claim.
- **Human question:** Confirm whether the double separator is present in the publication version and whether a correction is needed for machine/human extraction clarity.

- IPW/GEE and row-level adjustment details mean interval/P diagnostics are approximations only where the source supplies compatible two-sided 95%-CI definitions. They are labelled diagnostic and do not replace reported analyses.
