# Stable Numeric and Reporting Relationship Inventory

## Scope and inventory method

This inventory independently covers all 40 mapped direct-source PDF pages: D001 pp. 1-9, D002 pp. 1-15, and D003 pp. 1-16. It is rebuilt from the canonical main and support quantitative evidence maps, their source-linked locations, the complete source-coverage ledger, and direct PDF confirmation where a candidate was identified. It does not use prior candidate, checker, verification, critic, disposition, or report material.

`N001` through `N040` correspond to the 40 numeric/reporting relationships mapped from D001; `N041` through `N056` correspond to the 16 numeric/reporting relationships mapped from D002-D003. Inferential-only relationships retain their separate `S` inventory and are not duplicated here. A cluster is one relationship when its printed rows share a denominator, table rule, outcome identity, or defined calculation; all printed values remain in the cited canonical map.
