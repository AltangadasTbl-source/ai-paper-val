# Statistical Relationship Inventory — Pass 1

## Scope and method

- **Pass:** Statistical pass 1, independent source-grounded review.
- **Assigned source coverage:** all 40 direct PDF pages: D001 pp. 1-9, D002 pp. 1-15, and D003 pp. 1-16. The complete main and support quantitative evidence maps were used to enumerate relationships; directly supplied PDFs were consulted for the statistical-method and eTable definitions that govern compatibility checks.
- **Stable inferential relationships:** 25 (`S001`-`S025`). These include 12 mapped main-paper relationships and 13 protocol/results-supplement relationships. Pages without an inferential result are represented by the complete page-unit records in the two mapping artifacts and do not create duplicate statistical IDs.
- **Check convention:** `C` = point-estimate containment; `O` = endpoint ordering; `D` = sign/direction; `M` = effect-measure, reference, and scale label; `X` = same-result repetition; `I` = interval/P/test/statistic/SE compatibility when a compatible definition is supplied. `Definition-limited` means the source does not provide the test, sidedness, degrees of freedom, variance estimator, covariance, confidence-interval construction, model-estimand mapping, or adjustment rule required for that particular numerical reconciliation.
- **Candidate-discovery result:** no independent supplied-source contradiction was identified in this pass. No candidate identifiers are assigned here. No severity, validity, or correction is stated.

## Pass-1 limitations and exclusions

- The supplied sources do not provide test definitions sufficient to derive most exact P values from counts, intervals, beta/SE pairs, or weighting/imputation results. Those checks are explicitly definition-limited rather than treated as evidence of inconsistency.
- No source prints `P = 0`, `p = 0.000`, or an equivalent finite-precision display zero for a mapped inferential relationship. Values such as `.0001` and `<.0001` are nonzero printed values and were not treated as display-zero candidates.
- No prior candidate, checker, verifier, critic, endetail, or report artifact was read as scientific input.
