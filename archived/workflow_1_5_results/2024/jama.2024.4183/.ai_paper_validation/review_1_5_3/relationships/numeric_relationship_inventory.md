# Stable Numeric Relationship Inventory

## Scope and method

This inventory covers the complete canonical quantitative evidence maps for DOC-001 (PDF pp. 1-10), DOC-002 (PDF pp. 1-45), and DOC-003 (PDF pp. 1-36). It was rebuilt from the current supplied PDFs and current-source extraction only. It tracks numeric, arithmetic, denominator, percentage, population, interval, unit, label, reference-group, and rate/count relationships. Planned protocol and simulation quantities are retained as planned relationships and are not treated as observed trial outcomes. `N` identifiers are stable relationship identifiers, not candidate identifiers.

Tolerances are stated in the checker for each applicable relationship. For a displayed integer identity the tolerance is zero. For a count-derived whole percentage the rounding tolerance is plus or minus 0.5 percentage point at the displayed precision. Modelled Bayesian probabilities and ARDs are not forced to equal raw fractions unless the source expressly presents them as the same quantity.

## Inventory totals and limitations

- Stable numeric relationships: 72 (`N001`-`N072`).
- Relationship records bearing a candidate consistency issue: 18 (`N002`, `N009`, `N011`, `N017`, `N038`, `N041`, `N042`, `N053`-`N061`, `N071`, `N072`). `N009` and `N017` describe the same participant-flow issue and are linked rather than counted twice; therefore the distinct numeric candidate count is 17.
- No conventional P-value display-zero relationship was present. Coherent zero event/proportion cells were retained as count/proportion relationships and were not treated as display-zero P candidates.
- Limitations: modelled Bayesian estimates can differ from raw fractions; no unreported model convention was inferred. DOC-002 is a protocol/planning source without a supplied version crosswalk to the publication. DOC-003 pp. 10-12 contain apparent narrative transcription defects; each candidate is framed against its direct, matched eFigure/eTable comparator rather than an assumed correction.
