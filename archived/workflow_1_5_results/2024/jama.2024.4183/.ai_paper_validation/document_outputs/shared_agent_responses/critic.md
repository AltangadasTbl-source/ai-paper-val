# Critic decision

Critic stage retained 9 findings and rejected 1. The AI Training Restriction screen is separate and is not counted.

## Accepted

1. **C01 — Minor — Participant flow inconsistency.** DOC-001 p. 6, Figure 2: 2232 assessed, 1740 excluded, 491 randomized; `2232 − 1740 = 492`, while the exclusion subgroups reconcile to 1740. Verify by tracing and subtracting the eligibility-box branches.
2. **C02 — Minor — Arithmetic inconsistency.** DOC-001 p. 5, Baseline Measures and Demographics, Sex rows: each n=245 arm gives Female 105 (42.9%) and Male 145 (57.1%). Counts total 250, and `145 / 245 = 59.2%`, not 57.1%. Verify by summing counts and recomputing the percentage.
3. **C03 — Minor — Participant flow inconsistency.** DOC-001 p. 4 says the 40 CNRT and 35 varenicline participants “did attend rerandomization”; Figure 2 on p. 6 says they did not. Figure totals support nonattendance: `151 + 40 = 191` and `122 + 35 = 157`. Verify by comparing the sentence with both figure branches.
4. **C04 — Major — Cross-document inconsistency.** DOC-001 p. 3 Figure 1 and DOC-003 pp. 15–18 and 20 define CNRT+ with 2-mg lozenges; DOC-003 p. 14 E-Figure 1 caption specifies 4 mg. Verify by comparing the intervention definitions.
5. **C06 — Minor — Statistical reporting inconsistency.** DOC-001 pp. 5 and 7 show switch 0/41 and stay 2/77, print −3%, and say continuing was worse; DOC-003 p. 21 E-Table 4 labels switch versus stay but prints +3%. Switch minus stay is approximately −3%. Verify the subtraction in the stated order and compare all wording/labels.
6. **C07 — Minor — Statistical reporting inconsistency.** DOC-003 pp. 8 and 18 E-Table 2: under the stated K=1000 simulations, power is 0.948 at threshold 0.80 and 0.980 at the stricter 0.85 threshold. The stricter event is nested within the looser event and cannot occur more often under unchanged simulations. Verify the rows and simulation conditions.
7. **C08 — Major — Presentation inconsistency.** DOC-003 pp. 10–11 gives 1.0% (7.0%–1.3%) where p. 15 E-Figure 2 gives 5/51, 10% (7%–13%); it gives ARD 1.1% (−1.0%–22%) where p. 35 E-Table 11 gives 11% (−1%–22%). Verify the prose against the linked exhibits.
8. **C09 — Minor — Presentation inconsistency.** DOC-001 pp. 6–7 and DOC-003 pp. 9–10 use n=42 for varenicline continuation; DOC-003 pp. 15–16 E-Figures 2–3 use 0/77. Figure 2 gives 42 rerandomized plus 35 nonattenders assigned to continuation. Verify both continuation branches and denominators. This is distinct from C03 because it concerns the downstream analysis population.
9. **C10 — Minor — Presentation inconsistency.** DOC-003 pp. 22–24 E-Table 5 says no adverse-event differences exceeded 2% except nausea, but displayed differences exceed two points for pruritus (2.84), skin rash (3.65), headache (3.25), and concentration impairment (2.44). DOC-001 p. 7 includes the qualification “with nonoverlapping CrIs.” Verify the subtractions and compare the two statements.

## Rejected

- **C05 — Insufficiently supported.** The displayed race/ethnicity entries total 43 for N=41, but the supplied table does not establish that the categories are mutually exclusive. Without that definition, totals above N and 100% do not independently prove an arithmetic inconsistency. The visibly reversed “Other” cell does not close the evidentiary gap.
