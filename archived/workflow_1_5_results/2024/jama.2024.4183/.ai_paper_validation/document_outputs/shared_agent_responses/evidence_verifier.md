Authorized sources reopened: DOC-001 pages 1–10 and DOC-003 pages 4–35. DOC-002 and excluded DOC-003 pages were not opened. No second verification round was needed.

## Verified candidates

### C01 — Participant flow inconsistency

- **Location:** DOC-001 p. 6, Figure 2.
- **Evidence/basis:** 2232 assessed; 1740 excluded; 491 randomized. `2232 − 1740 = 492`, while the listed exclusion subgroups reconcile to 1740.
- **Human verification:** Trace the two outgoing totals from the eligibility box and subtract excluded from assessed.

### C02 — Arithmetic inconsistency

- **Location:** DOC-001 p. 5, Table “Baseline Measures and Demographics,” Sex rows.
- **Evidence/basis:** Each n=245 arm shows Female 105 (42.9) and Male 145 (57.1). `105 + 145 = 250`; `145 / 245 = 59.2%`, while 57.1% of 245 is approximately 140.
- **Human verification:** Sum the two sex counts within each arm and recompute the male percentage.

### C03 — Participant-flow/presentation inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-001 p. 4, participant-flow prose; p. 6, Figure 2.
- **Evidence/basis:** The prose says the 40 CNRT and 35 varenicline participants “did attend rerandomization”; Figure 2 says they “did not attend rerandomization.” The figure totals support nonattendance: `151 + 40 = 191` and `122 + 35 = 157`.
- **Human verification:** Compare the p. 4 sentence with the two Figure 2 nonattendance boxes.

### C04 — Cross-document inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-001 p. 3, Figure 1; DOC-003 p. 14, E-Figure 1 caption; DOC-003 pp. 15–18 and 20.
- **Evidence/basis:** The main figure and multiple supplemental definitions specify two 21-mg patches plus 2-mg lozenges for CNRT+, while the E-Figure 1 caption specifies a 4-mg lozenge.
- **Human verification:** Compare the p. 14 caption with the main figure and later supplement definitions.

### C05 — Arithmetic/presentation inconsistency

- **Location:** DOC-003 p. 19, E-Table 3, varenicline nonabstainer → CNRT (N=41), Race and ethnicity.
- **Evidence/basis:** The displayed category counts, reading the reversed “Other” cell as 2 (4.9), total `12 + 1 + 3 + 1 + 24 + 2 = 43`; percentages total 104.8%.
- **Human verification:** Read down this column, correct only the evident order reversal in “Other,” and sum the counts and percentages.

### C06 — Statistical reporting inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-001 pp. 5 and 7; DOC-003 p. 21, E-Table 4.
- **Evidence/basis:** Switch is 0/41 (0%); stay is 2/77 (3%). Switch minus stay is −3%. DOC-001 prints −3% but says continuing was worse; E-Table 4 labels switch versus stay and prints +3%.
- **Human verification:** Subtract Figure 3 rates in the stated order and compare with the prose and E-Table row.

### C07 — Statistical reporting inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-003 p. 8 simulation description; p. 18, E-Table 2.
- **Evidence/basis:** For the same K=1000 simulations, power is 0.948 at threshold 0.80 and 0.980 at the stricter threshold 0.85. The stricter event is nested within the looser event, so power cannot increase.
- **Human verification:** Compare the first two threshold/power rows and apply the nested-threshold rule.

### C08 — Presentation inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-003 pp. 10–11; p. 15, E-Figure 2; p. 35, E-Table 11.
- **Evidence/basis:** Prose gives 1.0% (7.0%–1.3%) where E-Figure 2 gives 5/51, 10% (7%–13%); prose gives ARD 1.1% (−1.0%–22%) where E-Table 11 gives 11% (−1%–22%).
- **Human verification:** Compare the quoted prose values directly with their linked figure and table.

### C09 — Statistical/presentation inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-001 pp. 6–7; DOC-003 pp. 9–10 and E-Figures 2–3 on pp. 15–16.
- **Evidence/basis:** Prose uses n=42 for varenicline continuation; both E-Figures use 0/77. Figure 2 shows 42 rerandomized plus 35 nonattenders assigned to continuation, totaling 77. The analogous CNRT prose includes its nonattenders in n=90.
- **Human verification:** Follow both Figure 2 continuation branches and compare their denominators with the prose and E-Figures.

### C10 — Presentation inconsistency

- **Locations:** DOC-003 pp. 22–24, E-Table 5; DOC-001 p. 7.
- **Evidence/basis:** The supplement says no adverse-event differences exceeded 2% except nausea, but displayed differences exceed 2 points for pruritus (2.84), skin rash (3.65), headache (3.25), and concentration impairment (2.44). The main article uses the narrower condition “exceeded 2% with nonoverlapping CrIs.”
- **Human verification:** Subtract the two treatment estimates for the cited rows and compare the supplement preamble with the full main-article condition.
