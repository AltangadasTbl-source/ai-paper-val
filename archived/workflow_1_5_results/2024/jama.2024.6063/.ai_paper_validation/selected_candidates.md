# Selected Candidate Issues

Ten deduplicated candidates were selected for one evidence-verification stage. The speculative duplication of the hs-CRP and fasting-glucose week-12 statistics was not selected because coincidence could not be excluded from the supplied materials.

## C01 — Primary-effect sign reversal

- Category: Statistical reporting inconsistency
- Priority: High
- Evidence: Main article page 2, Key Points reports mean difference +0.30 (95% CI −6.9 to 6.4; P=.94). Main page 1 Abstract, page 6 Table 2, and page 7 text report −0.3; supplement page 2 eTable 1 reports −0.27 and page 5 eTable 4 reports −0.3 with the same inference.
- Verify: Confirm visible signs and the stated comparison orientation in the supplied files.

## C02 — Krill adverse-event percentage denominator

- Category: Arithmetic inconsistency
- Priority: Medium
- Evidence: Main page 8, Table 3 reports 67 (50.7%) under krill n=130. Calculation: 67/130×100=51.5%; 50.7% corresponds to 67/132.
- Verify: Confirm the header denominator, numerator, and displayed percentage.

## C03 — Adverse-event subtype arm/count disagreement

- Category: Cross-document inconsistency
- Priority: Medium
- Evidence: Main page 8, Table 3 versus supplement pages 10–11, eTable 7. Main reports pain in extremity 1/6, gastroesophageal reflux disease 1/3, abdominal discomfort 0/3, and diarrhea 2/1 (krill/placebo); supplement reports 1/5, 3/1, 3/0, and 1/2. Main narrative agrees with 1/5 for extremity pain.
- Verify: Inspect both tables and the narrative; classify whether these are explicit same-term conflicts.

## C04 — Weight-bearing-pain week-4 copied changes

- Category: Arithmetic inconsistency
- Priority: High
- Evidence: Supplement page 5, eTable 4 reports weight-bearing pain baseline/final 127/100 and 141/108 but changes −84 and −103. The changes exactly repeat the adjacent Function week-4 row; their difference is −19, not the printed between-group +3.
- Verify: Confirm the row alignment, values, and duplication in the source table.

## C05 — Lower-leg-strength week-12 copied statistics

- Category: Arithmetic inconsistency
- Priority: High
- Evidence: Supplement page 6, eTable 4 reports strength baseline/final 66.5/72.6 and 65.9/70.2 but changes −2.8 and −4.2. The full statistics repeat the back-pain week-4 row.
- Verify: Confirm row alignment, signs, displayed means, and exact duplication.

## C06 — WORMS percentage calculation

- Category: Arithmetic inconsistency
- Priority: High
- Evidence: Supplement page 8, eTable 5 totals are 107 and 109. Printed percentages include 10 (12%), 16 (12%), 80 (72%), and 75 (72%), but the calculations are 9.3%, 14.7%, 74.8%, and 68.8%.
- Verify: Recalculate every category from displayed counts and totals.

## C07 — Injury-category total disagreement

- Category: Cross-document inconsistency
- Priority: High
- Evidence: Main page 8, Table 3 reports injury/procedural-complication events 11 krill and 6 placebo. Supplement pages 11–12, eTable 7 lists only six krill events and six placebo events under that category.
- Verify: Sum the visible eTable 7 terms under the exact category and compare with Table 3.

## C08 — Overall adherence denominator and summary disagreement

- Category: Cross-document inconsistency
- Priority: Medium
- Evidence: Supplement page 3, eTable 2 overall row says n=167 and reports 82 (98.8%) plus 81 (96.4%), implying arm denominators 83 and 84; the footnote says n=165. Main page 7 says 95% consumed at least 80% of softgels, whereas 163/167=97.6%.
- Verify: Confirm row, footnote, and main-text wording; determine which conflicts are document-verifiable.

## C09 — Incorrect adverse-event eTable cross-references

- Category: Presentation inconsistency
- Priority: Medium
- Evidence: Main page 8, Table 3 footnotes direct detailed adverse events to eTable 4 and serious events to eTables 5 and 6. Supplement contents/page labels place detailed adverse events in eTable 7 and serious events in eTable 8.
- Verify: Confirm every cited eTable number in the main footnotes and the supplement contents.

## C10 — eFigure caption/layout mismatch

- Category: Presentation inconsistency
- Priority: Low
- Evidence: Supplement page 15 caption maps baseline to “left” and 24 weeks to “right,” but the renderings are vertically stacked and unlabeled.
- Verify: Inspect the original PDF page, not text extraction alone, and determine whether the mapping is ambiguous.
