# Stable numeric/reporting relationship inventory

## Scope and method

This inventory is the numeric-consistency stage's complete package-wide register of the mapped numeric/reporting relationships. It was rebuilt from the two canonical evidence maps and their cited direct PDFs, without consulting legacy candidate, checker, verification, quality, or report conclusions. `N` identifiers are stable relationship identifiers, not candidate identifiers or adjudications. Inferential relationships mapped as `M-S001` through `M-S019`, `S-SUP-001`, `S-SUP-002`, and the eTable 1/eTable 4 estimate, interval, and P-value relationships remain assigned to the two statistical passes; this inventory records only their numeric/label implications where relevant.

Locations below identify the direct supplied PDFs. Native/layout derivatives are transcription aids only. Percentage checks use ordinary rounding to the precision printed: nearest whole percent has a tolerance of 0.5 percentage point; one decimal place has a tolerance of 0.05 percentage point; values displayed as `100%` tolerate the printed total and the underlying integer count.

## Inventory count and limitations

- **Stable N relationship count:** 28 (`N001`-`N028`).
- **Candidate-proposal count from numeric checking:** 11 distinct proposals (`NP-001`-`NP-011`). These are not stable `C` identifiers, severity ratings, validity findings, or dispositions.
- **Limitations:** The source does not define eTable 6 blank cells versus dashes. Table 1 does not print a denominator or missingness explanation for baseline WORMS categories. eTable 4's model-adjusted inferential contrasts cannot be replaced by simple descriptive subtraction; this pass reports only direct copied-value and internally inconsistent descriptive-cell patterns, leaving inferential compatibility to the statistical passes.
