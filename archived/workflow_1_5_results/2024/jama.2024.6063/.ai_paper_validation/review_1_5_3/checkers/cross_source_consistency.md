# Cross-source quantitative consistency check

## Scope and method

- **Assigned scope:** all matched result-bearing relationships in the main-paper and support quantitative evidence maps, across the main article, protocol, SAP excerpt, and results supplement (four direct PDFs; 41 mapped PDF pages).
- **Evidence used:** required workflow controls; the source inventory, source coverage, coverage manifest, and both complete evidence maps; plus direct PDF confirmation at every proposed location. No web source, old candidate record, old report, or old AI conclusion was used.
- **Matching rule:** results were matched only after checking population, analysis set, time, contrast direction, model/summary statistic, measure, scale/unit, reference group, and displayed precision. Planned protocol/SAP quantities were not treated as observed results. Raw descriptive baseline summaries were not equated with model-based table estimates solely because labels were similar.
- **Matched relationship groups checked:** 52.
- **Qualifying candidate proposals:** 5. These are proposals for later registration, pending human adjudication. This checker assigns no stable candidate IDs and makes no adjudication.

## Candidate proposal 1 — Key Points and other matched primary-result displays use opposite signs

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency; measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [Main article — PDF p. 2](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=2>), Key Points, prints mean difference 0.30; [main article — PDF p. 1](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=1>), Abstract; [main article — PDF p. 6](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=6>), Table 2; [main article — PDF p. 7](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=7>), Results; and [Supplement 3 — PDF p. 2](<../../../joi240048supp3_prod_1727199125.83025.pdf#page=2>), eTable 1.
- **Printed values:** Key Points: 0.30 (95% CI -6.9 to 6.4; P=.94). Abstract, Table 2, and Results: -0.3 (95% CI -6.9 to 6.4; P=.94). eTable 1 original data: -0.27 (95% CI -6.92 to 6.38; P=.94).
- **Comparison logic:** All locations describe the randomized groups and 24-week knee-pain VAS change, with matching intervals and P values. Rounding -0.27 to one decimal gives -0.3, not positive 0.30. The supplied sources do not define a common signed operand order; the direct issue is the opposite signs across otherwise matched displays.
- **Supported alternatives:** Key Points may intend an unsigned magnitude; another location may use an unstated opposite operand order; or a sign may have been lost during production. The supplied sources do not distinguish these possibilities.
- **Human verification steps:** Confirm the intended contrast orientation and whether any display is an absolute magnitude, then reconcile the positive Key Points value with the negative values elsewhere.

## Candidate proposal 2 — Placebo extremity-pain event count differs between Table 3 and the other matched reports

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency; rate-versus-count consistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [Main article — PDF p. 1](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=1>), Abstract; [main article — PDF p. 8](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=8>), Table 3 and adverse-event narrative; and [Supplement 3 — PDF p. 10](<../../../joi240048supp3_prod_1727199125.83025.pdf#page=10>), eTable 7.
- **Printed values:** Abstract and narrative: lower-extremity pain 1 event with krill and 5 with placebo. eTable 7: Pain in extremity 1 and 5. Table 3: Pain in extremity 1 and 6.
- **Comparison logic:** These are event counts, not participant counts or percentages, for the same randomized groups and category. The label wording differs only as lower-extremity versus in extremity; the same krill count, adjacent knee/hip entries, and eTable 7 reference support the match. Table 3 placebo 6 conflicts with the other three printed placebo 5 values.
- **Supported alternatives:** The two labels could conceivably denote different coding aggregates, but the article presents them as the same detailed category and supplies no distinction. A table-cell transcription difference is another supported possibility.
- **Human verification steps:** Inspect the full eTable 7 listing and the Table 3 row; check the adverse-event coding dictionary or production proof, if available, to establish whether one placebo event belongs in the Table 3 aggregate.

## Candidate proposal 5 — Narrative 95% adherence statement does not reproduce eTable 2's overall 24-week proportions

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency; denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [Main article — PDF p. 7](<../../../jama_laslett_2024_oi_240048_1727199125.7595.pdf#page=7>), Process Measures; and [Supplement 3 — PDF p. 3](<../../../joi240048supp3_prod_1727199125.83025.pdf#page=3>), eTable 2.
- **Printed values:** Main text says 95% consumed at least 80% of softgels over 24 weeks, citing eTable 2. eTable 2 overall 0-24-week row (n=167) prints krill 82 (98.8%) and placebo 81 (96.4%). The displayed numerators sum to 163 and 163/167 = 97.6%. Its footnote separately says adherence was calculated for available pill-count data and completers (n=165 [75%]).
- **Comparison logic:** The narrative cites the same eTable and uses the same >=80% threshold and 24-week period. Its 95% does not equal the pooled eTable proportion reproducible from the displayed overall row (97.6%, conventionally 98%), and no alternative denominator is stated in the main text. The eTable's n=167 row and n=165 footnote leave the narrative denominator unresolved.
- **Supported alternatives:** The 95% may use an unstated subset or an earlier table version. The supplied sources state no denominator that yields 95% from the displayed 163 adherent participants.
- **Human verification steps:** Obtain the adherence analysis definition or data-extraction specification; identify the numerator and denominator for the p. 7 95%; reconcile it with the overall n=167 row, 163 displayed adherent participants, and n=165 footnote before deciding which display needs correction.

## Limitations

- No workbook, CSV, DOC, or DOCX direct source exists; the four supplied PDFs were the complete source set.
- Potential differences were not proposed where time, population, model, or summary statistic differed. In particular, planned protocol/SAP analysis text was not substituted for the reported analysis, and eTable 2 does not fully define its overall adherence denominator.
- Direct PDF pages confirmed all five proposals. Reused text acted only as a locator and transcription aid.
