## Result-relevant quantitative and statistical evidence

### R1 — Endpoint adjudication timing and decision rule

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 97](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=97>)–[PDF p. 98](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=98>), Protocol §§17.1–17.2.
- **Definition/relationship:** Endpoint events are target-vessel haemorrhagic stroke, ischemic stroke, death, and target-vessel revascularization. A potential primary-endpoint event is reported within **48 hours**; the event-arbitration committee completes arbitration within **7 days**. It has **3 members**: two conduct back-to-back arbitration, and a third casts the decisive vote if opinions differ.
- **Matching main-paper key:** primary composite endpoint; endpoint adjudication; target-vessel event definition; time-to-event outcome.

### R2 — SAE observation and reporting definitions

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 98](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=98>)–[PDF p. 99](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=99>), Protocol §§17.2–17.3.
- **Definition/relationship:** An AE is any unexpected medical condition during or after device treatment, including symptoms, signs, or abnormal laboratory parameters, whether or not treatment-related. SAE criteria include death, life-threatening event, hospitalization/prolongation, serious disability, or intervention to prevent one of these outcomes. AE/SAE collection runs from informed-consent signature through study end, including patients who discontinue treatment early. Any SAE is reported in writing within **24 hours**.
- **Matching main-paper key:** adverse events, serious adverse events, safety population, follow-up safety ascertainment.

### R3 — Restenosis threshold during follow-up

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 97](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=97>), Protocol §16.
- **Definition/relationship:** In the experimental group, transient cerebral ischemia or ischemic stroke with DSA-diagnosed restenosis **≥70%** triggers expert advice on revascularization versus medical management; revascularization is not recommended for restenosis **<70%**.
- **Matching main-paper key:** target-vessel restenosis; revascularization; threshold/eligibility definition.

### R4 — Prespecified primary and secondary analysis framework

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 102](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=102>), Protocol §20.
- **Statistical definition:** Primary-outcome composite event rates and corresponding **95% CIs** are estimated by Kaplan-Meier survival analysis and compared with a log-rank test. The between-group hazard ratio and **95% CI** use a Cox proportional-hazards regression model. Secondary time-to-event endpoints use Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression. Common odds ratios for mRS use ordinal logistic regression. Secondary-outcome interval widths are **not adjusted for multiplicity**. Main analysis is intention-to-treat; per-protocol and as-treated analyses are sensitivity analyses. Software: SAS version **9.4**.
- **Matching main-paper key:** primary endpoint event rate; hazard ratio; confidence interval; log-rank P value; mRS common odds ratio; ITT, per-protocol, and as-treated populations; multiplicity statement.

### R5 — Protocol sample-size assumptions

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 103](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=103>), Protocol §20.1.
- **Statistical relationship:** Anticipated primary-outcome composite-event rates are **15%** (medical group) and **7%** (balloon-angioplasty group), an **8 percentage-point absolute difference**. Planned enrollment is **512** total (**256 per group**) for **80%** power at one-sided **alpha = 2.5%**, allowing **10%** dropout.
- **Matching main-paper key:** randomized sample size; observed primary-event risks; risk difference; one-sided testing convention; power and dropout assumptions.

### R10 — mRS scale definition

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 115](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=115>), Appendix Table 2.
- **Definition/relationship:** mRS is ordinal and hierarchical, normally **0–5** with higher score indicating more severe disability; **6** denotes death. - **Matching main-paper key:** ordinal mRS outcome; common odds ratio; mRS category distribution; direction of benefit.

### R11 — NIHSS scale and item scoring

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 116](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=116>)–[PDF p. 120](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=120>), Appendix Table 3.
- **Definition/relationship:** NIHSS is an ordinal scale from **0 to 42**, with higher score indicating more severe neurologic deficit. Item domains and maxima: 1a consciousness 0–3; 1b LOC questions 0–2; 1c LOC commands 0–2; best gaze 0–2; visual 0–3; facial palsy 0–3; motor arm 0–4 for each arm; motor leg 0–4 for each leg; limb ataxia 0–2; sensory 0–2; best language 0–3; dysarthria 0–2; extinction/inattention 0–2. Several physical-barrier situations use `UN` rather than a numeric score as specified in the table.
- **Matching main-paper key:** NIHSS baseline value, follow-up value/change, scale direction, outcome-unit definition.

### R14 — Protocol-version changes with quantitative implications

- **Source:** DOC-003 [PDF p. 124](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=124>)–[PDF p. 126](<../../../../joi240088supp2_prod_1746815064.36071.pdf#page=126>), Summary of changes.
- **Definition/relationship:** V2.1 (2020-04-14) defines recent TIA as **<90 days** and ischemic stroke as **14–90 days** before enrollment, attributed to **70–99%** stenosis. V2.2 (2021-12-22) adds exclusion-related thresholds, including supplying-artery stenosis **>50%** and several extracranial/bilateral-vertebral criteria at **>70%**. It explicitly changes sample size from **802 to 512**, citing 15% AMM versus 7% BA event rates and no interim analysis. V2.3 (2022-03-28) adds secondary outcomes including any stroke/all-cause death within **30 days** after enrollment or qualifying-lesion angioplasty, and target-artery revascularization within **12 months**.
- **Matching main-paper key:** trial version; eligibility window; stenosis cutoff; sample size; follow-up horizons; primary/secondary endpoint definitions; interim-analysis status.
