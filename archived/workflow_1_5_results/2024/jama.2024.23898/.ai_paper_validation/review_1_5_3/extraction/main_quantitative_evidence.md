## Trial identity, populations, outcome definitions, and analysis labels

- **N001. Trial population and randomization:** 590 adults scheduled for elective minimally invasive colon resection for benign or malignant disease were randomized 1:1: 295 intravenous (IV) lidocaine and 295 saline placebo. Enrollment was August 13, 2018 to April 11, 2023, paused March 20 to July 6, 2020; final follow-up was August 10, 2023. Source: DOC-001 [p. 1](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=1).
- **N002. Intervention contrast:** 2% IV lidocaine was a 1.5-mg/kg induction bolus followed by 1.5 mg/kg/h for 6 or 12 hours; control was 0.9% saline placebo for 6 or 12 hours. In the treatment description the bolus and infusion are based on ideal body weight, with a 120-mg/h (6-mL/h) maximum; the intended minimum duration was 6 hours and monitored wards allowed 12 hours. Source: DOC-001 [p. 1](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=1), [p. 4](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=4).
- **N003. Primary endpoint definition:** GI-3 at 72 hours after surgery is tolerating food and drink without significant nausea/vomiting for 3 consecutive meals plus passage of flatus or stool. GI-2 is tolerance of oral diet plus passage of stool. Prolonged postoperative ileus is failure to achieve GI-3 by 120 hours (5 days). Sources: DOC-001 [pp. 1, 4](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=1).
- **N004. Analysis populations:** After 33 postrandomization exclusions, primary analysis included 557 participants (279 lidocaine; 278 placebo); per-protocol analysis included the randomized treatment recipients (267; 265); safety analysis was 279 in each group because one postrandomization-excluded placebo participant received randomized drug. Sources: DOC-001 [pp. 1, 3, 5](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=1).
- **N005. Model and adjustment labels:** Primary and most binary/count outcomes used a generalized linear model with log link, adjusted for minimization variables (age, sex, center); time-to-event outcomes used Cox regression; repeated OBAS, QoR-15, and EQ-5D-5L outcomes used a linear mixed model with treatment-time interaction; total length of stay used negative binomial regression. All displayed treatment-effect intervals are 95% CIs. Sources: DOC-001 [pp. 5-8](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=5).
- **N006. Prespecified subgroup framework:** intended infusion duration (6/12 hours), operation (right/nonright colectomy), sex, age (<50, 50-74, >=75 years), and enhanced-recovery adherence; the main text says the interaction threshold was two-sided 1%, while the eFigure labels its subgroup intervals as 99% CIs. Sources: DOC-001 [pp. 4-5](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=4); DOC-004 [p. 2](joi240139supp3_prod_1741633738.18862.pdf#page=2).

## Abstract and matching main-text primary-result relationships

- **S001 / N007. Abstract primary result:** GI-3 by 72 hours: 160/279 (57.3%) lidocaine versus 164/278 (59.0%) placebo; adjusted absolute difference -1.9% (95% CI -8.0% to 4.2%); adjusted relative risk (RR) 0.97 (95% CI 0.88 to 1.07). Source: DOC-001 [p. 1](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=1).
- **S002 / N008. Matching main-text primary analysis:** the same 160 (57.3%) versus 164 (59.0%), difference -1.9% (-8.0 to 4.2), adjusted RR 0.97 (0.88 to 1.07), P = .54; primary-outcome data were reported in 100% of the 557 primary-analysis participants. Source: DOC-001 [p. 5](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=5).
- **S003 / N009. Per-protocol primary result:** 154/267 (57.7%) versus 158/265 (59.6%); absolute percentage difference -2.3 (95% CI -8.9 to 4.3); RR 0.96 (95% CI 0.86 to 1.07); P = .49. Sources: DOC-001 [pp. 5-6](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=5).
- **N010. Matching narrative claims:** the abstract, Key Points, results, discussion, and conclusion state no significant/no beneficial difference in the primary outcome and no significant difference in the 11 secondary endpoints. Sources: DOC-001 [pp. 1-2, 5, 7, 9](jama_paterson_2024_oi_240139_1741633738.12862.pdf#page=1).
