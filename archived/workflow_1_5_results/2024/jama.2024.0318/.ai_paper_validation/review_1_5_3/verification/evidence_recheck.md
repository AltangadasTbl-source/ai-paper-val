- **Consistency rule applicable:** The graphic directly connects one displayed parent population to two treatment-group branches. If those branches partition that displayed parent, their counts should sum to the parent count. The two enrolled treatment groups likewise should sum to the reported enrolled cohort.
- **Calculation or logical comparison reproduced:** `193 + 122 = 315`, and `315 - 305 = 10`. The enrolled counts reconcile: `166 + 96 = 262`. The reported enrollment percentage also reconciles with the parent count: `262 / 305 x 100 = 85.9016%`, which rounds to 86%.

The package does not provide the treatment-arm distribution of the 11 persons removed between the 316-person eligible cohort and the 305-person available cohort, nor a definition explaining whether 193 and 122 refer to the displayed 305-person population or to an earlier original-randomization population. A participant-level flow crosswalk is absent.
- **Source-grounded alternative interpretation:** The 193 and 122 counts may preserve original treatment assignments for a population defined before the 305-person availability step, even though the boxes are drawn as branches of 305. Alternatively, the 305 parent count, one or both branch counts, or the population label on one of those boxes may not express the intended flow definition.
- **Direct observation versus inferred explanation:** Direct observation is that the connected branch counts total 315 while their displayed parent is 305, a difference of 10; the enrolled counts separately total 262. Any explanation involving an earlier assignment cohort, a flow-label problem, or a printed count problem is inferred because the source does not define the branch population separately.
