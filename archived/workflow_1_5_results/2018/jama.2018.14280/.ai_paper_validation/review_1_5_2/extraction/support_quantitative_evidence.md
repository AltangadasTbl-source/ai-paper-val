## Cross-document keys and terms

* **Arms:** protocol/SAP use *lower* versus *higher* tidal volume; the final paper and DOC-004 display *Low* versus *Intermediate* tidal volume. Matched arm key: randomized low 4-6 mL/kg PBW versus conventional/intermediate 8-10 mL/kg PBW.
* **Primary outcome:** ventilator-free days (VFD) and alive at day 28: days 1-28 alive and breathing without invasive assistance for at least 24 consecutive hours; include later ventilation periods within 28 days (DOC-002 pp. 26, DOC-003 p. 8). Match key: `VFD_day28_all_randomized`.
* **Population:** ICU patients without ARDS, expected invasive ventilation >24 h; randomization 1:1, stratified by center and intubation location (DOC-002 pp. 17, 26; DOC-003 pp. 6-7). Match key: `ITT_no_ARDS_ICU`.
* **PBW formula:** male `50 + 0.91 × (height cm − 152.4)`; female `45.5 + 0.91 × (height cm − 152.4)` (DOC-002 p. 20; DOC-003 p. 7). Ventilator VT is mL/kg PBW.
* **Units/definitions:** FiO2 is fraction inspired oxygen; PaO2/FiO2 is mmHg in results; PEEP/airway pressure cmH2O; MV L/min; respiratory rate bpm; pHa arterial pH. Respiratory acidosis = pHa <7.35 and PaCO2 >45 mmHg. Lower denominators for ABG outcomes denote missing ABG measurements (DOC-004 pp. 5, 7).
