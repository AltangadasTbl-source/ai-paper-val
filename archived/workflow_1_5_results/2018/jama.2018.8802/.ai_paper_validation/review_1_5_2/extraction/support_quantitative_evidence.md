## DOC-002: protocol and statistical-analysis definitions

- pp. 4, 7: 2-arm, parallel cluster-randomized trial; 40 hospitals; allocation 1:1, stratified by province, hospital grade (secondary/tertiary), and baseline stroke-care quality. Match key: `design-clusters-40-1to1`.
- pp. 4, 6-7: patient population is acute ischemic stroke within 7 days of index event, age older than 18; diagnosis confirmed by CT or MRI within 7 days. Network frame is 563 hospitals in 27 provinces and 4 municipalities. Match keys: `AIS-within-7d`, `network-563-27plus4`.
- pp. 9-10: clinical pathway covers each of the first 7 acute-admission days and discharge. IV rt-PA measure is arrival within 2 hours after last seen normal and treatment within 3 hours; early antithrombotics and DVT prophylaxis have an end-of-day-two/48-hour window. Match key: `PM-window-definitions`.
- p. 12: monitoring feedback occurs after each 30 consecutive recruited patients per hospital and repeats every 30. Match key: `feedback-cycle-30`.
- pp. 13-16: nine predefined performance measures: four acute (IV rt-PA, early antithrombotics, DVT prophylaxis, dysphagia screening) and five discharge measures (antithrombotics, anticoagulation for AF/flutter, lipid lowering, antihypertension, hypoglycemic therapy). Exact eligibility/exclusions and care definitions are transcribed in UN003 below. Match key: `nine-performance-measures`.
- p. 16: composite adherence = interventions performed among eligible patients / possible interventions among eligible patients; all-or-none adherence = proportion of eligible patients receiving all performance interventions. Match key: `composite-vs-allornone-definition`.
- pp. 16-17: secondary outcomes are in-hospital death, new vascular event (ischemic stroke, hemorrhagic stroke, myocardial infarction, or vascular death), disability mRS 3-5, and all-cause mortality at 3, 6, and 12 months after onset. Match key: `secondary-outcomes-3-6-12m`.
- p. 17: sample-size inputs/target: baseline mean composite score 80%; detect 5% improvement; 80% power; 5% significance level; ICC 0.02; 40 clusters and approximately 4,800 patients; median 120 AIS patients per cluster; three cycles; 30 patients per cycle per cluster. The displayed design arithmetic is 40 x 120 = 4,800 and 3 x 30 = 90 per cluster, so the statement does not explicitly say that the three cycles account for all 120 patients. Match key: `sample-size-80-5-80power-ICC002`.
- pp. 18-19: ITT analysis; categorical variables as proportions, normal continuous variables as mean (SD), skewed continuous variables as median (IQR). Univariate categorical comparison χ2; continuous comparison Student t or Mann-Whitney U. GEE accounts for within-hospital correlation; qualitative variables use population-average OR and quantitative variables mean difference, both 95% CI. Composite GEE unit is a care opportunity, with dichotomous 1=met/0=not met. Time-to-event discharge/3/6/12-month effects use Kaplan-Meier curves and proportional-hazards Cox models. Sensitivity analysis includes contraindicated patients in the overall-population denominator. Match keys: `SAP-GEE-OR-MD-95CI`, `SAP-care-opportunity-unit`, `SAP-Cox-3-6-12m`, `sensitivity-include-contraindications`.

## DOC-003: baseline survey and measure definitions

- p. 2: baseline survey prospectively included 20 patients per cluster before randomization, used the randomized-phase eligibility and endpoint definition, and defines composite adherence as interventions performed / possible interventions among eligible patients. The overall composite was 80.2% intervention versus 79.5% control. Individual intervention/control percentages: IV rt-PA <2 h 22.6/13.0; early antithrombotics 95.4/88.4; dysphagia screening 79.6/88.1; DVT prophylaxis 14.4/7.8; discharge antithrombotics 91.6/91.6; AF/flutter anticoagulation 14.8/28.2; lipid lowering 66.9/64.6; antihypertension 69.4/71.2; hypoglycemic therapy 83.2/78.2. The narrative says no statistically significant between-group difference for the composite. Match key: `baseline-survey-composite-and-nine-PM`.
- pp. 3-4, eTable 1: exactly nine measure definitions and eligibility exclusions. IV rt-PA: arrival within 2 h and treatment within 3 h; early antithrombotics: within 2 days; DVT: non-ambulatory and by end of day 2; LDL threshold is >=100 mg/dL, prior lipid treatment, or LDL not documented. Eligible patients exclude stated contraindication/intolerance/refusal/terminal care; hospice/short-term general-hospital discharge; leaving against advice before day 2; additionally deaths before day 2 for acute measures other than rt-PA, and in-hospital deaths for discharge measures. rt-PA excludes missing/erroneous onset-arrival-treatment times, outside-hospital starts, and starts after 180 min. Match key: `eTable1-nine-PM-eligibility`.
- p. 5, eTable 2: the observed-1-year-mRS group total is 3,949 and loss group total 851 for each displayed binary characteristic. Age median (IQR) is 65 (56-74) vs 64 (56-74), P=.81; NIHSS 3 (2-6) vs 3 (2-6), P=.99. Exact binary rows are in UN006. The footnote says the first group has 1-year mRS data and defines NIHSS range 0-42. Match key: `eTable2-mRS-observed-vs-loss`.
- pp. 6-7, eTable 3: each intervention/control total is 2,400, and all 12 event/time rows report count/total (%), absolute percentage-point difference with adjusted 95% CI and P, plus adjusted HR with 95% CI and P. A patient may have different events, so summing event types exceeds the number of patients with any event. Adjustment set is age, gender, ischemic-stroke history, hypertension, diabetes, hyperlipidemia, AF, CAD/previous MI, smoking, admission NIHSS, hospital grade, region, stroke unit, teaching status, and neurological-ward beds. Exact rows are in UN007-UN018. Match key: `eTable3-new-vascular-events-2400-per-group`.
- pp. 8-9, eTable 4: overall-population sensitivity analysis. Composite is mean (SD) 85.3 (15.2) vs 80.9 (17.1), absolute difference 4.20 (1.77-6.63), P<.001, ORPA 1.36 (1.11-1.67), P=.003. Nine individual adherence rows report count/total (%), absolute difference and adjusted 95% CI/P, and ORPA/95% CI/P. Exact rows are UN019-UN028. ORPA means population-average odds ratio. Footnote confirms adjusted patient and hospital covariates and measure-window definitions. Match key: `eTable4-overall-population-sensitivity`.
