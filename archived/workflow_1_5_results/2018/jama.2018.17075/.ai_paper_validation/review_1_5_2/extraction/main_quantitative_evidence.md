## Extraction limitations

No OCR was required. Figure 2 provides patient counts, axes, and distribution-definition text but not printed hourly medians/IQRs; Figure 3 provides an unlabeled graphical GOS-E distribution. These graphical values are therefore not invented as exact numbers. References, affiliations, and non-study external-result citations were not mapped as trial-result relationships.
