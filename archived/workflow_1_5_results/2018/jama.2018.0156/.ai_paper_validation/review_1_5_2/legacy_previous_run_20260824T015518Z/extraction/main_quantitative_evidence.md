- **Source:** `jama_jabre_2018_oi_180004.pdf`, pp. 1-9 (printed pp. 779-787).

- **Scope completed:** every PDF page and every result-relevant displayed or matching narrative numeric/statistical relationship in the main article. This is an extraction inventory, not a candidate assessment.
- **Study frame:** France/Belgium multicentre, parallel-group randomized noninferiority trial; BMV versus ETI for advanced airway management during OHCA. Enrollment March 9, 2015-January 2, 2017; follow-up ended January 26, 2017. Randomization 1:1, stratified by center, random blocks of 4-8. Primary endpoint: day-28 survival with CPC 1-2. The stated absolute noninferiority margin is 1%.

### Additional results and matching discussion (PDF pp. 4, 6-8)

- Hierarchical center-random-effect primary analysis: difference 0.05%, one-sided 97.5% CI -1.70% to infinity (PDF p. 4).
- PP primary: 4.3% versus 4.2%; difference 0.08%, one-sided 97.5% CI -1.74% to infinity; P for noninferiority=.12 (PDF p. 4).
- Post-hoc Center No. 5: n=115 (BMV 56, ETI 59); CCF ETI 87% versus BMV 86%, difference -1% (95% CI -4% to 2%), P=.70; pauses >2 seconds: BMV 27 versus ETI 16, difference 11 seconds (95% CI 7-15), P<.001 (PDF p. 4).
- Other post-hoc exclusions: ECMO-CPR/organ-donation related n=91; BMV-before-ROSC intubated patients considered in ETI analysis n=155 (PDF p. 6).
- Discussion repeats that point estimates were 4.3% versus 4.2%; it describes the result as inconclusive and potential underpowering (PDF p. 7). Conclusions repeat failed demonstration of noninferiority or inferiority (PDF p. 8).
