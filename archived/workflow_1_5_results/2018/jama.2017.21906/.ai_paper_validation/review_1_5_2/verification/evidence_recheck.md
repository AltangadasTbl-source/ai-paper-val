- **Applicable consistency rule:** A repeated adjusted result with the same named outcome, intervention-versus-control direction, point estimate, lower confidence limit, OR, and OR interval should retain the same risk-difference upper confidence limit unless a distinct analysis is identified.
- **Calculation or logical comparison reproduced:** The upper limits differ by `8.40 - 8.10 = 0.30` percentage point. The risk-difference point estimate and lower limit match exactly, and the OR and both OR limits also match exactly.
- **Necessary inputs available:** The outcome name, crude group values, adjustment context from the Table 2 title and footnote, contrast direction, risk-difference estimate and interval, and OR and interval are present. These inputs are sufficient to reproduce the printed repetition mismatch.

- **Source-grounded alternative interpretation:** One occurrence may reflect a transcription or production error, or the narrative may reflect an unlabelled distinct output. No supplied-source statement identifies a second analysis or establishes which upper limit is supported by the analysis output.
- **Direct observation versus inferred explanation:** Directly observed are `8.40%` in Table 2 and `8.10%` in the narrative, with all other displayed adjusted-result elements matching. Any transcription, production, or distinct-output explanation is inferred and is not established by the supplied sources.
- **Exact remaining human question:** Which upper confidence-limit endpoint is supported by the final adjusted in-hospital β-blocker analysis output?
