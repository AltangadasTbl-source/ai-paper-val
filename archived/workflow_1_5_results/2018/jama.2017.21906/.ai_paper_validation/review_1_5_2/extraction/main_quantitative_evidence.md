## Study frame, abstract, and analysis definitions

- **PDF p. 1, abstract:** stepped-wedge cluster trial: 63 hospitals; Nov 10, 2014-Nov 9, 2016; last follow-up Dec 31, 2016; five predefined steps. Control total 10,066 (steps 0-5: 2,915; 2,649; 2,251; 1,422; 829; 0). Intervention total 11,308 (0; 662; 1,265; 2,432; 3,214; 3,735). Primary composite: death, reinfarction, stroke, or major bleeding at 30 d.
- **PDF p. 1, abstract:** 21,374 eligible randomized; mean age 60.6 (SD 12.0) y; 16,183 men (76%); 13,689 STEMI (64%); 21,079 completed (99%). MACE: intervention 5.3%, control 6.4%. Temporal-and-cluster adjusted RD -0.09% (95% CI -1.32 to 1.14), OR 0.98 (95% CI 0.80-1.21).
- **PDF p. 2, Key Points/methods:** repeats 63 hospitals, 21,374 participants, and 30-d event rates control 6.4% versus intervention 5.3%. Randomization strata were projected recruitment <=200, 201-500, 501-1000, >1000; 12 or 13 sites crossed at each step.
- **PDF p. 3, analysis plan:** target 15,750 participants, 63 hospitals, five steps, 80% power for a 2.4% absolute MACE reduction; assumed 5% loss, ICC 0.05, 2-sided alpha .05. Primary model: mixed-effects logistic regression, random hospital and fixed 4-month-step time effect; secondary covariate-adjusted model lists age, sex, MI type, SBP, heart rate, creatinine, acute heart failure, cardiogenic shock, resuscitated arrest. Interim: 12 mo, O'Brien-Fleming z>2.797/P=.005; final z>1.977/P=.048. Secondary outcomes exploratory (no multiple-testing adjustment).
