# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- The review is limited to the four supplied PDFs. It does not use external literature, publisher systems, raw participant data, author files, manuscript proof history, or prior audit derivatives.
- The package does not contain row-level paired outcome data, row-specific complete-pair counts, unrounded estimates, standard errors, covariance matrices, or authoritative statistical output. The dependent-t diagnostics for C009-C012 therefore apply the printed table-level or subgroup n and the printed test description; each remains a question for human adjudication. Direct ordering/containment checks for C013-C014 do not depend on those missing quantities.
- The supplied supplement prints WinBUGS code and parameter dictionaries but does not include executable analysis files, compilation/run logs, posterior draws, or a parameter-to-publication export. C005-C007 establish documentation inconsistencies, not an effect on executed computation.
- eTable 4 does not state its adverse-reaction denominator, analysis unit, or rounding convention. C004 retains patient-versus-treatment-set and rounding alternatives.
- The supplied package does not define an alternative INQoL composite scoring range that explains values above the printed 0-to-100 bound.
- Figure 2 individual effects are graphical and do not print exact numerical coordinates for every point; the review did not invent precision from plot geometry.
- Native and layout text were usable for all 83 PDF pages, and page renders were available for visual alignment. No OCR was required. Dense table/code geometry remains most reliable when checked against the supplied PDF and fresh page render together.
- Protocol planning values and cited background results were kept distinct from observed trial results. This review does not audit study design, clinical conclusions, novelty, misconduct, or raw-data validity.
- Every candidate is a quality-control observation pending human adjudication. No candidate is an AI correction, disposition, or conclusion about paper validity.
