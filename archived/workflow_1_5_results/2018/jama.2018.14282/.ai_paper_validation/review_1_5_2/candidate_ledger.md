## C018 — Arm-attributed support-needs percentages use the overall-population denominator

- **Category:** Denominator, proportion, or total inconsistency
- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 PDF p.6, post hoc outcomes](../../jama_azoulay_2018_oi_180109.pdf#page=6)
- **Source evidence:** The paragraph begins `In the overall population` and then reports `153 patients (19.7%) randomized to high-flow` and `31 patients (4.0%) randomized to standard oxygen`.
- **Rule/calculation:** 153/776=19.7% and 31/776=4.0%, so the printed percentages are arithmetically compatible with the stated overall-population context. - **Alternative / human question:** The introductory phrase supports intentional overall-population percentages. Should the denominator basis be repeated beside the arm-attributed values to prevent extraction as within-arm risks?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication
