\hypertarget{summary}{%
\chapter{Summary}\label{summary}}

This catalog is generated directly from all \textbf{14 original
\texttt{final\_report.html} files}. It converts all \textbf{71 verified
error records} into a fixed JSON-style structure: actual location(s),
the observed problem, and the expected corrected state. The \textbf{8
uncertain candidates} are listed separately and are not treated as
established errors.

\endhead

\endlastfoot

Arithmetic inconsistency & 10 & 1 \\

When the reports do not identify which conflicting number is
authoritative, the \texttt{expected} field says that the locations must
be reconciled against source output instead of inventing a replacement
value. Counts remain report-level instances because the two ImmunoSep
packages contain overlapping findings.
