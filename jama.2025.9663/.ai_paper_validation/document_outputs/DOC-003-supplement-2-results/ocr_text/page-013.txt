eFigure 6. Distribution of days alive and free from organ support at 30 days

Panel A shows the cumulative proportion of patients in each treatment group with each value

of number of days alive and free from organ support during the first 30 days following

randomization, with death listed first on the axis corresponding to a value of -1. Curves that

rise more steeply indicate a less favorable distribution in the number of days alive and free

from organ support. The height of the curve at the start, at -1, indicates the mortality on or

before day 30 in each group. The height of the curve at any point from 0 to 29 days indicates

the proportion of patients with that number of days alive and free from organ support, or fewer,

or who had died by day 30 (e.g., the height at day 20 indicates the proportion of patients that

survived to 30 days with <20 days free from organ support or who had died by day 30). Panel

B shows the distribution of days alive and free from organ support as horizontally stacked

proportions for each treatment group. Blue represents worse outcomes and yellow represents

better outcomes.
