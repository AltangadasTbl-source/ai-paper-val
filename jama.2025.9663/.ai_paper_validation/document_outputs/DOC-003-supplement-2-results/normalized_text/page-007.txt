Days alive and free from organ support at 30 days

Days alive and free from organ support at 30 days is a            n ordinal composite outcome with
death by day 30 being ranked as the worst outcome. Organ support was                        defined as receipt
of respiratory, cardiovascular or renal support during any ICU             stay within the first 30 days
following randomization, according to the UK Department of Health Critical Care Minimum
Dataset. Patients who died on or before 30 days  following randomization                                             were assigned the
worst possible score of −                                                                              1. Surviving patients were ranked according to the number of
calendar days (00:00 to 23:59) on which any respiratory, cardiovascular or renal support was
received at any time during that day, starting from day 1 (the day on which the patient was
randomized) up to and including day 30. Any days outside of an ICU                                                                    were assumed to be
free of organ support. For patients recruited when the site was collecting data to Version 3.1
of the CMP Dataset Specification (when only total calendar days of organ support were
collected, and not organ support on each                        day), all organ support received was                                                        assumed to
be at the start of the critical care stay and therefore the total days of organ support was
assumed to be the maximum of the days of respiratory, cardiovascular and renal support
reported, up to 30 days.
