## Population and participant flow

- **Abstract (PDF p 1):** 16,500 randomized: conservative oxygen therapy, n=8258; usual oxygen therapy, n=8242. Primary-outcome data were recorded for 16,394 (8211 and 8183, respectively).
- **Figure 1 (PDF p 4):** 52,747 screened; 3,514 excluded for age/FIO2 criteria; 49,233 met inclusion criteria; 10,754 met exclusion criteria; 38,479 potentially eligible; 21,979 eligible but not randomized (14,865 missed/identified too late; 6,589 clinical decision; 272 nonclinical decision; 213 prospectively refused consent; 40 no reason); 16,500 randomized. All data removed at participant request: 28 conservative, 38 usual (66 total). Primary outcome recorded: 8,211 conservative and 8,183 usual; consent for linkage of secondary outcomes: 7,463 and 7,595; 12-month follow-up: 6,534 and 6,518.
- **Results, Sites and Participants (PDF p 4):** 66/16,500 (0.4%) requesting removal were excluded; primary outcome could not be determined for another 40 (0.2%) owing to failed linkage. The multiply imputed primary-analysis population was 16,434 (8,230 conservative; 8,204 usual). Enhanced data collection: 2,489 (1,252; 1,237).

## Primary outcome

- **90-day all-cause mortality - abstract (PDF p 1), Results (PDF p 6), Table 2 (PDF p 8):** 2,908/8,211 (35.4%) conservative vs 2,858/8,183 (34.9%) usual. Adjusted, multiply imputed RD 0.7 percentage points (95% CI -0.7 to 2.0; P=.28); RR 1.02 (95% CI 0.98 to 1.06); OR 1.04 (95% CI 0.97 to 1.11). Available-case adjusted RD 0.7 (95% CI -0.6 to 2.1). The Results text additionally reports unadjusted RD 0.5 percentage points (95% CI -1.0 to 2.0).
- **Article conclusion (PDF pp 1, 9):** conservative oxygen therapy "did not significantly reduce" 90-day all-cause mortality / did not reduce it compared with usual oxygen therapy.

## Key secondary outcomes

All Table 2 estimates are adjusted and multiply imputed unless stated otherwise (PDF p 8).

- **ICU stay among survivors:** median 7.3 days (IQR 3.6-14.9; n=5,211) vs 7.7 (3.8-15.3; n=5,290); sHR 1.00 (95% CI 0.96-1.04), P=.97.
- **Acute-hospital stay among survivors:** 20 days (11-40; n=4,791) vs 21 (10-42; n=4,906); sHR 0.98 (95% CI 0.94-1.02), P=.27.
- **Days alive and free from organ support at day 30:** median 16 days (IQR -1 to 25; n=7,327) in each group (usual n=7,444); proportional OR 1.01 (95% CI 0.96-1.07), P=.64. Component 30-day mortality: 2,435/7,449 (32.7%) vs 2,427/7,573 (32.0%).
- **Mortality at ICU discharge:** 2,122/7,334 (28.9%) vs 2,161/7,453 (29.0%); RD -0.1 percentage points (95% CI -1.3 to 1.1), P=.94.
- **Mortality at acute-hospital discharge:** 2,533/7,335 (34.5%) vs 2,535/7,458 (34.0%); RD 0.5 (95% CI -0.8 to 1.9), P=.46.
- **60-day mortality:** 2,637/7,449 (35.4%) vs 2,617/7,573 (34.6%); RD 0.8 (95% CI -0.6 to 2.2), P=.25.
- **1-year mortality:** 2,295/5,636 (40.7%) vs 2,314/5,755 (40.2%); RD 3.3 (95% CI -0.7 to 7.3), P=.34.
- **Time to death - Results (PDF p 6):** adjusted HR 1.01 (95% CI 0.96-1.05); reported as not significantly different. The same text states no significant differences in ICU/hospital stay or days alive/free of organ support.
- **Serious adverse events - Results (PDF p 6):** 58 (0.7%) conservative vs 29 (0.4%) usual.

## Intervention-exposure claims

- **Abstract (PDF p 1):** supplemental-oxygen exposure was 29% lower with conservative therapy.
- **Results, Oxygen Exposure (PDF p 6):** total exposure 20.3 vs 28.7 100%-equivalent hours; difference -8.4 hours (95% CI -10.8 to -6.0), 29.3% lower. Mean (SD) median FIO2 0.31 (0.14) vs 0.35 (0.15); median SpO2 93.3% (2.8%) vs 95.1% (2.4%); median PaO2 71.5 (13.9) vs 79.5 (17.9) mm Hg. Figure 2 (PDF p 7) displays separation over the first 10 days in 1,252 conservative and 1,237 usual enhanced-data participants.

- Diagnostic subgroup: HIE RD -1.3 (95% CI -6.2 to 3.6), OR 0.94 (0.76-1.17); sepsis 0.9 (-1.6 to 3.3), OR 1.04 (0.93-1.17); acute brain injury -3.2 (-12.8 to 6.5), OR 0.86 (0.56-1.34); none of prespecified subgroups 1.0 (-0.8 to 2.8), OR 1.05 (0.96-1.16).
- COVID-19: no 1.0 (-0.4 to 2.4), OR 1.05 (0.98-1.13); yes -3.7 (-9.3 to 2.0), OR 0.85 (0.66-1.09).
- Ethnic group: Asian 1.9 (-6.1 to 9.9), OR 1.10 (0.74-1.63); Black -6.1 (-15.7 to 3.4), OR 0.71 (0.41-1.22); White 1.3 (-0.3 to 2.9), OR 1.07 (0.99-1.15); Mixed -0.0 (-15.0 to 14.9), OR 1.00 (0.39-2.52); other/not stated -0.3 (-4.6 to 4.1), OR 0.99 (0.79-1.23).
- **Results (PDF p 6):** no statistically significant interaction for those three prespecified subgroup variables; post hoc severity-of-illness subgroup also reported non-significant. The article describes weak evidence of increased harm in the first-10-per-site data-collection subgroup, with no difference for the random enhanced-data subgroup compared with standard data collection.
