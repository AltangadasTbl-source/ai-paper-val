eFigure 5. Infection Disposition at Day 15
Abbreviations: CI, Confidence Interval; OR, odds ratio.
This endpoint applies on day 15 and the clinical state of the infection was considered
as: resolved, according to the criteria for infection resolution provided in eTable 3;
treatment failure, if infection persisted or relapsed; Superinfection, if a new infection
due to a new pathogen emerged; and intermediate, if the outcome could not be
classified in one of the above categories.
The OR for worse disposition/outcome includes superinfection, treatment failure and
intermediate infection disposition
