# Coordinator candidate selection

Scientific audit scope: DOC-001 PDF pages 1–10 and DOC-003 PDF pages 4–35. DOC-002 and DOC-003 pages 1–3 and 36 are Not Audited by Design. The three checker outputs were deduplicated into the following 10 candidates for one evidence-verification stage.

1. **C01 — Participant flow arithmetic.** DOC-001 p. 6, Figure 2: 2232 assessed, 1740 excluded, and 491 randomized; 2232 − 1740 = 492.
2. **C02 — Baseline sex counts.** DOC-001 p. 5, Baseline Measures and Demographics: each n=245 arm displays 105 female and 145 male; 105 + 145 = 250.
3. **C03 — Rerandomization attendance wording.** DOC-001 p. 4 says the 40 CNRT and 35 varenicline participants “did attend rerandomization”; Figure 2 on p. 6 labels the same groups as not attending.
4. **C04 — CNRT+ lozenge dose.** DOC-001 p. 3 Figure 1 and DOC-003 pp. 15, 17–20 define CNRT+ with 2-mg lozenges; DOC-003 p. 14 E-Figure 1 caption says 4 mg.
5. **C05 — E-Table 3 race/ethnicity total.** DOC-003 p. 19, varenicline nonabstainer → CNRT (N=41) column: displayed category counts sum to 43 and percentages to 104.8%; the “Other” cell also reverses n (%) order.
6. **C06 — Varenicline switch-versus-stay direction/sign.** DOC-001 pp. 5 and 7 show switch 0/41 versus stay 2/77 and report switch-minus-stay RD −3%, but the prose says continuing was worse; DOC-003 p. 21 E-Table 4 labels switch versus stay and prints +3%.
7. **C07 — E-Table 2 nonmonotone power.** DOC-003 pp. 8 and 18: for the same simulations, power is 0.948 at posterior-probability threshold 0.80 and 0.980 at the stricter 0.85 threshold.
8. **C08 — Decimal-shifted secondary-outcome prose.** DOC-003 pp. 10–12 includes values such as 1.0% (7.0%–1.3%) and 1.1% (−1.0%–22%), while linked E-Figures/E-Tables on pp. 15–16 and 33–35 show 10% (7%–13%) and 11% (−1%–22%).
9. **C09 — Varenicline-continuation denominator.** DOC-001 p. 7 and DOC-003 pp. 9–10 use n=42, while DOC-003 E-Figures 2–3 on pp. 15–16 use 0/77; DOC-001 Figure 2 shows 42 rerandomized plus 35 nonreturners assigned to continuation.
10. **C10 — Adverse-event summary versus displayed estimates.** DOC-003 p. 22 says no adverse-event differences exceeded 2% except nausea in VAR, but E-Table 5 on pp. 22–24 displays multiple absolute differences above 2 percentage points; DOC-001 p. 7 uses the narrower condition “>2% with nonoverlapping CrIs.”

Candidates not selected after deduplication/prioritization remain documented in the checker responses. Selection does not imply acceptance.
