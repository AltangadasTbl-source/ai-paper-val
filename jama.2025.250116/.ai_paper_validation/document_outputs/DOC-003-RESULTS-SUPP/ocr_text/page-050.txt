eFigure 6. Time to Reversal of Sepsis-Induced Immune Dysfunction
The reversal of immune dysfunction was studied in almost half of the studied
patients, according to the study protocol. Groups were compared by Cox regression
analysis and the HR was censored by Day 28. Blood measurements were done
before start of the study drug and they were repeated on days 2, 4, 7, 15, 21 and 28.
Missing draws were for patients dying or discharged earlier. This was defined as a) at
least 15% decrease of ferritin for patients with MALS remaining decreased over
follow-up time draws; and b) increase of the absolute number of HLA-DR receptors to
more than 8,000 per CD45/CD14-monocyte for patients with SII remaining above
these values over follow-up time draws. Patients not having at least two serial
number of blood draws to confirm the permanence of the changes were considered
not to attain reversal of sepsis-induced organ dysfunction.
