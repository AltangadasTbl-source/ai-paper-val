## Candidate comparison points for later verification (not error determinations)

1. **eTable 2 red-wine baseline summary:** PDF p. 7, eTable 2, row “Red wine (g/week), Baseline, median (IQR)” reports intervention **33 (0, 29)** and control **4 (0, 29)**. The intervention central value is greater than the displayed upper IQR bound (33 > 29). This is visible in both native text and the direct render. A human should check the source table/typesetting and underlying table export; no conclusion is made here about the intended value.

2. **eFigure 2 versus the direction of continuous-change results:** On PDF p. 26, the labeled intervention/control compliance percentages are lower for intervention for ↑MUFA (72%/77%), ↑MUFA:SFA (79%/86%), ↑fruit (58%/63%), and ↑vegetables (56%/64%), each plotted with p<0.001. In contrast, the relevant continuous mean-change contrasts favor higher intervention values in eTable 5 (completers, PDF pp. 14–15) and eTable 8 (baseline-value replacement, PDF pp. 21–22)—for example 12-month MUFA +0.9%E (0.6 to 1.2), p<0.001 (p. 14) and MUFA:SFA +0.2 (0.2 to 0.2), p<0.001 (p. 15). The differing analysis sets and undefined compliance thresholds/denominators preclude an inference. A human should verify the figure legend/color assignment and retrieve the author-defined compliance rules before comparing these summaries.

3. **eFigure 1 cross-display check:** PDF p. 25’s plotted pattern and p values visually align with eTable 3’s *completer* baseline-plus-change estimates (PDF p. 9), rather than serving as a clearly labelled display of the all-randomized baseline-replacement sensitivity analysis in eTable 6 (PDF p. 16). The figure lacks a stated analytic population in its caption. A human can resolve this by checking the plotting dataset or figure-generation code; this is a provenance/label check, not an inconsistency determination.
