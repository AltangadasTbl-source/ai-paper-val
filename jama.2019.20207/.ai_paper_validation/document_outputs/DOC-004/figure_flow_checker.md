## Candidate 1 — Total-vegetables panel omits its measurement unit

- **Category / severity:** Presentation inconsistency / minor.
- **Issue statement:** The eFigure's `Total vegetables` panel does not state a measurement unit, although the directly corresponding eTable row and main-article Table 2 both identify total vegetables as servings per day.
- **Reported visual item:** DOC-004, PDF p. 3, eFigure, left panel: title `Total vegetables`; y-axis ticks 0–15; x-axis labels `Baseline`, `12 m`, and `24 m`. No unit appears in the panel title or axis label.
- **Comparator:** DOC-004, PDF p. 2, eTable, row `Total vegetables (servings/day)`; DOC-001, PDF p. 7 / printed p. 146, Table 2, row `Total vegetables, servings/d`.
- **Logical comparison:** The eFigure depicts the same named dietary measure at the same three time points as the eTable, and the visible values are on the same 0–15 scale. The two tabular sources explicitly define that measure's unit as servings/day, but the visual does not display it. This is an omission of measurement context, not a discrepancy in the plotted values.
- **Bounded impact:** A reader of the figure alone cannot determine whether the plotted total-vegetables values represent servings/day or another scale; the unit is recoverable from the linked table.
- **Human verification:** (1) Inspect DOC-004 PDF p. 3, left eFigure panel for a unit in the title, either axis, or caption. (2) Inspect DOC-004 PDF p. 2 eTable and DOC-001 PDF p. 7 Table 2 for the label `Total vegetables (servings/day)` / `Total vegetables, servings/d`. (3) Confirm the candidate if the panel contains no unit while the linked table labels do; resolve it if a visible unit was missed.
