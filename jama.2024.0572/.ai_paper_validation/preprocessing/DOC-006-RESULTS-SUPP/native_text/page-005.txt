eFigure 1. Illustrative CT Perfusion imaging in a patient with occlusion of the M1 segment of
left middle cerebral artery.
The overall rCBF and Tmax maps are provided in the first series, whereas the second series
provides an illustration for maps using rCBF<30% and Tmax>6s thresholds. The region with
severely reduced relative cerebral blood flow (rCBF) <30% (pink region on the left) is estimated
to be irreversibly injured due to ischemia. The region with time to maximum intensity (Tmax) >6
s (green region on the right) represents delayed perfusion and thus brain area at the risk of
infarction in the absence of restored blood flow (reperfusion). The ratio of tissue volume with 1)
Tmax >6s and 2) rCBF <30% is termed the mismatch ratio. The volumetric difference between
the two regions is termed the mismatch volume and estimates the volume of ischemic penumbra
(brain tissue that is at risk of infarction but can be salvaged if blood flow to the area is rapidly
restored). For this illustrative case, the rCBF <30% volume was 94ml and Tmax >6s volume was
131ml. Thus, mismatch ratio was 1.4 and mismatch volume was 37 ml.
