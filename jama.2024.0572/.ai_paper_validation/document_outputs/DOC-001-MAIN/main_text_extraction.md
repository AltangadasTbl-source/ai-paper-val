## Main-article conclusion claims

- EVT compared with medical management improved clinical outcomes across a wide spectrum of infarct volumes/ischemic injury and penumbra profiles; enrollment with minimal penumbra was low. In EVT-treated patients, outcomes worsened as presenting ischemic-injury estimates increased. **PDF p. 1, abstract *Conclusion and Relevance*; PDF p. 12, *Conclusions*.**
- The text characterizes the 150-mL-or-greater core subgroup estimates as having wide confidence intervals and describes few patients with very large cores (>150 mL) or no mismatch. **PDF p. 10, *Discussion*, para. 1; PDF p. 11, *Discussion*.**
- The article states that specific Figure 3 predicted probabilities are unvalidated/calibrated and should be interpreted with caution. **PDF p. 11, *Limitations*.**
