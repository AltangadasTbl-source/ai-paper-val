Key Points
Question Does augmentation of enteral protein improve
outcomes in critically ill patients?
Findings In this cluster randomized, crossover, open-label trial of
3397 patients from 8 intensive care units, additional enteral
protein did not increase days free of the index hospital and alive at
day 90 compared with usual care (median difference, −1.97 days).
Meaning Augmenting enteral protein for patients in the intensive
care unit does not improve outcomes.
