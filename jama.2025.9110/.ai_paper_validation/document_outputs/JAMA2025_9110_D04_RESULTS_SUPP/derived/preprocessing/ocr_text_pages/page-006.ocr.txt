Four chains were implemented, and 1000 posterior samples were retained from each chain after a burn-in of 1000
iterations, resulting in 4000 samples per parameter for the calculation of posterior summaries (mean and 95% credible
interval, which is calculated from the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the posterior samples). Trace plots and standard
Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) diagnostic criteria, including the potential scale reduction (R) statistic and the
effective sample sizes (ESS), were used to check convergence.

Duration of invasive ventilation
Tobit mixed effects modelling was performed using the metobit function in Stata, which by default, reports effect
estimates for the latent variable assumed by the tobit model. Stata’s margins function, with dydx(treatment variable) and
predict(ystar(0,.)) was used to derive an estimate of the treatment effect on the observed outcome. An estimate of the
ICC was obtained using the postestimation command estat icc.
Subgroup analyses
P-values for the test of interaction between treatment and each subgroup was derived from a likelihood ratio test
comparing the quantile mixed models excluding and including the interaction term using the Irtest function from R’s
Imtest package’.
A subgroup specific treatment effect estimate for the primary outcome was obtained from the relevant coefficient
estimates from the interaction model (treatment (main) effect estimate for reference subgroup and treatment (main)
effect estimate plus interaction term estimate for the non-reference subgroup) and corresponding 95% Cls were
calculated using the block-bootstrap method.
