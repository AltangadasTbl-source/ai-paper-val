        Recovery:
        No recovery period is required; patients can immediately mobilise after the assessment is
        complete. Rarely, some patients may exhibit temporary leg/foot paraesthesia which settles
        spontaneously.


        Objectives and analysis:

        The objectives of this sub-study are exploratory – our hypothesis is that MSNA will identify a subset
        of patients with autonomic dysfunction that have an altered or distinct response to rate control
        therapy.
        Change in sympathetic activity from baseline to after treatment with rate control will be analysed in
        comparison to change in heart rate to understand if sympathetic activity broadly interacts with
        treatment effects. The results will be used as pilot data to formulate a new research study
        investigating the role of autonomic dysfunction and outcomes in patients with AF. An analysis plan
        for this sub-study will be agreed before access to data.
