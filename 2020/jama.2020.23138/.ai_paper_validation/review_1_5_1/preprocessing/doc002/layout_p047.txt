        10.1 Recording and Assessment of Adverse Events

        Targeted adverse events as they appear on the CRF will be reportable to the RATE-AF Trial Office
        up to 30 days post last IMP administration. Any SUSAR related to the IMP should to be reported
        irrespective of how long after IMP administration the reaction has occurred.
        Adverse events will be recorded in the medical records and CRFs. Most AE/ARs that occur in this
        trial, whether they are serious or not, will be ‘expected’ treatment-related toxicities due to the drugs
        used in this trial.
