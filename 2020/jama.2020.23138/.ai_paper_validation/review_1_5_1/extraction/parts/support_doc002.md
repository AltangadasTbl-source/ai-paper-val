# DOC-002 fresh support extraction — RATE-AF protocol

## Scope and method

Direct source: `joi200126supp1_prod_1607962892.5372.pdf`, PDF pages 1–69 (all assigned units). Fresh direct extraction was performed with `pdftotext` and `pdftotext -layout` for each page, retained as `preprocessing/doc002/native_p001.txt` through `native_p069.txt` and `layout_p001.txt` through `layout_p069.txt`. Image-only Figure 1 and Appendices A–B were rendered fresh at 160 dpi as `render_p016.png`, `render_p068.png`, and `render_p069.png` and visually inspected. No OCR was needed: native text was usable; rendering was necessary only for graphic content absent from native extraction.

This is a protocol, not a results supplement. It supplies planned-result definitions, populations, contrasts, time points, measures, denominators, analysis rules, formulas, and operational schedules that can match main-paper results. It reports no trial-arm outcome results.

## Cross-location observations needing main-paper matching

1. `N1002`: protocol uses both “physical component summary” and “physical functioning domain” for the named primary endpoint. The contrast is not resolved inside DOC-002; do not collapse the terms without checking the matched main-paper result.
2. `N1016`: protocol’s clinical outcome label says BNP but the laboratory assay section names NT-proBNP and provides NT-proBNP units/range. This may be a shorthand or a label mismatch; identify the main-paper analyte and units before adjudication.
3. `N1012`: Table 1 makes 24-hour ECG at 6 months and serum digoxin at 6/12 months parenthesised/conditional, so unavailable-case denominators must not be assumed equal to all randomized participants.

No candidate ID is assigned in this mapper artifact. Coherent planned thresholds and operational numbers are not treated as result inconsistencies.
