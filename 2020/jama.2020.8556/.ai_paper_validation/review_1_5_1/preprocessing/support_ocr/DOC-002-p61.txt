Short title: | PRINCESS SAP

CAAD by infection (UTI, RTI, skin, GI, unexplained fever)

Separate Poisson regression models will be fitted to explore the treatment effect on CAAD for each
infection type (UTI, RTI, skin, GL, unexplained fever). The length of observation time per CHR will be
included in the model. The means, standard errors, adjusted IRR will be provided alongside 95% Cl
and p-values.

Incidence of infection

The mean infection rate will be compared by trial arm using Poisson regression. The length of
observation time per CHR will be adjusted. Results will be presented as the means, standard errors,
adjusted IRR between trial arms, along with 95% Cl and p-value.

Site of infection

Separate Poisson regression models will be fitted to compare the mean infection rate for each type of
infection (UTI, RTI, skin, Gl, unexplained fever). The length of observation time per CHR will be
adjusted. Results will be presented as the adjusted IRR between trial arms, along with 95% Cl and p-
value.

Mean duration of infections

A comparison of the mean duration of infections will be carried by fitting a two-level linear regression
model, with participants nested within care homes. The adjusted mean difference will be provided
along with 95% Cl and p-values.

Cumulative number of infection days

The cumulative number of days that participant experienced a suspected infection will be compared
between trial arms using Poisson regression, with the length of observation time per participant
included in the model. The means, standard errors, adjusted IRR between treatment arms will be
provided alongside 95% Cl and p-values.

Incidence of antibiotic-associated diarrhoea

The mean incidence rate of antibiotic-associated diarrhoea will be compared by trial arm using two-
level Poisson regression. The length of observation time per CHR will be adjusted. Results will be
presented as means and standard errors, the adjusted IRR between trial arms, along with 95% Cl and
p-value.
