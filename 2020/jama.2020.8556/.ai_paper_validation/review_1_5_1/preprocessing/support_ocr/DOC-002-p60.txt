6.2 ANALYSIS METHODS

6.2.1 LIST OF METHODS AND PRESENTATION

Primary analysis

The differences in the mean cumulative antibiotic administration days (CAAD) will be compared
between trial arms. A two-level Poisson regression model, with responses from care home residents
(CHRs) nested within care home, will be fitted in order to evaluate the effect of oral probiotics on
CAAD for all-cause infections.

The CAAD count will be included as the outcome. Participant gender will be included in the model, as
it was minimised on. Since the observation time for each participant is likely to vary, the length of time
observed will be included in the model.

The results will be presented using the mean and standard error for the rate in each trial arm, the
adjusted incidence rate ratio (IRR) between trial arms, along with the 95% confidence interval (Cl) and
p-value. Negative binomial regression will be used in the presence of over-dispersed count data.
Secondary analysis

For the analysis of each of the secondary outcomes, models will include participant gender as a fixed
effect and care home as a random effect.
