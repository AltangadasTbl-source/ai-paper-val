5.4 WITHDRAWAL/FOLLOW UP
5.4.1 LEVEL OF WITHDRAWAL
The level of consent withdrawal will be tabulated and classified as following:
e Withdrawal from entire trial and does not want any data or samples already collected relating
to them to be used
e Withdrawal from trial, study product and all subsequent trial follow-up (sample collection and
data collection), but data and samples already obtained up to this point can be used
© Withdrawal from study product but happy to continue with follow-up processes (to include
data collection and/or sample collection) — this may be due to a request to withdraw, or as a
result of a change in the participant’s condition or circumstances which justifies the
discontinuation of the study product
¢ Withdrawal from study product and all subsequent sample and questionnaire data collection,
but happy for routine care record data to be collected.
5.4.2 TIMING OF WITHDRAWAL
Participant may withdraw consent to participate in this study at any time. Information on the timing
of withdrawal will be presented in the CONSORT diagram, with numbers and reasons for withdrawal,
in relation to the key study time points (after screening, before baseline, before randomisation, after
three months follow-up, etc.}.
5.4.3 REASONS FOR WITHDRAWAL
Reasons for withdrawal will be tabulated and compared between trial arms to ascertain any
differential bias that may be present in those who have remained in the study.
