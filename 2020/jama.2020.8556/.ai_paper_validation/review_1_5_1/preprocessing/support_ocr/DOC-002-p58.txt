6.1.2 TIMING, UNITS AND DERIVATION OF PRIMARY OUTCOME
Cumulative antibiotic administration days {CAAD) will be collected for up to 12 months post-
randomisation. The CAAD will be expressed as a rate, with the number of exposure days (i.e. time at
risk) as the denominator and antibiotic administration days the numerator. If a participant has been
prescribed an antibiotic but this has not been administered, this will be regarded as not taken and will
not count towards the analysis. The focus of this outcome is on antibiotics for systemic infection/s.
Therefore, any topical antibiotics or antibiotics (of any route) prescribed as prophylaxis will be
excluded from the numerator. Prophylactic antibiotics will either be defined in free-text or defined as
antibiotic administration (same type) lasting for > 30 days. These exclusions apply to all CAAD
outcomes. Assumptions around CAAD that will be made during periods of hospitalisation are listed in
Appendix IV.
