Short title: | PRINCESS SAP

Cumulative number of antibiotic-associated diarrhoea days
The cumulative number of antibiotic-associated diarrhoea days will be compared by fitting a two-level
Poisson regression model. The means, standard errors, adjusted incidence rate ratio will be provided
along with 95% Cl and p-value. .
Health utility (EQ-5D-5L)
Health utility will be compared between trial arms by fitting two two-level linear regression models to
scores on the EQSD scale at three months and final follow-up individually, with participants nested
within care homes. Baseline reported EQ-5D-5L scale will be controlled for as a covariate. The adjusted
mean difference will be provided alongside 95% Cl and p-values. Proxy- and self-completed versions
will be analysed separately.
Health status (EQ-VAS)
Ina similar way, the EQ Visual Analogue scale (EQ VAS) will be compared between trial arms by fitting
two two-level linear regression models to scores on the EQ VAS at three months and the final follow-
up. Baseline reported EQ-VAS will be controlled for as a covariate. Proxy- and self-completed versions
will be analysed separately. The adjusted mean difference will be provided alongside 95% CI and p-
values.
Well-being (ICECAP-O)
Ina similar way to the above, well-being will be compared between trial arms by fitting two two-level
linear regression models to scores on the ICECAP-O scale at three months and the final follow-up.
Proxy- and self-completed versions will be analysed separately.
Ever been hospitalised
The proportion of participants who have reported hospitalisation over the follow-up period will be
estimated using a two-level logistic regression model. The adjusted odds ratio (OR) will be provided
alongside 95% Cl and p-values.
Cumulative number of hospital days
The cumulative number of hospital days will be compared between arms by fitting a two-level Poisson
regression model. The means, standard errors, adjusted incidence rate ratio will be provided along
with 95% Cl and p-value.
