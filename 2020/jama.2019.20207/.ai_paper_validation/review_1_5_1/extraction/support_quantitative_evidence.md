- **PDF pp. 5, 15-18:** Eligibility: diagnosis <=24 months, >=10 cores, <25% positive cores, <=50% any core, stage <=T2a, PSA <10 ng/mL, age 50-80; Gleason <=6 if <=70 and <=7 (3+4) if >70; three run-in 24-hour recalls and <6 fruit/vegetable servings/day excluding juices. Objectives are clinical progression, treatment incidence, anxiety, and HRQOL.
