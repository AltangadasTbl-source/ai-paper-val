## Page-complete map

### PDF page 1 — Abstract

- **M-N001 — Trial population and allocation.** The study was conducted at 91 US urology and medical oncology clinics. It enrolled 478 men aged 50-80 years with biopsy-proven early-stage prostate adenocarcinoma; enrollment ran January 2011-August 2015 and 24-month follow-up ran January 2013-August 2017. Randomization was MEAL intervention `n=237` versus control `n=241`. Intervention target: 7 or more daily vegetable servings. Primary-analysis population: 443 eligible patients (93%).
- **M-N002 — Baseline descriptive measures.** Among 478 randomized patients, mean (SD) age was `64 (7) years` and mean (SD) PSA was `4.9 (2.1) ng/mL`.
- **M-N003 / M-S001 — Primary event and effect estimates.** There were 245 progression events: intervention 124 and control 121. The unadjusted hazard ratio was `0.96 (95% CI, 0.75 to 1.24)` and adjusted hazard ratio was `0.97 (95% CI, 0.76 to 1.25)`; the contrast is intervention versus control for time to progression.
- **M-N004 / M-S002 — 24-month progression-free percentages.** Intervention `43.5% (95% CI, 36.5% to 50.6%)`; control `41.4% (95% CI, 34.3% to 48.7%)`; printed difference `2.1% (95% CI, −8.1% to 12.2%)`. The statistic is Kaplan-Meier progression-free percentage at 24 months, not a raw event proportion.

### PDF page 2 — Methods and population definitions

- **M-N005 — Randomization and stratification definitions.** Eligible patients were randomized 1:1. Dynamic allocation was stratified by age (`<70` versus `≥70` years), race/ethnicity (African American versus other), and time since diagnostic biopsy (`0-12` versus `>12 and ≤24` months before registration).
- **M-N006 — Eligibility thresholds.** Age 50-80 years; stage cT2a or less; diagnosis within 24 months of baseline; biopsy with `≥10` cores; grade group 1 in men younger than 70 and grade group 2 or less in men aged 70 or older; PSA `<10 ng/mL`; `<25%` total cores and `≤50%` of any single core with adenocarcinoma. Baseline fruit/vegetable consumption `≥6 servings/d` was an exclusion criterion.
- **M-N007 — No applicable result relationship.** The remainder of this page describes background, governance, and intervention setup. It contains no additional outcome result requiring a numeric cross-location match.

### PDF page 3 — Outcome definitions, assessments, sample-size basis, and analysis population

- **M-N008 — Intervention dose and dietary assessment.** The intervention encouraged `≥7` daily vegetable-fruit servings, each defined as one-half cup raw/cooked vegetables or fruits or 100% vegetable juice, including `≥2` servings each of cruciferous vegetables and tomatoes. It comprised four phases: 6 calls/1 month, 4/2 months, 4/4 months, and 8/16 months; counselor training was 80 hours. Dietary assessments occurred at baseline, 12 months, and 24 months, with `≥3` complete interviews required at each time point; plasma carotenoids were measured at baseline and 12 months.
- **M-N009 — Primary outcome definition and time origin.** Time to progression was from random assignment to PSA `≥10 ng/mL`, PSA doubling time `<3 years`, or pathological progression: `>25%` cancer-positive cores, `>50%` of any 1 core positive, or ISUP grade group `>1` for those younger than 70 and `>2` for those aged 70 or older. Death without progression and elective treatment without progression were censoring events as specified.
- **M-N010 / M-S003 — PSA and sample-size calculation.** PSA was assessed every 3 months. At 6 months PSADT used baseline, month 3, and month 6 values; from month 9 onward it used all available values provided there were at least 3 measurements. The two-sided log-rank design used `α=5%`, sample size 418, and `≥80%` power to detect 20% control versus 10% experimental progression during 24 months; 57 combined events were specified. Under an exponential TTP assumption, 20% versus 10% corresponds to HR `2.1`; with a 10% dropout assumption, targeted enrollment was 464. The pilot-study dropout rate cited was 2%.
- **M-N011 / M-S004 — Analysis-set and model definitions.** Database freeze: April 9, 2018. Primary analysis excluded randomized participants later deemed ineligible by eligibility or central-pathology review; those without documented progression were censored at last follow-up. TTP used Kaplan-Meier and log-rank methods; Cox proportional-hazards models estimated HRs and 95% CIs. Supportive Cox models adjusted for the 3 stratification factors, and an intention-to-treat analysis used all randomized participants. The proportional-hazards assessment used a Kolmogorov-type supremum test plus graphical checks. A biopsy-only sensitivity analysis and a post hoc shared-frailty Cox model were also specified.

### PDF page 4 — Figure 1, statistical-model detail, and result population

- **M-S005 — Correlative and secondary analysis definitions.** Time to treatment was random assignment to alternative therapy; death before alternative therapy was censored. Treatment-free percentages used Kaplan-Meier and comparison used log-rank; treatment numbers/percentages were compared with Fisher exact test. Dietary and carotenoid outcomes used linear mixed-effects models with study group, categorical time (baseline, 12, 24 months), and group-by-time fixed effects; a participant-specific intercept; appropriate contrasts; likelihood-ratio and Wald tests. Missing outcome data were assumed missing at random. Plasma carotenoid models could use log transformation. Secondary/correlative endpoints were exploratory, all P values two-sided, and `P≤.05` was the stated significance threshold.
