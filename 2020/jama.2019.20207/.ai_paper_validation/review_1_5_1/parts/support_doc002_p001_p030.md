Table 1 and Table 2 percent changes cannot be exactly recomputed from their rounded one- or two-decimal displayed means in several cells (for example, control total vegetables 3.7 to 4.2 is 13.5% using displayed values, while the table shows 12%). The protocol reports means rounded for display; this is a rounding-sensitive observation, not a candidate or a conclusion.
