    2.0     OBJECTIVES

          2.1     Primary Objective
                  To determine if a telephone-based dietary intervention compared to no intervention will
                  decrease clinical progression in AS patients.

          2.2     Secondary Objectives

                2.2.1 To compare the incidence of active treatment (surgery, irradiation, local ablation, or
                      androgen deprivation) in AS patients receiving dietary intervention compared to no
                      intervention.

                2.2.2 To compare prostate cancer-related anxiety in AS patients receiving dietary
                      intervention compared to no intervention.

                2.2.3 To compare health-related quality of life in AS patients receiving dietary intervention
                      compared to no intervention.
          [See Section 10.0 for correlative science objectives.]
