## Result-relevant quantitative evidence

### Trial frame, interventions, and populations

- **N100 — Trial frame and treatment quantities.** PDF p. 1 states a 2×2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled factorial VITAL trial among 20,000 men and women aged at least 60 and 65 years, respectively, with 5 years of follow-up. The planned vitamin D3 dose is 2,000 IU/day; marine omega-3 is EPA 500 mg/day plus DHA 500 mg/day. PDF pp. 3 and 15 repeat the 20,000 full cohort and 5-year frame. **Matching main-paper key:** randomized population; vitamin-D and omega-3 intervention dose; follow-up duration.
- **N101 — Planned analytic eligibility.** PDF p. 3 says up to 20,000 could be enrolled but anticipates 18,200 meeting additional VITAL-DEP eligibility criteria. PDF p. 9 repeats 20,000 recruited to VITAL and 18,200 eligible for depression-prevention analyses. This is an expected planning denominator, not an observed enrolled/analytic total. **Matching main-paper key:** depression-free/eligible analytic population and exclusions.
- **N102 — Demographic and subgroup denominators.** PDF p. 8 plans 10,000 men aged at least 60 and 10,000 women aged at least 65 (50% each). Expected ethnicity is 1,400 (7%) Hispanic and 18,600 (93%) non-Hispanic. Expected race is 5,000 (25%) African-American, 500 (2.5%) Asian, 400 (2%) American Indian, 80 (0.4%) Pacific Islander, and 14,020 (70.1%) White; race and ethnicity can overlap. PDF pp. 1-2 and 15 also specify African-American representation of 25%. **Matching main-paper key:** baseline demographic table, race/ethnicity definitions, prespecified African-American interaction.
- **N103 — Nested case-control sampling and assays.** PDF p. 5 specifies 500 depression cases and 1,000 controls (total 1,500), matched on 5-year age group, gender, follow-up time, and season of blood draw. PDF p. 19 calls this a 1:2 matched nested case-control study in 1,500 persons. It assays baseline pre-randomization 25(OH)D, EPA, and DHA. **Matching main-paper key:** any biomarker/nested case-control analysis; sample and matching variables.
- **N104 — CTSC subcohort.** PDF pp. 2 and 14 describe a random CTSC subset of 1,000 participants, evaluated in-person at four VITAL-affiliated CTSCs at baseline and 2 years; p. 16 identifies the CTSC subcohort as n=1,000 and says it needed a separate IRB application. **Matching main-paper key:** CTSC-only subgroup analyses; this protocol does not establish a full-cohort result denominator.

### Outcome definitions, sources, time points, and units

- **N105 — Composite depression endpoint and follow-up measurements.** PDF pp. 3 and 16 define “depression” as clinically significant depressive syndrome, comprising DSM-IV/ICD-9 major depressive disorder, dysthymia, adjustment disorder including depressed mood, and depressive disorder NOS. Questionnaires occur at baseline/year 0 and follow-up years 1, 3, and 5 (PDF pp. 5 and 16). **Matching main-paper key:** incident/recurrent depression endpoint definition and assessment schedule.
- **N106 — PHQ-8 score scale and thresholds.** PDF p. 5 uses PHQ-8 at an algorithmic cutoff of at least 10 for a specified follow-up-letter condition and at least 15 for letters regardless of recent diagnosis/treatment. PDF p. 17 defines the continuous PHQ-8 range as 0-24; scores at least 15 and at least 20 signal moderate-high and high severity, respectively. It says the instrument differs from PHQ-9 by removal of the suicidality item. PDF p. 20 repeats PHQ-8 at least 10 and at least 15 solely as safety-contact thresholds. **Matching main-paper key:** PHQ-8 scale, repeated continuous outcome, and any reported threshold analysis. Do not conflate safety thresholds with the primary endpoint algorithm.
- **N107 — Administrative-code table and validation quantities.** PDF p. 18 Table 1 lists depressive-disorder ICD-9 codes: 296.2, 296.20-296.26, 296.3, 296.30-296.36, 300.4, 309.0-309.1, 309.28, and 311; subrows specify MDD, dysthymia, NOS, and adjustment disorder codes. The same page reports a cited validation: κ=0.67, sensitivity 56.6, specificity 99.4, positive predictive value 92.8, and negative predictive value 94.4 for ICD-9-CM versus medical records. These are cited validation-study characteristics, not trial estimates. **Matching main-paper key:** outcome ascertainment and claims-code endpoint definition.
- **N121 — Internal table-reference label.** Immediately above the displayed table on PDF p. 18, the prose says ICD-9 codes identify depression “(Table 3),” whereas the table caption directly below is “Table 1. ICD-9 Codes Identifying Relevant Depressive Disorders.” **Matching main-paper key:** none; this is a within-protocol table-reference label. It is a possible source-grounded label-consistency candidate for later ledger/recheck, not assigned a C ID here.
- **N108 — Incident/recurrent eligibility and person-time rule.** PDF pp. 18-19 state that incidence eligibility is determined using PHQ-8, DIS, global questions, and CMS data; a first qualifying incident event may be PHQ-8 algorithmic MDD/other depressive disorder, self-reported clinician diagnosis, DIS MDD/dysthymia symptoms, qualifying ICD-9 encounter, or antidepressant registration. Earliest applicable questionnaire-receipt, CMS-visit, or dispensing date defines onset, subject to a hierarchy preferring the most specific method. Figure 1 (PDF p. 19) routes those with relevant history but no active/recent condition to recurrence eligibility and those without history/current PHQ-8 depression to incidence eligibility. In analyses, person-time runs randomization to event or censoring and stops after incident depression; the protocol says incident cases will not later be double-counted as recurrent cases. **Matching main-paper key:** analysis population, endpoint-adjudication algorithm, event date, and risk-time denominator.
- **N109 — Biomarker measurement units and precision.** PDF p. 20 specifies 25(OH)D2/D3 using NIST SRM 972; both 25(OH)D radioimmunoassay and plasma fatty-acid assays have intra-/inter-assay CVs below 10%. EPA, DHA, and total n-6 fatty acids use fast gas chromatography/robotic transesterification. **Matching main-paper key:** biomarker units/assay reliability and measurement-method footnotes.

### Planned analysis, sensitivity, and monitoring definitions

- **N110 — Primary time-to-event analysis.** PDF p. 21 specifies intent-to-treat main effects of vitamin D3 and fish oil. Kaplan-Meier curves estimate cumulative incident/recurrent depression; log-rank compares agent versus corresponding placebo. Cox proportional-hazards models estimate HRs and CIs, adjusted for the other intervention, age, and gender. Follow-up ends at depression endpoint, death, loss to follow-up, or trial end, whichever is first; proportional hazards will be tested analytically and graphically. The protocol specifies two-sided alpha=0.05 and SAS 9.1. **Matching main-paper key:** primary incident/recurrent time-to-event model, HR, CI, P value, covariate adjustment, censoring definition.
- **N111 — Planned sensitivity analyses.** PDF p. 21 proposes censoring follow-up at report of taking less than two-thirds of study medications during the preceding year as a compliance-related analysis. It proposes using CTSC clinical data to assess outcome misclassification and sensitivity analyses to correct full-cohort treatment-effect estimates/CIs. CTSC loss to follow-up, if incomplete, will be handled with intention-to-treat sensitivity analyses. **Matching main-paper key:** adherence, missingness, outcome-misclassification, and CTSC sensitivity analyses.
- **N112 — Longitudinal PHQ-8 model.** PDF p. 21 defines a mixed-effects model for four measures (years 0, 1, 3, and 5), fixed effects for treatment, time, and treatment×time, with within-person correlation; it estimates mean differences and 95% CIs. Secondary analyses account for antidepressant initiation. **Matching main-paper key:** continuous PHQ-8 endpoint, mean difference, CI, time point, model terms, and medication-adjustment/sensitivity analysis.
- **N113 — CTSC analysis.** PDF p. 21 plans survival analysis for the composite depression endpoint in the high-risk group and MDD in the subsyndromal group. CTSC continuous PHQ-8 uses two measures with fixed effects for CTSC site, treatment, time, and treatment×time, estimating 95% CIs and P values for two intervention main effects on change baseline-to-2-years. **Matching main-paper key:** CTSC subgroup/interaction results, change-score definition, effect scale, and site adjustment.
- **N114 — Nested case-control and effect-modification definitions.** PDF pp. 21-22 plans comparison of deficient 25(OH)D below 50 nmol/L (20 ng/mL) in cases versus controls; stated deficiency prevalence is about 35-40%. Interaction contrasts are 25(OH)D deficient versus sufficient and EPA+DHA above versus below median, with exploratory low omega-3:omega-6 ratio. Other multiplicative interactions: other intervention; African-American race versus White for D3; age; gender; baseline intakes above/below median; comorbidity; and, for D3, region/latitude and physical activity. Parameters use SAS Proc Mixed and two-sided alpha=0.05. **Matching main-paper key:** biomarker threshold/unit, interaction reference groups, effect-modification P value/model.
- **N115 — Interim-monitoring rule.** PDF pp. 11 and 25 state annual DSMB examination and Haybittle-Peto interim monitoring adjusted for multiple looks: compare interim z=3 standard deviations, p=0.0027; final results may be interpreted at close to nominal significance. The rule is a stopping guideline, not a primary-outcome significance threshold. **Matching main-paper key:** interim monitoring and any adjusted/nominial alpha explanation.

All displayed subgroup values and the expected-RR/expected-eligible footnotes are transcribed in the paired relationship table. **Matching main-paper key:** planned incidence power, expected RR; it is not a reported RR or observed sample size.
- **N117 — Table 3: recurrent-depression power.** PDF p. 23 Table 3 uses expected eligible totals: overall 2,730; women 1,856; men 874; non-Hispanic White 1,777; minority 953; African-American 683. Overall power at expected RR 0.90/0.85/0.80/0.75/0.70/0.65/0.60 is 38.2%, 70.9%, 92.1%, 98.9%, >99.9%, >99.9%, >99.9%, respectively. **Matching main-paper key:** planned recurrence power, expected RR; not an observed result.
- **N118 — CTSC power inputs/results.** PDF p. 23: of estimated 855 eligible CTSC participants, high-risk persons are about 60% (n=513); placebo 2-year incidence assumed 25%; planned power is 80% and >95% for single-agent RRs 0.6 and 0.5. For subsyndromal symptoms, 21.3% current prevalence in people at least 65 years is cited; the protocol anticipates 23% (n=196) of eligibles, assumes placebo 2-year MDD risk 35%, and projects 80% and >90% power for RRs 0.5 and 0.4. If 2-year risk is 45%, it projects 80% and >90% power for RRs 0.6 and 0.5. **Matching main-paper key:** CTSC planned subgroup denominator, risk, RR, and power; do not compare these planning assumptions directly to achieved results.
- **N119 — Nested case-control power inputs/results.** PDF pp. 23-24 estimates about 900 depression cases overall or about 500 with blood samples, matched 2:1 to controls. It assumes 35% or 40% vitamin-D deficiency among controls and omega-3 low-level cut points at the bottom 25% or 33% of the control distribution. It projects at least 80% power to detect RR=1.4 and >90% for RR=1.5. The cited observational odds ratios of 2.6 at 25(OH)D 10-19.9 ng/mL and 11.7 at below 10 ng/mL are external-study context, not VITAL-DEP findings. **Matching main-paper key:** biomarker nested-case-control sample/matching, planned RR/power, and unit cut points.
- **N120 — Interaction and continuous-outcome power.** PDF p. 24 assumes two-sided alpha=0.05, single-agent RRs 0.90-0.75, and interaction RRs 1.00 (additive) to 0.60; it projects at least 80% power for important interactions, e.g., additional risk reduction at least 30%. For PHQ continuous outcomes, it defines a 25% difference versus the baseline mean of non-depressed persons as clinically meaningful and projects >99% power for primary aims/African-American D3 differences; it projects >99% power for a 30% difference in high-risk CTSC participants and 90% for a 30% difference in subsyndromal participants. **Matching main-paper key:** interaction effect scale and continuous-PHQ effect/scale; all are power assumptions.
