# Critic Review

## Accepted scientific findings

### F01 — Major — Cross-document inconsistency: omitted mHealth cluster

- Location: DOC-003 p4 eTable 2; DOC-003 p8 eTable 5; DOC-001 p5 Table 1.
- Evidence: eTable 2 contains 17 mHealth sites totaling 680 participants and 168 prior-attempt “Yes” responses. It omits site 2012, which eTable 5 identifies as an mHealth cluster of 40 participants. Table 1 reports 178/720.
- Basis: `720 − 680 = 40`; `178 − 168 = 10`.
- Verification: Sum the mHealth rows in eTable 2, compare with Table 1, and confirm site 2012 in eTable 5.

### F02 — Major — Cross-document inconsistency: adverse-event direction reversed

- Location: DOC-001 p5; DOC-003 p15 eTable 10.
- Evidence: Main text says dry mouth, irritability, and anxiety were more common with mHealth. eTable 10 gives irritability 283/699 vs 145/334 and anxiety 233/699 vs 123/334.
- Basis: 40.5% vs 43.4% and 33.3% vs 36.8%; both are lower in mHealth. Only dry mouth follows the stated direction.
- Verification: Sum non-None severity categories by arm and compare with the prose.

### F03 — Minor — Arithmetic inconsistency: site 2008 death percentage

- Location: DOC-003 p9 eTable 6; DOC-003 p8 eTable 5.
- Evidence: Site 2008 is `5 (7.5)` deaths with denominator 40.
- Basis: `5/40 = 12.5%`, not 7.5%.
- Verification: Confirm the site denominator and recalculate.

### F04 — Minor — Arithmetic inconsistency: death-cause percentages

- Location: DOC-003 p6 eTable 4.
- Evidence: With 27 usual-care deaths, Drug user and Severe pneumonia are each printed as `1 (7.4%)`.
- Basis: `1/27 = 3.7%`, not 7.4%; other conventional one-decimal discrepancies include 3/52, 8/52, and 16/27.
- Verification: Recalculate every percentage using column totals 52, 25, and 27.

### F05 — Minor — Statistical reporting inconsistency: adverse-event percentages

- Location: DOC-001 p5; DOC-003 p15 eTable 10.
- Evidence: Main text reports nausea 23.0% vs 22.3% and diarrhea 7.5% vs 7.5%. eTable 10 yields control nausea 71/334 and mHealth diarrhea 51/699.
- Basis: `71/334 = 21.3%`, not 22.3%; `51/699 = 7.3%`, not 7.5%.
- Verification: Sum mild, moderate, and severe counts and divide by each arm total.

### F06 — Minor — Presentation inconsistency: adverse-event population unidentified

- Location: DOC-003 pp15-16 eTable 10.
- Evidence: Complete symptom blocks total 699 mHealth and 334 control, but the table does not identify this population or missingness.
- Basis: Denominators differ from randomized 720/360, complete-case 667/318, and death-excluded 695/333.
- Verification: Sum severity rows and compare with documented populations and notes.

### F07 — Minor — Statistical reporting inconsistency: ITT label after deaths excluded

- Location: DOC-001 p3 and p6 Table 2; DOC-003 p13 eTable 9.
- Evidence: The main article distinguishes primary ITT from a post hoc death-excluded analysis. Table 2 ITT is 720/360; eTable 9 calls a 695/333 death-excluded analysis ITT.
- Basis: `720 − 25 = 695`; `360 − 27 = 333`.
- Verification: Compare eTable 9 title/denominators with methods, Figure 1, and Table 2.

### F08 — Minor — Presentation inconsistency: eTable 6 title/body mismatch

- Location: DOC-003 p9 eTable 6.
- Evidence: The title promises death rates and unsuccessful tuberculosis treatment outcomes; the body contains only arm, site ID, and deaths.
- Basis: No column, row, or note presents unsuccessful-treatment outcomes.
- Verification: Compare the complete title with all displayed columns and notes.

## Uncertain candidate

### C07 — PP versus complete-case label

- Location: DOC-001 p3 and p6 Table 2.
- Evidence: Methods describe complete-case analysis; Table 2 labels 667/318 rows PP. These equal `720 − 53` and `360 − 42`.
- Reason: The package does not establish whether PP is erroneous or the authors’ label for the same population.
- Verification: Consult the analysis-population specification for separate PP criteria.

## Rejected candidate

### C09 — Subgroup scheme differs from the prespecified list

- Reason: The added SMS-reading subgroup is document-grounded, but the package does not explicitly state that every subgroup in eTable 8 was prespecified. Treating the unlabeled inclusion as an error would be speculative.
