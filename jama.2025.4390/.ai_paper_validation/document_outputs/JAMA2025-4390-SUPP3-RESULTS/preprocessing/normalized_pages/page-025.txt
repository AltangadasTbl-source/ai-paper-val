eFigure 3. Proportion of participants who withdrew or were lost to follow-up
Figure shows the proportion of participants from each allocation group that withdrew or were lost to follow-up over the duration of the trial.
Dashed lines are all participants withdrawing or lost to follow-up – many of whom we could continue to follow using administrative health
data. Solid lines are participants withdrawing or lost to follow-up who we could not continue to follow. Only 3.4% of bedtime group
participants and 2.6% of morning group participants could not be followed until the end of the trial.
