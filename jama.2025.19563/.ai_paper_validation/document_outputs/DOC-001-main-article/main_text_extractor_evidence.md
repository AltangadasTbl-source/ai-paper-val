## Main-article conclusion claim

The conclusion states that, among adults with prediabetes and overweight or obesity, referral to a fully automated AI-led DPP was noninferior to referral to a human-led DPP for the composite outcome based on weight reduction, physical activity, and HbA1c. Anchor: PDF p. 9, `Conclusions` (journal p. 2087); also Abstract, PDF p. 1.
