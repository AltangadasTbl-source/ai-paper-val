## Mapping limitations

- The PDF’s multi-column table/figure geometry was resolved by the fresh layout text and the supplied fresh renders. Native text alone is not sufficient to retain every display alignment.
- DOC-001’s narrative directs post-hoc numerical outcomes and antibiotic detail to Supplement 2. Those values are intentionally not reconstructed from DOC-001 and are assigned to the support-source mapping lane.
- The stated correction notice identifies a cervical-category-definition correction but does not reproduce the corrected/previous wording on this main-paper page; this record preserves the notice and current displayed category values without inference.
