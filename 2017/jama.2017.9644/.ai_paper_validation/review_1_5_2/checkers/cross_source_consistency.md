# Cross-source quantitative consistency review

## Scope, method, and status

Complete matched-result review of the three direct supplied PDFs: DOC-001 main article (10 PDF pages), DOC-002 protocol version V1.1 dated 24 July 2015 (14 PDF pages), and DOC-003 statistical supplement (6 PDF pages). The review used the fresh native/layout text, rendered-page assets, the main and support quantitative evidence maps, and both stable relationship inventories. It did not use legacy audit outputs or external sources.

Each comparison below was made only after matching (or explicitly distinguishing) population, time point, assigned strategy versus received device, endpoint definition, analysis set, contrast, statistical model, scale, and displayed precision. `N001`--`N081` and `S001`--`S031` were covered as applicable to cross-source matching. A repeated value is not treated as a mismatch when it has a different stated denominator, analysis set, or endpoint time.

## Provisional quality-control signal for candidate-registration review

### CS-01 — Figure 2 has a mixed count/percentage display label

- **Category if registered:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Exact source locations:** [DOC-001 p. 7, Figure 2A--B](../../../jama_lapergue_2017_oi_170084.pdf#page=7).
- **Printed evidence:** Both horizontal axes are labelled `Patients, %` and run from 0 to 100. The group labels give `n=192` and `n=189`. The numbers printed inside the A segments are `8, 2, 18, 92, 72` and `5, 5, 22, 84, 73`; the B segments are `26, 6, 39, 66, 55` and `20, 9, 32, 61, 67`.
- **Comparison logic:** Each internal sequence sums exactly to the stated group `n`, so the internal labels function as participant counts. The segment widths and 0--100 horizontal scale function as percentages. The joint axis wording does not explicitly state that the bars are percentages while the embedded labels are counts.
- **Supported alternatives:** This may be an intentional 100%-stacked bar convention with count annotations, rather than an incorrect quantitative result. The counts reproduce Table 2 endpoint totals exactly, and no numeric mismatch was observed.
- **Human verification steps:** Inspect the production figure specification or underlying figure-data labels. Confirm whether the intended axis should say `Patients, %`, `Percentage of patients`, or whether a `No.`/`n` label should accompany the embedded values. Confirm that any editorial revision preserves the matched Table 2 values.
- **Status:** Provisional signal only; no C ID or adjudication is assigned here.

## Protocol-plan provenance item (not an observable printed-result mismatch)

### PP-01 — 2015 protocol plan differs from the published methods; amendment/SAP provenance is absent

- **Locations:** [DOC-002 pp. 6--7](../../../joi170084supp1_prod.pdf#page=6); [DOC-001 pp. 3--4](../../../jama_lapergue_2017_oi_170084.pdf#page=3); [DOC-003 p. 2](../../../joi170084supp2_prod.pdf#page=2).
- **Direct observation:** The supplied protocol is explicitly version V1.1, dated 24 July 2015. It specifies a 322-participant premise and chi-square/rate-difference plan. The publication specifies 380 participants and mixed logistic regression; DOC-003 details the published model family.
- **Why it is not treated as a printed result mismatch:** These are plan-era documents, not two displays of the same completed result. No amendment history, final SAP, database-lock plan, or document showing which plan governed the final analysis is supplied. The 70% to 85% premise can also be expressed as +15 percentage points or approximately +21% relative, so the protocol wording alone does not establish a scale error.
- **Exact remaining human question:** Was there an approved protocol amendment or finalized SAP that changed the primary analysis and sample-size assumptions before database lock, and if so, is the published method consistent with that controlling document?

## Excluded apparent discrepancies and limitations

- The eTable frontline `n=175` is not recorded as a mismatch with 170 actual stent-retriever receipts because the flow diagram documents five stent-assigned patients receiving contact aspiration, and its arithmetic reconciles 175 patients undergoing a frontline device procedure in that assigned group.
- Device-row sums were not compared to participant headers: the study permits multiple passes, rescue treatment, and device switching; the eTable is a device-detail table, not a mutually exclusive person-category table.
- Incomplete imaging-subgroup denominators were not treated as missing partitions because the article and supplement identify availability-limited imaging measures, and neither source says that categories exhaust the ITT population.
- This review cannot establish whether the Figure 2 mixed-label convention is a production defect or intentional, and cannot adjudicate protocol-to-publication plan changes without an amendment/SAP provenance record. Native/layout text and rendered pages were legible; no OCR limitation affected matched comparisons.
