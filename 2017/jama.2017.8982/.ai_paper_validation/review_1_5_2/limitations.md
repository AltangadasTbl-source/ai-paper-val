# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- This review checks quantitative reporting consistency in the three supplied PDFs. It is not a raw-data, broad methodology, clinical, misconduct, novelty, or conclusion-validity audit, and it uses no external literature.
- For C001 and C002, the source package does not provide the table-production calculation, weighting, export, or rounding rules. C002 also lacks a definition showing whether narrative `8 to 12 weeks` is intended to denote the tabular `8–11 weeks` bin.
- For C003 and C004, variable-specific nonmissing denominators, missing-observation counts/categories, and the exact complete-case percentage rule are not printed.
- For C005, the operational age filter, the number of infants exactly 60 days old, and whether the eTable and eFigure were generated from identical filtered records are unavailable.
- Exact statistical reconstruction is limited because raw logistic odds ratios, standard errors/test statistics, covariance, working correlation, variance estimator, degrees of freedom, individual-test sidedness, the exact odds-ratio-to-adjusted-risk-difference conversion, and a common confidence-interval/P-value inversion rule are not supplied.
- The protocol specifies planned Bonferroni control while the reported analyses label Hochberg-adjusted P values; no amendment or final decision-rule history is supplied. Planned and final methods were therefore not treated as identical-result comparators.
- The post hoc race/ethnicity narrative has no supplied stratum effect estimates, tests, P values, variances, or multiplicity rule, so its statistical-significance wording cannot be mechanically reconstructed.
- Aggregate documents cannot establish whether any candidate changed the paper's conclusions or entered a systematic review, meta-analysis, guideline, or other evidence product. Potential downstream effects are therefore stated only as conditional data-extraction risks.
