# Cross-Source Consistency Review

## Scope, sources, and matching rule

This lane reviewed the complete fresh relationship inventories in `extraction/main_quantitative_evidence.md` (DOC-001, N001-N049 and S001-S026) and `extraction/support_quantitative_evidence.md` (DOC-002 and DOC-003).  Direct-source checks used only the fresh layout/native text and rendered result pages listed in `evidence_asset_inventory.md`.  No legacy audit derivative or external source was used.

Occurrences were compared only when population, analysis set, treatment contrast, endpoint definition, time horizon, measure, scale, model/event definition, reference direction, and displayed precision matched.  A prospective protocol quantity was treated as a planned definition or assumption unless the report explicitly represented it as an observed result.

## Qualifying candidate proposal

### Proposal 1 — conflicting Cox hazard ratio for spontaneous-delivery analysis

- **Primary category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Exact locations:** [DOC-001 p. 5, Primary Outcome narrative](jama_saccone_2017_oi_170144.pdf#page=5) states, for the analysis to 34 weeks, “hazard ratio, **0.36**; 95% CI, **0.54-0.87**” for spontaneous preterm birth. [DOC-001 p. 6, Figure 2 panel B](jama_saccone_2017_oi_170144.pdf#page=6), labelled “Spontaneous delivery only,” prints “Hazard ratio, **0.68**; 95% CI, **0.54-0.87**.” The protocol’s matched survival definition is [DOC-002 p. 14](joi170144supp1_prod.pdf#page=14): gestational-age time scale, spontaneous delivery event, elective deliveries censored, hazard ratios estimated.
- **Identity match:** Both DOC-001 occurrences describe the spontaneous-delivery survival analysis for the randomized groups and share the exact displayed 95% CI endpoints (0.54-0.87). Figure 2A (all delivery types; HR 0.70 [0.55-0.88]) was excluded because it is a different event definition.
- **Printed-value rule:** For one identified Cox hazard-ratio result, the narrative and its cited spontaneous-delivery figure panel should print the same HR at the stated two-decimal precision. Instead, 0.36 != 0.68. Further, the narrative pair is internally incompatible in ordering because 0.36 lies below its printed CI lower bound 0.54, whereas 0.68 lies within the shared CI.
- **Supported alternatives:** The narrative HR may be a transcription error; the Figure 2B HR may be erroneous; or the locations may unexpectedly refer to differently specified models despite the same outcome label and identical CI. The supplied package does not identify which is authoritative.
- **Human verification question:** In the analysis output for the spontaneous-delivery Cox model through 34 weeks, what are the exact HR and 95% CI, and should the p. 5 narrative read 0.68, 0.36 with different CI endpoints, or another value?
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.

## Explicit non-candidates and limitations

- The protocol’s adjusted logistic OR plan versus the report’s unadjusted RR is a disclosed analysis change in DOC-001, and OR versus RR are different measures. It is retained as a matched-plan distinction, not proposed as a numeric contradiction.
- Planned sample-size assumptions, feasibility counts, and administrative dates in DOC-002 are not observed trial results.
- SPTB and overall PTB (spontaneous plus indicated) were not compared as identical endpoints. Nor were Figure 2 all-delivery and spontaneous-delivery panels.
- eTable 1 organism rows were not added because overlap is not defined. eTable 2 component outcomes were not required to sum to the composite because a participant can have more than one component.
- No `P = 0`, `p = 0.000`, or equivalent display-zero issue was proposed; none was used as a contradiction.
- This lane checks matched-source consistency and labels. It does not replace the dedicated arithmetic, statistical, evidence-recheck, or final quality-audit lanes.
