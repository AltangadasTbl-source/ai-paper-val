# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- The supplied package does not include authoritative Cox coefficient, standard-error, or model-output records needed to determine which spontaneous-delivery hazard ratio in C001 is intended.
- The supplied article and supplement do not state whether percentage-point estimates were generated from exact fractions, internal unrounded risks, or subtraction of displayed rounded percentages. This prevents final resolution of C002-C005, C007, and C008 within the package.
- Bootstrap resamples, complete risk-difference CI construction outputs, and authoritative contrast-direction records are not supplied for C006, C009, and C010. The printed non-containment can be checked, but replacement intervals cannot be inferred.
- Exact sample-size simulation code and allocation assumptions, mean-outcome variance inputs, and Wald-model covariance/standard-error inputs are absent. Those relationships were checked only to the level supported by supplied definitions.
- eTable 1 microbial categories are not declared mutually exclusive and therefore were not summed against the positive-swab total.
- The main article notes a correction to cervical-length category definitions but the supplied package does not reproduce prior wording; no correction-specific mismatch was inferred.
- Dense table and figure geometry required fresh layout text and rendered PNG confirmation. Native text was usable on every page, so OCR was not required.
