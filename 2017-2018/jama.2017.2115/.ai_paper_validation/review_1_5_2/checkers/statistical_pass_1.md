### SCAND-001 — death-difference confidence interval appears incompatible with the printed flow counts

- **Relationships / exact evidence:** S007; DOC-001#page=4 says 7 treatment and 9 placebo deaths and reports death difference `.002` (95% CI, `-.006 to .037`). The direct PDF Figure 1/narrative identifies denominators 1156 and 1147 randomized participants.
- **Observation:** The point difference is compatible with counts: 9/1147 - 7/1156 = .001791, displayed as .002 (placebo minus treatment).
- **Reproducible diagnostic calculation (not a reported analysis):** the ordinary unpooled-binomial Wald SE is sqrt[(7/1156)(1-7/1156)/1156 + (9/1147)(1-9/1147)/1147] = .003463. Its nominal 95% interval is approximately -.0050 to .0086. The printed upper endpoint .037 is far beyond this diagnostic count-scale interval, whereas -.006 is near the diagnostic lower endpoint.
- **Why proposed:** The stated flow counts and stated CI appear quantitatively discordant on the same death-proportion contrast. DOC-001 p.3 names group-comparison tests but does not identify a CI method for this flow value; consequently the calculation is diagnostic and the proposal is not a final correction.
- **Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** `.037` may be a transcription/typesetting endpoint issue, or a nonstandard CI method may have been used; the supplied source does not resolve this. No unreported CI procedure is inferred.
- **Missing definition:** exact death-difference CI construction and contrast orientation.
- **Human question:** Recompute the death-proportion CI from the analysis dataset using the reported method, then verify the printed upper endpoint and contrast label.

### SCAND-002 — outside-study vitamin-D difference CI spans zero while its P value is .002

- **Relationships / exact evidence:** S012; DOC-001#page=5 Table 2 reports outside-study vitamin D3 intake (visits 2--9): 740 (691--789) IU/d, N=1099, versus 869 (803--934) IU/d, N=1094; between-group mean difference `-128.1` (95% CI, `-209.5 to 46.6`), P=`.002`.
- **Observation:** The reported means support a negative treatment-minus-placebo difference (740-869=-129), close to -128.1. The printed interval is ordered and contains -128.1, but includes the null zero; the displayed two-sided P=.002 is far below .05.
- **Reproducible rule/calculation:** For the same two-sided 95% contrast interval and null hypothesis, an interval that includes zero is incompatible with a corresponding two-sided P=.002. **Diagnostic only:** changing only the printed `46.6` endpoint to `-46.6` yields -128.1 +/- 81.5, an approximately symmetric 95% interval; the implied SE 81.5/1.96=41.6 gives |z| approximately 3.08 and a two-sided normal-tail P approximately .002. This does not prescribe a correction.
- **Why proposed:** The CI and P are printed in the same Table 2 contrast row. DOC-001 p.3 supplies two-sided testing but does not name Table 2's CI/test construction, so the source should be rechecked rather than treating the diagnostic as a replacement analysis.
- **Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** A missing minus sign before `46.6` would reconcile the displayed mean, CI, direction, and P. A different unreported CI/test pairing is also possible but not defined in supplied evidence.
- **Missing definition:** Table 2 variance/CI method and the exact test producing each P value.
- **Human question:** Verify the upper CI endpoint against the analysis output/source file and confirm whether it was `-46.6` or `46.6`.

### SCAND-003 — Table 2 placebo calcium sample size exceeds the reported randomized placebo cohort

- **Relationships / exact evidence:** S012; DOC-001#page=5 Table 2 lists outside-study calcium intake (visits 2--9) as treatment N=1099 and placebo N=`1994`. DOC-001#page=1 and #page=4 both state placebo randomization N=1147; the same Table 2 adjacent outside-study vitamin-D row lists placebo N=1094.
- **Observation / reproducible rule:** A row headed “No. of Participants” for a placebo-group participant-level mean cannot be 1994 when the supplied direct article reports only 1147 placebo participants randomized and no source states repeated measurements are counted as distinct participants. Thus 1994 exceeds the maximum eligible placebo-participant total by 847.
- **Why proposed:** This is a direct denominator/label contradiction within DOC-001. It is distinct from SCAND-002 because it concerns the calcium-row N, not the vitamin-D CI/P pair.
- **Alternative source-grounded interpretation:** The value may be a transposition or typographical error, plausibly `1094` given the neighboring placebo N, but the supplied source does not establish a correction.
- **Missing definition:** Whether this cell intentionally counts something other than unique participants; its column header says participants, and no such alternative definition is supplied.
- **Human question:** Check the Table 2 source data/production proof to establish the intended placebo calcium N and whether it was participant- or observation-level.
