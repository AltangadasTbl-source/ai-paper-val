# Cross-source and repeated-location consistency check

## Scope and method

This lane checked the complete canonical numeric inventory `N001`–`N069` and
statistical inventory `S001`–`S038` against the supplied direct PDFs only:
`jama_lappe_2017_oi_170019.pdf` (DOC-001), `joi170019supp1_prod.pdf`
(DOC-002), and `joi170019supp2_prod.pdf` (DOC-003). Fresh PDF text and
layout assets were used to locate evidence; each comparison below was checked
against the direct PDF page cited. No previous audit derivative was used.

Before comparing printed values, I matched population, time window, contrast,
analysis set, event definition, model, unit/scale, purpose (planned/pilot versus
actual), and displayed precision. A value from a figure curve was not treated as
an exact printed endpoint unless the caption or associated text supplied it.

## Qualifying proposal

### XCAND-001 — Figure 1 discontinuation counts conflict with later vitamin-D/placebo discontinuation total and percentages

- **Category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Status:** Pending Human Adjudication.
- **Exact evidence:** DOC-001 Figure 1 on [p.4](../../../jama_lappe_2017_oi_170019.pdf#page=4) prints “238 Discontinued intervention” in the vitamin-D-plus-calcium arm and “246 Discontinued intervention” in the placebo arm. The accompanying component counts on the same figure sum to those values (11+93+134=238 and 16+76+154=246). DOC-001 narrative on [p.7](../../../jama_lappe_2017_oi_170019.pdf#page=7) prints: “During follow-up, 304 participants (13.2%; 12.4% of the vitamin D3 + calcium group and 14.0% of the placebo group) stopped taking the vitamin D or placebo supplement.”
- **Matched comparison rule:** Both locations describe the completed randomized trial, follow-up discontinuation, and the assigned vitamin-D-containing study supplement/placebo contrast. The Figure 1 arm counts sum to 484. The narrative total is 304; its group percentages imply approximately 143/1156 and 161/1147 participants, summing to 304 after displayed-percent rounding. The difference is therefore not explained by rounding.
- **Direct observation:** The source prints 238/246 in the flow figure and separately prints 304 total with 12.4%/14.0% group percentages in the narrative.
- **Inference requiring human adjudication:** Under the ordinary reading that Figure 1 “Discontinued intervention” denotes stopping the vitamin-D-containing assigned intervention, these are conflicting counts for the same follow-up construct. The package does not define on the figure whether “discontinued intervention” instead means a broader composite (for example, stopping either component or another protocol-status category), nor does it link the 304 narrative total to Figure 1. That unresolved definition could explain the numerical difference; it must be confirmed from the study records or production source before any correction is chosen.
- **Source-grounded alternatives:** (1) Figure 1 correctly reports a broader discontinuation construct than the narrative’s vitamin-D/placebo stopping endpoint; (2) the narrative total/percentages are correct but Figure 1 has a label or count issue; (3) both are valid but use an unstated, materially different time window or event rule. The supplied PDFs do not distinguish these alternatives.
- **Human verification question:** What exact event definition, component(s), time window, and participant-level rule produced the Figure 1 238/246 “Discontinued intervention” counts, and does it differ from the p.7 304 participants stopping vitamin D or placebo? If it does not differ, which printed counts and percentages should be corrected?

## Lane conclusion and limitations

The repeated primary, safety, serum, and post-hoc result displays that could be identity-matched passed after precision and analysis-set matching. The one proposal above is a direct, reproducible mismatch but remains conditional on an unresolved Figure 1 definition. Protocol projections, pilot analyses, and external-study values were explicitly retained as non-comparable rather than converted into candidates. DOC-003 graphical curve endpoints were not used as exact numeric evidence; only its printed captions/statements and the direct PDF pages were compared.
