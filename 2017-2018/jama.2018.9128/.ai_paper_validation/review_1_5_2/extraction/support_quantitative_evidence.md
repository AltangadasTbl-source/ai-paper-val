## Direct observations requiring downstream comparison, not candidate registration

1. DOC-002 p. 110 calls the final schedule six points but lists baseline, 3, 9, 12, and 36 months—five enumerated occasions—while DOC-002 pp. 14-15, 16, and DOC-003 eTable 1 explicitly include 24 months. This map flags the exact prose omission for cross-source checking; it does not assign a candidate ID.
2. DOC-002 p. 64 independently calls the schedule T1-T6/six points while listing seven occasions through 48 months, and calls its school-readiness program a 7-data-collection-point program. This exact revised-protocol observation is PN020 and a match key for C010/C012, not a candidate assignment by this mapper.
3. The original protocol uses “BMI Percentile”/“BMI%” as the primary outcome label (DOC-002 p. 16), whereas the final SAP and DOC-003 eTable 1 report/model BMI in kg/m². These may reflect a protocol evolution or distinct outcome descriptions; compare the final analysis and main-paper wording before any conclusion.
4. Original and final sample-size tables have identical numerical cells but change the table number (9 to 5). Original and final SAPs deliberately change the primary test/model details as documented on p. 113; this is not itself a reporting inconsistency.

## Completeness and limitations

All 121 assigned PDF pages were directly covered. DOC-002 is a protocol/SAP packet and therefore supplies planned definitions, model specifications, schedules, and inputs rather than a complete observed-result dataset. DOC-003 pp. 5-8 were visually inspected through supplied rendered PNGs; their plots do not print enough numeric coordinates for additional numerical mapping. OCR on DOC-002 equation pages was used only as navigation; rendered images remain authoritative for glyph identity.
