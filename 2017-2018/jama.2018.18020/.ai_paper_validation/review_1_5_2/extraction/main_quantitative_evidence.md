## Completeness notes and limitations

- All physical pp.1-10 were inspected. Result-relevant extraction includes abstract, Key Points, methods definitions that determine numeric interpretation, narrative results, Figure 1-3/captions, Tables 1-2/footnotes, follow-up/adverse-event/adherence text, discussion/conclusion repetitions.
- Dense Figure 2 contains plotted individual patient points and 95% CrIs but does not print a numerical value table for every point. Its exact printed group n, threshold, patient identifiers, dropout identifiers, and caption definitions are captured in N001/N007/N009/S003. Individual point estimates are visually encoded rather than supplied as exact text and are therefore not transcribed as invented precision.
- Table 2 changes and effects are retained as reported values; the map does not substitute arithmetic recomputation for the stated mixed-model or dependent-t-test estimates.
