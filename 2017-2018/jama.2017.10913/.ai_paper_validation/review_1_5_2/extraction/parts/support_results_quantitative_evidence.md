## eMethods (PDF pp. 2-5)

### Quantitative setting and intervention definitions (p. 2)

* UTH is described as a 1,500-bed referral hospital. Its emergency department has a 4-bed intake room, a 4-bed Acute Bay, and 35-40 additional beds; internal medicine has over 400 beds, average occupancy above 80%, and average HIV prevalence 50%. The ICU has 10 beds and 8-10 mechanical ventilators, occupancy above 95%; the country population is stated as 13 million.
* Overall emergency-department stay ranges from 6 to 48 hours. Emergency-department rounds occur twice daily and inpatient-ward rounds once daily.
* For a usual-care fluid bolus, the written order is 1-4 L "fast," infused over 30-60 minutes. Usual-care fluid dosing is absolute volume rather than weight based. These quantities define the fluid-order table on p. 8; they are not outcome estimates.

### Patient-safety thresholds and time windows (p. 3)

* SSSP-2 was designed in 2012. The cited external context mentions 48-hour mortality, but it is not an SSSP-2 result and is not mapped as a study result.
* Respiratory rate and oxygen saturation were measured hourly during the six hours after enrollment and at completion of each litre of fluid. Fluid was to be discontinued for respiratory rate increase >5 breaths/min, oxygen-saturation decrease by 3%, or JVP >=3 cm.
* Sepsis-protocol fluid volume was limited to 4 L in the first 6 hours after emergency-department presentation.

### Measurement and operational definitions (p. 4)

* Baseline data were entered within 24 hours of enrolment. Vital signs were collected at baseline, 2 hours, and 6 hours. JVP was measured in cm above the sternal angle; 3 cm (approximately 8 cm above the right atrium) approximated central venous pressure 10 mm Hg.
* Additional blood volumes: serum bicarbonate 4 mL; one 4-mL tube at baseline and an additional 4-mL tube 48 hours later; mycobacterial culture 5 mL for HIV-positive patients.
* Whole-blood lactate was measured at enrollment and 6 hours after enrollment. Admission diagnoses were based on history/examination before laboratory or imaging results. 0.9% saline and lactated Ringer's were classified as isotonic crystalloids.

### Model-development population and definitions (p. 5)

* Enrolled population: 209. Primary outcome: in-hospital mortality, 85/209. Secondary outcome: 28-day all-cause mortality, 109/194 with 28-day follow-up. The document states 6-8 degrees of freedom could be included in each multivariable logistic model.
* Prespecified covariates: study-group assignment and SAPS-3, no interaction term; no data reduction. The primary logistic model uses group as categorical (sepsis protocol compared with usual care) and SAPS-3 continuous. A sensitivity analysis dichotomizes SAPS-3 into quartiles. The same covariates are used for the 28-day mortality model. No independent validation was performed.
