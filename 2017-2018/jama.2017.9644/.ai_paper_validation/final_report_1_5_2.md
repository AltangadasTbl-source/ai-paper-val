## C001 — Figure 2 combines a percentage axis with count-like embedded labels without stating the embedded unit

**Candidate statement:** Figure 2A-B uses an axis labelled `Patients, %`, while in-bar integers sum to group sample sizes and reproduce count numerators.

**Category:** Rate-versus-count inconsistency

**Exact source locations:** [Main article — PDF p. 7](<../jama_lapergue_2017_oi_170084.pdf#page=7>), Figure 2A-B; [Main article — PDF p. 6](<../jama_lapergue_2017_oi_170084.pdf#page=6>), Table 2.

**Source evidence:** Figure 2A contact values `8, 2, 18, 92, 72` sum to 192 and stent values `5, 5, 22, 84, 73` sum to 189. Figure 2B likewise sums to 192 and 189; both axes state `Patients, %`.

**Reported-versus-comparator:** Percentage-axis wording versus count-like embedded values and printed group `n` labels.

**Reasoning procedure:** Compare the printed axis, segment labels, and group totals; then match the relevant segment sums to Table 2.

**Calculation:** `8+2+18+92+72=192`; `5+5+22+84+73=189`; Figure 2A `92+72=164` and `84+73=157`, matching Table 2.

**Alternative source-grounded interpretations:** This may be an intentional 100%-stacked chart with count annotations; its numeric content reconciles.

**Mechanical evidence recheck:** Cited content and Table 2 comparator found and reproduced; the missing item is an explicit in-bar unit definition.

**Quality-control relevance:** A mixed scale and annotation convention should distinguish the embedded unit.

**Potential downstream evidence impact:** If confirmed, an extractor could misclassify an internal integer as a percentage or a count; no propagation is asserted.

**Human verification steps:** Review the production figure specification and confirm whether in-bar labels were intentionally unlabelled counts.
