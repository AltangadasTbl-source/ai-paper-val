## DOC-002: protocol, analysis definitions, amendments, and DSMC material

### Protocol outcome and treatment definitions

- **SN001 — DOC-002#page=14-15 (Version 1 synopsis):** planned multicentre randomized trial, `n=512`; severe TBI; intervention starts with 2 L 4°C 0.9% saline to 35°C, then 33°C for 72 h; control 36.5-37°C. Primary outcome is favourable GOSE 5-8 at six months.
- **SS001 — DOC-002#page=15:** secondary estimands: proportional-odds probability of equal-or-greater versus lesser GOSE; sliding dichotomy; EQ5D/AQOL8; favourable GOSE 5-8 among survivors; all-cause mortality at ICU/hospital discharge and six months; bleeding and infection incidence.
- **SN002 — DOC-002#page=19:** Version-1 outcome definition repeats primary favourable GOSE 5-8 at six months and the same secondary outcome family. Eligibility: nonpenetrating severe TBI, GCS <=8, age >=15 and <60 y, injury within 2 h of paramedic arrival, intubated.
- **SN003 — DOC-002#page=21-22:** Version-1 protocol defines pads on about 40% body surface; control 36.5-37°C; cooling 33°C for 72 h; bleeding-related withdrawal criteria include chest drain >300 mL on insertion or >100 mL in first hour, and >2 units packed red cells with >2 long-bone lower-limb fractures. Rewarming 0.25°C/h after 72 h if ICP <20 mm Hg; sustained ICP >20 mm Hg for >5 min triggers recooling; maximum hypothermia 7 d.
- **SN004 — DOC-002#page=27-28:** Version-1 primary sample-size relationship: baseline favourable outcome 50%; target 65% (15% ARI, 30% RRI); 364 evaluable (182/arm), 80% two-sided power. Inflation: 5% long-term loss ->384; 12% hypothermia withdrawal plus other allowance ->496; 0.3% one-interim inflation ->498; two run-in participants/site ->512 total. Supporting cited values: meta-analysis RR 1.46 (95% CI 1.12-1.92), P=.006; NNT=7.
- **SS002 — DOC-002#page=28:** Version-1 secondary ordinal design: proportional-odds cumulative logit over eight GOSE categories, reference ln(OR)=0.62 and one-sided linear-rank test; 182/arm stated to have 96% one-sided power.

- **SN005 — DOC-002#page=69:** Version-9 synopsis: patients randomized prehospital or ED; cooling uses up to 2 L 4°C saline to 35°C, then target 33°C for 72 h; control core 37°C ±0.5°C. Primary: proportion GOSE 5-8 at six months. It also describes exploratory secondary interaction of time to 33°C with dichotomized GOSE and cooling effect by age.
- **SN006 — DOC-002#page=73-74:** Version-9 secondary definitions include ordinal GOSE/sliding dichotomy, EQ5D/SF12, favourable GOSE 5-8 among survivors, mortality at six months/hospital discharge, bleeding/infection, and health economics. Inclusion: GCS <9, age >=18 and <60 y. Exclusions include randomization >3 h after injury, persistent systolic BP <90 mm Hg, GCS=3 with unreactive pupils, and specified bleeding risks.
- **SN007 — DOC-002#page=76-77:** Version-9 temperature specifications: no cold fluid if temperature <35°C; control target 36.5-37.5°C; fever >38°C treated with paracetamol/temperature devices, target 37°C ±0.5°C; cooling target 33°C; cooling may be withheld for bleeding and withdrawn if not initiated within 48 h. Rewarming after 72 h, with ICP-triggered recooling and control-pyrexia avoidance for 96 h.
- **SN008 — DOC-002#page=83-86:** Version-9 statistical framework: primary sample calculation assumes 50% to 65% favourable outcome, 364 evaluable, 80% two-sided power, inflating to recruitment target 500. Full ITT excludes only post-randomization consent withdrawals; midpoint dichotomized GOSE modeled as binomial. Secondary analyses include ordinal proportional odds, Kaplan-Meier/log-rank and Cox HRs for time-to-event outcomes, compliant/per-protocol, as-treated, and average causal effect analyses. Interim safety: 125, 250, then 300/350/400/450 randomized; at n=450 assess 28-d mortality; no planned futility stopping.
- **SS003 — DOC-002#page=84-86:** Statistical definitions include two Haybittle-Peto 3-SD interim analyses in the sample-size narrative; final alpha .05 without other multiplicity adjustment because primary outcome is defined. The version-9 protocol explicitly defers detailed SAP to a separately published plan.
- **SN009 — DOC-002#page=108-110:** AKI substudy: primary severity is percent decline in eGFR from baseline to worst eGFR through day 10 or RIFLE Risk/Injury/Failure class; secondary cumulative RIFLE categories, AKI-free days, dialysis and biomarker measures. The displayed design cites 80% power for a continuous difference of 25% of SD and about 6% absolute difference with 250/arm.
- **SN010 — DOC-002#page=112-118:** POLAR-BEAR energy substudy: primary mean energy expenditure by indirect calorimetry over the first 7 d; secondary daily/period-specific values including fever >39°C. Its displayed sample calculation uses observational cohort n=478 (details in page 118).
- **SN011 — DOC-002#page=119-126:** biomarker/TEG substudy defines serial biomarker sampling soon after randomization and at 24, 48, 72 h; TEG metrics include R time, alpha angle, maximum amplitude, and lysis; samples at 48 and 72 h after randomization. TEG source distinguishes measures/units and procedures but gives no trial result table.
- **SN012 — DOC-002#page=128-132:** propofol PK substudy defines hypothermia 34.0-35.0°C and records concentration (mg/L) against time (min); it includes 60-min pre-sampling cumulative dose and temperature profile. The plotted concentration curve is a procedural/illustrative profile, not a trial-result table.
- **SN013 — DOC-002#page=139,174-179:** amendments document changed eligibility/temperature/run-in and analysis plans. Page 139 records exclusion if unable to randomize within 2.5 h, control 36.5-37.5°C, fever >38°C, withdrawal if cooling cannot restart within 48 h, and lead-site run-in n=6. Pages 174-179 record change from ITT to modified ITT, DSMC safety checks at 300/350/400/450, and added CACE for binary and continuous cooling-compliance definitions.
- **SN014 — DOC-002#page=181-187:** the package contains citations/links to published SAP, SAP update, and SAP correction but those linked documents are not embedded: page 181 identifies the published SAP; p182 and p186 carry only URL/title references and pp183-185/187 are blank.
