## Candidate 2

**Uncertain arithmetic/presentation: eTable 5 total percentages appear rounded downward at exact half percentages.**

- Location: PDF p. 17, eTable 5.
- Peripheral edema: 1/80 plus 11/80 = 12/160 = 7.5%, displayed 12 (7%).
- Dizziness: 4/80 plus 24/80 = 28/160 = 17.5%, displayed 28 (17%).
- Ordinary nearest-integer half-up rounding would give 8% and 18%, but the supplement states no rounding rule.
- Bounded impact: two total-column percentages; counts are unchanged.
- Status: Uncertain pending verification of the denominator and publication rounding convention.
