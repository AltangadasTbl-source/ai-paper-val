## Study population, design, analysis definitions, and flow

- PDF p. 1, abstract: randomized open-label, blinded end-point trial; 160 permanent-AF patients aged at least 60 years with NYHA class II or higher dyspnea; England recruitment 2016-2018; last follow-up October 2019. Digoxin `n=80`, 62.5-250 micrograms/day, mean 161 micrograms/day; bisoprolol `n=80`, 1.25-15 mg/day, mean 3.2 mg/day. Primary: SF-36 PCS at 6 months, 0-100 higher-is-better; MCID 0.5 SD. Seventeen secondary end points at 6 months and 20 at 12 months.
- PDF p. 2: randomization was 1:1, balanced by baseline modified EHRA class and sex; enrolled patients were aged 60 years or older with permanent AF and NYHA class II or greater symptoms.
- PDF p. 3, Figure 1: 551 assessed; 390 excluded (161 unwilling/unable, 100 lacking symptoms or NYHA below class II, 50 not permanent AF, 38 limited life expectancy, 22 bradycardia/heart pauses, 12 prior study-drug intolerance, 7 other); 161 randomized. Eighty assigned and received at least one dose in each group. One participant withdrew after randomization before therapy (footnote). At 6 months: digoxin 4 deaths/0 alive withdrawals, bisoprolol 5 deaths/1 alive withdrawal; primary analysis 76 vs 74. At 12 months after 6-month status: digoxin 0 deaths/2 alive withdrawals/1 did not attend, bisoprolol 2 deaths/0 withdrawals/0 did not attend; primary analysis 73 vs 72. One participant completed 35 of 36 SF-36 items at 12 months.
- PDF p. 3: SF-36 scale 0-100, higher is better; MCID 4.1-9.2; EQ-5D summary index 0 (death) to 1 (complete health), MCID 0.18; AFEQT 0-100, higher is better, MCID 5 points.
- PDF p. 4: planned 144 randomized patients for 85% power at two-sided alpha .05 to detect an SF-36 PCS SD effect size 0.5; planned 160 allowing 10% death/loss at 6 months. Full analysis set: randomized and received at least one dose; analysed by randomized therapy regardless of withdrawal/crossover. Bisoprolol/alternative beta-blocker was reference. Models adjusted for baseline score when applicable, sex, baseline modified EHRA, age, and baseline LVEF; continuous outcomes used adjusted mean difference, NT-proBNP and 6-minute walk used log-transformed ratio of geometric means, binary/categorical outcomes logistic/ordinal logistic regression, event counts chi-square. Complete-case analysis/no imputation; secondary analyses exploratory, two-tailed .05 significance threshold.
