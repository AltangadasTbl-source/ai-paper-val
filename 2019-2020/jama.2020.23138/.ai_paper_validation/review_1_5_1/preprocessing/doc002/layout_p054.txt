        11.7 Archiving

        Archiving will be authorised by the BCTU on behalf of the Sponsor following submission of the end
        of trial report.


        Principal Investigators are responsible for the secure archiving of essential trial documents (for
        their site) as per their NHS Trust policy. All essential documents will be archived for a minimum of
        25 years after completion of trial.
        Destruction of essential documents will require authorisation from the BCTU on behalf of the
        Sponsor.



        12 Statistical Considerations

        12.1 Outcome measures

        12.1.1      Primary Outcome
        Patient-reported quality of life (QoL) - SF-36 physical component summary score at six months



        12.1.2      Secondary Outcomes
        Patient-reported QoL:
            x    SF-36 global and domain-specific scores at 6 and 12 months

            x    EQ-5D-5L summary index and visual analogue scale at six and twelve months
            x    AFEQT overall score at six and twelve months
        Cardiac function:

            x    Echocardiographic LVEF at 12 months
            x    Diastolic function (E/e’ and composite of diastolic indices) at 12 months
            x    Functional assessment
            x    Six-minute walking distance at 6 and 12 months
            x    Change in European Heart Rhythm Association (EHRA) class at 6 and 12 months
        Biomarkers:
            x    Change in B-type natriuretic peptide (BNP) levels at 6 months
            x    Change in heart rate using 24-hour ambulatory ECG



        12.1.3      Feasibility Outcomes
            x    Recruitment target of 3 patients per week across all participating centres.
            x    Compliance and reasons for non-compliance
