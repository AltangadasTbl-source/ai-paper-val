5.5 BASELINE PARTICPANT CHARACTERISTICS

5.5.1 LIST OF BASELINE DATA

Participants will be described with respect to age, gender, mental capacity, ethnicity, height, weight*,
ulna.length, mid upper arm circumference, clinical frailty scale, prescribed antimicrobials in the last
four weeks and use of other medications in last four weeks (proton pump inhibitor, laxative and
vitamin D supplement), both overall and separately for the two trial arms.

*Due to the fluctuating nature of weight in this population, only weight measurements taken up to
three-months prior to randomisation will be reported.

5.5.2 DESCRIPTIVE STATISTICS ‘

Participant flow and recruitment

Summary statistics on patient assessment, eligibility, recruitment, withdrawal and dropout will be
collated for both trial arms and form the CONSORT flow diagram for clinical trial reporting. Specifically,
for each arm, participants randomly assigned, receiving intended intervention, completing the study
protocol, and analysed for the primary outcome. Any protocol deviations will be described with
reasons as well as dates defining the period of recruitment and follow-up.

Baseline data

Appropriate descriptive summaries and graphical illustrations of baseline demographic, clinical and
questionnaire data will be presented (see Appendix Iil for example table), Descriptive summaries for
the minimisation variable, gender, will be produced at site level. Analyses by trial arm will be
performed when all follow-up data has been obtained and cleaned. Baseline data will be used to
describe balance between trial arms and generalizability of the study population. There will be no
formal testing of between-arm differences for any variables at baseline.
