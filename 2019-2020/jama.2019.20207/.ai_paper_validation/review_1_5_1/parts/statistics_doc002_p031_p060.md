## Statistical observations without adjudication

- `P2-O005` (PDF p. 42): The directly rendered page states interim superiority alpha=.0025, then parenthetically says a one-sided P value “smaller than 0.5” and statistic >0 imply experimental superiority; futility is P>=.5 or standardized statistic <0. The .5 statements can describe test direction/sign rather than the alpha-based rejection boundary, but the wording is potentially easy to misread. Preserve this exact relationship for statistical passes; do not infer a contradiction absent reported interim results or a formal monitoring specification.
- `P2-O006` (PDF pp. 40,42): HR 2.118 (20% control vs 10% experimental) and HR=.472 in recalculation are reciprocal under opposite arm orientation. The relation is numerically coherent to reported precision.
- `P2-O007` (PDF pp. 31,40-41): The composite endpoint, age-specific biopsy thresholds, PSA threshold, and PSADT threshold recur consistently. The support source differentiates PSADT's computed continuous timing rule from the binary <3-year progression trigger.
- `P2-O008` (PDF pp. 40-42): Planned superiority testing is one-sided whereas initial primary power and recalculation specify two-sided alpha=.05. This may reflect design versus monitoring conventions; the supplied text gives no analysis result or additional alpha-spending formula that would establish an inconsistency. Retain as a definition-matching issue for later human/statistical review.
