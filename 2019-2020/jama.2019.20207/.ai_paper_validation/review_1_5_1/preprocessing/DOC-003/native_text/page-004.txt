The correlative science objectives pertaining to plasma carotenoid levels will be addressed in a
separate statistical analysis plan and, furthermore, selected statistical analyses will be
performed at the University of California San Diego (UCSD). Specifically, the group at Moores
UCSD Cancer Center will analyze the carotenoid concentrations to assess if diet intervention
changes were achieved within the study and whether any changes differed between the two
study arms; these analyses support protocol correlative study objective 10.1.2.1. The statistical
analyses to support the remaining correlative study objectives will be performed at the Alliance
Statistical and Data Center.

1. Intent-to-Treat
The intent-to-treat population will include all randomized subjects.
2. Modified Intent-to-Treat
The modified intent-to-treat population will include all randomized subjects, however,
subjects who later become ineligible by centralized pathology review of their baseline
tissue specimens will be excluded; we anticipate that no more than 10% of subjects will
become ineligible for the study following central pathology review [4].
