520   3.9.6 Pharmacogenetics: Processing of DNA: Whole blood on all consenting participants will be sent directly
521   to the NIMH Center for Collaborative Genetic Studies (‘the Center’) for the generation of cell lines and
522   extraction of DNA. Blood samples will be sent as they are collected and shipped at room temperature using the
523   Center’s established protocols that are designed to minimize trauma. In the rare event that a sample is lost in
524   transit, or if the sample is unsuitable for DNA extraction, or if a cell line is not successfully made, we will obtain
525   a repeat blood sample to send to the Center. DNA will be shipped from the Center to Dr. James Kennedy’s
526   genetics laboratory at CAMH in Toronto within 4 months of the blood sample being received by the Center.
527   With the exception of data entry personnel at Cornell, the study’s research staff will not be privy to the genetic
528   data until after the study has finished, so as to maintain the blind.
529   Genotyping: We will use standard TaqMan methods on our ABI-7500 for SNP markers and ABI-3100 for
530   repeat polymorphisms. To ensure a clear result, any ambiguous genotypes will be discarded and the
531   participant’s DNA will be re-genotyped until the results are unambiguous. 10% of the participants will be re-
532   genotyped to check for error rates (the test error rate in Dr. Kennedy’s lab is 0.5%). We will type selected
533   SNPs (functional and certain tagged SNPs) across the candidate genes described in Section 3.6, and also
534   explore any highly promising genes emerging from the literature. We will test both single markers and
535   haplotypes.
