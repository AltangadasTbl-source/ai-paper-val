357   phase of the proposed study will use open-label combination treatment, we anticipate that more patients will
358   agree to participate in the proposed study compared with STOP-PD where participants had a 50:50 chance of
359   being randomized to olanzapine plus placebo. Based on 38 months of recruitment, the recruitment goal
360   equates to an average 2.6 participants enrolled per site per month.
361   Enrollment in the Stabilization and RCT Phases: In the double-blind combination treatment arm of STOP-
362   PD, 63/129 (48.8%) participants who were randomized to combination treatment met remission criteria at
363   some point during treatment, based on the criterion of a Schedule for Affective Disorders and Schizophrenia
364   (SADS)101 delusion item score of 1 (‘definitely not delusional’) and a minimum of 2 consecutive assessments of
365   a 17-item HAM-D score ≤10. In addition, 10/129 (7.8%) combination treatment participants had the absence of
366   delusions, a CGI improvement score of ‘very much’ or ‘much’ improved, and a 17-item HAM-D score of 11-15.
367   Thus, 56.6% of participants who were randomized to combination treatment experienced remission of
368   delusions and remission or substantial improvement of depressive symptoms. Given that treatment in the
369   acute phase of the proposed study will be open-label, we anticipate that remission/near-remission will be
370   higher and drop-out due to withdrawal of consent will be lower than under the double-blind, placebo-controlled
371   conditions of STOP-PD.11 We therefore predict that 60% of participants in the proposed study will achieve
372   remission of psychosis and either remission or substantial improvement in depressive symptoms, allowing
373   them to proceed to the stabilization phase. Nevertheless, we plan to recruit 392 patients to the acute phase of
374   the study, which will facilitate reaching our target recruitment to the RCT should remission plus near-remission
375   in the acute phase be as low as 50%.
376           In the STOP-PD stabilization phase, 10% of participants who experienced remission with combination
377   treatment had a relapse (Section 3.8). In contrast, Rothschild et al.41 found that none of the patients with PD
378   who responded to combined fluoxetine and perphenazine had a relapse or dropped out during 12 weeks of
379   open-label continuation treatment. Based on these data, we estimate that approximately 10% of acute phase
380   full/near-remitters will not complete the 8-week open-label stabilization phase of the proposed study, either
381   because of relapse or discontinuation. Thus, we predict that 176 participants will be available for the RCT.
