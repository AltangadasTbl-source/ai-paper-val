448   3.9.5 Assessments and Measures:
449   Primary Clinical Measures pertain to the study’s aims and hypotheses and/or eligibility criteria. They are:
450   Structured Clinical Interview for DSM-IV-TR (SCID-IV),106 the GRID version of the 17-item HAM-D,98,107
451   Delusion Assessment Scale (DAS),96 delusion and hallucination items of the SADS,101 Scale for Suicidal
452   Ideation (SSI)108, CGI,99 and MMSE.100 The 26-item Informant Questionnaire for Cognitive Decline
453   (IQCODE)109,110 will be used to screen out clinically significant cognitive decline that began prior to the index
454   episode of PD, to help avoid the inclusion of persons in the early stage of dementia.
455   Secondary Clinical Measures: Anxiety symptoms, medical burden, executive dysfunction, psychomotor
456   change, and ‘treatment resistance’ during the index episode of depression have each been associated with
457   increased risk of relapse/recurrence of major depression.111-115 These variables will therefore be included as
458   covariates in the Cox model examining risk of relapse (Section 3.10.3). The pertinent measures are: anxiety
459   subscale of the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale,116,117 Cumulative Illness Rating Scale,118 DKEFS
460   Stroop Conditions 1-3,119 the CORE97, and the Antidepressant Treatment History Form.120
461   • Given the age range of study participants, there will be 3 additional cognitive tests to help characterize the
462   sample at entry in to the RCT; these tests, which target previously described impairments in PD121 and late-life
463   depression,121,123 will be: i) a test of delayed recall (RBANS Immediate and Delayed Recall,124 ii) a test of
464   psychomotor speed (RBANS coding task124), and iii) a test of cognitive flexibility (DKEFS Trail Making Test
465   Conditions 4 & 5.119 The DKEFS and RBANS tests were selected as they have norms available for the entire
466   age range of our proposed sample and the two DKEFS tasks and the two RBANS tasks were co-normed.
467   • Measures of non-metabolic safety will be: UKU Side Effects Scale;125 Simpson Angus Scale;126 Barnes
468   Akathisia Scale;127 Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale;128 Orthostatic hypotension assessed after standing
469   for 1 minute, following 5 minutes in a supine position; and Falls Log (incidence and number of falls).
470   Anthropometric Measures : The selection of anthropometric measures and metabolic parameters is based on
471   Consensus Recommendations regarding the clinical monitoring of patients treated with atypical
472   antipsychotics.129 Subjects will be weighed, and their height measured, in light clothing without shoes. In
473   addition, pre-morbid weight (average weight in the year prior to the onset of the index episode of depression)
474   will be obtained from the patient’s primary care physician’s records. When pre-morbid weight is not available
475   from primary care physicians, the patient will be asked for this information. Pre-morbid weight will be used to
476   calculate weight loss during the index episode of depression; depression-related weight loss will be taken in to
477   account in a secondary statistical analysis of weight change during the proposed study. In addition to weight,
478   body mass index (BMI) and waist circumference are key clinical measures of body fat.129 BMI, a measure of
479   weight relative to height, is an index of total body fat. Waist circumference, measured at the umbilicus, is a
480   measure of abdominal obesity, which is more specifically associated with cardiovascular risk and metabolic
481   syndrome than BMI.130 BMI and waist circumference will be assessed at each study visit. Separate secondary
482   statistical analyses pertaining to Aims 2-5 will be conducted, using each of these measures as an alternative to
483   weight.
484   Metabolic Measures: Homeostatic model assessment (HOMA), a measure of insulin resistance131,132 and the
485   primary measure of glycemic function in this study, will be calculated based on an established formula,132 each
486   time fasting glucose and insulin are measured. HbA1c will be a secondary measure of glycemic function, to
487   assess the longitudinal stability of serum glucose levels during the course of the study. Triglycerides and
488   cholesterol (total, LDL, HDL) will also be measured.
