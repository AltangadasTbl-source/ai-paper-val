# Figure and participant-flow audit — DOC-001 and DOC-004

## Scope

- **DOC-001:** `jama_combes_2025_oi_250087_1766516490.94011.pdf`, PDF pp. 1–10 (main article).
- **DOC-004:** `joi250087supp3_prod_1766516490.97011.pdf`, PDF pp. 1–18 (result-relevant supplement).
- Inspected participant-flow and result-relevant figure page images, including captions, legends, axes, labels, visible annotations, and counts, and compared them with nearby text and tables.
- Protocol, SAP, and administrative documents were not inspected.

## Candidate issues

**No document-verifiable candidate figure-flow issues identified.**

## Reconciliation evidence supporting the no-candidate result

1. **Eligibility and randomization flow reconciles.** DOC-001, PDF p. 4, Figure 1: 209 assessed − 4 excluded = 205 randomized; 101 assigned levosimendan + 104 assigned placebo = 205. These totals agree with DOC-001, PDF p. 1, Abstract—Design/Interventions, and PDF p. 4, Results—Study Sites and Patients.

2. **Treatment receipt and analysis labels agree with the intention-to-treat population.** DOC-001, PDF p. 4, Figure 1 shows 101/101 levosimendan participants received assigned treatment, while 102/104 placebo participants received assigned treatment and 2 did not; nevertheless, 101 and 104 are included in primary analysis. The adjacent Results text explicitly reports the same 2 placebo nonrecipients, and DOC-001, PDF p. 6, Table 2 uses arm denominators 101 and 104. The 2 levosimendan participants lost to follow-up are not depicted as removed from the intention-to-treat analysis; Table 2 footnote d identifies the 2 censored primary-outcome observations as 1 patient still receiving ECMO at day 30 and 1 patient lost before a competing event.

3. **Primary-outcome categories reconcile to arm totals and censoring.** DOC-001, PDF p. 6, Table 2: levosimendan 69 successful + 15 weaning failures + 15 deaths before weaning = 99, with 2 censored per footnote d, giving 101; placebo 71 + 21 + 12 = 104. DOC-004, PDF p. 10, eFigure 1 provides the same decomposition in more detail: levosimendan 69 successful + 11 postweaning deaths + 4 LVAD + 15 deaths before weaning + 2 censored = 101; placebo 71 successful + 12 postweaning deaths + 5 transplants + 4 LVAD + 12 deaths before weaning = 104. Those detail counts agree with DOC-001, PDF p. 4, Results—Primary Outcome.

4. **Mortality-curve endpoint counts agree with Table 2.** DOC-004, PDF p. 11, eFigure 2 displays cumulative events of 23 placebo and 26 levosimendan at day 30 and 26 placebo and 28 levosimendan at day 60. These match DOC-001, PDF p. 6, Table 2 (30-day mortality 26/101 vs 23/104; 60-day mortality 28/101 vs 26/104) and DOC-001, PDF p. 4, Results—Secondary Outcomes.

5. **MACE-curve endpoint counts agree with eTable 3.** DOC-004, PDF p. 13, eFigure 4 displays 36 placebo and 35 levosimendan events at day 30, and 39 placebo and 36 levosimendan events at day 60. These equal DOC-004, PDF pp. 5–6, eTable 3 values of 35/101 vs 36/104 at day 30 and 36/101 vs 39/104 at day 60.

6. **Serious-adverse-event distribution reconciles to Table 3.** DOC-004, PDF p. 17, eFigure 8: levosimendan patients with zero SAEs = 42, so 101 − 42 = 59 with at least 1 SAE; placebo patients with zero SAEs = 43, so 104 − 43 = 61 with at least 1 SAE. These match DOC-001, PDF p. 7, Table 3, “Any serious adverse event”: 59/101 and 61/104. The displayed per-count bars also sum to each arm total (101 and 104).

7. **Subgroup figure values and directions agree.** DOC-001, PDF p. 7, Figure 3 and DOC-004, PDF p. 18, eFigure 9 report the same subdistribution hazard ratios and confidence intervals for acute myocardial infarction (0.73 [0.37–1.40]), myocarditis (2.16 [0.97–4.80]), postcardiotomy/post-heart surgery (0.73 [0.46–1.15]), and other causes (1.61 [0.78–3.33]). Figure 3’s “favors placebo” for ratios below 1 and “favors levosimendan” above 1 is consistent with successful weaning being the favorable event.

8. **Other graphical cross-checks produced no unambiguous discrepancy.** DOC-004 eFigure 6 (PDF p. 15) visually represents the medians/IQRs in eTable 4 (PDF p. 7), including the single levosimendan epinephrine observation of 2.00 μg/kg/min at day 7. DOC-004 eFigure 7 (PDF p. 16) reports hospital-stay RMSTs of 28 and 35 days and ICU-stay RMSTs of 17.5 and 19 days, consistent with DOC-001, PDF p. 6, Table 2 values after whole-day rounding (28 vs 35 and 18 vs 19).

## Ambiguous display not promoted to a candidate

DOC-001, PDF p. 6, Figure 2 includes rows labelled “No. of patients at risk” and “No. of patients with event” under a competing-risk plot. Their joint interpretation is not defined in the caption, and the displayed values are not suitable for a simple mutually exclusive subtraction because competing-risk/subdistribution risk sets can be constructed differently. The plotted endpoint estimates and the final event counts elsewhere reconcile. Consistent with the audit rule not to infer a contradiction from an ambiguous visual pattern, this was not reported as a candidate issue.
