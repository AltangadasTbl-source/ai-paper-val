## Primary outcome and competing events

**Outcome definition.** PDF p. 3 (journal p. 62), Methods—Outcomes: primary outcome was “time to successful ECMO weaning within 30 days following randomization.” Success required the patient to be alive without ECMO, another mechanical circulatory-support device, or heart transplant 30 days after ECMO removal. Competing events were weaning failure and death while receiving ECMO.

| Outcome | Exact location | Levosimendan (n = 101) | Placebo (n = 104) | Effect estimate / CI / P value | Reported interpretation / related evidence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Successful ECMO weaning within 30 days | PDF p. 4 (journal p. 63), Results—Primary Outcome; PDF p. 6 (journal p. 65), Table 2, primary outcome; Figure 2 | 69 (68.3%) | 71 (68.3%) | Risk difference 0.0% (95% CI, −12.8% to 12.7%); subdistribution HR 1.02 (95% CI, 0.74-1.39); **P = .92** | Text: “Successful ECMO weaning … occurred in 69 of 101 … and in 71 of 104.” Figure 2 reports the same sHR. |
| ECMO weaning failure (competing event) | PDF p. 6 (journal p. 65), Table 2 | 15 (14.9%) | 21 (20.2%) | Risk difference −5.3% (95% CI, −15.2% to 4.6%); sHR 0.72 (95% CI, 0.37-1.38); **P = .32** | Table 2 footnote: failure = second ECMO run, another mechanical circulatory-support device, heart transplant, or death within 30 days after ECMO separation. |
| Death before ECMO weaning (competing event) | PDF p. 6 (journal p. 65), Table 2 | 15 (14.9%) | 12 (11.5%) | Risk difference 3.3% (95% CI, −5.6% to 12.1%); sHR 1.32 (95% CI, 0.62-2.79); **P = .47** | Results—Primary Outcome states both competing components were not significantly different between groups. |

## Main-article conclusion statements

- **PDF p. 1 (journal p. 60), Abstract—Conclusions and Relevance:** “early levosimendan administration did not significantly reduce the time to successful weaning of ECMO compared with placebo.”
- **PDF p. 7 (journal p. 66), Discussion opening:** “early treatment with levosimendan was not associated with a shorter time to successful ECMO weaning.”
- **PDF p. 8 (journal p. 67), Conclusions:** “early administration of levosimendan did not significantly shorten time to successful VA-ECMO weaning. These findings do not support the routine use of levosimendan to improve outcomes in this patient population.”
