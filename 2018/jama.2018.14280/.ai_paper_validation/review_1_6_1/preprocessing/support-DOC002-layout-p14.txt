NL47013.018.14                                                            PReVENT



     Need for a new randomized controlled trial of ventilation in patients
without ARDS
The ICU community explicitly requests a well–powered trial comparing
ventilation with a lower tidal volume to ventilation with a higher tidal volume in
patients without ARDS [12, 26]. This trial should use patient–relevant and only
objective outcomes, including duration of ventilation and ICU– and hospital
length of stay (LOS), amongst other outcomes. The ‘PRotective VENTilation
in patients without ARDS at start of ventilation’–trial (PReVENT) is the first
randomized controlled trial comparing lower tidal volume ventilation with
higher tidal volume ventilation in patients without ARDS at onset of ventilation
that recruits a sufficient number of patients to test the hypothesis that lower
tidal volume ventilation benefits patients without ARDS at start of ventilation
with regard to clinically relevant endpoints. Second, PReVENT is the first
prospective study that will evaluate the incremental cost–effectiveness of
lower tidal volume ventilation in patients without ARDS.
