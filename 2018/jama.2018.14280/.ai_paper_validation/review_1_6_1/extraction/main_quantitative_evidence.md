## Statistical and result relationships

### S001 — Randomization, hypothesis and oversight

[PDF p.2](../../../jama_simonis_2018_oi_180108.pdf#page=2), introduction and Methods; p.7 Discussion. Randomized patient-level low versus intermediate strategy, 1:1; random blocks minimum 2 and maximum 6 patients; stratification by center and intubation location (inside/outside ICU). Concealed central automated allocation. Superiority question for ventilator-free days and alive at day 28. Oversight committee blind to primary endpoint at 2 predefined time points; continued trial; no interim analyses performed. Discussion calls analysis intention-to-treat and protocol pragmatic. P.8 Limitations states intervention nature prevented blinding.

### S002 — Intervention specification and duration

[PDF pp.2–3](../../../jama_simonis_2018_oi_180108.pdf#page=2), Interventions. Low starts 6 mL/kg PBW, volume-controlled or pressure support; decreases 1 mL/kg PBW each hour to minimum 4. Pressure support minimum 5 cm H2O; volumes >8 mL/kg PBW at minimum support accepted. For severe dyspnea/discomfort, more sedation need, respiratory rate >35/min, uncontrollable acidosis or asynchrony, increase tidal volume 1 mL/kg PBW/h. Intermediate starts 10 mL/kg PBW volume-controlled; if plateau >25 cm H2O decrease 1 mL/kg PBW/h; pressure support targets volume while maximum airway pressure <25. Check/readjust ≥every 8 h. Additional sedation/muscle relaxants solely to permit strategy prohibited. Maximum assigned-strategy duration 28 days; resume assigned strategy for reintubation within period. PBW formulas N057. These are report-to-plan matching keys, not comparisons already performed.

### S003 — Primary endpoint and interpretation scale

[PDF p.4](../../../jama_simonis_2018_oi_180108.pdf#page=4), Outcomes; pp.1–2 primary labels. Ventilator-free days and alive at day 28: days alive/free of invasive ventilation from randomization, provided unassisted breathing >24 consecutive hours; repeated qualifying free periods ≥24 h summed; intubation/extubation timing captured in hours and ventilation duration calculated from hours, primary expressed in days. Ventilation >28 days assigned zero ventilator-free days. Without tracheostomy successful unassisted breathing means extubation without reintubation within 24 consecutive hours; with tracheostomy means no ventilatory assistance ≥24 h. One additional free-and-alive day approximates 15% reduction of expected ventilation time. P.6 Discussion describes composite incorporating duration in surviving patients and mortality; p.7 says mortality differences not expected. No separate printed death-handling equation appears in this main endpoint paragraph.

### S004 — Sample size inputs

[PDF p.4](../../../jama_simonis_2018_oi_180108.pdf#page=4), Statistical Analysis. Estimated n=952, 476/group; power 80%; difference 1 ventilator-free day at day 28; baseline SD 5 days; α=.05; allowing dropout 20%. General reported P-value sidedness is 2-sided. No detailed sample-size equation or software function appears here.

### S005 — Primary analysis, sensitivity and missing data

[PDF p.4](../../../jama_simonis_2018_oi_180108.pdf#page=4), Statistical Analysis; pp.5–7 analysis-set narrative. Primary t tests and 95% superiority CIs. Sensitivity generalized linear mixed model with hospital and intubation location stratification variables as random effects. Primary missing data <1%, only complete-case analysis. P.5 all 961 considered, incomplete day-28 follow-up 6; Figure 1 475/480 actually included; discussion intention-to-treat. Software R 3.4.1. Main paper gives no standard error, degrees of freedom or t statistic.

### S006 — Longitudinal ventilation and observed directions

[PDF pp.4–5](../../../jama_simonis_2018_oi_180108.pdf#page=4), Statistical Analysis and Intervention; p.8 Discussion. Mixed-effect longitudinal models for tidal volumes over time, random intercepts hospitals and patients, time continuous. During first 3 days tidal volumes/airway pressures significantly different; plateau/driving pressure lower, respiratory rate higher low group; minute ventilation/PEEP no significant difference; PaO2/FiO2 no difference; PaCO2 higher and pH lower low group. P.8 links CO2 retention/respiratory acidosis to both volume-controlled and pressure-support restriction; no more sedation, mostly spontaneous ventilation. Numeric supporting keys eTables 1–3 and eFigures 2–4 in REPORT Supplement 3. No numerical P or coefficient is printed in these main narrative claims.
