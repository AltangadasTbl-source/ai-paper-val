## Findings

### F-XS-001 — Composite endpoint aggregates care opportunities in the plan and supplement but averages patient proportions in the main report

- **Locations and document roles:** PLAN [DOC-002 p16, lines216–220](../../../joi180070supp1_prod.pdf#page=16); REPORT [DOC-001 p3, Outcomes](../../../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=3); REPORT [DOC-003 p2, eAppendix](../../../joi180070supp2_prod.pdf#page=2). Affected reported values occur in REPORT [DOC-001 p1, abstract](../../../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=1), [p2, Key Points](../../../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=2), [p5, primary Results](../../../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=5), and [p7, Table 2 composite and all nine measure rows](../../../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=7). PLAN [pp18–19, lines272–279](../../../joi180070supp1_prod.pdf#page=18) and REPORT [p4, Data Analysis](../../../jama_wang_2018_oi_180070.pdf#page=4) both describe care-opportunity odds analysis and provide important analytical context.
- **Printed statements/values:** PLAN defines the composite as total interventions performed among eligible patients divided by total possible interventions among eligible patients. The REPORT supplement repeats that definition and expressly says it used the same endpoint definition as the randomized phase. The main REPORT defines each patient's performed/eligible ratio and expressly says it was calculated for each patient and then averaged. Table 2 reports composite means 88.2% (SD15.1) and 84.8% (SD18.2); the abstract/text report adjusted difference 3.54% (95% CI0.68–6.40), P=.02. Care-opportunity odds analysis is separately described and reported as ORPA1.39 (1.12–1.72), P=.003.
- **Comparison logic and reproduction:** For performed measures a_i and eligible measures b_i, the plan/supplement's pooled ratio is sum(a_i)/sum(b_i); the main report's equal-patient average is mean(a_i/b_i). These assign different patient weights when eligibility differs. The nine exact Table 2 event counts sum to 10699 intervention and 9930 control; eligible counts sum to12064 and11688. Pooled percentages are 100×10699/12064=88.6853% and100×9930/11688=84.9589%, rounding to88.7% and85.0%, rather than88.2% and84.8%. The counts are integer quantities, and these percentages lie outside the printed composite rounding intervals [88.15,88.25) and[84.75,84.85). This calculation is a check of the endpoint definition, not a comparison of adjusted estimates with crude ratios. The adjusted difference is not required to equal the crude pooled difference3.7264 percentage points.
- **Focus area:** D (planned/report primary endpoint definition); also the cross-report label/definition tier because the supplement explicitly identifies its definition with the randomized phase.
- **Proposed review domain:** `protocol_report`.
- **Proposed primary relationship:** `P_R`; **All relationships:** `P_R, S_C`. No plan-internal contradiction is asserted. The care-opportunity inferential analysis is not itself classified as a conflicting relationship merely because it differs from a descriptive patient average.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** The composite co-primary endpoint represented by88.2% vs84.8%, its meaning as a patient-average versus an opportunity-weighted adherence quantity, and the correspondence of that reported result to the prespecified composite endpoint. The finding does not establish that a different definition would change statistical significance.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** The main report clearly identifies its patient-averaged descriptive quantity, but the plan and supplement explicitly define an aggregate opportunity quantity, and the supplement calls that the randomized-phase definition. The exact printed counts show that these are numerically distinct here. The shared opportunity-based OR analysis does not explain whether the descriptive endpoint definition changed, whether the plan's wording intended a patient-level average despite stating an aggregate ratio, or how the adjusted absolute difference relates to the two defined summaries. This concerns endpoint weighting rather than an abbreviated clinical name.
- **Supported alternatives and missing definition:** The main report may have adopted a patient-average descriptive endpoint while retaining opportunity-based odds inference; alternatively, the plan/supplement may have intended the patient-average definition but described an aggregate ratio. A disclosed clarification of weighting or an endpoint-definition amendment is not supplied. No preferred analysis is proposed.
- **Human verification steps:** Read the three definition locations together; verify the nine Table 2 event/eligibility counts and the pooled totals; distinguish the descriptive means from the adjusted difference and ORPA; establish which weighting was prespecified and which quantity the composite absolute-difference analysis estimates. Adjudicate whether the discrepancy warrants clarification.
