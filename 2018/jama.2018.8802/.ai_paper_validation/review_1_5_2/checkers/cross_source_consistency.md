## Candidate proposal

### XCAND001 — baseline-survey total does not reconcile with stated per-cluster enrollment

- **Primary category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency.
- **Exact locations and printed values:** DOC-001 Table 1, PDF p6, `Baseline Survey, No. (%)` column: `Hospitals, No.` = 40 and `Patients, No.` = 801. DOC-003 eAppendix, PDF p2: “20 patients per cluster were prospectively included” in the baseline survey. N072 is the DOC-003 p2 baseline-survey numeric block and confirms this same C007/CROSS_SOURCE_CHECK_COMPLETE proposal, not a new candidate.
- **Rule and comparison:** For the matched pre-randomization baseline survey, 20 patients per 40 clusters implies 800 patients. The printed Table 1 denominator is 801.
- **Supported alternative:** One cluster may have contributed 21 patients, or the per-cluster number may have been an operational target. Neither supplied location states this or calls 20 approximate.
- **Human verification steps:** Check the baseline-survey enrollment log/analysis dataset; confirm number of contributing clusters and each cluster's baseline enrollment; clarify which printed location, if either, needs amendment.
- **Remaining human question:** Did all 40 participating clusters contribute exactly 20 baseline-survey patients, and if so, why does the `Baseline Survey, No. (%)` column print `Patients, No.` = 801?
