 Introduction  42 
 Stroke  is  a  major  cause  of  death  and  long- term  disability  in  China.  The  annual  cost  43  of  stroke  care  is  about  5.76  billion  US  dollars. 1- 3  Improving  stroke  care  quality  and  44  clinical  outcomes  is  a  national  priority. 4  Large- scale  randomized  trials  and  systematic  45  reviews  have  established  the  efficacy  and  safety  of  interventions  for  acute  ischemic  46  stroke  ( AIS),  such  as  intravenous  recombinant  tissue  plasminogen  activator  ( IV  rtPA),  47  antiplatelet  therapy,  anticoagulation  for  patients  with  atrial  fibrillation,  deep  vein  48  thrombosis  ( DVT)  prophylaxis,  swallowing  dysfunction  management,  statin  treatment,  49  antihypertensive  and  hypoglycemic  treatment. 5- 16  Nevertheless,  the  adherence  to  these  50  evidence- based  interventions  is  suboptimal  in  China. 17- 19  Research  studies  in  other  51  countries  have  demonstrated  that  quality  improvement  programs  can  be  effective  in  52  improving  the  quality  of  stroke  care.  Interventions  include  clinical  pathway  or  53  standardized  order  sets,  training  and  distribution  of  educational  materials  to  heath  care  54  providers,  and  audit  with  timely  feedback. 20- 22  Combination  of  different  methods  55  targeting  different  barriers  have  been  shown  effective  in  patients  with  acute  coronary  56  syndromes. 23,  24  However,  to  date  these  quality  improvement  interventions  have  been  57  limited  to  high  income  countries  and  not  evaluated  in  less  well  developed  health  care  58  settings  such  as  China.  59 
 Using  hospitals  participating  in  the  China  National  Network  of  Stroke  Research,  we  60  design  a  cluster- randomized  trial  to  evaluate  the  effectiveness  of  a  multifaceted  61  quality  improvement  initiative  to  bridge  the  gap  of  evidence- based  medicine  in  AIS  62  management  in  China  ( GOLDEN  BRIDGE- AIS).  63

 Study  aim  64 
 The  aim  is  to  evaluate  whether  a  multifaceted  quality  improvement  intervention  will  65  improve  the  adherence  to  individual,  composite  and  all- or- none  measures  for  patients  66  with  AIS  presenting  within  the  first  7  days  from  stroke  symptom  onset  and  reduce  the  67  incidence  of  in- hospital  death,  a  new  vascular  event,  disability,  and  all- cause  death  at  68  3,  6,  and  12- months  after  the  initial  symptom  onset.  69

 The  stroke  quality  improvement  interventions  105 
 The  multifaceted  quality  improvement  intervention  includes  an  evidence- based  106  clinical  pathway,  written  care  protocols  for  implementation  of  performance  measures  107  ( Figure  2),  a  full- time  quality  coordinator,  and  monitoring  and  feedback  system  for  108  the  AIS  performance  measures.  A  local  quality  care  initiative  ( QCI)  team  at  each  109  intervention  hospital  is  established  to  take  responsibility  for  the  implementation  of  the  110  multifaceted  intervention.
