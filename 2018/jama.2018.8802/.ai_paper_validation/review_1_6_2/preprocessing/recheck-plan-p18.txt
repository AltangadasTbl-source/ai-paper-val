253   Statistical analysis plan

254     An “intention-to-treat” analysis will be performed. Baseline characteristics of

255   hospitals and patients will be analyzed to assess cluster differences between treatment

256   and control groups. Possible control variables will be identified and included in the

257   final models. Categorical variables will be summarized as proportion; continuous

258   variables with normal distribution will be summarized with mean and standard

259   deviation (SD), and those with skewed distribution will be expressed with median and

260   interquartile range.

261

262     In univariate analysis for comparisons between groups, χ2 test will be used to

263   compare categorical variables, and Student t test or Mann-Whitney U test will be used

264   to compare continuous variables.

265

266     In multivariate analysis, a generalized estimation equation accounting for

267   within-hospital correlation will be used to compare between the intervention and

268   control groups at the patient and hospital level for modeling proportions and

269   quantitative variables. The effects of intervention will be demonstrated according the

270   features of variables. Qualitative and quantitative variables and will be expressed as a

271   population average odds ratio and the mean difference separately, both with 95%

272   confidence intervals (CIs). The composite measure was expressed as the odds of

273   fulfilling care opportunities that each measure for which a patient was eligible for.
