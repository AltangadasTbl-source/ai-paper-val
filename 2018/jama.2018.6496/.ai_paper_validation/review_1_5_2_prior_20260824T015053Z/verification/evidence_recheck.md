- **Direct observation versus inferred explanation:** Direct observation comprises `n=381`, `n=376`, denominators `352` and `344`, and footnote oxygen-saturation missingness `43 (19, 24)`. The claim that these must reconcile is a direct application of the same-field available-data identity. Any transposition or distinct-time-point mechanism is inferred rather than printed.
- **Exact remaining human question:** Which values define baseline oxygen-saturation availability—denominators `352` and `344` (implying `29` and `32` missing) or footnote b's `19` and `24` missing—and, if both displays are intended, what separately defined field or time point connects each display to its denominator?
