# Cross-source consistency check

## Scope and method

This checker completed a fresh-source reconciliation for DOC001 (main article, 11 PDF pages), DOC002 (protocol, 25 PDF pages), and DOC003 (Supplement 2, 13 PDF pages). It used the canonical inventories `N001`–`N055` and `S001`–`S038`, both fresh mapper artifacts, fresh OCR text, and the corresponding fresh rendered pages where a table or figure layout was material. No legacy audit derivative or external source was used.

An occurrence was compared only after matching its population, analysis set, time point, contrast, model, measure, scale/unit, reference group, and displayed precision. Planned protocol statements and the clustering-by-physician supplementary reanalysis were not treated as duplicates of an unadjusted published estimate merely because they use the same numerator and denominator.

Direct-source links used below: [DOC001 main article](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf), [DOC002 protocol](../../../joi180054supp1_prod.pdf), and [DOC003 Supplement 2](../../../joi180054supp2_prod.pdf).

## Distinct qualifying cross-source candidate signals

### Signal A — Published first-attempt duration endpoint differs from the protocol endpoint

- **Candidate category:** Measure, label, or scale inconsistency.
- **Identity rule:** The same named first-attempt timing outcome must have the same start and end events before its reported seconds, median, difference, Kaplan-Meier curve, or hazard ratio can be interpreted as the protocol-specified endpoint. Population, device contrast, and first-attempt restriction match; the endpoint does not.
- **Published/main evidence:** DOC001 defines “the duration of an intubation attempt” as elapsed time from laryngoscope-blade insertion to blade removal from the mouth ([PDF p. 3](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=3)). Table 3 and its footnote apply that blade-in/blade-out definition to first-attempt duration ([PDF p. 7](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=7)); DOC003 eTable 1 repeats it ([PDF p. 3](../../../joi180054supp2_prod.pdf#page=3)). The displayed all-patient medians are 38 versus 36 seconds in DOC001 ([PDF p. 1](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=1); [PDF p. 7](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=7)) and DOC003 ([PDF p. 2](../../../joi180054supp2_prod.pdf#page=2)).
- **Protocol comparator:** DOC002 calls the planned secondary outcome “Time to intubation (first attempt only)” and defines it as time from beginning of the attempt to inflation of the ETT cuff when the tube is in the trachea ([PDF p. 9](../../../joi180054supp1_prod.pdf#page=9)); its measurement section again directs the research assistant to time that definition ([PDF p. 10](../../../joi180054supp1_prod.pdf#page=10)).
- **Comparison logic:** Blade removal and ETT-cuff inflation are distinct events; the latter can occur while the blade remains in the mouth, and blade removal can occur after cuff inflation. Thus the supplied documents identify non-interchangeable timing endpoints, not merely a wording or precision variant.
- **Source-grounded alternative interpretation:** The final report may intentionally have replaced the planned endpoint with the blade-in/blade-out duration endpoint, or a protocol amendment/statistical-analysis specification may exist but is not among the supplied sources. DOC003 consistently supports the published blade-in/blade-out endpoint; it does not document why it differs from DOC002.
- **Human verification question:** Was the published first-attempt-duration outcome prospectively amended from beginning-to-cuff-inflation to blade-in/blade-out, and if so, where is the amendment and which endpoint generated each reported median, difference, curve, and hazard ratio?

### Signal B — Reversed interval ordering in the main all-patient first-attempt-duration display, with cross-document corroboration

- **Candidate category:** Cross-document numeric inconsistency (also a numeric/interval-ordering inconsistency).
- **Identity rule:** For the all-randomized-patient, Bougie-minus-ETT+stylet median first-attempt-duration contrast, a two-sided interval must display its lower endpoint before its upper endpoint. The contrast, population (N=757), unit (seconds), and group medians (38 versus 36 seconds) match across the locations below. The inference model is explicitly retained as a distinction.
- **Main-table evidence:** DOC001 Table 3 prints `1 (4 to −1)` seconds, with 38 (29–51) versus 36 (25–54) seconds and P=.24 ([PDF p. 7](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=7)). The printed lower/upper sequence is 4 then −1, which is descending and does not contain the printed positive point estimate under the ordinary lower-to-upper interval convention.
- **Supplement comparator:** DOC003 eTable 1 prints the same medians and point difference as `1 s (−1 s to 4 s)` ([PDF p. 2](../../../joi180054supp2_prod.pdf#page=2)). Its footnote says the group columns are unchanged while difference, P value, and interaction columns were recalculated to account for clustering by physician ([PDF p. 3](../../../joi180054supp2_prod.pdf#page=3)).
- **Comparison logic:** The two sources use the same endpoint definition (blade in to blade out), same all-patient group medians, same contrast direction, and the same two interval endpoints. DOC003 orders `−1` before `4`; DOC001 reverses them. The main P=.24 and supplementary P=.95 are not compared as a contradiction because DOC003 expressly uses a clustering-adjusted model.
- **Source-grounded alternative interpretation:** The reversed DOC001 order can be a typographical transposition in the unadjusted table. Because DOC003 is a separate clustering-adjusted analysis, its interval cannot alone prove that the unadjusted CI was numerically identical, though it provides a supplied-source display with the same endpoints in conventional order.
- **Human verification question:** Does the unadjusted Table 3 output support `1 s (−1 to 4)` rather than `1 s (4 to −1)`, and were the unadjusted and clustering-adjusted interval endpoints coincidentally identical after rounding?

## Limitations

The supplied package has no protocol amendment, statistical-analysis-plan revision, raw data, or code. Therefore the timing-endpoint change cannot be resolved from the supplied sources, and the exact unadjusted interval-generation output behind the Table 3 ordering cannot be reproduced. Fresh OCR was adequate for text; the fresh rendered Table 3 and eTable pages were used for the interval/order comparison.
