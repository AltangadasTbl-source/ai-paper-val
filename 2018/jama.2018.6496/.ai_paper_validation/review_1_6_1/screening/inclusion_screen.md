## Decision justifications

- **F-XS-001:** The protocol explicitly ends its planned time-to-intubation outcome at cuff inflation, whereas both reports explicitly end the displayed duration at blade removal, an unresolved endpoint-definition comparison that can change how the abstract's 38-versus-36-second result is extracted.
- **F-XS-002:** The protocol distinguishes the primary outcome, first-pass success, from its main difficult-airway analysis population, and the interim section's immediate 500-enrolled/1,000-patient projection explains an all-enrolled first-pass-success calculation without importing the final main-analysis subset restriction.
- **F-ST1-001:** Its duration comparison duplicates F-XS-001, and its directly stated hypoxemia-window comparison is retained as another occurrence of the same cuff-inflation-versus-attempt-completion timing-anchor pattern, without registering separate numerical or hazard-ratio consequences.
- **F-ST1-002:** A replacement of the operative accrual rule would require that attaining the difficult-airway target caused closure before the planned calendar/total-enrollment endpoint, while the supplied September 2016-August 2017 period, 757 total patients, and 380 difficult-airway patients also permit completion at the planned one-year endpoint.
- **F-ST1-003:** As for F-XS-002, the quoted interim section projects all enrolled patients using the primary outcome measure and does not explicitly restrict that interim calculation to the final main-analysis difficult-airway subset.
