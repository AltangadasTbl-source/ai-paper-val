This figure displays estimates of the time until successful intubation by group for all patients.
The hazard ratio for first attempt success the bougie group was 1.12 (95% Cl 0.97 -1.30), with
endotracheal tube plus stylet group as reference. Vertical ticks mark the time point when the
intubation attempt for one or more patients ended in failure. The assumption of proportional

hazards over time was not upheld.
