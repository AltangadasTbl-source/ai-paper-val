## Evidence units by PDF page

### Page 1 — abstract and title

Location: [DOC-001 p. 1](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=1). Trial period September 2016-August 2017; adults aged 18 years or older; emergency orotracheal intubation with Macintosh blade. Randomized intervention totals: bougie `n=381`; endotracheal tube + stylet (ETT+stylet) `n=376`. Primary population: at least one difficult-airway characteristic (listed as body fluids obscuring laryngeal view, obstruction/edema, obesity, short neck, small mandible, large tongue, facial trauma, cervical-spine immobilization). Secondary outcomes: all-patient first-attempt success, success without hypoxemia, first-attempt duration, esophageal intubation, hypoxemia.

Abstract result population: 757 randomized, mean age 46 years, women 230 (30%), 757 (100%) completed; difficult-airway population 380. Primary success: bougie 96%, ETT+stylet 82%, absolute difference 14% (95% CI 8% to 20%). All-patient success: 98% vs 87%, difference 11% (95% CI 7% to 14%). First-attempt median duration: 38 s vs 36 s. Hypoxemia: 13% vs 14%. Trial registration NCT02902146.

### Page 2 — numeric design/background and population definitions

Location: [DOC-001 p. 2](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=2). Background estimates: 310,000 US ED intubations in 2014; approximately 12% with adverse intubation-related event in a 2002-2012 registry; 85% first-attempt success and hence 15% failure in another registry; bougie use in less than 5% of ED first attempts; approximately 60% of ED intubation patients estimated to have anatomic/other difficulty features. Site: 109,000 annual ED visits. Eligibility/exclusion and difficult-airway-characteristic definitions match page 1.

### Page 3 — device, outcome, and statistical definitions

Location: [DOC-001 p. 3](../../../jama_driver_2018_oi_180054.pdf#page=3). Randomization: 1:1, permuted blocks of sizes 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; 2 strata (obesity/cervical immobilization vs neither). Bougie: 70 cm, 15 French, 5-mm diameter. Tube-resistance procedure: retract 2 cm and rotate 90 degrees counterclockwise.

Primary success definition: successful tube placement with the first device passed during first laryngoscope insertion; no bougie/ETT+stylet passed during that insertion counted as failure. Hypoxemia definition: oxyhemoglobin saturation <90%, or baseline <90% followed by absolute decrease >10%, by continuous oximetry. Duration: laryngoscope insertion to removal from mouth.

Power/design: target 374 difficult-airway patients; 80% power to detect 9 percentage-point difference (95% vs 86%) at two-sided alpha .05. All enrolled patients included in final analysis, principal analyses intention-to-treat. Reported methods: difference in proportions/median difference with 95% CI; Hodges-Lehmann median differences for continuous variables; chi-square for binary outcomes; Wilcoxon rank-sum for continuous outcomes; two-sided .05 threshold. Duration analysis used Kaplan-Meier/log-rank and unadjusted Cox hazard ratios; proportionality assessed with Schoenfeld residuals/data visualization. Unplanned subgroup interaction tests had no multiple-comparison correction; post hoc physician-clustering analysis; no imputation.
