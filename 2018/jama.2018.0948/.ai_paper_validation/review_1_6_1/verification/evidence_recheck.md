The possible additional circulatory subset is the symmetric difference (F minus V) union (V minus F), with any patient already meeting another composite component not necessarily adding an event. This is a logical eligibility comparison, not a patient-level count calculation.

Let W mean qualifying ward death and I qualifying death in the first hour of urgent ICU admission. Protocol category 7 explicitly states ward death with no transfer: W. Report component d states W OR I. The possible additional death subset is I outside W and outside any other qualifying composite component. Protocol categories 3 and 6 can already capture some I events via treatment, but the supplied source does not establish universal overlap. Both plan and report death text allow intent to perform CPR without delivered CPR. The report's additional ICU death window therefore cannot be removed solely by treating every such death as a delivered-CPR event. No changed death count is asserted.

**Display-precision interval check:** Completed. These fall within the half-unit intervals for their two-decimal displays: 0.50 → [0.495,0.505) and 0.84 → [0.835,0.845). Thus the rates reconcile. The printed 386 equals 127+259 exactly. Repeated rate ratios, interval limits, P values and differences match at their displayed precision; no additional numerical inconsistency is claimed.
