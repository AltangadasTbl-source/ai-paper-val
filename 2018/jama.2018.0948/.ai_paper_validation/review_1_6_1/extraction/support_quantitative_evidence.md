## Planned design, endpoints, populations and analysis relationships

PLAN-01 (DOC-002 pp. 1, 7-10, 15-16): parallel hospital-cluster trial, intervention documentation+score-matched recommendations versus established usual care. Hospital strata <200 versus >=200 eligible beds, balanced 1:1 within strata. Planned recruitment 22 hospitals (20 effective plus 1-2 attrition), all eligible ward patients >37-week gestation/<18 years, >200 eligible admissions/year/hospital. PICU/NICU-only care and direct ICU admissions dying before eligible ward care excluded; eligible ED admissions depend on documentation format. Exclude hospitals introducing MET-RRT or already using severity scores. Six-month/26-week baseline, five-week implementation interval, 12-month/52-week intervention; 78 measured weeks and 83 elapsed weeks. Run-in five weeks in 20-30 beds; later hospital-wide implementation. Match main Methods and participant flow.

PLAN-02 (DOC-002 pp. 10,24; DOC-004 pp. 6-7): primary all-cause hospital mortality includes DNR patients who received eligible ward care. Binary numerator deaths; hospital discharge denominator; report per 1000 discharges. Secondary mortality without DNR records no DNR at death. ICU death, PIM/PELOD, ventilation, readmissions and process/resource outcomes are additional secondary outcomes. Main key secondary is SCDE/late ICU admission, one or more respiratory/circulatory/CPR/death criteria. Protocol p.24 categories 1 early and 2 non-invasive support do not qualify; categories 3 respiratory, 4 circulatory, 5 combined late transfer, 6 CPR/ECMO, 7 ward death qualify. Intubation at transfer/within one hour ICU; >60ml/kg fluid in preceding 12 hours; protocol circulatory row joins fluid and inotrope/vasopressor with 'and'; report definition p.6 joins with 'and/or'. Protocol ward death criterion is ward death without DNR; report adds death within one hour urgent ICU admission. Record distinctions as source specifications, without assigning an issue. Main Table 2 SCDE and main Methods are matching keys.

PLAN-03 (DOC-002 pp.11-12,24,28; DOC-004 p.6): cardiac arrest includes chest compression or death with absent vital signs/no compression without prior DNR, including within one hour ICU. Preventability scored 1-6 by two blinded physicians; explicit qualifying scores 4,5,6; consensus discussion and third adjudicator if needed. Protocol p.11 prints '>4' followed immediately by included scores 4,5,6; Table7 p.28 states 4 or more. Protocol Table6 legend p.27 refers to intensity scale 4 or5, while p.11 identifies arrest as6 or7. These are locators/definitions, not diagnoses. Match main cardiac-arrest outcomes and kappa.

PLAN-04 (DOC-002 pp.12-15; DOC-004 pp.6-7,12-13): urgent ICU departure <6h from confirmed/definite-possibility transfer; intraoperative urgent cases included irrespective of delay. Readmission before third midnight following discharge (second full calendar day); denominators hospital or ICU discharges as applicable. VFD alive and unventilated over28 days from first ICU admission in each period; discharged early assumed alive/unventilated by protocol p.15. Hospital/ICU/technology days count whole or part00:00-23:59 day. Report PIM from first12h and PELOD first24h/whole stay; higher scores poorer condition; PELOD0-71. PIM inverse logit 1/(1+exp(-PIM)); minimum -8.4137 ->0.02%, example +9.7778 ->99.9% with PaO2=40, BE=-35. Match main ICU Table2 and supplemental eTable4.

PLAN-05 (DOC-002 p.16): primary hospital-level logistic model, dependent logit mortality proportion, intervention indicator, baseline mortality logit and hospital-size stratification variable; weighted by hospital size. Same logistic model for ICU mortality by ward/OR initiation, readmissions and mortality after DNR. Poisson hospital counts for SCDE, code-blue, stat and urgent consultations, adjusted intervention+baseline rate+hospital strata. Linear hospital-mean models for clinical deterioration nature, ward days, PIM2, PELOD, VFD, ICU days and therapy days, weighted by hospital size; baseline mean and strata. Documentation linear model unweighted because equal sampled records; baseline and strata. Surveys descriptive and size-weighted linear group comparisons. All analyses adjust baseline and hospital strata. Planned subgroups: hospital size, MET availability, ECMO availability, ward-initiated urgent ICU admission. Outcomes analyzed once; no interim analysis. Match main Statistical Analysis, sensitivity and subgroup results.
