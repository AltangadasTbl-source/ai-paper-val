Level2: π0i=β00+β01 A_i+β02 I_i+β03 F_i+r0i; π1i=β10+β11 A_i+β12 I_i+r1i; π2i=β20+β21 A_i+β22 I_i+r2i. Combined equation has corresponding fixed intercept,linear,quadratic brackets and random r0i+r1iT+r2iT²+e. β00 control male initial BMI at centered age, β02 baseline arm difference expected0; β03 female initial-BMI effect; β12 intervention linear growth, β22 intervention acceleration. Unstructured variance-covariance matrix stated. Primary LRT2df H0:β12=β22=0; joint P<.05 demonstrates intervention effectiveness; nonsignificant joint test does not demonstrate effectiveness. Main match keys: fixed effects,time polynomial,random effects,covariance,ITT,ML,primary joint P.
