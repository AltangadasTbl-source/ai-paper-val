## Review rules and limitations

Counts are integers, so no count rounding is assumed. A displayed decimal x at precision d represents [x−0.5×10^(−d), x+0.5×10^(−d)]; arithmetic comparisons propagate these intervals. Percentages use their explicit row denominator, then arm randomized denominator only where appropriate. Adjusted means, Poisson risk ratios and mixed-model coefficients are not required to equal crude differences or ratios. All eTable2 moderator strata/validity denominators were checked independently; subsets are not additive partitions unless labeled exhaustive. No person-time, hazard ratio, or crude odds-ratio result is supplied. Graphical coordinates without printed values cannot furnish exact arithmetic. No raw data are supplied. These limits do not create omitted units.
