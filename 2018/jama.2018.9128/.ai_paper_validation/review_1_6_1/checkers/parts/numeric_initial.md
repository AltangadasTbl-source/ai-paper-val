## Findings

### F-NUM-001

Shared school-readiness session duration differs between revised protocol and report.

- **Exact compared locations and roles:** REPORT [main article physical PDF p3, Control condition paragraph](../../../../jama_barkin_2018_oi_180075.pdf#page=3); PLAN [revised protocol physical PDF p63, design figure](../../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=63), and PLAN [p64, lines2407–2408](../../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=64). Original PLAN [pp13–15](../../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=14) is version context, not the decisive comparator. Amendment register PLAN [pp106–107](../../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=106) and final-SAP disclosures PLAN [p113](../../../../joi180075supp1_prod.pdf#page=113) were checked for context.
- **Printed inputs:** REPORT: curriculum consisted of six30-minute group-based activities concurrently with data collection, received by both arms. Revised PLAN figure: six45-minute control sessions. Revised PLAN narrative:45-minute school-readiness/school-success program during each of seven collection points. Original protocol described12×60-minute quarterly sessions. The six-versus-seven revised narrative is separable from the duration comparison and includes later optional follow-up context.
- **Rule and calculation:** Match the shared school-readiness program rather than obesity-prevention coaching. Revised figure45minutes versus report30minutes gives15minutes/session; six-session totals270 versus180minutes,90minutes difference. Even treating integer minute durations conservatively as rounded intervals, [44.5,45.5] and[29.5,30.5] are disjoint; six-session totals[267,273] and[177,183] are disjoint. The count is exact program structure rather than a rounded sample count.
- **Focus area:** D, planned versus reported duration of randomized intervention/comparator elements.
- **Proposed review domain:** protocol_report.
- **Proposed relationship:** P_R; compared document roles PLAN and REPORT.
- **Affected result or extracted value:** comparator and common-program contact duration associated with the reported36-month primary BMI trajectory comparison; an intervention data extractor would record180 versus270minutes of shared school-readiness content from these locations.
- **Why context does not resolve it:** Both locations identify the school-readiness program delivered to both arms. Neither the immediate paragraphs nor reviewed amendment/SAP disclosure text identifies30minutes as a shortened delivery or separates45minutes into30minutes curriculum plus another component. A reader cannot select the applicable planned/delivered duration from the package at once. The original60-minute version demonstrates evolution but does not explain the revised45-to-reported30-minute difference.
- **Alternatives:** The delivered sessions may have been shortened; the45minutes may include components outside the30-minute activities; or the report/protocol may describe distinct versions. These are possible explanations, not established facts. Actual session composition or the applicable dose amendment is missing.
- **Exact human question:** What planned and delivered school-readiness session duration applies to the six common sessions in this trial, and what supplied version or component definition reconciles the revised protocol's45minutes with the report's30minutes?
