## Numeric rules

Counts are exact integers. A value printed to d decimal places admits the half-unit interval x ± 0.5 × 10^(-d); arithmetic differences and ratios propagate both input intervals. Model-derived estimates are not equated with crude means or risks. Growth coefficients are linear BMI difference/year or quadratic BMI difference/year²; graphical risk differences are probabilities, not ratios. Counts from different valid-data subsets are neither additive partitions nor the randomized population. Exact graphical coordinates and unprinted software inputs are not manufactured.
