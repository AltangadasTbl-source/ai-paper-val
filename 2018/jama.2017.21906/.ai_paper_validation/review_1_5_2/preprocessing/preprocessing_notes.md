# Preprocessing Notes

Fresh direct-source preparation covered 80 of 80 PDF-page units. Native and layout extraction was performed anew for every source. Twenty result-relevant visual pages were rendered at 180 dpi: DOC-001 pp. 1 and 4-10; DOC-003 p. 4; and DOC-004 pp. 17-27.

DOC-004 pp. 3-16 have an unusable fresh text layer. The existing supplied OCR was accepted only because its manifest confirms the exact current source SHA-256 and page numbers, and because the user explicitly requested its reuse to avoid OCR. No Tesseract or other OCR engine was run in this review. The direct PDF is the controlling evidence for any later visual confirmation.
