# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- The package does not include raw participant data, final model objects, production tables, covariance matrices, or response-level analysis outputs. Therefore the review verifies printed relationships and source-defined rules but cannot determine which discrepant printed value is supported by the underlying output.
- The supplied sources do not provide all model variance, standard-error, degrees-of-freedom, sidedness, multiplicity, or test-specific inputs needed to independently reconstruct inferential statistics. No unsupported reconstruction was used.
- eTable 1 does not provide a source-grounded definition of its complete-versus-missing follow-up contrast order or CI procedure that resolves the printed intervention-minus-control footnote.
- The package includes one dated statistical analysis plan but no later amendment or separate final prespecification record that resolves the age-band or hospital-type subgroup list differences.
- The supplied sources do not establish whether `heparin` exhausts the study's broader `anticoagulant` component definition for the optimal in-hospital medication composite.
- DOC-004 pp. 3-16 have unusable native/layout text. At the user's direction, the run used only the supplied page- and SHA-matched OCR as fallback and checked relied-upon exact values against the matching page images/direct PDF. No new OCR was performed. Direct PDFs remain authoritative.
- eFigures on DOC-004 pp. 24-27 do not print exact point labels, so plotted point estimates were not inferred or subjected to unsupported arithmetic reconstruction.
- This review is limited to quantitative and statistical reporting consistency in the supplied package. It is not a broad methodology, study-design, clinical, novelty, misconduct, or raw-data audit.
