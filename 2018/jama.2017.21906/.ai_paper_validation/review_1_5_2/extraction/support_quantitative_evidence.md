## DOC-002 — protocol, version 22 September 2014

- **pp. 6-7 (design and planned totals):** cluster-randomized stepped wedge; 4-month baseline, five cohorts/steps, subsequent implementation at months 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20; 12-14 hospitals per cohort, 60-70 hospitals/clinics, 2-year duration, and planned 15,750 subjects. The stated primary comparison is 30-day MACE before versus after implementation while accounting for hospital, cohort, and time.
- **pp. 9-10 (endpoint/population definitions):** primary objective is 30-day MACE in ACS; MACE is death, myocardial infarction, stroke, or GUSTO major bleeding. Secondary outcomes include 30-day SAQ, economic assessment, in-hospital/discharge medication prescription rates, lifestyle advice, in-hospital and 30-day expanded MACE, and performance-measure concordance. Eligible patients have STEMI or NSTEMI and are at least 18 years; a 2,200-patient subset has SAQ/economic assessment.
- **pp. 10-13 (timing and data capture):** 30-day follow-up is by telephone or in-person contact; no single-hospital maximum is specified; in-hospital data entry is within 7 days after discharge and follow-up/SAQ/economic entry within 48 hours of contact. Follow-up ends at 30 days after hospitalization.
- **pp. 15-19 (event and monitoring definitions):** expected 30-day MACE components are death, MI, stroke, and GUSTO major bleeding; serious/unanticipated event reporting has 24-hour notification, 10-business-day reporting, and a 15-day post-withdrawal trigger. One interim DSMB analysis occurs at one year. The protocol repeats the SAP sample-size specification: alpha .05, 15,000 patients, 80% power, detectable 2.4% absolute difference from 10.4% baseline MACE, inflated to 15,750 for up to 5% discharge-to-follow-up dropout.
- **pp. 20-21 (data-quality sampling):** CCDC reviews aggregate conduct quarterly; site audits use 5% of source documents and a separate random 5% sample of discharged patients for existence confirmation; data are submitted biweekly and aggregated quarterly.

## DOC-003 — statistical analysis plan, version 17 June 2015

- **pp. 2-5 (objectives/design/endpoints/sample size):** confirms five hospital steps after a 4-month baseline and transitions at months 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20; Figure 2 says 12 hospitals per cohort. ICC=.05. The power design is alpha=.05, n=15,000, 80% power, 2.4% absolute difference from baseline 10.4% 30-day MACE, and n=15,750 allowing up to 5% dropout. Primary MACE is all-cause death, MI, stroke, and GUSTO major bleeding. Secondary endpoints and medication composites are explicitly defined; the two substudies use a 2,200-subject subsample, and medical-therapy concordance is >80% of recommended drugs.
- **pp. 6-8 (planned calculation rules):** ITT includes every patient recorded during 24 months and allocates intervention exposure by randomization irrespective of implementation timing. Continuous variables: n, mean, SD, median, min, max; categorical variables: n and %. Missing 30-day outcome means no data after outpatient follow-up or three telephone attempts; complete-case and multiple-imputation sensitivity analyses are planned. Primary and listed secondary clinical endpoints use mixed-effects logistic regression with random hospital effects and fixed 4-month time effects; baseline comprises 12 clusters in five groups. Sensitivity adjustment may use GRACE; prespecified subgroups are hospital size/toolkit use, age <65 versus >65, sex, and STEMI versus NSTEMI.
- **p. 8 (interim rule):** one-year efficacy interim comparison is usual-care hospitals versus steps 1-2 after implementation; O'Brien-Fleming thresholds are z=2.797, P<.005 (interim) and z=1.977, P<.048 (final). Adverse-event interim review is semiannual.
