## Results narrative: recruitment, exclusions, and baseline populations

**Location:** [pp. 5-6](../../../jama_huffman_2018_oi_170166.pdf#page=5). Recruited 22,557 from 63 hospitals: 67% private, 14% government affiliated with medical colleges, 19% nonprofit/charity. Exclusions: biomarkers 954, post-thrombolysis transfer 132, outside period 91, time discrepancy 6. Of 21,374 eligible participants, 295 (1%) incomplete outcome data: 215 not followed by enrolling site and 80 unreachable after three attempts. Intervention/control contained 11,308 (53%)/10,066 (47%). Narrative overall values: 60.6 y mean age, 76% men, 31% tobacco history, 44% diabetes, 64% STEMI, and control/intervention symptom-to-door median (IQR) 240 (120-840)/255 (111-825) min. Included vs missing-follow-up troponin 1.3 vs 4.6 ng/mL, P<.001. Quality-assurance narrative: one site prompted 10% case sampling; two additional sites; 13 changes to primary outcome (1% of overall events).
