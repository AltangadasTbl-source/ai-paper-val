## Extracted definitions and quantitative evidence

### Neurological, ventilation, and safety definitions

1. **DOC-002 p. 101 — mRS.** The form defines mRS 0=no symptoms through 6=dead, with total marked `0-6`. This is a six-step ordinal range with seven values, not a percentage or count.
2. **DOC-002 p. 102 — CPC.** CPC 1=good cerebral performance, 2=moderate cerebral disability, 3=severe cerebral disability, 4=coma/vegetative state, and 5=brain death. This scale definition is relevant to the SAP's `CPC <=2` favourable-neurological-function endpoint (pp. 122/131).
3. **DOC-002 p. 104 — VAS.** The bag-valve-mask difficulty measure is a 0-100 VAS, with anchors `No, not at all` (0) and `Yes, extremely difficult` (100).
4. **DOC-002 p. 105 — Han classification.** Grade 0=mask ventilation not attempted; 1=ventilated by mask; 2=ventilated with oral airway/other adjuvant; 3=difficult ventilation; 4=unable to mask ventilate.
5. **DOC-002 p. 110.** The V1.4 comparison defines technique failure as 28-day mortality, procedural regurgitation, or procedure failure; procedure failure is failure to ventilate in BVM or failure to intubate in the intubation group. It repeats the primary endpoint as 28-day survival with CPC <=2 and adds that, with pre-randomization neurological disability, survival with the same degree of disability is favourable. This amendment qualification is an exact comparator for later endpoint interpretation.
6. **DOC-002 pp. 109, 114-116.** Amendment materials distinguish named expected complications from observed event data. Aspiration pneumonia is added to both intervention-group lists; bag-mask failure is added to the BMV list. No numerator, denominator, rate, or risk is displayed.

### SAP design, endpoints, and methods

7. **DOC-002 pp. 119-120.** The SAP describes a multicentre prospective open randomized non-inferiority trial. The primary objective is 28-day survival with favourable neurological function, TI versus BVM; secondary objectives include hospital-admission/28-day survival, neurological outcomes, immediate/serious adverse events, and difficulty of intubation/ventilation. Interim analyses were planned but cancelled because of rapid enrollment; only a final analysis was done and no multiplicity adjustment was made.
8. **DOC-002 p. 120 — sample-size display.** Historical inputs: BVM favourable-neurological survival 3%; TI 2%; non-inferiority margin 1%; 956 patients per group; power 0.8; type-I error 0.025; total recruitment 2,000; 5,000 simulations; Newcombe-Wilson score method. These are planning quantities, not observed trial outcomes.
9. **DOC-002 p. 121 — hypotheses and populations.** H0: pi(BVM)-pi(TI) <= -0.01 (BVM inferior); H1: pi(BVM)-pi(TI) >= -0.01 (BVM not inferior). The SAP says all tests are two-sided with alpha 0.05. Main analysis uses ITT and PP; it defines ITT as randomized patients analyzed by randomization group, PP as randomized and treated without major violations/deviations, and AT as by ventilation technique actually received.
10. **DOC-002 p. 122.** The primary parameter is day-28 survival with favourable neurological function, defined as CPC <=2. Secondary parameters are hospital-admission survival, day-28 survival, ROSC rate, and IDS score for intubated patients. Safety measures include named intubation complications, BVM VAS difficulty, named BVM complications, and Han classification.
11. **DOC-002 p. 123.** Missing primary-endpoint data in ITT are imputed under worst case as no success; a sensitivity analysis with different replacement methods, including multiple imputation, is conditional on large PP/ITT differences. Secondary criteria receive no imputation. Continuous summaries include n, mean, SD, min/max, 25th/50th/75th quantiles, and two-sided 95% CIs; mean/median/min/max/SD use one additional decimal. Categorical percentages use all non-missing values as 100%, are rounded to one decimal, and may not sum to exactly 100%.
12. **DOC-002 p. 124.** Primary non-inferiority is based on a two-sided 95% CI for pi(BVM)-pi(tracheal); non-inferiority is accepted if its lower limit is greater than -0.01, with exact CI if needed, for ITT/PP/AT. For rate-type secondary criteria, chi-square tests on proportions and 95% CIs for odds ratios and differences are planned; quantitative criteria use t test or Mann-Whitney according to distribution. Secondary PP/AT analyses are planned. Tests are two-tailed, type-I error 0.05, `P<0.05` significant, using SAS 9.4. Safety is ITT/AT; serious/non-serious AEs are tabulated per group; dichotomous endpoints use chi-square or Fisher exact and a 95% OR CI.

## Cross-source mapping notes and limitations

- The SAP's primary outcome wording (`day-28 survival`, `CPC <=2`) directly matches the post-hoc eTable outcome label. The SAP's BVM-versus-TI contrast and the eTable's explicitly signed `BMV(%) - ETI(%)` absolute difference align at the label level.
- The eTable post-hoc denominators (971/978 and 863/1174) are analysis-specific and are not interchangeable with the eTable 1 randomized-group denominators (1018/1022). The stated exclusions/reclassification explain that they represent different analysis populations; no subtraction is inferred beyond what is printed.
- The SAP's `P<0.05` significance convention and its chi-square/Fisher options are the supplied methodological context for eTable 2. Row-specific selection, sidedness beyond its stated P-value note, and CI construction for the post-hoc table are not supplied.
- DOC-002 p. 134 remains an explicit unmapped-content limitation because it is empty in both current-run text modes and has no permitted OCR. All other assigned source pages received a page-level map.
