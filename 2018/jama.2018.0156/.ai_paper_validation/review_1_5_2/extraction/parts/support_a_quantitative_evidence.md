## Original-protocol statistical design and rules

| Evidence ID | Exact source location | Printed definition/value | Relationship or use |
|---|---|---|---|
| AS001 | DOC-002 PDF pp. 11, 35-37 (printed pp. 10, 34-36) | Two-arm prospective randomized open non-inferiority study. Main analysis uses both ITT (all randomised, regardless of treatment received) and PP (randomised and treated without major violation/deviation). | Establishes analysis populations and trial objective. |
| AS002 | DOC-002 PDF pp. 11, 36-37 (printed pp. 10, 35-36) | Primary ITT calculation: two-sided `95%` CI for `pi_bag - pi_tracheal`; non-inferiority accepted if lower CI bound is `> -0.01`; exact rather than asymptotic CI if necessary. | Primary contrast is an absolute success-proportion difference, bag-mask minus tracheal intubation, with a 1-percentage-point margin. |
| AS003 | DOC-002 PDF p. 36 (printed p. 35) | H0: `pi_bag - pi_tracheal <= -0.01`; H1: `pi_bag - pi_tracheal >= -0.01`; after non-inferiority, a difference test will be performed. | Restates intended decision rule. Endpoint and CI direction agree with AS002. |
| AS004 | DOC-002 PDF pp. 11, 37 (printed pp. 10, 36) | Secondary rate/proportion outcomes: chi-square test on proportions; `95%` CI for odds ratio and differences. Quantitative outcomes: t test or Mann-Whitney according to Gaussian/non-Gaussian distribution. | Defines test families conditional on outcome type/distribution, but supplies no observed test outputs in this scope. |
| AS005 | DOC-002 PDF pp. 11, 36 (printed pp. 10, 35) | Interim analyses after `50%` and `75%` of inclusion, for futility and/or sample-size recalculation using ADDPLAN. | Planned interim timing; not an observed analysis result. |
| AS006 | DOC-002 PDF pp. 11, 37 (printed pp. 10, 36) | Sample size assumptions: BVM favourable-neurological survival `3%`; TI `2%`; margin `1%`; `956` patients/group; power `0.8`; type-I error `0.025`; total recruitment `2000`; based on `5000` simulations using Newcomb-Wilson score method. | Sample-size definition. `956 x 2 = 1912`, leaving 88 above the stated minimum pair total within a planned total of 2000. |
| AS007 | DOC-002 PDF p. 37 (printed p. 36) | Serious adverse events tabulated by treatment; dichotomised endpoints analysed by chi-square proportions with a `95%` OR CI; exploratory prognostic-factor analysis by multivariable logistic regression. | Distinguishes planned safety and exploratory analyses from the primary absolute-difference non-inferiority analysis. |
| AS008 | DOC-002 PDF p. 37 (printed p. 36) | Missing primary-endpoint data: ITT worst-case imputation (no success); if large PP/ITT differences, sensitivity analyses using alternate replacements including multiple imputation. | Missing-data rule applies to primary endpoint and is needed for cross-document interpretation of denominators. |
