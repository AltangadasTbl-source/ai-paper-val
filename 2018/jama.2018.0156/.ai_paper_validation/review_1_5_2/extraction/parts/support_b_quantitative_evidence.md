## Candidate signals retained for later checking (not adjudications)

1. The SAP (DOC-002 p. 122) lists its safety-endpoint components without aspiration pneumonia, whereas the later v2 protocol amendment explicitly adds aspiration pneumonia (pp. 114-116). This is a version-date/definition difference; it is not itself a candidate because the SAP is dated 18 Feb 2015 and the amendment is later. Exact final analysis specification, if needed for a matched result, is not supplied in this shard.
2. The SAP primary endpoint uses CPC <=2 (DOC-002 p. 122), while the final protocol expressly qualifies pre-randomization neurologic disability (p. 110). The published paper retains CPC 1-2. The supplied sources do not establish that this qualification changed any reported count; retain as a definition-comparison key only.

No candidate ID, severity, or disposition is assigned in this mapping artifact.
