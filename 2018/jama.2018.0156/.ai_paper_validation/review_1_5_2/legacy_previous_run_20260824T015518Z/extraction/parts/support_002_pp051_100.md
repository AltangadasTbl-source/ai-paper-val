## Result-relevant quantitative and comparison keys

The detailed numeric relationships are assigned shard-local identifiers in `relationships/parts/support_002_numeric.md`; inferential definitions are in `statistics/parts/support_002_statistical.md`.

Key support-to-main matching fields exposed by this shard are: treatment contrast (initial bag-valve-mask ventilation versus tracheal intubation); primary endpoint (28-day survival with favorable neurologic function, CPC <=2); outcome time point (day 28, with operational follow-up window +7 days); primary analysis population (ITT, supplemented by PP); effect scale (difference pi_bag - pi_tracheal); noninferiority margin (-0.01); and 95% two-sided CI decision rule.

## Extraction limitations

- No CAAM participant-level or aggregate outcome results appear on these PDF pages; background figures are explicitly cited external studies and are retained only as labelled protocol context.
- The IDS table on p. 52 was transcribed from authorized supplied OCR because the fresh page text contained its heading only. Its printed symbols are partially degraded (for example, OCR renders subscripts inconsistently); the component descriptions and category thresholds are retained, while the exact typographic notation of every component subscript is not asserted beyond what the supplied OCR shows.
