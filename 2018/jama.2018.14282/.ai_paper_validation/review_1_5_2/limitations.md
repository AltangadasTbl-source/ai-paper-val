# Limitations and Missing Definitions

- The package supplies four PDFs but no patient-level data, statistical code, model output, standard errors for most reported effects, unrounded estimates, or exact production rounding rules. Mechanical checks therefore use printed identities and do not infer unreported conventions.
- Nineteen stable records concern one-decimal count/percentage production. Their printed counts and displayed denominators are available, but the source-specific rounding or complementary-percentage rule is not stated.
- C017 lacks the unrounded confidence-interval values and any definition of distinct abstract versus narrative calculations.
- C018 is explicitly introduced as an overall-population statement; its remaining issue is denominator communication and possible within-arm-risk misreading, not incorrect overall-denominator arithmetic.
- C022 lacks the actual Gray/log-rank test output, event/censoring data, and confirmation of which procedure produced each printed P=.17.
- C023-C024 lack unrounded hazard ratios/intervals and a figure reference-group definition. Their printed results are near reciprocal and compatible with hidden precision; direct reciprocals of all already-rounded inputs do not reproduce every displayed digit.
- C025 lacks an authoritative change history explaining the main article's 779 total versus two 389-person arms and the published protocol/SAP total of 778.
- C026-C028 lack definitive signed-estimand, intervention-label, and endpoint/time-horizon clarifications.
- Supplement figure plots expose labels, axes, risk sets, and printed markers but not underlying plotted patient-level or central-value data; the meaning/threshold of the eFigure 2 significance markers is not supplied.
- DOC-002 pp.78 and 129 are sparse/blank non-result pages. All result-relevant pages had usable native/layout text, so no OCR was necessary.

These limitations leave the specified human questions open. They do not constitute adjudications, authorize ID suppression, or establish an effect on the paper's conclusions.
