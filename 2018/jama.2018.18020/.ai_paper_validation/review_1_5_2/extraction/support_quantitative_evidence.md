## Cross-support occurrence keys and handoff notes

1. **Published analyzed population:** DOC-003 eTable 3/eFigure 2 state N=27; eFigure 1 explains 4 with two sets plus 23 with one set, and eTable 3 notes patients 3,6,7 dropped out. DOC-003 eTable 2 lists baseline information for 30 patients. This is a population-flow relationship, not an assumption that every baseline row has an interim analysis.
2. **Published adverse-event key:** eTable 4 uses apparent n=30 from its count/percent combinations. Patient 7’s serious toxicodermia on eFigure 3 has matching event label/timing evidence.
3. **Effect direction/threshold key:** across DOC-003 code, figures, and interim table, the meaningful clinical difference is 0.75 IVR points and effect is placebo minus mexiletine; positive values favor lower stiffness with mexiletine. Compare posterior probabilities only on this same scale/direction.
4. **Protocol-only key:** DOC-002 plans n=30, flexible 1-4 sets, 200 mg three times/day, an 0.75-point threshold, and individual Bayesian interim rules. It does not print the published trial’s outcome estimates; any later cross-source check must call out prospective versus reported context.

- **S records:** S501-S534 (34 inferential-statistical relationship records, including 27 individual interim results).
- **No OCR limitation:** fresh native/layout text was usable, and rendered-page checks were sufficient; no OCR was needed.

Their quantitative result-defining labels/timing were mapped from native/layout evidence and relevant rendered pages; no unreported numeric outcome is inferred from diagram geometry.
