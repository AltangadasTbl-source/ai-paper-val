## Numeric and operational relationship inventory

### N-PB01 — Handgrip series and relaxation windows

Source [DOC-002 p.33](../../../../joi180136supp1_prod.pdf#page=33), PLAN. Within-patient repeated measurements: five ×3-second MVICs with 10 seconds rest; compare initial/final contraction relaxation; three sets separated by 10 minutes. Times defined by 90%→10%, 90%→50%, 90%→75% MVIC. Unit is relaxation duration (time); smaller duration represents less myotonia. Figure 2 is a measurement photograph, no plotted data. Main matching keys: quantitative handgrip myotonia, first/fifth contractions, warm-up, relaxation-time endpoint.

### N-PB02 — Biceps force and EMG definitions

Source [DOC-002 p.34](../../../../joi180136supp1_prod.pdf#page=34), PLAN. Three MVC measurements define maximal force by peak; 60% MVC contractions ≥30 seconds, 10-minute rests. Needle EMG minimum 500 ms, three nonendplate areas; 1+/2+/3+ ordered discharge intensity thresholds, with 2+ requiring >half locations. Main keys: biceps transient paresis/force, EMG graded myotonia. No numerical trial result.

### N-PB03 — Safety stopping thresholds

Sources [DOC-002 p.38](../../../../joi180136supp1_prod.pdf#page=38) and [p.39 Figure 3](../../../../joi180136supp1_prod.pdf#page=39), PLAN. Figure: individual ECG triggers second-/third-degree AV block, prolonged QT >500 ms, QRS >150 ms, other serious ECG abnormality according to cardiologist; individual serum mexiletine >2 µg/mL therapeutic range or noncompliance 0–0.25 µg/mL triggers stop; any cardiac AE/SAE triggers individual stop. Figure population review wording: cardiac events in “more than 10% (three patients)” or ≥2 same cardiac event, then convene/review and decision continue/stop. Text and figure are separately retained, including event categories and inequality wording. Main keys: safety monitoring, exposure, individual discontinuation, population stopping.
