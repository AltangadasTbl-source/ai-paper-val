# Construction record — 2024 v.2.3.1

The read-only preflight matched `baseline_inventory.json` exactly, including all seven source SHA-256 hashes, 10 paper sheets and 91 unique keys. The annual baseline was v.2.1.5.3, not a later version. Its USAGE, DESIGN, VALIDATION, actual annual workbook, maintenance/build.mjs, codebook.json and source catalog were read. The catalog's 2024 Candidates/Evidence/Links/Relations subset equals the annual source sheets cell-for-cell. Annual membership was taken from that workbook, not DOI years. No old builder was executed. No other year was built or modified.

Construction uses Python 3.12.3 and local openpyxl 3.1.5 / et_xmlfile 2.0.0, installed in `.venv`. The initial sandbox network attempt failed DNS resolution; a normal approved network installation succeeded. No global packages were changed. Dependencies, package cache, source code, reports and fixtures remain inside this year directory. There is no runtime automation.

`build_v_2_3_1.py` loads the annual workbook and preserves the four source sheets' values, all paper rows and A–C/E baseline content. It retains the navy/white headings, wrapped text, freeze panes, common/required yellow, optional blue and inactive gray appearance. E/H/K widths are expanded to accommodate option codes. Blank M–Q spacers are narrowed to width 3 and hidden. Paper sheets use an A:R AutoFilter instead of Excel Tables, so spacer headers can be truly blank. Whole-row sorts must include A:R. Navigation to paper sheets, embedded evidence and original sources is added. Original source href cell text is preserved; hyperlink relationships are separately rebased for the output directory. Final release links are tested from the final directory.

All obsolete workflow sheets, names, validations, conditional formats and formulas are replaced; Primary_snapshot is removed. Setup contains one identity, optional name, role and year. Start, Checks, Decisions, Paper_counts, Protocol_plan_table, Codebook and Manifest describe only independent primary review. Scientific option definitions are copied without changes except an appended clarification for discuss. Source narrative and historical evidence paths may contain words such as `checkers`; these frozen evidence references are not review logic and are intentionally preserved.

D/R and identity are blank, F=no, and E remains the editable frozen AI suggestion. H–L contain editable applicability formulas. G checks identity including ASCII/control/nonbreaking whitespace, valid dispositions/domains, within-paper key uniqueness/membership, applicable required and optional values, stale inactive values and valid completion flags. Results use INDEX/MATCH candidate-key lookups rather than row-position matching; normal Excel formulas provide all review-time calculations. Automatic full recalculation on open is enabled. Inputs stay editable regardless of domain or formula result.

Decisions contains this reviewer's effective assessment and outcomes only. P_R/P_P precedence is retained. S_C/S_W substantive confirmed/probable statistical findings are primary_temporal; substantive report-internal findings are secondary_temporal. discuss has no outcome. Paper_counts explicitly adapts the old result gate: every row must be primary-ready and none may be discuss before outcome counts appear. Independent completion and unresolved totals remain separate.

**Protection policy:** no protected ranges were introduced. Rule_defaults is visible and unprotected. All cell styles, named styles, row/column dimension styles and used cells are explicitly unlocked; serialized cellXfs/cellStyleXfs and absence of sheet/workbook protection are checked. The first staging build exposed an implicit locked dimension style. It was corrected before release; the two failed pre-release artifacts were removed and rebuilt. Intermediate workbooks under staging are construction artifacts, not reviews or deliverables.

Both blank roles were constructed from the same baseline before any review. The only cell-value differences are Start B2, Setup B4 and Manifest B5, which identify the role. Synthetic edits are isolated in tests. Release templates were copied with exclusive file creation and revalidated at their final paths. Source hashes were rechecked afterward. The builder refuses other years, paths outside this year, release targets and overwriting existing workbook names. Future corrections require a separately named version and review of changes; never regenerate a completed review.

Validation details and limitations are in VALIDATION.md and release/validation_report.json. The bounded Python formula probe is supplemental construction testing, not a substitute for Excel or a compatible calculation/rendering engine. No formula cache recalculation, desktop Excel test or visual rendering is claimed.
