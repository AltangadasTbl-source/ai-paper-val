# 2018 independent primary review — v.2.3.1

The two release workbooks each contain the same 10 papers and 119 candidates. Assign two different people to R_A and R_B and give each person only their corresponding file. Role labels do not identify people. Each reviewer completes their own assessment independently.

1. Open your assigned template and Save As a working copy in the same folder, preserving relative source links. Enter your own reviewer ID in Setup B2 and optionally your name in B3.
2. Use Papers to open each C_ sheet. Click candidate keys for embedded Evidence. Read Candidates, Links and Relations, and consult original documents through the source hyperlinks.
3. Choose D: retain, exclude or discuss. Confirm or change the AI-suggested domain in E. Fill applicable H–L judgments using the Codebook. Add optional English notes in R.
4. After manually reviewing a row, set F to primary_done. G should show PRIMARY READY. Inspect Checks and the readiness column in Decisions for unfinished work.
5. When Checks shows PRIMARY READY, save, close and deliver your completed workbook. Keep ordinary Excel backups. Results are already in Decisions, Paper_counts and Protocol_plan_table.

For retained statistical_results, H/I are required and J is optional. For report_internal_inconsistency or plan_material, K is required. For protocol_report, L is optional. All other judgments are inactive. D and a valid E are required even for exclude/discuss; their H–L judgments must be cleared or not_applicable.

Untouched judgment formulas show blank when potentially applicable and not_applicable otherwise. Selecting an answer replaces that formula. A blank is no answer. Changing domain or disposition does not erase a literal answer: clear stale inactive values and fill newly required fields. Optional active judgments may be blank; not_applicable is invalid when active.

Before changing a completed row, manually set F=no, revise and review, then set primary_done again. Excel cannot automatically erase an entered completion flag.

Discuss records an unresolved independent assessment and may still be PRIMARY READY. Checks displays its total separately. Its outcome remains blank. A paper's outcome counts remain blank until every row is ready and none is discuss. P_R/P_P retained findings are descriptive, never temporal.

All cells are editable, including formulas and source cells. There are no protected ranges, passwords or unlock steps. Edit only your inputs during ordinary review; preserve keys, formulas and source material. Undo accidental edits or recover your backup.

Use Excel alone for review, calculations, saving and delivery. Do not run construction scripts or regenerate a reviewed file. No macros, refresh, synchronization, external workbook, or runtime automation is required. Embedded evidence remains available even if original source links are unavailable. Moving a working copy to a different directory can break relative links; keep it in the release folder or locate originals manually.
