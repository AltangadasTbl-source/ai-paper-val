eTable 1. Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria
Inclusion criteria:
     (1) aged 18 to 80 years
     (2) with histologically confirmed gastric adenocarcinoma located in the lower or middle third of the stomach
     (3) staged as clinical T4aN0–3M0 based on preoperative imaging
     (4) Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 – 1
     (5) American Society Anesthesiology physical status (ASA-PS) score of I – III
Exclusion Criteria
     (1) pregnancy or breastfeeding women
     (2) bulky lymph nodes
     (3) tumor invaded to the duodenum
     (4) previous gastric surgery
     (5) severe tumor-related complications such as bleeding or perforation required to emergency surgery
     (6) prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy
     (7) receiving neoadjuvant chemotherapy
     (8) other malignancies within the past 5 years
     (9) severe conditions contraindicating laparoscopy.
